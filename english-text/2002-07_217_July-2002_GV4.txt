[GV4:P0067] July 2002

[GV4:P0068] We have an outdated worshipping habit of getting things done by offering something insignificant to God. This is not the way; we should worship God to seek His grace. What should be done to attain God's grace? We have a duty towards God. We must understand it and perform it with a sense of duty. Because we have not done so, we have been committing errors in our worship of God for a long time.

[GV4:P0069] There is discord between husband and wife. As they continue to quarrel into their old age, their children will also follow suit. This is what I have observed to this day. Only in a few homes is this lack of culture absent. You have never entrusted your children with the knowledge of what is not being done for you here at the ashram. You have not entrusted them with it until today. I do not know what will be done tomorrow.

[GV4:P0070] God does not need your vows and offerings. Are we supposed to offer vows and offerings just to find a righteous path? If you wish to offer something, you may do so. Why? When such a custom has emerged in society, you can do so to learn some kind of lesson from it. That can be done in a temple or an ashram. However, that is not the matter I am discussing with you regarding the worship of God. There is a state of ignorance that our ancestors have perpetuated to this day. By uttering certain words in the very nature of that ignorance and trying to triumph through those words, you are holding on to certain karmic flaws.

[GV4:P0071] Wherever you go in the hilly regions, people speak in grandiose terms and act as if they possess great culture. However, not even a trace of culture can be found in them. By constantly exhibiting their culture, they have become like Westerners showing off their culture. What is that culture? It is only an external culture. It is not internal. They have decided that internal culture is unnecessary. This has infiltrated every home and its inner core.

[GV4:P0072] Since the practice of making vows and offerings has been going on for a long time, it has evolved within the temple system and grown to a large scale. In order to resolve the complications caused by this, God has imparted a certain knowledge to us over various periods. It was given through numerous great souls. Yet, we still have not understood that knowledge today.

[GV4:P0073] Christians are the community that habitually preaches the greatest sacrifice. But is there sacrifice among Christians now? The meaning of sacrifice is to practice living truthfully. Are Christians living truthfully now? Christians stand like compasses pointing towards the West. Other than the culture of the home remaining among some poor people, it does not exist in a single household that has enough to eat, drink, and wear. Why? It is the Hindus who have shifted towards that today. They have also adopted the practice of vows and offerings similar to that of the Hindus. They are even prepared to commit any kind of violence in the name of God.

[GV4:P0074] Muslims claim that they govern according to laws determined by God. Is the divine law that one can commit any kind of plunder? If this is the situation within the Islamic religion, which emerged only a short time ago, Hindus possess a history that has undergone immense changes over time. Hindus embraced oracular deities and became devoid of culture. When their authority waned, they attempted to appear great by displaying foolishness. Similarly, there is caste, and castes within castes. Those who belonged to a higher caste began to live by flaunting its greatness. Those who were not considered great began to live like Westerners. Because of this, humanity has drifted away from its true nature. As human nature drifted away, a realization that both men and women need freedom has spread.

[GV4:P0075] The sense of freedom is good. But what is freedom? It is assumed that freedom is men and women properly arguing and justifying their stances. That is not freedom. What shortcomings have we experienced in the past? The women in the house must know this. What was the situation amidst those shortcomings? How did it happen? Teach them this first. Due to those shortcomings, we have received a misguided life. When the uncultured Western culture began to spread, it became even worse.

[GV4:P0076] Aside from presenting models of sacrifice, there is no actual sacrifice among Christians. Who has become Christians? Those who lived as the most downtrodden among the Hindus. When they acquire money and capability, they will not stay grounded. They will only behave without any manners. Today, Christians and Muslims in Kerala have become wealthy. However, their culture is completely different. They speak eloquently with sweet words, but they will not translate it into action. This is a path towards danger.

[GV4:P0077] God has given you the most suitable opportunity. But you will not take it. I will only speak and behave truthfully with you. What do you do? You squander it. What can I do? What kind of freedom have you envisioned? It is an inferiority complex born from having lived in servitude. This is one kind of freedom. Another is the freedom of an arrogant life. Yet another is the freedom derived from fighting over caste and religion. Is this the freedom of humanity?

[GV4:P0078] Have you felt this idea to be true? If so, you are the ones who must put it into practice. It is for you to implement that I have done this. People assume that this is how freedom should be sought. This freedom is not freedom. It is a freedom that drags our children into error.

[GV4:P0079] The greatest ignorance lies in the hands of the educated. In uneducated children, flaws are seen much less. Wherever wealth, capability, and education have risen, arrogance has also risen. The culture of Mayyanad, the culture of Kottayam, and looking more broadly, Ernakulam, Kochi, and then a different culture in Kozhikode. Thus, how many types of cultures exist in Kerala itself! If you are imparting such a cultureless knowledge, what have you decided to give to your children? I am asking those who come and reside here. The reason you Hindus, Christians, Muslims, and people from Brahmins to Chandalas come and stay here is because I have love and obligation towards you, and you towards me. I have taught you after having done something myself. But that is not what you have perceived. You live here as if carrying a resentment that is the greatest accumulation of an undignified life. You must not bring your old resentment in here and express it. If you express it, your children will be lost. Then I will not be able to do anything. Why do you commit such perversity here? I am telling you this after addressing the flaw that has come down through your lineage, and altering even the freedom of worship to a different path so that the culture of your home may change. But you are unable to think, understand, or obey it.

[GV4:P0080] I have eradicated the tradition that you have been worshipping. I have not done this by looking at whether it is Hindu, Christian, Muslim, or Buddhist. The core of that old culture has been uprooted from your tradition. Have I ever considered whether you are a Brahmin or a Chandala? What I am asking is whether you have ever thought that at least one child who has lived here to date should grow up in this new culture. The experience I have received from everyone who worked near me, and from their entire families, is pain. If that is not the case, you must say so.

[GV4:P0081] Why have I done this for you? If you did not need it, then why did you make me do it? Is anyone among you prepared to live according to that standard and show it to me? Are you living here to corrupt this concept given by God and ruin yourselves?

[GV4:P0082] After taking hold of all your old baseness and doing the work to liberate you from it, the first thing you are told is that you must abandon the principles of caste and class (Jati-Varna Dharmas). You must tell me if you have abandoned them. If you had abandoned them, this is not how you would be behaving here. Is it not a culture forcibly imposed from somewhere that you are holding onto as your asset? Is this why you are being nurtured with such great effort? Have I done things for you while preserving my own self-interest? Please tell me; I want to know this from you yourselves.

[GV4:P0083] You should not assume that I am speaking only about the matter of caste. I categorically state that not a single one of you has been careful in any matter. What is the purpose of sugarcoating these pleasant words to me? For what reason did you come here, believe in this, and reside here? That is what you must tell me. If anyone has acted with genuine affection towards this concept, towards me, and towards the institution, tell me that. Other than living engrossed in the act of feigning prosperity and friendship wherever you look to create a non-existent wealth, what have you done for this? If you do not wish to instill your culture in your children, you must move away from here. Otherwise, you will only leave after destroying this and turning it into a culture of division.

[GV4:P0084] You are performing actions with your focus on some distant goal. Whom are you trying to appease; are you showing this baseness to appease God? If you exhibit this anymore, I will not stand with you. Those who remain within and those associated with this should act only after understanding that. I am saying this to you with absolute certainty. You only need to believe in this, live here, and associate with this if you have the resolve to live truthfully. You do not need to say anything for me to hear. You just need to live and show me. I do not need to hear you say anything. I will not believe it.

[GV4:P0085] You have become the Shudra class. Any mistake that arises from you is not intentional. When you act, it will only happen that way. How many among you came having perceived this before seeing me physically! You can increase your spiritual merit only if you handle this with the understanding that it is a manifestation of divine will. Otherwise, neither doctrines nor miraculous feats are being preached here. What will you gain if I perform miracles?

[GV4:P0086] Someone performed a miracle. Someone portrayed themselves as a great person. Because of that, what inner peace do we attain? You are witnessing this directly. You are directly experiencing spiritual merit. There are so many people who have known me for a very long time. Because they lacked familial fortune, they went astray. What is meant by familial fortune? Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, Shudra. It has been understood that this is the era where the class standing in the fourth quarter possesses authority, hasn't it? What authority? The Shudra class holds the authority for the knowledge of Brahman. When speaking of the Shudra class, there is a Brahmin and a Shudra in each of our homes. This is the rationale. But this is not what has been conveyed to us. Instead, Varnasrama Dharma was celebrated, and we were hidden under that class and reduced to a caste. This has grown to such an extent that no one can do anything about it. Today, if we take India as a whole, there are one hundred and eighty-four castes. Let each person reflect upon where we have reached.

[GV4:P0089] Experience, Not Vedanta, Is the Truth

[GV4:P0090] For us to breathe, the lungs must function. For the lungs to function, there must be a sky around us. We observe, or rather state, that there are numerous spheres around this atmospheric nature of the earth. What do we see within this solar system?

[GV4:P0091] We shattered the skies of interstellar space and reached the moon. Even having broken through thus, other than revolving around the sun, what would happen if we crossed beyond it? We become bewildered upon hearing the stories of these journeys. In the rotation involving the sun, the moon, and the earth, other spheres do not catch our eyes. Yet, they certainly exist. We have not yet seen what exists in these spheres.

[GV4:P0092] Navajyothi! The word Navajyothi means nine types of light. We are the people who, even after seeing these nine types of light, claim they do not exist. If this is not visible, then where has it been hidden? When a celestial vibration occurs amidst the layers of clouds standing on parallel borders, our eyes cannot reach its subtlety. It exists. But we do not understand it. Therefore, we do not know how to apply it. If we deny the existence of what truly is, we cannot move beyond it. Every discovery has a limitation. That is all we need to say. Do not say it does not exist. That is what is good for us.

[GV4:P0093] We feel utterly afraid to recognize what is good. Here, we fail. Growing from small to great, or growing in the arrogance of education, wealth, and mastery, how far will we go? We have a human experience that cannot recognize this. It has come through our customs. We currently stand in this predicament.

[GV4:P0094] It is said that Brahmins are the ones who destroyed everything in the nature of customs. How does that Brahmin's house look? Did they prosper after destroying it? If there are twenty-five houses in a village, at least two girls in them will be found unmarried. What is the reason for that? If they did not prosper, then who are you saying did prosper? We do not know what the deep roots of this are.

[GV4:P0095] It is said that God is formless. And that is true. How far can each of us reach on this path that remains formless? That is why the matter of the solar system was mentioned earlier. No matter how many times these nine colors are multiplied, or in what all ways they are handled, they will still remain. Who said this color does not exist? This color is not something that can be eliminated merely by saying so. This is what was referred to as the limitation encountered in experience. If someone denies the existence of what truly is, why should you believe them? If an item is requested and it is not in that particular place, does it mean the item does not exist at all? Whether one is a rationalist or a salvation-seeker, what exists simply exists. It cannot be eliminated.

[GV4:P0096] We state certain justifications regarding Yuga Dharma (the law of the age). However, it has merely not been understood that what is stated is Yuga Dharma. As long as it is not understood, we cannot complete a transition phase. For example, the time is now such-and-such—after a while, this light will assume a different nature. Progressing in this way, there will be a junction for it. Similarly, a junction occurs after every three hours. We are experiencing this junction. But we are unable to resolve it. In this situation, how will we resolve the Yugasandhi (the junction of the ages)? This is what was referred to as our limitation.

[GV4:P0097] We do not know how the juncture of the ages (*Yugasandhi*) operates. It is to make this known that the great souls (*Mahatmas*) were made to act. They strove in various ways for such a long time. It is in this context that we celebrate Sree Krishna, Sree Rama, Sree Shankara, Jesus, the Prophet, Buddha, and Mahavira. We cannot move forward by saying that none of them existed or made any effort. But we need to know the extent to which they have operated. For which era did they work? We know the different times in which they operated. However, there is a distinct period between the times they worked. What we do not know is how to determine the nature of that distinct period.

[GV4:P0098] We must determine this. Only after that should we move forward. Otherwise, errors will occur. That will not be an error like the ones that have happened so far. It will be a much greater blunder. We need an instrument to determine this. And you also need the knowledge to accept that instrument. Will a deaf and ignorant person be able to hear if their head is drilled? In the present situation, each of us is a fool. Let this foolishness remain. But we must surely overcome it.

[GV4:P0099] How can we slip and fall at the juncture of the times when the people called great souls have operated? We cannot proceed by abandoning these great men. If we say that Jesus does not belong to our religion, does it mean that Jesus is not the truth? Would it be right to say that Sree Shankara and Sree Rama are not ours? What will we do with that reality which does not disappear even if we reject it? By not accepting this, we become complete strangers in the path of God. To avoid becoming strangers like this, at least believe that all these truly exist. The one who says they do not exist is not going to reach anywhere. They certainly exist. How should we view this reality?

[GV4:P0100] The state in which we all exist and act is the waking state (*Jagrat*). Similarly, we experience the dream state (*Swapna*) and deep sleep (*Sushupti*). Beyond this knowledge, there is visionary knowledge. It is our soul (*Jiva*) that acquires that knowledge. What is the state of knowledge that the soul perceives beyond the waking, dream, and deep sleep states? That is *Turiya* (the fourth state). It is in that knowledge that we can comprehend the courses and dynamics of our various lifetimes. We must perceive this. If we cannot perceive it, we must believe what those who have perceived it tell us. There is no other logic to this. We must have the karmic awareness to understand and painstakingly acquire what the seers impart. Everyone needs this awareness. In the journey of the soul, we can realize that even a blade of grass, if it exists, needs this awareness. This is the knowledge we attain in the state of *Turiya*.

[GV4:P0101] To what stage of this knowledge did these great souls reach and speak of before they departed! No one should say that these states of knowledge of the soul do not exist just because they are explained with such grand words. This is real. How will we perceive this reality? It may not be that all of us perceive it subtly. Acknowledge its existence to the extent of our experience. Believe the words of those who say there is more beyond it. If you cannot agree with it, you may choose not to believe it as well.

[GV4:P0102] Regardless, it is a truth that there is a formless space. Which heaven (or ether) this formless space is, comes later. It is from there that one perceives what exists. Saying "Alas, I am defeated" also happens from that very state. One aspect of this is saying, "O Lord, what have you shown me, who firmly believed that there is nothing other than You." Reaching the state of what was said, they stood there. That is the position where that religion stands. We should not say it is not so. Whatever those who saw and spoke said is true to the extent they have seen. Just because we have not seen the boundary of truth, we should not say that nothing exists beyond it. Saying so does not make it non-existent. What we need to know is how far this reality operates. This is where these great souls have reached and halted. If they faced a limit, it is no wonder that we also hit a wall.

[GV4:P0103] The great souls have taught us from the point they had reached. There is no need to say that it was too little or too much. You may not even be able to experience and move past what has been stated here. To experience it in such a manner, there must be another dimension to it. That is the truth of *Guru-Laghutva* (the relationship of the heavy/great and the light/small).

[GV4:P0104] Otherwise, our ancestors existed. Our parents existed. Each of us is the lightness (*Laghutva*) of their discoveries. That lightness is what has come as the Guru. We perceive whatever conceptual power has arrived in the word 'Guru'. It is through that we attempt to see the boundless. That requires an innate predisposition (*Vasanajanyata*). Human beings who perceived everything in the position of saying "not this, not this" (*Neti, Neti*) lived here. There are such great souls who have passed away. We must understand that there are also those who are yet to come. We need an instrument to compare those who have passed, those who exist now, and those who are to come. That is what is called *Guru-Laghutva*.

[GV4:P0105] Some speak of Vedanta. Why speak of Vedanta? Isn't it enough to state what exists? Let us first understand what is. Then we can understand what is not. That which exists remains formless. It is beyond thought, akin to the sky. If it has such an ethereal nature, in what all states does it exist? This can only be possible if the Guru reveals it. There are many who have seen this. The Guru is not merely the one who sees, but the one who reveals it.

[GV4:P0106] Now, how will we comprehend *Laghutva* (lightness)? It was said that to see what exists, one must hold onto the rightful heir. The Guru is the one who tells where the seat of greatness lies and what that greatness looks like. From the Guru, one can know what Karma is. The Guru is the master of an intellect that creates greatness through instruction and practice. That is what one must hold onto. By utilizing that, we will reach a position entirely devoid of the defects of Karma. That position is the seat of Truth. It is from there that everything else must be perceived. That is *Guru-Laghutva*.

[GV4:P0107] Only that which exists can be shown. That which is said to not exist cannot be shown. It is merely a stream of words. This refers to the Karma resting in the evolutionary nature of the soul, which operates anywhere as the atom of an atom. Never say that it does not exist. It is real. The goal is to know how to understand that reality. Both men and women have equal rights to it.

[GV4:P0108] We must only proceed there after discerning the boundaries of the great souls. Suppose we cannot reach there. Does that mean it ceases to exist? We can attain it. But even if we reach it, there are difficulties; if we do not stand on the side of love, then it will cause harm.

[GV4:P0109] How can this reality be perceived across these three states of waking, dream, and deep sleep? Seeing what the Dharma resting in the subconscious mind is after that, is a special state. What is that state? Isn't it said in Tamil, *'Thoongamal thoongi'* (sleeping without sleeping). It is not sleeping, though others believe one is asleep. It is something attained in a state of absorption (*Laya*) with closed eyes. This can be seen with eyes closed or open. Only a great power that has reached the *Turiya* state can reveal a *Karmayoga* that permeates the waking, dream, and deep sleep states. How many heavens must be crossed for that is a secondary matter.

[GV4:P0112] The truthful aspects of life and the ethical aspects to be followed

[GV4:P0113] Some people may have faith in God. Some may not. Some people may not immediately understand if they see a novel model in the realm of faith. We cannot say if it is due to their fault or the fault of their land. Because that is the familiarity of the worship we have been maintaining. The majority are people who ask if it isn't enough to keep faith in the mind without showing it outwardly.

[GV4:P0114] Guru: Is faith enough in the mind, or should it be shown outwardly in action?

[GV4:P0115] Answer: In the mind is enough.

[GV4:P0116] Guru: In that case, isn't it enough to eat with the mind? Isn't it enough to get married with the mind? After all, it is the mind that is given to God. God also resides within the mind itself. This is an arrogant attitude, speaking like those who are left over after everything is finished. This is not your own opinion. It is saying something you heard someone blabbering about.

[GV4:P0117] The cultural richness necessary for our homes is absent in any of the religious doctrines we have seen, heard, or known. What is the religious doctrine of the Hindus? One places a local deity (*Madan*) and addresses it as the Lord. Another attempts to reach *Brahman* directly. Yet another does neither, but approaches through a Guru. The one who tries to reach God directly can never be guided properly.

[GV4:P0118] It is not my duty to draw you closer by feigning affection. For when that affection is absent, will you not simply leave? Is it sufficient to keep you close merely by speaking of love? I shall speak of love; that is a different matter entirely. However, you will only grasp this if you absorb these earnestly spoken words with the gravity they deserve. Otherwise, it will slip away.

[GV4:P0119] There are several Devi temples in these regions, which many people celebrate with great pomp. Yet, these individuals never truly unite with one another. It is rare to find someone who does not display a sense of ego after expressing irritation towards another. An elder brother will maintain a stance of superiority. A younger brother will harbor the attitude that the other need not act superior. Even in the absence of outward conflict, they will stand in mental defiance. This is a distinct characteristic of South Travancore.

[GV4:P0120] What if one goes south to the Neyyattinkara taluk? Is there anyone there who does not boast about the greatness of their *Potti* (Brahmin) ancestors? All of this is a kind of mischief. Why engage in such boasting? What is there to gain for anyone? This is merely akin to claiming, 'My grandfather had an elephant'. It is difficult to change such a corrupt nature that one has heard, known, and witnessed since childhood. This is not the case of a single household or a single village. From Chirayinkeezhu southwards, this characteristic is widespread. Then there is the usage of certain terms like *Akkachi* and *Thankachi*. This represents a sort of culture from Tamil Nadu. The moment they cease to show happiness, they will start using abusive language. This culture is far worse than Western culture. Elder brothers, younger brothers, sisters, and siblings may exhibit a great deal of love and respect for each other during childhood. However, as they grow up, they display a rather repulsive attitude. What is the core intent behind my words? Where do you end up when the thoughts from your youth go astray as you move forward? What is the linguistic style here? 'Swami should say, should take, should give'—an inherently commanding tone. It is not spoken with malicious intent; it is simply their customary manner. But if you go north, this is considered a grave error. Every place has its distinct attire and language. Similarly, each caste has its own deity. The temple of an Ezhava will not suit a Nair. The temple of a Nair will not suit an Ezhava. Can a God thus divided ever be the true God? This is the fundamental blunder committed not just by India, but by the entire world.

[GV4:P0121] Only one or two members from your household will come here; the rest will not. If those who do not come fail to understand the ways here, they will end up quarreling with you. What will you do then? You will try to compromise with everyone. Can you abandon your home? Can you abandon this place either? What will your situation be then? Sorrow will never cease. You will be completely unable to form a deep bond with what you truly cherish.

[GV4:P0122] You perform *Guru Pooja* here. What is *Guru Pooja*? A Guru can be worshipped by offering a single drop of water, by lighting a lamp, or with elaborate decorations. In places like Kanchipuram, Rishikesh, Vrindavan, and Ayodhya, ascetics (*Sadhus*) are worshipped. What does the term *Sadhu* mean? There are people around you who live by doing manual labor and begging. Their souls possess no merit; they are bound by the power of sin. Hence, they are merely poor or destitute. *Sadhus* are not like that. Having experienced abundance in food, drink, and clothing to the point of satiation, they renounced everything. Such a person is entirely complete. A true householder (*Grihasthashrami*) should also be like this. Therefore, can you ever become a true householder if you constantly boast about someone else's greatness? What you must attain is the virtue of your own actions. That must first be received from God. You must realize that invoking God is not for God's sake, but entirely for your own.

[GV4:P0123] Before God, all are equal. Age is not a criterion for this. Some young children may possess greater wisdom than their elders. That is an attribute of their birth and the quality of their soul. Some are born with all the fortunes but end up unable to achieve any goodness. Born in the house of Kubera (the god of wealth), they turn into vagabonds. Conversely, one born as a vagabond may transform into a Kubera. Why did one become a vagabond? Was it by throwing away everything they had? One must understand that it is due to the inherent quality of their soul. The path to inquire, understand, or take the effort to discover how that soul came to be, and in what form, is not prevalent among us. We simply live reacting to what we see day by day. All the great beings have given indications of this.

[GV4:P0124] Who is the greatest? The gentle ones, or the virtuous (Sathukkal). These virtuous ones praise what they see day by day. Great sages praise what they see day by day - it means they spoke according to the era. Not according to the situation as you assume. What is the era you live in, thinking and making others think about how you should live in that era -- interacting with home, land, and country -- is Karma. Through this long association with the country, a misguided course of Karma has reached our hands. It has happened not just to one or two people, but to everyone. This familiarity has turned into the form of caste, religion, and class, and even God has been divided - thus the God of the Brahmin, the God of the Nair, the God of the Ezhava, the God of the Chandala. Those from Nair downwards are not allowed to chant the Vedas. Then what will become of him? He is a servant of the upper class. He cannot perform rituals directly; he cannot perform pooja. To perform pooja, one must study the Vedas. These are the mistakes that have come in the human condition. How do we change this degraded state?

[GV4:P0125] Some among you living as father, mother, wife, husband, and children may have good fortune. You must learn to safeguard that fortune. You should not handle that fortune in a manner thinking, "Father has made some, I too must make some, I can carry it if I want, or throw it away." Instead of this fortune, what if the father and mother have nothing, they need to be given food, medicine, and clothing, the sisters have all come of age, and there is nothing at all; what would you do if such a situation arises? You have everything, you do not need to go searching and suffering for anything. What should you do in this situation?

[GV4:P0126] How did fortune come about? How did misfortune come about? Most people assume that circumstances brought all this about. Those with knowledge will not understand any of these arguments. If someone who roams around playing and showing off in the streets is caught, bathed, and given good food and clothes, will he stay? So none of this is based on circumstances. All this lies in the part of the mind's knowledge. We do everything based on the knowledge of the mind. The foundation of the mind's knowledge is the course of Karma that has grown old with our home, land, and country. Nothing good will appeal to him who has grown old in this state. The only thing he can enjoy is the ways of the marketplace. Even if a life that has grown old like this is born into a family, he will only show the behavior of the marketplace.

[GV4:P0127] Before we were born, there were crores of years in which our ancestors lived and came into being along with our home and land. It is after that we have come to live for ten or a hundred years. Even in this, several years of childhood lived without knowing anything are gone. Then for some time, we lived taking care of household matters and other things, as a wife, husband, father, and mother. Your fortune was utilized for all these lives. That fortune existed in life as something acquired in the past. This fortune is of a lakh kinds for a lakh people and of a crore natures for a crore people. These differences are innate qualities of birth. This quality is the result of changes that occurred over eras. Through these eras, errors have also occurred.

[GV4:P0128] How can this error be changed? For that, one must perform Karma and acquire merit. But can we do any kind of Karma other than the Karma that is with us? That is, whatever habits and practices exist in the home and the land will be transferred to us as well. There is no good solely for us. Can a court or a police station be set up exclusively for each individual? If you cannot understand the good and bad of all this, the roles of the home and the country will all be transferred together. Thus, the character transferred over eras is what resides in each of you.
