Gurukalabrahmaniveditham

There was a time when the world had no realization about the Brahma determination or the glory of God. It does not mean that the knowledge is complete even now. Except for getting the opportunity to do only the things that were told from Brahma in those times, the world could not take that knowledge. The complete realization about the Brahma determination was seven

Only a Manvantara and three Chaturyugas. The great Sanat Satyatmanan came at the end of the third Chaturyuga. Although he knew that he was the heir to the Brahma resolution, the secret of his own life was not fully revealed to him until his last days.

I am stating here what has been revealed in my experience. I do not know if there is anything beyond this.

A Manvantara is the conception of the Saptarshis. By that conception, a solar system creation is formed. The solar system has nine planets with form and subject to the theory of evolution. The greatness of Rahu and Ketu in it has not been determined. These planets spread into twelve zodiac signs and give twelve results. It is not certain what results Rahu and Ketu should give in it. But there are destined things said for everything. An incompleteness is seen in both of these.

The Saptarshis gave a conception for a solar system creation in the calculation of nine planets, twelve zodiac signs, and twenty-seven stars. Scientifically and by some other experiences, there are those who call it seven spheres. These seven spheres have form and nature. But Rahu and Ketu sometimes travel out of place while circling like this. Therefore, we should not desire its full result. Because no one can determine how the time difference will be when traveling around in the karma of a Jupiter year. That is, their movement is not fixed. They travel in an unpredictable order.

There is a measure of Brahma light for the movement of the solar system. It may shift here and there and its effect may be felt. But it will not leave this sphere. If there is a difference of one minute in this movement, it will cause an error of up to a yama. It cannot be accurately determined. But in our calculation, there are minutes, seconds, nazhikas, vinazhikas, and even the most delicate trudi.

Whatever calculations there are, there should be no error in the greatness that came in the conception of the Saptarshis. That is the path of truth. That is human life.

There is a junction for Manvantaras, Chaturyugas, and Yamas. Sandhyavandanam is done as a karma to remove the Yamasandhi. There may be those who are awake for three yamas and at most five yamas. But we have been told the time of worship in all eight yamasandhis.

There is a calculation of five gurus for an era. These gurus are the ones who bring the calculation of Sandhyavandanam according to the Brahma resolution and the recognition of times. But we cannot say how many great people come in the Yugasandhi. In the four yugas, the Brahma resolution is described to us. If an error occurs in the Yugadharma at any time, that error cannot be recovered later. It takes a lot of effort for that. The lives and words of great people prove it.

Satyatmanan said the Chaturyakya. In knowledge, scholars and commoners fell into a pit in such a way that times could not be recognized. If you look at the past twenty-five Chaturyugas, this truth can be seen. Our subject is what was revealed about where the error occurred in this truth.

As proof that this mistake has occurred, there are lives that have crossed thousands of hurdles before us. The life of a human, a pitru, a deva, a rishi, and a sannyasi! Having seen what the life of a muni is in these lives, what the life of a rishi is, and coming down, what the life of a deva is, and what the life of a rishi under a deva is, the path guided by the rishi standing above the deva is the truth that exists through the words of the rishis. There was a time when nothing was known about this truth. A skill that was acquired by being told where this truth exists above the Trimurti system was completed in Varkala. That occasion was a time of physical and mental torture. It is impossible to count or finish telling what kind of torture it was. To take this back, we are indebted to that Brahma resolve that made that Brahma resolve a person. The life of mahatmas is one that has come without being able to decide what is right and wrong in the Manu lineage and how to fulfill it.

Six Manvantaras have passed. The calculation is seventy-one Chaturyugas for each Manvantara. Four Yugas make one Chaturyuga. The calculation is five Gurus for the resolution of each Yuga. The smallest of the Yugas is Kali Yuga. This itself is four lakh thirty-two thousand years. Dwapara is double this. Dwapara and Kali together make Treta Yuga. Treta Yuga and Kali together make Krita Yuga. The period from the birth of Sree Rama to the birth of Sree Krishna has come after many lakhs of years, at the rate of five Gurus for each Yuga. Sree Krishna was born as a great man who fully knew the Kali Dharma before the beginning of Kali. Manu has evaluated that Kali, the fourth quarter, belongs to the Shudra class. That is, Kali is the time when the full right of Brahmajnana is decreed to the Shudra, fully understanding all the subjects that are to be ruled and made to rule in the fourth quarter. It is as an example of this that Krishna was born in the Yadava clan. The original mistake occurred twenty-five Chaturyugas ago. It was mentioned earlier that the word Aham Brahmasmi originated from a great fall that occurred when a rishi named Satyathranan thought about honoring the truth and saw what he was and what his predecessors were, and took up the decisive truth that has come as Chaturyugas and Manvantaras. Later, when it was completed with four sentences, it was not conceptual completeness, but literal completeness that was brought about. That is the mistake that has been continuing for twenty-five Chaturyugas. Gurukula Brahmaniveditham is an action that corrects this mistake and brings about conceptual completeness. This ceremony used to be held in a different way before.

The Shudra class are ignorant, which is why it was easy to fool them. Every one in the Shudra class are children of that mistake. There was a ceremony in the past to start the alphabet at the age of five. This ceremony was performed equally for boys and girls. This had an advisory nature. This will be done before the age of five. There is no such ceremony among the Brahmin community. Among them, there is no ceremony other than starting the alphabet. The ceremony kept for this among the Shudra class is Kettukalyanam. Similarly, Thirandukalyanam, then Kalyanam, Pulikudi Kalyanam, etc. When a girl is born and so many ceremonies are performed, that house will definitely become impoverished. So if four girls are born, there is nothing to say.

A ceremony shown among the Brahmins is the Guru giving advice and starting the alphabet. What they show in the marriage ceremony is an action ordained by the birth of Vyasa. It was through a Sankalpa Karma that Vyasa got the name Krishna Dwaipayana. Vyasa told us as if it came from Krishna's mouth. Vyasa in which all Yugas

It is not certain whether it has come. It is widely accepted that Vyasa is the son of a fisherwoman. The mythological histories that divided and explained the four Vedas and the six Shastras are the prescribed, written truths for Hindus.
