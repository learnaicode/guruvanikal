[GV1:P2465] January 1990

[GV1:P2466] This is the Kali Yuga. Vedic mantras and conceptual rituals must be transformed to suit this epoch. The tenet 'One caste, one lineage' is an indicator of this transformation. Thus, we must become the children of one God. The monotheistic system that emerged in each era has been revealed to us in different manifestations. An ethereal voice—Prophet Muhammad described it as, 'Let him impart it.' Muhammad received this enlightenment at the age of forty through his wife. That is, it was his own wife who first proclaimed that the Prophet was a great soul.

[GV1:P2467] From then on, he began to preserve it. Relying on it, when the vision of prophethood emerged, he began to face opposition. Unable to withstand that opposition, he fled to safety. After enduring hellish torments for a certain period, he attained prophethood and returned. He fought and emerged victorious. However, the enemies were waiting. They massacred seventy-three of his followers. That is the reason for the turmoil witnessed among them even today. Wherever governance can be assumed, there is war. They have been doing this since the time of the preceptor. They did this out of ignorance. They lacked that much foresight. They do not even know what this religion truly is. It suffers from such a tragic fate. Muhammad's religion has only ninety years left. After that, it will come to an end.

[GV1:P2468] This virtue in India exists because many sages emerged as visionaries. India will never aggressively invade any other country. That is a part of this virtue. This itself is the foundation of India's culture. No other country in the world possesses this culture.

[GV1:P2469] Now, let me say a few words about the conceptual consecration at Santhigiri. The consecration here was revealed. The exact same model that was revealed to the Prophet. As mentioned earlier, the Prophet's religion has only ninety years left. Their assertion of the 'Final Prophet' is correct. That is, no one else will be born under the discipline of this religion. The span of one thousand five hundred years, as an influence of prophetic vision, culminates with that. With the disappearance of that visionary lineage, another prophetic vision emerges. The Omkara on the lotus! When asked what this was for, the reply was that it would be explained later. Twenty-nine years ago, someone received a vague vision of this in Varkala. Sometime after receiving that, eighteen years ago, its complete form was attained. A form akin to the first verse of the Guru Gita, 'Achintya Avyaktaroopaya' (the inconceivable and unmanifested form).

[GV1:P2470] This is how all prophets receive it. It will be implemented for the duration of their era. They will impart it for posterity before departing. Jesus could not reach that extent. That is why the cross itself had to be established. Since Christ could not be given another form, God is invoked using the very cross on which he sacrificed himself. The cross, thus, became a conceptual deity.

[GV1:P2471] 'Sankalpamaathrena Parameswarah' (The Supreme Lord through mere conception) means that if one conceptualizes God, whatever lies within the Supreme Lord's conception can be perceived. When speaking of Shiva, this state is briefly described as follows: 'Upon crossing the six steps and reaching there, Shiva becomes visible.' What became visible? The form of Shiva... the magnitude of Shiva was perceived. It means that one realizes what Shakti is, what Shiva is, what the stature of the Trimurti is, what the position of Devendra is, and what the state of Parashakti is when that power evolves completely. After that comes the divine presence of God. It is upon reaching the presence of God that one attains the capability to articulate the states that transcend all these, one by one. Vedanta begins there. The ultimate culmination of knowledge! That is, the knowledge regarding a state that can neither be drawn nor expressed adequately! The Omkara on the lotus is the representation of that knowledge. The knowledge that reveals what ultimate knowledge is.

[GV1:P2472] Disciple : So it does not mean that temples are unnecessary, but rather, what a temple ought to be according to contemporary knowledge, is what has been demonstrated here?

[GV1:P2473] Guru : Exactly. If it is not demonstrated in that manner, you will be deceived. The underlying meaning of the statement 'Jinns rule the world' is an illustration of this deception. Such conceptual deities persist and inflict madness, intense sorrow, and ignorance upon the people.

[GV1:P2474] Causing one to be born as a virtuous person, another as wealthy, another as destitute, and another as profoundly sinful... God does not cause births in such a manner. This occurs as the result of the karma that each individual has committed. Being born differently as someone with distress and pain, an ignorant person, someone devoid of beauty, the destitute, the vile, the despicable, and the aggressor—none of these are God's conception.

[GV1:P2475] Guruvani

[GV1:P2476] March 1990

[GV1:P2477] Guru : What name do you give to a visionary? A psychologist? One who perceives science through the mind is a visionary. Not a psychologist in your language. The teaching of psychology is something the Westerners have imposed upon your heads. You became absorbed in it. You went there, studied all their disciplines, and carried them back here. And you discarded everything that was here. This is what has transpired.

[GV1:P2478] Disciple: There is no peace on a global scale. Sikh terrorism, the crisis in Iran—thus, there are numerous domestic and international problems in all parts of this globe. How can a resolution be brought about for these problems and this lack of peace through the Guru's path?

[GV1:P2479] Guru: The present state is one where a lack of peace is inevitable. This is the Kali Yuga, after all.

[GV1:P2480] What is the dharma of Kali? To rectify all past mistakes.

[GV1:P2481] Shouldn't there be a lack of peace for that? Everything must be churned up.

[GV1:P2482] When it is churned, all the old, unwanted things will be forgotten automatically. Or they will be made to be forgotten. A lot will be destroyed. Many will wander and cry out for peace. Then, they will seek guidance for it.

[GV1:P2483] If peace is to be attained, what kind of peace should it be? Peace according to the Divine Will. The fourteen Manvantaras and their explication represent the Divine Will. This is the seventh Manvantara. When the seventh Manvantara arrived, a mistake occurred. We have been informed that this Guru lineage is meant to rectify this mistake. We cannot move forward without rectifying the mistake that occurred in this Manu lineage.

[GV1:P2484] Disciple Is the Guru saying that this lack of peace will continue temporarily?

[GV1:P2485] Guru : This will not be resolved unless the lack of peace continues temporarily. This must occur in all nations of the world. A situation must arise where everyone pleads for peace. If there are no difficulties at home, one would not inquire about the home! One inquires about that difficulty only when peace at home is lost. There is that expansive mindset that extends to the home, the land, the country, and the world. To discover it through that inquiry, a path must be established somewhere. The path we have received for that purpose is what we have established. This then continues as a lineage and its believers.

[GV1:P2486] Disciple: In which direction is this path of ours heading?

[GV1:P2487] Guru: This path belongs to two thousand four hundred and forty-four Gurus and their lineage. Of these two thousand four hundred and forty-four, the lineage of seven Gurus comprises one hundred billion each. Under the rest, there are forty billion each. That is how vast the number of indwelling souls (Antaryamis) is. All of this has been grasped here. This entire concept was received as a disembodied voice (Ashariri). It has been received entirely through visions over thirty-one years. It is not something taught or demonstrated by someone else. This was attained through our clairvoyance. Jesus honored this concept by standing before children with vision and weeping, saying, "Children, do not lose this." All other preceptors also plead in tears in the same manner. It is the conceptual form of such an idea that has been consecrated here for you.

[GV1:P2488] Disciple: These problems we see today, that is, international issues, slavery, racial discrimination - will there be a solution to this current situation in the world when the prevailing lack of peace comes to an end?

[GV1:P2489] Guru: It will end. But don't we need the right children for that?

[GV1:P2490] Disciple: Won't such children come?

[GV1:P2491] Guru: They will come. But it will happen only through the children we prepare. The householders (Grihasthashramis) you see today must become good. The poor man, the cart-puller, the boatman, and the scientist are all human beings in the same way. We must examine what this human life is and assign to each the duty (Karma) they deserve. That duty must be imparted first from the family. Otherwise, educated people like you will drink black tea and head to the fortress of intellectuals. Then they will return from there as bookworms. The one who returns will sit somewhere and harm humanity. The intellectual who goes to America will go there and facilitate the sale of their war materials here. If a button is pressed in America, a fire will break out here. Intellectuals will act and cause others to act in that manner.

[GV1:P2492] Disciple: This tendency of ours to look up to the West is our shortcoming and misfortune, and therefore, we must somehow free ourselves from it. This is what some knowledgeable and substantial individuals among the intellectuals say as a good thing.

[GV1:P2493] Guru: That is well and good. They concoct a global language with twenty-six letters and impose all these unnecessary things upon us, who are the possessors of fifty-six letters. Isn't that the only thing you are able to respect today? Those who believe in this, especially children who grew up here, have spoken to me in that manner. They asked me how I, someone who has never even stepped onto the veranda of a school, could converse with such great scholars.

[GV1:P2494] Once, two research scholars were summoned here. They had advertised in Kerala Kaumudi stating that they were conducting research in twenty-seven branches, including Ayurveda, Siddha, and sorcery. So I thought, let them research this as well. I invited them for that purpose. But they would not come. Even after offering to bear all their expenses, they would not come. Then I sent someone to them. Finally, they agreed to come. Do you know what they said when I spoke to them about this? They said, "We believe in all this. But our science does not accept it." In the end, they asked about their diseases, their family's illnesses, and other difficulties. They agreed with everything that was said and then left.
