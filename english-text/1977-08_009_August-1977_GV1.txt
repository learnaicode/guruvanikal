[GV1:P0150] August 1977

[GV1:P0151] Question: How can one believe the stories of the Rajarshis recorded in these Puranas?

[GV1:P0152] Answer: Currently, during the tenure of many rulers, some political enthusiasts often write histories to destroy the goodness of certain individuals. In this, we seem to have forgotten the national leaders who endured the sacrifices of holy souls. It appears to have been omitted from the histories written after attaining independence. The stories of contemporaries, fellow students, and colleagues are the ones that have been left out. And that too, in this democratic rule. Then is it any wonder if it was omitted by those who ruled in an absolute dictatorship?

[GV1:P0153] Question: Looking at this experience, is it only the Rajarshis who have done so?

[GV1:P0154] Answer: Not only among the Rajarshis, but also among common people and in the deity system, there are people who have attained direct realization and through it performed certain occult powers and magical feats. The shortcoming is that strong historical records could not be created for all of them. It was only through alliances at some administrative level, or through some other kind of knowledge or wealth, that it could be brought into a record. Those that have come through a true devotional nature are exceedingly rare.

[GV1:P0155] Question: So, do you give importance only to devotion?

[GV1:P0156] Answer: I do not give importance only to devotion like that. I am one who believes that everything has its own goodness in its respective measure.

[GV1:P0157] Question: Isn't it easier to lead a family life in the path of devotion?

[GV1:P0158] Answer: Yes. The path of devotion is the most suitable for family life.

[GV1:P0159] Question: What is the reason for you to feel that nothing else is easy?

[GV1:P0160] Answer: Practicing yoga techniques requires considerable time, and it involves certain changes in dietary habits depending on place and time. None of that may be convenient for family people. Therefore, there is quite a delay in carrying forward yoga practices. There are some ritualists who practice Vedic methods. They too have a specific time schedule for mantras and tantras. They will experience it as quite a delay.

[GV1:P0161] Question: Then what about the state of a Jnani (wise one)?

[GV1:P0162] Answer: The Jnani truly evaluates all these aspects. Viewing all these moderately, experiencing an inspiration from God, he moves forward acting on his own.
