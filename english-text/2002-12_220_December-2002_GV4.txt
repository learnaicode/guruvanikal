[GV4:P0716] December 2002

[GV4:P0717] What is the first thing that you, as householders (Grihasthashramis), should consider? Instead of reflecting upon what must essentially be considered, you are creating chaos by feigning utter cleverness. You are exhibiting that reckless exuberance even in certain wedding celebrations. Instead of understanding the true essence of marriage and demonstrating the dharma that must be imparted to children, husbands, and wives, you are merely displaying reckless exuberance.

[GV4:P0718] Struggling to educate and raise your children is your fundamental duty. You are the ones who have come to fulfill that duty. There is nothing extraordinary to claim after having done it. However, when you perform this, the spiritual merit to transform that action into *Dharma* (righteousness) must become ingrained in you. You should not assume that this nobility can be attained from anywhere else other than through the ashram. If you attempt to achieve it from elsewhere, you will end up losing your children and your wife. Whether you perform actions from your home, from your homeland, or by traveling across the entire world, you will suffer loss. If it leads to such a loss, you must abandon that action altogether. Instead, you should not desire to gather and hoard everything in your hands. Due to the lack of this knowledge, the actions performed by some people under the guise of devotion are fraught with numerous errors, ultimately turning them into entities unfit to exist either at home or in society.

[GV4:P0719] You are oblivious to this. You do nothing other than conducting your own spectacle alongside the grand spectacle of the world. Only if you possess the realization that you are doing nothing, will you be able to recognize what you have actually done. To what extent does our action measure up to our knowledge, what is its utility, how much of a model has it been for others, and how much *Dharma* have we envisioned and imparted to our children through our lives? You neither find the time nor possess the capability to contemplate such matters. On the contrary, you display your arrogance by exaggerating the magnitude of your deeds while making your wife and children sit as an audience. This is the greatest betrayal you inflict upon your wife and children. Alternatively, it is the ignorance of leading a life unfit for this world by heedlessly following the words of a wife, and then taking immense pride in rambling about every single word of that life. If you fail to understand that this is what resides within each of you, who can do anything to help?

[GV4:P0720] Your attitude and knowledge are not inclined towards understanding this. You do not even learn to walk a single step on that path. Those who come here try to learn it to a small extent. However, that is insufficient. It is not that I have not shared this knowledge with you. I am imparting it to you to the best of my ability. But the moment you turn away, you brush it all off, just as one brushes off dust. If you discard it in such a manner, what can I possibly do?

[GV4:P0721] You bring your children and leave them here (in the ashram). You are bringing and leaving here those who are unfit to be kept at home. These children are a burden to the house. It is this burden that you are imposing upon the ashram. Why do you bring and dump those who are useless at home here? What would be the outcome if one who is incapable of behaving in accordance with one's own home, who cannot live in harmony with any other home, and who has grown up with the wretched habit of treating the entire land as their personal property, is brought and dumped in the ashram? Having grown up with this wretched habit, they preach numerous ideologies. They merely put forth justifications attempting to rationalize the wrongdoings they have been committing. However, having understood that this is your habit, has anyone undertaken any action to prevent it? At the same time, you possess a peculiar tendency to remain silent even after knowing, seeing, or hearing things. You lack the words to speak against it. You do not have the habit; nor do you have the capability to act. Or, if you do act, it goes astray.

[GV4:P0722] This has been part of your experience to this day. Understand this and act accordingly. A great *Dharma* that must be cultivated by you, your children, and your children's children is being followed here. You must not attach any other justifications to this *Dharma*. There may be numerous justifications. But none of them can be appended to this. Why?

[GV4:P0723] You are undertaking and executing actions ranging from the realm of demons to the city of phantoms. There is one who has become the leader of the city of phantoms. Let it be anyone. Transcending the city of phantoms to become divine, one observes how many demons, deities, sages, and visionaries have existed, acting out of entanglements, both good and bad, everywhere. It is only after transcending this that one imbibes the distinct state of gods and goddesses. This is precisely why it is said that a Guru is necessary. While providing guidance for this, the Guru might act sternly towards us. However, in ashram life, that would be an established precedent. One requires the power of endurance to comply with this. Otherwise, staying in the ashram will make you corrupt. Furthermore, you will also bring disrepute to the ashram. This is what I have been able to earn from you. Understanding this, if you do not contemplate and live in a manner to prevent this danger from gripping the children who live without comprehending the will of the ashram, there is no point in bringing and tying them up in the ashram. Instead of contemplating this, what are you doing? To accommodate your justifications and to achieve your objectives, you associate with various people in various places, grasping onto the very same old destruction, establishing it within your home, your land, and your inner self, and attempting to systematize it. How will you transform this ignorance? It is only after attaining this awareness that you should come, go, and stay in the ashram, and seek all aspects of existence.

[GV4:P0724] Thousands of families, including those who have believed in this place from the early days, complete their knowledge at teashop gatherings. They go and drink black coffee in teashops that operate with fires burning, without opening doors or windows. If one listens from outside to four people drinking black coffee, it sounds like a thousand people drinking *payasam*. This is the sound of swallowing heat. And sitting there, they decree words intended to purify the land and its people. Then, once dawn breaks, it becomes their collective, desire-driven action. They cannot return from there until they have celebrated it at least until eight o'clock. In the next phase, after somehow passing the time until five in the evening, they will wander around cities and slums. Thus, there is a constant struggle involving the city and the slum. Who have you become through such acts of completion? If they are employed professionals, they have discovered greater pretense. Both husband and wife have jobs. Their sole thought is how many marks their jointly raised children can attain by providing tuition for every subject. It is precisely when they have exhausted all avenues for this that the boy in the neighboring house scores one mark higher. Then, this mother cannot sit still due to the agony. When the father returns, the rest of the quarrel is directed at him: "You are nowhere to be found around here, just lying around." If necessary, they will fight among themselves, and both will attempt to find completion by blaming each other. As these children grow up, they will turn out in a multitude of divergent ways.

[GV4:P0725] It is the *Dharma* of the householder stage that performs such actions that you have studied and internalized. In some households, if there are five children, they hold jobs and govern in five different countries. Thus, having learned disasters disguised as the ornaments of Western nations, these wealthy individuals are propagating their version of *Dharma*. These are the misdeeds you are committing. To whom will you answer for all of this? Does your life possess any spiritual merit after committing so much? Is the trajectory of your life's actions capable of upholding *Dharma*? What is the tragedy of a life? Having to raise fools, barbarians, and the vile is indeed due to the tragedy of one's own life.

[GV4:P0726] It is seen everywhere that householders lack the habit of thinking about what work they are doing. If you grow up like this, you and your children will waste the opportunity given by God and go astray on another path. I know that even thousands of experienced householders have gone wrong. I am not just saying this as empty words. Having established the system of Gurupooja here, explaining its merits and demerits, and telling you how to reach the path of attaining merit through the duty of the times, I am trying to direct you towards it. But you do not have the persistence for that. You are showing the same antics towards this as you showed to a sorcerer, a tantric, or a scholar.

[GV4:P0727] What you need are not these antics. I am neither a scholar nor an ignorant person. If asked whether I am something else, I am not that either. I am entrusting you with the work God entrusted to me. You have not understood this yet. I do not know when you will understand. Standing beneath the mess created by everyone, you are managing it and pretending to complete the karma.

[GV4:P0728] Only a Guru can tell the story of the city of vampires (Vethalanagara). The foresight of that Guru is the karma of repeatedly observing the Yugadharma (duty of the age) and demonstrating it in action before the people. This Vethalanagara is a terrifying realm. It will not step aside for anyone. Whoever touches it will only lie there bewildered and finally tear it away and leave. Apart from that, no one has anything else to do here. If this is to be uprooted from your life, there is only that aspect called Gurupooja.

[GV4:P0729] Once, a Supreme Court judge, while talking about his wife, asked me if a ghost possession could be broken. This possession brings destruction to that life. This man is someone who travels seeking paths in science. Furthermore, he is someone who causes headaches to poor children. This man has the ability to turn people into non-believers instead of believers and to turn them into a lifeless course of karma. This is the problem of not having gained experience in living in the sacred aspect of the relationship called Guru and disciple to remove the afflictions of one's own family and loved ones.

[GV4:P0730] Good books were taken from cultural centers like Nalanda and Takshashila in India, portrayed as parts of various sciences, and brought back here to be taught. This is the work the British did. Along with that, they imparted their culture here. Today's scientists and lawyers are the ones who imbibed that culture. It is that lawyer who says that even though his wife has died, her ghost is with him. That man is trying to sustain it. This is the boundary of science. There are some other boundaries like this. The stories told by Socrates' disciples are taken in parts, studied, and distributed. This is the boundary of our professors. Today's philosophers also have this boundary. The conclusion of this philosopher is the opinion of today's world.

[GV4:P0731] This is not what we need. Realize through experience. We must understand that the previously mentioned scientist-lawyer, a person who is entitled to highlight India's identity as a Supreme Court judge, only has the karma of following a ghost. Then what is the difference between the educated and the uneducated? The mentioned ghost is not just an ordinary ghost. It is the ghost of a learned Brahmin's wife. No matter what science they talk about or whether they have imbibed Western culture, they know the logic and karma of this. That is that inquiry. What does a non-believer who gets their guidance know? You must understand that the philosophy they distribute is not the philosophy that belongs to their belief.

[GV4:P0732] We respect everything. We do not discard anything without respect. Ghosts? They indeed exist. Demons? They too exist. If they did not exist, how did the names originate? Just as a tool is made and given a name, this is the realm of naming what exists. Only a wise person (jnani) can do that. There are many types of such wise people. Everyone knows about praying to a ghost, making it appear, and achieving desired things. The story of a ghost appearing from the stone on which feet were rubbed and washed is told in Tulsidas's story. It is enough to state what exists like this. If an educated person quotes each of these and says the path of wisdom is science, that becomes the authority here.

[GV4:P0733] But this is not what we need. Stories are fine. But we need the karma that brings the essence (experience) through the story and liberation through the essence. The act of dismissing things by telling a story and laughing 'hahaha' will not do us any good either. Does the nature of a lame person mocking others—saying 'walk like me if you have the guts'—suit us when the spiritual path reaches a dead end?

[GV4:P0734] If you talk about the karma that brings liberation through essence, it won't even enter the minds of ashrams. Because ashrams are entangled and completely submerged in puranas and epics. Therefore, they cannot do anything. This is not what we need. We need the karma that brings liberation to all demons—even up to Vethalanagara—through experience.
