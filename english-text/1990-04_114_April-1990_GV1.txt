[GV1:P2496] April 1990

[GV1:P2497] Perceiving what lies in one's soul and expounding upon it is

[GV1:P2498] clairvoyance. It is said that God has not created any of us as insignificant beings. If so, what is the reason we live like animals? What the complete form of a human being is should not be known through research, but through knowledge. That knowledge is a distinct method. The inner soul - know about it. It is the inherent past karma. The knowledge required to unify the karma of the individual, the family, and the movement must be brought to us. There must be a path for that. That is what we have established here.

[GV1:P2499] Disciple: This karma, the consecration made here, the beginning of this movement - I understand that this is something that has come through you in accordance with divine will. I also understand that this is a center of peace and a center of spiritual renaissance. What are the ways you see ahead to bring this extensively to the people?

[GV1:P2500] Guru: One of our systems has already been mentioned. It is the family that must stand at the forefront and act for this. The method of renewing whichever aspects the family acknowledges is paramount. When we say family, it includes wife, children, education, wealth, and mastery. It has already been stated how an error made while acquiring education can cause danger. Along with that, it will turn not just the country, but the world itself upside down. It has reached a stage of karma where even scientists are at a dead end, not knowing what to do. If we are stuck in this manner, it means that whatever we have discovered, learned, and taught—thus almost three-quarters of our efforts until today—have not only gone to waste but have also sown destruction. We must provide a solution for this to the family through ourselves.

[GV1:P2501] We must know what the course of karma demanded by the times has been. This era has a light of its own. This era embodies a Dharma that has arrived according to the calculation of the Chaturyugas in each of the past seven Manvantaras. We are creating a course of karma where the merits and sins of this Dharma must be repeatedly resolved and removed. By identifying the errors inherent in past creations, we must implement a course of Dharma suited to these times through this creation. This must be completed over an extended period. As its foundation stone, we need a mental growth that never falters. Otherwise, someone will deceive us too, just as in the past.

[GV1:P2502] Disciple: Like what?

[GV1:P2503] Guru: Varnashrama Dharma was introduced among us, creating caste. The Shudra himself was divided into numerous castes. The characteristics of the Vaidika (Vedic practitioner) were also defined. With the introduction of the Vedic order, they abandoned the truth. That is, when Brahma Jnana (knowledge of Brahman) was abandoned, the Vedic order became dominant. Through that Vedic order, temples and temple-dwellers (Ambalavasis) were fabricated. A section of people emerged to accept and gather it. These people are the temple-dwellers. A small minority of only ten to fifteen percent who came from somewhere—the Vaidikas—this group has taken over the ritualistic authority of all the people here. How did that happen?

[GV1:P2504] Disciple: I do not know how it happened.

[GV1:P2505] Guru: A part of that has already been explained earlier. Shaivism, Shaktism, and Vaishnavism were introduced, and through them, the Vedas, Shastras, and Epics were exalted. They were then established within the Vedic religion. The foundational form of Varnashrama Dharma is what spread and flourished within the Vedic religion. Caste originated from that. Then, they cleverly declared, "Brahma Jnaneti Brahmana" (He who knows Brahman is a Brahmin). The true meaning is that whoever possesses Brahma Jnana is a Brahmin. Today, if we look at the Avarna-Savarna system, the knowledge tradition of the Avarna survives in twenty-five percent here.

[GV1:P2506] Disciple: I did not understand what is meant by the knowledge tradition of the Avarna.

[GV1:P2507] Guru: Shaivism and Shaktism.

[GV1:P2508] Disciple: Does it mean that only a Brahmin can become a knower of Brahman (Brahma Jnani)?

[GV1:P2509] Guru: No, it means that being a Brahma Jnani was turned into the property of a specific caste. That is not the practice here. This danger occurred when they altered that practice according to the Manvantara and tried to introduce the Vedic order.
