July 1998

What is the general perception about an ashram? In a general perception that has passed and currently exists, ashrams are of four types - Yoga, Bhakti, Karma, and Jnana. These four are the very foundation of the earth's culture. There might have been many other related characteristics to this.

The course of action of householders should be viewed with great importance. Because they are the ones who should be helpful in the Karma Kanda (section of action). Brahmacharis and devotees are very few. They should only be that much. They are guides, after all. Therefore, it is enough if their number is small. But householders are not like that. Sannyasis and devotees must all arise from the householder. Therefore, it is the householders who need to improve.

To improve, a center is essential. It is said that religious principles are united. But customs and rituals are different. None should be denied. Only by respecting everything can the inner essence of each be understood. The essence of various religious principles is the same. I have learned this without a doubt through my life of experience.

Some deny the temple system. They can deny it. We also do that, don't we. Blind denial will do more harm than good. Denial should be with the discrimination of cause and effect. It should be by gathering and summarizing the scientific awareness and essences in religious principles. Sage Charvaka rejected many things taught by the Vedas using logic. It is not enough to say that observing and experimenting by knowing through self-experience are the methods suitable for scientific examination. The essences known through examination must be gathered, summarized, and established through action.

The Hindu religious code or culture is very vast. Not just Christians and Muslims in India, but almost all religious sects are those separated from Hinduism. They separated because of the excess of hardship they had to suffer from it. That is, Hinduism made a mistake somewhere. But the mistake made by Hinduism is God's will. Very few people know this. But even those who know do not act with determination.

We know about the Varnasrama Dharma, don't we. Since the time the world was created, changes have been happening to us according to the times. The people of the Satya Yuga were like gods who could see God directly and pray. When the Satya Yuga passed, the times also changed. How many Chaturyugas and Manvantaras have passed! One Manvantara is seventy-one Chaturyugas. Looking at this, how insignificant is a human's lifespan. Within this insignificant period of time, we can only acquire a very small amount of knowledge.

Even though a human's lifespan is very short, we should be able to know at least the harm brought by Varnasrama Dharma during that time. In Varnasrama Dharma, there is no place for a Shudra. But there is a Shudra in every home. There is no point in denying that. He works innocently for everyone. The Brahmin knew what reward God had kept for his innocence - the right to play, the authority to rule. That is why he was even given military leadership. Because of the arrogance of the kings, the Shudra could not escape from the bottom.

and struck, transferring all the power of sin onto his head. Even a Shudra becoming a king was according to the Brahmin's rules. Even the king, who was elevated to the status of a Brahmin by being passed under the belly of a golden cow and tied with a thread, was not permitted by the Vedas. He was only able to hear the Murajapam. Can the subjects of such a king hear the Vedas? In the Varna-Ashrama Dharma, a Shudra was caught and made a king. But he has no knowledge. This ignorant king is the visible god! Thus the people became ignorant and ruined. Buddha came to change this. Before that, at the beginning of Kali Yuga, Krishna came. He opposed everything that existed. He declared, "Sambhavami Yuge Yuge" (I will be born in every age).

How difficult is it to transform the embodiments of Kali in today's Kali Yuga into brilliant Yuga Karma Yogis? The situation is that there are only black Englishmen molded by the British in India today. These poor people completely imitated the British. Not only dress and language, but even administration is an imitation of them. Our tradition is one of having kings who only obeyed what the Viceroy said. We do not have our culture and our religion. Even one who has studied philosophy thinks our culture is just stories; and the West's is history. Thus we are slaves in every way.

In Kali Yuga, there is no place for a deity. In Kali Yuga, the place is for the Guru. How many great souls came and declared this truth! First, Moses came. When he said that the words he spoke were received from God, the world mocked him. Two thousand years later, Jesus came. When he was crucified, he showed his form to those who believed. Even though they were fishermen and had to endure severe punishments, they worked with determination. Those who worked multiplied twofold. The Jews were exhausted before their sacrifice. Christians still have that tradition today. They were able to establish the world's government. Among them, they came to India and did a favor. If Madan became Mathai, he could walk on the main road. We swallowed both the good and the bad of the West equally. We imitate them in everything. When they came here without peace and grew their hair long, every beedi roller and boatman here started growing their hair long.

Our country suffered such a fate because our mothers became ignorant. Our ancestors (Pitrus) have no purity. Children will be born in the family according to the zodiac sign in which the Pitru stands. Children with intelligence and logic will be born in a family where Pitru purity has been achieved. In that field, Brahmins have always been at the forefront. The entire Shudra lineage turned out to be drunkards. Even if he is knowledgeable, he will be perverse. The Brahmin, however, did another trick; three Sambandhams (alliances), one Veli (marriage). The one in the Brahmin's house is Veli. All others are Sambandham. To complete any ritual he performs, he needs the woman he married (Veli). Or if she is not there, he will show the Yoni Mudra and perform a Tantra. We are satisfied with his Mantra and Tantra, aren't we? What knowledge can be imparted to a lineage that has degraded to this level?

No Acharya has ever come who has performed Pitru purity in its complete ritualistic sense. Shankaracharya performed Vedic rituals. Even Buddha said that if you believe, everything will change. We must know that there are many Pitrus who have reached the position of Buddha. Pitrus (indwellers) are filled in all subtle realms. These indwellers are the ones who killed Jesus. Pitru dominance has been established in that position. Pitrus rule over the positions of all the Acharyas who have risen.

The light that Buddha saw, the ray of light mentioned in the Quran, the tongue of fire in the language of Christians - thus whatever religions describe that consciousness in whatever ways

Whatever has been described, all of that is here. The children here see that divine energy in all these ways. Ancestral purification, which no one has done with complete purity until now, is being performed here. The ritual of gathering all ancestors and merging them into the auspicious light is carried out here. If ancestral purification is done here, then you have no ancestral bondage, and no ancestral curse. Then what you need to focus on is how to nurture your lineage. The result of what you do today will bear fruit after twenty-five years.

We do not deny anything. Each is correct to its own extent. But, what is absolutely essential for us today? Good children must be born in our families. Our children must have the knowledge to independently achieve whatever we are running around for. There should be good Karma Yogis, Jnanis, and Sanyasis in your home. You, who are the foundation for such a noble creation, must have a clear understanding of the ashram.

Guruvani
