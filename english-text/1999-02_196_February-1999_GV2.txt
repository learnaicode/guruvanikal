February & March - 1999

The righteous should not seek Dharma in Tantra Santhigiri's concept is not a religious karma. It is a karma that points out the mistakes that have happened among the people and corrects those mistakes to help people come forward. Correcting the mistakes of the past way of living is what should be called reform. When it is said that pointing out mistakes is reform, that statement itself becomes a mistake. One should be able to say that there is such a way out to correct the mistake. That is Dharmic karma.

If reform is to be called Dharmic karma, the testimony of that karma must be the light form of life that stands as a continuous flow in the nine planetary spheres. We have a knowledge that considers everything as full of light, described as a special feature of Jyothi. This feature is mentioned from the first sky onwards. It is obtained from the light form of life that is filled continuously in the nine planetary spheres. It is the knowledge that the light inside a green tree is exactly the same as what is inside all living beings. The knowledge that does not understand this is what is found in Vedantas, and in the karma where historical truth was written by mixing Puranas, epics, and Vedas. Our knowledge is that everything from a blade of grass, to the water content shared in the five elements, and the five sheaths namely Annamaya Kosha, Pranamaya Kosha, Jnanamaya Kosha, Vijnanamaya Kosha, and Anandamaya Kosha, sustain life. Scholars have also tried to divide the five sheaths into six. Although that attempt is good for showing scholarship, and fits logic and intellect, it does not align with the truth. The extreme struggle of Yogis to develop life and make it a mere accumulation of knowledge is unbearable for man. This is a mistake that occurs in the course of completing scholarship by combining Yogi, Karmi, Jnani, and Bhakta, and understanding that it is the covering of one.

Scholars should at least see it this way. Today's science has grown by taking each atom from the atomic cells and working on it, understanding through intellect that the storage of new light happens from a single atom. Scientists had also established that this atom cannot be split. Now, who split this atom? The theory that an atom cannot be split is like the completion of Advaita. There are things said based on science. The path taken by one who speaks like that is not right. What is understood from this?

There are examples of this happening in nature without a tree. What it is like is that the objective application of emotional charge should only be spoken by a truthful person who classifies and makes the life experience felt. It is regarding the matter that travels a long distance starting from the Annamaya Kosha, operated in each cell by the great effort over a long period by truthful souls who sought the source of compassion in such great men, that it has been brought and stopped in the Anandamaya Kosha. The mathematics of the language created by Gundert is done in the same way as Vedanta is included in the Amarakosha, a dictionary of synonyms beginning with 'Amara Nirjjara Deva'. Rejoicing by taking many reservoirs and calculating that all are one is a flaw in karma, just like rejoicing by stating the mathematical description in the Amarakosha. One reservoir is not like another reservoir. How can a person who does not understand that all these reservoirs are taken and flowed into the sea, and that even this sea is contained within the earth, classify and shape life?

Beyond the seventh sea, there is an Ezhilampala or Ezhinampala. This is an imagination that is added to each person's logic. Does writing like this mean that they have seen it? Similarly, rejoicing by calculating the light of experience that occurs when crossing each sky, and only when the repetitive capacity of the past three periods reaches the fourth quarter, will one see that there is nothing where the Advaita religion speaks. Why does this scientist calculate sitting in a place where there is nothing like that? Will one become wise if this is called a vision and the empty or non-empty karma residue filled with scholarship is fitted into the intellect and created? The Amarakosha and Gundert's dictionary are fitted into the intellect in this way.

The science of the soul stands beyond that - beyond intellect, beyond thought. There is no room for thought there. There is no room for the intellect to work either. Here, the scientific scholarship of creating calculations in imagination and the scientific scholarship of creating a great language among languages (Sanskrit - the refined language) are not different. Both these sciences were confined within the covering circle of the Trimurti, whose face of knowledge was lost. This is scholarship. Therefore, no one understood the aspect that needs to be worked on in the fourth period. This is the mistake that has come to lie in the Trimurti system. This has never worked in the fourth period.

The meaning of Kali, the fourth period, is manifold. It is how karma works in each cell and reaches. If the capability of the grass is seen and its description is given, it is complete. When each grass is given each medicinal quality and completed, it is complete. Then it was turned into plants. When the repetitive capacity of plants comes, it is a different completion. For example, Ramappacha. When it itself undergoes renewal by joining the two fortnights, the bright fortnight and the dark fortnight, it becomes another completion.

Vision is the means to act like that and bring an end. That is what is calculated up to the seventh heaven. This is a truth that resides only in a few era-men who happened across eras.

If one says Sankara lineage, then there is no obstacle. One can go in anywhere. This is an over-trick used by all the Shudras in South India.

How would it be if this over-trick is put into caste? There are people who have acquired knowledge in each. In your own language, an encyclopedia. Just like a botanical garden that preserves plant diversities by writing and studying the names of plants, each movement in the name of these wise men is an encyclopedia that preserves each course of karma. Each is a system of karma where this knowledge and ignorance flow mixed together. Each will attain purity, distribute purity, and attain purity again.

What is the action called vision? Vision is the dialogue between the guru and the disciple, recognizing the different states of cause, effect, gross, and subtle. It is the correction of knowledge between the disciple and the guru. It is not a theoretical justification that destroys action by merely talking about revelation and realization. This is an error in action that keeps theory as knowledge. This is the fault of not recognizing the difference between Hindu and Sanatana and putting it into practice. This is an orphaned martial arts school where the gymnasium of the intellect works in people who believe in God. The expression 'orphaned martial arts school' (Nadhanillakkalari) is not only in Malayalam but in all languages. The visionary aspect to understand in which heavens this orphaned martial arts school is affecting must be obtained from the guru.

Our loss of values is that we did not get the realization that Ishwara, God, Brahman, and Parabrahman are all different according to the determination of God or the determination of Brahman.
