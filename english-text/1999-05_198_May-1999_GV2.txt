GURUVANIKAL · May 1999 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P1522 to GV2:P1533
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

May 1999

God does not insist that He should be worshipped; if you must worship, do not distort it.

Only one who has can give; only one who knows can make known.

Why does society in general worship God today? Why do they go to places of worship and ashrams? For very trivial, immediate material gains. It ends with breaking coconuts and lighting lamps. The return journey is worse than the going.

Small gods for small castes. Big gods for big castes. We have been behaving so far by standing behind this system that has been passed down.

The fulfillment of monotheism is necessary in Kali Yuga. Because Kali is eligible for liberation. And the Shudra for Brahmajnana.

It is better to internalize sacrifice than to propagate it.

Rather than accumulating wealth for children, it is better to raise them to be capable of working.

For us living in this world, a 'one' is necessary to stand together. Confining oneself to that one, pervade everything. That one which pervades everything is the truth.

Only a visionary who knows the three times (past, present, and future) can save you.

The Guru works to bring about transformation in society during a specific period.

For those who believe and act upon the words of the Guru, it is experienced as truth and God. The concept of God is realized through the Guru.
