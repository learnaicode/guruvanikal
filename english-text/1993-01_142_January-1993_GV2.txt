GURUVANIKAL · January 1993 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0448 to GV2:P0458
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

January 1993

It has been seventeen years since we got this path of ours as an idea. It was unclear for nine years of that. Although I have suffered a little, I have not done anything to get this much. What we have got today is a state that no one can even think of. God has made this done in such a way that it is equally useful to a householder, a yogi, a worker, and a wise man. It has been taught in such a way that every house gets it. This is a new model. There is no model available now. It was there in the sages before. Either because of the greatness of time or because of our mistake, it slipped away today. That is why the previously mentioned government drives us.

Sanatana is not a religion, it is dharma; it is culture. It is efficient. Great men have advised how to carry it forward. But we could not take it. From Nair downwards could not get close to this. Because it is the Varnasrama dharma itself. All these dharmas are there in our house itself. The Brahmin who does the Vedic work, the Kshatriya who rules, and the Shudra who stands without touching anywhere, this situation has been going on for a long time.

In Kali, it is Manu's decision that the rule reaches the Shudra. But they showed a trick to keep it in hand and rule. That is the Varnasrama dharma. The order of worship went down. It was decreed that the sound 'Om' should not be pronounced. The Vaishya was beaten down. Then caste came. Man became like wild grass. Reached a situation where there is no knowledge at all. The people became a mad class knowing only from 'Om'. Along with this, the rule was also given in the hands of the Shudra. Although the rule was given to the Shudra, the Brahmins kept it without imparting spiritual knowledge until his thirst was quenched. Thus the position obtained by the Shudra became useless. If the father dies and there is no right to offer bali, then what is the story? The old Nair families led a very degraded life. The Brahmin kept a relationship. But there is no relationship with them. Could earn some money. Because of that, the mother's name came into existence without the father's name. If a father without paternity is accepted, the ancestor will not get liberation. The children or nephews of this father should take up the paternity and work for the paternity and thus become pure. Otherwise, Madan, Marutha, Mantramurthi, Amman, and Yakshi - this ancestor will stand behind each of these kept inside and wander. Later, if born from this, they will become mad without knowledge. Justice and fairness will not be there in them. If children are born to such people, they will become those who do not hesitate for anything with desire only for money and women, and Kali will increase and lead the world itself to the Kali of Kali.

The earth we inhabit is encompassed by nine planets and twelve zodiac signs. Only through this can a human be born. Children born with pure paternity must exist in this Kali Yuga. For that, offering sacrifices by standing under a god or goddess is not right. In today's condition, a Guru who has surpassed the position of a god, found the presence of God, and provided guidance from that position (state) - a Guru who has acquired knowledge from that position, accepted the Brahmopadesham, understood how to apply it from time to time, and advises - is what is needed. That Guru is the one who must correct the evils of the present time.

What is called Hindutva today? All the ways suitable for degradation are present in it today. How should the degrading elements in this be torn apart? It is a lineage that tore apart our rights, kept us within a fence, and made us uncultured by not allowing us to be educated. If this continues, what is the situation? Whom should we, and who should rule us? Does this not mean that we had no right to think about this? Children born when the zodiac signs changed without paternity became arrogant, beat and destroyed each other, and became people with a history that is disgusting to hear. Tired of seeing this, Buddha advised the Pancha Shila. He advised living like siblings born from the same womb. He said this two thousand five hundred years ago. You don't have to look at your predecessors, Buddham Sharanam Gachchami. We have nothing to give to God. God only has to give to us. Buddha learned what the government was while sitting at home. When he remained silent, he was married off as a condition for it. Unable to fight, he hid and did penance. Then he received a word from God (in the light). Because he was a Rajarshi, kings and subjects obeyed the word he received. When it progressed, people who understood nothing did vile and despicable acts in Buddhist monasteries. Brahmins waited for it as a convenience. The unjust were driven out of India itself. Some fled and hid in Ceylon. Everyone left in the Buddhist monasteries was beaten to death. What created the Purappadu instead of worshipping God is the remainder of that. The panegyrists who were there before that, Buddhist monasteries should be made into Devi temples.

Brahmins cleverly brought back the Vedic system. Meanwhile, Shankaracharya came and advised Advaita. He said that the evil that has happened is because science and the Vedas are not known. For that, he established four Mutts and placed Acharyas there. There too, the Vedic person came, did not give entry to anyone else, and monopolized Sanyasa for Brahmins only. Thus we became poor people with nothing.

Due to the glory of time, today we are independent. Muslims and Christians came for trade, saw the hooliganism, stabbing, and cutting here, told stories, got close, won over with money, and seized the rule. They even became emperors. Through their rule, they imparted education and some culture.

Later we cried out for freedom. We gained freedom, but what did we gain? What is today's freedom? Did we gain anything? No. We gained nothing. Is what we have accepted today Western culture, Indian culture, or a hybrid of both? Everything shines here. The Christian here and the Muslim here are all Hindus. They did not come from Europe or Arabia. You can't go to Europe or Arabia and take paternity, right? When there was no other way, they changed their religion. That's all. Everyone is a sibling. What does a Hindu have today other than the sinful power of arrogance and boasting that my uncle has killed ten people? In this state, the merit was destroyed, and the wandering children had no way to live here. If they can live a little, the pride that they cannot talk to the neighbors will grip them. What is needed now to change this culture?

This is your past history. You must think about this aloud. Many great men have tried to change this. Nothing has been accomplished. We need a decision on what we will be tomorrow. How you can accept this idea, and thus how the efforts of great men can be made fruitful - that is what is going to be said next.

Guruvani
