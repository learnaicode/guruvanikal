[GV1:P2303] November 1988

[GV1:P2304] We are expanding the stream of light of our mind. Not according to the calculations stated by the yogis. By developing it, I have been able to see what that karmic path is, which grows the inner connection (the Kundalini power) from the triangular shape to the thousand-petaled lotus. It is a manifestation of the success of life. That applies only to an individual. It is what is called Kundalini power according to yogic principles. When it reaches the Manipuraka chakra, its shape and nature change. By then, all types of colors would have combined and acted together. In this world, the atomic penetration of everything of all kinds can be seen. This is the realm of the atom. We can see the special operation of these atoms functioning together with blood, flesh, and marrow. It is explained using color itself. Divinity comes only if a particular color is joined. It is not in another. The element of darkness will be mixed in that. In the other, the element of light will be mixed. To specify a certain light, when a vision is completed, for a state to be fulfilled, knowing it entirely in its gross, subtle, causal, and effectual aspects, seven thousand four hundred and forty-four visions must occur. The completion of seven such stages is the divine presence. How can this be explained now?

[GV1:P2305] This cannot be discovered by science. The reason is that science has not grown to that extent. I am not saying this to deny that science exists. Science has simply not evolved enough. How many people can do this? They will break down when they reach that point. A scientist can only reach the complete form of research envisioned by a scientific discipline, right? Thinking beyond that, wasn't there a science here that executed things through the nine planets, operated within the twelve zodiac signs, discovered its calculations to give to us, reached out to the stars, and repeatedly explained to us the innate qualities that reach us up to their fractional calculations? That very calculation is what is being discussed here. It should not be misstated.

[GV1:P2306] In this manner, only an authoritative Guru who has ascended to the position of Devendra, or to the position of the Lord of the Ganas (it is said that Ganapathi has the root authority for this), through the karmic path that has grown up to the Trimurti system, can explain all this. What is the authority of Mula Ganapathi? Or, to what extent does the karma of the state called Parashakti reach in that position? How minutely and scientifically has all this been realized. It needs to be known by further refining and consolidating it. Only then can we be convinced of its evolution. Otherwise, it will not be comprehended.

[GV1:P2307] A karma I perform here to change this—for example, someone says they have a disease. Is the disease real or not? It is real. Then what is the cause? There is no effect without a cause, is there? When discovering the cause, if it came through their karmic path, it is that. Or, if it came through their ancestors, it is that. Or else, if it came through their family worship, it is thus. Categorizing it in this way, the karma that needs to be performed by this person will be made to be done by them alone. Only such fully completed karma will take hold here. When it takes hold, there will be the pain of karma in their body. It will be in the body as well. It will also reflect in their actions. It will happen. After that, if they desire self-realization, they will be guided on that path. Speaking of it is useful only if they have the right to it. Even if not, this can be told to some people. Because it relies on their desires. When desire increases, that increase will affect the karmic path functioning within.

[GV1:P2308] Knowledge is being able to tell what karmic path should operate in any person upon seeing them. That karma will aid in liberation. There is no doubt. Each of us has a karmic path right from birth. How does that come about? Shakta, Shaiva, Vaishnava—these are worshipped as divine, aren't they! Until reaching Advaita, one can only function according to this, right? Advaita is something else entirely. What should one do as long as they are in Dwaita (duality)? Is it right to reject Dwaita? Dwaita cannot be denied. It can be discarded after that knowledge is attained.

[GV1:P2309] Where will the aspect that people comprehend as *Maya* ultimately lead? Must one not attain the state of Agastya, who reached the position of self-illumination? Can this be achieved through mere words? It requires concerted effort. Only such deliberate action can yield results. One must inevitably undertake this *karma*. An individual must independently determine the course of their *karma* and *dharma*. This cannot be decided by another. Therefore, a center of knowledge dedicated to this is essential here.

[GV1:P2310] Guruvani
