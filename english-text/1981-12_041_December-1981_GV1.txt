[GV1:P1084] December 1981

[GV1:P1085] Disciple: Kerala politics, or Indian politics, or world politics - do you have anything to say about this?

[GV1:P1086] Guru: Isn't this what has been said in general - about human life?

[GV1:P1087] Disciple: That was just mentioned briefly in passing, wasn't it? I only asked to clear my doubts.

[GV1:P1088] Guru: What do you mean by clearing doubts?

[GV1:P1089] Disciple: There is an instance here where some politicians, by establishing their rule, even gave gratuity to toddy tappers.

[GV1:P1090] Guru: In a way, one can only say that it is correct. Looking at the current situation, is it worse than current politics? It is just that toddy tappers were paid wages for the work they did. Isn't candidate selection based on caste happening all over India? Then, is giving gratuity to toddy tappers a trick? Toddy tapping, distilling, and drinking have been prohibited fields since ancient times, which subjugate the community.

[GV1:P1091] Disciple: Did you say it subjugates the community in the name of the profession, or about its characteristic effects?

[GV1:P1092] Guru: The characteristics are indeed good. It is just that a habit of handling everything excessively has emerged. Does anyone respect the words of a drunkard? Does he himself know how to respect his own words? Then how will others respect them? Similarly, won't the one who makes and serves it also be included in that? Such people certainly cannot think about the consequences or make a decision and implement it after thinking. If he also gets a gratuity, will he be able to study and improve in this world? What will happen to his children? It is certain that his children will never study. Thus, the intention will complete the old ways too. The trick is that what is older than the old has been completed. Then, we must believe that what is thought of as politics existed here for some time. There is a memory that the light of the predominance of its sacrifice began to be seen from 1944 to 1948. After that, it continued until about 1960 in the name of that weight. I am not saying that some minor things did not happen in between. They have happened.

[GV1:P1093] Disciple: What do you mean by minor things?

[GV1:P1094] Guru: This is just like the proverb that there is no one who would not lick their fingers if they dip their hand into a pot of jaggery. It could be for status, prestige, or making money in any form. Be that as it may! What does that matter to us? What we need is to understand what endures among the people currently involved in all these things, and how they should be able to endure in the future. We must provide an opportunity for the people who are thoroughly disillusioned by seeing all this to think and learn completely. What we need now is to lovingly bring about a sense of unity regarding everything among the people, as mentioned earlier. However, unity in love can only be brought about through a certain kind of fear. Cultivate that fear as if it were towards God, a father, or a mother. Cultivate love and respect. Regardless of what the children of Bharat are involved in, attempt to create a way of life, at least in Bharat, that serves as a model for the world. Through that, the people of the world will desire it themselves. It is not necessary to have a model similar to Russia's in Bharat. Since the situation has not deteriorated to that extent, and since it is not a developed country like America, those methods are not suitable here. The aspect known as India's socialism can only be implemented in one way today. If today's castes or religions can create various political groups, the believers here who think, learn, and teach should stand together to form a national organization, recognizing that believers are one, irrespective of religion and caste. Religious leaders should not be included in this. They should only provide guidance. As Mahatmaji said, one should not desire to cling to it, but merely achieve the objective. Thus, when believers also become those who fear out of love, they will have to respect each other through mutual love. We cannot sustain anything without such love and respect. It seems that on Indian soil, the concept of love is used like a gas. An example of this is communists going out at night to create problems. If a wife and children go to the temple together and someone questions it, they say it was done for their sake. This might be done because they cannot entirely deny a matter that is deeply ingrained in the people. It should be exactly like that. Otherwise, the dynamic between the capitalist and the worker, as well as the attitude of posing as a leader, will inadvertently change. Only then can we believe that there is a government truly belonging to India.

[GV1:P1095] Disciple: Are you certain that such a thing will happen?

[GV1:P1096] Guru: Yes, certainly. It will undoubtedly happen. It is beyond doubt that Bharat will thus become a model for the world. A small proof of this is the emergence of hippies. Even though India is in such a chaotic state, Bharat is still called a spiritual nation. Therefore, wishing that it may happen to the world as the resolve of Brahman, let us conclude this by praying to God.

[GV1:P1097] Disciple: So, based on what we have heard from you, do you have anything further to inform us regarding any doubtful aspects of this solar system, which consists of nine planets, twelve zodiac signs, and twenty-seven stars?

[GV1:P1098] Guru: Yes. As mentioned earlier, this is the era of the seventh Manu. This means that half the time since the origin of the world has passed. It has yet to function for another half of its time. We must believe that there are an unknown number of solar systems in this universe.

[GV1:P1099] Disciple: Do you have any absolute proof for that?

[GV1:P1100] Guru: Yes. One hundred and four solar systems have been observed. Ours is a medium-sized solar system among them. There are stellar constellations seen between them. These stellar spheres are realms where the aforementioned virtuous souls reside. Divine creation has endowed each solar system with a unique nature.

[GV1:P1101] Disciple: Do you believe that any other solar system is more powerful than this one?

[GV1:P1102] Guru: There are numerous other solar systems of greater size and special power. It cannot be said that they are exactly like this one. There are also solar systems with entirely different characteristics compared to this one. Everything in nature undergoes change. A cluster of ten solar systems forms one measurement unit. Thus, there are many stellar spheres that control the existence of the solar system. Those stellar spheres observe and sustain all of this.

[GV1:P1103] Disciple: Why did you say that?

[GV1:P1104] Guru: Just as there is a gravitational field of attraction between this earth and the solar system, there is an attractive force between these stars as well. Accordingly, based on the virtues and sins of a particular solar system, each sphere within the solar system may draw closer to another, or to a stellar sphere.

[GV1:P1105] Disciple: What could be the reason for such drawing closer?

[GV1:P1106] Guru: I mentioned earlier that a human being has only two things to do once born in this world: virtuous deeds and sinful deeds. The result of the sinful or virtuous forces we accumulate as we are born, die, and exist in this world—as its weight increases, it may draw closer to the solar sphere or any other sphere for either destruction or survival. If that drawing closer leads to destruction, fire might erupt due to air pressure itself, causing it to melt and collapse. Alternatively, extreme cold might arise, leading to a state where nothing can survive. Or, everything could be entirely upended, resulting in either complete destruction or partial survival. Conversely, if it draws closer to a stellar sphere that has accumulated virtues, it might cause some destruction but then function in a good state again.

[GV1:P1107] Disciple: In that case, can we know or guess whether it is a region that has committed sins?

[GV1:P1108] Guru: Virtue and sin are not what we merely intend them to be. They are the experiences that befall us if we do not act according to the righteous order ordained by Brahman. We should not assume that we possess the knowledge to understand this and act accordingly.

[GV1:P1109] Disciple: If so, then how do we distinguish between good and bad?

[GV1:P1110] Guru: God has given us a medium to distinguish them. That is the Manus who have passed before us and their lineage. We can only accomplish our actions in accordance with that. If we fail to act accordingly, such things may happen.

[GV1:P1111] Disciple: In that case, if a celestial body undergoes any destruction, will it cause the destruction of this solar system itself?

[GV1:P1112] Guru: No. That will not happen. It may be destroyed or grow due to the pressure of air, water, or other solid substances. Only its physical form will change; its ethereal nature cannot be altered. The change in that ethereal nature occurs just as the specific eras of each Manu change.

[GV1:P1113] Disciple: Are you saying that this solar system will undergo a change only after the era of the Manus is completely over?

[GV1:P1114] Guru: Yes, it is precisely so. Only at such a time will a change occur to it. Otherwise, the calculations would have to be radically altered, not just from this world, but from many worlds. Therefore, God will not do such a thing.

[GV1:P1115] Disciple: So, you mentioned earlier that there are some spheres that are more powerful than this, right?

[GV1:P1116] Guru: Yes, there are. There are certain spheres capable of magnetically attracting not just our actions, but our very earth itself.

[GV1:P1117] Disciple: In that case, a situation could arise where the earth is destroyed or something else happens to it.

[GV1:P1118] Guru: Didn't I mention earlier that it will happen?

[GV1:P1119] Disciple: According to what you said, that happens as a result of sins, doesn't it?

[GV1:P1120] Guru: We must believe so. Only then can we do good deeds and inspire others to do them. Shouldn't we inspire good deeds to the extent of our capacity or knowledge?

[GV1:P1121] Disciple: Should we understand from your words that an earthly nature known in this manner will emerge?

[GV1:P1122] Guru: Yes, that is exactly how it must be understood. Because, according to the calculation believed to be the era of the Manus, only the time of seven Manus has passed. If we have been able to acquire this much knowledge during the time of seven Manus, how much more must be acquired during the era of the next seven Manus.

[GV1:P1123] Disciple: In that case, shouldn't we realize that, based on our knowledge of virtues and sins, we must prioritize virtue?

[GV1:P1124] Guru: Absolutely.

[GV1:P1125] Disciple: But the people today do not seem to understand this and act accordingly, do they?

[GV1:P1126] Guru: Never mind. People will function on their own. If we examine the knowledge that emerged in Russia recently and compare it with the knowledge from fifty years ago, we can discern whether they have progressed or regressed. Similarly, other communist nations, wealthy countries among the Eastern nations, and various other countries within our own Asia have not declined in terms of knowledge; rather, they have ascended.

[GV1:P1127] We know that each of these nations is experiencing a certain anxiety, unsure of what to do as various changes have occurred everywhere. When this anxiety turns into restlessness, humanity will naturally reach a state where it begins to contemplate what must be done. The divine will shall ensure that this happens. If a feeling arises that things are not proceeding according to the cosmic will, and a disturbance occurs in nature, they will all cry out and rectify themselves.

[GV1:P1128] That is the only extent to which their discoveries can survive. Therefore, we must live and act with a firm resolve that everything should unfold according to the cosmic will. Let us pray that all of humanity realizes this.

[GV1:P1129] Guru Vani
