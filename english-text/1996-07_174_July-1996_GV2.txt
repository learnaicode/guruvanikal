English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

July 1996

If you look at the ideas of any Acharya, you will not find anything bad in them. Followers of Jainism walk with their mouths covered. The compassionate intention behind this is to avoid destroying even microorganisms. Can we say that is bad? How many microorganisms within us are destroyed? How many outside are destroyed? I don't feel that the work of modern science, which has the ability to detect these, is any less. The content of a theory that has repeatedly established a ritualistic rule that imparts much more love than this modernity is what was indicated above. How much have we and the crowd that includes us loved to imbibe this love? If we can pass on this loving thought to our children, it can be seen as a source of great love.

We are raising children to become the owners of the karmic destiny of offspring who have imbibed sin in various ways and compete in various ways. How can they take on this source of love? Instead of teaching children to repeatedly follow a ritual that imparts love, we have only imparted conflict in them. In this disobedient growth, in this loveless respect, we are completely the culprits. Loveless respect is mostly seen among those who think they know something among us.

There is a greatness of every kind in respecting each other. In the matter of respecting each other, Kerala had a blessedness compared to other places. But today it has been completely ruined. There was a time in Kerala when uncles and other women lived without conflict. If you nurture that unity, you will not be able to reach and act upon any righteous karma or dharma.

What do we need today? If there is sinful power in the karma done by our ancestors, it is a theory to destroy it and cultivate merit instead. Dharma is respecting how much of a share of fortune there is in the theories stated so far, how much of it we can take, and how we should be in that karma. We have not reached anywhere in that dharma. In the karma thus respected, love is at the very forefront - love for God. Just as we impart love to a plant we plant, humans have a karmic inclination to impart love to everything. The respect for human life is a great meritorious act that voluntarily takes on the state of dharma. If we are to be able to bring children to a dharma theory steeped in respect, we must know what we have done, or what society has done - what the ancestors did, who claimed to belong to the same society while behaving with enmity towards each other. For this, not only do those who are said to be knowledgeable do nothing, but they also deceive us with caste speeches and by showing love like vote-catchers pinching a child's nose. This deceit of the Lord is destroying dharma. Does this Lord, who has lost dharma, have any love for karma? Any loving respect? Where have we, who have lost humility, reached? We have simply decayed into a state where we cannot find the functional capacity of a life.

We can do a few things to change this. For that, we must know about the four yugas - Satya Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dvapara Yuga, and Kali Yuga. We have been told that Kali is very bad. But it is not so. Kali is completely good. Everyone who has come to say that Kali is good has been hurt. The harm we have earned through the repetition of the dharma of other yugas in the current Kali is beyond even thinking. Because if what has passed is repeated again, it will become a meritless decay. It will be seen put in a rotten thing. Reaching a karma that gives mind to it in such a way, even self-awareness will be lost. A reform for that

If you need it, you must find out what the flaws are in the gurus who have come so far and in their religious doctrines, what they have done, to what extent they can be followed, and where the destructive nature of hatred will lead. If we can discern this, we will become completely good, no doubt. Then you won't have to share anyone's property. You will be able to guide your children to the virtue where dharma resides in the act of sharing love. Thus, by coming to knowledge, you will reach self-realization from knowledge.

Previously, Karinkuttichathan was worshipped in this place. That is not just hearsay for me, but something I have seen. Besides that, there is another special thing here. A sick child was recently brought here from Vaniamkulam. When we looked into the illness, this affliction was caused by Odiyans. If asked why these Odiyans do this and complete it, they themselves do not know. This karma exists not only in one doctrine but in all doctrines. Bhajan math, temple, vanakkamasappura, church, thaikkavu, mosque - we speak giving respect to each of these. Is this respect enough only for us, or do others need it too? Or is the God of Hindus not acceptable to people of other religions? If so, why did Hindus convert to other religions? Everyone talked and talked about this until it became like something wrapped in white and put in hot water. You need to reach a knowledge that you can grasp without the doctrine flowing away unnecessarily like this.

In Kerala, recently three gurus have come among the Hindus - and then some as contemporaries. Anyway, none of them came to ruin you. They have done whatever good they could. But you don't know how to take this goodness. You have tried to take this by following the footsteps of the religious doctrine written with this conflict. That is not how it should be; you should take this from the knowledge that destroys the conflict from the doctrine and takes only the goodness. In that karma, you will change to the dharma seeking virtue. It is not enough if someone else says this for you, we must know and take it ourselves.

Imam means guru. The guru who can tell us about our spiritual matter. How is that? Jagrat, swapna, sushupti (waking, dreaming, deep sleep). What happens in sushupti, what happens in the sushupti while in jagrat - this Imam must tell us. Hindus show another guise for this. They say, when the mind gets peace, its strength will decrease. Then the body will not be able to sit straight. Therefore, a device is needed to keep the body straight - in Tamil, kannakol - so that it doesn't droop. But what is the use of just putting a kannakol on the neck like this? This is the truth that has come in the special love of the life where consciousness is merging. Karma is needed to take back this special love.

We must know from an organizational traditional nature what all is done here to raise the younger generation in that karma which has been known and made known through our ancestors. Then we will understand, no one is bad, everyone is good in their own way. But we have to go very far forward. Why? There might be tied-up demons in what they worshipped. This leaves the complete form of thoughts about God and shows the noise of a chaotic dance. Through this chaotic dance, they actually spend money, complete it by telling fabricated stories, and make it seem that their God is perfect. When we try to change this from such Hindu logic and other logics, we must remember the depth of the word spoken by the Prophet, the matter spoken about the samadhi of Sree Rama, Ramatirtha, and Shankaracharya, and similarly many other things deceived by those established in yoga. There is a practice to take back these karmas. The subject you need to know for that is not the logic that Hindus strongly speak up to heaven. Saying that everything will be alright by the grace of God is the hollow word of a person without substance. Nothing will be alright like that.

This is not the issue. Can the story of karmic successes and failures over a very long period be fixed with just a single word?

To prevent this mistake from happening to at least your children, we are purifying the karma that has reached an impassable state, which your ancestors accumulated as demons, gods, and goddesses, and which has now passed on to sages and ascetics. That flawed karma is as vast as the sky. There are such accounts in all religions.
