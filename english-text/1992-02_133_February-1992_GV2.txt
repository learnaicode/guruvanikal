February 1992

Along with devotion, some negative aspects also arise. What are those negative aspects? What all can we change in it? What all need to be completely discarded?

The experiences of those with and without mental knowledge will be different. Mental knowledge can be experiential and Vedantic. Whether one has mental knowledge or not, the first negative emotion that arises along with devotion is related to the five senses. The five senses represent the five qualities of the body, don't they? These qualities quickly rush into the mind. At that time, the mind must be protected without putting these qualities into practice.

Due to the lack of vasana, no matter how highly one can think, emotion will also be present along with it. One could say it is out of love. But there is no devotion. That is not good. Devotion must be separated from this. If you want unadulterated devotion, you must work daily and steadily to eliminate that adulteration.

There are many reasons for this in many religions. In Islam, women are not allowed to enter the mosque. But they can be buried there. There are mosques where wise women are buried. For example, Thuckalay Mosque and Beemapally.

Why is this so? It is the spiritual knowledge contained in the respective religion. This knowledge (Vedanta) is not in Christianity.

In the initial stage of devotion, our topic is how to control the five senses that rush into the mind. Suppose a certain emotion has taken hold without changing. Then at times, devotional thoughts will also be regained. We should try to increase the time we get like this. If we try, we can maintain this time for up to a day. If kept like this, it can be achieved in six months.

For this, there must be a dedication somewhere. Somewhere does not mean to a devil. It should be dedicated in a form you like with purity of heart. It is best if it is to a Guru who is God. If you see a bird, it is very beautiful. But if you check its nest, it will be a mess of stench. Man is much more stinking than this. Why is there an attraction for men and women to this stinking body, the seat of the eight passions? Out of love, and more so out of beauty. The eye only sees, it is the mind that brings closer. If this cause of attraction is not changed, it will destroy. Therefore, whenever these tendencies rush into the mind, they must be changed through action and dedication.

Is the paddy in the granary? How can one dedicate to the paddy that is yet to come into view inside the granary? In that place, one should dedicate to the Guru. That place should not be assigned to a deity. Only a Guru can be a witness to any action and dedication. A deity cannot be that. Any bad thing can be dedicated to the Guru. When dedicated to the Guru, it will be destroyed. How is it destroyed when dedicated? The course of action can be seen in the light. Then the Guru can be seen in the place of the course of action. With the Guru coming to that place, the bad aspect will catch fire and be destroyed, and good will come.

The state of Guru is equal to God. Guru and God are not different. A favorite deity or a Guru's gift is accepted as a medium to see God. There is a difference between these two. The favorite deity (Goddess) will keep the worshipper under it. Guru is not like that. The Guru state is an indescribable lightness. Because the Guru's light is filled in the five elements. The five senses are its transformation, the Guru's light filled in the five elements then becomes knowledge through the five senses. That knowledge then spreads to the Annamaya Kosha, Pranamaya Kosha, Vijnanamaya Kosha, Jnanamaya Kosha (Manomaya Kosha), and Anandamaya Kosha.

It was mentioned earlier that everything in the world is created by the Annamaya Kosha. (In the absence of Prana, nothing grows.) Once the knowledge as light reaches the Annamaya Kosha and Pranamaya Kosha, all this can be recognized in the part called the mind's knowledge. That recognition becomes a special recognition in the Vijnanamaya Kosha and is completed in the Anandamaya Kosha. Then there is only bliss, nothing else remains.

That bliss is the bliss of life. When bliss comes with special recognition, the Guru's life will shine in all living beings and exist as the Paramatma. This is the attainment of the ultimate goal. The experience that starts at a small level becomes dense and acquired by the passage of time, the advice of other wise men, and self-knowledge. This ultimate attainment must be experienced and known. Asking and knowing is not good. To experience and know, there will be no more curiosity. Once the experience begins, you can sit near the Guru who advised and ask and know.

Only then will it become clear. In any other way, it may lead to doubt and doubt may eventually lead to a decrease.

What should be the disciple's dedication? Whatever one does, it is all for the Guru. With this attitude of dedication, one should see all the attributes of God as the Guru. Then it will be understood that the Guru is one who has seen, experienced, and realized everything from merging with the five elements to the knowledge perfected in the Anandamaya Kosha. Then the Guru, God, Brahman, and Parabrahman all become one.

Growing with knowledge through birds, animals, and all living beings, rising to the highest levels, when one becomes capable of knowing what God is, one realizes through what all this life has worked for the knowledge that is touched by the five elements and perfected in the Anandamaya Kosha. The state of Guru is a personality that has gained freedom from all those conditions and flowed with self-realized knowledge.

To the true disciple who approaches that Guru, when a small experience is received testifying to this Guru, his inner self will whisper that this is the path to the ultimate goal. Here he does not need to ask. The fulfillment that cannot be attained no matter how much sacrifice one makes is obtained by the grace of the Guru that comes through dedication. It is enough to act with the firm belief that it will be obtained.

Thus, by operating, developing, and destroying the blissful experience that comes through love, sacrifice, and compassion in the five sheaths, one can know how it worked in nature in the beginning, middle, and end until destruction. Then one can know what the disciple is, what the Guru is, and why the Guru became a condition. Thus, it will be clear that the things to be asked and known should be by sitting near, and if sitting near, there is no need to ask. The problem is not whether near or far, the mind is the problem. Closeness is for minds. No matter how far away, one can hear them talking sitting near. Because the body has been taken away, there are some things to ask and know. Once known with cause and effect, questions and answers gradually decrease and eventually the need for them disappears. Now, what should be the purpose of the Guru?

Elevate the disciple to be like oneself and a little more. The Guru, the disciple, and nature become one. Nothing remains that does not fit into it. It was said to elevate the disciple a little more than the Guru. The best example of this is Sri Ramakrishna-Vivekananda. The Guru has no literacy or worldly knowledge. But the disciple has both. When self-knowledge was imparted to such a disciple, it became much broader.

One should try to change the mind day by day and elevate it through action. Patience should be gradually developed. The love for what is believed should shine through humility. The disciple's love and humility will be according to his logic, ability, and luck. If there is a slight deficiency in the mind, it should be immediately changed through dedication. One should not pray desiring anything. His resolve should be that the quality that is useful to me should be useful to everyone through my action.

When devotion increases, there will be coolness in what is loved. The humility that I have done very little will come. That devotion is what lasts, what must be maintained. Only by seeing the Guru as Parabrahman and loving him to that extent can one achieve. For that, everything must be dissolved. One must acquire intellect for intellect, love for love, and humility for humility. If one has the good deeds of past lives, one will see the Guru

You will experience it immediately. You will also understand that it is the complete love of the Guru. What is needed first is love and humility towards what is seen. It should happen spontaneously without one's own knowledge. Such people can endure any sacrifice for the sake of attaining God. They will be sweet-spoken. If these qualities are imparted to children, that is enough to improve the world. God is the same everywhere.

Guruvani

March 1992

If you do this carefully, countless people will come and go here. Through you, not only you or your home, but the world will benefit. The mind should be enslaved only to God. If you firmly think about what you perceive today in such a mental state, within six months you will be able to discern the course of the world. Within three years, you will develop a nature capable of clearly understanding and predicting the past, present, and future of any individual or country.

If you grant yourself dignity, great things will happen through you. People will say that the good things happening in the world are happening through you. Any fatal disease will be cured in a week.

The Guru should not be provoked to anger towards the disciples. It will cause great losses. It is enough if one of you develops the habit of thinking. Everyone will become alright. You should think about what we should transform these things given by God into. No one can come here and commit any wrong. He will be destroyed. I might not do anything for them to be destroyed. Because they are people who frequently come and fall at my feet. But nature will destroy them. Then I will not be able to do anything.

Our goal is to provide peace and tranquility to the world. This action is like pulling a mountain tied with a comb thread. If it comes, it is a big mountain that comes. If it goes, it is just a comb thread. There must be such a strong loving nature. Only after we experience its sacrifice and its love will we have goodness.

Suppose one of your colleagues has a small flaw. Four people should not get together and talk about it to each other and make it bigger. If you do that, another harm will come from it. But if it is handled secretly in the form of advice, that flaw will change. All that is our experience here, isn't it.

No matter how much noise they come with, you see them calm when they come here. The reason for this is the glory that has come to this atmosphere through our prayer. Previously, those who came here came for healing. Now knowledgeable people and those curious to know about spiritual matters are coming. That too is the result of our prayer. God will give us double the result of what we do. No matter how educated a person comes, they bow to us. What did the monk who came from Bangalore say? He said he couldn't express the glory of the air he breathed from the moment he entered this gate. He said he hadn't experienced such a pure atmosphere in any other ashram.

Because this atmosphere is pure, no one can enter with unwanted behavior. It is an environment here where such people cannot stay. You only need to think about what to do with the time you get and how to use it. You will get results for what you do and what you think. Cash income may be less, but prosperity is more.

The noisy demons seen in the world today must destroy themselves. Only then will we be satisfied; that is our goal. It is enough if two or four of you go out and work -- all these noises will be destroyed, and material and spiritual prosperity will be strengthened in the land. That is where the survival of our idea given by God lies. Whatever work you do, your intention should be this -- this idea should survive with the world, and through it permanent peace and tranquility to the world. When working, the feeling that 'I did all this, I brought it about' should not come.

That will do harm. What if someone sees? What if they don't see? Doing it for something, not to show anyone. You must think with your intellect and act very carefully. When that happens, good will come into action without our knowing. This atmosphere will always be calm and glorious. The thinking habit that takes shape from it will be strengthened through action, shaken, troubled, and finally become full of goodness. Whoever among you does whatever work sitting anywhere, everyone should together intend to come forward in that work being done. Those working inside and outside the ashram should know each other and share love. That is true love, merit.

Many obstacles will arise between the time the capacity for action moves from one state and reaches another strong state. There, dedicated action completely devoid of ego will reach the goal. The intention that 'God, make the big and small actions I do fruitful, whatever I take and give, let it be fruitful' should always be there. There will sometimes be moments when devotion grows, the mind fills, and intentions arise. If done strongly at that time, all harms will be cut off.

Let's go. Three or four intelligent people should get together and think about how to implement this in the world. Everyone should pray that those who work and those who have vision come forward. Only that will help the upliftment of this lineage. Through the lineage, the country, and then the world. This is the nature of the spread of this idea.

How can the love of God be instilled in these blind people? Before that, if one worships God, if one develops love for God, one must think about what the consequences will be. We have the notion that worshipping God will bring complete happiness. But what I have to say is about an experience completely opposite to this. This body is the cause of sorrow. Those who worship God will experience sorrows from time to time. Because the vasanas (tendencies) that have been residing in us for ages through countless births are being untied one by one. Occasionally, happiness will also come. We must give importance to that happiness. It is an imperishable happiness. But there are those who live according to what they see without paying attention to any of this, considering themselves and being considered by others as knowledgeable. We can see them going to London and Paris for their own happiness and to bring happiness to others. They only feel happy if they go there. At the same time, if their own mother is lying nearby unwell, they don't even have the time to go and check on her. What is all this? It is a lack of love. If the parents have money, they will find the time to inquire about them. But what should be our perspective regarding happiness?

It is not like going to the market, the hospital, a political speech, or a temple; there is a love here that is above all that. Everyone participating in the Akhanda Namajapam here experiences a calmness, knowingly or unknowingly. Those who come here somehow blend in with the ways here. A calmness from this atmosphere and from certain individuals, we must preserve that. Even if there is nothing in action, a calmness is felt. The reason for that is that God makes the actions we do fruitful. Now there is more to change and come from this atmosphere. This has been accumulated here a long time ago. It is the foundation needed for this lineage.

What is the nature of this era? It is corrupted by spreading demonic qualities. In my childhood, there was a time when there was a notion that producing an unnecessary sound or word was a mistake. Today it is exactly the opposite. A time ruled by the demonic. God has a plan to change this. The pain of this is first felt by the sannyasi. A situation where he is not given any kind of help or convenience. Everything around him will be completely unfavorable to him. No one will obey him, and the opposite side of it will be very strong. If he is spiritually inclined, all the demonic forces will attack him and make him suffer inexpressibly. However, where a chicken is slaughtered and an oracle dances, there is no difficulty. Those with the nature of an oracle will not be able to stand here if they come. But there are some sannyasis who seem to have reached the perfection of sannyasa. Those in whom devotion and humility are strong. Such people will unknowingly stay here.

The reason for all this is that there is not even a trace of the demonic here. To completely remove the demonic nature, we established a karma here a long time ago. God has informed us about it as completion. Two thousand four hundred and forty-four gurus and their entire lineage have been shown to us. Among them, the first seven gurus have ten thousand crore disciples each. The rest have four thousand crore each. The demonic nature in all these guru lineages has been eliminated.

The gurus were brought into the divine light. It took four and a half years to purify these guru lineages that stood in a corrupted atmosphere. Even after that, many completions took place. Finally, in 1973, God announced, 'What I had desired for ages has been fulfilled.' With this, the disciple lineage of these gurus has been taking birth all over the world since '73. Children higher than them will be born in this lineage. Our prayers, resolves, and actions are for that.
