[GV3:P0001] Guruvani

[GV3:P0002] June 1999

[GV3:P0003] 'The space is self-luminous' - One should see the Guru in that self-luminosity. From there, one can see how vast the possibilities of that Guru are. This is the principle of the soul. Vedantins, researchers, and psychologists have nothing to do here. How many theories are there? How many postgraduate disciplines and research departments? But the one who took pride in being a theorist went his own way, and the poor man ended up nowhere. The tradition is now reaping the fruits of such actions. It has to be experienced. There is no point in blaming God for it. If the fruits of karma are to be taken back, there must be a way, a path that everyone can take together.

[GV3:P0004] Great souls like Jesus and the Prophet have done their karma for humanity across various eras, sacrificed themselves, and shown us the way. Those who are eager to reap its benefits are trying to see their doctrines in many different ways. This is the aftermath of a betrayal. Do you want to be saved from this betrayal or not? What path have you chosen for that? If any path is enough, isn't it enough to become a Christian or a Muslim - isn't it the path of the Prophet?

[GV3:P0005] What is needed is a dharma that aligns with the karma. That dharma must be sought, found, knocked upon, and opened. That is, one must seek and find a visionary who is capable of predicting what will happen until today and believe his word. Even if anyone says their word is wrong, it is not wrong. Their word is the will of God. The will of God must be respected. Grounded in that belief, one must investigate the special provisions of karma capable of sustaining the dharma of the times. By bringing the aspect of knowledge into karma, this visionary guru wipes away all sinful karma after seeing the weight of sins and virtues, transforming the soul into a virtuous one to bring it from that karma to dharma. We must see and believe in such a Guru as everything. This Guru is capable of showing the entire nature to us, the owners of karma that lies absorbed in nature. Through the glory of creation and that glory itself, this Guru will show us ourselves. The Guru will show that the Guru is present in all glories. But he is none of these. If one examines all that is said to be 'I', the inside is not many, but becomes one.

[GV3:P0006] When one comes to believe that this Guru is everything, seeing its point of origin, one sees creation and preservation. Then, this seen object is neither this nor that, nor is it the meaning of what is seen. Becoming courageous with the clarity of real meaning, it settles softly, softly. To reach this state of settling, who should the Guru you hold on to be?

[GV3:P0007] It is not easy to get such a Guru. Even if found, he will not open. To open, one must know how to knock. When one becomes courageous and settles softly upon realizing the true meaning, they have seen through the states of waking, dreaming, deep sleep, and Turiya. But these states exist in Turiya and Turiyatita. To show who stands in that state, what state must the Guru have reached? He must be someone who has gone beyond Turiya and Turiyatita. This state is what is meant when it is said that after crossing six steps and reaching there, Shiva can be seen. We could then see what Shiva was, what Shiva's karma was, what our possibilities in that karma are, and what we are going to become. We have only just begun to see it. It is later that the value of Shiva must be found. Thus we were brought here. There, the space devoid of name and form is self-luminous.

[GV3:P0008] The Guru is showing what the soul principle is. If you believe, you can find it. If you knock, it can be opened. By believing, finding, and knocking to open, all the deities of creation, preservation, and destruction have been seen. Only later is the luminous goal, which is the primordial form of all this, understood.

[GV3:P0009] The intellect of discernment will arise like ten million suns rising - this is what is said here. How can you take this? We have a difficulty in this. Because we ended up believing in all these ways. Our ancestors also lived this way. We have received this birth as a remnant of the karma done by our ancestors; and also due to the fortune of our soul. This fortune of the soul must be enlarged. Liberation must be given to the ancestors. We must take up the right of our karma and become a soul-form that shows what virtue is through dharma. Thus, one must reach a position of experience and karma worthy of making a man a Deva, a sage, an ascetic, and a liberated wise man.

[GV3:P0010] What is experiential worthiness? How far is it? All past karmas must be integrated and brought forth with a new attitude. To say it a bit more subtly, it is not that we bring it, but God makes us do it. Everything happens according to time.

[GV3:P0011] From a grain of sand to whatever things are created and destroyed in this world, everything has its own wholeness. It is with the support of these wholenesses that this world itself exists. Looking at it this way, it is clear that even wholeness cannot exist without the support of another wholeness. Humans, without knowing the mutual support of wholenesses, support here and there, seeing greatness in each thing like a frog in a well. While living in this world understanding this, a 'One' becomes necessary to stand firm on. The truth is that if one acts firmly rooted in that One, the action will be in everything. Otherwise, the action will become so empty that it won't even feel like an action was done.

[GV3:P0012] If you stretch your gaze into this atmosphere, you can see how many atoms are flashing and fading within the span of a coin. It is enough to think that we are just one among such countless atoms.

[GV3:P0013] Nothing is non-existent. If thought to be non-existent, it will lead to emptiness. All atoms have mutual access. It must be understood that this penetrating nature will reach everywhere like gas. Isn't the nature of the mind also like this? Whatever our desire is, the mind will enter into all those desires like a gas. Whether good or bad, the mind will enter that way. Some ascetics are examples of this. Through certain worship-like behaviors, they will believe and worship goddesses and gods as God. It will become a worship according to their desire. Among many such people, some will feel like showing off their prowess in it. Then the desire to apply supernatural powers (siddhis) will arise. This siddhi will be to attract people or to achieve selfish things. Whatever it is, the desire itself is the goal. One person's desire may also cause immediate harm to many.

[GV3:P0014] Since the time of creation, the world has stood as a mixture of good and bad. Thus, we, who are a mixture of good and bad, and many others like us, must think and know what this world is before performing miracles upon miracles. Otherwise, to undo the destruction caused by these miracles, one will have to constantly strive by performing more miracles. Thus, the efforts of both these groups became empty. One created, the other destroyed. No one gained anything either. This is how the so-called ascetic strives and fails. None of them did this with the desire to be bad. They slipped in their actions.

[GV3:P0015] If we do not have the innate nature to act aiming at the will of God with our minds set on the worship of God, this flaw will unknowingly take hold of us. Such flaws come to us who act in numerous external and internal matters.

[GV3:P0016] To regain the merit in karma, an era-appropriate guidance is absolutely essential. In the Kali Yuga, guidance must be given by weighing its intensity and measure. For this, there must be a clear understanding of what Kali is. The notion about Kali that people and everything else are possessed by Kali, and that it is a time when humans are ruined, unable to do good, is incorrect. We ourselves say that we have arrived on this earth according to the fruits of our past karma. If so, a little result of what was done in Treta Yuga and Dwapara Yuga will also come in this Kali. If it comes, shouldn't the good done then also come? That is, when acting in the fourth era evaluating the good and bad of the past eras, it is an absorption that reaches a liberated state that is neither good nor bad through the results of actions where the weight of sins and virtues has been balanced. There is no time like Kali where any thing can be brought forward if one works hard. That is why our ancestors, the sages, desired to be born in the Kali Yuga to attain liberation.

[GV3:P0017] Evaluating and knowing all the things that have come till today, rectifying their flaws, systematizing them according to the times, giving a form to the coming state, and striving to bring it forward is what is happening at Santhigiri. This is the knowledge given to us as Brahman.

[GV3:P0018] When systematized in an ashram nature, some differences arose from the ashram nature that has existed till today. The traditional practice of completely following the path shown by the predecessors is what is regularized here. God has entrusted us to systematize the worship methods of Vaishnavism, Shaivism, and Shaktism according to Hindu logic. This is an effort to bring a 'One' in place of all three. That is how much distance there is in the experiential worthiness of this ashram. No one else has anything to do in this.
