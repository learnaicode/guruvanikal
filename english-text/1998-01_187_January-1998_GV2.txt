GURUVANIKAL · January 1998 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P1324 to GV2:P1338
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

January 1998

Do not taint your faith. Never do it for the sake of love from another person. We must value the love for our life and the love for our lineage. Until now

If you haven't done it for that, you should at least start doing it from now on. When you do so, many sorrows will come. Let the good come. We shouldn't pay attention to that. If good comes, let it be good.

We have never looked up to a position of knowledge until today. Or if someone has spoken a word of knowledge, we have only jumped on it and destroyed it. Having caused destruction of lands and homes to the extent that neither man nor woman can grow on earth, we have changed into the position of a kind of ignorant people who are nothing and are conducting a test of strength. What is the benefit to us from this test of strength? When this test of strength reaches our children, what will it become? We don't have to go anywhere looking for fighting, murder, and robbery. It will be right in our homes. A tragic situation will arise where a good girl cannot be raised to become a good mother tomorrow. Instead of organizing and growing the love, affection, and respect from the mother and the knowledge of the father together, and spreading the light of that knowledge to the world, the father and mother are fighting each other.

You must decide what is needed for this. All the love and all the compassion to make such a decision and be brave are here. But you are not able to do it. The men and women growing up in your home are the ones who should impart a goodness of this world. You, the father and mother who are householders, will have to think, worry, grieve, and change according to this action. Otherwise, no matter how many sages or prophets come, nothing can be done here. Our history until today is proof of that. How many thousands of gurus and emperors came after Sree Krishna. What could be done here? Haven't we only been able to reach an action of fighting and winning? To change this, instead of leaning backward, we should see what happened in the past and move forward with its solution. Men and women, big and small, should be able to come forward in this way.

If each woman and each man does not have this care, you will not be able to escape. It is not to give the good and bad of the course of action done by the rulers like the imperial position which were our past miseries, but rather what should be the existence of goodness without evil coming into our hands, what should parents be, what should we be - parents are the ones who should make children capable of bringing them back to this thought. Similarly, one side is to remove the deity, the ancestors, and the sage from in front of you and bring your action forward there. This is the action of this ashram. But you do not understand it to its extent.

If an ancestor has many flaws, some of those things will be seen in you too. While this remains true, if you associate and act according to your tendencies, will you get any benefit or knowledge? Will you be able to get rid of those flaws? Will you be able to implement anything good in your home? Then what is the benefit? Father, mother, children, husband, and wife will all become a burden. This is a course of action where we ourselves are destroyed.

Regarding sages, not all of them can live as wise men. At the same time, if they grow according to their knowledge, we do not know how to utilize them. This is the truth of it. If we only sit to find fault with them, how will they correct their mistakes? We must correct our own mistakes. Sages must also correct their mistakes. This is its side. Otherwise, this side of the mistake will not end. If anyone says by Vedantic imagination that it will end like that, it is not a side that will happen.

Regarding India's dharma, a degraded dharma exists today. In this dharma, people have lost a way to survive. Therefore, even though many great men sent by God Himself came here, no one was able to fulfill God's will. How many of our scholars are qualified to ask how this happened? They have only been able to show scholarly pride by writing and teaching fragments of Vedanta. What exactly is the karma called Vivekachudamani by Shankaracharya? Scholars have elaborated on it and pushed it into a karma that has become oneself. That is, the karma called Vivekachudamani is considered to be confined to oneself. How can a karma that is confined to oneself be convinced to oneself? That means even scholars do not have this knowledge. Or if anyone has that knowledge, these scholars praise him as a great man and a great siddha. Moreover, this mere thousand-year history has been prolonged by calling it Adi Shankara. Now, if asked what is the reason for saying Adi Shankara, they close it by giving various examples. How will we achieve anything through the Adi Shankara in these examples?

Madhvacharya came to fill this gap. He also concluded that it can be patched up with Dvaita. What do you see Dvaita as? As two. Only by evaluating crores of systems can one understand what this Dvaita is. When portrayed following the footsteps of Advaita philosophy, it will end up in the fulfillment of Aham Brahmasmi, or the four Mahavakyas. Then, by formulating fragments of Vedanta, they will make it something that belongs to no one.

Who are these Siddhantins (theorists)? They are a set of people who came handling Siddhi (mystic powers). Along the way the Siddhanta originated, they evaluated the medical theories of Agastya and Bhogar, and made them tools to preserve the body. Thus, they discovered that one can live longer. But this is the practice of Yoga. We also have the knowledge written that one without Yoga is a corpse. And we never understood what Yoga is.

Setting aside the four Yoga rules of Hatha Yoga, Hamsa Yoga, Chandrika Yoga, and Raja Yoga, this has been included in Karma Yoga. Sri Krishna has entrusted us with Karma Yoga. What is Karma Yoga? What happened in the waking state (Jagrat), what happened in the dream state (Swapna), what happened in deep sleep (Sushupti), what happened in the fourth state (Turiya)? This must be known through experience. Then it becomes Turiya consciousness. All great men have said that all this should be practiced near a Guru. This is a truth in our experience here.

What becomes clear in this experience? In some Yuga dharmas, the Guru and oneself existed - one sees what all was done at that time. Both men and women see it equally. But even after seeing all this, you have an inability to stand firm. That shortcoming of ours is not our fault. The position of the knowledge passed down to us by our heritage is only that much. What do you see this great glory, imparted by God to be imbibed, as?

Today, there are men and women among you who fully see the Trimurti system. Coming through the Trimurti system, on which paths the Guru stands there - that is the difference in states. Men and women are seeing this. If this difference in states is not carefully observed and understood, you will not be able to grasp the word received from the Guru's mouth.

What is the transformation of state? That is, you are seeing it starting from a blade of grass. Starting from this, you yourself are seeing an interstellar nature that you pass through. There are seven stages in it. You are crossing all these seven stages. We must remember that the previously mentioned theorists cannot even explain two of these seven stages. Except for some people mentioning certain calculations, even great men do not say much about this. When the sixth step is crossed, it means the position of Devendra is crossed. Once the position of Devendra is crossed, the form is lost. Or one reaches the position of Parashakti. A woman must cross this position. There must be at least one mother in this lineage who has thus crossed the position of Parashakti. That is, the fourth state must be completed. The fourth state is completed when the six steps (bhumikas) are completed. Theorists and philosophers have written and completed this in many ways. But each of you must think about what the experience of our lineage is. You must also think about what kind of knowledgeable people you are to have this experience. If both men and women are to get this philosophical knowledge, what state must the Guru you get have attained?
