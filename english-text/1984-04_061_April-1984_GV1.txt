[GV1:P1387] April 1984

[GV1:P1388] Today, this is not what we need. Today, we have moved far beyond all that and come a long way. However, despite coming this far, we have not been able to achieve what we need. It is not that we have failed to internalize it in our minds. But it is doubtful whether any human being has been able to manifest even a slight change in their behavioral disposition. For instance, an ascetic is unable to do it, and a householder is unable to do it. Then who else can achieve it here? If a householder fails, an ascetic should be able to—and if an ascetic fails, a householder should. Both these groups have failed. Then how can people leading other kinds of lives achieve it? Either the householder should cultivate it themselves, or the ascetic should facilitate it. Both have not materialized. Those we call our ascetics have erred. The householders harbor the misconception that these matters are not for them to practice, but are meant exclusively for a specific group of people. It is as if doing good has been deeded entirely to them. We assume we do not have to do any of this; we can just wander about being bad. We must display foolishness. Seeing all this, we must teach others as well and lead them along this same path. We should be capable of putting on any masquerade for that purpose. We end up demonstrating life in that sense.

[GV1:P1389] What we have heard about people called ascetics is that they at most consume some green leaves. Even then, not all green leaves. An ascetic is one who abstains even from water. That is one group. But I have mentioned this many times before. They achieved this state by consuming poison. Such practices were demonstrated by the royal sages (*Rajarshis*). Seeing his poor subject doing immense good for the people merely with a call to "God," the royal sage felt—this is a terrible predicament, I am being subordinated to him—he pondered over this for a long time. However, there is a way to deceive him. Some ascetics themselves taught him that. What does it mean to consume poison like this? Certain substances belonging to the toxic minerals category (*Pashanas*) are placed inside the stems or at the roots of our cultivated crops as fertilizer. Thus, it is gradually mixed into the food. Today, we experience this even without consciously doing it. It has come to a point where there are no substances without added ammonia. These are being applied to the roots of many plants. Similarly, if poison is continuously ingested in small increments, eventually, consuming a small amount of poison will no longer have any effect.

[GV1:P1390] Thus, among the leaf varieties, it starts with the holy basil leaf and concludes with the *Kanjiram* (poison nut) leaf. It stops at the *Kanjiram* leaf—specifically, its tender shoot. That is referred to as a rejuvenation therapy (*Kalpaseva*). After this come the medicinal varieties. These medicinal herbs do not have the bitterness of the nine toxic minerals (*Navapashana*). Other ingredients apart from *Navapashana* are brought, processed, and consumed as medicine; following this, *Navapashana* itself is purified and ingested. Each has its specific consistency and dosage. After this, there is a combined *Kalpaseva* incorporating everything.

[GV1:P1391] When this stage is reached, he will develop an aversion to food. Then, a very minimal amount of food will suffice for him. Consuming milk, fruits, or similar light diet will be enough. This is what is termed as a prescribed diet. After that, a cave with nine openings or faces is constructed. The stipulation is to spend a specific number of hours at a particular cave entrance. This is done by observing the northern solstice (*Uttarayana*) and southern solstice (*Dakshinayana*). Therefore, during the *Uttarayana* period, one must sit at specific cave entrances. For the next six months, it will be at the *Dakshinayana* entrance. These specific cave openings are constructed only after studying all these factors thoroughly.

[GV1:P1392] After that comes the matter of caretaking. It is said that boys fall short when it comes to caretaking. Girls are better in that regard. Yet, they are easily swayed. They have a disposition where, if they develop an interest, they will act even forgetting their parents. If they place their trust in someone, they will forget everything and follow him. Whatever they are following, they call him their lord. They will simply go after him. So, taking advantage of this convenience, he will win her over. One must be mindful of that too. There are two aspects to what I am saying. Once she is won over, she will remain steadfastly committed to the purpose for which she came. Then, no matter what is offered, she will not turn back. There is no other instrument in this world so unhesitatingly willing to forget the parents who raised them, their locals, and their family. Nevertheless, this is used for that purpose—this is what is called a lawful wife (*Dharmapatni*). Whatever the *Dharma* may be, no matter what, if she is kept close and taught all this, she will look after everything meticulously. By meticulously, it means she will constantly watch to see if there is any deficiency while he is seated. But if he sits, he will slant or fall. Why? Hasn't his body been ruined? This body of his has become completely worthless, like an old rag. Why has it become like a rag? By continuously consuming these three categories, the body has become severely flaccid and distorted. That is a bodily transformation that occurs when consuming this medicine. She will be instructed on the regimen for administering the next medicine to reverse this condition and the protocols for nursing him. She will be taught beforehand exactly what needs to be done. It is after such instruction that she is appointed for caretaking. She will be given some attention and affection. In light of this, she herself will be appointed. Then she will execute this flawlessly.

[GV1:P1393] On the contrary, if a young boy is entrusted with this as a disciple, he will cleverly stand nearby and think, 'When will the Swami wake up next? Let me go and come back in the meantime,' and he will just walk away. Slowly looking back time and again, he will move away and then make a run for it. Even hearing the sound of this running is enough to frighten the ascetic. This has happened to many. These disciples will deceive them like this. But with that, the ascetic's breath (life) will depart. This is something that depends on a fraction of a second. If that precise moment is missed, then everything is ruined; thereafter, neither this food nor medicinal variety will go down his throat. He will die before it even goes inside.

[GV1:P1394] Therefore, it is with all these precautions in mind that a woman is chosen for this. And what if all this is consumed? It is widely believed that all this is a remnant of divine grace. This is nothing divine; it is merely a gimmick he has mastered. Displaying this gimmick, he consumes all this and emerges. It is used over a period of seven years. Six months of regimen, and six months of its preservation. When practiced like this for seven years, seven layers of skin are shed, and truly, aging and greying will not occur. The calculation is that this body can be sustained for about two thousand years. It is brought to this state. The conditions under which this must be achieved have also been written down. This is not called knowledge but rather an attainment (*Siddhi*). Others assume that *Siddhi* means it is bestowed by God. This is not given by God at all.

[GV1:P1395] Because the body is conditioned in this manner, neither food nor water is required thereafter. If any venomous creatures touch him, they will die, but nothing will happen to him. No matter what block of ice he goes and sits on, he remains unaffected. So, seeing all this, we assume—Oh! What a great man he is, while we cannot survive even a single day without water and food. This was devised by the royal sages just to confound the poor common man. Because the Shudra lacked knowledge, he construed this as divine grace. When this Shudra came to rule, many such gimmicks were propagated here.

[GV1:P1396] Guruvani
