Malayalam source paragraphs GV4:P0794 to GV4:P0823

[GV4:P0794] April 2003

[GV4:P0795] There is a karma that we have been performing up to this very moment today. That karma possesses a truth and a purity. We must act with the awareness of this. One should not simply believe whatever someone else says. The knowledge and experience required for us to act are bestowed upon us by God. We should not squander what God grants us. Whatever God bestows will undoubtedly be beneficial to all of us.

[GV4:P0796] No matter who we are, we are individuals who have lived based on a certain faith. This applies to people of any caste or religion. When faith went astray somehow, with the repetition of religious dogmas, and through further misinterpretations and retellings, it became impossible to even comprehend the practical application (karma) of the doctrine. Each one of us is the remnant of such outdated theories of karma. No one can rectify it instantly. Depending on the hardships endured by some individuals and their willingness to suffer, the magnitude of the error can be reduced. When a suitable opportunity arises, it will diminish rapidly. Then, the impact of that error will be akin to a mere cut on the finger where a major calamity was destined to occur.

[GV4:P0797] Our subject is how this archaic karmic trajectory can be resolved. If anyone claims that this karmic path—which we and our ancestors have spoken of, practiced, performed, and caused others to perform until today—does not exist, it will not simply disappear. To assert that it will vanish just like that is sheer foolishness. No matter what we do to reinforce it, it will not be eradicated. However, concerning us, there is a divine grace. God Himself has revealed a solution to that stale karma which we have been performing knowingly or unknowingly up to this point. Both men and women in the household must strive equally to realize that solution.

[GV4:P0798] Once the educated and the uneducated, the foolish and the intelligent, the capable and the incapable—all realize that the greatness of God is one and the same, they must not succumb to arrogance thereafter. Alternatively, if an intelligent person becomes arrogant, one can only let him proceed on that path. Being arrogant about one's education and wealth will yield no benefit whatsoever.

[GV4:P0799] Each one of us is the remnant of the ignorance that proclaims, 'God is confined to my caste; God is confined to my religion.' That means we are standing on the wrong side of knowledge. It is a fallacy to assume that some great soul has taken away all my mistakes and the resulting deficiencies, and that I merely need to sit idle now. These mistakes are not something that any great soul can simply wash away.

[GV4:P0800] As far as we are concerned, we must first attain the knowledge to experience the karmic flaws—or the karmic merits—that have originated through our birth. Experience it with awareness. Let sorrow come if it must; nevertheless, I must exhaust it—this should be our objective. Saying, 'Oh, I went there too, it's all the same,' is not true knowledge. If you act upon such a misconception, unimaginable destruction will befall you. No matter how much pain or hardship we endure, we must handle and resolve this.

[GV4:P0801] How are we to know what our ancestors have done? Demanding that wealth and nobility should accrue equally to everyone is something that is not going to happen anywhere in the world. One group will claim that they are working hard and suffering. What work, whose work, and for what purpose? What if one works for the welfare of the people? Or rather, what exactly is the welfare of the people? Let each person contemplate this. The house of the one who struggles for the welfare of the people will be ruined, that is all. Some will say they have suffered greatly and have not been able to know what love is. Therefore, these matters cannot be reconciled merely through such statements.

[GV4:P0802] We will only receive what we are destined to get through the karma we painstakingly perform with love, humility, and respect. If one decides to act impulsively, uttering trivial things without love and guidance, it will merely be a rash leap. Nothing will be gained from it. Things will only unfold according to the dynamic course of the soul. If we possess everything but are in a state where we cannot even swallow a drop of water, is that not the misfortune of the soul? Others hold many things that could be done for us. Yet, no one does anything. What can be done? Just because others give us something for a day or ten days, will it suffice for us? Do our children not need to grow up as well? What we must understand is that none of this constitutes the karma of a sense of justice. One can fabricate and say many things about the sense of justice. One can even decree destiny. But that is not the destiny of his soul, is it? Regarding this destiny, men and women are entirely equal. If anyone claims otherwise, it is a guidance that pushes one completely toward a misguided path.

[GV4:P0803] We observe diverse types of politicians and religious ideologues. Do their assertions reflect the true trajectory of the world's karma? Must anyone even articulate the nature of the world? The fault lies neither with those who have made such pronouncements nor with those who reiterate what they have heard. Nothing can be accomplished by making impulsive statements without comprehending the underlying sorrows, knowledge, and experience, as well as their profound depth, spanning the past and the future. Indeed, nothing has ever been achieved in this manner. When such overwhelming evidence stands before us, why do we engage in unwarranted speech and actions?

[GV4:P0804] Like you, I am someone who has suffered a great deal. However, I do not dwell upon those hardships. Could I survive if I were to think like that? It is true that I have suffered. That is its inherent nature. God bestowed a certain dignity upon that suffering. If God provides for us, we must be willing to share it with others. That is true sacrifice. For that, wealth, capability, and resources will all be required.

[GV4:P0805] What is the root cause of our suffering? It is the unbearable burden of our lineage. What constitutes that burden? It is the lack of dharma, fortune, and spiritual merit in our lives. Can one simply go somewhere and discard it in a single day? You should not tell anyone such things. What is being said is incorrect. These are merely deceitful tricks of religious dogmas, pseudo-spiritual justifications, and supposed miracles designed to manipulate people. No one should ever believe in any of this. As far as we are concerned, we possess God's grace. This is the absolute truth. However, one must experience the pain to earn God's grace. We may possess absolutely no knowledge regarding such matters. When we complete our deeds of good and evil, we repeatedly perform rituals according to the doctrines of our respective religions. Consequently, we dance to the tunes of others' logic. What does it matter to you how others dance? That is not what I am stating. No one should assume that they can achieve salvation through contrary actions. Should we presume that it is the father's fault, the mother's fault, the society's fault, or the nation's fault? Everyone bears some blame. The land of our birth lacks fortune. What is the reason for our existence in such circumstances? Is it not sufficient to realize that we are even more unfortunate than that?

[GV4:P0806] Indeed, we are worse than everything else. Our ancestors were those who brought about destruction by mistakenly perceiving loveless actions as love, heavily veiled by an inability to ever comprehend the true trajectories of life. When God grants us an opportunity to transcend this, you—whether as father, mother, husband, or wife—must never behave in a manner that forsakes it. Only then can you attain salvation. Once you abandon it, you will be left with absolutely nothing.

[GV4:P0807] Everything we presently do is divinely ordained action (karma). If we abandon all these and walk away, neither our dharma nor our karma will reach completion. You can eradicate this only if you foster an unwavering resolve, love, and respect to consciously eliminate your sufferings along with the sins and merits accumulated through the ignorance of your ancestors. Otherwise, you will never be able to discard it. Eventually, you will abandon your faith as well. This has been my experience.

[GV4:P0808] Let our ancestral doctrines be either right or wrong. We need not attempt to attain salvation by debating their validity. We have been born. Now, what can we do regarding all this? We can contemplate a time when God has bestowed some grace upon us as an opportunity for this purpose. The trajectory of karma—which enables us to mutually understand, communicate, act, and inspire action—must be a love that aligns with dharma. It must never be a love that depletes our spiritual merit. Destruction will ensue in such cases. You must act with unwavering detachment in this matter. If God provides us the opportunity to methodically execute and facilitate this, we must accomplish it. One must fiercely hold onto that determination—that love and respect. If love, humility, and respect are lost, all that is good will subsequently follow suit and perish. A majority of our ancestors were those who strayed in this manner. We must not be provoked by observing them. You must never believe that someone will grant us anything if they perform purported miracles or display the complete manifestation of mystic powers. It will merely result in the eradication of our love.

[GV4:P0809] Having understood these principles to some extent, you must strive relentlessly. Strive earnestly without squandering time. How many individuals do we see before us who have thus wasted their time and opportunities? Some, overwhelmed by an unbearable excess of supposed love, will exhibit many dramatic behaviors. However, after a while, all their love vanishes! I can only say that a thief has stolen it. I cannot draw you closer by narrating other tales. You may grasp as much as your knowledge and love permit.

[GV4:P0810] Regarding knowledge, I do not intend to thrust profound wisdom upon you. Your knowledge, experience, and maturity are limited. Consequently, you are unaware of the nature of the sins and merits of your ancestors. Let that be whatever it may. I have been born, and I have grown up. Various things have occurred, either in this lifetime or in previous ones. I must attain liberation from all of this. This should be our prevailing thought. Otherwise, there is no point in becoming agitated upon hearing someone else's assertions.

[GV4:P0811] Many have fallen because they failed to act with this realization. Therefore, I am stating this directly to you. We can collectively accomplish whatever can be uplifted through our intrinsic love, respect, and humility. You will experience various afflictions such as disease, sorrow, hardship, and poverty. These are indeed universal among human beings. Certain ailments remain uncured despite treatment. Sometimes, treating one illness leads to another. Yet others are incurable diseases. Although we may cite numerous reasons for all these, the fundamental truth is the lack of spiritual merit in our lives. What can be done to remedy this deficit of spiritual merit? This is an profoundly elusive matter. It remains incomprehensible even to devout believers and those who present themselves as great visionaries. Furthermore, it is immensely difficult to find an individual who can provide a resolution for this.

[GV4:P0812] This issue remains consistent across Hindus, Christians, Buddhists, and Jains alike. We tend to celebrate the traditions we have been practicing for a while. When such a conceptual framework is imparted, this karma transforms into something akin to a disease and a hardship. This afflicts many individuals. Yet, no one articulates the root cause behind its occurrence.

[GV4:P0813] The practice of the majority is to place faith in whichever deity their ancestors worshipped and to occasionally visit and offer prayers. However, one must not assume that this constitutes true devotion to God. We can practice devotion to God in all possible ways. The prevailing concept is that God exists in everything. That is not the case. This conceptual nature has emerged due to numerous errors committed because we failed to comprehend the concept of God by acting in accordance with each respective era.

[GV4:P0814] Numerous individuals who lived in grandeur, and precisely those who lived performing numerous good deeds, have become entangled in such concepts. Would it not raise doubts if one were to state that the cause for all this is a lack of spiritual merit in their lives? Would wealth and capabilities accrue to those entirely devoid of any spiritual merit? However, we must study and ascertain how to utilize that merit, in what form of action that merit remains eternally sustained, and what that merit should be transformed into through our lives. We have been conditioned to believe that upon acquiring a little wealth or capability, one should live grandly, look down upon others, and elevate oneself at their expense. What spiritual merit exists in the lives of us who harbor such mentalities? We possess the cumulative prior experience of having lived in this manner for millions of lifetimes. How can this be transformed?

[GV4:P0815] We live, grow, and die. What is the benefit to our soul (jeevan)? We scrub, bathe, and carry around a stinking body. What will we do if there is no life in this body? What if the soul becomes like a rotten tree? How can this be resolved—no one comes forward to explain all this. Where do we go when we suffer the most? To an astrologer, a sorcerer, some temple, or some swamis. No one should assume that we will attain the aforementioned merit (punyam) from these places. There is nothing special about such a belief. What is the deficiency in our soul? This is the goal: how to remove that deficiency? We do not know this at all.

[GV4:P0816] What we have heard of is the system of the Trinity, or the system of prophets, or the path of the Guru—whatever it may be, shouldn't one follow the path where the word of God is received? If we follow these paths, what will we be able to know about our ancestors after four thousand or five thousand years? If we proceed according to the calculation of epochs (Yugas), billions of matters are involved in it. Can we proceed only after knowing all this? None of these traditions have ever taught us about the Yuga Dharma. This is the reason for the deficiency in all people. We cannot immediately live according to the Yuga Dharma either. But this is the time destined for that.

[GV4:P0817] Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Shudra—Varna Dharma is defined by these four. It has not been stated for whom and in which epochs (Yugas) this Varna Dharma applies. Since it is taught that the Dharma of Kali Yuga is bad, most people assume that no Dharma needs to be followed in the Kali Yuga. But that is not the case.

[GV4:P0818] To say that 'Kali is in the Shudra' implies that the Shudra has absolute authority. That is, even a Shudra has the right to Brahma Jnana (knowledge of the ultimate reality). Only a Shudra in the Kali Yuga can attain Brahma Jnana. We must firmly believe that through the Dharma of Kali Yuga, a Shudra can acquire the right to Brahma Jnana. Otherwise, no matter what politics, caste, or religion we speak of, our sorrows will not disappear. The deficiency stemming from the rituals and worship practiced by our ancestors will not vanish, regardless of the realm they exist in. Every household leads a life that has grown stale in that deficiency.

[GV4:P0819] There are many people living in forests and on seashores, worshiping minor gods for minor castes. They have no knowledge of what God is. When they do things based on what they hear and see, everything goes awry. Later, they die and end up somewhere.

[GV4:P0820] There is a sky around this earth. Beyond the layer of the earth, there are three outer spheres—the Earth, the Moon, and the Sun. Between these, certain spheres travel at distances beyond the reach of our eyes. They are called Navajyothis. Otherwise, they are referred to as the sky (Aakasham) or the earth (Bhoomika). There is a calculation like that. Can we function only after knowing all these calculations? These things exist whether we know them or not. We may not be able to comprehend all this within a single lifetime.

[GV4:P0821] Let me tell you some things you can do. When our minds go down while we are doing something, it is due to a deficiency in our soul. Getting a disease while growing up and engaging in various activities is also due to a lack of merit (punyam) in the soul. We are unable to accumulate merit for the soul. This is the collective shortcoming of humankind. An astrologer will look and tell you to do this or that. Doing those things will not yield any results.

[GV4:P0822] Our ancestors who lived here have become gods, goddesses, sages, ascetics, or enlightened beings. Where do they stand after death? Coming as their descendants and dying again, billions of souls thus end up in domains devoid of merit. Children born in those domains become diseased, fail to get what they need for growth when they reach that stage, and lack the ability to accomplish things—this is generally called family curse (kudumbadosham). This is not just a family curse, but a deficiency of the soul. This must be corrected through life itself. Not by rejecting one's husband, wife, or children, but by adequately caring for them. One should not do anything by abandoning their children, father, or mother. We should be able to achieve all this through a life lived together. A fundamental ritual (karmam) that enables this is performed at Santhigiri. That is the Guru Pooja at Santhigiri.

[GV4:P0823] Guruvani
