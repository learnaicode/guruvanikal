[GV3:P1100] January 2001

[GV3:P1101] There will be many paths from Kanyakumari, the southern tip of India, to reach the Himalayas. Even if one comes from the north of the Himalayas, there will be many paths. Similarly, on the journey to see Brahman, many souls will block this Avadhuta. According to the logic of Hindus, this includes everyone from Mantramurthi, Yantramurthi, and Tantramurthi to Brahmarakshassu. It is this Brahmarakshassu that is called King Jinn in Islamic logic.

[GV3:P1102] Among us, there are many tombs, burials, and places where devotional matters are learned and spoken, and Vedas are taught and practiced. This is done more by Muslims than Hindus in South India, and more by Hindus than Muslims in North India. The results arising from this are very difficult to explain and convince. There are respective positions (states) in spirituality. These are souls who have departed after seeing these positions here and there.

[GV3:P1103] Looking at the familiarity of the realm of experience, there are three layers in the skull. When experiential familiarity comes, the atoms in these layers will undergo a change. The body will also change. Along with this change, the spiritual path will also change its course. There are countless such souls in this nature who have changed their path. They also have the experience of showing (four or five) magical powers. Seekers of spirituality have to pass by subduing the influence of these billions of fallen yogis who strayed from the path. This is also the condition of those who come with a divine mandate. Normally, this may not be possible. Thus, their path also goes astray. If one strays in the logic of siddhis and yogas, nothing can be done. Even if the influence of this strayed path is very little, unless it is removed, one cannot be saved. You are all those who came from such a lineage. Because this path of deviation exists in all religions. None among you is unincluded in it. I have done a karma for you by removing that and purifying it. Therefore, no one should ever bring any yoga practice, any logic of siddhis, or their glories and place them alongside this idea.

[GV3:P1104] It is very difficult to raise someone who can preserve the idea without making mistakes in external karma like this. Because when a person grows in this state, these aforementioned fallen yogis will fight in a way that no one can resist. In what all ways they have tried to destroy me! No one will understand any of this. This is not something that becomes right if linked and spoken with something else. The philosophy you try to link must one day harmonize with this. You should not criticize it. Moreover, the prowess of the great soul who brought it must be seen as a model to the extent it has grown. If great souls are belittled, it will be a grave mistake. Because the Indweller (Antaryami) is waiting for that word to emerge from you, to infiltrate through it. The voice of the Indweller will pass through the tone of the one who speaks and pull him into error. This is the real Jinn that Islam spoke of.

[GV3:P1105] When infiltrating one by one, millions of these yogis and brahmarakshasas will also be standing there. Who can hide this truth? Sri Narayana Guru has said this in 'Daivachinthanam' (Thoughts on God). To sit until Samadhi for three months, seeing these jinns and their beastly amusements, what a sacrifice the Prophet made! He attained Samadhi after proclaiming that these jinns are ruling the world.

[GV3:P1106] The rulers who have come in the tradition of any philosophy or religious doctrine existing in the world today are nothing but the play of these fallen yogis. Playing like this, eventually everything will become victims of natural disasters. Not a single religious theorist has understood this. Regarding Hindus, what do they have other than taking the doctrine of some great man and telling stories? All the scholars in this world have written and filled exactly that. But God will not sink this all at once. He will show it little by little here and there.

[GV3:P1107] This era is the era of Manu. But nobody knows this. Without stating the current Manvantara and which Chaturyuga it is within the time calculation mentioned in Manusmriti, there is no point in saying anything. When saying this, one should not denounce any temple. One should not denounce any religion or any thought. It should remain. Until it goes away on its own.

[GV3:P1108] It is your experience that bears witness to you. That will then come into karma. Experience exists in many places. There are experiences in many paths in North India. Santhigiri's experience is nothing like those experimental natures. We must look from where the mistake occurred in the Manu lineage. This mistake is the reason for the creation of all religions. The original mistake is forgetting the spirit of the truth that Manu had meditated upon. Following that, the demonic grew completely. That demonic sits inside the house. The mother has no obedience. The children have no obedience. Four people in four different ways. If there are ten people, five have no faith.

[GV3:P1109] Our idea starts from where Manu dharma ceased. The practice here provides an introduction to it. The Acharyas and Prophets in all religions have strived to impart this introduction. But that lineage did not accept it. Therefore, that form will disappear from their experience. To prevent that from happening, a child must be with you. The Guru himself will raise that child. Because the Guru raises it, it doesn't have to wander lying on the lower steps. All households that came in this faith must have understood this truth. That child will execute the Guru's karma and dharma. Thus the Guru's dharma will be executed. This is the time when the Guru's dharma can be brought out like this.

[GV3:P1110] The physical structure of the Acharyas who came in each era is different. There is karma that came in each era through each one who came in each structure. This is being told and shown to this visionary child. This visionary will explain this. No one else is supposed to explain the meaning of the vision. This disciple can see and explain what all the Acharyas have said. That is why it was said not to belittle the Acharyas. Mistakes might be seen. But what right do you have to speak? A single person who has at least religious consciousness, let alone spiritual consciousness, will not belittle an Acharya.

[GV3:P1111] The Acharyas have given the account of lights, haven't they? Here, children with vision see and state this account of lights. And they are able to determine and state that the light of the gods and goddesses standing in those realms is like this, with this nature. For humans, it can be a god or goddess. But when looking at their permanent nature, they are demonic forms. Even if it is a demonic form, one should not say it does not exist. If said, that demon will enter immediately. This will not correct anyone. It doesn't know how to correct.

[GV3:P1112] What is the benefit received by believers here? If any demonic forms (indwellers) stand as an obstacle to the vision of the visionaries here, there is no need to even think; just calling out to the Guru or feeling pain in the mind is enough. The Guru will arrive immediately. Immediately, this form will bow and move away, or illuminate and move away, or dissolve and move away. This benefit belongs to the Guru. It is the Guru's compassion.

[GV3:P1113] So getting such a Guru is the greatest of fortunes. Forgetting such a Guru is the misfortune of the world. This is said in the Vedas and Shastras. Everything is said and finally testified in the Guru. How can such a Guru be recognized? That is, the Guru who makes the disciple capable of seeing everything as existing, giving it its existing position, discarding what needs to be discarded, and accepting what needs to be accepted!

[GV3:P1114] Guruvani
