[GV1:P1594] June 1986

[GV1:P1595] * Continuation of the April issue

[GV1:P1596] Question: In the past, it is said in the Puranas and elsewhere that great things were accomplished by manifesting the gods, isn't it?

[GV1:P1597] Answer: That is more or less true. Because the knowledge, qualification, and intellect of the writers are mixed into it, errors will occur in the Puranas. Those who study such errors, consider them as truth, and practice them will also suffer the same folly. They commit worse mistakes than atheists. Their souls will have to struggle immensely to attain salvation.

[GV1:P1598] Question: What do you say is the reason for that?

[GV1:P1599] Answer: Because this soul acts and learns in this manner, remaining somewhat aware and unaware of the fruition of its actions, it may happen that they cannot live respecting anyone during their lifetime. The reason for this is the arrogance that they are the ones who have experienced and known certain things. Beyond that, it is their unwillingness to see anyone else.

[GV1:P1600] With this, all of their actions will begin to go wrong. Thus, they will lead a life that ordinary people cannot endure. They will be unable to speak respectfully of highly spiritually faithful people. They will even reject them. Many such people can be seen in various places during the exact same time period. Upon leaving their physical body, their souls will remain embedded in the atmosphere. They will not get a rebirth quickly. Standing there, they will gather souls lower than themselves alongside them. Thus, they will become a group. They will torment ordinary people and mislead highly devoted individuals.
