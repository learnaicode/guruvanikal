GURUVANIKAL · February 1991 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0015 to GV2:P0044
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

February 1991

Material things are essential for man. Without material things, one cannot understand or respect anything. Our body is karma; it is material. What can be achieved without the material body? To accept the dharma of the body, karma is needed. Only the knowledge gained through this will be of use to us. Position also belongs to that knowledge. How have we, men and women, big and small, imparted this knowledge through our lives?

There was a time when one could diagnose a disease by feeling the pulse. As it became more modern, it reached a point where it could not be done without touching the flesh, blood, bones, and marrow. No matter where or what is examined, it is impossible to know where the disease is lodged, and all undiagnosable diseases are grouped together and put into a language that is difficult to understand. We are thus enslaved to each language. There must be a change to this mistake. It is the householders who must correct this mistake that has come about through age. Only they can correct this mistake. One has to struggle a lot for that. One has to endure much more hardship for this than the hardship experienced in the fields of husband, wife, children, education, wealth, and lordship. Let this karma of setting things right begin with each person's own home first. This should be handled in a way that we and everyone can understand. Because from birth until one is able to walk, one can only grow in the environment of the home. A mother who grows up like this will have that tendency in her blood, marrow, and flesh. To develop this tendency in the mothers at home, men must first develop it themselves. The environment of the home should be developed as much as possible in that way. When that effort comes with the attainment of merit, the very method of learning the earth's knowledge will change. When there is no such knowledgeable purity of purpose, the karmic path of mankind itself goes wrong, the dharmic path goes wrong, they become children who sow destruction, and the goodness that the earth has as God's gift will also be lost. This must be done according to the changes of the times. It is because this is not done that thinkers, administrators, caregivers, householders of the world, and all those who work for the welfare of the people have to regret that the karma they do has fallen into a vicious circle. Even those who preach great traditions of teachers and spout moral advice and Vedantic hymns in public have realized that their own lives and the overall life of the society they lead have fallen into this vicious circle. They are those who do not think about the things that have come as God's mandate or evaluate the duties of the times. Therefore, the householders have been molded in such a way that the people are caught in the grip of devotion, liberation, logic, or atheism, and are ready to throw themselves into a great fire pit that is about to come.

All the methods that the Westerners once invented to save themselves, enduring shivering cold and ice, have now been turned back to destroy us today. Despite having a nature suitable for living in any way and a land with rich agricultural yields in our possession, we sat idle, doing nothing, making others work, playing the lord, and today we are experiencing the consequences of making others do the work of a slave. That very karma has returned. The situation has come to us where if we farm, we have to ask someone else to harvest it.

To harvest the entire crop, one must stand with folded hands before the reaper. Otherwise, half the crop will have to be left in the field. Thus we stand in the midst of all degradation. We have no water here, no justice, no fairness. What kind of freedom are we experiencing? We, who have no freedom, say we are free! Do we have anything to consume today other than polluted air? Is there any of the five elements that is not polluted? This situation is slightly better in our country. Because such large companies and factories are not operating in our country. Thus the world is being destroyed. Our feeling is that it is getting better. A few ignorant people who sing praises that technology and science are making things better; a few people who are not involved in any of this; a few ignorant people who sing yes to whatever is said! This cycle of karma, which does not know how to think or make others think about the constant destruction happening in the world, makes your life itself empty. I am once again reminding you of the things that have been announced to the world for some time now as the word of God. The technology of the West, which shows its greatness by writing, drawing, and conducting experiments to prove whether the greatest invention is ours or yours, is destroying the people not only through attacks from the earth and the sky but also through ideas, actions, and governance. I am reminding you once again that if the householders of the world do not think about this, they are voluntarily preparing to throw their own generations into the great fire pit that is soon to come. Because, no matter how they are created, is it possible not to send one's own children into that very society?

What is everyone doing in the name of politics? It is an action that perishes day by day, an action that must perish. If children who know nothing follow them, their life itself becomes that. It is when half their life is over that they think back. By then, it will be impossible to return. If they return, they won't be able to live, right! Leaders will admit when half their life is over that this is not right. If you ask any leader closely, they will admit that this was a mistake. If you think carefully, you will understand that this is a first-class adverse effect done to the younger generation. But we have never realized what a great mistake this is. If householders desire and act together, this evil will fly away from the earth itself. It is enough if you understand that this is a deception, it will go away on its own. As long as you do not understand it yourself, you will not be able to respect and believe it if this is told to you. No one needs to intentionally discard this action. It is enough if you continue doing your duty with knowledge.

What is that duty? It is the completion of the karma acquired in previous births. You cannot know and complete all previous karmas. You don't even need to know it. It is enough if you learn to believe. This is what Jesus said: your faith will save you. You must learn to believe, right. You are being given a place to believe. Standing firm in that source, you must act and complete each one. You, your wife, and children are caught in the same karma. Whatever you attain, they must also attain. The father must be a person who has attained love through complete faith, karma through love, merit through karma, and fortune through merit. A mother's womb worthy of this is also required. Have we raised a mother with such qualifications? Does the father, who should be the god of the children, at least reach the knowledge to show them God? Your search should be for this. No matter what else you search for and give, it will not be enough. This is karma, qualification. The father and mother who reach this qualification complete the offspring of the world. This is not something a sannyasi should attain, you householders must

What can be done if you walk away abandoning it? Caste, religion, and race are not applicable to this family life (Grihasthashrama). The dharma of the time should guide you as a light.

This is Kali Yuga. The dharma of Kali is the guidance given by a Guru who, beyond mantras and tantras, has completed the Shakta, Shaiva, and Vaishnava traditions, acquired merits through rebirths, attained liberation, and returned as one free from karma. Kali overflows in a way that proclaims even the ideas of Jesus and the Prophet are not enough. To fulfill the dharma of Kali, the wife and mother in our homes must be as good as the wife and mother of the Prophet. At first, the Prophet could not recognize the word that came from the light. He thought it might be his imagination. It was his wife who recognized and encouraged it. They were that good and qualified. You should be able to nurture a wife who says, "I will pray for you to attain goodness." Only by attaining such qualification and performing karma can you become the inheritors of the karma to redeem the era. Otherwise, no matter how great one is, one who is unqualified in karma will not get this. For this ever-changing interplanetary cycle to bestow goodness from their respective positions, God's mercy is essential. People should get this knowledge through the governments. The people and the governments must accept this appropriately. If one pretends not to have heard or seen any of this and remains immersed in mirage-like calls and soothing words, how can one think and understand the possible interplanetary destructions that may occur? If India is to be developed as the basis for the eradication of poverty among the children of the world and as the source of God's true word, we must maintain constant vigilance to accept this bodily dharma through our lives.

Guruvani

March 1991

There are seventy-one Chaturyugas in a Manvantara. Kali is the smallest yuga in a Chaturyuga. But when compared to Western calculations, this Kaliyuga is very large. Westerners only have a calculation of six thousand or seven thousand years in total. Kaliyuga alone has four lakh thirty-two thousand years. Thus, Indians have a calculation of countless Chaturyugas. Why did such destruction come upon Hindus who have such vast calculations? How many theories are there here? How many postgraduate fields of study and research departments are there?

The one who takes pride in being a theorist went his own way. The poor man was left nowhere. The result of the karma that came in this way must be experienced. There is no point in blaming God for that. If the fruits of karma are to be taken back, a method (path) that everyone can take together must be adopted. Great men like Jesus and the Prophet have brought before us and shown us the things they sacrificed by doing karma for humanity over different times. Those who are interested in reaping its benefits are trying to see their theories as many. This is the aftermath of a deception. Should we desire to escape from this deception or not? Which path have you adopted to escape? If any path is enough, then becoming a Christian or a Muslim is enough, isn't it -- isn't it the path of the Prophet? Regarding those theories, except for the fact that time has passed (Islam has ninety more years), they are the words of the Prophet.

Whoever is born will die after a certain period. Within this much life, having earned father, mother, wife, husband, education, wealth, and lordship, how much is virtue, how much is sin, how much is fortune, and how much is misfortune? How should children understand a father who drinks toddy and beats their mother? The logic that children must experience the fruits of their karma is a kind of evasion. This evasion is not what is needed. What is needed is the knowledge and humility to ask and understand something that cannot be understood from those who know. At least a good attitude to speak openly about things is required.

When you get an opportunity to know about the karma part that makes one think about what destiny one needs and what is not needed, do not have the attitude and language that "I am also smart." After you understand that destiny, the love, affection, and richness to share it with others should remain within you. That richness can only be achieved through sacrifice. Without this sacrifice, no matter how much gold you accumulate, you are not going to gain anything. Nature will surely destroy it.

If each person has a course of karma, that course of karma will remain in their life. If the father does it, in the father; if the mother does it, in the mother; if the children do it, in the children. If one does not deliberately try to change it, it will remain unchanged. There are two paths to change this -- the path of the Guru or the path of the Prophet. The other is the path of the Devas. The time of the Deva path is over. History proves that the Deva path was followed in the Treta and Dvapara yugas. Later, Krishna, Buddha, the Parayi Petta Panthirukulam, and the eighteen Siddhars said that the Deva path is not suitable for Kali. Without listening to or obeying any of this, one had to lie down getting beaten and scolded on the logic of "the lord's rice and the slave's labor." Because someone who came from somewhere gave food, education, and freedom and saved them, a section has attained courage in karma. If you are those who have attained the courage of karma, broad-minded children capable of showing what the courage of dharma is will be born in your home. That will only happen in householders; not in ascetics. How much have you tried with your life to raise such a lineage? Aren't you raising a lineage of offspring whose arrogance grows as wealth increases, doing all sorts of unwanted things? To prevent that from happening, with humility and qualification

You should think, feel, know, make known, and act with the richness that comes when karma-gati (the path of action) and dharma-gati (the path of righteousness) - through which both merit and fortune can be earned - come together. A sanyasi (ascetic) must be one who advises this path. The false coinage of royal sages who sat on treetops listening to stories without drinking water has been implemented here in the name of sanyasa. That is a poisonous system. It is not any of this; what is needed is dharma that accords with karma. It is that dharma which must be sought, found, and knocked open. That is, one must seek and find a visionary who is capable of saying that such and such will happen by such and such a time, and believe his word. Even if anyone says their word is incorrect, it is not incorrect. Their word is the will of God. The will of God must be respected. Grounded in that faith, one must inquire and know what the special rule of karma is that is capable of sustaining the Kali dharma.

By bringing the aspect of jnana (knowledge) into karma, in order to bring one from that karma to dharma, this visionary performs the action of seeing the weight of merits and sins, wiping away all sinful karma, and transforming the jivatma (individual soul) into a virtuous soul. We must believe in such a Guru as our everything. This Guru is capable of showing the entirety of nature to us, who are the owners of karma merged in nature. Through the glory of creation, and through that glory, this Guru will show himself as well. He will show that the Guru is present in all glory. But he is none of these. Regarding this, Narayana Guru says, 'If you inquire into all that says I, I, it is not many inside, but becomes one.' When one comes to believe that this Guru is everything, seeing its point of origin, one sees creation and sustenance. Then, 'neither this nor that, this seen object is not the meaning of what is seen, but the true meaning,' and becoming courageous, softly, - softly one will settle. If you are to be brought to this settling mentioned by Sree Narayana Guru, who would the Guru you have caught hold of be?

It is not easy to get such a Guru, and even if you do, it will not be firm. How should one knock to make it firm? Becoming courageous, realizing it is the true meaning, softly - softly settling down, one has seen up to jagrat (waking), swapna (dream), sushupti (deep sleep), and turiya (the fourth state). But these states exist in turiya and turiyatita (beyond turiya). What state must the Guru who shows who is standing in that state have reached? He must be someone who has crossed turiya and turiyatita. This is what is meant by saying that when one crossed the six steps and reached there, Shiva became visible. It became visible what Shiva was, what Shiva's karma was, what validity we have attained in that karma, and what we are going to become hereafter. It has only become visible. It is later that the value of Shiva must be discovered. Thus we have been brought here. There, one must see the Guru who is devoid of names and forms - ('The place is self-luminous.'* We believe in that 'self-luminous'). From there, one can see the extent of that Guru's validity. This is the principle of the Self (Atma-tattva). What can theoreticians, postgraduates, and psychologists say here?

The Guru is showing what the principle of the Self is. If you believe, you can find it. If you knock, it can be opened. By believing, finding, and knocking it open, one has seen all the deities of creation, sustenance, and destruction. It is later that the luminous goal, which is the primordial form of all this, is made understood.

'The intellect of discrimination will come like ten million suns rising.' That is what is said here. How can you take this? We have a difficulty in this. Because we have come to believe like this. Our ancestors also came and went like this. We have received birth as the remainder of the karma done by our ancestors. And also according to the fortune of our life. The fortune of this life must be enlarged. Liberation must be given to the ancestors. We must take up the right of our karma and, through dharma, show what merit is, and transform into the form of the Self. Thus, man has come to a position with karma worthy of becoming a deva, a rishi, a sanyasi, a jnani, and a liberated one. What fortune!

Unayumathikulesha drishyajalam; Punaravide thripudika poorthinalkkum Svanavumadangumidam swayam prakasham! (Atmopadesha Shatakam, Sree Narayana Guru.)

Guru Vani
