[GV3:P0831] How do these Yugas change? Three Yugas have passed. Satyayugam, Tretayugam, Dvaparayugam. In Satyayugam, everything will be equal as truth for all of us. Tretayugam came. We did not get back the karma of that Tretayugam. The karma of Satyayugam is not for Tretayugam. The karma of Tretayugam is not for the Dvaparayugam that followed. In Kaliyugam, which is the fourth quarter of a Chaturyugam, there should be a completion for all these three. The reason we did not get back the karma of these Yugas is because we do not know what all karmas have happened, and the divine will that gave the opportunity to fulfill this Yugadharma, which was divided into four as Karmi, Yogi, Bhakta, and Jnani. The Jnanis see this from the origin. This is to be completed again in the Dvaparayugam. That Yugadharma was not fulfilled and taken up. Tretayugam, Dvaparayugam - in these two Yugas, those who did not seek salvation in Satyayugam and who were not Jnanis were sent as Gurus or Devas. This is the time for this Deva and Devi. Why are they given time like that? When man becomes a Deva and the completion of the Deva comes, then it is the departure of the Jnani. A Tyagi (renunciate) or a perfect Sanyasi should be in accordance with the departure of the Jnani. This is the dharma of Kali. It is due to a flaw caused by a Brahma curse that no one becomes a Jnani. The reason for that is the ignorance that came through the four dictums like 'Aham Brahmasmi' and later the Panchamahavakyas.

[GV3:P0832] The practical aspect according to this Yugadharma comes through the Rishis. Although the Rishis are not under anyone, when the time of the Deva changes, they do not wish to go beyond it. The Devas keep the Rishis as their clan gurus (Kulaguru). The kings keep them as their clan gurus. While advising the kings, they try not to let them go from under the Deva. That is why this Ashvamedha Yaga is conducted. When talking about Sita, stories were made up that Sita was found by plowing. Thus, making father Janaka the foster father of Sita is because the story was covered up. Without knowing the meaning of Yugadharma in a way suitable for this, the Brahmin became a Tantri. He became a Tantri and also an authority of Yugadharmas. How far will this Tantri reach?

[GV3:P0833] Do not believe that this Tantric karma is non-existent. It is for all three times. The third time was made into a Jnani. That is Kali. At the end of Kali, it is changing to Satyayugam. The calamity that the Yugadharma did not take place in that way is due to the Brahma curse. This has crossed twenty-five Chaturyugas. No story outside of this has come to date. Then some Gurus among the Jnanis wished to tell the truth here and there. But, when it came to three traditions—Vaishnavas in the Vaishnava tradition, Shaivas in the Shaiva tradition, and Shakteyas in the Shaktheya tradition—the Vaishnava was made a person of Satyayugam. There was no one to cross over the Deva and go beyond. When operating this way, Jivatmas who were qualified to become even Brahmarakshass could not pass through. Along that path, they took the name of Yogi and remained in the Kalpaseva tradition and in the fortress of Siddhi networks completed using various Rasamanis. This is the Mulika Siddhanta (herb theory) of Bogar Ezhayiram. This is also what Agasthyar's Pantheerayiram says. Then the Tantrasamucchaya called karma was covered up here. They aptly named it Tantra. The Vedas and Itihasas themselves had to be forged so that they would never deviate from this Tantric aspect. Thus, when coming along that path, it is seen that it stood without a way to cross it until the time of Buddha.

[GV3:P0834] But, it is not the Buddha we see today. This is what 'Yatra Yogeshwarah Krishna' says. Therefore, a value judgment had to come stating that the Buddha we see today has no karma.
