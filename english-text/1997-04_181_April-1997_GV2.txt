GURUVANIKAL · April 1997 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P1146 to GV2:P1156
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

April 1997

There are quite a few people who have had the opportunity to come and stay in this ashram. After some time, they have a lot of sincerity, love, and respect. But there is a task that needs to be completed in our imagination. Until it is completed, everyone should have a restlessness and a conflict regarding it. How long have I been coming here, why did I come, what were my thoughts when I came, who would I have become by now, what is my duty towards this divine work having undergone such a transformation - matters like this should make everyone restless. That conflict is goodness.

Most of the activities here are done by householders. This uniqueness brings a part of a new knowledge. What is this new knowledge? In the past two eras, crores of people have come and gone to determine the fate of the yugas. The past two yugas mentioned are the Treta Yuga and the Dvapara Yuga. We don't need to make any major evaluations regarding the Satya Yuga. Because we have heard that the dharma of the Satya Yuga happened according to God's will. Therefore, it is enough if we evaluate the Treta Yuga and Dvapara Yuga. Many such Treta Yugas and Dvapara Yugas have passed like this. India has the authority over all these yugas, their actions, and their evaluations. No other place in the world has the authority to evaluate the actions of the yugas. The reason why India alone got this God-given authority is because of the actions of the great sages here. The great sages did not work to fight and win against anything. They were the ones who strove to bring joy, respect, mutual love, and a sense of cooperation among us. One aspect of the action they did with that desire passed in the Satya Yuga. However, in the Treta Yuga and Dvapara Yuga, it was not the action desired by the sages that took place. Thus, it has been going wrong for twenty-five Chaturyugas. The fundamental mistake has happened in the order of worship.

When we say worship, it should not mean just the worship in our country, but worship in all parts of the world. Worship is an aspect of the greatest freedom. When evaluating freedom, our country has often been unable to maintain that freedom. People from many countries have attacked and subjugated our country and destroyed our freedom of worship. It has happened so many times. The reason for this is that we failed somewhere. For these twenty-five Chaturyugas, we have not been able to find out where the real cause of this failure lies. Moreover, it has not been proven how the knowledge and familiarity of the Yuga Dharma have been lost over so many yugas. Highlighting the miraculous deeds of sages, telling wonderful stories of yogis who live for many years giving up food and water, talking about the valor of princes - it is by saying all this that we take pride in our Arsha culture. Finally, these celebrations come to an end by jumping and dancing in front of some devil. We have been taught that the God who shows this test will save us. Let this God save us and straighten us out or implement our duties and dharmas, but because such a familiarity has come among us, neither a man nor a woman has been able to bring about a truthful life. So many kings ruled in a state that cannot be fully depicted even by a single language existing in the world. They changed and established culture and language in their own ways. As a result, India was left without even a single language to stand together. The plight that India has reached now, to unite the underlying aspirations of the people

It lies in the inability to transmit it. Even if the 'unity in diversity', which is described as the unique heritage of India, sprouts up somewhere, we have the helplessness of not being able to transmit it into our lives.

Before speaking about realizing this unity in people's lives, and the action and culture required for it, I need to reveal some truthful aspects. There are some important occasions in my life, and the knowledge and pains that have come together during those occasions. Since you do not try to evaluate and understand these insights and pains, I am recording them again in another way. I did not live my life thinking that I would have such a mission or such a sacrifice. All I had was an imagination of love. As that imagination of love transformed and began to spread among the people, I wondered if this worldly life was this painful. Even though I lived among ordinary people for six or seven years with the burning of this pain, no one could understand that there was such an ache beneath this life. No one knows why fellow human beings who do not experience this level of pain fail to understand it. But this pain is a tradition.

Generally, we hear many things about the greatness of the guru-disciple relationship. That is somewhat true as well. But no one has been able to understand that the goodness in the guru-disciple relationship only reaches a certain part of the truth. The goodness planted by the guru in the disciple grows, and the fruit of that goodness is determined through the experience that comes from the disciple. The experience where everything grows in the disciple and becomes material wealth, knowledge, mastery, and enlightenment. The blessedness cultivated in him in this way is for the survival of dharma for all times! The pain when none of this aligns with the guru's expectations is unimaginable. Thus, life itself becomes a struggle for life. In a life lived as a synonym for struggle, the very course of a tradition—the tradition mentioned earlier—grows and stands tall. The unchanging course of 'I am big, you are small'. This course has brought about a series of pains in every way.

So, what has society gained? Only an asset of not being able to love anyone. This asset has expanded and reached the familiarity of a land and the familiarity of a country. This can never be set right. This lack of love has become an action through many ages. Our effort is to bring a change to this. This life of effort is what is called ashram life.

I have convinced you of this many times. I have made you experience it truthfully. But what I see in you is a familiarity that cannot be transmitted in your home. The character that cannot be brought about in your home cannot be brought out outside. Even as father, mother, and children, you do not unite. This lack of unity has come to your neighborhood, the country, and all parts of the world. It is not easy to change this. Because it is a familiarity that has been respected and worshipped for so long. It is a familiarity that has been passed down through lords, kings, emperors, and various other forms of government. People have even assumed that this is truthful knowledge. Now there is no need to abandon that knowledge. Because we have the freedom to live in a better way based on this knowledge of ours. Moreover, if knowledge and wealth can be tied to this government, there is a situation to achieve anything. Therefore, in front of knowledge, in front of truth, we are mute and blind. Reaching all the systems of life, a large government has arrived that does not know how to behave with whom, and it is deceiving us with the size of that government. Staring at such a system, what, where, and how can anything be done?

What has come down through the ages must change completely. That is, what has been worshipped ancestrally must change. What needs to be changed and re-established in every age has not been changed for many ages. Thus, the dharma of the age has not been carried out. That is the root cause of our destruction. Our efforts must reach the extent of establishing this dharma.
