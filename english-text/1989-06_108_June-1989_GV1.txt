[GV1:P2388] June 1989

[GV1:P2389] Guru: What all attributes have been harmonized to sustain this sphere called Earth? Sea, backwaters—arriving at this, why do you think a sanctuary has been created even for the smallest atoms (meaning 'plankton') within the sea?

[GV1:P2390] Disciple: In order to maintain equilibrium in creation or nature, everything is interconnected and each stands in its respective place.

[GV1:P2391] Guru: Yes. If you agree to that, in this sky—the sky is the part without a veil on earth, isn't it!—how many spheres like the earth are sustained?

[GV1:P2392] Disciple: There are more than we can imagine.

[GV1:P2393] Guru: There are. However, there is a limit to what we can imagine, isn't there? There is also a limit to the actional potential we have imagined, discovered, and utilized from it; how much is that small limit seen to be? What if we conceptualize it to the extent of a sphere called a solar system?

[GV1:P2394] Disciple: Science imagines that as well.

[GV1:P2395] Guru: How many spheres do we conceptualize there are in this solar system?

[GV1:P2396] Disciple: Nine.

[GV1:P2397] Guru: Yes. The Sun, the Moon, and the Earth stand within the enveloping circle of these nine spheres. Should we or should we not see the growth and decline of the Earth, which has reached the act of being cooked on Earth by the dust particles of the moon and the intense power standing as the extraordinary force of the sun?

[GV1:P2398] Disciple: We must see it. But it is not easy to see.

[GV1:P2399] Guru: It is not easy. Can't this be said only by someone who has grown to that level? Don't we need to know how much foresight there is in what he says? It was with this foresight in mind that I mentioned earlier—Jesus sacrificed his very life, unable to break out of the sphere of influence of the Jews or the emperors of that time for about two thousand years. With that, it remained incomplete. Even before that, evidence was given to India. Knowledge was imparted to the most ignorant among us. Shudras! A group of people who were somehow embraced and raised, from the Harijans to the monarchy here that is considered to stand highest in the Shudra class. If it is said that a Pulayan ruled here, wouldn't at least some people find it difficult to accept? But that is the truth. There is a history of Cheraman Perumal ruling here. There is a connection between Cheraman Perumal and Pulayanarkotta. Cheraman Perumal's kingdom extended from the shores of the Bay of Bengal to the shores of the Arabian Sea. A tradition fostered in this manner existed here much earlier.

[GV1:P2400] Disciple: A system where a powerful person rules, regardless of the community.

[GV1:P2401] Guru: When speaking of Ettuveetil Pillamar here, a history was written to glorify and praise them. Was it not, in fact, the plunder of Ettuveetil? Is not the history of Marthanda Varma the history of a boy named Marthandan, who grew up witnessing the administrative plunder by the Ettuveetil Pillamar against the family of Marthanda Varma, who were relegated to a mere half-status? The very place name Maharajamuttam (Marayamuttam) is a part of this history. There is a house there called Puthuveedu. They received a hundred acres. That was a reward for keeping Marthanda Varma hidden. Similarly, a Muslim woman also received it. While being chased, when he reached that place and could not proceed further, he ran back towards the west and saw an Umma (Muslim mother) sitting there. Saying "Umma.. please save me", he fell onto her lap. Then the Umma took a 'Muttakku' (head covering) and covered him. Then she remained silent. The Ettuveetil Pillamar came and asked this Umma: 'Someone ran here, didn't they? Did you see?' The Umma said that he went that way. They ran in that direction, and Marthanda Varma got up from there and ran. The place where he came running to is Puthuveedu. There is a reason why it is called Puthuveedu. Someone who accompanied him said: This is a Thiyya's - Ezhava's - house. What of it, is it not Puthuveedu (a new house)? When he came to power, a hundred acres were given to them without any tax liability.

[GV1:P2402] Such plunder was happening even then. This administrative cycle has proven that when an injustice occurs, another injustice takes over the administration and creates another form of justice; this is a matter concerning the administrative cycle. But the foresight of the world is not like this. Knowledge is given in advance to them (the Acharyas) regarding what this solar system has accomplished here. A visionary is one who sees what is transpiring inside this solar system. The special transformation involving the earth, the sun, and the moon. Are there not places in some corner of the world where there is neither day nor night, but merely blocks of ice? The light of the sun, the earth, and the moon does not reach there. We have not gone and seen these places ourselves. In these places, during certain periods, the ice parts and gives way. When the ice parts, people pass through. The ice then accumulates again, and thus it has remained there without any destruction for billions of years.
