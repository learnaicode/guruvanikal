[GV4:P1074] June 2005

[GV4:P1075] Narayana Guru resigned from the SNDP Yogam. What was the reason? You say that Narayana Guru Swami was the one who created the SNDP Yogam. Chattampi Swami was the one who wrote 'Vedadhikara Nirupanam' (Critique of Vedic Authority). He did not teach us a culture of critique belonging to the authority of the Vedas. Just because we criticized it, does it mean we become that? Sree Sankara has spoken about Advaita. Do we become that? What are you thinking about? If we worship gods and goddesses and die under them, is there any salvation for us? Without explaining all this to us as a change of state, our girls were fascinated by something, fell into it and decayed, brought up in a way that is worthless, tied and unleashed. Would you like it if I say this? Is this only in my house? Is it there in your house too? Just like this kolam thullal (ritualistic dance), bull dance - isn't it called a bull? - it's not a college. You go to college, right? When it goes south, it becomes a 'kaaleju' (bull). You call it college over there. But this is not like that. They go to 'kaaleju' (bull-lege). So they dance like bulls and come here, and their behavior is like this bull itself. This is a kolam thullal of the western way of life. How will you or I change from this? So can you change your children? Can you change, can I change, can anyone in the world change? Why are you keeping quiet? This came like this and became bad, and became a profession and the profession of a profession, and the big rulers took all the income from other places and put it in their country, and imposed all the authority of their country on us and asked us to pay tribute. What is this tribute? Paying tribute is its correct name. That means it is not a tax. They fought and conquered our country. After conquering, we have surrendered to them and are singing praises, imagining that they will look after our country. It is this many lakhs, this many crores. So in this karma that has come and gone like this, aren't we accustomed to all this? So when they went to the position of those imperial rulers, what kind of atrocities did they commit? With which attackers did they get it done? Which emperors have ruled like that and prospered through violence? These are the subsequent governments that come. Today, those who give all the buttermilk and milk, who are they? Let it be anyone. Why are they giving? So as we hear, see and know this, there he, who is called the capitalist, collects tribute like this, educates the children of his country, makes these machines, gives them back to us, takes that money and they even give a grant. Seeing this, how would it be if the capitalist here caught hold of this grant? Does he need anything else to shatter? The workers are poor. They don't know anything. They have a determination that the item they buy here for eight cash or ten rupees is sold there for ten thousand rupees. But when the labor charge for this, the company's work, the company's wage, the loading and unloading charge for this are all done, what will this capitalist and this worker get? Can someone without crores of money give this in this bata? So these workers, without understanding this, sell it there for a hundred and one rupees, the item we give for one rupee. Not only that, our item has another way. When the best item reaches there, they give it at a reduced price to know the value of our country. Whose neck is this on? So isn't it true that we are piling up faults in the karma that we do with full knowledge? This is governance. The rule of violence is like this. And then all these rulers got together and brought a democracy. That is this bank of an international character. Isn't it so? If we take money from this bank, will we become rich again? We will be ruined. We will become debtors. This is our situation today. Like this, most people are those who say this change of state and worship this devil. Then some went calling out to the deity. Some others are those who called out saying everything is fine otherwise. Another person saying nothing is right, all this is a lie. Which is right? If our children see and learn all this, will they ever be saved? If you can say, tell me. As long as this child is not saved, we are in that prison. What did you understand? This child, one became a fool, one the dirt of complete immoralities, another the breeding ground of destruction. All three of these are gone, no salvation. Is there any way for the manes who have passed through the salvation to be saved? You are not thinking at all. That is, if you come running to go, I cannot teach you anything. This is not what you need. How much time have you wasted playing children, husband, wife, lordship, education. How much have you suffered for the good of your life? I am not sending you the way I said earlier. Otherwise, I am not just singing a song to you. You must understand it. Am I singing a song to you and making you happy? It is a hardship, life itself is a hardship. For us who are born and standing only in a course of karma living as male or female, isn't the life of each of us one that is completed by combining the ignorance and knowledge of this female and male? Let this come as a child, an adult, a grandfather or a grandmother. If it comes, isn't it the same? Even if this dies and decays, isn't it the same? I am asking if you understand this. I am not telling you Vedanta. If I have said any Vedanta, you must say it now. Regarding you, this is very far for you. But it is very close. Why? I am showing you one thing. You are getting it unknowingly and knowingly.

[GV4:P1076] 'Jagrat svapna sushuptinam

[GV4:P1077] Sakshipoorthi namonamah'

[GV4:P1078] Seeing all these devils in the waking state, one is seeing the Guru. It is the same in the dream state as well. The same in deep sleep too. So it means the Guru is catching hold of it. Thus, we had led the worst possible life. We are seeing all of it. The worst immoralities have been shown. We are seeing all of it. Returning, we see the Guru again. We are taking up karma from that Guru and moving forward. So this is a secret between the Guru and the disciple. It is the 'secret of secrets'. That is 'Guhyad Guhyataram'. That is what the author of the Gita says. Other than this author of the Gita, no one has stated this as a revelation. This is the truth. Without understanding this truth, you are studying the story of following the poets and completing the sixteen thousand and eight. But you have never understood what it is.

[GV4:P1079] Guruvani
