[GV3:P1133] April 2001

[GV3:P1134] God has a purpose in having created the nature of our ashram at this level. We have a valuable reform. That is the system of worship here. We should understand what the value of our worship is and the reform that has arisen from it. Gurus and lineages have existed before this too. They have all accepted and given the guidance of their predecessors. We do not have such predecessor familiarity, or rather, God has not given it. However, the values of all the predecessor Acharyas have been combined and given in this. This is how it should be studied. We are not just following the path that someone else has taken.

[GV3:P1135] It is said that the messenger of God receives words. That is called a divine voice (Ashariri). They kept it a secret. It came to light through the disciple after the Guru's time. But we implement the words received from the light while the Guru is still alive. This is the difference. We did not create these words and deeds. This is its greatness. In ancient times, we worshipped gods and goddesses. Then we faced obstacles. To overcome those obstacles, God granted us the continuous chanting of the name (Akhandanamajapam) at Santhigiri. This chanting was received at Varkala. It was not done there. It was started only when it was informed again here. That is God's will. That will of God is clearly taking place here. No one else has received such a system of worship except the Manus.

[GV3:P1136] Everything entitled to Manu has been preserved. But ours is a path to correct the mistakes in the Manu lineage. Such a divine resolution comes to those who have the influence for as long as the period of the Manu lineages (the total period of the Manvantaras). The greatness of bringing everything here without getting attached to anything! Such an arrival happened as a result of waiting for ages. If we think about this greatness, it is fathomless.

[GV3:P1137] For you to get this thought, how far should you think! No matter how much you do or how much sincerity you show for this, it is not too much. You must think about how far it can be taken forward with respect and honor, acknowledging its worth. Highly cultured or very wealthy people have not come here. It is not anyone's fault that such culture and wealth were not imparted here. If it had been imparted, we would not be able to understand the ignorance of ours and our ancestors.

[GV3:P1138] There was a time when the Varnasrama Dharma was brought here and ruled. There is no need for Varnasrama Dharma in Satyayuga. It is a period when the entire human race lives as divine beings. When Treta Yuga came, chanting of mantras started due to the desire for a change. Forests and hills were chosen for that. Some experienced light, sound, and mantras from within that forest. None of that is man-made. Everything is God-made. That is, sound and mantra are created by God. Once sound is heard from God, man will not create anything, or rather, he will not be able to create.

[GV3:P1139] At first, there was no language. There were only gestures. The first language also originated from God. With the change of eras, commands from God ceased. Therefore, they sat in the forest and called out in pain. Then it manifested. Sitting in the forest and doing penance brought diseases. When called out in pain, medicines were advised. What was advised in that way became the Veda. For example, Ayurveda - the Veda of life. God taught language through mantras. For instance, all language mantras are interrelated. It is rare to find refined languages that have not crossed the letter 'Ma' in the word for mother.

[GV3:P1140] From darkness came light. Words began to come from it. The world seemed like an illusion. Everything came into being by the mere thought of God. We can get a piece of clothing today if we pay money. How hard the great ones worked to create it! Who knew it was in the cotton plant? Similarly, the origin of fire. The ascetics made gunpowder for good. But we use it for evil. Looking at it from that perspective, we have gone down in every field of civilization. But in some other fields, we have moved forward. Rockets and airplanes were discovered through divine voices. When it was understood that they would eventually be used for destruction, the disciple was advised that this is of no use to us. However, the disciple insisted on making an airplane. He (Bharadwaja) went to Lanka, made the airplane, and brought it to India. With that, that country (Lanka) was gone.

[GV3:P1141] Indians respected the Guru's word. Westerners elevated it. Thus, they were able to lift and take away all the sciences of India. India lost those sciences. Even today, we are on that lost path. India's culture did not emerge today or yesterday. But in matters of culture, we are only able to respect the Westerner today. All rich people follow the English model. Why should we blame the children!

[GV3:P1142] The Varnasrama Dharma was not created by Indians. Foreigners came here, taught the language, and managed it intelligently. Even if they are in the forest, Indians have honesty and culture. Therefore, using the foreigner's intelligence and ability, we were enslaved. Because we lack knowledge, their supremacy came here as something ordained by God. They came here and gave monarchy to the people here.

[GV3:P1143] After the Treta Yuga and Dwapara Yuga passed, there was no place for gods. Once something is complete, what is needed next? It must perish, and something else must come. Before the time of the Prophet, Westerners had more diversity than us. When they abandoned it and embraced oneness, they prospered. Christians and Muslims have the Trinity system. All are good for their respective times. In the circumstances of India, there is no strength to let go of the Trinity system. Isn't it bound by scripture! No one has been able to refute this. To change this, one must work with that much strength. Oneness has been spoken of since Buddha. But the concept of the Trinity is still continuing. Today's modern science is only fit to be thrown in the dustbin after a hundred years. What happened now after saying that anyone can use Penicillin?

[GV3:P1144] There is not even the name of a single person who wrote Shruti and Smriti. Everyone sat and wrote as Narayana! As if it were a matter created by poets. What is the way to escape from this? Narayana Guru himself has said that the use of Narayana Siddhi turned out to be a mistake. By showing Siddhi, everyone was defeated. In the final stage, everyone also agreed. But it could not be communicated to the people. Nothing has happened here except through God. But we have a shortcoming; we cannot accept this unless it is said by Westerners. Since they did not know Vedanta, they went for experimentation and research. Indians believed that light and words would come from God. Aren't both the same?

[GV3:P1145] There is a notion that women should not enter the ashrams of Hindus. Dayananda Saraswati established an ashram where both men and women could join. Kalampaara Sivanandan and Kanhangad Ramananda Swami also worked understanding that the ashram is useful for both men and women. Even after Buddha, Shankaracharya, and Narayana Guru came, we could not remove the Trinity system. An act of worship has to be given and practiced, right? Narayana Guru said that temples are no longer needed; they have served their purpose. Although he said that what is needed now are schools and ashrams, it could not be put into practice.

[GV3:P1146] I have not looked at any poetic creations imagined by people. If I had taken and looked at Manusmriti, it would have been a total confusion. We were not given knowledge so that we wouldn't be confined within this fence. That is why we got a new path. Everything is able to happen according to God's will. Nothing that is in Santhigiri today is mine. It is not yours either. You must make it your own. This field belongs to God. God has given us the fortune to do this. Your actions will be of use only if you manage to impart it to at least ten children among us.

[GV3:P1147] Guruvani
