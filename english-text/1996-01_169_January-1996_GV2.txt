Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0971 to GV2:P0981
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

January 1996

Today everyone is getting the karma to be free. It is the special rule of the time that has come for five thousand years. Anyone can do anything from carpentry to Vedic karma. The government itself is making it happen. Everyone can get an education. Because we did not have an education, we had the ignorance of calling upon God by talking about caste and religion. The serpent worship that originated in ancient times, the kalari that originated in various ways after that, deity worship, the rituals and karma to gain strength and ferocity in various forms of worship, and other special rules - how will our children understand this tradition that we have gained? What do the father and mother teach about this? If we can select and study the things taught by the father, mother, and others, or the right and wrong that have come in the Vedic system, can we achieve anything? Since we are affected by all kinds of shortcomings in this way, it will take a lot of time to fulfill the Yuga Dharma. Moreover, once a Yuga Dharma begins, it will change into another.

It is seen from the calculation that Kali is the shortest period. Kali has four lakh thirty-two thousand years. Can we call upon God after this long period is over? The word is that Kali will not be accepted. How did Kali become unacceptable? No one should assume that Kali will not be accepted. Kali is good for everyone. Our ancestors have taught us till today that Kali is destruction. It is because of the evil of Kali that children behave incorrectly. Thus, completing the good and bad of Kali is what has been known till today. How can we be complete in this knowledge? How can we know and act upon at least the Dharma we have? This will become a karma passed on by the ancestors, which will never be suitable.

God has given birth to many great people here and there. You should respect the people God sends to fulfill the karma mentioned earlier when they come before you. But you have not been able to do it so far. You did not respect Krishna, who came as a born wise man. You covered it with a story, turned it into the most corrupted story; turned it into a story and a play, and it is shown as Kathakali or by imparting all the madness in a drama. And how much can we gain in the karma of showing and informing that this is the special intellect of the time? When we look at each of these like this, we have not understood at all that God has been working for us for so long. Then you can ask, it is said that God is everything, then why not just die a single death. God will not die like that. You have heard a calculation for a long time - Kalpa. If you look at it with that calculation itself, it is talking about the karma that has come for so many Chaturyugas and the Kalpa that has been acting and not acting for so long, or the karma that has come as Manvantaras. Can we just go straight after studying all this? No. Then what should be done? If a prophet or an Acharya or any such Mahatma comes according to the times, how will we know about this person? This is what is called everyone's lack of knowledge.

In the past history, Kali Yuga is the shortest. How can we, who cannot even take and see the past three periods, know that long history about this Kali Yuga? How can historians who have no clear understanding of anything, who have taken something said by someone and written something, create a clear calculation and tell the history of a long entity? No matter how much it is extended

In short, all they can say, and that too unclearly, is just the story of the last ten thousand years.

Even the great souls who came after Sri Krishna were born in very low categories to try and reform us. But we didn't understand it. We each fell and rotted in a course of karma where we couldn't respect anything, draining the good essence and becoming like gathered trash. In that state, we have become bad.

How can this situation be corrected? A great soul himself must tell you that. No one else can do it. Only the individual who has earned the right over the ages can correct you. How will you recognize the person who comes to correct you? How will you know that it is the truth? The karma you heard, saw, knew, and worshipped has reached the most corrupt state, turning into numerous seeds of evil that we ourselves created to prevent our own betterment, and we are each the remnants of that. How can we become better? Through what will we improve? We don't even know this karma. Then how can our father and mother explain it to us?

Look at a recent picture, of a Pandya king. The description of this Pandya's wife is like this: This Pandya's alliance is from Kanchipuram. In Kanchipuram, there are massive carvings and similar grand things. In Sanskrit, it is said, 'Nagarishu Kanchi' (Among cities, Kanchi). Thus, having studied about Kanchipuram, the 'Nagarishu Kanchi', we keep praising it. Whatever it may be, regarding the educational wealth of this city, we can agree that it is the greatest thing in our country. It is said that the Pandya who married and brought this woman built various kinds of temples. When it came to the karma of what is called her favorite deity, the Pandya built the Madurai Meenakshi Temple. When the temple was properly finished, the king asked this woman: Now, who is greater, your father or me? The woman said, this doesn't have an inch of the greatness of what my father built. It is said that the woman's head was cut off. The beheaded woman and him have been sculpted and placed there.

How do we know history from this? Because we have been rolled through such untrue history and fabricated stories, we have no information about anything. With that, a narrative of this kind emerged. The first thing that happened was that if one needed to cut, dig, and eat, iron tools had to be invented first. We know how long it has been since this iron thing was invented. But cutting and digging were done on this earth long before that. How was that done? They have deceived us by saying this. All these stories we see today did not happen in one or two Chaturyugas; they have sprouted and flourished over thousands of Chaturyugas. Occasionally getting destroyed, hidden, overflowing again, and flooded with water, it has evolved to this state. Thus, changes will come according to the times.

But this is not what we need to understand regarding ourselves. We must absolutely find a special dharma to act for their good and our good, after knowing how our ancestors lived and the sad stories of their lives. Because we don't have this special dharma, we don't know about karma. Therefore, we internalize all the madness shown by these caste fanatics, and in that internal struggle, life becomes a competing exhaustion. To give strength to this, some bad things are fabricated into stories in the name of God.

Guruvani
