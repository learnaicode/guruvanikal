[GV1:P1591] May 1986

[GV1:P1592] Among those existing within the solar and interstellar division—ranging from demons to Yakshas, Gandharvas, Kinnaras—up to the dwellers of the realm of gods—down to human beings who, according to Hindu tenets, come from the ancient lineage of Manu—as these beings who stand as masters of karma, with or without faith, are born, the truth of life, which is referred to as family dosha or karma dosha acquired through the tendencies belonging to their respective religions or rituals, and the good and bad karma involving discord and unrighteous living within it, cannot be truly valued and understood by everyone in relation to their own lives. If one carefully observes these learned men who are known as the greatest intellectuals, scholars, or logicians, they can be seen at a dead end, enduring the misfortune of being unable to string together their own lives multiple times and preserve it as a model. The offspring involved in this and a few people who accept them have prospered through karma as inhabitants of the earth. The secret of life of such prosperity is experienced in such a way that not even a single individual who led this aforementioned life could make his followers accept it, or find satisfaction in his own life through a belief, and this is also evident in the soul that remains as his subtle body. Look entirely at the lives of great men who desired sacrifice through life, prosperity through karma, and sustenance through dharma. Recognize these as the most crucial chapters in human life and act accordingly. It can be seen that some rare individualities among the Jivatmas involved in this, who act as mediums and are known as dwellers of heaven, whose good and bad karmas can be understood and acted upon, lived in this physical world itself, demonstrated a life for the benefit of others, and then departed from this earthly existence.

[GV1:P1593] Guruvani
