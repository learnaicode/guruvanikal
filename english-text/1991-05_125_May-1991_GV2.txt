Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0060 to GV2:P0071
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

May 1991

Like everyone else, I too was someone who believed in Hinduism. It was like that until the age of fourteen. It was only later that I understood that the goal of faith can only be achieved through a Guru's path. What kind of Guru's path? The Sri Ramakrishna Mission seemed a little better. Even in that, there is no one with realization. Apart from one person having an experience, no one else has it. After that, I sought the lineage of Sree Narayana Guru Swami. I spent about seventeen years in Sree Narayana movements. But even there, I could not find anyone with personal experience. Jagadeeswarananda Swami was the person who lived in the room next to mine. He is the one who taught the Brahmasutra to Agamananda. When I told him about my experience, he said this: 'Child, you need to see an experienced person for this. I have studied some Tantra and Vedanta. I have passed Shastri from Kerala. I have studied and taught at Banaras University for about twenty-one years. In this life of mine, I have been able to meet only one person with experience. When I asked him, he avoided me saying, child, even though you are a good soul, this is not enough for spiritual realization.' This is what Jagadeeswarananda Swami said. Then my thought became, where can I find such a person.

Finally, I found a Pathani Swami. He was an experienced person. Even though he was a Pathani, he was not someone who denied anything. No matter what faith or what kind of person approached him, he was someone who would look at everything and say what kind of person they are. What did you understand by saying a person who looks at everything and speaks? He is a Pathani. A person belonging to the religion of Islam. He would call upon Islam and ask - what is that. Asking Islam means it is a realm belonging to his religion. But he would similarly call upon and ask a ghost, a deity, a sage, and a wise man belonging to Hinduism, and explain it to us.

This is the path here. I will tell you what happened after I had the experience, from the time I started following this path seen here today. It has been twenty-nine years since I started following this. During this time, both men and women here have had experiences. But none of them came with the knowledge and desire to attain self-realization, use it for themselves until the end, and utilize it for the land, the people, and the world. I am talking about the realm of experience. They will talk about family matters and local matters. They should talk. There is an obligation to talk, isn't there? So all that can be said. But

It was necessary to evaluate the experience here conceptually, in a meaningful way, before saying other things.

This is an idea taught by God. When saying idea, it is an idea about gods and goddesses, prophets, and gurus. It does not contain the idea of gods and goddesses. Beyond all that, the idea here is about the guidance of the lineage of sages. I don't know what kind of concept you have when you say sage. If it is a sage who worships gods and goddesses and stands under them, such sages will not get this right. That is, those who have not made the experiences of how many people have been born and died their own, do not have the right to this. Something like that is said about Krishna -- 'Yatrayogeshwarakrishna'. It is this superhuman who was born, nurtured, and maintained dharma through his actions who, at the beginning of Kali Yuga, pointed out the flaws in the actions of the Shudra and guided that this is not it, this is not how it should go. Take refuge in me alone - 'Mamekam sharanam vraja.' Those flaws will disappear.

After that, Parayi petta panthirukulam, eighteen siddhas and many others came. In Kerala itself, three gurus - Shankaracharya, Sree Narayana Guru, Chattampi Swami - came. We assume that their spiritual energy still exists. But we did not see their active life. Because they were sannyasis, we kept them at a distance. Having kept them at a distance, we take pride that they are great. But we did not get close to them. Then what is our relationship with them?

Those who worship Narayana Guru Swami should not ask or state their caste. But do they sit without asking or stating caste? Then one of his words -- do not tap, do not drink, do not drink -- they follow it with dignity! Another thing is that S. N. D. P Yogam was created by Narayana Guru himself. He included people of all castes in what he created. But when he saw that they made it the property of caste, he resigned from it in mind, word, and deed.

Because it was written like that, there was a case with the Dharma Sangham for ten years after the Swami's samadhi. Later, a verdict came in favor of the Dharma Sangham. It was said that they are the ones who behave without caste. Whatever it may be, we are not able to bear this, or love it, or achieve it through action, or understand the severity of sins and virtues. Society is not ready to accept it with its dignity.

Now the things here - most people come here for healing and similar things. Ninety-nine percent of the diseases are cured, some are not. It will be said in the beginning that the rest cannot be done. Even so, people will try to get it done. But when they think they can get it done, they have a lack of mental firmness or something like not being able to do it together at home comes up and it will not be possible. But for the majority of people, it is not like that. They work and get it done as if they have the karma to do it with interest until the end. You should understand this and each of you should come here and find out as much as the experience here. If you cannot find that what is here is guidance, there is no point in you coming here. If you do not find it like that, you will only get the result of going to temples and making offerings.

This is a place of experience. Experience is to see and know in the state that the sages have called Jagrat, Swapna, Sushupti, Turiya, and Turiyatita. As said earlier, calling upon the demons, gods and goddesses, acharyas, and other great souls of all religions

The person who was my guru was one who had attained the authority to stop you and say, "You are this, you have this flaw, you must do this." I am following the method taught by that person. What is here is not a path I created by studying. It cannot be said that this method exists in many places. The blessing of the Goddess, the blessing of the God, the blessing of the Supreme Power (Parashakthi) - thus everywhere there is nothing else to see but blessings and curses. And then they pronounce it adding 'Om'. What do they pronounce? 'Om Kali'. Or whatever it may be, adding Om to everything and pronouncing it is nothing but a Hindu fabrication. But you should not assume that because of all this, it has become a Veda, a Shastra, or an Upanishadic saying. You do not need such notions. What you need is the karma (action) that must be done properly.

Who should a householder (Grihasthashrami) be? A householder must be one who knows well what he has to do, what his capacity for action should be, and performs it with constant vigilance in actions that are useful to you, your children, the land, the people, and even the world. This capacity for action is left in you, not in me. Only if you have that understanding can you believe and accept this idea. This idea is not mine. It is enough to see that I am merely an instrument. But you must know how this idea was received. This idea has been received from crores of people like gods, sages, devotees, ascetics, and wise men. In this Kali Yuga, an attempt was made to give this idea to two thousand four hundred and forty-four gurus. But they themselves came, stood, and said, "This idea is such, we did not get it, Appa! Children, we could not take this up." These subtle beings said this with their hands on their chests, crying. They do not belong to one religion or one caste. These are the words of great souls born all over the world in all religions and all castes -- Children, do not lose this, this is for the children of the world. So its importance is this: this idea meant for the children of the world must be implemented through you. That is why you come here. You must show the world a discipline of customs, a life, a karma - grow it, and show through your life how to respect the greatness of life. A responsibility has been entrusted to you to reveal what karma is and how dharma should be maintained. Without having the capacity for this, you should not be people who just look at the greatness of the place you believe in, getting bewildered seeing some blessing and curse or some test. First, you should have this knowledge, pass it on to your children, and then to the children of the world. Pray to God to have this ability.

Guruvani
