Guruvani November 1993

Today, ages have changed. Inner light, outer light, Atmajyothi Paratpara, Jyothirjyothi, Swayamjyothi. Atmajyothi itself is of many kinds. Each person carried each of these through each birth. Those who carried it like that have taken birth as Gurus. A Madan, Yakshi, or Rishi can become a Guru. There are those who are born as Gurus to Gurus. Those who come as Gurus like this will lead the dimensions of vision from the waking state, dream, deep sleep, Turiya, and Turiyatita and lift them up to the presence of God. The position of Devendra and Ganapathi's

An individual soul can be guided beyond its status, even up to the state of showing creation, preservation, and destruction.

Therefore, how careful should you be? Those who wish to become a Brahmachari or Sanyasi have the opportunity here to seek and attain what they need. No matter how many times and where you fail, you must seek again and again. Those who come and stand here must have such intense determination and dispassion. They must know every small and big thing that happens here on a daily basis. A Brahmachari must be at the forefront of everything. What is the characteristic of a renunciate? No matter what he has to endure, he will always be eager to instill goodness in the hearts of others.

You know what today's rulers are thinking: how to copy the justice and laws of foreigners. They are the ones who seek ways to bring science forward based on small experiences and thus destroy their own culture. Today, it is possible to take photos of subtle forms in the atmosphere. Even if that is possible, it is only a physical manifestation. However, spirituality is misunderstood today. Although Vedanta expresses the idea that by talking and writing extensively, one will eventually reach the truth, it does not seem that anyone has reached the real truth to date. Truth is merely the light of the soul that penetrates and stirs the course of karma, which is subtle in its inner nature. We have that method here. This is also a method that gives importance to those who have Brahmanhood. Our understanding is that there is sinful power even in the practice of the Vedas and Shastras. The basis of this sinful power is a competitive spirit between the Dravidian and the Aryan. Even today, there is a feeling that being an Aryan means being great. But we must remember one thing. The Dravidian lived with truth at the forefront.

The Aryan, on the other hand, came from cold countries. Seeing the forests, hills, and plains here, he stayed. He took control of the education system and the administrative system through intellect. The circumstances here were favorable to him. Imparting knowledge, culture, and love, he studied the original texts and understood even creation, preservation, and destruction. He even wrote the Amarakosha and made everything his own. Brahmanhood became his monopoly. But the Dravidian always lived only with truth at the forefront. He always lived by worshiping in the forest. Even today, there are Malavedans and Mannans who live like that. The forest will act as they say. It cannot be said that Dravidians are not great people.

When a new culture was imparted, truth was abandoned for deception. There was a time when even an elephant or a tiger would move away on its own. The deity who was grasped and manifested in the Treta and Dvapara Yugas is still worshiped today in the mental culture of that forest tradition.

Times have changed today. Everything must change with the times. Westerners have had a history since the time of Abraham. Similarly, many Acharyas came here, reformed many things, and created history. No one respected it here. However, even today, even wild animals respect the words of the Malavedans. What is the reason for this? Even though they are forest tribes, their minds are not distorted. Their women are truthful. The Aryans, on the other hand, studied our culture, made calculations for themselves, and lived as great people. But through the changes of time, they failed to maintain the truth. They wrote many praises. They built a fortress so that the Dravidians could not even peek in. But in Kali, they failed to repeat and evolve. They only wished to live as pleasure-seekers forever.

Even today, there are women among them who walk around holding palm-leaf umbrellas. There is an aura of purity in all their ways. Even today, their only desire is that ours should spread. If you go to North India, you can understand this very clearly. Some among them tried to change this. But it didn't happen. Those who tried could only speak for a transformation from the old. They couldn't give anything new.

The downtrodden seen today were once high-ranking. But they were not allowed to become high-ranking traditionally. If you go to North Malabar, such people have more beauty. They also have a good culture. Their women are as honest as the Vedic authorities. A culture existed there that Arabs and the English could not penetrate. But it became very old.

Although many Acharyas have been brought in this Kali Yuga, they also became exhausted believing that if they win in tricks and logic by adding some old things, they can ascend the Sarvajna Peetham. Therefore, they had to return to the old itself; just like taking the medicine from here, changing its appearance, and bringing it back here.

Dayananda Saraswati has revealed Manu's calculations in 'Veda Paryatanam'. Even those who have studied this will only say the old things. Because if they say that, their position will decrease. Nabi and Jesus were able to establish some boundaries and set up some children. That is why they were able to bring about a global transformation. Although Buddha came here, is there anything left of it today? Hinduism does not have a single person to say that.

Who has newly transformed Hinduism? Although Shankaracharya, Narayana Guru, and Madhvan did some things, a mistake was made by adopting the Trimurti system. We see the history of Vivekananda and Ramatirtha going to foreign countries. But apart from showing the old things themselves, nothing else is seen in it. This now stands in our contemporary history. However, in the field called the Trimurti system, there is a historical truth written back as the son of Brahma until the birth of Manu. The calculation of this Manvantara and Kalpa is said in different ways in different places. Because all the above-mentioned people performed some Siddhi or sleight of hand or Parakaya Pravesha, or completed the action that makes Hatha Yoga naked by calling it Raja Yoga, Hinduism has made many such mistakes and still lives in that old culture.

How can this be changed? We are experiencing all the guilt of what ruled us and made us rule today. Only an Acharya can change this. This can only be changed through time and humans growing with time. But you don't have the mental strength to instill this truth in you!

The humble nature of the old should be taken and absorbed and made into a tool to bring it into action. Mind, word, and deed must become one. Along with that, the emotional distortions that get mixed up should be removed. It is difficult to let go of old habits. It can only be changed over time by establishing something new. Determination is absolutely necessary for that. What helps to maintain determination should be brought in as strength and what does not should be discarded. It is difficult to bring it without letting go of the habit. Human effort is needed to change the path of a condition. And God's grace according to its attainment. One must think that one is caught in Yoga. Whatever shortcomings (vasanas) we have must be overcome through a familiar Guru. One should not have any doubts about that Guru.

Now, how can a true Guru be found? What comes in the waking, dreaming, and deep sleep states (vision) can only be correctly perceived without the intellect. In this world, truth alone does not exist. It means that what is not true will also come in waking, dreaming, and deep sleep states. Discerning what is right is possible only through consciousness. Consciousness contains the Guru. One must surrender to that Guru who encompasses consciousness. Visions will come to ordinary people as well. A vision is a mixture of the past, present, and future. Who we were in Treta and Dwapara yugas, what the present should be made into, what we should become in the future, and how to discard the past - visions are given to understand all this. Like a local drama, both good and bad will appear in the vision. There must be a place to ask and know about it. Only what is received verbally from the Guru should be accepted. You must discard the implied meanings that suit your intellect. Once the latent tendencies of past lives, which exist in a subtle form, are shown, they must be removed. It should be known not through the intellect, but through consciousness. If you are convinced that what connects you is Yoga, you should develop a sense of dedication by clearing it through consciousness without looking at worldly knowledge. What if consciousness is tied to the intellect? The intellect will spread and give importance to knowing through worldly knowledge. That is, if a vision is thought of through the intellect, mistakes will happen. If it grows in the intellect, it will not become clear. Even husbands and wives should not discuss visions among themselves. Because they do not have such knowledge. It should only be communicated to the Guru. The Guru alone is the authority to listen to, correct, and elevate the vision.

Guruvani
