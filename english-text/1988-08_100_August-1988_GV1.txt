[GV1:P1966] August 1988

[GV1:P1967] Disciple: What can we, who have no knowledge about such a profound subject,

[GV1:P1968] understand? How can we understand?

[GV1:P1969] Guru: The fact that such a matter exists in the world is known at least to a few among the Hindus.

[GV1:P1970] It has been spoken about a little at a time, on multiple occasions, by various people.

[GV1:P1971] Disciple: Who are they?

[GV1:P1972] Guru: Sri Krishna is the one who comes foremost.

[GV1:P1973] Disciple: What the Guru just said is not entirely clear. It would be helpful if you could

[GV1:P1974] elaborate on it.

[GV1:P1975] Guru: The first evidence is indeed that even before Sri Krishna's birth, Kamsa received

[GV1:P1976] a notification in accordance with the divine will. Along with that, Kamsa felt inclined to deny it because the royal authority of the *Shudra* class lacked humility. Wherever the *Shudra* class has attained royal power, it has been like that.

[GV1:P1977] Disciple: Knowing that he would lose his life upon hearing the ethereal voice, wasn't Kamsa's action a primitive aggression for survival,

[GV1:P1978] rather than a mere denial?

[GV1:P1979] Guru: Once it is seen as divine will, how can it be an aggression for survival?

[GV1:P1980] What Kamsa displayed was something inherent in the oppressive nature of royal authority. The arrogance of not knowing what constitutes the gentle or the virtuous. That is the ignorance of the *Shudra*. Later, Buddhism emerged. It was the *Shudra* class within it who completely denied it with the help of the Brahmins.

[GV1:P1981] During Sri Krishna's time, the state administration was in the hands of the *Shudra*, wasn't it? Even within Sri Krishna's Yadava clan, the lack of humility and wisdom to respect the gentle and the virtuous later surfaced. Isn't making Samba dress as a woman and incurring the sage's curse evidence of this? With that, didn't the Yadava clan fight among themselves and perish?

[GV1:P1982] Wherever you go, you can see that the *Shudra* remains the same even today. Even though Sri Krishna came as such a great personality in the past, and granted that the Brahmins or all these *varnas* glorify Sri Krishna—don't we have good evidence that they still could not truly respect him?

[GV1:P1983] Disciple: Could you elaborate a bit more on the evidence that they did not respect

[GV1:P1984] him?

[GV1:P1985] Guru: Without accepting Sri Krishna's words that those who worship the ancestors go to the ancestral realm, those who worship the deities

[GV1:P1986] go to the realm of deities, and those who worship me will attain me, the Vedic religion was re-established in Sri Krishna temples. Imposing a Vaishnava nature on Sri Krishna, the Vedic religion, which did not hesitate to draw a sacred thread around Sri Krishna's neck, performed all sorts of mockeries here! Can't you guess? As for the clan in which Sri Krishna was born, even though Sri Krishna became a king, they still remain in the status of Harijans today. They have not been able to respect that clan either. In South India, so many people belonging to the Harijan or *Shudra* class held administrative power. Cheraman Perumal was one such person. Pulayanarkotta stands as evidence for that.

[GV1:P1987] I have heard that a fisherwoman whose illness Sri Ramakrishna cured was a woman of royal stature. This woman was a devotee of Kodungallur. Unable to enter temples in Bengal, she came here to Kodungallur. Here, the idol could be seen up close. One day, the Goddess showed her in a dream: "Why do you come here; can't you place me there?" Thus, she immediately had a form of Bhadra sculpted. After it was sculpted, she realized that a Brahmin had to consecrate it, not her. So, she kept the sculpted form safely in a box. She had another dream, instructing her to have Sri Ramakrishna Deva perform the consecration.

[GV1:P1988] This woman fell ill. Her husband was a Brahmin. If a royal woman dies, a Brahmin cannot remain there. Hence, he met Sri Ramakrishna and expressed his sorrow. Within a few days, she recovered. She had vomiting and diarrhea. The Swami caught it. He was bedridden for three months. When such great historical truths exist, why are you asking all this? There would be so many similar things to say.

[GV1:P1989] Disciple: Are you saying it is because of an inability to respect Buddha

[GV1:P1990] ?

[GV1:P1991] Guru: It is not about the inability to respect Buddha. Because he was a king,

[GV1:P1992] everyone accepted him. For instance, Emperor Ashoka allowed his own children to work for the cause. With that, it became the norm that celibates (*Brahmacharis*) could operate anywhere. Buddhism spread in India and abroad. For about eight hundred years, Buddhism remained in power. The followers of the Vedic religion waited. For convenience, some *Shudra Brahmacharis* in Buddhism abandoned their celibacy. Consequently, not only did the people turn against them, but the Kshatriyas and the Brahmins who aligned with them collaborated to use the *Shudra* class itself to beat and drive away Buddhism.

[GV1:P1993] Once the Buddhists retreated, bad rumors spread about them. Many Buddhists were indeed killed. Moreover, in their places of worship, instead of the eulogies of Buddha, they consecrated Bhadrakali and created rituals like *Vela*, *Padayani*, and *Poora-idi*. Don't you know about the offering called *Poora-idi*, where young maidens carry *Thalappoli* around the temple, and they set up mortars and pestles, pound turmeric and charcoal together, and scatter everything around amidst frantic dancing, drumbeats (*chenda*), and a demonic atmosphere? All of these were implemented here by the rulers who came after Shankaracharya.

[GV1:P1994] Disciple: Before Shankaracharya?

[GV1:P1995] Guru: I mentioned earlier that Buddhism existed here for eight hundred years.

[GV1:P1996] Remains of Buddhist *viharas* glorifying the attributes of Buddha have been excavated from many places in Kerala itself. When faced with opposition, those among the followers of Buddha who had wealth, capacity, and resources fled to countries like Ceylon and China.

[GV1:P1997] Then some physicians remained here. The reason is that a physician cannot be created overnight, right? There were also a few administrative personnel. The Ezhavas or Chekavars were the military commanders here, the administrators of that time. Later, untouchability and caste discrimination were introduced and implemented here in order to destroy them.

[GV1:P1998] Disciple: It is said that Shankaracharya created the caste system, right?

[GV1:P1999] Guru: Shankaracharya had to collude in this and do many things.

[GV1:P2000] It was done to prevent the remaining people from being beaten to death. Advaita was introduced with the concept that all should live as the children of one father. However, it was not implemented because it was impossible to step down into the impurity and work. The followers have written extensively to make it appear that the *Varna Dharmas* were prescribed by Sankaracharya. Adi Shankara did not do that.

[GV1:P2001] Disciple: Can it be said that all this was ordained by God?

[GV1:P2002] Guru: No, it was not ordained by God. When there was a lack of people with administrative or martial capabilities, they created untouchability by making the Shudra class fight among themselves.

[GV1:P2003] Disciple: In places like Kannur, Thalassery, and Kozhikode, the Brahmins who embraced Buddhism

[GV1:P2004] were ostracized and degraded. The degraded Brahmins are the Thiyyas. This is what Kerala history states. Is this what the Guru is also saying?

[GV1:P2005] Guru: Exactly that. Later, when the Malabar rebellion occurred, the thugs prepared to harass the Thiyyas. Again, a number of people belonging to their community fled to Coorg. Many men were hacked to death. It was mostly the women who fled.

[GV1:P2006] Thus, the Ezhavas were degraded in every way and transformed into an untouchable caste. Some were included in the Harijan category. The rest became the poor, the farmers, and the physician families. Out of necessity. Those who faced such severe untouchability, those without formal knowledge, became physicians. Sanskrit became their own—how did the Sanskrit of the Brahmins reach those who were considered unapproachable and untouchable? Reading history reveals that physicians emerged among the Nairs only within the last three hundred years. The Nair community itself did not exist. The scholars later wrote and created sixty-four sub-sects within the Nairs themselves. Aren't Valappat Maman Vaidyar and Kakkanat Vaidyar, who came from the Ezhava tradition, renowned? How did this happen?

[GV1:P2007] Disciple: In the olden days, the appellation Harijan did not exist, did it, Guru?

[GV1:P2008] Wasn't it Gandhi who popularized such an appellation?

[GV1:P2009] Guru: Harijan means belonging to Vishnu or belonging to God, doesn't it?

[GV1:P2010] In that sense, weren't those in Krishna's lineage at that time the people of Hari themselves? Weren't the people of Hari left unrespected even then?

[GV1:P2011] Disciple: Wasn't it in the name of Rama that Gandhi surrendered himself and performed his actions?

[GV1:P2012] Guru: Gandhi held firmly to the name of Rama and the concept of Ramarajya.

[GV1:P2013] That was a mistake made by Gandhi. Are not Rama and Ramarajya, which became obsolete due to the Yugadharma (the duty of the age), belonging to the Treta Yuga? As a result of going after worship, Gandhi's mission remained unfulfilled. His organization did not listen to his words. Despite being such a sacrificial figure, not only did India fail to respect or obey that man's words, but he was also shot dead. And that too was done by a Brahmin. If Mahatmaji had been a Brahmin, perhaps he would not have been shot. Is not this much evidence enough to completely expose the statesmanship here?

[GV1:P2014] Disciple: For a moral culture to emerge in a manner suited to the times,

[GV1:P2015] what should be done?

[GV1:P2016] Guru: What must be done has come through Sri Krishna and Buddha.

[GV1:P2017] It has also come through the Parayi petta panthirukulam (the twelve clans born to the Pariah woman). The eighteen Siddhars belonged to all clans, starting from Brahmin, Kshatriya, and Vaishya. Was it not Thiruvalluvar, who said 'Ondre Kulam Oruvane Devan' (One caste, one clan), who belonged to them? The *Thirukkural* of such a Thiruvalluvar stands very strongly as the core of Vedanta. It is also a part of knowledge. With the scholars' method of closing their eyes and creating darkness, isn't all this equivalent to having gone into the dustbin?

[GV1:P2018] In libraries in America and Europe, the Gita, the Bible, and the Quran may hold the first position. Does that mean that Christians in Britain and America as a whole will accept the Quran or the Hindus? Will they obey?

[GV1:P2019] Disciple: Are you suggesting that the public generally enjoys the Gita and the Thirukkural

[GV1:P2020] merely as literary forms?

[GV1:P2021] Guru: What is the doubt in that?

[GV1:P2022] Disciple: Can you suggest a path that can be

[GV1:P2023] followed by humanity as a whole?

[GV1:P2024] Guru: Are not the things that existed in the past

[GV1:P2025] applicable to humanity?

[GV1:P2026] Disciple: Distinct from them, do you have anything to suggest that does not lead to religious hatred

[GV1:P2027] and makes human cooperation more evident?

[GV1:P2028] Guru: In principle, there is something to suggest. If we do not discard it

[GV1:P2029] as we did in the past, an authoritative step towards permanent existence for the entire world is being implemented through our current ideology. Our beginning is from where Narayana Guru, Buddha, and Krishna left off.

[GV1:P2030] Disciple: How is that? I do not understand.

[GV1:P2031] Guru: The Supreme Soul Sri Krishna came and demonstrated something through His actions

[GV1:P2032] before leaving. He demonstrated the matters of the Devas (gods) and the Pitrus (ancestors) and assumed agency for it Himself. That is why it was said, 'Mamekam sharanam vraja' (Surrender to Me alone). He removed the scholars and court poets of that time, as well as Bhishma—who was an expert in archery and attained the bed of arrows—and Drona from this world, and re-established the government itself. He Himself proved that sages and the enlightened are its guides.

[GV1:P2033] The enlightened ones do the very same subject that the sages perfect. They implement it in its entirety. That is why Vyasa, in the language of realization, said, 'Yatra Yogeshwarah Krishnah' (Wherever is Krishna, the Lord of Yoga). The perfect evidence for this is that Krishna took birth as an enlightened being Himself. Another proof is crossing all the states of the Devas, attaining the presence of Ishwara, and stepping into the presence of Brahman. That is the eighth state.

[GV1:P2034] Having understood all this and established the administration, Krishna tells Arjuna that, since the Yadavas themselves ignored the sages, Dwarka itself would sink due to that sinful power. Not only could they not be saved, but they were also going into renunciation. It proves that the Dharma of Kali had begun there. The truth is that, because the birth happened in the Dvapara Yuga, despite doing everything, it could not be completed.

[GV1:P2035] Respecting this, Buddha, who studied all texts, stopped everything else and tried to awaken people by making them chant only 'Buddham Sharanam Gachchami'. It must be an enforcement of the very same thing that the great Thiruvalluvar stated. Sankaracharya, who was elevated in Satva, realized that his caste would not change merely through the study of Puranas and epics. There is a story told about this. It is said that Parvati, in the guise of a Pariah girl, lay on the road wailing, and when he shouted 'Hoy Hoy' to avoid untouchability, the poor woman lamented, 'Son, if you, who are pious and wise, do this, will others touch me? Therefore, lift me from the path and lay me aside.' And accordingly, he moved her. When the frail, bone-like woman felt very heavy, he thought about the reason right there. The story goes that when he recognized her as Parvati, he saw that beauty and wrote the *Soundarya Lahari*. That must be regarding spiritual enlightenment, which is why it is written that when he shouted 'Hoy Hoy', she retorted asking whether it was the body or the soul that should move away. Writers might have fabricated him as an Advaitin. In spirituality, there is only one argument. It might have been done for the survival of the Vaidikas, to satisfy through court poetry, and to maintain the hegemony of the Brahmins. In doing so, the truth might have been forgotten. Human, after all! Mistakes can happen. Intelligent people should understand that.

[GV1:P2036] *Janmana jayate shudrah* (By birth, everyone is a Shudra)

[GV1:P2037] *Karmana jayate dvijah* (By action, one becomes a twice-born)

[GV1:P2038] Is it not shown by all of them through this Gita verse that the time is what the Shudra is entitled to and must achieve through Karma? Has it not been proven that the Shudra lost the ability to understand this? This is exactly what Narayana Guru also did. In order to help the Shudras recognize what Vedic practices and idol installations (Prathishta) are, he gave all of that to them, and then said that none of it is needed anymore and what we need are educational centers. The Murukkumpuzha temple with "Truth, Dharma, Compassion, Empathy" written on it and the Kalavamkodam temple where a mirror was installed, have enlightened us. Despite developing the S.N.D.P. for social growth from the age of thirty to seventy, and abolishing the worship of ghosts and evil spirits, puja rituals, and even the *Kettukalyanam*, they could not understand it because of the severe Shudra nature inherent in them. That is why the Swami had to establish the Dharmasangham. Thinking of the Shudras who had worked until then, he had to regret, "Oh! These poor people will not get this." Even amidst physical ailments, the pain that stood before him was that he could not do anything. When there was no improvement even after the combined treatment of Maman Vaidyar, Olassa Mooss, and Panavally Krishnan Vaidyar, Dr. Samuel was brought in, implying that this treatment was not enough. He evaluated the treatment up to that point and said, "We cannot provide any further treatment beyond this; let us pray for him for five minutes," and then knelt down and prayed with everyone.

[GV1:P2039] When he cried during his Samadhi saying, "I don't have a child," the ignorant devotees of the Swami at that time thought he was grieving over not having a biological child. Thus, the karmic dosha of acting against what the Swami worked for still persists. An explanation of this has been provided in the Santhigiri magazine under the title "The Mistake of Not Knowing What a Guru Is."

[GV1:P2041] that is of such magnitude?

[GV1:P2043] correct it, and put it into practice. This era is suitable for it. What is happening here today can be seen in all the nations of the world. And what happens there can be seen here. Media has expanded that much.

[GV1:P2046] to work for the full period of a Yugadharma has been bestowed upon us as Brahman.

[GV1:P2049] in particular, served for seventeen years not just at the headquarters of Narayana Guru but at its branches:

[GV1:P2050] Disciple: Could this idea not be introduced and developed through the ideology of

[GV1:P2051] Narayana Guru?

[GV1:P2053] Disciple: Why?

[GV1:P2055] but he also established puja rituals and composed hymns and praises. Due to that tradition, the Vedic religion in Kerala has reached a stage where it exists only among the Ezhavas, next to the Brahmins.

[GV1:P2056] Disciple: Cannot the Nairs or other communities belonging to the Shudra varna

[GV1:P2057] adopt this?

[GV1:P2059] —

[GV1:P2060] Disciple: What do you mean by saying that it fell into the hands of the Nairs in the name of the

[GV1:P2061] Brahmins?

[GV1:P2062] Guru: When the Nairs gained administrative power, they engaged in Sambandham (alliances) and absurdities

[GV1:P2063] and became Brahmins. Now they have neither Brahminhood, nor Shudrahood, nor Karma, nor Dharma, nor humility. Due to the complete embodiment of arrogance, they have lost everything. The wisdom to respect and assimilate has also been lost. I have proof for that. One Velupillai Swami from Kottarakkara came to the Aluva Advaita Ashram after a journey. He was a householder and a wealthy man who had set out to become a monk. During prayer time, he was sitting under a mango tree, gazing at nature. A Swami went up to him and asked why he was sitting like that at this time.

[GV1:P2064] "I am a Shudra. I do not want any Ezhava prayers"—was the reply. It is clear that the monk Velupillai Swami did not even know what Varna was.

[GV1:P2065] When they had enough to eat and clothe themselves, they were ready to stand as the king's men. They tried to establish it. The reason is the community itself. That is why that community did not accept Sri Chattampi Swami's *Vedadhikara Nirupanam*—which is entirely a denial of the Vedas, as you know. It is because they could not comprehend the greatness of an Acharya's nature that this happened.

[GV1:P2066] Disciple: Is progress possible without assimilation?

[GV1:P2067] Guru: They will assimilate. They will be made to assimilate. Progress is possible. It is the divine will (Brahmanischayam).

[GV1:P2068] Progress will undoubtedly occur in all actions. There are not just one, but many proofs for that even now.

[GV1:P2069] The great country of India transformed into many islands and then became the continent of Asia.

[GV1:P2085] Although this aforementioned imperial power of Britain was nurtured and elevated upon the ideology of Jesus, when their arrogance grew, they were ousted in a manner that would destroy them, retaining the same capitalistic nature and the same ideology. [GV1:P2086] Here, for this purpose, a man named Ramasamy Naicker brought the Dravidar Kazhagam into practice in Tamil Nadu. [GV1:P2106] In the same position, we too had to experience it as evidence up to the sixth state. Yet, some of the disciples of that time had urged me, saying, 'We can easily do all these things, right?'

[GV1:P2107] It is not that I have not done a few things. And they have yielded results. There was an incident of *Chathaneru* (poltergeist stone-throwing) and a fire breaking out inside a concrete building at a house below the Varkala Sree Narayana Mission Hospital. Since the owner of the house was not there that day, his younger brother Mahani, a medicine merchant—anyone can inquire and verify this—informed me about the matter. I sent a letter through him. 'To the dear Chathan, please do not harm these poor people anymore. With love, Karunakara Guru.' This was the content of the letter. I instructed them to read it at all four corners of the house, read it at the doorway with a lit lamp, and then frame it and hang it in the front porch. With that, the stone-throwing stopped. I do not know if it is still there today. There are many such instances. Eventually, I decided I did not want such *Siddhis*.

[GV1:P2108] Later, these kinds of things stopped yielding results. All of this happened within this sixth state. The very person who had visions about this state became estranged and left. They themselves do not know why they got upset. Six or seven people who had good visions left along with him. The locals would certainly believe what people who worked so closely say, wouldn't they? [GV1:P2265] September 1988

[GV1:P2266] I started speaking only after the age of nine. I studied in an *Ezhuthupallikoodam* (traditional village school). Thus, the only knowledge I acquired was what the *Ashan* (teacher) taught me. Otherwise, I never went to a regular school. The *Ashan* kept me there until I was fourteen. By the time we reached the *Siddharoopam* (basic Sanskrit grammar text), the *Ashan* became physically weak. Therefore, I left that place. I did not study anything after that. Subsequently, it was the *Ashan* himself who took me to the Kalady Ashram. At that time, Swami Agamananda was teaching the *Brahma Sutras* to a person named Swami Jagadiswarananda. After passing the Shastri examination from our region, as he was traveling to North India, a person named Swami Madhavananda in Karnataka took him under his wing and taught him. He lived in Benares for twenty-one years. He had the ability to effortlessly recite texts like the *Siddhanta Kaumudi* fluently, back and forth.

[GV1:P2267] I asked him about this, linking it with my own experience. He replied, "Alas, my child, all of this exists. Although the scriptures state the truth, I have not even seen the light of a lit incense stick. So, I cannot speak about it." I asked many people in this manner. I did not get any evidence from anyone. Later, I met a Pathani Swami. It was from him that I could understand this. Based on his advice, I realized that whatever names exist in this world, they all genuinely exist. If we, consciously or unconsciously, utter a name or conceptualize something, it exists. However, humans have not learned where and how to utilize it. This is what I gathered from my own knowledge.

[GV1:P2268] One can worship using the knowledge up to the *Trimoorthi* (Trinity) system, and one can even see its physical manifestations without any distortion using their naked eyes. There is no need to doubt it. Have I not seen and realized it myself? I have no reason to doubt. Its sub-deities, and even ghosts and spirits (*Bhoota-Pretha-Pishachas*), are real. But we have never contemplated what they can do, or what we should do about them. That is, we have not thought about the *Dharma* (duty/nature) of Kali Yuga.

[GV1:P2269] If Kali Yuga has its own *Dharma*, it belongs to the Shudra. Many *Brahma-jnanis* (knowers of Brahman) have emerged among the Shudras. Sree Krishna is the evidence for that. He belonged to the Yadava clan, didn't he! The history of the Yadavas not only spread among us, but we preserve it tightly even today. Then came the *Parayi Petta Panthirukulam* (the twelve tribes born to a Pariah woman). In the meantime, the eighteen Siddhars also joined in. We have not been able to unite any of these and transmit them through a single language or form, as one unified Indian tradition. We call ourselves Hindus. What we have done so far is behave from behind a system that has been transmitted in such a fragmented way—where small castes have minor gods and upper castes have major gods—making it impossible for us to stand united and pass it on.
