[GV1:P2358] May 1989

[GV1:P2359] Disciple: Is not the concept of the entire life of the world simply sacrifice?

[GV1:P2360] Guru: Many different matters, many different lands, many different spheres—in short, what remains undiscovered is vast. What is the evidence that it has not been discovered? When viewed through the productive system of Christ, the karmic trajectory of the Hindu appears insignificant. Do you pay attention to whether there is goodness in *karma*?

[GV1:P2361] Disciple: Are you referring to Hindus or the people of the world?

[GV1:P2362] Guru: Whoever it may be.

[GV1:P2363] Disciple: No one in the world pays attention to it. People like that are very few in number.

[GV1:P2364] Guru: They do not pay attention. Today, do we not have a scientific system that conducts even interplanetary studies? But does anyone pay attention to who I am by birth?

[GV1:P2365] Disciple: Science has evolved to that extent.

[GV1:P2366] Guru: No matter how much is discovered, the focus has not been on understanding why we must reach there, why we are doing all this, or what similarities exist between that, this sphere, and the sphere situated between them if we do this. Nor has there been any thought about the benefits of doing so. The only thought was that we must proceed in such a manner. Did the scientists inform you?

[GV1:P2367] Disciple: No. Once, everyone in the world was terrified upon hearing that Skylab was going to crash down. Because nothing could be done. If it falls, it could fall anywhere. We could die. Thus, there is a constant danger of death. Moreover, if nuclear weapons are detonated, severe cold will follow - a nuclear winter. Then there will be darkness. The educated populace of today is on the brink of such terror.

[GV1:P2368] Guru: The educated populace stands on the brink of such terror. At the same time, what about a kind of ignorant populace called today's political scientists?

[GV1:P2369] Disciple: They know all this, Guru.

[GV1:P2370] Guru: You should not say they know. It is not that it is hard to know. They cannot know. Why?

[GV1:P2371] Disciple: They are blind with the intoxication of power.

[GV1:P2372] Guru: Yes. When one sees that something burns, just as one quickly throws away the fire, upon seeing that it burns, they adopt yet another condition. A visionary is not like that. He does not act this way. Such a visionary himself will state for how long it is valid. He alone possesses the authority to state the duration for which his idea will endure.

[GV1:P2373] Disciple: Is Christ saying 'for about two thousand years' and Krishna saying 'Sambhavami Yuge Yuge' (I incarnate from age to age) examples of visionary foresight?

[GV1:P2374] Guru: Yes, that is visionary foresight.

[GV1:P2375] Disciple: 'I will come again'. Doesn't 'I' mean someone who acts as an instrument of divine will?

[GV1:P2376] Guru: Yes.

[GV1:P2377] Disciple: That human, that human form at that time..?

[GV1:P2378] Guru: There are four categories of the enlightened: Brahmavit, Brahmavaryan, Brahmavarishtan, and Brahmavarishtan. It is revealed through one belonging to these categories of the enlightened. The subject that teaches who a Brahmavit is, how long he exists, and what all he can do is different. Similarly, the category of prophets. As mentioned earlier - the path of action standing on a conceptual path formed by concepts like Shaivam, Shaktheyam, and Vaishnavam. The path of those called Brahmavit, Brahmavaryan, Brahmavarishtan, and Brahmavarishtan is not that.

[GV1:P2379] Disciple: Is this a concept?

[GV1:P2380] Guru: Yes, it is through conception that one discovers, isn't it?

[GV1:P2381] Disciple: What does concept mean..?

[GV1:P2382] Guru: Why was I born here? What am I?..

[GV1:P2383] Disciple: A contemplative concept?

[GV1:P2384] Guru: Yes, what should be known? How should it be known?

[GV1:P2385] Disciple: Can it be called meditation?

[GV1:P2386] Guru: It can be called meditation, chanting, or action; whatever you wish. But there must be a method of ascending step by step. That is what was said initially. Man grows, reaches a formidable stage of development, and from there turns downwards like compassion - towards insects. Having grown this much and discovered even methods of meditation, man recorded to the extent he saw how far the expansiveness of his mind would reach and where it would not through meditation. As best as he could. In times when there was no paper and pen like today, hiding in the forest, on charcoal or somewhere else, he discovered it and wrote it on trees. Or left it recorded on stones. Followers come and develop it. If you tell religious followers that the Prophet was beaten with whips and staffs, will they like it? There is no interest in contemplating why that great soul made that sacrifice. It became like love for a master, love for a brother, love for a guru, or it grew into love for God. The follower has nurtured this into blindness without knowing any of this. Yet, this very blindness is also present in the man who evolved first. But having grown in that manner, his thought is what returns to show how the atomic principle exists even in these insects.

[GV1:P2387] Guruvani
