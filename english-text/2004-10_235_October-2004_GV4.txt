[GV4:P1010] October 2004

[GV4:P1011] Each of us needs this qualification. Our children absolutely need it. We are completely ignorant about what we must do to achieve this. If we are to eradicate the karma that has accumulated in us over countless periods due to this ignorance, it is impossible except through these children. Therefore, unless each of us moves forward on a path where we attain the knowledge, experience, and familiarity to handle this properly, at least for the sake of the children, we will be forced into a situation where God will never forgive us. The reason for this is that, through our lives and the hardships we have endured, or due to the various tendencies aimed at destroying someone, we have reached a stage where we make offerings and sacrifices, trying to get things done by giving something to God for everything. Having reached such an alien nature, we ourselves are doing it in a way that brings harm upon us.

[GV4:P1012] Each of you must know and make others know whether we can bring about any change in this, and if so, what it should be. We must also preserve that knowledge without allowing any errors to creep into it. To preserve it, we ourselves must possess intrinsic knowledge. We cannot accomplish any of this merely by practically understanding the traditional knowledge that shows us what our life is, and by acquiring all the understanding required to discern right from wrong within it. That is not what is being said. We must focus only on how much knowledge we could acquire during our lifetime, the errors in what we have acquired, and foresee the mistakes that may occur in the future, realizing that this mistake is not only in our hands but in the hands of the entire world. It is exceedingly difficult for us to remove this from the grasp of the world. Now we are all sitting together like this. This has already become an imprint of nature. Based on this present state, which has been imprinted over many periods, and the karma that is going to unfold in relation to the past and the future, where do we stand? We should only think about how we must handle this henceforth. In this regard, God has given us sufficient evidence to understand it concisely and to gain immense knowledge from that brevity.

[GV4:P1013] That is to say, our ancestors have been performing certain acts for ages; they did so with good intentions, but we suffer from the misfortune of not being able to distinguish whether it is good or bad. This is because we, as well as our ancestors, have been unable to investigate and discover where we stand today, having unconsciously deviated by doing many things in the name of worship, driven by the catastrophe of worship that has been practiced and propagated until now. With this sorrowful realization, will we receive an ordained karma regarding how to accomplish this or not? If we do, we cannot look back at the myriad ages of past errors that we must traditionally preserve and correct. Then what is needed? Even among those who know something—such as palmistry or the various aspects associated with our horoscopes—there are countless people who remain ignorant of this. What is known is little, and when we look at what is known, we are standing at ground zero. Why?

[GV4:P1014] Our ancestors have departed and are wandering in the karma of worship we have been practicing. It is not just us; when we say our ancestors, it is not enough to mention two or four generations. Having seen a great light of countless bygone eras, and witnessing what destructions have been sown as seeds within this light, how will we uproot and remove them? That is our concern. It is not enough for us or our country; we are entirely heading towards a karma that strengthens evil thoughts, having grown and spread to all parts of the world, standing firmly and powerfully poised for this battle. Seeing this, do we or our children have the time for anything other than challenging it or taking a form of retaliation against it?

[GV4:P1015] We do not have time to look after our own affairs. Therefore, we must move away from this karmic dosha where cause and effect have become entangled. There is only one way for us to do this. As mentioned earlier, if we proceed by discussing Yoga, Karma, Jnana, and Bhakti, our lifespan is insufficient even to reach that stage. Or else, what can be achieved through our lives? It is not that either, then what is it? Our children must step into the karma of untying the imprint that nature stamps upon them—an imprint that has remained fixed for ages, coming to them as a result of the karma we undertake throughout our lifetimes and its consequences. Instead of questioning how to untie it or whether to untie it, you should not become exhausted by overthinking it.

[GV4:P1016] Why? This is not something that can be resolved by mere thought. As long as we remain in complete ignorance regarding all this, we cannot comprehend it through thinking. But we can do one thing. Give priority to our children, proceeding with the determination, love, and respect that at least our children should be able to perfectly fulfill what God has ordained for each of us. Giving that priority means acknowledging that God has given us the knowledge to alter the karma performed by our ancestors. You are more or less aware of this. If any of our children reaches this state—existing within this interplanetary realm—think of the sphere of influence of countless planets under which our Earth, Moon, or Sun operates together in a karmic process! Only if we have conviction in this karma can we realize the harmful thoughts we have harbored because of it and understand that we must change from it. Unless we prepare a background of good knowledge regarding what this change is—a process that involves feeling pain and causing pain—we need capable children who can take on whatever comes to us or to this era, be it destruction or good, bad or evil. We cannot fully take it on ourselves. Nor can we completely reject it; we have acquired an incapacity that renders us unable to do anything.

[GV4:P1017] Guruvani
