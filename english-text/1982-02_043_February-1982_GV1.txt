[GV1:P1143] February 1982

[GV1:P1144] Our lineage has become entirely ignorant of such matters. The reason is the flaw in the *Trimurti* system and the Vedic decree of untouchability towards the Shudra. As the Shudra was relegated to a very low status, the ignorance of our lineage increased significantly. The Shudra exists in our homes, in the homes of Vaishyas, Kshatriyas, and Brahmins. However, this Shudra is rarely discussed here. To maintain class consciousness, every caste today asserts its class identity. A situation has arisen where people are unwilling to accept someone as a Guru or acknowledge their Guru-hood unless they belong to their specific caste. As evidence of this, starting from Adi Shankaracharya, there are individuals who mention the names of various Mahatmas and speak of them grandiloquently, while calamities continue to befall not just Kerala, but the entirety of India. If asked how many people actually internalize and act upon these teachings, the narrative changes entirely. The auspicious acts of India were accomplished by numerous such Gurus. Let it be remembered that a distinctive power is constantly observing and studying us.

[GV1:P1145] Although God has nothing to learn, He watches how our actions unfold and how the actions of the soul translate into *Karma*. The actions and lineage of Manu serve as an example of this. A society that functions within its respective eras and experiences deprivation manages to survive without completely succumbing to it, exhibiting a certain degree of wealth, intellect, and capability, and thereby passes through life acquiring some fortune. It is easy to observe and understand these matters. What we are practicing now is an easier path to this. That easier path has been bestowed upon us by God Himself.

[GV1:P1146] Gurus are of four categories: *Brahmavit*, *Brahmavaryan*, *Brahmavarishtan*, and *Brahmavareeshtan*. Among these four Gurus, the *Brahmavits* hold the status of the *Trimurtis*. The *Brahmavareeshtan* holds the status of a Rishi. Only two of these Gurus actively engage with the world. The rest do not participate extensively, nor are they accessible to you. This is because they do not descend frequently to act in the worldly realm. They merely speak of what they observe as they pass by. It is difficult for you even to approach and see them. Even if you do see them, you would lack the intellect to comprehend what they say. Although their practical engagement is minimal, their knowledge is profoundly exalted. They merely set forth demonstrating the humility born of love. By internalizing them

[GV1:P1147] *Continuation of the month of January

[GV1:P1148] you will not be able to function. This is not because they lack love, or because you lack love for them. Moreover, there is no provision to accept two Gurus. The Guru will impart advice or guidance to only a select few. Nothing else will be obtained. Whatever you receive comes solely from the *Brahmavit* and *Brahmavaryan* Gurus. Beyond these four categories, there are other Gurus. They are born in accordance with the times. God Himself ordains their birth through a special decree. Certain Gurus born without inherent supernatural powers (*Siddhi*) are examples of this.

[GV1:P1149] Ramananda Swami is the Guru of Kabir Das. Based on the *Bhaktavijayam* and the Puranas, the soul of Kabir Das is understood to be that of a *Brahmarshi*. Kabir Das was not born of a womb (*Yoniputra*). His birth occurred under extraordinary circumstances. It is not something that can be easily explained and comprehended here. It is said that the *Trimurtis* requested Kabir Das to be born in a specific manner. Only then could they have him act according to their intentions. At that point, Kabir Das informed the *Trimurtis* that he could not be born of a womb. He agreed to incarnate only after they promised to facilitate his birth through an alternative means. We are listening to the history of such a great soul. However, what that great soul speaks about the world, the Guru, and the lineage encompasses vast and profound domains. It is then that we truly comprehend the greatness of the Gurus.

[GV1:P1150] In South India, obtaining Gurus of such magnificence has been exceedingly rare. Even if such an occurrence were to happen, our inclination is merely to subordinate them to a deity rather than preserving their legacy in accordance with their greatness. The reason for this is the advent of Shudra governance here. By 'Shudra' here, it refers to the class-Shudra (*Varga-shudra*), not the caste-Shudra. This Shudra was compelled to do everything. Among us, excluding the Brahmin, Kshatriya, and Vaishya, all others are considered Shudras. By establishing governance through the class-Shudra, diverse types of subjects came under the rule of a single monarch. This implies that the king lacked discernment. The one called a king attained power through brute force, not by legitimately occupying the position of a Kshatriya. There is historical evidence for this. The history of Travancore itself serves as an example. The *Ettuveettil Pillamar*, Marthanda Varma, and the *Ettara Yogam* were all members belonging to the Shudra category. Following this, women were adopted from a Kshatriya household. This Kshatriya family was the Mavelikkara family. These are all historical facts. We have not had the opportunity to reflect upon this.

[GV1:P1151] Extremely arduous labor was extracted from the Shudra. History from a thousand years ago does not indicate the existence of caste here. The caste system seen today has emerged merely within the last millennium. Prior to that, the only castes were the aforementioned four foundational classes (*Varnas*).

[GV1:P1152] You will understand the greatness of the Guru only when you comprehend the blunder we have committed. Upon coming to South India, the very concept of the Guru faded away. Apart from offering verbal praises, you have never had the administrative experience of respecting and obeying a Guru. You are also ignorant of your own domestic traditions. This lack of knowledge caused the position of the Guru to degrade so drastically. Due to the absence of a Guru tradition (*Guru Sampradaya*), we have encountered numerous afflictions.

[GV1:P1153] What is the reason the people in South India lacked a complete Guru tradition? The era during which we treated each other as untouchable castes upon sight was not insignificant. That is where you committed your first error. That is what brought a king here. Subjects raised in such a country were prohibited from acquiring education. They were not allowed to handle wealth. The state administration was never intended for those devoid of education and wealth. Thus, they were trampled and suppressed. Those who ruled with tyranny, eating and drinking whatever they could extract, along with a few corrupt officials who thrived on bribes, created a new class. This class was forced to perform all kinds of labor. Due to harsh and menial labor, a community grew in utter ignorance, much like wild grass. Meanwhile, a few within this system sat back and enjoyed luxuries.

[GV1:P1154] Under the cover of such comforts, the local rulers acquired the power to do anything and destroy anything. The king also needed their power. They were given positions of honor. You do not need to know any of the Vedas, Shastras, or Upanishads behind this. If you go out at dawn and work until dusk, you will get a measure (idangazhi) of paddy. The work is for an idangazhi of paddy. Not even for an idangazhi of rice. Out of this idangazhi of paddy, a little is given to the shop to buy salt or chili. The rest is parboiled, pounded, cooked, and consumed to pass the time. Today you might say it is starvation. No. How long ago did the habit of wearing good clothes come about? In those days, none of the workers could afford a dhoti costing more than ten chakras. Even on a taluk basis, you might find about ten such houses. There would certainly be no more than that.

[GV1:P1155] How can we acquire any kind of knowledge while leading such a life? Instead of leading a life endowed with such knowledge, our inability to attain it is due to the grand schemes of the kings. It is impossible even to count the rich people who showed a kind of perversity, stretching their legs to immerse themselves in all the worldly pleasures, making the poor work to help them and destroying them through labor, and those who tolerated these debaucheries to lead an opportunistic life by exploiting them.

[GV1:P1156] When transitioning from the rule of the Ettuveetil Pillamars to the reign of Marthanda Varma, Marthanda Varma's own history states that he beheaded those who applied intellect, logic, and knowledge. Because if they lived, his own head would not remain. The administrative histories before us show that the women of these Ettuveetil Pillamars were captured and married off to fishermen, while the men were beheaded. The government was the 'Ponnu Thampuran' (the golden king). It is said that the Ponnu Thampuran ruled and washed away everything here. Who was washed away? The poor - they were washed away as he pleased. Anyone of some standing was beaten to death. The others were ruled using brute force, much like the British administration did, and through this rule, they became the masters.

[GV1:P1157] The British struck with the butt of their guns. Then it was set right. When this happened, the followers of Vedic rituals and the godfathers here stepped down and started offering enchanted flowers. They chanted mantras after praying for many days. When those who had said they could just pluck and throw them away realized that the butt of the gun would hit their own backs, they cleverly became their slaves. Not only did they become slaves, but they also betrayed the country to them. Gradually, it became about paying tribute. There are records that the king here paid eight lakhs as tribute in those days. Yet, the royal rule continued like an empty shell. We can understand without asking anyone that the people who lived under this rule had no awareness or discretion about good and evil. In this context, demanding "we want freedom, we want freedom," a struggle was waged as a challenge against the British for about a couple of hundred years, and freedom was attained.

[GV1:P1158] Guruvani

[GV1:P1159] March 1982

[GV1:P1160] When freedom was achieved, some of the culture of the British, Muslims, and Christians had spread here. The people handling the administration here—the Hindu administration—out of allegiance to the West, distorted the very history of India, like cutting and shining a torch. Swinging a fire torch in a circular motion from a distance makes it look like a massive fireball, doesn't it? But it doesn't have that much size. Similarly, they do not know the story. They could not understand anything about the history of the Hindus—or what glory their ancient history held. What happened because of this? The nation and politics vanished from here. Everything became like a mashed heap of coir pith. In this state, like handing over governance to an ignorant donkey, power is being handed over to anyone, saying, "You rule." Everyone is ruling.

[GV1:P1161] But even though you have done all this, God has brought about one thing. The time of the author of the Gita says - 'Sambhavaami Yuge Yuge' - what needs to happen in every age will happen in the world from time to time. It means that if you do not accept it, it will kill you. Accepting it is a duty that people must do wisely. It is an undoubted fact that this will come. Now, Christianity, which is only about two thousand years old, and Islam, which is about fifteen hundred years old, are saying things in a renewed form of this. If you look at their history, you can see that before that, things proceeded without any order or discipline.

[GV1:P1162] Due to the government here as mentioned earlier, the flaw of not being able to know gurus and great souls, and the ignorance of people who lived without education, wealth, and enlightenment, we do not know the guru tradition. Or even a puranic tradition is unknown to us. What happened to us from this is complete slavery. However, an era called the hybrid age by the author of the Gita has happened here. Everything is a hybrid today. Starting from humans, birds, and animals to plants, there are hybrid species today. There is adulteration and hybridization in fertilizers, milk, and all food items. Unadulterated honey to be added to medicines is not available. In this state, the age of hybridization has arrived.

[GV1:P1163] Now religious syncretism will come. It is about to come soon. When religions realize they cannot win by fighting each other, a syncretism will emerge. Even though caste is spoken of now, there is a certain shame in explicitly expressing caste loyalty. Still, they will ask secretly - is this the only name? Is there any extension to the name? In the past, when people from places like Thalassery, Kannur, and Kozhikode came to Thiruvananthapuram, one would only see their names. Without adding a tail (caste surname), with just this name here*, when they couldn't get their things done, they too started adding tails. Now everyone has added names like Nair, Pillai, Menon, and Kurup. But in the north, in their native place, this is not there. Thalassery, Kannur, and Kozhikode, if you go to these places, this can be clearly known. From this, we must understand that it is this caste that has destroyed us. This caste, how can we remove it from here? If you want to know the Gurus, if you want to develop love for them, this caste feeling must leave your hands. Otherwise, when you develop love for your caste, you will be unable to absorb the greatness of the Guru. Haven't you heard some people saying - 'Everything is fine, but if only he had been born in the womb of a Nair woman ...'. Then it would have been great', they would casually say. Just imagine how deeply this matter is seated within. This should not happen to us.

[GV1:P1164] In one part of the Manusmriti, it is stated that the varnas of great souls should not be asked. Varnas do not refer to castes. It refers to the four varnas mentioned earlier. He might be a Shudra. Even so, it should not be asked. As an example for this, it is said that the source of a river and the birthplace of great souls should not be investigated. But what are we doing? It is only after inquiring where he was born, who he is, and whose son he is, that we begin to accept a Guru.

[GV1:P1165] If you are to understand the part referred to as the 'lie of the Gurus', there are some other things as well. We can never know these Gurus completely. Moreover, we do not know how to respect and understand the position of the Gurus. Because we suffered the misfortune of not knowing, and because we did not get the opportunity to respect and understand this, we have developed a lot of ignorance inside Kerala or even in South India itself. We can change this only if you become more aware. Only then will you understand if you are told what is called the 'lie of the Guru'. Let me tell you a small method of the Guru's lie. If you look at the history of the Prophet, or if you look at the history of Jesus, Kabir Das, or Ramananda, certain qualities in them

[GV1:P1166] *(In Thiruvananthapuram)

[GV1:P1167] can be seen standing out. What is this knowledge that stands out so much? The Prophet was a person who lived like an ordinary man until the age of forty. He was the son of an ordinary carpenter who carved and sold idols. The history of Jesus is also like that. But how high they stand in history. Mere words - to say in our language, 'ethereal voices' (Ashariri) - were truthfully being received only by that great man. Those received words, written down from time to time, became scriptures. Similarly, several great souls belonging to that, and several disciples following that, emerged. Or the 'Moulvis' who came after that. They took whatever someone wrote down, added to it, and made it into many things. But, even in that, many fabricated stories have crept in. A book has been published recently, making it seem like Muslims are the cause of all the transformations in this world. It has only been two years. But surpassing all this comes the history of the 'Bahaullah' people. When these two histories do not agree with each other, they will fight. Even now, all these Muslim countries are fighting. It is only going to worsen soon. This religious tangle is the reason for this.

[GV1:P1168] Pakistan was separated from the people in India today. When they got the government in their hands, how many great souls did they kill. They tore apart and killed those who worked for the people and the country. The reason for that is a curse. We must believe that they were not able to see the Prophet in a perfect manner. Because they killed the children of the Prophet and others. It is seen that the heads of seventy-three people were chopped off. That curse is what remains in them today. Whenever their government comes, it will chop down a few. Look at the history of Iran and Iraq. Aren't these all countries in Asia? How many leaders were killed in Pakistan and Bangladesh. They are killing countlessly everywhere there. But it is not known outside.

[GV1:P1169] In India, under the rule of Hindus, killing in this manner is rare. Not that it doesn't exist, it does. We have the history of shooting down Mahatmaji and the history of ruling by imprisoning great men. Why has all this been said? It is for you to know the destruction caused by what you did to Gurus and great souls.
