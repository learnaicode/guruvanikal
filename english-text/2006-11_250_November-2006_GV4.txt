[GV4:P1444] November 2006

[GV4:P1445] The author of the Gita has spoken of that special intellect to ponder how many eras have passed. We must respect that. All this running was done ultimately to fulfill that. Understood? Now, the path Jesus ran and the Prophet ran—didn't all these mentioned Mahatmas run? They ran to fulfill it. But they could not. Didn't we make it run from here? Where did it stop? Didn't it go and stop in another continent? Having gone there, wasn't this Dwaraka finished? Look at that Dwaraka even today, isn't it in the calculations? It is. That is one thing told to Arjuna. You take these children and women and save them. There is no other way now. So Sri Krishna, having told Arjuna what to do for this and having shown him, took him along. That is why the disciple was taken along; he was taken to repeatedly fulfill that disciple. What happened when they went there? It was an era where we could not identify the boundary of this action bridging the Yugas. So, Kali Yuga is this many years, Treta Yuga is this many years, Dvapara is this many years, and these three combined is Satya Yuga. Isn't this how we have been convinced? But what can be said about what lies in between this? Shouldn't it cross from one to another? How much time did that take? Is there such a condition? What should we do in this condition?

[GV4:P1446] Disciple: When that change happens, it will become a beneficial change.

[GV4:P1447] Guru: Yes. Is it enough if we just imagine it to be beneficial?

[GV4:P1448] Disciple: We must naturally accept it. Water will turn into steam in that temporary state at a hundred degrees.

[GV4:P1449] Guru: Yes. After becoming steam, won't it become water again? What is the recourse for that?

[GV4:P1450] Disciple: That is the transformation of energy.

[GV4:P1451] Guru: I do not know about energy or anything else. Will this become water or not?

[GV4:P1452] Disciple: It will become water.

[GV4:P1453] Guru: Where will it go? It will return. So that is it. That is exactly what was said. You give some parallel. I find nothing special in it. Is there such a thing or not? How can what exists be destroyed? Where will one go to destroy it?

[GV4:P1454] Disciple: What exists can neither be destroyed nor created.

[GV4:P1455] Guru: If we start speaking transcendently about such a state, where will we stand?

[GV4:P1456] Disciple: We will get nowhere.

[GV4:P1457] Guru: When every religious doctrine, karma doctrine, and yoga doctrine were combined, the exact condition changed. What has been divided into four epochs has manifested as this Yoga, Karma, Jnana, and Bhakti. According to the capacity of the jiva (soul), one can ascend through them. However, without recognizing the level at which the jiva's capacity stands, one simply practiced yoga, performed karma, or practiced bhakti and departed. It is not his fault. That is what he is within the society. Isn't it? How would he know? By what means would he know? Thus, it was first declared, "Vāsanamayam jagat sarvam" (The entire universe is infused with latent tendencies). Not just our entirety, did you understand? It means not entrusting total liberation to just one entity. Assigning knowledge, fortune, and significance to different epochs was a mistake. Herein lies the Yugantara Dharma (the duty across epochs). Who is to state this, who is to see it, and who is to undertake it?

[GV4:P1458] Disciple: Isn't practicing the dharma of the respective epoch (yuga) the only thing that can be done?

[GV4:P1459] Guru: That is true, but about this epoch — one person says five thousand. Another says two lakhs, yet another claims it is even more. Another person asserts it is over four lakhs. Then another states it as eight thousand or nine thousand. It goes on like that, mentioning figures beyond four lakhs, nine lakhs, and sixteen lakhs. What can be done about this? Do we face such an entanglement before us?

[GV4:P1460] Disciple: Yes.

[GV4:P1461] Guru: The question is how to untie it. Do you agree that demons exist?

[GV4:P1462] Disciple: Yes.

[GV4:P1463] Guru: Then is there a God (Deva)? Is there a prophet?

[GV4:P1464] Disciple: Yes.

[GV4:P1465] Guru: Is there not a designated period for the Guru?

[GV4:P1466] Disciple: Yes.

[GV4:P1467] Guru: Then isn't there a Kala Dharma (duty of the times)?

[GV4:P1468] Disciple: Yes.

[GV4:P1469] Guru: What should be done here?

[GV4:P1470] Disciple: In each Kala Dharma, does not that jiva function in accordance with its individual nature?

[GV4:P1471] Guru: It can only function in that manner. Thus, we stand beneath the visionary who emerged through the beliefs or customs shaped by the jiva's past actions.

[GV4:P1472] Disciple: Is not one continuously born into the very state that one has attained?

[GV4:P1473] Guru: When you say being born... Didn't we say earlier that it will turn into water again?

[GV4:P1474] Disciple: If it becomes water, isn't it becoming water while waiting for completeness?

[GV4:P1475] Guru: Yes. What doubt is there about that?

[GV4:P1476] Disciple: If a sprouted seed is roasted, it will not sprout again, will it?

[GV4:P1477] Guru: Having said that this copra (dried coconut) will not sprout, it would be good if there were no other copras. I am speaking about their yogic calculations. If there are no other coconuts, your copra will not sprout. For it to become a copra like that, whatever times and periods need to be lost must indeed be lost. Shouldn't they? Isn't it the evolutionary state of the jiva? Wasn't it said that it should cease to exist? We have not seen to what extent that goes. One should speak only if one has seen it. Then, which skies does this evolutionary state belong to? That is this Nalukettu (traditional four-part structure). There are also those who say, 'He who hasn't built ten is a pig'. Let him build it. When practicing all these yoga methods, I am speaking in your language. So there is this Nalukettu. Shouldn't we know what it was tied with and what broke it? So this is how we must understand it. The reason is that specific doctrines were brought and bound upon us without knowing the duration of the yuga dharmas. We have not received the correction regarding what the dharma is to retrieve what has come and gone. We must understand it. So, if we cross the four skies, we must articulate what the dharma is that crosses the seven realms (Bhumikas) that are mentioned. Would yogis disbelieve that? I am saying that it will go and stand up to there.

[GV4:P1478] Guruvani
