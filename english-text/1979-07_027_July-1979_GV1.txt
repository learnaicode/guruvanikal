[GV1:P0447] July 1979

[GV1:P0448] Question: Guru, it would be beneficial if you could elaborate further on the differences between a Sanyasi and a Rishi that you explained last time.

[GV1:P0449] Answer: I have already stated that a Sanyasi believes his actions are right and expects others to revere him. If he is displeased with the actions of others, he may even abandon them entirely. Alternatively, much like Sage Durvasa, he might adopt a stance of destroying the wrongdoers before proceeding further. He believes that his will must prevail, regardless of who is destroyed in the process.

[GV1:P0450] However, an individual who is both a devotee and a Rishi will attain that position without much deviation. With compassion and mercy towards everything, and mindful that everything should be forgiven as ordained by God, he is able to function moderately and appropriately in due time. Such individuals do not desire occult powers (*Siddhis*) at all. A Rishi is one who acts with the conviction that whatever grace God bestows upon him through his sacrifices is sufficient. This is the difference between a Sanyasi and a Rishi.

[GV1:P0451] Question: Then you had mentioned the matters ranging from Madan, Marutha to Mantramoorthi.

[GV1:P0452] Answer: Yes, I did not mention the Yogi. The other entities—ranging from Madan up to Mantramoorthi—exhibit various forms to display their occult powers and make humans experience them. Consequently, people quickly notice this and propagate that he is a realized soul, which brings about a multitude of other consequences. Deluded by this, they become arrogant, assuming this to be the entirety of knowledge. As a result, their devotion and righteous actions falter, and many become ensnared. That soul itself will be deprived of liberation and will have to wander and undergo rebirths for a very long time.

[GV1:P0453] Question: Regarding rebirth—were you referring to previous births?

[GV1:P0454] Answer: Yes.

[GV1:P0455] Question: So, are we to understand from your statement that one can perceive past births and related matters?

[GV1:P0456] Answer: Yes, one can observe the soul entering other lower life forms and different wombs, up to its meritorious aspects. It is such individuals who have evaluated and spoken about birth and death.

[GV1:P0457] Guruvani
