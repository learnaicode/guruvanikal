[GV3:P0761] February 2000

[GV3:P0762] Most of us cannot maintain the discipline of the ashram. We must know one thing. No other ashram in Kerala or even in India has a discipline like that of our ashram. Hospitality is the greatest etiquette of the ashram. Whether a king comes, we will receive him. Whether a beggar comes, we will receive him. There is a specific way for everyone according to the ashram's rules. No show-offs are allowed there. The ashram should be considered the greatest. Humility is its defining feature. That is enough for us, let us do it this way - this is the attitude and language in the ashram. When I went to Kalady, very rich Nairs were the workers there. There are some Brahmins. They will not eat tempering with onions. Onion is considered asuric. This is a kind of guise. They don't know that onion is strictly a medicine.

[GV3:P0763] We must have studied the great sannyasins. We shouldn't behave with them like ignorant people. There is caste in your very behavior. If they see a brahmachari, sannyasins will be very careful. If one is a brahmachari, he should wear a reasonably proper mundu or a towel. There should be another cloth separately to wipe sweat. When coming among people, this towel must be kept aside before coming. No one should be allowed to touch it. They should not use a vessel used by someone else. These are the rules of a brahmachari.

[GV3:P0764] Everyone should know the discipline of the ashram. Society should be brought to peace by learning and teaching the ashram discipline. A discipline has been established here that brings a sense of respectability to Brahmins, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas, and Shudras alike. That respectability has been brought even to the dressing style of men and women. I have established this by struggling and thinking, evaluating the karmas, dharmas, and customs that have occurred over the ages. I am not just exercising authority for the sake of it. I have not spoken anything that is not factual. Most people are unable to realize what I have done. The youngsters standing here, boys and girls, are copying this ignorance.

[GV3:P0765] I have studied the places I interacted with very well. The people of Varkala are innocent. No one in Kadakkavoor, Vakkom, Nedunganda, or Kayikkara can be controlled. There is a mix-up among men and women in big houses. It is more in Karunagappally and Karthikappally. It is all a kind of vulgarity. The people of Varkala learned this and became spoiled. Thus our culture fell far behind. If we go north, except for Palakkad, everywhere else has a different discipline. That is not the discipline of Hindus. It is the discipline of Muslims and Christians. The culture of Palakkad district stands apart from all this.

[GV3:P0766] What I meant is, the brahmacharis standing here have nothing to learn from anywhere else. Because all existing ashrams are on the English model. If you see the children coming from Sivagiri, you will understand that. They also have a kind of descent and mingling, twisting and turning among men and women. More than the girls coming from outside, you have to be careful with women above thirty years of age. It is very difficult to correct them. Because, they will have some kind of arrogance. I am not saying you should see them as bad when they come here. You should tell them we don't need that anymore. They will obey that. We must pay attention to our lives. We shouldn't get stuck in the pampering of our mothers. What is the pampering of mothers? Mothers have one behavior in houses without boys. Another behavior in houses with boys. This is the pampering of mothers. The girls coming here from these houses do not know any of this behavior. But they also don't know the behavior here. You should tell those coming here what the discipline of this ashram is. You have nothing to learn from them.

[GV3:P0767] What do Ezhavas and Nairs think an ashram is? All they know is not to eat fish, be pure, shouldn't even walk through that compound - that much only. They know nothing else. You do not understand what all I am doing to fix a house. You don't even understand why I scold those standing near me. I will draw everyone near and push them away at the same time. That is a matter only I know. If I see incompetence, I will draw them near. If incompetence is seen, pushing them away saying they are useless, not good, is dangerous.

[GV3:P0768] What is the nature of an ashram? An ashram is a place that corrects the useless and makes use of the good. It will do everything possible to make use of them. Only then will his karma increase. Otherwise, he will become waste. Your knowledge about ashrams is limited. The only ashrams you have seen are Sivagiri and Sri Ramakrishna Mission - both are ashrams of sannyasins. Very few have come there as brahmacharis. If someone shows a little devotion, others will cry out, "Oh, he became a sannyasi." Hearing this, this sannyasi will feel elevated. He might be longing to get the respectability of a sannyasi. For someone who already has one or two children, this talk will be an added encouragement. Then he will renounce everything! Such useless sannyasa exists there. They might have education and wealth. They are good at spoiling others. He is someone who grew up through this filth, isn't he? He didn't become a brahmachari and leave. If a sixty-year-old man goes and stays in some ashram for two days and comes back, the women at home will ask, "Do you need all this now?" Then he will get such publicity in the native place too.

[GV3:P0769] We ourselves must realize that our people lack knowledge. We must change them from this lack. That is the prayer in this ashram. When doing this prayer, not just them, but all the great souls who wished to walk the path of truth in the worship methods that have occurred till today are getting liberated from their shortcomings.

[GV3:P0770] What is this liberation? It is not the liberation mentioned in the Puranas or Vedic hymns. It is the liberation that is the knowledge known by the great souls themselves about when their karmic flaws occurred, how long it will take to be resolved, and when they will return amidst humanity after that. God is leading us to such a karma. Marrying off men and women here and keeping them here to perform certain sankalpas is for the arrival of these great souls. Its sign will be shown to them even before the child is born. At least twenty-five out of a hundred people will get these signs in a vision.

[GV3:P0771] What is your understanding when you say vision? Even the one who practiced chathan seva (black magic) shows plenty of special siddhis once he gets a vision. Amazed by that, we fall into adharma just like following the path of people afflicted with karmic flaws. This is like the adharma of lighting a lamp and worshipping the ghost of someone who died in an accident. Not knowing what all will happen to us and our children if we worship all this, even foreign countries show a lot of sorcery nature. They are called civilized. You must think about where all this demon goes and shows this. A truth shown by God to bring a change from this is this ashram and the prayer here. Each one of you are the children entitled to achieve this fortune. Children born in any part of the world after nineteen seventy-three especially have that fortune.

[GV3:P0772] This change will not come running immediately. After every Jupiter cycle (12 years), there will be a change in the world's government. All this governing nature seen today will be changed. This change will be through these children. Coming like that, these children will work and show what this world's dharma is, and how the karmic flaws will change. It will happen completely for your third generation. God is making the guidance of such a great theory happen here.

[GV3:P0773] Guru Vani

[GV3:P0774] March 2000

[GV3:P0775] The world has had a prayer system at all times. But what is the prayer in Santhigiri? What is it for?

[GV3:P0776] The prayer at Santhigiri is a prayer given by God to rectify all the shortcomings of whatever types of householders there are in the world, and whatever shortcomings we have. So you must have an intention - we will do this prayer to the best of our ability, according to our capacity and knowledge, and the rest must be provided by God. For that, we will go through the path given by God.

[GV3:P0777] Through that, you should develop an idea of how your children should be. It is for that, householders are retained here and many things have been set up for them here. But most of you have the misfortune of not being able to obey it.

[GV3:P0778] Why? Not because nobody wants it. Your tendencies grip everything tightly. "I want to be good, I should be useful to everyone"ڈ - such desires exist in at least some. But whatever desires you have, this tendency intertwines with life in a way that cannot be eliminated. This karmic flaw drags you down. By keeping any kind of hollowness or vanity, no one can change this karmic flaw that has become a tendency.

[GV3:P0779] What should we understand when we say householder? In what our ancestors believed and worshipped till today, there are those entitled to get peace, qualified to get liberation, and qualified for mukti. We have maintained everything according to its respective qualification. We have not heard of this idea till today. It includes pitru, devas, devi, rishi, sannyasi, and everything. It is difficult for us to know all this. Or even if known, how many can fulfill it? It cannot be done by any of you.

[GV3:P0780] This must be done. If you cannot do it, your children must do it. The means for that purification I have told everyone till today - Guru Pooja! This purification - pitru purification, deva purification, rishi purification - no one has been able to maintain, and no one is able to. From each karma prescribed for that, all of you are going backward. This is my experience till today. This is something that doesn't happen even if you wish for it. That is, it's not that nobody has the desire. Some kind of inabilities happen.

[GV3:P0781] Wishing to get a change from this misfortune and making others wish, some men and women come and stay here. They also do the work they can. But no one is able to do it properly. For this, prayers have been conducted here at different periods. Due to the mistakes that happened traditionally to us and our close ones, we are unable to take this. Therefore, passing that goodness to future generations, at least through them, we must be able to bring that goodness to all people. It is not enough if that goodness comes only to our land or country, that goodness must come to the whole world. All the hardships of the world must go away. All the troubles that have come in the governments of the world must go away - this should be your constant sankalpa.

[GV3:P0782] We are moving forward doing such a karma. What is it? God has gifted us a karma that helps towards the possibility of liberation, or the possibility of mukti for pitrus, devas, rishis, sannyasins, and everything. And God Himself is making us do it. Because we are not capable of achieving it. But then you might think, why make us struggle like this, couldn't God Himself just do it? It's not like that.

[GV3:P0783] Millions of gurus and their followers came for this. They could not achieve it. There is a reason for it.

[GV3:P0784] In the aspect of the knowledge passed down as Sanatana and Hindu, we are nobody. But we have to take this back. Through us, through our children, the world must get this. This is our goal. For that, the prayer given to us by God is our prayer.

[GV3:P0785] There are so many crores of souls in the subtle atmosphere who have completed in the deva karma. They all must get liberation. In addition, there are lakhs of souls called rishis and sannyasins, and rishis and sannyasins who have gone below and above the deva. We have mostly heard about the rishis and sannyasins who have gone in the prayer of the deva. But there are souls standing beyond that. Although these souls can remain in higher planes, they cannot get liberated. The reason is that they cannot cross according to the ordinances of time's dharma.

[GV3:P0786] We can see; the karmic power acquired by each lineage of prophets, each Guru lineage, and each religious theorist is varied. In many, there are mistakes that occurred due to the far-sightedness of various karmas. The capability to change this karmic power is lacking for that soul. We and our ancestors do not have that capability. Because there are crores of souls standing across the deva status with crores of rishis in the nature of Hindus or Sanatanis, and prophets and apostles. What understanding do we have regarding them? However, those who came by God's will have tried to say some things up to their vision. We haven't been able to take at least that much. The reason we couldn't is that we could not recognize Sanatana and Hindu.

[GV3:P0787] When the Hindu theory was mixed up, it had only three faces. Shaktheya, Shaiva, Vaishnava, or Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva - we are groping at the bottom of what was said and fixed like this. That path of karma, which has been operating from its beginning till today, has become the foundation for this world. It is not easy to change this. Who is it impossible for? For the lakhs of gurus and acharyas who came before. We can do it. Only we can do it. In the righteous initiation mentioned by those great souls, they have seen and told the difficulties or obstacles that came to them.

[GV3:P0788] When determining the time of great souls, it should be taken at least from the time of Sree Rama. It is not enough to take the prophets, gurus, or acharyas who came recently. We must repeatedly fulfill them, their lineage, and the obstacles they faced, which they themselves have revealed. For that, God has gifted us the prayer and Guru's path at Santhigiri.

[GV3:P0789] Guru Vani
