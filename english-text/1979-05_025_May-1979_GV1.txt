[GV1:P0421] May 1979

[GV1:P0422] Question: Guru, kindly elaborate further on the special prayers in this ashram. You have spoken about them previously.

[GV1:P0423] Answer: I had indicated earlier that those who come here must manifest the virtues of the ashram. As a result of penance performed over countless lifetimes, God has granted us something like this. Even if we cannot accomplish anything good here, we must at least have the intention to avoid spreading evil.

[GV1:P0424] When returning from the ashram, one must accept and implement at least one teaching. Then, opposition will arise from home. The home will represent the opposite side. If things do not work out, you must resolve in your mind: 'May this benefit those who are favorable to it.' One should harbor the thought, 'If I ever build a home, I will sustain this truth.'

[GV1:P0425] The discipline of the ashram is deeply touching and experiential. One must attribute greatness to the facilities available here today. None of us came with prior learning. It has evolved into this state through the passage of time and experience. Periodically, various customs were adopted. No one reflects upon whether there are flaws in these customs or whether this is all that we require. If someone is seen growing their hair, everyone else imitates it. They do not pause to consider the reason behind it.

[GV1:P0426] Cultural trends change as frequently as daily cinema schedules. Conventions must be in a manner that brings us goodness; otherwise, they will cause harm. What does one understand when told that this is an ashram's convention? There are many practices here. Those without knowledge will not comprehend them.

[GV1:P0427] One learns to pray through the Bhajana Matham and the temple. It will be comprehended through the Guru. If one has understood this, they would have contemplated all its aspects upon arriving at the ashram itself. One must have the conviction that the prayers here will eradicate the adverse karmic effects of our ancestors. These prayers will certainly succeed in destroying sinful forces and bringing about divine merit.

[GV1:P0428] Guruvani
