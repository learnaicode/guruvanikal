June 1995

Thus, the mistake that came and went through the four Yugas is not the mistake of this country alone. It is the mistake of the whole world. The karma of rectifying this mistake will happen only in Kali Yuga. In all other Yugas, the path of the respective Yuga Dharmas is taught. If the Guru is qualified to grant liberation, he guides those who come to him according to their qualifications. To a Brahmachari according to his capability, to a householder according to his capability, to a great man according to his capability - thus when guiding him to reach the knowledge that he is doing the karma of the world, he became unable to take it. Because this glory itself cannot be known. Even though Krishna came, it could not be known.

Even though knowledge was given in advance, the ruler Kamsa could not take it - you should not rule like this, you should rule looking at the sadhus. Sadhus means the good people - you should rule only as the Sanyasis (Gurus) say. Kamsa could not take it. Because he was also on the path of that mistake that happened many Yugas ago. For those standing on that path, all aspects of pain will be shown to bring about a special feeling. And when Kali Yuga begins, these mistakes must be rectified. Then we may lose many things. Because Kali is the path that determines one of these - from Deva to Devi, from Devendra to Parashakti, from Shaktheya to Vaishnava - determining that taking one is enough.

But we have been taught exactly the opposite. That Kali is useless. We have lost everything of our own. The only thing left is the Western way. Things happened in such a way that their karma came. Religious theories also came through many great men. But we have become far from all this, or have been made far, in such a way that we cannot carry any of this. Truth has become unknown to us. Krishna came to show this truth. Not only to us, but even to that house it was not understood. Ours became a tradition that could not be understood even after getting a divine voice. Therefore, he also perished. His tradition also perished. Not only that, we lost the statecraft without understanding what it is. That is why the Portuguese, the Dutch, and the Mughals attacked us.

So a mistake happened to our ancestors and teachers. We must know how that happened. God has given us a correction for this. You are unable to understand this special provision given by God. This is the result of the karma done so far.

When our ancestors die, their souls go and stand under this goddess, god, or devil. This ancestral purification is done here so that this soul can get some goodness through birth, or to make it do some goodness after birth. If we tie up the ignorance of our parents and the shortcomings of our souls and go after pleasure, life will only be wasted. This is a time when we are made to do something by reaching some research center in the part called the knowledge series of material pleasure. But we were not taught what freedom is. What is freedom? We must remember that even Mahatmaji received the kicks, spits, and blows of those who attacked and conquered us. After that, God poured many good things upon us. The main ones responsible for that goodness are the Parayi petta panthirukulam. Especially Thiruvalluvar. Even after they all came and spoke, we did not understand what freedom is. Therefore, by giving birth to great souls in foreign lands, that lineage was given the facility to capture the government. They not only came here and subjugated us, but also created a situation where we can live honorably only if we adopt their language and dress. Today the world market is their monopoly. Kali is the time destined to know what we have done to our lives to make this ours.

Through this favor of time, we are taking over and doing the karma of our ancestors. Can we do it? The people of the god of one caste cannot go near the god of another caste. The magnitude of the ignorance formulated among us like this is what has destroyed us. An example is the Saraswat Brahmins. It is assumed that Saraswat Brahmins are the real Brahmins. To some extent, it is true. But how much respect do we give it? We consider it a joke to take some of their words and imitate and mock them under the name of Namboothiri jokes. They are the ones who started living honestly with great difficulty without using any intelligence. But there is a shortcoming that everything went through the path of tantra.

Our ignorance, born and brought up through the trinity system that has crossed many Chaturyugas, has not been able to create anything of our own or maintain what was ours at one time. At the same time, many intelligent people and thinkers were born in foreign lands, developed science and technology, and took over the fields of industry and commerce. All this was here before. Why doesn't what belongs to India become India's own? Because we can only sit around talking about the greatness of our uncle and cultural centers like Nalanda and Takshashila. We didn't do anything.

Thus we drowned in every way. To escape from this drowning, what we need is a way of life according to the Yugadharma. Everyone who came to tell us the way of life according to the Yugadharma made a mistake. This is what we must clearly know. We are sunk in the deception of the story. No matter who does good, an interest will come and destroy it. They will tell a story for that and make us believe it. Just take an example from this Chaturyuga itself to understand that. Even though Mahabali ruled very well, God came, stepped on his head, and pushed him down to the underworld.
