[GV1:P1406] June 1984

[GV1:P1407] Prayers related to the full moon (Veluthavavu) have been given great importance among ordinary Hindus since ancient times. After that, we can see that the Arabian people also gave it importance. Buddha has also given it a place. This full moon day has a special quality of good and bad happening equally.

[GV1:P1408] All creation happening on earth takes place with the help of the moon. Without the dust particles of the moon, fertilization will not occur in grains, humans, or other animals. Thus, creation takes place with the help of the moon. It is sustained by the power and heat of the sun. The earth is what comes from the combination of these two. Two phases have been established - the dark phase and the light phase. Great souls, having discovered with divine permission how creation has occurred in this world, have made us understand it.

[GV1:P1409] Hindus have proceeded by attaching a divine concept to everything. For example, they have assigned divinity to the five elements (Panchabhootas). It is done so that humans would respect and practice it. It does not mean they are God. When divinity is assigned, they will be handled without being dismissed or disrespected. Just because they are handled without disrespect, no one should assume that they are God. When we drink water, we drink it thinking of God. Similarly, when a driver drives a vehicle, or a digger takes the spade to dig soil, or a beedi roller takes the scissors, they do it remembering God in their hearts. But one should not misunderstand that they are worshipping the profession itself.

[GV1:P1410] Such customs have come into all religions. The reason for that is the paths discovered by our ancestors were not insignificant. Those who came later saying that the moon and the sun are gods did so either to mislead ignorant people or because they themselves were misguided. Otherwise, no one has ever said that they are gods.

[GV1:P1411] Because the Treta Yuga and Dvapara Yuga were the times of the Devas, these Devas themselves have been depicted even as God and Brahman. Since it was the era of the Devas back then, such things would happen. But after entering this Kali Yuga, in order to repeatedly bring about the Kali Yuga, God very carefully brought forth various great souls (Mahatmas). Then He would create several people as their followers. No matter how they are created and brought forth, some people will hold on to the old ways and act in opposition. Truly, it cannot be said to be their fault. Because they have a familiarity with what they have been practicing. Beyond that, there is a caution in the subtle world that one should not transcend this status of the Devas. From demons to the Trimurti, everyone maintains that caution.

[GV1:P1412] It is said in Islam that the kingdom is ruled by Raja Jinns. In fact, that is correct. It happens in such a way that if a person is born with purity, capability, and competence akin to the deities, this subtle power will align with him and make him operate it. To keep someone with considerable intelligence, capability, and competence at that level and prevent them from progressing, an ego is imparted to them. It instills an ego in him, making him think, "I am the ruler," or "I am intelligent," or "I possess knowledge and wealth—I am competent." However, a person who possesses all these things never gets the wisdom to think: "Oh God! You have given me so much intelligence and capability, but I am not worthy of any of this. What is the state of my body and mind? What significance does my capability have? What will I become if I lose my consciousness the next moment, or what will happen to me if my body is afflicted with an incapacity or disease? What have I achieved in my lineage?" If one has wisdom, one should think in this direction as well, cultivate a more grounded nature, and combine humility, strength, and logic to attain the fortune of finding completeness through karma. How many of us have preserved this? No one has. Because of this, we have incurred numerous shortcomings.

[GV1:P1413] The previously mentioned specific power known as the 'subtle world' (sookshma lokam) is indeed the individual souls (jivatmas) themselves. Remaining there, just as the rays of the sun and the moon fall, its rays too are constantly falling. It will initially approach those jivatmas who possess a virtuous mindset among the virtuous. However, when he seeks to progress towards liberation (mukti) or the status of a deity, he will be obstructed there. This has been the experience of all the great souls who have existed to date. When such an obstruction occurs, no matter how great he is, his mind will be diverted and shift towards something else. Alternatively, if his merit (punya) is immense enough to transcend his fortune and innate nature, he might be inflicted with an ego capable of destroying that merit. Thus, certain phenomena exist within this subtle realm.

[GV1:P1414] You might harbor doubts regarding whether all these things truly exist. They certainly do exist. Nothing that is non-existent has a name in this world. However, the great souls have already provided us with evidence demonstrating that humanity holds a position beyond all this and that humans are capable of overcoming all these obstacles.
