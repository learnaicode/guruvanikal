[GV3:P0803] June 2000

[GV3:P0804] Today we have many types of worship methods. Those were told for us by the Mahatmas. But a situation has come where we are not able to understand it. In the name of worship, what we have is only a familiarity with what was practiced and performed in some era. If we get a new idea, we do not have the ability to absorb and act upon it. Something that does not give us the opportunity to act in that way is situated very secretly among us. It is over a long period that Chaturvarnya was completed. It is not known how this Chaturvarnya came about. This is what we call the Varnashrama system. It is said that this was brought in with the concept of Varna according to the times. But have we been told with certainty what those times are?

[GV3:P0805] It is said to be Satyayugam, Tretayugam, Dvaparayugam, and Kaliyugam. It is also said 'Kali Shudrankal' (Kali belongs to the Shudra). If it is to be said 'Kali Shudrankal', there must be a reason for it. Otherwise, it would not be said so. We do not think about this because of our ignorance. There is evidence for it. Before the birth of Sri Krishna, Kamsa got to know about that birth. But he could not think about Sri Krishna's birth or give respect to that birth. What all did Kamsa, who got the birth of a king, do to prevent Krishna from being born? If such knowledge was given in a royal family, how would a Shudra take it? Shudra is not mentioned here to denote a caste. According to Varnashrama Dharma, all people born in the fourth quarter can only be born as Shudras. If this Shudra is to become a Dwija (twice-born) by karma, holding onto what should he perform the karma? What are those karmas?

[GV3:P0806] No one has thought that good karma is not needed. But what is our knowledge regarding good karma? We would place a crude stone (Madan) and call it 'Lord'. In this state, who can perform good karma? When saying 'Kali Shudrankal', who are the Shudras? There are Shudras in all times. There are people of all four Varnas in all times. That is, the time for all Varnas to attain purity is the time of the Shudra. That is Kali. We have never thought about how to take up this purity until today. Many great souls were born in the Shudra clan after Sri Krishna. Although great souls were born in other clans, more Rishis, Sanyasis, Yogis, Karmis, Jnanis, and Bhaktas were born in the Shudra clan. The Shudra clan does not even know what they were able to achieve.

[GV3:P0807] There was a prediction that Sri Krishna would be born; he was born. The rest were all killed. If this is the knowledge held by the upper Shudra, which Shudra can be expected to take up this karma? The Shudra class is one that does not even know why Sri Krishna was born. There are two hundred or three hundred castes within the Shudra class. Everyone says they are better than the other. When class hatred grew like this, Sikhs, Jains, and Buddhists came. Advaita was preached. Dvaita was preached. None of this could take up this karma. What should come next?

[GV3:P0808] Seeker: There should be an idea that transcends all this.

[GV3:P0809] Guru: Yes, but don't we need a familiarity with it? Three Mahatmas were born in Kerala. We could not recognize these three. Thousands of Mahatmas were born like this. We did not recognize any of them. Therefore, they could not achieve anything. What is the solution for this?

[GV3:P0810] Seeker: People should think about spirituality.

[GV3:P0811] Guru: It is not like that. They do think. They just don't have great knowledge,

[GV3:P0812] that's all.

[GV3:P0813] Seeker: There were some epoch-makers (Yugaprabhavan). At the same time they were born in this Kaliyuga by divine will, souls that had reached a certain stage in previous yugas also came in the system of deities (Devi-Deva). These Acharyas could not move to a state higher than that.

[GV3:P0814] Guru: None of those who came were bad people. Thinking of them as bad is ignorance. We should think from the limits of our knowledge. Otherwise, the words will become worthless.

[GV3:P0815] Seeker: There needs to be a divine leadership.

[GV3:P0816] Guru: Yes. That is needed. What I am saying is that even though it was given, you could not take it.

[GV3:P0817] Seeker: Epoch-making Gurus come and create their own structure. Following that, those who come later create various systems.

[GV3:P0818] Guru: Yes.

[GV3:P0819] Seeker: Through this structure, the instructions and principles of the Gurus disintegrate in many ways. Thus that blessedness, or that divinity, is lost. Those systems turn into various types of vested interests.

[GV3:P0820] Guru: Yes. Like building a trap. This cannot be called worshiping God. You do not understand it this way. This basis is not there for the worship of God either. What exists now is not a divine basis, nor a prophetic system. It is just a Vedic religion meant for mere chanting.

[GV3:P0821] Seeker: Each individual should get the light directly.

[GV3:P0822] Guru: It won't be received like that. We have the merits of our birth, don't we? That merit should increase in our lineage. There is no other justification for this matter. What should be done to increase this aspect of merit? Will it happen if castes are separated? Will it happen if classes are separated? Isn't every child of yours - even the government administration - operating by separating like that? What should people do?

[GV3:P0823] Seeker: People should have the eligibility for it.

[GV3:P0824] Guru: Even if there is eligibility, it cannot be known. Now, aren't Jesus and the Prophet prophets? We have no way to take the words they have spoken. It is celebrated that Sri Shankara preached Advaita. Does saying the meaning of the word mean that God will come?

[GV3:P0825] Seeker: Only a lineage with God-realization can know this.

[GV3:P0826] Guru: Yes. That is correct. But we don't have such a lineage. Is it enough to just preach for that? What could you do for it? Even if it is done and placed in front of you, you won't be able to take it.

[GV3:P0827] Seeker: That requires sacrifice. We are unable to do it because of our inability.

[GV3:P0828] Guru: Such inability has happened to you. Not that you are bad people. Your ability is lying at the bottom. If it is brought to light, you will have everything. I have done the work for that here.

[GV3:P0829] Guruvani
