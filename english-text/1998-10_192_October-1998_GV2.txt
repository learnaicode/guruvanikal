English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

October 1998

The purpose of birth should be to fulfill the divine will of the Almighty, who is the master of all the human beings born and dying across ages, not just the spiritually meritorious of ancient times. Whether material or spiritual, the good aspect is to realize the right and wrong that have come and gone in the world so far, and to know the reason for that right and wrong. We all know that if there is a right, there will also be a wrong along with it. Whatever human nature has been passed down, from the highly intelligent to the utter fool and those in between, all are born as heirs to a system of karma.

It is written that our doctrine is one that does not hurt even a blade of grass, a worm, or an ant. Moreover, some gurus of certain lineages walk around with their mouths covered to control the air going in and out while speaking. In principle, all this is correct. But how can the active karma of human life be fulfilled through these methods? Or else, how can men and women, young and old, realize the field of belief in the guru lineage? Today, we stand together in a meaningless karma of pretending to be a scholar or an ignoramus by talking too much about the gravity or guidance in the karma of speaking with an intellect that does not lose its dignity and an intellect that does not distinguish between honor and dishonor.

The aspect of Hindu or Sanatana dharma is a philosophy that shows compassion to life or living beings. The religious doctrines contain a philosophy that has been controlled for billions of years. Does it mean that it is complete if a scholar adds a sloka by bringing it into the form of vowels and consonants through literal interpretation and extending it to an infinity where one cannot distinguish between what is and what is not? Similarly, we possess an ignorance where doctrines have been formed through learning, teaching, knowing, and not knowing for a long time. Although we can go like that if we remember the meaning of any religious doctrine, do you think that all the meaning brought to us from our ancestors who acted by imagining a devil or each name and form that has come and gone so far has been completed with these fragments of Vedanta? Or else, many historians came and wrote over time, great scholars, not just Siddhantakaumudi, Laghusiddhantakaumudi, but if we say we should only take the good and discard the bad in the karma that has been copied for millions of years with right and wrong mixed together, with what will we discard it? With what will we take it? This habit of thinking has become necessary and essential for all of us.

Today's subject before us is the many historical truths that have been lost, along with the ultimate truth of many theories that are considered to be the meaning of this world. We look at that historical truth through the visionary who says everything is karma and the visionary who says there is no karma at all. From which side are they speaking? Both have spoken truthfully in two ways. In what way did this karma come into being, and in what way did it cease to exist? We all know that if there is karma, there will be a cause and effect in it. Many people - many great men - have tried their whole lives to find the extent of the glory of the time we were born, where these two are intertwined, without establishing that it exists or does not exist. Should we understand that all investigations are complete if these truth-seekers have strived for us over many periods and read or written up to the encyclopedia to establish that everything in the world exists?

We know a truth: I, mine, my father, mother, children, wife, husband, and thus the matter of measuring the enjoyment for the good of all that is mine is everything we said earlier. If everyone applauds and passes the best word among those measured, and goes about babbling the goodness of that word, should we believe that the knowledge of dharma, karma, jnana, and vijnana, and the familiar karma and dharma have been transformed and their pros and cons have been returned?

However, experience so far has proven that it is not like this at all. The goal of all the truth that begins by saying I am speaking the truth, I have seen the truth, is that each person's own truth or own life belongs to a large, broad mind, a goal of a long time. What have we been able to learn as good and bad that we have studied so far? That is true for all thinking people. Thus, the yoga, karma, jnana, and bhakti that form the truth spoken knowingly and unknowingly about anything are all true as long as there are humans in many ways. If we speak pure and true words about that truth, how can we reach it by giving karma and dharma for ages, whether truth and untruth are yet to come or not?

Can the experiential truth that originated and was experienced from such thought be completed by vocalizing it with words like this? It speaks of good and bad

What have we been able to do so far other than establishing it on the side? This stands before us as a big issue. From humans to gods who rule and make others rule the world, which has emerged as good and bad irrespective of religious differences. What have we achieved with a life that bargains with the dharma and karma of the special rule seen by respecting and making others respect the karma path that has come through the concept of God-Goddess or the prophetic system and guru system of the respective religion, writing and making others write stories, playing and making others play, using words that can reach up to the path of salvation if necessary, and putting it into practice?

Our goal is to complete the qualifications of the respective times of the fourteen Manus known as the Manu lineage. What it is like is that each Kaliyuga has been brought about as the end nature of each Chaturyuga by evaluating the karma and dharma spoken about Manu and karma in the karma path called Swayambhu. The reason for bringing Kali to such an end is to carefully know what Kali Dharma is after reaching the light of time as humans, grass, flowers, plants, and medicinal plants. If we think about how we have passed through the Manu lineage, the trinity system, the Vedic religion that emerged from it, and the Varnashrama dharma, have we been respecting and worshiping the truth imparted by the Vedanta religion by carefully studying the Vedas and epics? This cannot be counted and said. Therefore, Buddha, reaching the attainment of the world of Nirvana with the three refuges, thought for many times and showed the three refuges without any other way. Kaliyuga is ordained for us to reach at some point in a truth that has been sought after for many times. The truth that this body is for the benefit of others is that karma, dharma, and truth work together by taking and seeing the right and wrong of the other three times. Our theoretical religion is to take and see such a time-bound nature and work it out.

The information is that seventy-one Chaturyugas make up one Manvantara in the Manvantara calculation. The twenty-eighth Chaturyuga of the seventh Manu (Vaivasvata Manu) out of such fourteen Manvantaras is currently going on. The trinity system and the Sanatana religion system are to see and act from where it went wrong. The goal of Kali is to take and see all that has been acquired and complete it. Instead, our Vedanta religion has only one God, only one Brahman, only one prophet, only one Guru. This is where we have made a mistake. To correct this, that Brahman determination itself has stood as our support and shade and worked for many times. Evaluating the respective times, our inability to stand dissolved in the karma of seeing and completing it is the reason for our destruction so far.

Anyone with some knowledge knows that any state is completed by the Kali period. Guru Pooja at Santhigiri is a karma of its own. The six Manvantaras and the first three Chaturyugas of the seventh Manvantara shown by God or Brahman are true. Manvantara is an imagined dharma that has been lying as Manvantaras up to the measure of life, maintaining the path of dharma by working while standing in this true karma. When followers of such a Manu come, they are called visionaries. In the same way, a visionary who continuously completes a Manvantara in guru paths has many followers in guru-disciple relationships. In that, each guru or each prophet completes the right and wrong in the Kaliyuga itself, counts each state in the special situation of God, makes it pure, and attains the truth.

Thus, there is a visionary who has completed many Chatur Yugas. There is a visionary who has divided a Yuga among five gurus. Among them, the fifth is a person who works here and there and evolves from one to another. It is this fifth person who transfers that Yuga to the next Yuga. Thus, the guru who completes a Chatur Yuga is also a visionary, and a Manvantara is composed of many such gurus. Reaching fourteen such Manvantaras is written as a Kalpa, which is a Brahma Kalpitam. If it is calculated that a guru who has discovered this much has completed one day of Brahma, how many days and nights combined would make up the lifespan of this Brahma.

In reality, a human being does not even need to think and understand such things. Because, with the measure of his life, what can he think about this? Or else, what can be thought out about this in one lifetime? But it is not like that, there should be an action in life where a human lives and learns what he has done until the end of his life. We do not know the secret that we all live to know and live the blessedness achieved through the sacrifice of working until the end of life by visionaries, gurus, and thousands of gurus in many ways, who have seen and spoken in the gross and subtle, in cause and effect, that a theoretical action of good and bad exists here as billions of humans lived and performed actions. Therefore, even if we cannot describe and know all this, if we do not know the truth of life that attains dharma through an action sacrificed for us by someone who knew, they will become nothing one by one.

The number one has value only when we start counting as one. Otherwise, in the action called numbers, one will become nothing. Similarly, the meaning of each of our lives is the counting of many lives. Even after saying this in this way, in many places, it has come to be said as just a single word.
