[GV1:P1463] December 1984

[GV1:P1464] Householders (*Grihasthashramis*) are incapable of preserving a spiritual lineage. They can solely protect their offspring. The term lineage, in this context, refers not to biological succession, but to the *Guruparampara* (Guru lineage). Nevertheless, the householder possesses the inherent right to provide the requisite support and sustenance to that spiritual lineage. Jesus introduced a novel system of worship. When individuals placed their faith in it, their lineage prospered. Through the worship paradigm introduced by the Prophet, that lineage also flourished. Buddha similarly introduced a system of worship to this land. However, precisely when it began to flourish, the Brahmins, establishing their stronghold here, methodically corrupted and compromised its descendants. Consequently, the progeny and women within that tradition were subjected to moral decay. As a direct result, Buddhism was compelled to depart from this land. It was the entrenched presence of this caste-based hierarchy here that necessitated its expulsion. All of this was systematically orchestrated utilizing the Shudras as instruments.

[GV1:P1465] Observe the myriad pathways the Divine has manifested for you across various realms. Yet, you fail to embrace them. If, despite being provided with such profound opportunities, you continue to reject them, it will inevitably culminate in natural calamities. Thereafter, no remedial action will be viable. The spiritual legacy we have inherited today is sanctified and everlasting. The prophetic revelation associated with this states: "If we pray with unwavering devotion and humility, the initial manifestation within the next fourteen years will be a monumental political transformation." Concurrently, religious scholars and adherents will inherently begin to undergo a paradigm shift. These religious adherents, having transformed, will be compelled to transition into a realm where they actively seek out genuine virtue. Precisely when politicians, consumed by internecine conflicts, begin to discard their ideologies as futile, a new generation will arise, actively seeking genuine rectitude. This resurgence will emanate from the foundational grassroots level. This phenomenon will subsequently catalyze a global transformation. It is prayer and profound intention (*Sankalpa*) that prepare us for this impending reality. We shall be able to witness firsthand how that prayer comes to fruition here. By spiritually grasping your ancestor and invoking them to this plane, they ascend towards the Divine, propelled by the very prayer you offer from your position. Upon their return, the divine grace permeates into your karmic realm. This process will systematically agitate the afflictions deeply rooted in your life force, fundamentally altering your very character, and gradually, albeit slowly, transform everything. However, this process necessitates a minimum gestation period of twelve years. Occasionally, it may demand the entirety of your lifetime. In the interim, we will be obligated to endure multifarious hardships, adversities, and sacrifices. Do not harbor any fear towards these tribulations. One must inevitably endure slight hardships prior to the manifestation of profound goodness. We must cultivate an unshakeable resolve that states: "Irrespective of the circumstances, I am prepared to endure anything for the sake of my future lineage. I shall make any requisite sacrifice to nurture and elevate my succeeding generation. I require neither personal abode nor homeland, but my lineage must be unequivocally secure."

[GV1:P1466] That is why the story of Bhagiratha was mentioned earlier. He threw away the rule of the kingdom and went to perform penance. Agastya also did the same to purify his ancestors (pitru). When we consider the hardships and sacrifices these figures endured to purify their ancestors, the path God has given us is much simpler and greater. This is not about acquiring supernatural powers (siddhi) or imparting profound knowledge. Yet, it is the very essence of knowledge. There is no other path for anyone on earth. Even after receiving such a noble path, instead of internalizing and practicing it properly, you are always preoccupied with thoughts of whether the husband is qualified, whether the wife is qualified, or whether there is wealth. Whatever that may be, we must fervently pray and embrace this significant opportunity we have received. You must pray with such determination, envisioning that your prayer is capable of wiping away the vileness of this world. Thus, your prayers will provide great support and shelter to this world.

[GV1:P1467] All of that can be seen by your children and by yourselves—those who have the vision to see. Today, there are no less than fifty such individuals in this lineage. If one of our children attains spiritual vision, even the Holy Trinity (Trimurti) would bow before them before departing. That is its state. Narayana Guru Swami said exactly this regarding it—at the pinnacle of philosophical inquiry, one sees everything as one.

[GV1:P1468] Everything we see is the light of this oneness. Therefore, such a supreme thing is what we have obtained.

[GV1:P1469] Here, the Shudras have not had an institution until today. Shudras outside have had them. Both the Prophet and Jesus were Shudras. The term Shudra refers to the Shudra among the four varnas. Thus, the Shudras outside prospered. Those here have not been able to survive. You are standing under the Chandal-like Brahmins. In Kali Yuga, the Brahmin is a Chandala. Brahmins who neither drink alcohol nor lie are rare today. If you look in the Palakkad district, houses among them without insanity and leprosy are rare. They have been rendered Chandalas in that manner. However, God has bestowed prosperity upon the Shudras. Within 25 years, the community affectionately called 'Harijans' by Gandhiji in recent times will develop a culture greater than yours. The Ayyankali Memorial in Kerala is an example of this. The absence of arrogance in them was the foundation of their growth. Although a little arrogance has crept into them today, God will tolerate it. This is because it happened out of their ignorance, not out of defiance. But today, God has already given them a position.

[GV1:P1470] However, there are two groups who refuse to agree to anything. They are the Ezhavas and the Nairs. Generally, the Ezhavas display arrogance and foolishness. Even today, they have the habit of having a full drink poured and saying, 'My Swami, just a little more.' The Nairs are not like that. If you go to Neyyattinkara and observe, even if they have nothing to eat, they will wrap a towel around their neck and walk with regal pomp. You have degenerated to this level. There is no visible effort among you to change all this. It is not that you need asceticism (sannyasa), but rather the capability to live. The first step towards that is the purification of the ancestors. It is because of the lack of this that you put on all these acts. When children are born while the ancestral spirits are astray, they will be inclined to do even worse.

[GV1:P1471] This is a lineage empowered by God to rectify this. Therefore, celibates (brahmacharis) are necessary to lead this. Otherwise, the institution will not survive as it grows. You may speak grandly. But if you do not like it, you will step out and say something else. Will the descendants of such people look after the institution later? Then will the institution you built survive? This is why it is said that the institution of the Shudra does not endure. They build many institutions. The Shudra lacks the diplomacy for this. Rights and wrongs will occur. One must work through them instead of dismissing them with blame.

[GV1:P1472] Have you not seen 'Sivagiri' now? What terrible things are being said from the outside. But see if there is anyone willing to work for it for 4 days. They will only go if there is profit. Isn't Chempazhanthy located nearby? How many people look after it? Other than strutting around and conducting festivals just to ensure 'let our names also be included,' what else do you have? Chenkottukonam is being built up, isn't it? What do you have to maintain there traditionally? However, at Abhedananda Ashram, you have formed a committee in such a way that it is impossible for him to stay there. Today, he merely comes and goes occasionally. Will anyone other than someone with a slight caste feeling remain among you?

[GV1:P1473] You must not bring this lineage to that state. No one should mock the lineage you bring forth. Rights and wrongs will occur within it. You, who are householders, must try to resolve them yourselves. This is your time.

[GV1:P1474] If you do not respect and uphold this, it will slip from your hands. Then some natural disaster or something else will come and destroy everything. Do not allow that to happen. Our efforts must be directed towards preventing that.

[GV1:P1475] Guruvani
