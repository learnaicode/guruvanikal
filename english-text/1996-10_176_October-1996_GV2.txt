GURUVANIKAL · October 1996 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P1072 to GV2:P1084
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

October 1996

What should faith be for a human being? There are many occult powers and sleight-of-hand tricks. Believing in any of these is not faith in God at all. Should God show occult powers before us? Should God immediately give money to someone who prays and then discard them? We can take many such examples. The virtues of our women are within us. Looking at it that way, father, mother, children, and everything else are within us. Everyone is seeking condition after condition in faith. How can we change this? How can we hold on to the truth in this action? We, who can see it through experience, know what it is. If you take great souls as evidence, you will understand a small thing. Gandhiji himself is an example. What happened to Gandhiji, who tried to instill national consciousness? National consciousness cannot be instilled just like that. Why? As long as we do not see whether there is an industrial connection behind it, that national consciousness is inadequate.

What can we do when the dynamics travel inside and outside us and get destroyed in such a way that one person cannot adapt to the situation of another person or country among us? We can do nothing. How can you, who speak and act from the midst of various kinds of ignorance, correct yourselves? This is what you should think about. A house cannot be run without the involvement of husband, wife, children, father, and mother. We should know that there is a danger zone with us that these children cannot be protected. Whatever antics the father and mother show at home, the children will copy them. This situation has spread to the neighborhood and the whole world.

Faith will save. Again, what is faith? If any offerings or vows are offered in the name of faith, it is attached to our life, mixed with merit and sin, is it sufficient to remove the negative aspects of action? Neither mantras, nor tantras, nor knowledge conditions match our life. You must understand that this is purely a birth quality that we had when we were born. We must fully accept it and act upon it. There is no point in father, mother, husband, or wife saying this. This is absolutely true. Then they don't think about any of this. Don't they have education, wealth, and ability? They do.

The Mughals came and ruled this country. The Christians came and acted in such a way as to make people believe that they are the original form of this world. Can anyone find fault with the Hindus for claiming the greatest antiquity by saying that they are the descendants of Manu?

Manusmriti belongs to the world of the Guru. What is the reason that we, who rose up carrying the Manusmriti of the world itself, go nowhere? Manusmriti is the knowledge of the world itself. If you study and study up to a great encyclopedia, up to an encyclopedia of all knowledge, should you think that you know everything? As long as you, who stand on the side of knowledge, do not know that knowledge is the knowledge of the dynamics of life, you cannot do anything. It is not enough just to know; one must be able to go as far back as possible and acquire knowledge. Another thing, if we mention all the things of many siddhas and prophets who have worked at the forefront and settled down, it does not mean that we have swallowed all that knowledge. There is no point in any of this. There is no point in playing with the meanings of vowels and consonants combined with rhythm and beats, if needed, to speak like a storyteller and enjoy standing aside.

This is not what I am telling you. I haven't even gone to study like that. I am not speaking about something I have studied. But it is also something I have studied. That is the only way I can tell you. That is, I can only give you an explanation of this. I cannot even point to a single house to explain this subject completely. Because you have not understood it that way. So this untouched knowledge, but in Narayana Guru's language, or in Advaita language, touch and know. Touch and know one by one. If you spend your whole life seeing, knowing, tasting, and acting upon every tree and plant, how will you study humans? Therefore, you cannot study and complete anything. There is no point in going like that.

What is belief? How many churches do those who believe in Jesus have? As how many churches are they operating in the world? What is the situation in the Islamic tradition where one had to ask if Jinn rule the world? Why do we have this fight among us? Are they senseless? They have sense. But the misfortune of not being able to tolerate and forgive has gripped them. In reality, there is no caste. Then what is there, Varna. In this Varna caste, they finished writing stories without knowing where Varna is mentioned and went after Puranas. These Puranas, in Tamil language, mean old things.

The karma of old tendencies has come to each of us as a body containing the movements of acquired merit and sin. This is destiny. No one else can say anything about this. When we try to implement this destiny, our relatives, our mother, our wife, nothing will align. Or if they align, then the house will become useless. Understanding this path, everyone is slowly stepping back.

What will we do with them? The cause of this trouble stands with the world. It is impossible to measure and settle the right and wrong that comes to us in connection with this matter. And no one should think that it can be measured and settled. Then, there is a knowledge that belongs to human creation. But it has no closeness to our life. It is just like seeking a way to live a mere life. Like a merchant, a spinner, a weaver - like many things.

But a person cannot do anything by learning all these jobs together. Learn and teach according to aptitude. Whether female or male, teach according to aptitude. That is right. When teaching this aptitude, money and capacity increase for our home and land, and it becomes useless only for the home. Everything else is fine. Thus every house is being destroyed. What can you do for a house that is being destroyed like this? You must be careful about the situation that may arise when holding onto belief tightly. Because you are the ones who lived fighting, divided by caste, religion, and class.

If you ask anyone, no one has a caste. If there is no caste, then why behave like this? Another thing is that those who speak big things have no answer about what they say. Thus you are growing as the heir of the kingdom of heaven who has found the complete decay of a bad life in every way. How can this be changed? It cannot be changed. Then what you can do is hold onto one thing tightly. That's all.

Guruvani
