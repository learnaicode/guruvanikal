GURUVANIKAL · April 1998 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P1369 to GV2:P1385
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

April 1998

There may be someone with a certain kind of personality serving with us. If there is a dark side to their character, we should recognize it and try to correct it. But if it cannot be corrected in any way, then do not try to correct them. Because they will never be corrected. The deepest part of their mind is, as some theorists or ascetics say - the darkness inside that theory.

There are many kinds of religions in the name of religion. None of this is our subject. The respect of love, the strictness of humility, the survival of dharma - this is our religion. In it, virtue increases and one does not see two instead of one. God is giving you an opportunity for this. You should not lose it. Your merit is the effort to bring it to the people in whatever way possible. Otherwise, none of you should desire to be smart, or to see others as small or big through it. Let whatever comes in us come. If all of you work together in this knowledge, within five years that goodness will return to you in such a way that you cannot even determine where and what you did. That will be useful to you forever. We should have a concept that we should be able to introduce our traditional knowledge in that useful karma.

This subject is a new knowledge as far as we are concerned. That is because we have another knowledge that we have worshipped and learned. That knowledge belongs to the Trinity system, or Shaktheyam, Shaivam, Vaishnavam. In this, each theorist thinks his own is great. If none of this, the varna dharmas of Shudra, Vaishya, Kshatriya and Brahmin can be taught. Other than teaching all this, we have never been taught worship according to the times.

What is time? Krita Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dwapara Yuga, Kali Yuga. This is heard as Kali. While all other yugas have their own dharma, Kali also has its own dharma. What have we been taught about the dharma of this Kali? Kali is bad. We were born, grew up and will die in this Kali. If so, can we live after Kali is over? Another thing, can we leave the dharma of Kali? Then which dharma should we take? We were never taught this. Isn't it because this was not taught that Kali became bad?

What is the dharma of Kali? Kali is Shudra Kal. What is our understanding of Shudra? The one in the last quarter, the one in the fourth quarter. Kali Yuga is the time when the one in the fourth quarter, who was denied knowledge, gets knowledge. That is, the dharma of Kali is to pave the way for the Shudra to attain Brahma Jnana. That doesn't mean others won't get Brahma Jnana. We - whoever is born - belong to the dharma of the Shudra varna. Even though the Shudra is said to have the right to Brahma Jnana, Kali is a time when all wise men, all theorists, and all evil-doers have the right to liberation. Still, what is the reason for specifically mentioning only the Shudra? In other yugas, the Shudra was not even given the right to perform karma. That knowledge somehow came about. One without purity of karma cannot practice dharma. To practice dharma and reach the attainment of virtue in that dharma, some reason

A Shudra has not been given any knowledge to reach the truth. This concept of Dharma is very flawed. Thus, we have become unable to recognize the Dharma of the times. We have a knowledge based on worshiping, loving, and believing according to what we were told. It is in this knowledge that Kali became bad and the Dharma of Kali disappeared.

Every human being is born, grows, and dies. This is the law of the earth itself. To know this as a principle and act upon it, and that too for the people to act together, no dress, language, or knowledge has been given. Getting it is also impossible in this systemic order. This shortcoming is not just for the Shudra and the Vaishya - the Kshatriya and the Brahmin could not take up their respective Dharma either.

What are the rights of Kshatriya Dharma? What has this Kshatriya, who has returned as a Yogabhrashta, done? We have been spun around in mischief without knowing how to ask any of this. Just as we have been spun around in mischief, even the virtuous are spinning. When we say virtuous, it means the one who is entitled to Brahmajnana. It is said that even he has been destroyed.

This is a difficult subject to understand. As far as we are concerned, even if we study for our entire lives, it is difficult to understand this. For example, what is the condition of Sree Rama? What do we know about Sree Rama, who passed away more than seventeen lakh years ago? We are celebrating what we heard someone say. The story has been written and completed in such a way that we cannot even think about what Sree Rama is or what Sree Rama's end was.

Why did Sree Rama go down to the river? A story has been written for that too. This is something that happened in this Chaturyuga itself. Sree Rama is a man of the Treta Yuga. What Sree Rama could not do, no one has been able to do to this day. We have not received any different knowledge for that. No one has even thought about why those who came after him could not do it.

This mistake did not happen recently. This fourth quarter of this Chaturyuga has only passed five thousand years. Moreover, how can we understand the magnitude of this mistake while staying within the smallest language in Kerala, the smallest land? Will these South Indians themselves understand? If we take South India as a whole, there is a Karma that has been written and completed up to the Dakshinamurthy Stotram. The Dakshinamurthy Stotram is only for South Indians. North Indians do not have it. How can we take a knowledge beyond these periods of time with the knowledge in these Karmas that have been written and kept like this? Along with this, many Upanishads, Vedas, Shastras - coming like this, all these have become intertwined into a knot that can never be untied, in such a way that none of them can be sustained. What should we do with this, in what way should we take it? This is our subject. This is not a subject only for the Brahmin, the Kshatriya, or the Chandala. It is said that Sree Rama's culture is being developed in South India. What is the point of bringing a culture from seventeen lakh years ago here? Why did Sree Rama go down to the river? Our scientific truths, special stories, Vedas, and Puranas and Epics are all a helpless acquaintance where we cannot ask and know any of this.

Let us all think together whether we should change from this or not. Even if we think, what are we going to get? Who can do this? Can we go beyond that without liberating Sree Rama? Thus, there is a need to liberate all the Gurus who have come to this day.

In recent times, many gurus have come and gone.

What is the real historical truth? We are seeing the time difference that comes as the earth, moon, and sun revolve. The karma that occurs in this time difference is always happening. How long has it been revolving like this? What will it be like when this Yugadharma - the nature of the passing of time - comes? We have never understood this. What is the point of us talking about sciences, puranas, and Vedic epics without understanding this? When we act directly according to Yugadharma, what will we do with our ancestral guru paths, prophetic paths, and theoretical paths? We cannot proceed without taking up these karmas. Can we just say that none of this exists and move on? For example, we all know about the twenty-seven stars. But we can see billions of stars in a single orbit. How did this happen? Which science can explain it? Other than making us believe by calling it a galaxy, no science is clear on how these stars came to be. What we need to understand is that all these are great souls. Because these great souls did not get the right to Jivanmukti (liberation), they themselves became yogis and stood there. How can one cross them and go there? It is impossible to cross over in any way.

Everything is playing within the horizon of the twenty-seven stars. But there are billions of stars situated inside this solar system. How can a soul cross all these stars? Be it a ghost, a god, or anything else, that is not an issue at all. The tragedy is the inability to cross this trap.

As far as we are concerned, this is clear. If those who have done Guru Pooja here are told that they should only do this, no one will understand this capacity for action. There is no point in you coming here without understanding this. Even standing here is of no use. You should not lose this experience anywhere. Then I will not be able to do anything. You have not received this experience by being sanyasis. This is something not seen anywhere else in the world. Anyone can show any amount of Siddhi (mystic powers). But you will not be able to see this capacity for action anywhere. This is not my greatness or your greatness. God has given you the destined karma.

Guruvani
