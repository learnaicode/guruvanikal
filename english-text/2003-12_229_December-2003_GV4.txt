[GV4:P0957] December 2003

[GV4:P0958] Now, how should we take it, this is the perspective of that renouncer. How do we know that renouncer? Is it enough to see it spreading and flourishing? No. What we see is the karmic cause of its gross form. We must see how the subtle exists after completing this gross form. A karmic potential like that is a truth that remains only in the Guru of the ages. Passing this through you, you must understand an address from you in the part called father and mother. Mother, Father, Guru. Then it is said that through the knowledge in the mother, these children know what the paternal world is. Thus, having found the maternal and paternal positions, the premature deaths of that maternal lineage and the path reached thereafter. The father's too is exactly the same. After both of them have reached, that is where the part that says *Mathru Devo Bhava* must conclude. Where does it manifest and stand. Likewise, where does the deity stand. Moving from there, it is of no use saying the karma that has passed through this aeon, calling it the Guru. That is not what we need to search for, but to achieve. Achieving that should be along with the household. Otherwise, it will be like the sannyasa that comes in old age, leaving the house. That will never be right. This should not come in old age. What knowledge should a mother have before a child is born. Along with that, this father too. It is they who grow and bring this child to the Guru. So, where does the maternal nature reach, and along with it, the deities of worship that went through whichever paths of the father, do they stand in the imagination of a sage, in the imagination of a Guru, or in a simple imagination - this is an explanation. That is the vision. So, that Guru's imagination reaches everything there, and this mother, father, and Guru - these three to the disciple. That fourth imagination of God should be obtained formless. Now, where we speak of such a God and such a God, that form must be shaped by this Guru. That is the greatness of the Guru. So, this is not gross concerning you. You should not assume so. Now, among those who have come with me, there are at least six or seven people to take, to say that this is the truth. Not in the sense that they can say it exhaustively. Such truth should be taken up with our life, undertaking all the mistakes that have come into our world, and acted upon. Wearing this with determination and humility, performing karma, bringing it to virtue, that virtue must be brought to fortune. What fortune is that? The wealthy man has a fortune. There is a fortune in stating the law with this yoga. Raja Yoga is where all yogas are combined without any specific yoga. What is called Raja Yoga is not something practiced. We should not assume so. Practicing is not a Raja Yoga at all. It is a knowledge that is practiced through experience. That is what I said, what is the primordial nature of the mother and the father? What is it? Whether it is the path of the Guru, the path of the deity, or the path of the sage - the Guru shows how it has arrived and where it stands. So, in which Kali Yugas have I myself been born? How long have the Guru and disciple come and gone? Thus, how long have the god and goddess come and gone? Merely as a worm and an insect? A termite or an ant performing penance? It did that for light, but it did it, and it bore fruit. What is it? As soon as it sprouts wings and sees the light, it will run, fall there, and die. Is a light like that enough? This is what is called morphological transformation. The karma remaining in the finality of that form, remaining at the end, must be seen from there. That is a path. Then, the egg and the worm changing, thus all the eggs changing and reaching an animal-like state, how did this Guru achieve the special differences in karma that these animals exhaustively count? This is its summary. Otherwise, if you dismiss it saying that these are not things to be taken up, nothing can be taken up. This disciple must see how this Guru achieved it. It will not happen just if the Guru says it. Then, where the Guru and disciple remain, this maternal state, paternal state ڊ both having arrived, how does the Guru stand? In all of this, what does the Guru stand imagining? How does the Guru transform this into a consciousness of transition? This is the subject. Otherwise, a lot of visions come countlessly, and therefore, most people fall into the karmic flaw of counting it as bad. But do not go like that. Without going like that, we have a side. What is it? The path of the Guru.

[GV4:P0959] But what should this path be? This Guru should be a disciple whom every sage and every deity worships, having attained the inner nature of this path. However long one carries such discipleship, that burden is eventually removed and shared between the Guru and the disciple. After sharing that, the next step is to unite with this Guru. Upon uniting with the Guru, the disciple must ascend yet again and stand even further ahead; they must not remain below. This is the greatness of the Guru-disciple relationship. Therefore, it requires a disciple who has grown to such a level to reveal the Guru. Otherwise, merely proclaiming it to the whole world, elaborating and attempting to perfect it, will not enable us to fulfill it. This is what I have to tell you now. You might mistakenly believe that you can ascend through various Vedantas or reach the ultimate end of the Vedas, but you will not attain it thus. That is not how it works. Growing through experiential realization in the roles of father, mother, children, and wife—in the karma of *Matru Devo Bhava* (revere the mother as God)—the extent to which we strive to sift through and resolve the past births of our mother is the duty a son or daughter must genuinely perform. It is not merely about providing rice, buttermilk, milk, and ghee; they will get those if they have money. If there is no money, they will not, and let that be. Understanding that cultivating this state is the true essence of a Guru, this Guru completely transforms the disciple into an incarnate being (*avatarapurusha*). When speaking of this incarnate being, it is not an avatar of a single era that is required. Which Manu, or how the role of the seventh Manu—Vaivasvata Manu—has been enacted and concluded, and where it presently stands, constitutes the true quest. Only by inquiring in this manner can you move forward. You must fulfill the dharma of Kali, which encompasses all good and evil, to reflect on where you stand in the karma that you understand, desire, and execute. That would be best for all of us. It is not for everyone to merely search for it; we must genuinely seek it, perfect it, and rather than just passing it to the next generation or the one after, we must integrate into the Satya Yuga along with our children and our children's children. I am telling you this with the sole desire that you become capable of a karma that helps everyone achieve this mindfully.

[GV4:P0960] Guruvani
