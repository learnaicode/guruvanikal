[GV3:P0753] November 1999

[GV3:P0754] So it is meant that there should be a disciple for us to directly see and know these things. For that, there were two children who grew up here like this. I got all this done by them. It was Sree Krishna coming and standing and doing it; making them do it. After doing it like that, I was again told to keep quiet for two and a half years. Kept quiet. Later, this was started and brought to today's state. What is called today's state is nothing else. To say it, much has to be said. What is called today's state is the dharma of Kali. Because we were made to believe that this Kali dharma is bad, we kept it in some way - except that some Puranas say something - but it won't stand under them. That will just go away like that.

[GV3:P0755] If asked how this came about, when a great soul looked whether his karma itself is complete, he saw it like a completion. It is a mistake that happened then (the usage of the Chaturvakya and the subsequent events). It's not that he wished to do wrong. The truth we received is that twenty-five Chaturyugas have passed since that mistake. No one among us can slip away and escape from that truth. The meaning is that many Kalis, that is Kaliyugas, twenty-five Kaliyugas, have come. Out of that, the twenty-fifth Kaliyuga is the current one. Through that Kaliyuga, despite bringing many people, a change could not be brought to this. Though these gurus were brought by giving some teachings and knowledge, they went up to the state of Trimurti, spoke, and stood there. Only the matter of light was spoken above that. All the Upanishads say only the same. Wherever you look, it's the same.

[GV3:P0756] What is this light? What is the nature of this light? What kind is it? Which color? How many colors will one color change into? This will move forward only if we get to know all this. Other than that, siddhi magics or other types of things are not for us to maintain. Nobody should hold such thoughts. Due to some holding such thoughts, a huge mistake occurred even among our people. What do they see? Not one deity or a thousand gods or goddesses. Considering why what was achieved with one deity cannot be achieved with millions, they tried to handle it that way. But it was lost. They could not do anything. It is then they understand that it is something that shouldn't be done like that. What we have heard and known are some things from the stories of deities. None of these stories reach the presence of God perfectly. It will only reach somewhere else.

[GV3:P0757] Now, this is briefly noted down for you to understand. There are only a few brief words of this in this book. It is not easy to fill this by writing. If needed, some children who have to be born in a great divine conception must come and write this. Otherwise, the educated people of today cannot write it. The reason is what they learned and taught is the job of speaking the wonder of this by studying it in various transformed states like Pandita Mahopadhyaya or Pandita Siromani, through the Sanskrit aspect repeated since the form of siddha seen till today. And not their experience. So it is said like this and twenty-five Chaturyugas have passed. This is its truth. For you to get this from this, some people who will become your children will come. That is what you must watch out for. How? For each of these, people will come to any house belonging to this Guru lineage. That is its lineage. After getting married, a thing you don't hear about is happening here now. After getting married, without sending them home - some stand for seven days; there are some who stood for a month. Let it be however. When they are seated in a sankalpa, they themselves see their deity of worship and the things they believed in. From then on, an achievement is happening for them. That achievement is arriving in their karmic path of origin. It is coming back as your children. This is its subject. Most people won't even be able to understand it. It is God's will that this should come in your children themselves. It is not I who have to do it. So you must behave worthily and take it. It is a path shown by God. You will have to accept many things to keep this path.

[GV3:P0758] What is our order of life? One, employment opportunities set apart as good and what can cause harm in it. How should this be, how should you do it and take it? Similarly, education, you cannot determine how long it will go forward. There is such a state for this. Times have changed. Now this caste cannot bring a karma. There will be changes in education and corresponding employment sectors. That will be coming within a few hundred years. It is not the way we can see today. So that too won't happen unless it comes through your children. That is why, the householder is not to take sannyasa, but it is the householder who lets out various aspects of sannyasa, or considers and implements and makes it work. That householder life sits in you. Through such children of yours, the path of travel of the world is being shown. Rather, there is no magic trick or special siddhi in this - or a nature of blessing and cursing - nothing of this is in this. You will be able to know what that blessing and cursing is. As proof for that, there are people sitting in this group now, among you men and women who are householders, who have seen Rishis, Tyagis, Sannyasins, and Devas up to this Trimurti status. But to go above that is a different way. You can understand that and achieve it only with this karma. This mentioned householder system is the opening of the door of a truth to make your children work forever to implement this dharma. Otherwise, it cannot come through a sannyasin. The sannyasin is not the one to bring it. Many people acted out all such aspects of sannyasa and did it for all this time. But none of that is the lineage of sannyasa. You have thought about all this. By hearing it told, you have acquired an ignorance making you believe that one who attained God-realization doesn't even need water, are people who don't even need dry leaves. All that is wrong, and wrong on top of wrong. We cannot even ****************** what wickedness the karma adopted to avoid needing this water is. This started with Tulsi leaves and reached up to Kanjira leaves. Similarly, it reached all bitter roots and again taking its fruits and purifying it, eating it and then making siddha medicine and eating it, and after that, building a cave with nine faces and inside it. Then this person has to be shifted - shifted here and there - and kept. Cannot get up and move. All bones will become like cotton. Then cannot lift up. Therefore, he is being picked up and shifted. Thus the cave with nine faces is being shifted day and night. This theory speaks of an individual growing like this. What is it? Yoga knowledge, or various such sleight of hand - this is all it is. You have misunderstood that this is sannyasa. None of this is sannyasa at all. Nor is it any karma from taking sannyasa. If you think so, it's a mistake. Till today, our posture of each one is by looking at these swamis who show these wonders. It is something that shouldn't be shown. Go truthfully, let what has to come, come. Let what doesn't have to come, not come.

[GV3:P0759] In this one karma, you firmly have been gifted by God a great karmic siddhi to give to the world through your children, through your generations. You must get this done. For this, there are those who organize in whatever ways in these lands and wander around blabbering what they consider correct based on their own intellect and nonsense they caught onto by talking too much. None of that is ever correct for this lineage or this goodness that is going to come. Till today, whatever karmic flaws are there, we are to take all of them. So when we step down for it, we will get various kinds of sorrow, difficulty, trouble, lack of love, everything. Taking all that upon ourselves, as warriors of this karma, as a woman being the mother of the world, it is a true path given with the facility to work and settle it. This karma is an item gifted for you and me by God to give to the world without making mistakes. It must be guarded.

[GV3:P0760] Guru Vani
