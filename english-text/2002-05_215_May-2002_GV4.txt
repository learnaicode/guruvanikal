[GV4:P0024] May 2002

[GV4:P0025] To bring even the smallest matters to perfection, one must traverse through numerous sorrows. Our ancient history is an acquaintance with such an experience. When we say 'ours', it is not just mine or yours, nor is it merely that of our ancestors. We are trapped in a lack of functional capacity, having completely missed the knowledge that has been evolving over millions of years. Numerous Gurus and prophets have come and gone over time in an attempt to rectify this. However, there was a lack of knowledge and experience to understand or impart this. This is the predicament that has afflicted us.

[GV4:P0026] There are many people who say that we have made some mistake. But if asked what this mistake is, they do not know how to answer. Where did the mistake occur, and how did it happen—this is what remains unknown. The truth is that neither our ancestors, who were theoreticians of karma, nor the preceptors and prophets who came to clear ideological confusions, were able to identify where the mistake occurred. As far as these preceptors are concerned, and consequently for us as well, this remains a completely unfamiliar action. We have drifted away in this ignorance. Therefore, we were unable to embrace Dharma. Dharma must be adopted with humility. Instead of doing so, we have not practiced humility beyond mere lip service. This lack of humility is experienced in various forms. One is caste. Another is religion and class. How did these castes, religions, and classes originate, and when did they emerge? How did the circumstances arise for them to constantly change in this manner?

[GV4:P0027] Theoreticians belonging to the Trimurti tradition posited ten incarnations. Apart from those, there have been many other incarnations. Now, incarnations are appearing countless in number. If incarnations appear in such immeasurable numbers, which one will we accept? That is one problem. Then, there are some who are characterized as followers of prophetic religions and guru-centric religions. But what is the greatness of time? What is the belief appropriate for the times? We lack the knowledge to understand this, elucidate it from those lineages, and move forward. This error did not occur just today or yesterday. We must investigate at least from the time of Lord Krishna to understand and act upon this predicament. Through Kamsa, we can understand that the divine will had a prior preparation for the arrival of Lord Krishna. Since the arrival of Krishna to this day, we have never been ready to respect and understand that history or to take up any aspect of that hardship and work selflessly. Following Lord Krishna, numerous Gurus have appeared all over the world.

[GV4:P0028] Science has today provided us with the knowledge that, similar to our concept of this globe, there are countless planetary bodies in this space. It is a limitation that once a piece of knowledge is discovered, it stagnates at the point of that knowledge. We do not contemplate how it occurred while standing upon the acquired knowledge. Now, each of you who has studied English considers Ptolemy to be one of the greatest thinkers. His disciples have also followed the very same path. However, the tendency to stagnate at the point of discovered knowledge can be observed here as well.

[GV4:P0029] We cannot succeed by incorporating an estimation of two thousand, four thousand, or five thousand years into the life of a great soul. Even mentioning four or five million years will not suffice. We must account for at least a Chaturyuga as a minimum. A Chaturyuga encompasses a span of over 4.3 million years. That itself is categorized into various Dharmas. However, we know nothing about it. If we proceed with the calculations formulated by the English or the Trimurti tradition, we will have nothing to convey to the coming generations.

[GV4:P0030] To grasp the magnitude of this error, we need to observe the emergence of many thousands of great souls. It is reported that two thousand four hundred and forty-four Gurus have arrived since the commencement of this Kali Yuga itself. If this many people have come within a mere five thousand years, how many millions must have appeared within this Chaturyuga? This remains a massive question mark for us. Only if we confront this question somewhere and seize some opportunity can we manage it.

[GV4:P0031] The only ones we can take as a foundation are the great souls. Among these great souls themselves, we must know whose history and records will provide a favorable environment for our inquiry. First, we must acquire an understanding of the Gurus who have evolved within the Trimurti tradition up to this day, as well as the trajectory of karma that has been born, nurtured, and perished within that lineage. However, there is a fundamental obstacle to accepting this. That obstacle is what the Trimurti tradition says about Swayambhuva Manu. We must understand that Swayambhuva Manu, true to his name, was self-created or originated in a self-born karmic process. But he has been arbitrarily linked as the son of Brahma. This is where the previously mentioned limitation arises. There is no method anywhere that details what must be done to eliminate this limitation.

[GV4:P0032] Our ancestors speak about Lord Rama. Or they speak of the history that has occurred within this Chaturyuga. But let us take a glimpse into the final days of all these great souls. This will cause us pain. God has given us the opportunity to witness the boundaries of the lives of numerous great souls. For this opportunity to reach you, it must stem from a center of faith. When we say a center of faith, it must be a place that recognizes what the Trimurti tradition and the Manu tradition are. The path to discover and rectify this is to begin from wherever the Guru lineage, the Manu lineage, or the Deva lineage extends and culminates. Instead, tying it to the Trimurti is the mistake that has befallen all the great souls.

[GV4:P0033] To explain how this initial mistake occurred, much would have to be said. It is not necessary here. We only need to know from that fact about Sree Krishna which we failed to understand. Sree Krishna was born in the Yadava clan. What is the karma that determined that this great man, who worked for the people, would be born at a specific occasion before his birth? What did it ultimately become even after he was born? We have never thought about this. Why? We have not even considered that it happened that way. Until today, the only action that has taken place is reducing everything to Vedanta through constant repetition, lingering on the phonetic sweetness of the letters, and ultimately tearing it apart through interpretation. This is what has been preserved as Vedanta. We cannot prove the truth from this Vedanta.

[GV4:P0034] Some speak citing Ramarajya as a model. In what manner have we perceived that great man who suffered so much, bringing him to the forefront? There is no one to clearly explain Rama's stance to us. If we consider all the people who have come since Sri Rama until today as a whole, we will understand that they are not the ones to be adopted in the dharma of this era.

[GV4:P0035] What currently exists is the twenty-eighth Chaturyuga of Vaivasvata Manu. Where did things go wrong in the meantime? Until today, efforts have been made to rectify what went wrong. Where the error occurred is exactly what writers and thinkers have failed to find despite their search. What is thus unattainable is beyond thought. How can that which is beyond thought be attained?

[GV4:P0036] If one envisions the solar system by standing in the karma of a visionary, the subsequent knowledge is whether the solar system is subject to the Manvantaras or something else. It is this knowledge that should rectify you. Such knowledge cannot be instantly transferred to us. It can only be imparted very gradually. For this, God should not be reduced to a mere sleight of hand. We must come forward to reclaim and observe the eras in which dharma could be practiced through life, and having seen them, rectify the mistakes within them and put them into practice. To undertake this, one must observe the course of karma that exists in four states: gross, subtle, causal, and effect. Then, one must also observe when dharma should emerge. Since it is a dharma of the time, the dharma of Kali must be examined specifically. One must observe what Kali is about, in whom the dharma to be undertaken according to the familiarity of Kali is vested, and how far that person has reached, or where they stand. The person implementing the Yuga Dharma must know all of this.

[GV4:P0037] What guidance should that person provide for us? Regardless of whether one is a demon, a deva, a sage, or an ascetic, they must advise on what error has occurred in the course of dharma up to this day, and what must be done to correct that error. There is no point in advising anything else. Whatever is said about the theory of Yoga, the theory of Karma, or the theory of Knowledge, none of it is something an individual can easily step into. Then what is that karma? It is observing the course of karma that has passed, grasping that karma, and correcting it. That karma is worthy enough for even your children to be accepted as a contribution to the world. There is no point in saying here that I should be exempted, that I am a liberationist, a rationalist, a scholar, a mute, or someone devoid of any knowledge, or an ignoramus. All these merely fall within the realm of your verbal rhetoric. No one should try to win by saying such things.

[GV4:P0038] We must remember the tragic predicament we have fallen into here. By talking about rationalism and liberationism, we are deviating from the course of dharma and from the thought of what all harmonizes within dharma. When this happens, there will be a deficiency in the amount of knowledge required to understand each era. Then, the karma written and preserved by scientists and Vedantins will occupy that space. This is an obstacle on the path to truth.

[GV4:P0039] Truth is none of these; it exists within each of us. The fire of the five elements is within us. There is fire in everything in the world. In the realm of knowledge where the five elements are blended, the growth of the life force is at a standstill. One must know what has happened to it and in which knot the life force is entangled. Knowing this is the hallmark of a visionary.

[GV4:P0040] How will this knot be untied? If the visionary is to untie that knot and give it to us, we must have the knowledge that we will receive everything through that visionary. What has happened in the past is foresightedness; if one waits idly thinking that everything will be obtained from it, it will not come. Such people will suffer the tragic fate of a life force devoid of vitality. This is not what I am telling you. In the course of karma where you grow as male and female, the karma of birth and death lingers in everything, even in a single atom. What is the karma that has operated and settled within this atom? Who must you become to reclaim this dharma? What should be the lineage of Gurus that has attained this abundance of merit, and what should the Guru be? You must see and realize this for yourselves. It cannot happen except through such a path of knowledge. This should not happen just in one or two children, not in one caste or religion, and not in any single species of living beings. This subject is about discovering the achieved realization of the course of life moving through all living beings. Through that achieved realization, where do we stand now? This should not be said by a person who has become deserving or qualified by seeing and experiencing it. You and your children are the ones who must say it after seeing it. Why would you say so? A vigilant nature has been given to me, which must be activated by comparing it with your past times, as well as the present and future times. Knowing what this waking state, dream state, and deep sleep state are, one must also observe what all yields in deep sleep. My birth and death have thus come into the course of karma, known, done, and caused to be done in this manner. This truth is not to be spoken to those who say truth does not exist, nor to those who say it does.

[GV4:P0041] In the course of dharma where the Earth, on which truth stands as the foundation, and similarly the eight planets stand together, how has this truth operated and settled? It is the Guru's obligation to show this. Only a disciple seen by a Guru should explain it to you. Others should not explain it. You must understand that this is the experiential aspect of truth. This is not a dharma mixed with intellect in the foolishness of stringing together fragments of Vedanta. It is not about the ignorance of the scholar or the scholarship of the ignorant.

[GV4:P0042] What is your capability as householders—who are to take up the responsibility of the future times having seen the three periods of time—to show how we can uphold dharma, and how will you take it up? Otherwise, this is not meant for an ascetic. Let the ascetic take it or not take it. There is nothing much to say about that. If the ascetic wants it, he will take it. Otherwise, he will discard it and leave. Let him do whatever. You need this. You and your children. Every one of you who is alive needs this.

[GV4:P0043] The path of the Guru is to know the predicament and the destiny of that soul, which repeatedly manifests with the good and the bad clinging to the righteous course of bygone times. I am speaking to you about such a path of the Guru. No one other than Sree Krishna has spoken about this path. Jesus speaks about this most beautifully. Vivekananda also speaks well about it. How will that become good through us? Vivekananda's conclusion is that the Devas convened a meeting in Devaloka against this. So what if they convened a meeting? Will this not disappear only if the Guru is someone who stays in Devaloka? If it is a Guru who resides in Devaloka, it will disappear. This is what has happened until today. This is being told to you now by placing such a condition before you. By growing through this condition, you must get any child you see. Otherwise, there is no salvation for you. I am not merely reciting this to you; rather, I am placing such a karma before you. Understand whether it is the truth or untruth by observing it, and learn to perform karma by fulfilling it. Also, see how charity and merit will reach us. Let us reflect on who we are in that humble karma where the merit of that charity has reached fortune. Each one should become a person for that. This is what I have to tell you.

[GV4:P0044] Guruvani
