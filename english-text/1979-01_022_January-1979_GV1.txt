[GV1:P0398] January 1979

[GV1:P0399] Question: Guru, please explain what the special benefit of the Pournami prayer is.

[GV1:P0400] Answer: Regarding its result, there is an outcome that cannot be diminished by anything else . Pournami is what fulfills the purity of intention of many births, acted upon knowingly and unknowingly. The Pournami fast provides the benefits of all other fasts. This is the day when observing all these fasts makes the ultimate Guru worthy of liberation . Similarly, it is a day that brings perfection to worldly life as well. Darkness does not spread even a little bit . On one side, there is a quality of joy and coolness, and on the other, the ability to conquer everything . In this coolness , seeds gain more vitality. The quality to generate and sustain it will also be attained.

[GV1:P0401] If one attains Samadhi on a full moon day after accumulating merit over numerous lifetimes, it is considered complete Samadhi. The atmosphere is also serene. As one sets, another rises. Wherever their rays fall, this virtue flows there as well. This vow assists the Guru in merging with God and dissolving into Brahman. The Pournami (full moon) prayer provides an opportunity for everyone to gather and pray collectively.

[GV1:P0402] The fundamental resolve is that, whether one contemplates its benefits or not, our prayers should enable the eradication of all our flaws and facilitate world peace by distinguishing between good and evil in the world, in a manner that benefits all sentient and insentient beings.

[GV1:P0403] A unique alignment occurs among the planets, through which a special energy is imparted for the welfare of the world. A genesis beneficial to the entire world may also occur on that day. This is an opportune time for both creation and dissolution. Just as this state can be envisioned in a mango blossom, it is understood to function similarly within human beings.
