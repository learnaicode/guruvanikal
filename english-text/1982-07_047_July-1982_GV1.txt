[GV1:P1201] July 1982

[GV1:P1202] If one grows according to what is seen while going there, he will be hurt in accordance with this growth. Because faults will be found in everything he does. How to correct that? He will be corrected by getting agitated. For another person, just a look is enough. The knowledgeable one will understand from that look. The next person will be called and corrected verbally. A fourth person will be corrected by demonstrating it. For the one next to him, he will not understand through a look, nor if called and told, nor if agitated, nor if demonstrated. That is why it is said that if those standing near the Guru are not highly knowledgeable, mistakes will happen. The Guru will not give him an opportunity to repeat a mistake once pointed out. That is the Guru-disciple relationship. Then, no matter what kind, they will never do it incorrectly. Those who come like this are very few. That is why it is said 'one or two who meet the criteria'. How will people know about such a lineage of Gurus? They lack the ability to understand this. Because their past lives are not suitable for it. Understanding this, there will be two to four knowledgeable people in one place. There won't be more than that. Those four people together will control a hundred thousand people. They are placed there to control those hundred thousand people. We must obey them too. Only by obeying like that can we carry forward this tradition. Otherwise, if one asks what does it matter to him or who am I, this tradition will not continue. Sometimes what is said might be wrong. Because when interfering in many matters, mistakes will occur. Sometimes one might speak without knowing the story. Or he might be speaking when many are standing around. Then, by obeying and after being corrected two or four times, both the doer and the one causing it to be done will be corrected. Both will achieve a great state in two ways. If the opportunity to correct is never given, this will not be possible. Wherever this disobedience exists, it is finished. That lineage can do nothing. All this cannot be learned right from birth. It will only happen if we practice it and suffer inch by inch. Taking such a perfect birth, what all these great souls have suffered. We must see all that.

[GV1:P1203] You have not had this kind of experience in the Guru-disciple relationship. Even if it is not your fault, you must learn all this by laying it out. A realization comes to mind that this should not be done, or said, or that it is not part of our humility, and we will move away from it. Thus, when we change ten times, there will be a turning point. When this happens a thousand times, didn't you get a hundred turning points? This is what we need in the Guru-disciple relationship. It is not that easy for us to absorb this. It can only be taken through our inclination, companionship, and that humility. This is our success. It is quite difficult to learn all this.

[GV1:P1204] We have a flaw of saying, "I am standing nearby, I have seen, I have heard, I know, I have gone there," and so on. It is not something known just by seeing, hearing, or visiting. It is a culture. As long as one cannot adapt to that culture, one remains at a lower level. We usually say, "I am going to my brother-in-law's house, husband's house, or wife's house." Similarly, we say, "I am going to the bhajan mutt, to the temple, or to the ashram." I have been thinking of going to the ashram for many days, and today I have decided to go. Before going, do we think: why should I go, what should I do when I get there, how should I spend that day, how should I live for a month after returning, and what should I be until I go back? Our thoughts should be like this: I was able to go to the ashram. With what purpose should I go there? What should I achieve within a month after returning from there? How should I maintain my daily routine after coming back? What is needed when returning? If we correct ourselves every day like this, an inseparable bond with it will unknowingly be formed.

[GV1:P1205] Otherwise, how would it be if it is like a dog going to the beach every day without thinking anything? There is nothing to be gained by going to a temple or an ashram like that. We must have a sense of purpose as to why we should go there. There are husbands who earn for their wives, wives who need a job, wives who do not need a job, some people who need education, wealth, and enlightenment, and those who need none of these; there are people of such various natures among both men and women. Take each of these and proceed by thinking with a sense of purpose: why am I earning, why am I working, why am I going to be a husband, why am I going to be a wife? There is no harm in that. If you do not learn all this, you will fall into a trap.

[GV1:P1206] With one birth, you can eliminate multiple births or continue multiple births. That is why it is said, "Vasanamayam jagat sarvam" (The whole world is full of latent tendencies). Therefore, in order for you to contain it, sustain it, and acquire worthiness, a familiarity is being entrusted to you. That is what you need today. Learn to remove what has passed, assimilate what is present, and discard it on the point of a needle. That is possible only while alive. He will instill his resolve in it and depart. The value of life can only be understood from Gurus who teach in this manner. It cannot be learned from anyone else in the world. The more you associate with them, the more it will increase. This is success.

[GV1:P1207] Guru Vani
