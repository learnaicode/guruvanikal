[GV4:P1018] November 2004

[GV4:P1019] This was not given by God for our desires. We must strive to embrace a dharma where we have been made instruments to execute the divine will. For this dharma, you, your children, or our contemporaries alone are not enough. We need the application of progeny endowed with the intellect to remove the errors that have occurred over aeons. We have also been shown how to do this. There is no need to waste time idly talking just because it has been revealed to us. Let us always remain vigilant. That vigilance must become a truth preserved for the sake of our lineage. So, what shall we do with this truth? How shall we receive it? How should it be transmitted to this land and country through us? Should we revert to the karma of calling upon God while divided by caste, religion, and race? Or should we realize that this is a reality ordained by God for all of us? Rather than wasting time overthinking how we can reach the karma that we analyze, we should proceed by applying only the knowledge we have been given.

[GV4:P1020] What is it? If we are to move away from the karma that we usually refer to as that of yogis and karmis—or even the ones where astrologers satisfy us with offerings by telling the tales of deities—we have been granted a karma by God where we are immediately told what our ruin is, what caused our ignorance, and where we stand in this ignorance. However, because we do not realize that we are receiving such a great fortune, if we stand aside with our very petty minds and fight, where will we reach? We will not be able to achieve anything.

[GV4:P1021] Therefore, it is not just about our life; we should not be absorbing the heat of the departed souls from wherever they exist. We are the ones who must think about the temperature of those souls. Nothing can be done about it. Thus, it is the mistake that has occurred in the light of numerous eras passed across Manvantaras, whether through the prophetic paths, the system of goddesses and gods, or the system of the Trinity, which came about within and outside these modes of worship. This is being revealed to us. This is our greatness. What is being revealed? We have arrived at a very magnificent karma that gave us the opportunity to be born in a time ordained by God for this revelation, and for our soul to separate from our body at that very time. This is not just for us, but it should completely become an understanding for all kinds of people and even all living beings, and the merit acquired from this understanding should be passed on to our children; we must instill in our children the determination to stand firmly in the good deeds we do today. Through them, we should give that love to the world. That should be the goal of our life.

[GV4:P1022] So, regarding this goal, people might say that it is Vedanta being spoken, or that such things exist in the realm of knowledge. In this special karma, permitted to impart to us the aspect of knowledge from the divine presence, the experience each of us acquires should flow through our children, passing on to us, our ancestors, and the future generations; since we are in a karma that has been extremely delayed in adopting such an experience, we must not have any other resolve except the singular determination that we will perform our karma with God at the forefront, to the best of our understanding. That is all we have to do. By fulfilling that, we are clearing the path for the coming children to act, thereby eradicating the prolonged miseries of our souls. You must not drift away from this karma. Although this has been reiterated many times, it is understood that most people find it difficult to grasp. Therefore, it is impossible for you to grasp it if it is explained to you point by point. What is it? Let me say it in brief.

[GV4:P1023] Through the permission granted by God, we are able to see the karmic flaws that occurred across these Manvantaras, and we must observe the miseries of the great souls who acted as the greatest scholars and the most perfected sages there. For one, it is enough to look at our recent times. Going further back would be very difficult for us. Finally, before the beginning of this Kali Yuga, it was Sri Krishna who was born for us and demonstrated it for the welfare of this world. However, since we are nothing in that realm of knowledge, the scholars later rewrote it as well. We are witnessing the play of the narrative hidden behind the miracle, told as a story that created wonders through incarnation and birth once again. This is not what we need.

[GV4:P1024] Guruvani
