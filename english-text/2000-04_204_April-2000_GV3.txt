[GV3:P0790] April 2000

[GV3:P0791] It is said that Sree Rama was born after performing the karma called Putrakameshti. That is, God desired to bring good souls. Even the family life of such a Sree Rama had no peace. Then how will people like us be saved? But that Brahma power itself is doing everything to bring good souls among us. We must see it to its magnitude. Otherwise, we won't even be able to take it.

[GV3:P0792] In the name of worship, we are holding onto something, covering it all up, praying, intending, and tying our souls to it. This is the biggest danger that has happened to us. God allowed the time of Kali for us to overcome this. Without telling us what that Kali dharma is, a word was cunningly said regarding Kali, and a word was also said that it is bad - Kali for Shudras. When it was said Kali for Shudras, everyone thought it was for some lower caste people. Lower caste people couldn't even enter the temple. Later, permission was obtained to enter the temple. But that was for the achievement of the Vaidikas.

[GV3:P0793] In the old days, Vaidikas would not come among the people. Or if they came, they would only stay in some temple precincts. They would do some tantra or yantra and command the temple officials. They would obey that. However, only seven families had the authority to enter the temple. Starting from Illathu Nair, to Kartha, Unni, Menon, and finally Vilakkithala Nair. No one else could enter the temple. They also created some administrative power to make it seem that they were weak and useless. This happened all over India. When this situation came, only these seven families - Dasi families - were brought up inside the temple and kept in the absurdity called sambandham. Thus, a path that endangered the vast majority of people was brought about through the temple systems. Going astray as Devadasis, they grew up and perished without ever having the right to mukti or liberation. These people also didn't learn to use the intellect and reasoning given by God properly. The temple dwellers first learned this wayward behavior. They taught everyone else. The realm of knowledge that was closed by that teaching never opened to the light again. Vaikom Satyagraha is a proof of this.

[GV3:P0794] People have believed that the Vaikom Satyagraha took place to enter the temple. But it was to walk on the road outside the temple wall. When it is said that one shouldn't even walk outside the temple wall, what was the situation of the people at that time?

[GV3:P0795] But what are we being preached and made to believe even today? - Temple Entry Proclamation! We have reached today's state by crossing so many such hurdles. Why achieve temple entry freedom after struggling so much? Could not worship a good deity. But even if worshipped, there is no use. We saw the state of Sree Rama, who is said to be the highest among our kings. This is a story from seventeen lakh years ago.

[GV3:P0796] The history of one Chaturyuga is more than double this. We are so far away that we cannot take the true knowledge to know such a long history. But as far as we are concerned here, God has created an opportunity to say that we are not far from this knowledge. By removing all the hurdles faced by the past rishi lineages, bringing deva purification and pitru purification, and all such purifications, we have been brought to a karma where we can be born and claim liberation. This is our greatest victory and fortune. Being able to participate, do, and make others do this karma stands as our greatest glory. We have done so much work here to pass on this glory to children growing up as boys and girls.

[GV3:P0797] This is the Guru Pooja we do at Santhigiri. People with knowledge, familiarity, and experience, and those who were judged not to know, see, or hear God's matters, are equally participants in this karma. Its glory stands so glorious that they cannot even imagine it. This karma is a great effort to overturn the path of karma that has come to us historically. This effort is the completion karma called Guru Pooja. It is not a tantri or someone who has studied, completed, and acquired karmic qualification who does this karma here. We see a history called fulfillment traditionally in our ancestors. We don't need to understand these histories without missing one. But coming here, we got God's permission to execute this. You must preserve it. Otherwise, it will become a great offense. To add this karmic qualification, which is fulfilled in this way, to you householders, a prayer of so long a time and a life just for it has been set aside. In this seventh Manvantara, twenty-eight Chaturyugas, and twenty-eight Kaliyugas in twenty-eight Chaturyugas, have passed. What karmas and dharmas are to be completed in this Kaliyuga? This Trimurti system has made us believe that such a Kali dharma is bad.

[GV3:P0798] The karma we have fulfilled is not a matter achieved by any guide who came after Sree Krishna, or the prophetic religions that came with it, or the Hindu dharma, or any Sanatani. If we handle such a matter without respecting its greatness, it is an offense committed against God Himself. Understanding this perfectly, we must always be indebted to that will of Brahman that gave us the opportunity to resolve our karmic flaws. We are obliged to offer ourselves in that dharma. Regardless of the religion you belong to, you are receiving the completeness of the presence of Brahman, or the presence of Parabrahman. This is its greatness. Do not think this is merely a matter of a human. Being unable to do anything after getting the opportunity to achieve completeness is the biggest offense. If you understand this, you will get some knowledge that will make you understand on your own without asking how to act towards the dharma of the world.

[GV3:P0799] God has provided the ways for pitrus, devas, and rishis to be born, get liberated, and achieve while remaining there in the realms they have gone and stood. Each of you must be vigilant about those ways. This is the path to eternity not only for you, but for everything that is to be born and grow traditionally hereafter, even down to an ant and a termite. This cannot be achieved without your karma and dharma. You must understand this completely.

[GV3:P0800] Through you, God wants to bring a change to this traditional history. For that, each of you should work. Worship at the feet of the Guru. What does it mean? Arising within the principle, at the Guru's feet. Who is this Guru arising within the principle? Surrendering at the feet of such a Guru, one receives instructions suitable for liberation. 'Will give an instruction suitable for liberation, and birth will cease'

[GV3:P0801] The greatest path shown by God for such a karmic destiny to reach you - that is the path of Santhigiri. This path will remove the errors that have existed in women until today. This path will remove the nonsense of men who have reached this far by playing around and acting irresponsibly. This path will remove the dirt of our life grown in a time when one could not distinguish south, north, east, and west. A path that will never again give room for such a karmic dosha (flaw). Through the prayers at Santhigiri, the destruction of the world itself will disappear forever.

[GV3:P0802] Guruvani
