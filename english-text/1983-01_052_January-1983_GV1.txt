[GV1:P1247] January 1983

[GV1:P1248] Knowledge commensurate with the era is the paramount requirement for us all. We have largely failed to effectively transmit it. We are merely navigating through this existence. We are engaged in a multitude of activities. We offer various forms of guidance. We even nominally adopt several exemplary practices. However, all of these efforts lie in ruins, much like a book whose binding has completely unraveled. Therefore, it is imperative to rectify the fundamental errors of life and systematically restructure and bind it anew in a truly exemplary manner. These systemic errors have manifested globally. The root cause for this civilizational decline is the profound disparity between the distinct natural order of antiquity and the subsequent transformations wrought by the passage of time.

[GV1:P1249] Even in contemporary England, the arduous hardships that characterized their early history are no longer prevalent. This is primarily because they engaged in intelligent, concerted efforts and rigorous intellectual pursuits to ensure their survival and sustenance. However, a different socio-cultural defect emerged there. A prevailing ethos of egalitarian creation—specifically, an ideological state wherein one perceives all companions to be exactly like oneself—began to proliferate alongside their progress. While we also possess a semblance of this notion, ours is characterized by a subtle, yet crucial, distinction. Our historical way of life and our systems of governance were constructed in strict accordance with indigenous discoveries and the natural environment. Our geographic landscape is not comparable to that of the Occident. It is exquisitely beautiful and intrinsically suited for human habitation. A profound humanistic value was cultivated precisely through the sacrificial lives of our ancestors. We can acquire that supreme ethical value only by leading a life firmly rooted in similar principles of profound sacrifice.

[GV1:P1250] Furthermore, those who toil and suffer were relegated solely to that fate. No one desired their prosperity. Consequently—as if a stain had been smeared upon life—a circumstance naturally emerged wherein offering guidance became impossible. The adversity stemming from the marginalization of one's fellow beings ultimately afflicted the society as a whole. We must rectify this.

[GV1:P1251] Even those who held monarchical power remained oblivious to this. The reason being that they wielded immense influence by virtue of their education, wealth, and enlightenment. Consequently, they became oblivious to the plight of their fellow beings. Those who served as vassals to the monarchy were subjected to an animalistic existence. They perpetuated their own subjugation, surviving merely on their master's provisions. Even in the absence of any reward, they were conditioned to protect their lord. Consequently, their intellectual development was stunted, whereas their physical endurance increased. All of this served solely to nourish another. The descendants of the elite, who were raised in comfort amidst this paradigm, subsequently leaped back into enlightenment. This eventually degenerated into arrogance, insolence, and depravity. As a result, the intoxication of wealth, unhesitating in its excesses, set the rhythm of society. Thus, a barbaric reign followed in its wake.

[GV1:P1252] Although the populace harbors a desire to transform this reality, it remains an elusive endeavor. The primary reason for this is that the prevailing values here are anchored in the authoritarian power of purported great men. This dynamic eventually infiltrated the administrative systems. While initially well-intentioned, the mechanics of governance were ultimately driven by sheer selfish interests. Consequently, the capacity to assimilate its virtues or the ability to sustain oneself upon it never reached the general public. Instead, these benefits accumulated sporadically, culminating in the dominance of a select aristocratic elite. This, in turn, relegated the masses to collective ignorance. Consequently, the populace was driven into a state of destitution, bereft of the means to attain education, consume a wholesome meal, wear decent attire, or even establish a rudimentary semblance of order in their lives.

[GV1:P1253] When this transpired, it corrupted the very essence of dharma and karma. Due to the fact that a vast majority of the population endured immense pain and suffering, numerous great souls were compelled to incarnate in this land. Over various epochs, they imparted moral teachings and principles of righteousness unto us. Furthermore, whatever they could possibly bestow upon us, they perceived, actualized, and generously offered. That constitutes the cultural heritage and humanistic values of Arsha Bharata. The purpose behind the profoundly sacrificial and arduous lives of those great souls can only be fully realized if we manage to internalize, comprehend, and actively implement these teachings. We must remain mindful of this in both our actions and our daily existence.

[GV1:P1254] Guruvani
