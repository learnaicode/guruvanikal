English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

May 1996

When visiting certain places, it seems as though a certain muteness hangs in the air, as if nature itself has fallen silent. I often wonder about the reason for this right then. I am sharing some things I happened to observe. In the practices where locals worship and lead others in worship, invoking God by naming various castes and religions, we have taken divine worship forward in a completely ignorant manner. No one authoritatively explained to us the reason for this or its root cause. Ever since I realized this, I have spent many days reflecting on these matters, and as a result of a strong desire to highlight them repeatedly, what I am going to share here is a summary of what I have been able to observe and understand.

There is evidence that we had a wretched habit here of claiming that all human beings are one, irrespective of caste, religion, and class, while practically dividing ourselves and behaving based on these very differences. However, we never received a profound and far-reaching understanding of how or why this came to be. Furthermore, when speaking about the Vedas—specifically about the great sage who divided the Vedas into four—he is praised with terms like 'Krishnavarnam'. It is even extolled that this great figure is an incarnation of Krishna himself. But when we look at the 'Tamoguna' (mode of ignorance) that created the caste system, the destructive act that tore apart and ruined the culture of that great man is a terror beyond imagination.

There are Christians and Muslims in India. Or many other doctrines have arrived. It is purely out of India's ignorance that a group of people converted in such a way for the sake of salvation and survival.

Other than that, there is nothing to say about it. One must think that they had at least this much goodness because of that change. The decay that has accumulated over the ages of life is what affects everything. Our ancestors, the forefathers, are the remnants of that decay. When looking at the objects worshipped by these forefathers, it is a sad truth buried in the dirt that we cannot help but say they are completely unknown to us. This truth took on various colors, became the four varnas, and then caste. This is the origin of caste.

Varna protection has never taken place here. What is varna protection? How is it done? Even the thinkers among us do not know this. Even those who say that such things have happened according to the dharma of the ages do not have the knowledge to say what actually happened. The reason for this is the incorrect practice of worship over time. When looking at this very old history one by one, it has been seen that a large population lived and died in a way that is unimaginable in terms of the degradation of life. This population is what worshipped and was worshipped here. Nature has created such a situation for the many crores of people who have been lost for ages without attaining merit through birth or liberation through death. When we go to a place, these ancestors and gods will come running in the hope of getting something. They will only leave after getting something. They might have been trying for such an arrival for ages. With this arrival, it is fulfilled. Over three lakh crore ancestors and gods have achieved this in this way.

The authoritative ritual we perform for this liberation of ancestors and gods in another way is Gurupooja. In fact, it is the Guru who is worshipped. Then why is it specifically called Gurupooja? When a family approaches due to some kind of illness or other afflictions, when looking at them in a vision, it is found that their ancestors and the life forces they worshipped under the Trimurti system, such as Shaktheyam, Shaivam, and Vaishnavam, have been lost in a way that they cannot attain liberation, leap towards goodness, or come to an understanding. What is the subject that you parents and the children you raise need to know to recover what has been lost? Our home should have prosperity. For that, children and women should be good. Moreover, when looking back at each thing in the state of the country, do not lose the opportunity to realize what happens to us when we call upon the god created by caste and religion. You should have a determination to uproot and throw away the roots of destruction that have come upon your land, country, and the world itself due to the mistakes that have occurred in you, your children, and your brotherly love, or due to the mistakes committed by your ancestors, and to establish the truth in its place.

Each of us should have a sincere regret that we have not been able to show in our mutual love what the great men, whom some religiously call saints, apostles, and Imams, and whom Hindus call by many names, should be to us, other than dressing them up with books. From that regret, you will become good. When you become good, you will be convinced by the divine presence coming into you that the actions you are showing off today by fighting are not what you absolutely need. With that, you will have the realization that we are the children who have the authority to remove the past mistakes from this world. Thus, you will never be able to stay away from the karma given by God. This is dedication. In this dedication, to completely remove the sorrow that caused the downfall and to bring goodness to everyone regardless of caste, religion, and class

We can also act in the name of God. God has given us the principles of karma to do what is necessary in that regard.

The accumulated karmic result of a few of our ancestors, whether they were yogis, karmis, bhaktas, jnanis, or none of these, inspiring crores of people to behave badly, is what has led us to turn towards actions that invite all this destruction. A change from this is indeed God's will. Instead of bringing it to the level of human love, we have only covered up the great souls born for the whole world by telling stories to implement this divine will. We are not even able to accept Sri Krishna, who was born in India in recent times, that is, within a mere five thousand two hundred years. Not only that, we have understood the glory of such a great person in exactly the opposite way. What all fabricated stories have we been taught about Krishna, who came as the first guru of this era or this Kali Yuga. After that, Moses, Jesus, and the Prophet, though their era's influence is comparatively less, no one sees the karma they undertook with sacrifice for us. None of them shared their sacrifice for any caste or religion. They strove for actions that confer liberation to the source of human birth. But what has their lineage shown us? They have made us successors of a lineage that sowed the complete destruction of karma through words and deeds. To change from this, you must become truly blessed in this time ordained by God for you. For that, God always shows compassion to you. By completely fulfilling that compassion, you should be able to bear any sacrifice for the good of the world.

Guruvani
