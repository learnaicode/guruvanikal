[GV1:P2538] August 1990

[GV1:P2539] Birthday Message

[GV1:P2540] Kings, emperors, and Brahmins possess greater qualifications to wield administrative power compared to the Shudra varna. However, these Vaishya, Kshatriya, and Brahmin rulers lack sufficient knowledge on how to manage administration in accordance with the times once they attain power. The Shudra varna also possesses the qualification to rule. But whenever they attained power, they harbored the delusion that they had become everything. Under that delusion, taking the old Vedic system itself as their model, they were prepared to rule by imitating the upper strata. Consequently, unable to do it themselves, they were forced to have the Brahmins do it for them. Additionally, there is their own *swakarma* (inherent duty). *Swakarma* is one's own duty, after all. The premise is that the result corresponds to the action. In that *karma*, the entire Shudra varna lags behind. It is merely a sort of jumping, dancing, and commotion.

[GV1:P2541] Would the present-day Sabarimala frenzy evoke any sense of devotion, affectionate intellect, reverence, or proper conduct in anyone? Similarly, building Kali temples and indulging in all sorts of undesirable acts there for pleasure has become a custom among them. There is also an obstinate belief that through festivals, God must manifest and accomplish everything. For instance, in my mother's family, there used to be a family temple. It exists even today. And it functions well to this day.

[GV1:P2542] During my childhood, there was a tradition called *Thalappoli* there. They would erect a green pandal on a ground, gather there, and the *Thalappoli* would commence from that point. Before the procession covered half the distance, I witnessed the person who bestowed blessings strike his own head with a very sharp sword in his hand, inflicting a wound upon himself, and applying holy ash and other substances once the *Thalappoli* concluded. Upon reaching the temple and checking, there was neither a wound nor even a mark of the cut visible.

[GV1:P2543] These were the methods of demonstrating tests in those days. That is exactly what the people desired as well. People derived immense exhilaration through this. However, none of this was done with true understanding. Consequently, their entire system of worship descended into such crude practices. The rituals and emotional displays associated with it are all that they know. Today, if one goes to North India, one can witness the practice of cramming everything into a very small room, conducting some form of worship there, and collecting money. This tendency is more prominent in areas where goldsmiths or carpenters reside in clusters. The Vaishyas, standing apart from both these approaches, adopted wealth accumulation and other specific schemes. The entire Shudra varna degenerated because they adopted this level of worship. This is what is meant by the result corresponding to the action. When the era turned in their favor, they were unable to adopt a corresponding form of worship or understand what the outcome of their own actions would be. But Kali (the age of Kali) will not leave without executing its dharma. It will certainly execute it. A correction and a new path are required for this.

[GV1:P2544] The new path implies cultivating good character among the people. Governance is not about inciting the entire population to commit violence and showing off by ruling the state. True administrative authority lies in the merit acquired by submitting oneself to humility, engaging in right action (*karma*), and adhering to righteousness (*dharma*).

[GV1:P2545] Not just the kings of India, but the entire Shudra varna that has existed in the world to date has engaged in this very type of governance. Only if one is born like Buddha, Jesus, the Prophet, or other such great souls can a change be brought to this style of governance. In the past and present, numerous great figures have stated very clearly that there is a place for action. We also believe in their doctrines which have been integrated into our daily experiences. However, just as we have a fundamental lack of fortune in translating them into action, we possess a nature where, no matter what we receive, we discard it and run frantically after something else.

[GV1:P2546] Only if individuals resembling the aforementioned great souls are born among the people of the present generation can actions appropriate for the times be realized. Those who are born in such a manner must be born among us ordinary people. Regardless of where they are born, it will clearly come to the forefront. It will not be like a renunciation (*sannyasa*) adopted halfway through life, a renunciation attained by sitting near a forest and performing pujas, a renunciation achieved by sitting in bhajans at a temple, or some isolated renunciation that has emerged through the systematic practice of rituals. This is a soul that stands desiring birth in a permanent state, or a soul (*karma*) born by divine decree. Sri Krishna came into being in such a manner.

[GV1:P2547] What people can take as evidence is the birth of Sri Krishna. Before the arrival of Sri Krishna, seven were killed. Krishna arrived as the eighth and implemented the dharma of the era (*yuga dharma*). But today, all of that is being intelligently reduced to vulgar spectacles along the streets through poetry and stories. Similarly, a Goddess temple is a place that must be preserved securely. By transforming it with obscenities, toddy, and fish, they have reduced it to such a perverse system of worship that its parallel cannot be found anywhere in the world. What occurs even today at the seat of Calcutta Kali? What happens in other similar Goddess temples? If a good person is born within a system that has rendered action (*karma*) in this manner, how can they be recognized? Only a Guru who is an omniscient seer of the past, present, and future (*trikalajnani*), capable of perceiving the course of karma, can awaken that realization among the people.

[GV1:P2548] Didn't everything Lord Krishna said seem like entirely new concepts to us? If such a person emerges, they will certainly have to suffer hardships. The Prophet came and suffered hardships. Jesus and Sankaracharya also suffered hardships. But there were people willing to accept the hardships suffered by Jesus and the Prophet and make sacrifices. As far as Hindus are concerned, they celebrate this with vows, dances, songs, revelry, and festivals. The concepts they have embraced belong entirely to the Treta Yuga. Or rather, they are the superficial antics manifested in the Dwapara Yuga. We remain stuck in that. The culture required to transcend this can only be attained through a preceptor (Acharya). It is not enough to merely believe in that preceptor; one must also honor and uphold them. If you only believe, you will abandon it halfway. An example of why mere belief is insufficient is the fact that Chattampi Swamikal walked to all Nair households attempting to impart ideas suited for the times, but it did not materialize. The exact same thing happened to Narayana Guru Swami as well. Sankaracharya came and advised that one should live like the children of one father. He proved in every possible way that the existing concept of 'Parayi Petta Panthirukulam' was not the right path. However, not only was the Sudra class unable to grasp it, but they also destroyed it in every conceivable manner. This is *karma*. The majority are those who assign importance to *karma* but perform contrary actions. Then what is *karma*? What is the point of saying all this to those who deliberately think they only want the wages for the work they do, and absolutely nothing beyond that?

[GV1:P2549] Another evidence is that four thousand years ago, Moses brought a new culture and attempted to impart it. What happened was that his followers were severely beaten by soldiers. It is said that when they ran after being beaten and reached the Red Sea, he struck it with his staff, parted the waters, and helped his followers cross to the other side. When the soldiers entered this path, the water rushed back in, and all of them drowned and died. Even after witnessing such profound miracles, the people could not imbibe this inclination. It was after that Jesus and the Prophet demonstrated the model of sacrifice. Until then, only an affinity towards miracles within doctrines was shown. But Jesus and the Prophet operated from the very premise of the rigid Vedanta of the Hindus, which says 'let it happen through him'. Consequently, a number of people became Muslims and Christians. Those who converted to Christianity and Islam back then were a group of ignorant people. Their knowledge was as disparate as the sea and the chaff flower (*kadalum kadaladiyum*). Yet, through the firmness of their faith and conviction, they grew to be on par with others. Moreover, they were able to win others over to their side and suppress several others. Through this, they essentially imbibed another aspect of the ignorance characteristic of Hindus.

[GV1:P2550] The unique characteristic of *Kala Dharma* is that the Sudra class gained the authority to implement the divine will (*Brahma Nischayam*). Following Lord Krishna, numerous preceptors emerged from both the Savarna and Avarna classes to implement *Kala Dharma*. But none of them succeeded. The reason for that is *karma* and the fruits of *karma* itself. Because the souls (*Jivatmas*)—who performed distorted actions instead of righteous deeds and propagated the buffoonery of mischief—carry the strength of ancestors whose positions or zodiac signs have shifted (according to astrology), along with the *karma* and *dharma* of the yogis, you too are standing right there and playing along. You are unable to move away from there. For a transformation to occur, you must grow in monotheism and act by placing faith in a lineage of Gurus capable of realization. You still have time for that. Time has not run out. We are only standing at the beginning of the Kali Yuga. It can be said that Kali has already begun. The *dharma* of Kali will not be fully established in ten or five thousand years. But there will be a change going forward.

[GV1:P2551] In Western countries, during the era when idol worship and polytheism were prevalent, when prophets introduced their doctrines, the people, unable to fully embrace them, remained trapped in their traditions. But even that much cannot be assimilated by those living adamantly in India under the guise of Sanatana Dharma or Hindu Dharma. However, this will be implemented. The children who are born and grow intelligently will implement this. The present generation will be capable of accepting them. They do not possess the ignorance of the past. They are, after all, witnesses to some form of knowledge. There is no doubt that they will continue to search as long as the past testimonies remain incomplete. If they discover something good somewhere in their search, there is no doubt that they will respect and honor what they find, transform it into *karma*, and sustain it as *dharma*. That will undoubtedly happen. Once that *karma* is realized, *dharma* will become theirs. The virtue and its sustenance will rightfully belong to them.

[GV1:P2552] In the West, they were able to accept the visionary doctrines of the prophets to a certain extent. But Indians have not yet been able to attain that standard. This is because the ignorant Sudra class only has the experience of calling it Sanatana Dharma, valuing Vedic Dharma and other lower cultures, being captivated by their buffoonery, and expending money, energy, and dominance on it. At the same time, in Western countries, even if they lacked knowledge, they were prepared to embrace sacrifice with the desire to accept the doctrine. But in India, the rulers are clinging to the base of the Vedic religion—which stands tall displaying vows, offerings, and supremacy—and crying out, 'Oh God, we have no salvation, please save us.' In situations where both Vedic authority and governance rested in the hands of the Brahmin, he alienated the Sudra due to his inherent inclination. However, since the Sudra inherently possesses an inclination for *karma*, when he acquired the right to rule, he collaborated with the Brahmin and became willing to share the victories, defeats, and dedications of war together. The Sudra's inclination for *karma* outwardly consists of elephants, howdahs, umbrellas, torches, dances, songs, and installing the father figure beneath a tree. Since even an elevated Sudra has no *karma* beyond this, there was no awareness to comprehend that the *karma* performed by the Brahmin would bring them no good or that it would ultimately destroy them. That is precisely why this *dharma* deteriorated for such an extended period. The fundamental philosophy of Santhigiri's ideology is its reclamation.

[GV1:P2553] Now, one must reflect upon how the divine will (*Brahma Nischayam*) is to be observed in *karma*. For this, there must be a pleading voice from the people: 'Father, we desire to see the birth of a masculine noble soul qualified to eradicate our destitution, gain recognition for our homes, ensure the survival of the nation, and bring goodness to the world! May God fulfill this.' Growth through a lineage of Gurus is that salvation. Thus, the *karma* of the Guru lineage that emerges according to the divine will will move towards oneness, rather than diverging into multiplicity. Furthermore, no one will be able to deny the explicit opinions expressed by the preceptor of that lineage. There may be those who deliberately deny it. All the preceptors who have appeared thus far have faced such opposition from their contemporaries. You have learned from history that contemporaries employed all conceivable methods to harass the preceptors. It must be presumed that a number of people will be prepared to do the same even now. The people must also be willing to bear a share in these hardships. But you lack the course of *karma* for that. You still harbor the unawareness to grow in the consciousness of mantras, yantras, and tantras that supposedly create miracles upon miracles, and to believe that this karmic potential will be realized through individuals like astrologers who manifest it. This unawareness is the very cause of your destitution.

[GV1:P2554] However, as far as this ideology is concerned, no matter how powerful the opponents may be, their opposition will not sustain.

[GV1:P2555] Could one have even dreamed that the imperial and capitalist regimes that previously existed in India would be replaced by a democratic administration? Yet, did it not come to pass? However, the present rulers are also committing errors. Having attained power, they govern under the delusion that it was acquired through their own astuteness. Be that as it may, let them govern. But a new regime is imminent. The previously mentioned descendants are indeed coming—the descendants born into the lineage of Gurus. They will manifest karma through the power of their command. When that karma is established, dharma will become the heritage of this Guru lineage. The inherent merit accompanying it can no longer remain concealed; it will shine forth as a lineage where merit, virtue, prosperity, and the grace of the Guru are harmoniously integrated. It will possess the moral consciousness to reclaim all lost karmas. Intelligent disciples, comprehending this, will execute their karmas with the singular contemplation of how to integrate this ideology into their lives—contemplating why they were born, why they were raised, why they must live, and why they must die. Numerous disciples possessing this awareness will emerge.

[GV1:P2556] There is an order of rituals observed by Santhigiri. The dharma of that system will be fostered through these disciples. By displacing the contemporary position of the ancestors and altering the dharma of the deity occupying that position, these disciples will integrate it into their observances, much like the verses of the *Guru Gita* enshrined within the *Skanda Purana*. Until then, it will gradually yield results. It will require three hundred and fifty years to attain complete fruition. Within that period, the Guru-disciple lineage will achieve perfection. No one will be able to repudiate this lineage. This is because whatever violent tendencies exist in the world will annihilate each other through internal conflict. It will be as though all ignorance has been entirely eradicated. Karma and dharma will be rigorously implemented in all their depth and magnitude. There are reasons why this did not materialize in the lineage of the Buddha. The Vedic scholars and kings of this land colluded covertly, simultaneously employing Vedic rituals, Tantra, Hinayana, and Mahayana, to completely eradicate that ideology from this region. Furthermore, wherever else it took root, these Vedic stratagems systematically destroyed it. However, its destruction was inevitable. For on such a profoundly corrupted cultural foundation, it is virtually impossible for such a monumental dharma to sustain itself. One must embrace those who are worthy of embrace, reject those who deserve rejection; strike those who must be struck, and execute those who must be executed—this constitutes the paradigm of governance. That paradigm did not manifest within the Buddhist culture.

[GV1:P2557] What is yet to come is not modeled after the Buddhist culture. It will strike those who must be struck and execute those who must be executed. Sri Krishna had done precisely that. Santhigiri will continue this struggle until the forthcoming paradigms of governance and dharma reach their absolute perfection.

[GV1:P2558] Yours, Swami
