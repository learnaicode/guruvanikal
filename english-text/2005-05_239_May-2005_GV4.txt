[GV4:P1068] May 2005

[GV4:P1069] For that, you do thisٹ There are children who pass only nursery. Then there are children who leave without studying anything. There are those who study, make a mess of everything, wander around, and die like animals. There are those who study and overcome everything to succeed. Isn't that just a human way? What the trajectory is of the holy soul that has transcended this human way is a matter for later. You do not need to go searching for it like that. Why? It is because you are fortunate to be born in Kali Yuga. Why did we get such luck? Be it any animal, being lucky enough to be born in this Kali Yuga means salvation even for those who are like animals. Liberation. Liberated from one to another. This is the specialty of Kali. So, we were not made aware of the dharma of time. For that, they subjugated us by making it seem that Kali is no good. Who established that Kali is not good for humans as a whole? Be it the Vedic scholar or anyone else. If you celebrate Vedantas by citing grand theories, is it beneficial for the soul? This is proof of that.

[GV4:P1070] As I mentioned yesterday, each yoga practice is a theory. Raja yoga or otherwise, this is not what Raja yoga means. What is meant by Raja yoga? A rich man, without his knowledge, is born rich. Then, even after birth, he keeps seeking a high position like this. Someone else is born into a poor family. He too might search, search, search, and thus seek a path. Another person is not like that; he is born very rich and turns into a beggar. This is the difference in changing states. Just understand this much. You do not need to listen to any other logic. Because the era you are born in is Kali, you will have the karmic potential to follow the Guru's path. It arises on its own. But what is a Guru, what is a Deva, what is a Rishi, what is a Sanyasi, what is a Jnani, what is a Pithru, and what is the specific nature of each of those Pithrus—knowing these things is the difference in changing states. How many births I took in this changing state comes next. See, this is what you see. So, in what you see, my father, my mother, my husband, my wife, my children, wealth, capability—we see each of these like this, right? Why should this be shown like this? Do you understand my difficulty with you coming here just to run away? It is not enough for you to somehow get consent and just leave. The issue is that you do not understand this. Places where this is spoken about are rare. Agreed. But we do have a conviction that this exists. In that conviction, don't we need a karmic capability aligned with truth that we must cultivate? Will it do if I decide to take it up after this lifetime is over?

[GV4:P1071] Another thing we have heard is that young children don't need any of this. It is only when they get old... All these changes in states, what is it? Of the body - not of the mind. When the changes in the states of the body occur, it is enough to go and lie down somewhere and chant. You cannot chant standing up. The story we have been made to believe is that if you chant like that, God will come running. For that, they made up a story called 'Ajamila Moksham'. What can we do about the ignorance that is trapped in it? This is ignorance disguised as knowledge. These are the mistakes that you and I and whoever else has been born and come and gone have made. If you ask what is the proof for this, I am not just telling you. To your children - to yourselves - what is Jagrat, Swapna, Sushupti... We are getting an understanding of what is called Sushupti (deep sleep), what is called Swapna (dream), and what is called Jagrat (waking state). I slept well yesterday. That is a knowledge. Isn't it? We slept. Isn't that a knowledge that we slept? In reality, we must be convinced that we slept well and rested. Is this true or false? The same thing we are seeing in the dream. We went to such and such places, went in such and such ways, did bad things, did good things, did that, did this, did so and so, did like this, we will see a lot in a way that cannot be said. We don't know what this is. Then it is being shown in the dream in the language of this waking state. That, somehow, I had a lot of terrible dreams. This dream can come due to the size of the devil we worshiped. So if we keep this as God, doesn't it mean we are still the devil?

[GV4:P1072] Calcutta Kali or whatever Kali nature is described like that, none of this is Kali. Don't think it is Kali. This is only a special action of the form built in a special intelligence of man. They danced and danced and died, some of them. This is going into the atmosphere and staying exactly like this. We are born in this zodiac. So you can understand everything then. Is it clear to you? So we train these children like this and it is an ignorant feature that teaches that God is everything without knowing what a god, goddess, sage, or sanyasi is. We fall into this and rot. What can I do if you don't understand this? Whether you are a small child or an adult or a hundred years old, if you don't understand this difference, there is no point in living as a human being. We will have money, ability, wealth, and power. All that is the remainder of what we called for our father, for our mother, for our husband, for our children, for money, for wealth, at some point in time. None of this will save you. If there is, tell me. For example, if the rich have a little money and ability, we, the people in that house lived somewhat decently. Or as useless people. Let them live however they want. No matter how they live, they do not have the health of the servant in the next house. What is it? So if you keep this body carefully packed and tied up due to luck and walk around shining, hasn't your whole life gone? What did you understand? Let karma be. Let karma be whatever. What has your life become? If the father and mother learned by seeing this, 'Children, because father became like this, we got nothing. Because mother became like that, we couldn't do anything'. What happens there is a scattering of mutual blame. Then scolding the locals, family members, and everyone they see. Only we are good. Thus, taking pride in highlighting the weight of a karma that was gathered and shown by accumulating all shortcomings—the shortcoming of caste, the shortcoming of humans. Isn't this our greatness! Isn't it just like the organization of today's castes and classes? What is each of you saying? It is our organization. Whose is it? Whose is it? Who are we? When we say we, isn't it everyone together? How did it become only us in that? There is only an 'us' there. Basking at that end of the course of karma accumulated in this manner. Will this do you any spiritual good? Or will it do good deeds? Or will it do anything else? What are you doing?

[GV4:P1073] Guruvani
