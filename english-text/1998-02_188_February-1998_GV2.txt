Guruvani February 1998

The status of Trimurti, Devendra, and Parashakti - many calculations have been brought forward for all these. Then all this has been combined and created. What we see here in the vision is not that calculation. What we see is purely a knowledge. Some of you who have visions see that some pass through all these different states. Some may not be seen. Whether seen or not, one need not consider it as more or less. Passing without seeing is also good in a sense. Because when the discriminatory knowledge of all this comes, some will have all their doubts cleared. For some, an ignorance will remain such that doubts will never be cleared. That can be said. There is an easy way for you. That is, it will be seen as many previous births. That is how male and female are seen. That is a perfect proof.

This knowledge called Gudha, which was told after Sri Krishna came, is coming into your experience. When a thousand people come here, at least two are coming that way. Those who come that way must complete the fourth state mentioned earlier. There are those who complete the Trimurti state itself. Many Mahatmas have crossed the Trimurti state. But Devendra is not ready to let them pass upwards from here. For that, another justification has been made up by telling a story. He is a person who does all this like this.

The five elements! What have we been made to understand about the five elements? This story is an answer to the question of what the dharma of the five elements is. What are the five elements? It comes to the five senses and the completion of the combination of the five senses is in the five koshas. How the five koshas are combined is written and brought through Vedanta. You will never be able to cross this. Because Vedanta will not come there. How has this been achieved? By bringing in a cultured language, giving interpretations, the commentator consciously giving interpretations even to the letters, books like Panchadashi, Vivekachudamani, and Siddhantakaumudi have been created. In Siddhanta Kaumudi, the five elements, the five senses, and the five koshas have been determined, life and soul have been determined, and thus eighteen philosophical initiations have been stated. But it is not the experience and the different states that have been determined. That is, without bringing a visionary attitude to these philosophical initiations, the scholars have traveled their own way. India has a course of action that was taken up and implemented by the kings. Those who implemented it like that were also called Rajarshis. These Rajarshis did this philosophical initiation according to the Yoga theory. Saying that one without yoga is a lump, by making them obey the Yoga theory, these philosophical initiations have been achieved in action.

The knowledge of Panchasiddhi, which is added to the five senses, becomes koshas and we come out of the Annamaya kosha. Annamaya kosha, Pranamaya kosha, Manomaya kosha, Vijnanamaya kosha - reaching this far, it has been scattered. When it reached the Vijnanamaya kosha, then came the misfortune of not being able to retrieve what the other koshas are like. But the method of clearly determining these other koshas is here (in Santhigiri). The completion of the Kosha theory is according to the growth of life. This is where the distinction between life and soul becomes relevant.

Until these four states are completed, the soul must cross six steps to complete these four states. All the Mahatmas who have come so far have said that the four states must be completed. No one knows what to do for that. Whatever has been accepted as an original concept of the ashram is definitely not Siddhantakaumudi.

The story of Angulimala has also appeared in the history of Sri Shankara. The same attitude is seen in the Kali devotion in the history of Sri Ramakrishna. Sri Ramakrishna is seeing this Angulimala. In the place of this Angulimala, Kali has completed it, in the sense that everything

Seen as a goddess. Bhadrakali, Bhadram Kalanavaka! The knowledge worshipped here is being completed through this devotion to the goddess when talking about Sri Ramakrishna. Thus, trying to complete it by telling the story of Angulimala. But how will this be right? It cannot be completed like that.

The Vaishnava tradition has been brought in another way. Kaliyamardana is shown to have been done by Sri Krishna. By drawing Ananthashayanam, everything is depicted as merging into Mahavishnu in that idol. But how far will this depiction go? Without a Guru, one cannot reach there.

Such a vision is obtained here (at Shantigiri) by both men and women. But not everyone will get this completely. But when ten thousand people come, there will be four people who see this completely. Because these four people will pass through each small state. When ten thousand people come like that, there will be four people in it - as men and women. The meaning of this cannot be explained and finished now.

How can I clearly explain to you how the vision that came through the five elements, the knowledge understood through the five senses, is situated in the five sheaths, and how it has been transferred to the soul? But it can be transferred to the soul. That is the Guru! The Guru who is the master of all arts. The one who transfers these sixty-four arts to the soul is the truth.

What have we turned these arts into? Ottanthullal, Thiruvathira, Chakyarkoothu, and many such performances, and the Koothambalams for them, are depicted as a part of the temples like an Oottupura. How will we overcome this? The masters of arts have danced, played, bitten, and destroyed it to such an extent that we can never move forward from this, even bringing it to the level of theory. Thousands of men and women have followed this. They have traveled along the path of this god and stopped there, playing, jumping, and dancing. Those who have a vision here are seeing this. They are shown how they have reached there without falling into a state. This is one state.

This is the second state given to the Guru. This is what was indicated earlier about the ignorance that is not fully understood. The reason for not understanding can be explained separately. Because one cannot understand and act, one cannot acquire the qualification. If you cannot understand this through this life of a man and a woman, you will not be able to pass even the fourth state. You must pass at least the state of what is being shown. You don't have to be a sanyasi to pass like that.

Sri Krishna is said to be the master of all arts. A great man who could master all arts with just a flute. Meera (Bhakta Meera) saw this in a vision and took refuge in it. But how is the story of Krishna brought into Gita Govindam? We could not know what the author of Gita Govindam imagined. Therefore, we could not grow scientifically. We did not know the science that the sound of a sound that has passed through all sixty-four arts can be heard through this flute. If you cannot know the eternity of those Gopis, you will not be able to take the experience of truth you get from here.

You must understand that it is Krishna himself who has brought us to this state.

It is that great soul who removed a Brahmarakshas in the position of Devendra, who stood obstructing its journey of completion, from that position. This Guru Parampara is a lineage that has been able to see what the Brahmarakshas and many other indwelling spirits have made of these great souls and the world. This is not a story that can always be told. Only by bringing you to the level of experience can each thing be explained. By coming into the Guru Parampara to complete the karma, God himself has seen this great thing, rejoiced, reached it, and you are getting an ordained karma to choose as your right.

Guruvani

March 1998

Today, Indian culture is absent even in matters of food. It is absent in dress and language. Neither we nor our parents know why this Western style is adopted. In Brahmin families now, no one calls out 'Appa'. It is 'Daddy'. Because of this, all the harm falls on the heads of the children. In that respect, the Brahmin and the Chandala here are alike.

What will we do with our children who have reached this point? In which direction will we correct them? When young children who grow up seeing this enter family life, no culture of the family will be useful to them. If even the Brahmin here has an imposed culture in food, dress, language, and everything else, where will the Shudra, who does not know the culture of reciting the Vedas or the principles, fall? The Shudra is one who has not even peeked into a single Shastra. It is said that Shastras should not be touched.

Then what will this Shudra do here, other than sing as he learned, build a Koothambalam, and play? What is the reason for having large temples in South India? The reason is the Koothambalam. Koothambalam means play; that is, Kathakali. There are no such large temples in North India.

In the Dwapara Yuga, the Varna Dharmas were not properly followed. In order to maintain Varna Dharma, the Shudra was given the position of a Kshatriya. This is where the mistake lies. This was bound to happen. This is what is meant by not knowing the Dharmas of the time.

Kamsa's birth was in a Shudra's house. From this position, is it possible to play as a Kshatriya? We have never seen this, until today. There is no need for a Shudra to play as a Kshatriya. In reality, it was that great power - the will of Brahma - that brought power to the Shudras. That is why the disembodied voice told him not to look up, but to look down. But Kamsa did not understand that. He could only take what the visible God and Goddess said. To the Yadava's knowledge, there is no knowledge about this disembodied voice. By giving knowledge among the Yadavas, he himself is explaining what the will of Brahma is - you must do exactly this, otherwise, someone will be placed in your house to kill you.

This knowledge was given even before Sri Krishna was born. Why was this knowledge given among the Shudras like this? But Kamsa handled it in the way the Trinity appeared and told him. That is, God has no form. He is not familiar with the fact that God is formless. He also does not know that there is a luminous entity called God everywhere. He killed seven of the eight children born to his own sister. The truth is that he did not get his hands on the eighth one. Because he did not get it, he sent many Putanas into the world. Still, he did not get Krishna. Thus Krishna escaped. And then? Neither this Shudra class, nor the Kshatriya class here, nor the Brahmin class had any information about Sri Krishna. Therefore, Krishna and the Pandavas were chased all over the country. Krishna ran from here and reached another shore. After creating Dwaraka from there, Krishna could not pass through that state. When a person is born, he must cross the zodiac and mandalas in which he was born. Krishna was born with that precaution.

What is the color of the sky? The sky has no blue. Then how do we see blue when we look? Sri Krishna was given a form in that color. The house where Krishna was born does not know the meaning of this color of Krishna. The clan, the Kshatriya, the Vaishya, or the Shudra do not know. This

If our knowledge about five thousand years of history is like this, how will we know the history before that?

This knowledge is being given to us in a special way. Whether it relates to a Brahmin or a Kshatriya, this knowledge is a special rule. This is a knowledge that has not existed until today. How will we understand this? For example: there are families who perform Guru Pooja and families who believe without performing Guru Pooja. After all of them came here, whatever was in those they worshipped - Shaktheyam, Shaivam, Vaishnavam, all three - they are seeing it. Even so, none of them are able to grasp the full extent of this idea. None of you should think that you are highly intelligent and capable or less so. It can be both. You are not doing all these activities for this. You should not try to acquire this knowledge, which gives you the opportunity to implement God's will through your life over a long period, through just any path. None of you came here as great devotees. But it's not that you have no devotion either. There is some kind of love. That love is devotion.

If we count them one by one - father, mother, wife, wealth, education - we have love for everything. But the fulfillment of all these loves combined is God or Guru. So, surrendering all our ignorance and knowledge there, achieve whatever can be achieved with our life. Otherwise, never think of behaving in a mere fourth-rate manner. That thought will enslave us. Absolutely, if this knowledge is needed for the land and the country through our children, let God give it to the world itself, let us do what we can - this is the understanding you need. You shouldn't desire anything else. Let God give everything that is to be given.

Many of you have been doing many things here for a long time. My understanding is that your effort has yielded very good results. However, some efforts have not yielded results. The reason for that is a lack of recognition among some people who worked here before. That shortcoming is human nature. That doesn't matter. From now on, we must work together without ever letting go of the love, respect, humility, and compassion to align with the truth from within. That compassion should not leave from within. This is what Narayana Guru says: "Without leaving your holy body, the thought too." So, giving one's entire thought to that holy body, that holy body should reside within my soul. God should not be kept at a distance. Should not be distant. That is absolutely the Upanishad. When we say Upanishad, the meaning is to whisper in the ear. Not to say it out loud outside. That is, not to entertain people by telling the meaning. It is giving it in one's ear. That 'Upa' (near), that conversation or that concept or that action, that dharma which is with us, we are hearing it in our ear. It is being told in the ear. Not being said from afar. If said from afar, we will not get it. It is absolutely told in our ear. Understand this as truth regarding us. Experience it as truth.

Some of you tell me, I heard such a word. It was like this. It was told to me like this. Your greatness is exactly that. Do not think that there is any other greatness. Because unless it is useful for our soul, we will not get it wherever we go. We should have that understanding.

Our people have some shortcomings. They have been unable to take up some things. Even those who stood with me, I know, have been unable to take them up. Does that mean if I blame them and reject them, they will improve? Now their thousands of

What is the use of telling you about the good or bad deeds of past lives? That is not what we need. Today, God has given you an opportunity to honor and make others honor that service-mindedness of yours. Do not lose it.

If possible, when you act and realize that glory through love, you will see the emergence of truth. Otherwise, if you keep love somewhere else and just show something outwardly, it will not come. Love is dissolving and merging into every action we do. It is flowing into it. Imagine pouring honey from one bottle to another. It does not break. In this way, without being two, love is continuously pouring from our minds. We have followed so many mantras, yantras, and tantras. Whatever we do or don't do, if there is no love and respect in the mind, everything will be lost. Even if we don't have love, there is something God does to us. You have experienced it. It's just that most people don't respect it that much. Whether you respect it or not, God's representative will show love to you. That is the truth. It is shown to you and to the world, and thus reaching that state called the compassion of life, we become overwhelmed and unable to stand due to reverence. This is the truth.
