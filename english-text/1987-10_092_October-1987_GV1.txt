[GV1:P1749] October 1987

[GV1:P1750] I shall recount an experience of mine to you.* There is a difficulty in deceiving you by telling this. That is why I am speaking to you in this manner. If told in any other way, you will not be able to accept it. Several people have come to me. There are a few who have known me since childhood. I was a believer in God. I only knew that. I knew nothing else.

[GV1:P1751] I lived at home until the age of ten or fourteen. I had to commit a deception to leave home. I also knew that it was the only way to leave home. Is there not a custom among us to go to Palani? Stating that a garland and a vow were required for it, I wore them. Thus, I left under the pretext of going to Palani. I went straight to the ashrams. I engaged in various positions within the ashrams. I found no satisfaction from any of this.

[GV1:P1752] My entire desire was to attain a spiritual realization (*Anubhuthi*). It is the Sri Ramakrishna Mission and the Sri Narayana Mission that stand strengthened in Kerala today. I am well-acquainted with both of these. Then there are the lineages of Madhva and Sri Shankara—they do not prove to be of much use to the Shudra class in that manner. Because caste and such are still deeply entrenched there. Therefore, there is no salvation there. Near the very house where I was born, there is an ashram belonging to the Sri Ramakrishna Mission. I was associated with it as well. Apart from that, I spent seventeen years associated with the Sri Narayana Mission and its other temples. Not only was I associated, but I also handled several matters within its administration.

[GV1:P1753] They had arranged the rituals of worship and all other regulations. Whatever this may be, to speak of a realization from this—there was a realization lasting two days. Other than that, there was nothing. Thus, with the desire for a Guru, if I learned that an individual resided anywhere, I would leave the ashram citing some excuse. I would go and observe there. In this manner, I regularly visited a person who sat like an *Avadhuta* for ten years, going there one day a month. I would go stating that I needed to visit one of the ashram's branches. I would go there just for the namesake. Then, during the night, I would sit with this person the whole time. This person lay on the sand at the beach. Despite going for ten years, I gained nothing from this person. It was not because he did not know how to speak; rather, he simply did not speak.

[GV1:P1754] While things were thus, I found a monk through another person. He understood me as soon as I arrived. Yet, he said nothing. He said, "Let it be tomorrow." When I went the next day, he asked, "You are the person who came yesterday, aren't you?" This man also lay on the sand and in the bushes. "Why have you come?" he asked me. I replied in a single word. Saying, "You are the rightful inheritor," he embraced me and wept. He wrote down a mantra for me and said, "You must keep using this. Then realization will occur. You must come here when that happens."

[GV1:P1755] The experience came. I went. Everything is there. But it will take a very long time. I understood nothing. Even after two and a half years, there was no result. Meanwhile, difficulties, hardships, and tribulations were pouring in. I never did anything to warrant that. After this, whatever the experience was, it remained unclear. Aside from it being proven true, I do not know what it is like or how it is.

[GV1:P1756] You will only understand if it is stated as it is. If said in any other way, it will not be clear to you. Therefore, I only recounted my experience. You should pay attention to at least that.

[GV1:P1757] He himself had a desire to grant me a realization somehow. He would say it whenever we met. One day, a situation arose where I had to leave Sivagiri itself. I had not committed any mistake to warrant that either.

[GV1:P1758] Before that, he had shared a word with me. While wandering as an *Avadhuta*, he came there. One day, after the puja was completed, as I came out carrying the offering plate (*thattam*), a person acquainted with me came and said that a Swami was standing there inquiring about me. I immediately handed over the plate and went. He instantly said: "I am going on a journey from here. There is a change for you from here. You must not leave without seeing me."

[GV1:P1759] To leave after saying this out of nowhere! I could not believe it. Because I had been rendering service for so long. And not by taking a single penny. I had been there for such a long time. How could I leave from there? Even if asked to leave, would I muster the courage to go?
