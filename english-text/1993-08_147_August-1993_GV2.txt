August 1993

Life towards sacrifice

Action towards virtue

How should we view the past?

How should we view the present?

Virtue through life.

The beautiful sight of what life is, crossing the scars of past lives. When experiencing that beautiful, divine nectar beautifully, you will be fortunate to see what the action is that fills our solar system, rejecting the scholar and the ignorant, knowledge and ignorance. Take this with value and see and know what the path of dharma is, which highlights the calculation of the manvantara that this world has contained for human society to live as one.

Gaining a Guru will be the opening of a door to recognize what the dharma and adharma you do are. Like taking what someone else has accumulated without knowing and squandering it without pain, some dangers lie hidden in you, and in that stream of light you can see the action that makes you yourself judge virtue and sin. When that door opens without knowing any work, struggle, or hardship, you will think about what you have unknowingly become the owner of. After that, when it becomes your ego, do not waste the time God has given us to recognize and take each of these—the presence of Ishwara, the presence of God, the presence of Brahman, the presence of Parabrahman—which are contained in this unbrokenness obtained in this Guru-gain. Tie up the mind that runs towards multiple gains, forgetting the Guru-gain, to close down such unbrokenness. Do not lose the opportunity to see and know what the causes and effects are, called the holy feet, the Guru-gain given by God, so as never to forget the truth of life that becomes the scholar and the ignorant who praise to the skies the aspects of action that fall into the ego, which is the cause of the three gunas in the aforementioned language, that the causes and effects known by the three gunas in front of the Guru are strong matters. The opportunity has come to tell you this now; as an experienced person, without ego and with humility, I repeatedly request you.

Keep this in your memory daily so that God may have mercy on the ignorant who take leadership roles by saying the above sentences without wasting the opportunity to say in a refined manner at the same time as holy feet and social reformer. He who becomes a great scholar by cleverly calling the tools to be tied up as the bond of karma should also know this.

Lying in the sea, on the beach, in the sand, on the roadside, and in the river sand, dressing up as a Vedic scholar at the same time, bowing to the pure form made capable of the avadhuta lifestyle, without blocking the stream of light on the roadside, closing it in the presence of the heart, taking what is needed at the needed time, become worthy of the love and respect of good people.

Your Swami

Guruvani

September 1993

Various kinds of ups and downs have occurred in our lives. Therefore, old tendencies will stir up in our actions. The best thoughts and enthusiasm will rise up. But your capacity for action will not allow it to be executed. If you think of living in the ashram for a full two days, it won't be possible. You will leave before that. After doing a little bit, one will speak authoritatively. We must understand that this is unnecessary for us. It is fundamentally due to a lack of knowledge that one speaks so authoritatively.

Once devotion sets in, do not act impulsively with initial enthusiasm. If we do not reflect on who we were before, these flaws will affect it. Actions will be tainted with flaws. If a good action is mixed with bad, the goodness will fade. If you observe carefully, you can see that you have acted in various ways on the same day. There is a single word used by ascetics for this behavior - 'past tendencies'. That single word contains the account of births and eras. You can see color variations in many places on the wings. Those are the places where the soil has washed away.

Human life and tendencies are very, very old. Come to think of it, there are no other creatures in the world as filthy as humans. If a man or a woman goes astray, it will cause immense harm. Especially when it comes to women. It is a demonic form in which all the flaws in the world have melted together. But it must be transformed. I see myself giving priority to that. But one should not provide more facilities than necessary. Experience shows that if you do, they will climb on your head. These mischiefs of theirs were earned by the ancestors in the past. Now, it is the imitation of the Western world. It is the duty of men to make efforts to remove the flaws of womankind. Decide that I will dedicate my life to removing the shortcomings of womankind. But no one bothers to do that. Even if they do, it is of little use. But God has provided us with all the ways to do this karma. The reason is that God has compassion for them.

Compared to other ashrams, mostly young people come here. That is God's will. Men and women who come here go back with many good desires. The children born from those desires will turn out well. We have done many rituals here to remove the flaws in women. Along with that, you should also have an aching prayer that this flaw may be removed from us. Perhaps the result of the karma we are doing will come after a long time. That should not matter. Remember when the result of Jesus' sacrifice came into effect.

Today, you are in a situation where you cannot forgive a single word from each other. This hardship of ours is not for our life. It is for the future generations. All this has happened because of the flaws of the ancestors who have passed away. The destiny of ancestry will stand up to the measure of merits and sins. When born in ego, deformity occurred. Everyone has a path where ancestry was lost. We must always remember these truths.

Who was the Prophet? A person born without any education in a family that made and sold idols. How great is the history of that Prophet. His words became the Veda. It is because he moved forward acting only on the words received from God. But we do not know how to think like that. You were able to come here and work without knowing anything. That is a great fortune.

This is a time of mixture. It is clearly stated in the Gita. We can listen to the bad words of a thousand people. We can tolerate whatever anyone does. But it is enough to have the determination that we will complete this. Even sages have prayed to be born in this fourth era. Those who prayed like that have become gurus in this era. At least from now on, your prayers should be offered to God. This is the best time for a Shudra to become a Brahmin through his actions.

The sense of duty should not become distorted. If it does, the consequence is that one cannot even slightly comprehend the boundless greed for misdeeds that may accumulate. This current practical situation might be suitable for these times. It is a mistake only if it doesn't happen so. Because, in this earth that has been a fertile ground for goodness for a long time, if the evil of the desire for misdeeds does not occur, the virtues arising from the evil elements cannot be transformed. The words of the great souls might have been with this in mind. It has been described using 'Kali' itself - 'Kalikala Varnana' (Description of the Kali Yuga). The foresight, named 'Jnanadrishti' (vision of knowledge) or 'Darshana Drishti' (vision of philosophy), cannot be wrong. You should always have the sorrowful vision that an uncivilized guise, lacking the vigilance of how institutions sanctified by the words and deeds of great souls and the people operating them should be, might consume the world itself.

There are many reasons for such a sorrowful vision. The greed of the rulers, the dereliction of duty by householders, the hate-filled distortions of organizations, and the unfulfilled desire for sacrifice of the successors of ideal men are the main ones among them. That desire for sacrifice became a weightless measure. It became a posture where only language and attire have importance. A predatory nature is prevalent in all this. Through that predatory nature, seizing the governance of the country, sucking the blood of the nation, and manifesting religious conversion, denial, and destruction in a way that erases what is real, India was hijacked in the same way it was endangered for over two centuries, unable to distinguish between darkness and light. Occasionally, there are some lightning flashes. But they are also temptations in a shining guise. Succumbing to the enchanting beauty of this shining guise, the great nation of India and its people were crushed and became exhausted. Although the few survivors, who were crushed under the guises synonymous with deceit that flashed like lightning and were left with only their lives, realized the freedom they desired, distortions are overflowing to the extent that it seems it was a mistake.

Let me point out some of such distortions. Perhaps because the life left the body of that good man, Raja Ram Mohan Roy, those overcome by emotion severed the eyes of his statue. Another is that a statue of a holy son of Kerala was attacked, and the movement was treated negatively. If the children of Kerala themselves felt so, it means they did not understand that the love they desired has materialized a hundredfold in the form of this statue. The reason it is not understood is that Indian culture has been buried. Instead, a complete statue of Aryan culture has been raised. It is filled with malicious pride, capable of cutting and eating the true nature of man with its intellect. The previously mentioned flashing lightning is the result of a government that has lost its virtue, acting forgetting statecraft and human strategy. In the heat of this lightning of Aryan culture and governance strategy, Indian culture turned into ashes; along with some human remnants that were symbols of Indian culture. However, we also have some great men who rose from those ash heaps, combined the material and the spiritual, and created movements without borrowing ideas from the outside world - Buddha, Shankaracharya, Aurobindo, Sree Narayana Guru, Mahatma Gandhi. At the same time, some rulers and organizations, who have been corrupted by foreign cultures, dissolving their blood, marrow, and flesh in them, and refusing to leave them, perhaps unable even if they wanted to, have already corrupted those movements. Let me state one thing clearly. Such human remnants can only be rediscovered through philanthropic visionaries. That must be known through pain. Nothing that is not known through pain will have love.

A romantic obsessed with sinful love, who destroys the virtuous love of many, cannot discover this either. Danger lurks in romantic feelings that lack the discernment of good and evil. If there is a lack of knowledge about duty, as a result of a culture that reaches a state of being crippled by danger, animalistic cravings full of corruption and delusion will rise up, overturning all the good intentions of human society. That will lead to total destruction. I pray with the good desire that rulers and idealists with patience, foresight, and patriotism will use their intellect, effort, and lives to discover the previously mentioned visionary—an idea that cannot be corrupted by the time and actions of that visionary—and utilize it for world peace and harmony.

Guruvani
