[GV1:P1952] August 1988

[GV1:P1953] To rectify the errors that emerged in the novelty of antiquity and the antiquity of novelty, numerous great souls have incarnated on earth across various eras, known by different names such as prophets and avatars. It is evident that they all functioned in accordance with their respective times. When speaking of time, life itself constitutes a temporal span even for an ant. Similarly, for the animal kingdom, life is a dimension of time. This principle applies to everything from a mere blade of grass to the sprawling banyan tree, which achieves the absolute fullness of life within its species. The ancients utilize the banyan tree metaphorically in this exact sense. The fundamental reason for their growth is the presence of life. Contingent upon the variations of space and time, the cycle of growth, sustenance, and destruction is inherent not only in human beings but across all living creatures. Intelligence, too, manifests with distinct variations among them. What is the cause of this?

[GV1:P1954] Plants and trees are uprooted prematurely, and human beings face untimely demise. Those who perish in wars, those who lie waiting for death, and those who end their lives by hanging are all included in this paradigm. This encompasses those who believe that all these possess life, those who believe in the existence or non-existence of the power of sin, the intelligent and the unintelligent, the scientific scholars who consider themselves to have attained the pinnacle of intellect, and above all, the path of action, agency, and execution that operates without instruments or mediums—not merely on this earth but across the nine celestial spheres—directing the essence of the atomic life of the *Panchakoshas* (five sheaths), which has evolved through the five elements and is channeled into the five senses, configuring its distinct nature.

[GV1:P1955] When knowledge becomes subjugated to the lower nature,

[GV1:P1956] A fraction of a second feels like a thousand years.

[GV1:P1957] As stated by Narayana Guru, there can be no doubt for scientific or biological theorists that science would require thousands of years to thoroughly evaluate the virtues and vices of life encompassed within the evolutionary theory of the *Panchakoshas* of life. The process by which such an expansive operation of life creates wonders before humanity, most visibly through the earth, the sun, and the moon, is evident even to the most ignorant. Through the passage of eras, one can deduce that everyone—from demons who possess a subtle understanding of the potential of action extending to *Moola Ganapathi*, the lord of the foundational forces spanning from the elemental entities to the stellar constellations, to the *Vethalas*, the indwelling demigods who have attained the celestial realms, and even up to the holy Trinity (*Trimurti*)—is equally subject to pleasure and sorrow.

[GV1:P1958] The time referred to here is not the time mentioned earlier. The culmination of time occurs in terms of *Manvantaras*. The epoch of one *Manu* is documented as consisting of seventy-one *Chaturyugas*. The seventh *Manvantara* among these is the *Vaivasvata Manvantara*. We are currently in *Kali*, the fourth quarter of its twenty-eighth *Chaturyuga*. The *Dharma* (duty/righteousness) of the *Kaliyuga*, which is considered the fourth quarter (the others being *Satyayuga*, *Tretayuga*, and *Dvaparayuga*), is to contemplate and evaluate the virtues and sins accumulated through the actions of the preceding two *Yugas*, and to execute the actions as ordained by that divine (*Brahman*) resolve. This has been incorporated into the designated karma of the *Shudra* class. The term '*Shudra* class' denotes the ignorant, the despised, the lowly, and the caste-fanatics extending down to the *Chandala*. There was a system of karma that regulated even food consumption. The trajectory of karma that resulted in the categorization of emperors, kings, and Brahmins as the rulers not just of India but of the world, was a modern diversion of this. Although it is stated that the potent trajectory of all these karmas is destined to fall upon the *Shudra*, it is not easy to evaluate and articulate the ignorance that ensues when knowledge and capability fall into the hands of the world's *Chandalas*, who do not even understand for whom this is intended.

[GV1:P1959] This is because, when it fell into the hands of the ignorant *Shudra* class, and the context of the coming together of the Dravidian and the Aryan arose, bringing with it a succession of wars, victories, and defeats akin to karmic prowess, it is not difficult to observe that both the Dravidian and the Aryan equally harmonized their dedication, reliance, karma, and dharma—not merely in India but globally—to attain the true deity of fortune through the traditional system of divine worship. It has been proven that the most suitable lineage for this is the *Shudra* class. A proof of this lies in the divine voice (*Ashariri*) that occurred even prior to Sri Krishna's birth, warning that despite giving birth to him, he should not rule as a subordinate to the emperor. Since the king of that time could not be made to declare this, Krishna demonstrated it through his actions by slaying him and assuming the role of the rightful heir. It is in the sense that Krishna, who stood at the absolute forefront of divine status, embodied every deity, sage, ascetic, active participant, devotee, and prophet who had appeared until then, that the phrase "*Yatra Yatra Yogeshvara Krishna*" is uttered. In the visionary doctrine known as *Yugadharma*, asserting a prominent position, Sri Krishna profoundly and authoritatively expounded upon the duties of the age through the eighteen chapters of the *Gita*, while preceptors, gods, and sages provided an intimation of the future.

[GV1:P1960] Numerous prophets, gurus, and the lineage of the *Parayi Petta Panthirukulam* who came thereafter have vindicated this. It is evident that figures from the more recent past, such as Sri Shankara, Narayana Guru, and Chattampi Swami, emerged with distinct ideological missions. Whether they successfully achieved them or not is beside the point. Sri Shankara propounded Advaita, Madhvacharya advocated Dvaita, and Thiruvalluvar poetically championed the concept of 'One Caste, One Race, One God.' Chattampi Swami authored and completed the *Vedadhikara Nirupanam* to dismantle the ritualistic sections (*Karmakanda*) of the Vedic religion. Narayana Guru functioned practically, striving to elevate humanity through action.

[GV1:P1961] The dharma referred to here is the *Dharma* of the *Kaliyuga*. Whether a *Chandala*, a Brahmin, or anyone in between, the *Shudra* has advanced by acting in a way that makes him forget his own karma, merit, and prowess, which are essential for humans, as well as for the departed sages and gods, to attain liberation and virtue. The ultimate reward for a god or a sage thus far has been liberation. In order for this very same outcome to be realized for the entire *Shudra* class, an act in accordance with divine providence is outlined below. It proclaims a truth that is indispensable not just for a householder, but for absolutely everyone.

[GV1:P1962] The rationalist and the salvation-seeker, stricken by paralysis, are swaying unconsciously after consuming poisonous liquor to the brim. Each of us is a human being embodying moral decay, trying to pose as a scientist or a research scholar. This is because they are petty creatures who mix up the scientific and the unscientific without hesitation, eager to denounce each of the aforementioned. Today's rational beings, operating without distinction between the scholar and the ignorant, losing both *karma* and *dharma*, are like plants uprooted from a gymnasium. Consequently, the scholar, the ignorant, and everyone else caught between them are eradicating their lifespans within this vortex. To escape from this, I am utilizing this occasion to inform you of a clear and resolute matter.

[GV1:P1963] Discovered through states described as ethereal, manifest, and luminous, a path of karmic resolution is taking place here. This path enables one to act and cause others to act benevolently for all living beings in the world, while seeking the cessation of sorrow in my twenty-nine years of life. The *Guru Margam* (Path of the Guru) advised at Santhigiri is the greatest path of resolution, which, through a life of obedience according to the divine will (*Brahmanishchayam*), removes sorrow, brings merit through action, and carefully preserves *dharma* as its foundation. This *dharmic* life, which must be acquired with vigilance, is meant to reach not just humans but all living beings, by making each person enact the chapters of *dharma* that span from the atom to the infinite in the universe, thereby realizing it within oneself. It not only allows us to preserve it in our actions by theorizing the evolutionary paths of the solar system that we previously pervaded, but it has also become capable of achieving the complete realization of the lineage. Its detailed explanation is not to be publicized but only spoken of in person. Informing you that the medium that helps all people, forgetting caste or religion, to arrive at the singular doctrine of humanity is something you can trust and inspire trust in, and wishing you eternal peace so that this auspicious day may benefit you by enabling you to progress through it and, if necessary, making yourself worthy of liberation,

[GV1:P1964] Your Swami
