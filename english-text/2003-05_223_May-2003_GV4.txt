[GV4:P0824] May 2003

[GV4:P0825] I have not failed to see what the Acharyas have acquired and given to the world. However, some of them have introduced practices such as telling miraculous tales at the time of leaving their bodies, attaining dissolution by jumping into a river to discard the body, and the practice of wrapping the body tightly in a thousand cubits of cloth and immersing it in the river Ganga. Among another class of ascetics, there is the practice of sacrificing themselves by jumping into a gorge. Thus, our tradition has operated with many deceptive miracles hidden within rituals. You must think about what can be done to resolve the insignificance that has flowed away and perished in this lack of meaning.

[GV4:P0826] I am narrating my experience. It took many years to identify the aforementioned practices for what they are. Despite a state of helplessness, wondering what I was seeing, what was being said, and where all this would lead, I did not stop my quest. At one point, it was even said that I was beaten and driven out of Varkala. There was also a group who claimed that I left with two young women and was never seen again. Thus, there was a time when such talks amused me. I left Varkala in accordance with an intimation I received, which instructed me not to remain in a place where two people could see me. From there, I went to a house in Manamboor. The people of that house would frequently come and invite me. Two acquaintances also accompanied me. I moved because I received an ethereal voice (ashareeri) warning me that staying there would be dangerous. Those who came with me are the ones who dropped me at Manamboor. While there, someone came with a car to pick me up. I went to Paravoor with him. Upon reaching there, I witnessed the celebrations and affairs of an annual death anniversary ritual (aandushraddham). An evil spirit arrived there. After observing its activities, I returned to Manamboor the next day. When I returned, I was met with quite a reception. This house was situated at the entrance of a fish market. The shops and other properties there belonged to the owners of this house. The people in those shops were waiting with weapons like sticks and wooden clubs. The homeowner, perhaps sensing something at that moment, told me, 'Swami, do not stay here now. You should leave immediately. I will explain things later.' He brought me a dhoti (mundu) and gave it to me. And I left. Meanwhile, they were standing there raising their sticks and clubs, shouting loudly. By God's grace, nothing happened there. I had no idea why all these preparations were made. Without knowing anything, I departed. Only later did I understand the matter.

[GV4:P0827] Because the mother in that house was cured of her illness and the daughter was saved from danger, they used to visit me often. I had written a letter to her, 'Child, you are living in a respectable household. Do not forget that. Whatever the relationship between you two may be, I will talk and resolve it.' Seeing this, the mother and father felt that I had started to lead their child astray. I had sent this letter four years before I went to that house. It was a letter sent after performing a ritual for them. It was this letter that they finally received four years later when I arrived there. That is when they developed this 'affection' for me and felt like 'handling me properly,' as mentioned earlier.

[GV4:P0828] Always, my experience has been like this. Wherever I have gone, I have faced situations where I had to live as an object of humiliation. Even after coming here and spending six or seven years, such an experience occurred. Many patients come here, get treated (solely with sacred ash, holy water, and prayer), and are being cured. Among them, a woman was brought here by her husband for the cure of a mental illness; after being cured, she was taken back home. A few days later, that woman, accompanied by a relative, came to the ashram and expressed a desire to stay for two days. The next day, she was nowhere to be seen. She had not gone anywhere else. She was inside the well. Thus, incidents have emerged in life and performed a destructive dance of devastation. However, all this was not something a human could endure. That I could endure it is solely due to the grace of God. In this manner, in every state—by state, I mean the realm a sanyasi or a brahmachari ought to attain—I am achieving them one by one. How this happens is, according to the calculations of scientists existing in this world, reaching the level of atomic science (whether they call it energy, power, the magnificence of nature, or whatever name they give it)—it is the nature in which life and soul evolve through the covering of the five elements we constantly experience today, the knowledge of the five senses, and the realization of the five sheaths. To integrate all this so perfectly, no other science is of use except the assistance of efforts spanning multiple lifetimes. Because this is a truth within my experience, I am imparting it to you.

[GV4:P0829] An opportunity will arise to understand the experiential truth that is part of a greatness. Otherwise, it cannot be said that these are the adverse effects resulting from me going after any sanyasi, siddha, or deity. We possess a lineage that has been able to perceive up to the system of the Trinity as a visibly manifest dharma. You can firmly believe that it exists. Because, we cannot simply discard the actions performed and caused to be performed by our ancestors. That must come. And it must be grasped right along with it. That is the way to proceed. But when grasping it, you must have knowledge.

[GV4:P0830] What is that knowledge? When a god, goddess, sage, sanyasi, wise man, devotee, and person of action arrive, if there is no lineage of Gurus to impart and make them experience eternal peace, you should not take that path. If you do, you will perish. I have the evidence of thousands of experiences for this. Since that evidence is exclusively mine, I am unbothered by what anyone else might say. I have not embraced any of the traditions I grew up in. Initially, I just walked through them all. Then, I understood the extent of their knowledge. By God's grace, I acquired that knowledge. Taking further years, I comprehended its various aspects. Only after that did I wish to say anything to the people. It has only been a short while since I started speaking.

[GV4:P0831] How should you uphold this lineage? In the course of your karma up to this day, whatever demon, god, goddess, sage, sanyasi, or wise man has entered your guidance, the Guru's greatness will bestow all of it upon you in the animate and inanimate, in form and formlessness, and in light. Or else, it will be made known to you through the Guru's actions. Beyond that, you have received the lineage of a doctrine that is systematically explained. When you merge into this experiential truth, you must be careful about one thing. All you have to suffer, love, and respect is sacrifice. You have not been given wealth and other capabilities merely to rejoice. Let God Himself grant you wealth and capability. Let the fortunate ones enjoy it. Or let the fortunate lineage of descendants enjoy it. Do not let wealth and capability be destroyed through ignorance.

[GV4:P0832] If you hold on to a Guru, that Guru must be able to explain everything from demons to the realm of vampires, and beyond that, what the nature of the Trinity is, in which heaven they exist, and which sphere is their abode. Otherwise, you will fall into a trap, caught in the tumult of magical powers and thousands of tantric practices, and in the actions and inactions of royal servants. Living as subjects of all this, you do not realize that you are being pushed into the deep abyss of karma, capturing a poor section of people possessing some knowledge and making them fight each other. This is my knowledge, proven through experience. You who believe here possess that proof. Whatever demon, god, or goddess appears before you, if told to leave, they will leave. Or, if you say 'you will be burnt to ashes' two or three times, it will be reduced to ashes. That is the power of your Guru. In the name of the Guru, it will be consumed by fire. With that caution, you will be able to grasp this. Then, by earning merit through the karma of this path of dharma, a state of complete prosperity that you must collectively attain is encapsulated before you. You must perceive it broadly and pass it on to the children who have reached the stage of karma. That should be the supreme goal of life.

[GV4:P0833] We know thousands of sacrificial rituals. Thinking that one can attain completion by holding onto the karmic flaws of the Ashwamedha Yaga is ignorance and a path to destruction. It is a path that sows destruction and reaps destruction. When adopting the path of a Guru, one must believe in it completely. You should not hold onto it without substance and take it into your mind. You must be able to change from that method, no matter the hardship. Sitting and chanting mantras, observing a satyagraha in front of a God fortified by caste and religion will not yield any results. Nor has it ever. Those who live adhering to this must also make others adhere to it. This is the path God has given you to emerge forever from the deep pit of ignorance in which your father and mother were trapped. I desire that you should be able to grasp a great doctrine by traveling exclusively along that path. For that, what has been worshipped as Pournami is the most appropriate.

[GV4:P0834] Guruvani
