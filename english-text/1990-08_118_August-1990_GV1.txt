[GV1:P2560] August 1990

[GV1:P2561] Beloved human souls, please heed this appeal to God.

[GV1:P2562] The news we are currently receiving through newspaper columns and other media is undoubtedly capable of bringing anyone with a human heart to tears. Who would not weep at the events that occurred in Iran a few months ago, and in various other countries prior to that, as well as the news of an entire island being washed away? All those events were so heartbreaking that they compelled a priest to lament, "Father, where are you?" We have already been apprised of that news, published alongside the priest's photograph, as well as other news of similar intensity. Using microphones and notices, the Santhigiri Ashram had conducted extensive campaigns across the land, warning of an impending scarcity of rain and ensuing droughts. Following this, the likelihood of natural disasters was detailed with causal explanations through letters. This is a matter of public knowledge. Numerous individuals in the northern districts of Kerala paid utmost attention to the announcements from Santhigiri. During that period, an influx of letters from those regions continuously reached the ashram. This is also being conveyed to the lineage of ashram believers with that recollection in mind. The transformation that occurred in the state of affairs predicted by the Santhigiri Ashram within that very year was astonishing, even surpassing the forecasts of meteorological observers.

[GV1:P2563] One must reflect on whether prayer yields results

[GV1:P2564] It was nature that unleashed this devastation in Iran, Russia, and Andhra, was it not? Considering the high population density, one cannot even bear to imagine the situation if such a calamity had struck Kerala. Therefore, we must remember one thing: whatever we cast into nature must be done with the thought of God. Let those who act gently continue to do so. Even if they have amassed vast arsenals of war, because they adopt amicable stances without voluntarily preparing for war, only tens of thousands might perish in their lands where millions would have otherwise died. By the grace of God, no major wars will occur anywhere. Pray for divine grace. (As indicated earlier, the Santhigiri Ashram had prophesied impending natural disasters a year in advance, prayed for their mitigation, and urged the lineage of believers to sustain that remembrance. The resultant outcomes must also be observed in this light.) Even if a war occurs, it will only be on a minor scale. Pray for that as well.

[GV1:P2565] There is a possibility of natural disasters occurring on a massive scale; pray that their magnitude is diminished.

[GV1:P2566] Although the administrators are taking numerous measures to raise awareness among the people, they are proving ineffective. The reason for this is that the administrators are not embracing the contemplation of God. We know that many administrators have proclaimed that rivers of milk and honey would flow if they were given power. However, upon attaining power, they are unable to eradicate even the wicked thoughts of the populace. It would be prudent for human souls and administrators alike to reflect upon how even the industrial sector has been irrevocably altered. The contemplation of God is the panacea for all of this. Let us pray that the omnipresent divine forces of the world bless us by bestowing unprecedented goodness.

[GV1:P2567] A plea to those who are striving for wealth and vast fortunes. Are you unable to comprehend that the mischief your children engage in, even if temporarily, in colleges and other places is not only disgraceful but also sheer mockery that sows the seeds for their own destruction and that of the world?

[GV1:P2568] Until 1997, various kinds of hardships are likely to occur globally. Hardships of various kinds, including floods, droughts, and earthquakes. From 1997, they will begin to subside, and from 2005 onwards, tranquility will begin to prevail. As a remedy for these calamities, the people of the world are advised to abandon the concepts of deities such as Madan and Marutha, and conduct collective prayers, appealing to God within the framework of monotheism that is appropriate for this era.

[GV1:P2569] Those who deny the belief in one God* are leading an alien life. That is, they live merely to think, speak, and revel in a depraved existence. They are those who waste their lives by asserting ownership over inflicting as much oscillation as possible in the lives of the outcaste, the king, the ascetic, the wise, and the doer of action. This is the underlying motive behind their historical criticism and destruction of Gurus, and their continued destruction even today. Therefore, Vedic scholars must reflect deeply. The Vedic tradition in the Treta Yuga was conducted as a cruel sport. Need one narrate the story of a man who suffocated a thousand horses to death? The affliction known as Kali has materialized in nature, threatening to devour this human being completely. I hereby convey to you the Divine Word, a decree from the Supreme Nature of Eternal Peace, which loudly proclaims that unless you act with the consciousness that today's custom will become tomorrow's science, you are heading towards actions that will drag the entire world into destruction.

[GV1:P2570] Guruvani

[GV1:P2571] September 1990

[GV1:P2572] Guru: Most of the kings in South India are non-Brahmins. They rule according to the dictates of the Brahmins. Upon seeing a person wearing the sacred thread, if he formally prescribes a few ritualistic practices, the king immediately accords him due reverence, performs *padapuja* (washing the feet), and serves him a grand feast. If the person is slightly more learned, he is welcomed with a *nirakumbham* (a sacred full pitcher). This is considered a duty befitting the royal dignity.

[GV1:P2573] Disciple: But shouldn't this be done to a true knower of Brahman (*Brahmajñāni*)?

[GV1:P2574] Guru: Is this not a reality as well? To speak in the language of you intellectuals, is this not the reality? Do you not believe only what you can see with your own eyes? Do you not dismiss everything unseen as mere blind superstition? Therefore, it is impossible for you to perceive a true Brahmin. Are you not merely embracing a fool and degrading yourselves to the state of a dog?

[GV1:P2575] However, we have a correction for this path. That is why we have performed this *karma*. We have followed the path shown by God. As mentioned earlier, what we have here is either a lineage of billions of followers who believed under one preceptor, or the foundation of a glorious life and *karma* of that tradition. Can we discard this? Even to discard it requires divine permission. That is what is meant by saying it was shown to us.

[GV1:P2576] We discarded what needed to be discarded exactly as instructed. Such a *karma* has been established for the sake of future generations. Once one becomes part of this lineage, they can grasp (connect with) the ancestors (*pitrus*). No one else has the permission to grasp the ancestors. Only we possess that authority.

[GV1:P2577] Disciple: In the ancestral purification (*pitrushuddhi*) of the old methods—as the Guru mentioned, the practice of grasping the ancestors—such a method existed among the Hindus, didn't it? Does it not have any value at all?

[GV1:P2578] Guru: In the present context, it has no value. This is because the ancestors (*pitrus*) have been tied beneath the deities (*devas*). *Kali Yuga* is not the era of the deities. *Kali* emerged as a correction to the sage (*rishi*) tradition. It belongs to the *Shudra*. That correction must come from the *Shudra* himself. For the correction to emerge from a *Shudra*, who must he become? The *Shudra* must become a sage, a realized soul (*jnani*), and a liberated one (*mukta*). He must rise above the deities. There are several individuals mentioned in our scriptures—sages like Agastya, Pulastya, etc. They are of such nature. Twenty-five percent of them belong to the lower castes (*Shudras*). We failed to imbibe the wisdom that originated from them. Sri Krishna is the primordial Guru (*Adiguru*) of that lineage. Unable to honor Sri Krishna, we ended up in the mockery we witness today. We fell victim to the haste of transforming Krishna into Vishnu and presenting him in another form. It is no longer possible to draw anything from that. It refers to the multitude of preceptors born from then until now. Imagine, despite giving birth to so many Gurus over the past five thousand years, we could not assimilate their wisdom! The world produced individuals everywhere. Despite being born in myriad forms—as sages, realized souls, prophets, ascetics, Gurus, and yogis—we failed to understand them. However, all of them were looking backward at what their predecessors had said. Perhaps observing this, Ashan (the poet Kumaran Asan) said, "fortresses were built early on by the *Smritis*." They proved that fortresses could be built using *Shrutis* and *Smritis*. The preceptors who came before were unfortunately unable to break through this barrier. Because such a dead-end scriptural system prevailed here, if anyone came with a new idea and was seen ready to grant freedom to the poor, they did not hesitate to destroy that idea completely. Furthermore, when they looked backward, they became just like today's intellectuals.

[GV1:P2579] What are the intellectuals doing here? They are unable to control themselves or their own households. What else can they achieve then? If they groom a few youngsters to be like them, what disaster will not befall this land? Is anything else required for the downfall of a nation? They brought about *karma* merely through literacy. The Westerners achieved all this with just twenty-six letters. Growth inside a block of ice!

[GV1:P2580] We have a climate where we can survive with just a small towel (*thorthu*), or even without one if necessary. Changing all this, they made us adopt all the attires they used to protect themselves from extreme cold. And we accepted it. They provided us with every means possible to make a mockery of us. We embraced it. Thus, arriving as traders, they looted all the greatness that existed here and established plastic companies in its place. Is this not all that we have managed to achieve?

[GV1:P2581] Disciple: We have lost everything due to orthodox customs. We currently stand in a state of cultural and spiritual slavery. Can we attain liberation from that slavery through this new path of ours?

[GV1:P2582] Guru: What I am stating is a matter beyond any doubt. A gross manifestation of what can be practically implemented is established here. *Sankalpa Karma Manasa* is given as the medium of intention (*sankalpa*). That is, we have the authority to grasp the ancestors as mentioned earlier. Along with that, the authority to grasp the deity and as many ancestors who have gone under the deity's domain. The authoritative action (*karma*) that, having grasped them, grants liberation to what needs liberation, *karma* to what needs *karma*, *dharma* to what needs *dharma*, and destroys what needs to be destroyed. We are doing this much for a family. After doing this for a family, in that place, worship of God (*Ishvara Bhajana*) is given as the path of the Guru. This refers to the ancestors who have been worshipped, ranging from those placed in sacred groves (*kavus*) to the *Brahmarakshas*. Thus, the Guru represents the glory of a tradition that operates by knowing everything. What does that glory ordain? The *karma* of molding the masters of *dharma* by bringing them to the realm of wisdom-action (*jnana-karma*). Do you not understand that a family man (*dharmī*) who thus becomes the master of *dharma* is entitled to everything? This is our path.
