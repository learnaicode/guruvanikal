GURUVANIKAL · November 1995 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0942 to GV2:P0955
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

November 1995

To explain and see nature, one needs seven thousand four hundred and forty-four visions. There are seven such worlds. One group calls them heaven, the netherworld, this world, and the other world. Another group dismisses this saying no such thing exists.

Karma is said to be the active capacity of life. Karma and the vision originating from it are connected to the worlds. So, for the worlds to cease to exist, shouldn't the active capacity of life come to an end? How long does life function? Or, one must look at how long it has been since karma began to cease, like a dried-up tree standing still. Saying it doesn't exist won't make it so. When that tree lying in the soil completely decays into the soil itself, that karma will end. Similarly, no one has the right to say that this karmic world—where humans are born and raised, ruling over and being ruled by thousands of people, and performing karma through life—does not exist. Can the one who says it doesn't exist refrain from using this earth? If one wants to argue with words that all this is an illusion and none of this exists, such a person should stop using this earth. One walks around without wearing clothes. Another dresses up in all sorts of costumes and walks around like a clown. One walks around showing various antics like a madman. Some walk around showing all the qualifications that the civilized world calls merit, while having no actual merit at all. If one is perverse, another engages in the karma of eternal existence without any perversity whatsoever. Who can say that this karma, which exists in thousands of such ways, does not exist?

Why is he born who says he is neither born nor dead? Why should he eat, not eat, speak, or not speak? Vedantins say that there is only a feeling that 'I am'. Then let his body be cut down. Does he who says 'no' not think that he has to eat, dress, bite, lie down, and walk? If a person - a sannyasin - uses various medicines and takes care of his body, then he does not need food or anything else. He goes to some place and sits there. Even if a snake bites him, only the snake will die. If he goes and sits in a place like this, he can sit there for thousands of years without knowing anything. Even if someone who has been sitting like this without knowing anything for six or seven generations wakes up now, he will ask if the king who ruled then is still ruling today.

Someone from Mexico came and told me, Carlos, you saw there, saw here, did such and such things, I have done such and such things for so many births. This is a vision. Thousands of people come and tell me what they see in thousands of ways. They have done good and bad things. He experiences all these karmas or, in the form of maya, surrounds him and disappears. Or this body is the evolution of the jiva that has been surrounding him for thousands of births. He will talk about the many births he has had with that body and the thousands of karmas he has done.

What is all this seen? Waking, dreaming, deep sleep. In it, you can see a lineage that believes in a guru. If you ask standing in that lineage, there will be people capable of explaining. That is called the traditional guru-disciple relationship. Didn't I give a proof - Carlos said thousands of births. That is the guru-disciple relationship. If this guy sitting in Mexico saw this sitting there and had to tell it up to here! So many people like that.

This earth has a universal truth, a special power. The earth stands as a sphere. Similarly, this sky filled with thousands of spheres. The sun and the other nine planets circling as a solar system. These nine also rotate. Only the sun remains stationary - the solar system! Connecting with this solar system, twenty-seven stars, twelve zodiac signs, and nine planets have been calculated. There is no need to do anything to the person mentioned by measuring, adding, and subtracting its size and distance. He didn't come for any of that. What has he become? How bad has he become, how good has he become - he only thinks about this. The guru only explains this. There is a secret of life standing in the nature of many jivas, knowing and unknowingly, in the measurement and weight of this star and in its cause and effect. This cannot be known in one lifetime. This is where the guru's importance lies.

Seeing such worlds and paths of travel, he and the guru are talking. He and the guru are talking about good and bad. This is the journey to the path of truth. The guru will not ask if you have had such experiences in life. The disciple must tell the guru. Then the guru explains this secret of life, like Sri Ramakrishna to Vivekananda. Sri Ramakrishna reached a certain position and showed that position to his disciple.

In waking, dreaming, deep sleep, and then in both states of consciousness, one will see without any special rules. He is seeing his life and his karma-residue, in waking, dreaming, deep sleep, and in sitting and in both. It is in sitting that this turns - what god, what goddess

What is it, what is a sage, what is a sannyasi, what is a guru, thus the difference in gurus becomes clear. When it comes to that, the guru must teach it. Not just seeing, it can be seen clearly. Then, who is Vivekananda, who are the great sages who have come so far, who are the prophets who have come so far. All this must be included in this guru. One who is not like that is not a guru. Everything will be clearly obtained.

Thus, in this guru-disciple relationship over thousands of births, thousands of people would have been seen. There is no need to say whether it exists or not. It has been coming and going for many births like this. But the only relationship one has - only oneself and the guru. Even though one has thousands of relationships over many births, in the end, one sees only oneself and the guru. Therefore, no one else but the guru can fulfill this life of one's own.

The course of karma of one person is not like the course of karma of another person. Therefore, the guru does not interact with another person in the same way as interacting with one person. We will see ourselves involved in the karma of the whole world. All this will be seen in dreams. After the guru explains and teaches about the waking, dreaming, and deep sleep states, he will then pass on to the turiya state. The nature of the guru is to come in many characters, removing and taking up, doing and making do, knowing and making known, all this course of karma in the nature of all the prophets and all the gurus who have come so far. Vedantins ask why one looks at all these coming as mother, father, wife, children, husband, and so on in thousands of ways. If Vedantins interpret with letters, will this karma disappear?

Guru consciousness is of many kinds. One has to learn from the guru what a solar system is like. Thus, when passing through the seven spheres, form is lost. Then there is no form. Jesus talks about the third sphere in it. That an angel comes among the silver clouds. Thus, after each sky sphere, when passing through six sky spheres, form will disappear.

Now, this earth has a sky sphere. The sky sphere is called the open space. Thus, all seven sky spheres have open space. Thus, all nine sky spheres have open space. These differences must be corrected by the guru himself. There is no other medicine for it.
