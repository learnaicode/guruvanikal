[GV4:P1080] November 2005

[GV4:P1081] We have received a new method. There are four eras namely Satya Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dvapara Yuga, and Kali Yuga, and each era has its own distinct nature. The dharmas of the four eras operate in four different ways. In the Satya Yuga, men and women will function without error. The Satya Yuga has the combined duration of the other three yugas. The change of time and the dharma of time is God's will. That is stated in the Manusmriti. We, human beings, are the children of those who belonged to the lineage of Manu and worked desiring the presence of God.

[GV4:P1082] Hinduism has a very long history and an account of its own. Compared to Hinduism, Christianity's account is very brief. Hinduism is more than just a religion; it is a culture. The invading Brahmins settled on the banks of the five rivers and studied our history. They were those who saw God through their intellect. What they found here was a group of honest people devoid of deceit. They were people who would sleep wherever they pleased. They arrived at a time when the era was undergoing a change. Because times had changed, they could not see God directly. Consequently, they went to the forests and seashores, gazed at the horizon, and performed penance in various ways. They saw different kinds of light and forms. They carved what they saw onto trees and stones. When they contemplated it in their minds, they received words from the light. They received mantras. Thus, the great sages discovered each of these things. They carved many of them on stones, giving them form and color. Westerners called it the mind. The consciousness that is the mind, the light that is consciousness, that is the truth. We must learn to realize that it is the truth. We are those who perceive the truth not with the intellect, but with our eyes. The immigrant Brahmins came here and began to practice various things. They began to rule the country intelligently. They made strong-armed brutes into kings. They made them gods. They made people believe so. The Treta Yuga and the Dwapara Yuga were thus ruled by 'manifested gods'. In the Dwapara, the truth began to manifest. Finally, Krishna came. With that, it became evident that all this exists and that God can reveal what exists. This is history.

[GV4:P1083] The Aryans who studied history created caste. Historically speaking, Kali Yuga belongs to the Shudra. That means he is the one with the authority to handle governance. He is entitled to liberation. Then, intelligently, they put a price even on the alphabet. They took control of the Vedic realm. Then they made the Shudras beat the Vaishyas. This diverted the course of the time's dharma. Instead, for at least two or three generations, the Vaishya beat the Shudra. Thus, by doing slave labor, the Shudra became synonymous with the ignorant. Subjected to untouchability and ostracized for more than ten generations, the Shudra class became dark and exhausted, doing slave labor without food. After that, they became arrogant and great sinners. They became subjects of God's wrath.

[GV4:P1084] It is God's decree that there will be no king at the end of Kali Yuga. Otherwise, a king should have been ruling all this time. Thus, the one who chased and the one who ran both fell into the pit together. They lost their fatherhood (lineage). The one who lost his fatherhood died, decayed, and that 'pitru' (ancestral spirit) wandered, changed its astrological sign, and fathered fatherless children. In the meantime, many great souls came and said that you should see this irony of fate. Then these fatherless children called this great soul fatherless and chased him away with beatings. Thus, they caused Muslims to rule this place.

[GV4:P1085] In Kerala, the Brahmin was accepted as the Vedic authority. Because of his cunning, we were kept in the dark about everything. The English who came here were not gentlemen. The one who was useless there became the Viceroy here. They came here and created the attire, language, education, law, and government.

[GV4:P1086] Today, the era of hybridization has arrived. There is hybridization even in humans and a blade of grass. This is a very serious matter. We must protect God's word without passing this on to our children. God is in your favor. Jesus and the Prophet have made that clear. That messengers of God will come and mislead. Initially, I had doubts about that. Even when an Avadhuta repeatedly said this at Varkala Santhigiri, I doubted it. But the scolding I had to endure to discipline the girls was not insignificant. It was only when I finally received the revelation that I became convinced.

[GV4:P1087] We need a certainty here. This is not for a caste or a community. If Thiruvalluvar and Naranath Bhranthan did this, can't we do it? Think that if my gotra (lineage) will improve, I might even die for it. If those coming to the institution are worthless, they must be improved by enduring any sacrifice. We can certainly take this forward. Because through the Prophet, through Jesus, through Sree Narayana Guru, and through Chattampi Swami, God has given us the experience and qualification suited for the times. Even if we are cut to pieces, we can bravely say—didn't they nail Jesus....... We will build the dignity of the Guru. Jesus was ready to be crucified. Shankaracharya offered his head. But his disciples opposed it.

[GV4:P1088] A renunciate will endure anything to instill goodness in the hearts of others. A disciple must be extremely attentive. Devotion without attentiveness is useless. It is attentive devotion (Shraddha Bhakti). If that is the case, one will know whatever happens anywhere. One must do anything for those who come to be cured of their illnesses. One must do it even if it means being cut into pieces. There are those who speak Vedanta only for the good of their own children. That is not right; those who come for service will undoubtedly attain whatever they need from here. A position must be assigned to such people. One must be able to accomplish what is needed at the right time. Do not wait for a good time to come later. You might not get it sometimes. This requires a sacrifice of its own. Once that is understood through action, there is nothing more to attain beyond it. If there is sacrifice, one will not have to bow down anywhere. I lived in greater humility than you, in a state where all qualifications were subordinated to this. You do not need a better example than this. You can carry this model with utmost pride. No supernatural power (siddhi) or knowledge is required for this. The heartfelt experience you have received is sufficient. You should pray that this heartfelt experience sustains you till the end.

[GV4:P1089] Do not oppose those who do good deeds. Do not insult them. It is difficult for the greatness of ashram life to manifest in you. There are two reasons for that. First, South Indian kings in the Dravidian culture were Shudras. Second, the North Indian culture did not reach those from the Nair caste downwards. Therefore, you will not acquire their character. Furthermore, you observed and learned the lower forms of worship from Tamil Nadu. By giving importance to Shakta worship, people became ignorant. A section of such ignorant people became Christians. Instead of learning good things, they copied European culture here. Thus, Hindu culture ceased to exist here.
