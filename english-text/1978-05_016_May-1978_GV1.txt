[GV1:P0298] May 1978

[GV1:P0299] Question: What do you mean by religion of God?

[GV1:P0300] Answer: While Moses, Christ, and the Prophet came in Western countries, during that same time, Buddha, Shankaracharya, Dayananda Saraswati, and Guru Nanak came, and recently Sree Narayana also came embracing all this. Among this, it can be known that Buddhism, accepted as a government recognition, was somewhat implemented here. Due to the followers' custody or code of conduct or flaw, it moved away from here. Even though it went to other countries, a situation has arisen there too where it cannot be practiced. In India, which strongly accepts the oneness principle of Shankaracharya who taught Advaita, why are the antics of Dvaita itself dancing around? Similarly, recently, all the ideas told by Narayana Guru are being made like dog faeces wrapped in fine silk or mixing filth in a golden pot and calling it a pot of nectar. A solution to all this, if you carefully bring it about with an experienced guide and the aforementioned help and cooperation, the world can reach a path of radiant initiation suited to the times.

[GV1:P0301] Question: I can only proceed if you tell me what more you have to suggest regarding this.

[GV1:P0302] Answer: India has two philosophical initiations. One is the puranic natures of the Gods and their lineages. The other is Manu and the Gurus who were their lineages. The special powers of the Gods, their hymns - or the special hymns about them - Kali is not the time to implement them. We should understand that Kali is meant to highlight the evils and goods that have come up to this day, destroy the evil, and re-establish the good. Avoid the antics of worshipping some demi-gods who are not avatars. Carefully understand and respect the legends that have come through avatars. Recognizing that is only possible if there is a Guru. That Guru should not be a person standing under the Gods. They should be those who have left and known the triad of gunas. This is what Narayana Guru says

[GV1:P0303] The lamp that shines when the triputi ends

[GV1:P0304] Cannot be obtained by a fake ascetic-

[GV1:P0305] One must remember the secret of the Upanishadic utterance

[GV1:P0306] That is what is said. The lineage that goes and knows above that is the lineage of Manus. But due to the glory of time or the aforementioned glory of intellect, we can record that they too made some mistakes. If we carefully examine like that, we can see them trying to convince by showing some special siddhis and superhuman natures. Vedanta denies this. But seeing that these very people who try to apply Vedanta have handled it, we have to doubt whether they haven't made a mistake. Thus, in our knowledge, the knowledge obtained from the light of God is also like that, stating without doubt whether these people haven't made a mistake.

[GV1:P0307] Question: Have you received it in experience?

[GV1:P0308] Answer: Yes, there is. These are things received through the knowledge gained by me and some children with me, who have not received any knowledge of Vedanta, science, or anything else. We did not have the realization of Manus, Gurus, or Devas. When we were suffering and thought we would not be saved, and that evil practices—our own practices—would be imposed on us forever in this world, by the grace of God, in the light of this adverse experience of about twenty-one long years, all this was directly revealed to us. It is not possible to describe it here. It can only be explained by keeping experienced people and those who bless with belief close to us.

[GV1:P0309] Question: Are you saying that these two lineages went wrong?

[GV1:P0310] Answer: Yes, that is what has been said so far.

[GV1:P0311] Question: Does what you have said so far mean that there is no doubt in this path that you have known through belief?

[GV1:P0312] Answer: Yes, there is absolutely no doubt.

[GV1:P0313] Question: So, does what you said mean that if we act by believing and knowing the path you have received, it can be made complete?

[GV1:P0314] Answer: I did not just say it. I demonstrated it through action—by the grace of God. One—children without scientific knowledge explaining and understanding the origin, destruction, existence, and causal nature of the creation of a world!

[GV1:P0315] Two—can you not see in worldly family members and their children the realization of God in the experience of their hearts, or the experiential school of truth-seeking that should happen through a Guru?

[GV1:P0316] Question: Will you tell us the rules and regulations we need to follow for this?

[GV1:P0317] Answer: It is by teaching that, that such experiences are seen here.

[GV1:P0318] Question: We know that there are people who have had such experiences and reached here without seeing or knowing you. That is why we had to ask this.

[GV1:P0319] Answer: Yes, that is true. It was said that love is the main factor in this. When it is seen that you believe in someone who, knowingly or unknowingly, has maintained truth and sacrifice and become worthy of God's compassion—meaning someone who is clear in this path, that is, the path we received—when about five thousand people start believing, God will shower His mercy on some blessed souls keeping this Guru as the foundation. That is why the authors of the Bhagavata have conceptualized that 'My devotee is Myself'.
