[GV1:P2614] December 1990

[GV1:P2615] We Indians have become so corrupted that even nature has undergone changes. The disrupted sequence of seasons and the severity of the climate are not the true character of Indian nature. The merit (punya) of Bharat has diminished. From Sri Krishna onwards, efforts have been made to remedy this decline in merit. Not just Buddha and Shankaracharya, but all great souls up to Sri Narayana Guru have attempted it. However, we have not been able to fulfill it. We speak very highly of the great deeds of Sri Krishna, Buddha, and others. We claim that everything we do is in accordance with what they preached. But nothing we do aligns with their teachings. What they supposedly said is just what the scholars have claimed. How did this fabrication occur among us?

[GV1:P2616] We had neither the authority over the Vedas nor religious education. All we had were the poems written by scholars and the stories behind those poems. Even today, that is all there is. If this system is to change, something new must happen like an event. These literate individuals will not allow that to happen. Modern poets will not agree at all. This is because all that they have written will need to be changed, right? Even the methods of the government must change. Since this ideology is what sustains our concepts, no one can like it. No one will accept it either. If the people themselves undergo a change through a certain movement, this group will also change. If they are told that their course of action is flawed, even if they become convinced of it, they will not change it. This is the current state of affairs in this country.

[GV1:P2617] The Gurus came to change this. We would agree that whatever they said is good and acceptable. But have we been able to obey or adopt even a single word of it?

[GV1:P2618] Let me share the knowledge I have gained through spiritual realization. We have assumed that there is nothing beyond the Holy Trinity (Trimurti). Not just the Trinity, but whatever demons exist, they are all real. Those who make sacrifices and the fortunate ones can even see them with their own eyes. If one attains a life worthy of seeing such things, there is a tendency in this realm that prevents one from crossing over to the beyond. When the state of being able to cross over was achieved, many shortcomings were alleged. All of that was endured. Previous acharyas (spiritual teachers) also had to endure this. Under such circumstances, Jesus was crucified. It is not even certain where Shankaracharya's samadhi (resting place) is. The Prophet was whipped and beaten with brooms. Dayananda Saraswati was poisoned to death. How greatly was Sri Narayana Guru tormented? But no one has written the history of their sufferings. Isn't it considered improper to write so? We know a little bit. Beyond the claims made by their contemporaries, nothing can be known. Had the truth been told as it is and made clear to the younger generation, they would not have fallen into blindness. Subsequent generations were deceived by tales of superhuman traits. Therefore, it became impossible to believe anything. Because it was through realization, the true history could be understood. That is solely due to God's grace.

[GV1:P2619] It cannot be said that India has a capable and unified life. We do not have a single language or an attire of our own. What we have are many different languages and varied attires. What we bear and carry around are someone else's language and attire. We are inferior in every respect. When young children grow up, do we have something uniquely our own to practice and pass on to them? The culture we acquire is not our own. From nursery onwards, what is taught is a foreign culture. A child who is educated in such a way has no contemporary respect for their own father, mother, neighbors, or family members. They study in college under the impression that there is no need to show such respect. Even before reaching high school, politics grants them the freedom to commit whatever mischief they please. Then whom should he respect? There is no point in blaming these children of poor people. After all, they have to at least stand beside those who show off by adopting the style of absolute Western culture.

[GV1:P2620] No one has humility towards anyone. There is no humility in language, action, or even in dressing. The attitude is such that they are even ready to kill anyone. Just like teaching a judge's children, their peons taught the children to show arrogance and ruined them. Whoever adopted the Westerner's attire and language became corrupted. How wretched are we, who eagerly invite this nest of arrogance and hasten to praise it as superior? We have reached a point where we are absolutely helpless to say anything to these children.
