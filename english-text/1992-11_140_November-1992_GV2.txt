November 1992

What happened yesterday? Along with that, instead of copying wealth, ability, education, and moral state, what is each of us doing today as a duty to increase tomorrow's repetitive capacity, and what should we do - I remind you of the path I have found to be useful to you for such thinking habits and the actions we repeatedly continue today and tomorrow for our life's difficulties.

Have you ever thought about yourself? Running in the journey of life, how many years will you be in this world? Sixty or seventy, at most a hundred years. Just like a government employee retiring after their term ends. Then surely, doesn't this human birth you have received have a meaning?

If so, from whom will you get the self-awareness to know that meaning? Will you get it from a book-learned person? From a scholar? From a politician? Or is there nothing beyond this world? If so, any absolute villain could just do whatever they want and leave this world.

When you simply say that there is a power ruling this world, are you saying it after knowing anything about that power? Aren't your darling children playing and growing in front of you destined to become wives, mothers, husbands, fathers, grandfathers, and grandmothers tomorrow? Have you done anything to impart the necessary knowledge to them, who are to turn the wheel of governance and the wheel of dharma in tomorrow's world? Is it enough to just give them some material education? As parents who took up the karma of creating children who sow destruction to the world

Have you ever thought about your pitiful condition of having to pass away from this world?

Aren't we humans one? Aren't Hinduism, Christianity, and Islam created by us? Did the Supreme Lord Sree Krishna, Lord Buddha, Prophet Muhammad, Jesus Christ, Sree Narayana Guru, or Chattampi Swami ever speak of caste or religion to anyone? Aren't people with vested interests the ones who created caste and religion in the name of these masters and fenced them inside?

Aren't all these masters the common wealth of the world? Aren't Hindus, Muslims, and Christians the children of India? Why do the children who should work together for their home, country, and the world in the unity of love, embracing the religion of sacrifice, divide themselves in the name of caste and religion? Is it right to make people fight each other in the name of caste and religion using the names of these masters? Even if we do not accept them, can we trample their ideas down? Let the children who are capable of accepting their ideas accept them when they come.

Don't you need a thought that is beyond caste, religion, class, and color differences? Do you have a place to impart a sense of righteousness for your children who should give light to tomorrow's world? Seek it. Surely it will reach you without much delay. It is God's will. Only a visionary who knows the past, present, and future can save you. How will you recognize that visionary? He will be beyond the differences of caste, religion, color, and class. Jesus Christ, Aurobindo Ghosh, and Nostradamus give the world the proclamation of the arrival of such a superhuman radiance. If you are human, let this thought follow you. Santhigiri continues its message.

Guruvani
