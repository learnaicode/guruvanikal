[GV1:P0123] July 1977

[GV1:P0124] Question: What could be the result of that preservation?

[GV1:P0125] Answer: Some people say that Yoga disciplines state that if vigor is not lost, one will not suffer from old age and graying of hair, and will have a long life. It might be because of that.

[GV1:P0126] Question: Even with these Yoga disciplines, if it does not calm down, then how can the mind be controlled?

[GV1:P0127] Answer: The nature of controlling the mind when we face numerous sorrows - when we have no one to help us, or when a child dies, or when a husband dies - we often see women crying out loudly. It has been learned from them that when they repeat this for some time, their sorrow subsides, and within three months, the sorrow goes away. Similarly, one can surrender their sorrow by crying upon seeing a deity, a Guru, or a devotee who manifests divine light. Then he attains peace from that. A devotee who has none of this can pray and express his grievances to God by surrendering all his emotions with devotion. He attains peace from that.

[GV1:P0128] Question: You did not finish explaining how women can fully experience devotion to God?

[GV1:P0129] Answer: It is much easier for women than men to attain God through devotion. The proof for this are the wives of the Trinity, Satyavan Savitri, Arundhati, the women who lived with the sages, Anasuya, Priyamvada, Mary Magdalene whom you had deemed as the most fallen, Valli, Devayani, and if spoken of recent times, Sri Ramakrishna's wife, the disciples like Nivedita who came with Vivekananda, the Yogini who lived with Alathur Brahmananda - you are aware of such countless histories.

[GV1:P0130] Question: That is correct. When you said it, each one seems correct. But despite seeing so much, all these stories are fading away from the people. Why would that be?

[GV1:P0131] Answer: First, the changes in times brought about by the Chaturvarna system. Due to the changes that occurred over time for the Shudras and Vaishyas, it could not be passed on to the people. People only got to know the stories of some Siddhas or Yogis sitting somewhere and attaining name and fame. Such Siddhis and magical acts were mostly performed by Rajarshis. They have passed on many illusions to the people using money, knowledge, and wealth. Devaluing the deeds of ordinary devotees, poets and Rajarshis lined up behind them. Secondly, administrations changed in respective times, people of different religions ruled and brought their own religious customs, highlighting the malpractices of Hinduism and thereby covering up true devotion. They have also destroyed such historical records. Just as 'a tiger will eat grass if it has no other way', the scientific methods that came through them have instilled a business-style approach in us. Thus, the tradition of striving hard for worldly matters got ingrained in us.
