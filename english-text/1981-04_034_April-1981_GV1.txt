[GV1:P0681] April 1981

[GV1:P0682] Question: So, could that be the reason why most institutions established in this way are seen stagnating after the founder's time?

[GV1:P0683] Guru: Not only because of that. While that person is alive, there are certain things that need to be completely finished. It has to be corrected and completed through experience. Even if known, it becomes difficult to complete them. The reason is that all the human beings living with him are those who have thought and lived contrarily. Then, some differences seen are several poems by those who are called scholars. Thus, experiential matters cannot be imparted through a scholarly nature.

[GV1:P0684] What is called scholarship is like polishing. It is just like building with bricks and painting it white. If those who lived in a well-painted, good building are taken and made to live in a hut, one will see a scene where they cannot even turn around due to disgust and mess. They will not have the knowledge that the mansion originated from a hut like this. It is difficult to see a conscious effort to make the relief of sorrow in between a reality and to maintain the tradition through that karmic power succeed with everything falling into place.

[GV1:P0685] One who brings success in such a manner will have to experience sorrow like Jesus, the Prophet, and Guru Nanak. Contemporaries witnessing all this might see nothing right but only faults in front of them; upon seeing all this, some might decide to remain silent. Some will work a little and stop. Seeing such things, quite a few will withdraw, saying, 'Didn't you see so-and-so acting and getting ruined?' When hearing what those without this inclination say, assuming it to be right, everything gets pushed into despair.

[GV1:P0686] Question: You mentioned some states from the city of Vetalas to the world of Devas. There is a desire to know how that can be explained as well.

[GV1:P0687] *Continued from the previous issue

[GV1:P0688] Guru: It is difficult to comprehend that precisely. This is because only the masters who are born in accordance with the times will attain this completely. Others might attain it to a certain extent, but they will never grasp its complete dimensions. The knowledge required to observe and act from the very origin of the universe cannot be simply transformed into exceptional intellect. Even if one observes it, they will not obtain all the favorable circumstances required to execute and fulfill it. What else can be done then? One can only stop right there, isn't it?

[GV1:P0689] Once the realm of Vethala (Vethalanagaram) is crossed, a somewhat worthy state is achieved in the subsequent stages. That is, the departure towards the state of deities. That state will be seen as completely radiant. What one immediately receives is an easy path within the Trimurti system. The reason for attaining this is the positive things received as a rest from what happened prior to it. Before successfully crossing the realm of Vethala, we will have to experience and realize numerous sorrowful truths. The stones and thorns placed by God for this purpose are our contemporary human beings and even other creatures in this earthly world.

[GV1:P0690] The only difference between them and him lies in good thoughts and good deeds. Everything else is exactly the same. He will experience all the powers of that demon through his contemporary humans. They will view him as Hiranyakashipu. Words cannot express the difficulty in rectifying this widespread distress, which is as incompatible as milk and horse gram. However, attaining a position in this state of ascending to the deities serves as an interval of relief. To all other believers, he will be perceived just like the Trimurtis. Having seen this much, one cannot be faulted for unknowingly becoming arrogant, feeling, "I have become everything." However, neither this experienced individual nor the society that accepted him in a mimicking manner upon seeing his qualifications will be able to realize that all this merely demonstrates the cyclical and repetitive nature of the heavenly world.

[GV1:P0691] What happens here is neither anyone's fault nor merit. It is a place where such things naturally occur. If one looks at what the Puranas say about Kailasa, one can see what kind of luxurious traits and antics are described! You will understand that when you read the Shiva Purana. There are other Puranas that speak about the Vaishnavas. If you read about that, it is even grander. Thus, if you examine each Purana, you will find that the essential matters occupy only a very small portion of them. Transcending all this and bringing it to a resolution—as proclaimed by the Puranas themselves—takes multiple lifetimes. In our experience, this is also largely true. Overcoming all these means that a soul, which has been led purely and holily through lifetimes, will eventually reside in certain divine realms. Sometimes we hear the names of stars, don't we? Like the Shukra star, Velli star (Venus), and Arundhati star. There are many such stars, both large and small. There are those that are much larger than the Earth and those that are much smaller. It may not be possible to meticulously comprehend and analyze all of these.

[GV1:P0692] Question: What should we understand from your words about what needs to be done here next?

[GV1:P0693] Guru: Haven't you understood from what has been said so far? Isn't it clear that these celestial spheres stand beyond nine realms, similar to Earth? Beyond knowing about these star systems and all this, there is a higher state. Only then can this be known. Even at the very first stage of this Trimurti system, one is seeing and experiencing almost up to the state of Godhead. Do you think it is possible to experience, realize, and describe this, or to accomplish and conclude any one of these tasks?

[GV1:P0694] Question: From what I have heard, I can understand that it is indeed difficult.

[GV1:P0695] Guru: When it is this difficult to repeatedly accomplish even good things, imagine the magnitude of difficulty in knowing and realizing the qualities contradictory to everything we mentioned earlier. When he begins to repeatedly realize this, all his contemporary people who respected him and considered him good—even those who sang his praises until then—will look at him with disdain. They will act with enmity, torment him in every possible way, and drag him down, piling misery upon him. Eventually, only a cluster of these heaps will be seen. If one carefully examines history, one can see numerous great men who, despite working for the good, were transformed into something worse than beasts. One need not even look at history. Can't we see it if we look at the contemporary nature of today?

[GV1:P0696] Question: Yes, we can see that.

[GV1:P0697] Guru: Do you know how all of that is seen through us?

[GV1:P0698] Question: I have understood it. But it is not clear how exactly it happens.

[GV1:P0699] Guru: Currently, aren't you seeing a lot in the papers regarding various believers, rationalists, different godmen (Bawas), and the evaluations of scholars? Haven't you understood what all this implies? It means that no one can truly recognize anyone else. Just like in the past, each person is slipping from one state of blindness into another even now.

[GV1:P0700] Question: Then, in your opinion, how should this be corrected?

[GV1:P0701] Guru: Here, what is needed now is not correction, but realization. Will anyone among those mentioned admit that they are flawed? If anyone in the world says that one particular group among them is bad or anything of the sort, they will all gang up and resort to untoward actions. Each of the godmen is clever. The college professors and principals here are all qualified for their respective positions. The religious scholars and authors of the Puranas here are also qualified. Then there is a specific section of people who believe that only the Vedas and Upanishads are right. What they say is above all this. And then there are the rationalists and salvation-seekers, who act as if they have mixed all this up and swallowed it whole. Does someone who knows a little bit and tries to speak have any standing? There is a venomous breed of wasps. It would be fortunate if all of these people don't act like them, spreading toxicity and finishing him off right here.

[GV1:P0702] However, one cannot say that all this is wrong. It must be called right. If one discerns all this at a glance, the subjects appear in a way that suggests the right is slightly more prevalent. When examining the Puranas and stories, these godmen, such gurus, rationalists, and salvation-seekers are souls who have been repeatedly born and died over countless ages. If we observe closely, we can see whether they are the ones who have returned repeatedly in this era. Behind this is another group that pretends everything happens through them. All this is the growth of science. Can we, who experience and understand it to a large extent, say it is wrong? Similarly, if time moves forward a little, they will have to admit that a lot of this is flawed.

[GV1:P0703] For example, by the time the medicines discovered, tested, and released through laboratories are used on the public, they begin to declare them as flawed. Thus, they have reached a stage where they do not hesitate to declare things as right or wrong every second. Another group is standing firm here, ready to repeatedly assert that all this is correct. Recently, with the fall of Skylab, a lot of panic spread across the world, and numerous opinions regarding this have surfaced among the people.

[GV1:P0704] Shouldn't we understand that all this is causing some kind of disruption and that a danger lurks behind it? We are already experiencing the reality that numerous toxic gases have spread in the atmosphere, affecting humans and all living beings, causing various diseases. One must suspect whether purity of character itself has become akin to some sort of machine. It is bound to happen that way. Because, there is no doubt that consuming plants and food materials grown under the influence of these gases will affect not only our bodies but our intellect as well.

[GV1:P0705] We have overt empirical evidence laid out before us to substantiate this. The artists in our midst are virtually incapable of writing or performing unless they consume a specific kind of intoxicant. Once a small quantity of it is ingested, melodies flow spontaneously. Dance movements may ensue, whether they align with classical aesthetics or not. They attempt to compensate for bodily grace at least through exaggerated contortions. As this tendency proliferated, it eventually permeated even the ranks of healthcare professionals; consequently, if patients were to awaken suddenly from an unconscious state and behold these individuals—whose appearances make it indiscernible whether they should be regarded as doctors or the deity of death—they might instantly succumb to sheer terror. Perhaps this explains why certain patients die immediately upon regaining consciousness post-surgery. This remains our only logical conjecture. Those lying sedated under the influence of narcotics are essentially lying frozen in the proximity of death.

[GV1:P0706] Deep within the patient's psyche, the preconceived mythological narratives concerning the God of Death would instantly rush to the forefront. Consequently, their breath would cease. A minority devoid of such preconceptions might somehow survive. By the same token, the ominous formations emanating from these toxic gases might congregate in certain regions of this atmosphere. Once congregated, they possess the potential to precipitate any magnitude of catastrophe from the skies. It is entirely superfluous to state what might transpire thereafter!

[GV1:P0707] Question: You articulated earlier that certain individuals are forcefully stepping forward to assert that everything is absolutely correct. I have not entirely comprehended the meaning of that statement.

[GV1:P0708] Guru: In our society, a fervent race for education is perpetually disseminating among both the impoverished and the affluent classes. Generally, the progeny of the highly affluent do not engage in rigorous academic pursuits. They acquire degrees by leveraging financial power or through other means; ultimately, their primary destination is the political arena. They envision themselves as leaders. Automobiles and airplanes are placed at their absolute disposal without them even demanding it. Observing such privileges induces a state of euphoria that leaves individuals completely ungrounded. For whom does this happen? The descendants of the profoundly impoverished—those who lack adequate sustenance and clothing—also grasp the edge of politics under the influence of this euphoric illusion. They aspire to become leaders. The superficial rhetoric they have heard and witnessed metamorphoses into their ideological principles. Observing these dynamics, a select few individuals recognize this as an opportune mechanism and swiftly infiltrate the political sphere. Upon their arrival, they manipulate the masses by validating their actions as absolutely correct, eventually ascending to become the leaders of the existing leadership. Consequently, by asserting claims ranging from the trivial to the monumental, they reduce the populace to mere peripheral sentinels and commence their governance. This specific faction epitomizes the ultimate embodiment of those who actively rationalize these systemic anomalies as fundamentally correct. Viewed from this perspective, does it not become evident that our pervasive toxic influences have extinguished the vital consciousness within all living entities? Contemplating this paradigm, one is left with no alternative but to assert that all of this is absolutely correct.

[GV1:P0709] Question: If all of this is deemed absolutely correct, what then is the implication for how we are supposed to navigate this?

[GV1:P0710] Guru: The question is indeed valid. I am inclined to believe that your comprehension of this matter is akin to asserting that a tortoise possesses the fur of a camel.

[GV1:P0711] Question: It is nothing of that sort. I posed the question merely to attain clarity. I have exhausted myself reading the Puranas and the Upanishads. I have yet to ascertain where the foundational crux of this actually lies.

[GV1:P0712] Guru: I have repeatedly elucidated this to you in the past. One of the profoundly informative forms, born periodically in accordance with the Yugas, transformed into enlightened sages through their supreme sacrifice and divine grace. Posthumously, for the life forces radiating from various sacred planets and stellar constellations, only those sons who are born by divine providence—guided by the disembodied celestial voice that reminds them of the luminous verses of the Gita, the Upanishads, and the Vedic proclamations—can accomplish this through divine grace alone. These profound truths cannot be acquired merely through instruction, regardless of whether one is categorized as a scholar, a layman, or an intellectual. These revelations remain entirely inaccessible to both the rationalist and the mere seeker of salvation. Therefore, let us conclude these particular segments here. Expounding further will only transcend your comprehension.
