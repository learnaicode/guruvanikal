GURUVANIKAL · November 1994 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0772 to GV2:P0785
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

November 1994

It is not enough to live in the ashram like living in a normal place. This can only be done by suffering and feeling pain for many days. Many people have a notion that if they have a direct experience, everything is done. With the thought that everything can be achieved through the use of Siddhi, the realization of what Siddhi is and what knowledge is has been lost. All that used to happen at one time. But this is not the time of the Deva. Nothing is going to be achieved by manifesting the Deva. What is achieved in many births can be achieved in one birth in Kali. Understand what the basis of our action is and do it keeping that in mind. The easy way of eloquence that does not know what pain is, is wrong. This action that changes the course of human action is difficult. The course of human action and along with it the course of action that has been wrong for ages must be changed. Have you thought about why, in this place with so much wealth of experience, no matter how great the action is, one has to experience so much difficulty. All suffering and all pests must be dissolved and ended here. Therefore, this will only end by causing suffering.

There is no ashram discipline at all in South India. Not at all in Kerala. Why is that? Kerala was ruled by Nairs. The administration fell under the upper-caste hegemony. Therefore, it was not possible to impart knowledge to those below the Nairs. Among the rulers of that time, only in Kochi was there a Kshatriya. In other places, the ruler disguised himself as a Brahmin. Under the rule in Travancore, the people grew like wild grass. Humans got a liberation because the Christians arrived here. But that also did some harm. Today they are experiencing the result of the harm they brought to India. The British were the ones who ruled everywhere. Today even that name is not out there.

Now what do we need? As we travel along the path we have been told, whatever we do, we must plead and beg, 'We have no knowledge for this; how should we do this, what should we pray for. . . we don't know, father. . . . please give us everything we need'

You must pray. I will mention caste here. You shouldn't speak like that. Mistakes will happen. They will understand what I say. Chandalas and Brahmins are all members here. Everyone has the freedom to work equally here.

Because the kings who ruled Kerala were not Kshatriyas, they did not know how to respect a sanyasi. The king ruled as an obedient servant to the Vaidika. If the king is like this, how will the subjects be? What all should we be careful about when we work? A Brahmin knows rituals and rules. But they behave trivially in front of others. However, they do what is necessary in front of the Acharya. If you don't have this awareness, you will return to the old culture itself.

You have no sacrifice at all. It is said that Westerners have no sacrifice. But their hardworking nature is not small. In China, women go to work on the seventh day after giving birth. But here, how long is postpartum care? Don't you need four people to take care? A pond from Ochira to the north is a furlong long. What magnificent stone steps their women build at the bathing place in a place without even a pinch of sand! Seeing this, the wealthy Nairs also did it. The children of the same mother were divided into carpenter, brass-smith, goldsmith, and blacksmith, given a thread over the left shoulder, named Vishwakarma, and started worshiping. Women stayed at home and became bad. They are the ones who became the worst among women. Therefore, not only did they completely lose their beauty, but they also fell into severe poverty. Hybridization and lack of food - in this state, that entire class has degraded.

We must see this with pain. I am not talking about today's Brahmins. They are poor. Their ancestors did all this. If they do not understand this, aggressive behavior will occur. The Ezhava's ignorance is his arrogance. Tying a knot on the head, drinking toddy, and showing off that 'I am also someone' is all even the most educated can do. Some converted and became Christians and Muslims. What the arrogant person says to God is also the same: If what I think doesn't happen, I will throw you away. The forest dwellers come to the king with honey in a bamboo tube, turn their backs, and give it. What does he call the king? The Ezhava's nature is also the same - arrogance. The Namboothiris of that time fixed all the Ezhavas with laws. Some of those who got some culture studied medicine. The physician came and kept everyone else away. Ponnamban, Thandan, Chovan, Channar, Panikkar - these are all names bought with money. Sometime back, when I went to Varanappally, I saw Ezhava women themselves calling those belonging to it 'Kochamma'. This arrogance is also there in worshiping God. Worshiping God without humility, they became arrogant not only in education but in everything they touched. They don't even know how to show humility. All these traditions must be broken. This is what was said to be our hardship.

The Ezhava will stand with pride wherever he goes to show that his customs are great. They didn't even take what Sree Narayana Guru did and showed. First, he taught them to cook and eat. Then he taught them to offer Bali. He built a house for Vavubali. Finally, he sat himself for Guru Pooja. He took a handful of Guru Pooja rice and gave it as Prasad. But the community didn't understand it. Some will say Swami didn't tell for Pooja. Understanding all this, we should work with the knowledge that we have such a thing. Humility is the greatest. Obedience is even greater. And the qualification to match it.

I had a youth spent plucking flowers in Sivagiri and living in some Oottupura. Growing up in this nature, I have observed and learned everything. I have cried in pain on many days. How can those without love and obedience be reformed? If you have come here to do all this after staying here, you must do it even without food and sleep. When lifting up what has fallen, there will only be those who scold. You must suppress and destroy your mischief and do this with difficulty. Otherwise, it will be like Durvasa doing penance. Durvasa only had time to curse and do penance. People of all communities will come here.

Remember that this is the central place for all of them. You should not point out everyone's flaws like I do. It will hurt them. Mischief will also come. I say this to remove ego and bring humility.

We are small in number. Even so, people of all kinds are here. That is not because of our greatness. It is by the grace of God. You should remember that the service you do is service to the Guru. Service is service to the Guru; a word is a word spoken to the Guru. It is the love that cannot be found anywhere else that attracts people of all communities here. There is a uniqueness to the growth of all aspects of ours. It is growing without our knowledge, just as the country grows.

Those with vision will understand one thing if they observe carefully. The light seen here has a uniqueness greater than the light of gods and goddesses. Different types of lights. Those who see it must have the wisdom to recognize it. If not, they should ask the Guru and know. Only the Guru can recognize it. After creation, a long time passed without any evolution, not even knowing what the earth was. Even God's will had to be shaped by time. If man has a certain honesty, the world of gods and the world of Brahma each have an honesty, and that belongs to God.
