GURUVANIKAL · May 1995 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0860 to GV2:P0872
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

May 1995

The Trimurti tradition is the truth that is seen manifested. There is a course of action that reaches up to the position of the lord of the Ganas, just like the Trimurti tradition. Seeing this course of action, we believed that it was the truth. All this is correct. Because it has its own truth. But this is not enough for us.

What happens with the constellation of stars, what should be done after reaching that state - this is the boundary of the soul's good deeds. Who are we, who should we be in the course of action that stands at the lord of the Ganas? Creation will come in the world like the course of action. It is not two for man and woman; there is only one. What should be added to the children who are raised to the part of this knowledge, or what is the side that joins the part of that knowledge - today's historians do not know any of this. It cannot be known through any myths or epics either. We are standing in an action where there is no point in knowing this. You come and know all this slowly.

In the Manu lineage that has come so far, we are standing in the twenty-eighth Chaturyuga in the seventh Manu lineage. The opportunity you have got is what God has shown us to get the situation of this Chaturyuga completed in our lineage. It cannot be known so quickly to its fullness. The reason is that we have no information about Manu and the Manu lineage. We also have no information about what is said in the Manusmriti. We don't know what has been created through it either. Manusmriti is knowing what is needed and what is not needed. Manu is also a historical truth to know what the fickle state of the mind is. Through what should we know this?

There is a lineage guru (appointed person) to know this. We need to know how to reach that guru and how to follow that path. There are many types of traditional gurus. There are those among them who come only to tell what the Trimurtis are and what that tradition is. We are able to know not only such gurus but all types of gurus. No other guru lineage has such clear knowledge. Neither Christ nor the Prophet has this. It is said that the Prophet's prophethood is the last. But three months before his samadhi, he makes a begging prayer, saying, "What are you showing me, Father, who thought that there is nothing but you?" So a course of action that even great men like the Prophet could not reach has existed here for ages.

We have not known this truth. Krishna's situation is also the same. We should see Krishna as a person suited to the dharma of the time. That is what was informed before coming. What is it? If you don't rule properly, people will be sent to your house to kill you. Kamsa could not understand that because of the words ordained by God. That is, standing in the position of Pratyakshavaha Dharma

Nothing else could be known. The Trinity system covered up the truth in such a way that it was impossible to know what a Manvantara was or what time was beyond it.

If those things happened because Sri Krishna was born, then that is also Brahma's decision. How can one person change that Brahma's decision? You are able to ask if Brahma himself could not have kept that decision aside because of the previously mentioned calamity. It is not like that. There is a Kaladharma (duty of time). If you do not know and reach through Kaladharma, you will not be able to know and reach what the historical truth of this world is. Therefore, do not cross it without knowing what Kaladharma is.

There are many stories here that have convinced us that there is a divine karma behind our death and birth in this time itself. But it is not enough to just convince. It is a karma that must be experienced and convinced. This is what was said to be the thing to know without distinction of male-female, big-small. That is being done here. If you feel that those who know this and those who make it known should live in the name of God, if you think that you have a karma for that, how should you live? You must show life in such a way that it brings an element of how to take it and pass it on to the children. One thing must be careful when passing this on to girls. Girls will not stay with you for long. At most until the age of thirty. So if you do not raise girls carefully, you will get a mother who spreads destruction.

The Westerners ruled India for four hundred and fifty years. It is not enough to just say that our customs and heritage were lost because they ruled like this. We were not able to take many good things. Giving something to someone and taking something from someone is a great imagination of the mind. Our body is for taking it by growing that mind, being humble, and being righteous. So if we do something with the body, a karma that cannot do anything will come and climb on our mind and shoulder.

Those who lived under the bridge during the British rule came here and became Viceroys. They are those who studied a little and cannot do anything. If he is smart, he will be taken somewhere as a Governor. He has nothing. Not even a good rich man. He is the one who came here and became Viceroy. Those here only learned the bad things he showed. He doesn't know how to walk when he comes here. A good rich man, the biggest beggar, and everyone in between behave in their own way. What we learned by seeing this is a danger that happened to us. While we are alive, we should think that we got such a knowledge by connecting with something in the mind, and we should ache to be able to maintain some good dharma before leaving this nest. What has happened cannot be taken away. Let it be like that. When born, that life came up through the star and the zodiac. Can it be cut off? We are doing the greatest karma of the quality of birth. You do not know that. Why?

In Kerala and Tamil Nadu, there is something called Seemanthakarmam. This karma is done in the seventh month of pregnancy. The brain develops from the seventh month of pregnancy. To avoid mistakes when this brain develops, Puranas and epics will be read and heard. This is Seemanthakarmam. Brahmins will also give sattvic food like honey and ghee at this time, so that the brain and vasana become sattvic. In this place, the Shudra will mix tamarind and give it, making the brain and body tamasic. They were trained like this, calling it Pulikudi adiyanthiram.

We must know what the lives of great men are like. Similarly, we must know what the lives of martyrs are like. But we are kept in caste in such a way that we cannot know the true form of their lives. Hindus, in particular, have kept God in caste and are dancing and singing. All these crazy dances are in the name of God. Even martyrs are included in caste. It was an opportunity to be born in a tradition that came like this. On looking at it, it is good to be born like this in today's situation. As mentioned earlier, this body is to prevent Kali Dharma. Before the body, which is the instrument for prevention, is born, one must know what it is when entering the karma of birth. Otherwise, children of indescribable misfortune will be born. What can be done with these children? Many great souls came to change this misfortune. Based on Yuga Dharma, Krishna, who is seen in the forefront, was a Jnani by birth. We could not even investigate and find out his biography. Why Krishna came is still unknown. The wonderful stories of Krishna from birth are our wealth.

Guru Vani
