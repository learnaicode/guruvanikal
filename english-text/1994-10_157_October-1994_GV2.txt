GURUVANIKAL · October 1994 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0751 to GV2:P0771
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

October 1994

A person preparing to take Sannyasa will have to face many difficulties. Those who set out as Avadhutas should practice in the ashram and go forward with some karma in mind. If so, what is intended can be achieved. Only if one obeys as a Brahmachari for at least twelve years will everything become right. A single moment is enough, the Guru-disciple relationship might happen. The Guru will not have to talk to them like rubbish. That is how the rightful availability of the Guru and the rightful disciple happen.

Since childhood, I could see a form of light. It was Krishna's. And I didn't know how to express it. In the third birth, died at the age of twenty-five. Initiation was received at the age of twenty-five. On that day, Krishna was made visible. This is the reason for having that face. The face of Rishi Saibaba was shown in the position of the Guru. That too with proof.

'He who worships at the feet of the Guru...' Ezhuthachan has said. There is no need to look at the right or wrong of the Guru. The gods have committed many wrongs. Even those wrongs are praised. The Guru is above even the gods. The Guru can be seen above the state attained by the disciple. It is God who makes the Guru a Guru. The disciple sees what is contained in God's will. Therefore, it should be seen in its own nature. It is God who directs each soul. Even Gurus are of a thousand kinds. It is never possible to study the Guru. God is giving him intelligence every moment. Therefore, the Guru's word must be strictly obeyed. Did you see Parasurama obeying his father, who was his Guru? He killed his mother.

The Putrakameshti ritual was performed and yielded results. That is mantravada (sorcery). Religious gurus are like sorcerers. Only one with a tongue and life can say that you should do this, your life's purpose is this. I did not get this by desiring anything in my life. Since childhood, I knew nothing but the love of God.

My own life is proof that the tendencies of past lives will take hold of us. It is a fortune that the Guru is worthy. If a worthy Guru is found, a soul that has endured sacrifice will get another birth, and everything that was accomplished over time will be revealed.

The Gurus of the age might have done many things. But not all Gurus might be able to do so. If one tries to deceive externally, someone will deceive the deceiver. Only the truth should be revealed openly. Do not confuse things. Past lives should not be hidden. If so

It will be like writing the biography of Sree Narayana Guru. No sacrifice has been revealed. There are many flaws in writing the history of the Prophet as well. Disciple: How is it possible to record all the things that have been received? Guru: One should only say what one has received. It should not be written without a name. The one who has the karma to write will come and write. It is enough if you do the karmas you have to do here carefully. If Rama came as Krishna, then why worship Rama? Shouldn't someone be able to carry and fly those divine parts? Jesus and the Prophet had people to do what they said. Similarly, isn't it enough if you also have determination?

What do we need to change the animality that is full of vasanas? It will only be right if you offer it saying that there is nothing called mine. Depending on the context and situation, the Guru will say different things. If you obey that, it will flow together. Intelligent people, if not careful, will make mistakes. One who accepts the Guru will not look at the Guru's words a second time. Even if it is wrong, they will only try to implement it with difficulty. What a wicked person does will come to light. But a disciple will not repeat the Guru's words. The Guru's word is the glory of time.

What is the glory of time? What is the secret of keeping Tipu's samadhi decorated in this way? He died of a cut. But it is called samadhi. Tipu is a Muslim. But respecting his courage, the British themselves built the samadhi. Why is that? He had an honesty. I have spent a couple of hours there. India is always the cornerstone of the model. The people and the land of India are glorious. Being able to be born in such a land is a great fortune. Even though this land had so much glory, we are not able to unite. Even though there have been some damages due to an unexemplary lifestyle, no one can come here and do anything. Such people will only destroy themselves.

Communism has a nature of spreading everywhere like having no root. Where is its root? In Germany. Where did the capitalist nature in this come from? From the colonial system established by the British. The British were somewhat just at first. But when what was only in the hands of the upper class was transferred to the ignorant poor, this capitalist nature came up. The origin of this nature was in India itself.

What came to India through Krishna a thousand years ago, the British took away, studied and implemented here. If you study about arts, you will understand that. If you see the art of Christians, you will not feel any love. How did a nature of not knowing all this carefully come into us? A demonic nature has spread in us and is ruling the roost. The physical remains of all great people have been preserved in this condition itself. This is to show that it is a decision of God. It may come to be that it is somewhat destroyed according to some changes that occur in the yugas. A reason before creating history. Such things are being said here for you to be convinced about the nature of the yuga and to confirm it.

There is a place (Bermuda Triangle) in the middle of the sea where no science works. A devil with God's blessing has done this.

What was the mistake Tipu made? He thought that there is only Islam in this world. Whether an aggressor or a good person, if they came here and worked before us, India has a nature of seeing a glory in the resting of that physical body. All the mischiefs in the world should come and rest here. Do you think that if you understand this and sit idle without doing anything, your life will be spared? Those who handle art and culture should be especially careful about this.

You should pray that the nature of today's education system should not come to our education center. Education is to utilize this present time, to know about the future. It should be able to be imparted happily.

You must understand that the mistake that has been going on for twenty-five Chaturyugas has been passed on to us. For so long, this mistake has remained in the atmosphere and spread. It is as a result of this that the world has plunged into such a disaster. How should we respect the power that foretold this? Even if we can only act for a moment, we must do it right. If we do so, the result of that action will remain here. No one can take it away. Shouldn't we think and act on how to carry forward that foundational power of life? Who will you become if you work even a little bit for this.

How many Acharyas have come here recently? Didn't everyone starting from Buddha arrive? If the British, who were born in a nature where it is impossible to survive, could achieve this much, why can't we.

All kinds of nonsense will come here, we are catching it and leading it on a pure path. Our ability might be less. It is enough to display the ability we have without getting tired, we can achieve anything.

You are the ones who have come with the right to salvation like Uddhava or the Gopis. It is much easier for you than that. After Krishna, a few Acharyas came and imparted some virtues to the people. By combining these virtues, we have brought a new virtue. God is showing us things beyond the Yugas and making us do them. It is a very responsible matter for us. We must be able to work and consolidate all artistic and cultural values ourselves.

Guruvani
