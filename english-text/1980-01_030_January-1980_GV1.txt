[GV1:P0505] January 1980

[GV1:P0506] Question: Why is it that even after attaining complete spiritual knowledge, one is unable to apply it in practice and progress?

[GV1:P0507] Guru: There is no other recourse to this than accepting an experientially perfect Guru as one's preceptor and seeking their guidance. It was when the intellect became exhausted by observing all this that we witnessed the emergence of individuals like Ramasamy Naicker, who founded the Dravidar Kazhagam. The predicament they faced was identical. There was no alternative paradigm to offer in its stead. They merely presented and justified two discourses before the public: rationalism or liberationism. Like a mirage, these concepts merely resonate in the realm of imagination and cannot be experientially realized. Furthermore, even if experienced individuals imparted this knowledge, the prevailing administrative system and followers lacked the capacity to assimilate and act upon it, or to propel forward those who did.

[GV1:P0508] Question: Advocate, why do you say so? Just as there are followers of the Prophet and Christ, are there not people in India who have accepted figures ranging from the Buddha and Sankaracharya up to Sree Narayana Guru?

[GV1:P0509] Guru: I have already acknowledged that this is the case. It is true that the teachings of Christ and the Prophet were adhered to up to a certain extent. I do not assert that it was absolute. In India, acknowledging this fact poses significant difficulty; having ousted them from their true essence, great luminaries like Sankaracharya, Sree Narayana Guru, and Chattampi Swami have been appropriated by their respective communities, thereby completely repudiating their foundational ideologies.

[GV1:P0510] Question: Today, Narayana Guru, as well as Sankaracharya as a world preceptor, are widely celebrated. Similarly, Chattampi Swami is recognized and revered precisely for being an erudite sage, is he not?

[GV1:P0511] Guru: Did I not previously confirm that such recognition exists? However, they are manipulating all of it to align with their own vested interests. Sankaracharya, who propounded Advaita, undoubtedly deserves to be venerated as a world preceptor. Yet, the monasteries administered by his followers historically refused to even serve food to non-Brahmins. Even during Sree Narayana Guru's lifetime, history records that those who followed and worked with him later claimed that his legacy belonged exclusively to a specific community, asserting that it was the community that created and sustained these institutions. There is a monument at Kaithamukku commemorating his sixtieth birthday (*shashtipoorthi*). Following its completion, during a ceremony convened to welcome him at the Keedangamparambu temple, the poet Asan composed and recited the verse "Narayanamoorthe Guru Narayanamoorthe" in the Guru's presence; this is the very hymn that everyone utilizes today for daily recitation. Because it is used for daily recitation, the complete essence of accepting him as the Guru is embedded within that prayer chant. They conflated this acceptance, which was originally intended for their specific objectives, in accordance with their own interests. Furthermore, the Murukkumpuzha temple and the Kalavamkodam temple, where he consecrated the deities, are frequently cited by all of us. At the Murukkumpuzha temple, where the ideals of "Truth, Righteousness, and Compassion" were inscribed, the temple authorities of that era subsequently installed a *kuzhavi* (stone pestle) in front of the original consecration and named the deity Kalakanteswaram. Similarly, in front of the mirror consecrated at Kalavamkodam, they installed an Ardhanareeswara idol. Moreover, intertwined with this is the history of them persecuting him for over ten years over property and assets.

[GV1:P0512] Question: I have a desire to hear and understand this history.

[GV1:P0513] Guru: It is inappropriate to state it in that manner. I shall elaborate briefly. The community fundamentalists of that era, particularly those possessing some degree of privilege and prestige, completely refused to acknowledge the Guru. Realizing that his overarching objectives could not be fully realized through them, in the eleventh year, he convened a group comprising fourteen members at Koorkanchery in Thrissur. It was officially registered there. It was christened the Sree Narayana Dharma Sangham. The members of the Sangham held its inaugural board. When the photograph of that event was taken, it included Sree Sankarananda Swami, who served as the head of the Sivagiri Mutt and attained *samadhi* recently, as well as Nataraja Guru.

[GV1:P0514] Question: Then why was Nataraja Guru not present in the Dharma Sangham?

[GV1:P0515] Guru: According to the bylaws of the Dharma Sangham, two signatures were required to become a member. He affixed only one signature and refrained from signing the second time. To my knowledge, the Swami himself inquired about this, to which he replied, "Swami, I also need footwear and shirts." The Swami responded, "That is permissible." Nevertheless, he declined to sign.

[GV1:P0516] Question: But people often claim that he was chased away from Sivagiri.

[GV1:P0517] Guru: To the best of my knowledge, that is inaccurate. It is true that in subsequent years, he served as an advisor there. That occurred during the period when Achuthanandaji was the head of the Mutt.

[GV1:P0518] Question: Then what was the reason for him leaving Sivagiri again?

[GV1:P0519] Guru: He had already relocated from Sivagiri prior to that. The Gurukulam in Varkala existed even before he assumed the role of advisor. It is understood that the Gurukulam in Ooty was established after seeking the Swami's permission. During his tenure as advisor, a young man named G.N. Das was serving alongside Achuthananda Swami in Sivagiri. Assuming he had committed some transgression, Achuthananda Swami physically struck him. Asserting that the punishment was excessive, he took the young man along with him to the Gurukulam and never returned. This is the factual truth of the matter.

[GV1:P0520] Question: Therefore, the Sree Narayana Dharma Sangham and the Sree Narayana Paripalana Yogam were established with two distinct objectives, were they not?

[GV1:P0521] Guru: Yes, it became clear that Narayana Guru's ideals would not be implemented through the SNDP. That is when he did this. During this time, he was also a patient. On that route, he went to Madras for Dr. Pandala's treatment. When he returned from there, the Ezhava elites welcomed him at the Kollam boat jetty, brought him to the place where Neela Hotel stands today, where he stayed and once again reminded them of the community's affairs. Saying, "Alright, let it be. I am coming to Sivagiri," he sent them back. When he reached Sivagiri again, three days before the Swami's Samadhi, the community leaders arrived once more. They spread out a will for him to sign. At that time, it was customary to support and raise the Swami, who was bedridden with severe illness. He suddenly sprang up and indignantly asked the visitors, "We made this through sorcery and medicine, right? Won't you even let me lie down?" In his agitation, he stood up, and a clock donated by someone immediately fell over and shattered. At that moment, the leaders left. His Samadhi took place on the third day. For his forty-first-day memorial ceremony (Nalpathukuli), these community leaders arrived at Sivagiri with a stay order, and not only did the ceremony not take place, but they were not even allowed to serve cooked food there. It had to be served across the paddy field. Following that, a legal case continued for ten years between the Sree Narayana Dharma Sangham and the Sree Narayana Dharma Paripalana Yogam. On that occasion, as the case was progressing in favor of the SNDP, in order to destroy the records showing that the Sree Narayana Dharma Sangham was established, two almirahs were lifted from Sivagiri, brought to a rock at the place where the watcher Kunjiraman lived on the southern side of the Sivagiri school, and burned. Thus, after the case dragged on for ten years, when a Receiver was appointed, while sorrowfully searching through various records, a copy signed by him stating 'I have resigned from the SNDP in thought, word, and deed' was found. It was produced in the bench of Sankarasubba Iyer, who was a High Court judge at the time. Through that, the verdict came in favor of Sivagiri and against the SNDP. At the time of the Swami's Samadhi, the Dharma Sangham, which was only a year old, could not reach its required functional capacity. Consequently, the Sangham was unable to properly manage its ideals. The ascetics who came later did not work focusing on the Swami's ideals either. Because this word became a monopoly of the Ezhavas, ascetics from other communities could not join it. As a result, because the community, lacking sufficient experience and tradition, did not know how to respect ascetics, many quarrels and disputes occurred. Through the passage of time, it has become as if it is the property of one community. Now it is impossible to correct it.

[GV1:P0522] Question: So, even while he was alive, the Guru had to endure a great deal of suffering, didn't he?

[GV1:P0523] Guru: Absolutely. There are records showing that not only this community but other communities as well harassed him, starting from the time they uprooted the casuarina tree at Aruvippuram and chased him away from there. I am not discussing that now. From what has been said so far, haven't you understood what they have transformed the Guru's ideals they adopted into? Similarly, the Nair community has turned Chattampi Swami into the property of the Nair community. While the Swami was alive, he wrote and published a book titled *Vedadhikara Nirupanam*. The Nair community did not want it then. Today, as it has more or less come into practical experience, they want it. In the same way, Sri Sankaracharya was also built up by Vedic orthodoxy. They hid the Guru who established Advaita behind it. Therefore, without knowing the facts, there are quite a few people today who say that it was Sankaracharya who brought about the caste system. Haven't you understood from this? That is, the community itself is devouring these ideals and preserving them for their own exploitation to bring out something else. Following this, even the politics up to this day is aimlessly wandering without foundation, craving for something unknown.
