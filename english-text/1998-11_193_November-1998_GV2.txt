When someone begins to write the life story of a person who has lived to be a hundred years old, it becomes a composition mixed with right and wrong. Then one might ask, why write this? Through this, to understand a truth, it is not just the billions of souls like you and me who have lived, but the billions of human resources that represent the evolution of life. In that context, does God exist or not? Or if God does not exist, what is the point of us holding onto such a concept of God? When truth, dharma, and justice—which have been brought to the level of a world encyclopedia wearing the veil of knowledge—are presented as a picture shaped like a vast, unrecognizable forest or a huge mountain, can one understand the path of action that discovered the plants and medicinal herbs, from the banyan tree growing in that forest to the hardwood trees in all parts, and how and where to use them? We are proceeding in a manner where we do not know how to recognize it and formulate a guideline to understand its qualities. For example, if we look at people born and raised in the homes of physicians, they also know some medicine. They might know to pluck the root of a plant or something. We know nothing about the long period of hardship involved in discovering this. Or we could not respect and understand anything regarding that truth of life. One who administers a single herbal remedy or a raw medicine becomes the master of medicine. That person has no understanding of the pros and cons resulting from this. The disease might get cured. However, it is not to say that we should walk backward to learn and recognize every fact that has existed up to today. But regarding a theory of karma that has grown as mentioned, when we study its concepts, Narayana Guru has written a prayer fragment for children to read: 'Before time passes forward, building a fort with memories,' and 'Counting one by one, when the counted things end, the inner self stands still like the seeing eye' (like the eye of your mind). When we praise and learn the billions of theories of the guiding lights whose thoughts have evolved in that manner, how can we recognize the mistakes that might occur?

How will we recognize the truth that has occurred between the truth of a beginning and an end that has been stated? This is the truth. How can we explain the essence of the word 'truth', which is each of us who has grown up before us, not knowing where the end of the truth that starts with the assertion 'I have seen the truth' lies, or where the destruction of that truth lies? The word that completes it is truth.

There is only one. Thus, everything is one. When we try to understand the truth or untruth that has spread across the world in crores of knowledge regarding what kind of knowledge is the realization shown by taking a photo of the forest mentioned earlier—the summary that the essence of everything is one—in order to know the secret of the universe called truth or untruth, various studies we have today have emerged. With the advancement of those studies, if we talk about the facts, stories, and theories of science that discovered the atom and the subatomic particle, does it mean that we have understood the entire position of knowledge that has spread and risen to this day? Even there, the truth of 'you' and 'I' remains.

When saying this, we all know—our knowledge about the truth is limited only to the extent of our individual knowledge and what we have experienced and counted. But aren't each of us standing at the measure of the horizon, discovered according to the precision of the eye that measures the sky, the forest, the backwaters, and the sea that make up the world where the many living beings that have come so far have grown? Similarly, for us who state the measure of truth, there remains a truth of not knowing to whom, what, and how to explain it. That too is a truth.

Let us view an evolutionary theory that discovers such truths and untruths like this. Oh! His dream, or the dream I saw, or the waking state I experienced, or the deep sleep of 'I', the gross, the subtle, the cause, and the effect—it is about such a path of discovery that is spoken of from the atom to the absolute. Thus, each of us is a great repository of thousands of absolute wholes. Each of us has a nature of knowing the gross, the subtle, and the absolute through the thought of God, by distinguishing the gross, the subtle, the cause, and the effect that sees the absolute of each person in that way, or sees the truth of life. "He knows nothing, poor thing"—we only know a specific state of truth mixed with truth and untruth that is completed and stopped by saying such words. We do not reach the karma of the visionaries who say "if one counts one by one and the meaning ends." Thus, we cannot complete anything in the position of a visionary. We cannot even complete ourselves. The completion of such a thing is our subject. When reaching that subject, each of our subjects wanders away from the imagined karma like a sky-flower found by counting one by one. Previously it was thought that an atom could not be split. Now it has been discovered that even an atom can be split—atom, subatomic particle, or the ultimate atom.

Because we have reached such a level of learning, without the realization of the vision of karma and the path of karma that measures our lifetime and reaches up to the calculation of Manvantaras, we will fall into the group of saying "I know, I have heard, I have known," like completing the measure of demonic nature by talking about unrespectable outcasts and idiocy. Or else, each of us will stand at the point of concluding that "if all that is measured as 'I, I' ceases, within it is not many but becomes one." Thus, feeling that this ego called 'I' has completed the ego of many things, we continue as the essence of the ego. Having learned such an ego, how will you and I recognize the path of karma that has been completed as something else? Again, we say that it has become the completion of the truth of a scholar who recognizes the sound form using the dictionary of sounds formed by letters, the truth of an ignorant person, and each knowledge in between. This person who speaks of the completion of 'Aham Brahmasmi' (I am Brahman), and thus each person having become 'Aham Brahmam', completely the scholar, the ignorant, the forest dwellers, and the karma, dharma, merit, and sin in between,

Does it mean that the meaning of the Upanishadic light and secret of the forest dwellers has been completely understood? Does it mean that the truth, dharma, etc., which measure all this with intellect, dharma, karma, and merit, have all come under our control? We know one thing, a knowledge as big as us. Billions of our knowledge. But when we know or try to know that our evolutionary knowledge of life through the ages is completely inadequate, we can only see the sum of good and evil that aligns with the truth, dharma, and adharma that may come our way, within our measure. In this truth, there are those who are beginning to learn, those who have learned and reached a stage, those who have been promoted to the next class, and those who express the love and respect of each class. This should not be just enjoying telling the stories of the English, or ancestors, or doctorate holders, but when we come to know if we have achieved anything, it becomes a slight counting of what the part of knowledge is - or knowing the secret and the evolutionary truth of life of each one that stands completely counted one by one. Or knowing how the knowledge divided into the five elements, five senses, and five sheaths, and the mixed life are connected to the Supreme Soul. This is the theory of life. Everyone should not study and become a scholar, and end up seeking the condition of enjoying saying 'I' standing in the karma where everyone's scholarship dawns. For that too, there is a logical secret told by Narayana Guru, the essence of 'I' also continues. When we learn and reach this like this, like the differences in states or like the class system, everyone is great when standing in the sum of their class or knowledge or luck. But this is not the greatness of our knowledge. This is where the truth told by our ancestors dawns. Continuing like this is the secret of life. A long-sighted person or a state is said about a life that has been completed by long births and deaths with such a secret. The evolutionary secret of each life is the combination of thousands of such states. Thus, each theorist is a combination of thousands of evolutionary secrets. Thus, a great visionary who has gone and seen many truths is the one who should teach us the truth in an exemplary manner. The one who tells the meaning of many such truths is the visionary of the Yuga Dharma. This opportunity we got to be born is a Kali Yuga that has passed so much of such Yuga Dharma. The completion of such Kali Yuga is the complete secret that leads each of us and brings us there. Thus, the visionary of the Yuga Dharma is the one who separates and shows the truth and untruth through the path of vision that shows the ultimate secret of the secret of life. That is each Acharya. The Yuga Dharma is the combination of many such Acharyas.

Thus, as they kept writing, again realizing the unbroken and long dharma of the Yugantara Dharma, the knowledge of waking, dreaming, and deep sleep, and the light of the stream of light that always knows the truth became each completion. That is the summary of the Upanishads. The meaning of such shorthand is the life shown by the forest dwellers. It is not possible for us to distinguish and understand this. But we have an easy way. It was to say that that so much had to be said. We can also say it in a single word.

That is what is known as waking, dreaming, deep sleep, and Turiya. Without dismissing it as a dream, without dismissing it as the madness of waking, it is a veil of knowledge that recognizes the karma called waking, deep sleep, and Turiya. This is the theoretical secret of the ego that says Aham Brahmasmi. Without being destroyed by stating the theory in that way, without completing by stating the meaning of the theory, one step is said about the seven thousand four hundred and forty-four visions completed in the stream of light of the knowledge of the previously mentioned waking and deep sleep.

Regarding such a philosophy of vision, there is no need to complete it by saying it exists or does not exist. In the place of knowledge, knowing this much, through seven thousand four hundred and forty-four visions, standing in the supreme position called the presence of God, the presence of the Lord, which elevates each one of us, the truth of truths is the presence of God. Following that, the presence of the Lord. Thus, experiencing the Brahma presence of the other and the Supreme, what is spoken is the meaning of life.

The truth of the Guru is to explain and complete each aspect of knowledge or to know and complete it. Such a truth is the greatness of a life where realization and familiarity come together. The path of dharma of such a great stream of light is completion. The ignorance or knowledge that goes to see the end of the Vedas is to become exhausted by thinking according to one's own intellect, speaking of such completion as great or small. The attainment of Manvantara or the measure of Chaturyuga is that which passes through six Manvantaras and twenty-eight Chaturyugas of this kind of knowledge, where right and wrong are mixed, which is truth and untruth, taking back the changes, and where knowledge and the completion on the part of knowledge have passed many times.

This is the Guru's word that we must know and grasp. When that word becomes truth, the Guru also becomes God; the life dharma with the greatness of the Supreme. This is the word is truth, truth is Guru, Guru is God. It is to see and know this that the ancestors have spoken of the tradition of vision. The secret of knowledge called Grihasthashrama, Brahmacharya, Vanaprastha, and Sannyasa, which are the main parts of this, can only be completed by all of us if it is completed through the Grihasthashrami. Such completion is the continuous right of life.

This is what we have theorized as the Guru. The difference between the Guru who is created by the meaning of the word using the meaning of vowels called vowels and consonants by people called scholars or knowledgeable people, and the experienced Guru is this. The truth of Gurupooja in Santhigiri is to see, know, and complete this.
