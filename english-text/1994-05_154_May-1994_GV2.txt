English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

May 1994

Jesus did not implement his own doctrine. Even though they were silent, what sacrifices the followers made to implement Jesus' doctrine! Their sacrifice became a model for generations. In that regard, what have you been able to do? Respect each person only according to their humility and merit, and then let it go. Maintaining honesty in humility, gaining courage, and without becoming anyone's slave, only then should each of you live here. Without this firmness, do not handle a single thing here. If you can get the word, ask and learn, and complete the task, that is the greatest thing. The habit of confessing in one's own words and closing off must be discarded. It is bad. It brings far-reaching harm. It only has the effect of rubbing a burning coal in Kashi. What Jesus and the Prophet achieved was through the sacrifice of their followers. Shankaracharya also had such a situation. But the followers could not step down from their position and work freely. A mistake happened there.

Madhva's circle is large. Madhva worked by stepping down more than Shankaracharya. Shankaracharya handled things with scholarship and lost. This should be a lesson for your practice. Buddha or other great men like him did not come knowing who they were themselves. It happened from God's will. Buddha thought about the three things he saw. Seeing that Buddha would go into an ascetic nature, his father tried to avoid the circumstances. Since it was God's will, that attempt did not succeed.

It is in India itself that the beginning of all spiritual subjects lies. Along with this truth, you should also think about the special features of the times. We must act according to the times. Even if we are only ten people, there is a lot to do in this country where there is a law to pamper the thief. There must be action to overcome that law. How should that action be absorbed? When the part of pain for that comes, what will you do? When distinguishing right and wrong, there must be enough sacrifice to correct the wrongdoer without killing him. This is only possible if it hurts.

How does it hurt? The way of life must be disciplined. There should be no sense of failure. One must believe and obey blindly. Give blood and sweat, and if necessary, even life, to grow the movement. Who is there who does not call upon God when a natural disaster strikes? Is that what faith is? Then what is faith?

Unless you hold from the outside, it won't be clear inside, right? For that, patience is needed. Patience is the first part of faith. We ourselves must experience all our hardships. If you believe in the Guru, it must be through the greatness of the Guru and the disciple in the relationship between the Guru and the disciple. The example of Sri Ramakrishna and Vivekananda can be mentioned here. It was Vivekananda who brought Sri Ramakrishna to the outside world.

We have come from a new system. The lives of householders who believe in this should be a model for others. You and I are not together for belief - action must be based on this decision. If you are not in belief, I am not with you. If I am not in belief, you are not with me either; even if it is husband and wife. There is no need to look at whether the wife or the husband leaves through belief. My belief is what is great to me.

The prayerful dedication of the disciples - only through that can it be achieved, and it must be achieved. It must become the eternal possibility of human culture. If I do not err, the Guru will not err. If the Guru does not err, God's will will be done. The Guru and the disciple are the firm bond of this settlement. Essential things must be requested and obtained from God by the Guru and the disciple together. The sacrifice needed is to bring anyone available closer to the movement and make them work. God can do anything. If a word can be given from the sky, what can God not show.

Siddhi comes by itself. Siddhi has happened to me by itself three or four times. It happened like this in Varkala, Kayikkara, and Kallara. But that is not what we need. We should not stop there. We should have everything through God's mercy itself. You should pray that whatever is needed for this lineage to survive until the end, should happen through Your complete mercy. We ourselves should discard past mistakes. May God provide the way to discard them. We can do it that way.

The kind of independent thinking that exists in the nation today came about because of British rule. We unknowingly got caught up in the artistic flair of the Muslims. It is proof that we did not even have anything on our side to impart a unique artistic flair. That flair was not imparted in us. The flair of our country will evolve only through our lineage. It will not happen suddenly. Those in this lineage today are higher than the positions of their parents. You must have the determination to grow to heights. The previous generations could only see and hear what we have developed today. They could not develop timely functional capacity from it. They could not think about past values. But we can do it today. Science says that the great must stand near the great. If the great does not stand near the great, the great will perish. Our heritage itself must grow for our home and our country. Not the heritage of the West. There must always be a Mahatma in a country.

We must create good flairs for ourselves. For that, we ourselves must make an effort. If a person who has no place to stay raises his children within ten cents, it has its own merit. One must understand about that merit through experience. What good is there for the people, what good is there for the land, what good is there for the father and mother, what harm is there - one must be able to discern all this. The unique flair and heritage mentioned earlier - these parts must be known and developed from the Guruvadams. God has given us the opportunity to reclaim those lost parts. If it is not done properly, it will be lost again. It must be developed as a support and shade for God's will. If it is not developed like that, or if an attempt is made to develop it by holding on to worldly justice, God will not give it again. Murders and arson happen in the Islamic religion all the time because a culture suited to the times is not developed. The children of householders who believe here must be guided in this flair. In this generation, there is a difference like light and dark between those who are connected to this and those who are not.

Among Harijan children, those with high culture and those without are not the same. People from Nairs downwards have not been able to advance here. They are like moss on the surface of the water. Because they were born in homes without flair and grew up without action, they could not improve our culture. That is the reason for the universal disease in them. They are not willing to let go of the flair, and they cannot disagree with the truth either. That is why there is pain. Because of this kind of conflict

What a struggle it is to uplift a heated and ruined society! Only one who can love can be held within this. Don't this pain and the tendencies that generate pain all converge into one? If all these crash and shatter against a great power, everyone's actions will attain excellence. If one does not rise above base instincts, they will fall into the pit of despair.

Every man and woman here is the seed of a lineage. That determination should be present in every stage of their growth. If one can live solely aiming for the growth of the movement according to the Guru's will, it is God's will that is fulfilled through them. How blessed is that! You do not have to struggle at all to develop good tendencies suitable for that. You only need to obey the Guru's word. When you obey the word, one of the tendencies will untie. In its place, a new one will form. Along with this, there should be another determination: how much value and weight does the work I do have? I will not enslave it to anyone else. Our strong dignity blossoms in this way.

Humility and competence constitute dignity. A Brahmachari should be the symbol of this dignity. What do you do if a burning coal falls from the stove? You immediately pick it up and throw it back. Similarly, you must respond immediately to what you see. There should never be a lack of courage in that matter. If diplomats come near such people, it will be like coming near a block of ice. What if they fall among these hypocrites? They will have no way out.

The gain from the life here is very great. To achieve that gain, one must live forgetting caste and religion. Even if your beauty is lost, wealth is lost, or you have a disease, life will not be destroyed. It is enough to have courage. If there is courage, there is a blessed life. The gain achieved in this way is very great.

If we look back from the time of the Ettuveetil Pillamar a hundred years ago to the British rule, we can see that there are many things left unachieved by us. We must achieve all of that. It is a matter involving that much work. Our field of action is meant to accomplish that. One person must do the work of a thousand people; the work we have to do is that immense. If you work like that for one year, you will get the result of working for a thousand years. Within fifty years, the actions and thoughts of you poor people will become subjects of research in universities. For that, your humility and competence must rise to that level.

Guruvani
