November 1991

Every action here takes place according to the words received as a divine voice. It is not something figured out by my intellect or anyone else's. Receiving a divine voice means becoming qualified to be a guide to the world. For that, one must know what the karma that has been worshipped until then is. It took twenty-four years to understand all these karmas.

Seven years out of the last twelve years in Varkala, twelve years here, and another five years, making it twenty-four years in total. Even then, it was said that it was yet to be completed. Many times, the karma was performed saying it was completion. For that, Brahmacharis are needed. But there are no Brahmacharis either.

When I came here, there was a large line of Muslims. The rest were Hindus. Thus, many people started coming. They came for nothing else. Their various diseases were the reason. Even when these ignorant people came, an oracle announced 'completion' once. These people asked me what completion is. I also don't know what this completion is. But I understood that apart from what was investigated and known until then, there are other karmic paths to be found intellectually and otherwise. This is what is meant by 'do it with intellect, logic, and the power of yoga'. It should be done with the power of yoga. But with the power of which yoga should it be done?

Nine months after coming here, a woman jumped into the well and died. A child who came for treatment for a disease. Here there was a fairly well-off rich man. He brought his lorry and car and dropped people here. At that time, there was only half an acre of land enclosing this**. People broke the fence enclosing this place and entered inside. They stood ready in an aggressive mood with stones, sticks, and logs. A terrible commotion. Krodhrobam also came. Then the local police and all of them conducted a day and night search for three days. All that was here was a small hut. A number of letters were strung on a large wire so that they touched the floor. The only thing the inspectors had to take from here were these letters. They understood something from those letters. The people who wrote those letters initially addressed me as 'you'. Then it became 'Your Highness' and finally 'Guru'. There were letters from many such people. In addition, eight or ten baskets full of new clothes were stacked. Seeing that, they asked how this happened. I said that each person brought and gave it. Thus, on the night of the fourth day after all the inspections, they told the rest of the people to go, except for the ten or twelve people they had detained from here. Then they asked me what to do. I told them to do as the investigation proved. They would ask me from time to time if the Swami had anything to say. I told them to do as necessary.

Initially, these policemen and officials would not even drink water from here. They would only drink if they went outside. Later, they themselves started drawing water and cooking and eating. There was a mute person here. On the fourth day, he started speaking. They saw that this mute person had not spoken and started speaking on the fourth day. When they saw this incident and these letters, they themselves became convinced of this. After that, they stopped all their inspections. Its chief came, bowed, and apologized to me. They had unknowingly done many things. They had stepped into the room where I sit and lie down after applying ash. You are someone who knows how to forgive. Please forgive. Then you know how to purify all this. They said that I should stay here only after doing so. They said that I would have to come to his office in Thiruvananthapuram one day and then left.

That was the day before a birthday. In those days, there was no pandal or celebration like this for a birthday. It was just arranged somehow in the space available here. Some

People kept coming and going here. The police came that day too. To tell me that I had to go to their office. So, I had to go there as well.

Why am I saying all this? Is there a need to say all this? Yes, there is. You are people who believe and say that if you do good, you will get good. Believing so is not correct. You might get good. But that is in a different state.

The one who pulled the girl who jumped into the well and died to the shore snatched what was on her neck and ears. After all this commotion, I was asked what should be done with him. I told them to do as they see fit. I have my duty to do. Therefore, the officer left saying that I should come to my office. You know they have a special room. There is a system with a device like Doordarshan that receives messages from all four sides. They wanted to see me through it. They saw me. Gave me coffee and rice. Asked me to go. I came back. Again, many completions took place.

I am talking about trying to repair the houses of people who were victims of curses. This completion should be done by eleven Brahmacharis. One of them is a girl who was studying for B.Sc. at that time. According to Vedic law, there is a rule that older women should not enter, stand, or come. And that this is the history of the Maharshi. But there are other things in the history of the Maharshi. No one sees that. Let's take Suchindram itself. How did that happen? Suchindram means the place where Indra got purity. It was here that Indra got purity and recovered from the curse he caught.

Today we also have contacts. There is education, there are karma dharma cultural organizations. There is everything. It is among people who have all this that the karma that even Narayana Guru Swami had to face, as I said earlier, exists. I am telling the story of going to finish the rest of that karma. Out of eleven people, ten entered, and now only that girl who was studying for B.Sc. is left. When that girl started to enter, her grandmother stepped on her clothes. It means do not enter there. Because, what if this girl cannot be married later? That is a very difficult thing for them. The girl did not enter. Therefore, instead of her, another girl with vision was called and made to do this. This was completed by making this many people do it, and eight of them had a vision together. The symbol received in the group that had such a vision is the seal placed here today. 'Lotus and Omkara'.

So, when we tried to finish the rest of these curses, what all do we have to face. The girl who hesitated to enter for this karma - she had a vision earlier - got a disembodied voice. 'Will teach you'. It happened eight times like that. There was a drizzle that day. This girl sat still in that rain out of fear. She is a girl who is about to complete B.Sc. It was that girl who could not participate in this completion. Thus, completion has taken place twenty-seven times.

What is completion? As I said earlier, we are all owners of dharma who have received karma that can be contained by working with complete dedication. We have received such karma today. You don't even need to doubt it. My experience itself is the biggest for me. The proof for that is the experience this girl had following that. The next year at the same time after this, this girl got a disease. In her house itself. If any disease comes, they usually bring it here. The girl's foot

The nerves up to the head were twitching and beating. It wouldn't stop even if held. He was immediately taken to the hospital. Dr. Velayudhan is the son of this child's aunt. I told them to take the child there and show him. The doctor said he couldn't do anything. Later, seven surgeons took turns examining him. He was lying unconscious. He would shudder occasionally and lose consciousness again. There was no hope. They said if he regains consciousness, let him. They asked us to pray. After praying, on the twenty-first day, we were told that he would survive but there would be no use. Thus, his life was saved. Two boxes of ice had to be changed daily. That much heat. After ninety days like this, it reduced to one box. He got his life back. But he became useless for anything. He hasn't recovered yet.

What I was trying to say is this: this is the experience I had when I tried to complete a task that was cursed by Narayana Guru Swami. That place is today's Guru Vihar.
