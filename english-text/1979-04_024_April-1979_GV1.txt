[GV1:P0412] March - April 1979

[GV1:P0413] Question: Guru, you have spoken previously about the special prayers in this ashram. Please elaborate further.

[GV1:P0414] Answer: This Akhandanama Japam (continuous chanting of the divine name) was bestowed upon us to remove the obstacles we faced when we worshipped deities. We did not perform it there¹, but only began doing so when we were instructed again here². It is the divine will. It is taking place here in a distinct manner. It is not something that many receive. Usually, it is granted to the Manus. Even though it is brought forth by the Manu lineage, everything that rightfully belongs to Manu has been entrusted to us. Such decrees are for those who span the extensive era of a Manu.

[GV1:P0415] This is a path to rectify the errors within the Manu lineage. The greatness lies in bringing this here without being attached to anything. One gets such an opportunity only after waiting for multiple lifetimes. If one reflects upon this, it is unfathomable. To develop such contemplation, to what depths must you think? No matter how much one does or how much sincerity is shown for this, it will never be excessive. Acknowledge its value and strive to carry it forward with as much reverence as possible.

[GV1:P0416] Here, there are facilities for anyone to come and stay without any discrimination. They must reflect on this. One must contemplate and study how a facility that was unavailable to our predecessors³ has manifested here. No one else had the opportunity to walk this path. What, then, could it be? It is a different paradigm. How did we all receive an opportunity to exist like this? How was such an opportunity created irrespective of caste, religion, age, or gender? It is a miraculous event unprecedented in life. The era of practicing asceticism on mountaintops has passed. Asceticism is now required within our homes and in our minds. Those who come here must reflect the virtues of the ashram.
