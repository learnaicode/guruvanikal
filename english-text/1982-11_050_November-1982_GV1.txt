[GV1:P1229] November 1982

[GV1:P1230] Maintaining *Brahmacharya* (celibacy/purity) is extremely difficult. The term *Brahmacharya* does not merely mean abstaining from marriage. What all is encompassed within *Brahmacharya*? If such a person commits any mistake, they will immediately feel a sense of remorse—a realization that the error occurred by their own hands. They will promptly find a resolution for it and execute it. If anyone else attempts to harm them, they will console themselves by thinking, "He is doing all this to me out of ignorance." Alternatively, even if provoked to anger in the moment, they will forgive, endure, and handle the situation gracefully. Consequently, a quality of tolerance will naturally manifest within them.

[GV1:P1231] In addition to this, by being exposed to the adverse effects of such actions from numerous people, he will acquire profound experience. Through this experience, he will be able to reform a thousand people and introduce it to countless others. It is such an individual who subsequently enters *Sannyasa* (renunciation). What distance will then remain between him and a householder?

[GV1:P1232] Those who are referred to as householders remain deeply estranged from true life. The majority of householders are individuals incapable of even contemplating what human life truly means. Their existence is akin to that of animals. Each of you assumes that you possess substantial knowledge, education, culture, and experience in various matters. But have you ever reflected on how true this holds when translated into your actions? Even if we leave that aside, what happens when it comes to the role of a friend? You are mutually antagonistic, devoid of humility, willing to do anything for selfish ends, and so perverse that you are incapable of living in harmony with even a single person. Then, what place is there for your knowledge, culture, or honesty? Driven by the ego of 'I', you project yourselves as symbols of a life desperately yearning to lead a mere bestial existence. Would you be pleased when this is pointed out? How can one reform an individual who leads a completely deceptive life, brimming with sheer perversity—all while constructing a facade of arrogance? You possess neither the inclination to understand this nor the capacity to assimilate it.

[GV1:P1233] Like a soaring eagle, you are returning to this knowledge. At that point, the nature of the eagle will transform, and you will acquire the distinctiveness of a swan. This requires a serene intellect. No one possesses it. For instance, if you observe people seated in a gathering, you can discern where their gaze wanders. The perversions of the mind are spinning relentlessly like a machine. They are plagued by base thoughts, unable to remain steadfast or still in one place. They merely sit there; their minds are nowhere to be found in that vicinity. What can someone who spends their entire life in such a manner ever accomplish? Therefore, the knowledge to live with propriety must be cultivated first. It is that very knowledge which will nurture and elevate all of you.

[GV1:P1234] The commodities in our daily use today are the culmination of the lived experiences, practices, and immense hardships of our ancestors over countless epochs. As mentioned earlier, a profound legacy of sacrifice underlies the evolution of the very garments we wear today, from their conceptual origin to their final woven form. Ponder the sheer depth of this sacrifice. Similarly, consider our customary practice of igniting a fire to cook and consume food. During an era devoid of fire, who discovered it and through what means? What constitutes its true magnificence? What immense sacrifices must have been endured to achieve this! Do we ever contemplate such matters? This applies to every single aspect of human progress. From whose intellect did all these innovations emerge? Through whose benediction were they rendered usable for us?

[GV1:P1235] Take the example of clothing itself. How do we even comprehend the manner in which the conceptualization of its creation, its aesthetic appeal, and its functional utility—combined with earnest craftsmanship—came into existence? There must necessarily be a foundational concept within the mind. Who constructed that vision within the human consciousness?

[GV1:P1236] Today, we possess words, alphabets, and language to effectively communicate the thoughts that arise in our minds to others. Yet, we rarely cast our gaze back to an era when communication was conducted solely by uttering a few rudimentary sounds. The emergence of alphabets, words, language, and grammar is underpinned by the sacrificial lives of numerous great visionaries. We must retrospectively examine the immense sacrifices involved in the endeavor to create these linguistic structures. Such monumental achievements were only made possible by individuals who deeply contemplated and dedicated their lives to the welfare of others.

[GV1:P1237] The discovery of medicines follows an identical trajectory. It was the ascetic sages, characterized by their profound sacrifices, who discovered that a specific medicinal herb functions in a particular manner and serves the human body in a specific way. Today, armed with absolute ignorance, you have brazenly set out to mock and dismiss these very sacrificial figures. Reflect upon each of these aspects in this manner.

[GV1:P1238] Despite inheriting all of this in its present advanced state, you exhibit numerous despicable behaviors driven merely by a desire to display perversity. Such behavioral aberrations must be eradicated from within you. Consequently, it is the imperative of the present age to foster a new generation that brings about a radical transformation in its knowledge, culture, and character, enabling individuals to live exemplary lives that are highly beneficial to their homes, their nation, and the world at large. Cultivating such an enlightened lineage remains the fundamental purpose and ultimate objective.

[GV1:P1239] Guruvani
