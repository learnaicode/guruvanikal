[GV1:P1602] July 1986

[GV1:P1603] Kerala is a small state today. Although known to many in its novelty, believe that novelty is a purity of antiquity. What is considered this purity consists of the ideas and aspirations of great souls who have taken multiple births. They have not forgotten to demonstrate their courageous actions through these ideas. Certain individuals, standing isolated, deceived the people by manipulating these widespread ideas and aspirations into a deceptive web, much like a mechanical and cunning Chanakya-style administration. We have been convinced numerous times that the conditions where most of those who earnestly tried to awaken from this and failed, occurred during their own lifetimes. Poets and artists have been trapped and troubled by this. Having read all this and attained scholarship, some learned men and critics have set out to show that the asset of this came through them. Quoting their aphorisms, they try to bring them to the forefront by saying, 'Haven't you seen what so-and-so said?' Instead of hastily tying their intellect with the intellect of those ancients and declaring 'Here I am at the forefront', if they use the intellect and firmness bestowed upon them by God to independently reflect, understand the dangers that have occurred to date, point them out, and write at least a few words as their contribution to remind the masses about that karmic proficiency in a manner suitable for today's times, they can attain courage. Otherwise, caught in the cunning tricks that make one believe these are words from the lineage of Manu, mixing Brahminhood and the state of Brahman, stepping down to replicate it in the state of Shudras and Chandalas, suppressing praise, condemnation, Gita, Yoga, and karma, displaying a long list of dharma to conceal reality, and wrapping it in old, fabricated tales of karma, it will end up like a pot of nectar overflowing resembling dog excrement wrapped in exquisite silk through the perspective of objects that are impure offerings. I only wish that they can contemplate what is required to move away from the footprints of this deceit.
