[GV1:P1069] November 1981

[GV1:P1070] Disciple: How would it be appropriate if the very people who have thus far displayed intellectual cunning and troubled humanity in every way come to the forefront of this?

[GV1:P1071] Guru: They themselves must come. They are the ones who must accomplish and settle this. This is because, in India, it appears as though all the negative consequences of their various actions over a long period have accumulated, and the burden of sin has rebounded upon them. Therefore, the qualification for this belongs entirely to them.

[GV1:P1072] Disciple: Wouldn't it be better for this to materialize if Christians and Muslims take it up?

[GV1:P1073] Guru: It is good for it to materialize. However, there are some other shortcomings in that. It is a religion that has operated under a single prophet. It does not possess a very long history; it only dates back to the time of Moses. Furthermore, another offshoot called Islam has branched out from it. Both these religions have more or less accomplished what they could from their respective paths. Regarding India, this is even more significant. Through the British administration, they brought order and discipline to humans who had been reduced to a cruel, animal-like state due to the evils of the Varna-Ashrama Dharma; they systematized these poor people by first providing them with something to ensure nutritional sustenance. Similarly, they introduced an enlightened education system. As at least some of these poor people managed to attain it, certain religious and educational institutions rose here with British assistance, resulting in a situation where the administration itself empowered them to outmaneuver these cunning individuals.

[GV1:P1074] The Muslims are not like that. They merely handled a religious system. And then, applied some brute force. They too managed to secure a certain hold in this manner. Even though they secured such a hold, the majority of them are not people who maintain a good status. The reason for this is that they are individuals uplifted from various communities that were culturally deeply degraded. Therefore, no matter how enlightened they become through education and wealth, or how long they are seasoned in administration, they have not reached the path of Sanatana Dharma—an enlightened state combining orderliness, cleanliness aligned with Hindu Dharma, and a pure lifestyle blended with humility. The reason for this is the flaw that occurred when all the aforementioned communities merged and adhered to different religious systems.

[GV1:P1075] Disciple: Is that why they are at the forefront in activities like poultry and goat rearing?

[GV1:P1076] Guru: It is not only because of that, although it can be cited as one reason. It is also due to the advice from those who guide them in ways that foster their economic prosperity.

[GV1:P1077] Disciple: Quite a few such things are happening among us as well, aren't they?

[GV1:P1078] Guru: Yes. Seeing this, some things might have been done. However, that is not at the forefront. What is called Sanatana includes stories that have been passed down from ancient times to the stories up to the author of the Gita existing today. Putting all this together, one only sees a haste to establish Advaita. It is just words. There is no systematization in practice. One who sticks to the character of this Advaita, one who preaches this Vedanta - is so wretched that he cannot even treat his next-door neighbors or his own siblings with equality. That is why it is said that he cannot correct himself from this path. As for Muslims and Christians doing it, their time has only just arrived. Even that resulted in them fighting among themselves and causing various kinds of destruction. Thus, the time has not yet come for them to reach their own peace and survival. Not only that, according to the divine will regarding the era of Manu called Sanatana, it is difficult to find in the history of the world until today any other lineage with such deep reach, such a long-standing history of activity, or a religion that has discovered such a great culture. What is even more important is that the Hindu dharma has the responsibility to guide the lineage of this Manu to function and settle things in the remaining time. The Manu lineage is meant to provide divine dharma to the world. Therefore, the philosophical discipline that encompasses all these world religions is hidden within this. Similarly, it is not easy for the world to bring about another one like it. Thus, attention must be paid to bringing about a complete culture according to the divine will. Whether Christian or Muslim, all religions need to bring this into focus. Even though there is a realization that the complete culture encompassing all religions is one and the same, for each of them, many great people have come and enlightened them with the sacrifices of their respective times and the actions that embodied those sacrifices. Age, experience, etc., have built up many cultures. One can see that uniqueness piled up here and there in the Hindu dharma. It is doubtful whether other religions possess such a great value. To bring about a special character suited to this Kali Yuga by combining what is in other religions as well, all religious followers and all religious scholars must unite and evaluate the hybrid era of the author of the Gita. It is a path that must be completed harmoniously by those living today and those yet to come.

[GV1:P1079] Disciple: Is it in the sense of 'what is to come' that you inform us by saying certain things involving the twelve zodiac signs, nine planets, and twenty-seven stars, and make us understand a method that demonstrates that practical nature?

[GV1:P1080] Guru: Yes. Unless a path of action is implemented by evaluating these three periods, it is not possible to see unity in any other way or to experience in the future the actions done for the good of human society. For this, evaluating the cause, effect, macro, and micro aspects, removing its flaws to that extent can only become possible for our survival, which we preserved earlier, if it reaches up to four or five generations as a systematic progression of a lineage. The summary of the above is that it will not be possible for anyone other than the children who come by the grace of God.

[GV1:P1081] Disciple: Does this mean that all the religious cultures and administrative cultures that have come down through the ages until today should blend together as one, and its own path of practice should come forward as the core, form, fulfillment, and action in the value of human sacrifice?

[GV1:P1082] Guru: Yes. We must certainly understand that only if it happens this way can human effort be evaluated in submission to the divine will, value be assigned to the sacrifice that operates and culminates through it, and the peace and harmony of all of us who worked together in its goodness be realized. I conclude with a prayer that the divine resolve (Brahman's will) may bless us for this.

[GV1:P1083] Guruvani
