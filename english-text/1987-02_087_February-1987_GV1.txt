[GV1:P1685] February 1987

[GV1:P1686] After material and spiritual explorations, humans select certain sanctuaries and, according to their intellect or divine will, glorify them as holy lands, turning them into a distinct arena. These sanctuaries are viewed as holy lands where many go and perform penance according to their intellect, abilities, and capacity, and where the fruits of that penance endure for a long time. We must be very careful before setting out to such sanctuaries. That was the discipline in ancient times. Now, we see a decline in such discipline. Some might still go that way. As far as we are concerned, most people do not pay attention to this. The power of penance accumulated over countless ages, along with the struggles and steadfastness in duty of countless people, resides in the aforementioned places. Very few people think that we should be able to experience any one of these realms and possess the proficiency in action required to understand its authenticity.

[GV1:P1687] Even after the passage of ages and through immense effort, attaining an awareness of at least one epoch (yuga) is exceedingly difficult. This is because we have not lived our lives with such mindful vigilance. Furthermore, we have not acquired a sense of righteousness corresponding to the epochal duty (yuga dharma). We merely lived according to what we saw. Even among us, there is a section of people who somehow speak, act, live, and let others live to some extent. However, we must possess the wisdom—at least through careful observation—to understand the nature of the ascetic power of the great souls who have traversed across ages. The principal aspect of this pilgrimage must be undertaken in this manner.

[GV1:P1688] We undertake pilgrimages in various ways. Depending on our own sincerity towards it, we often go alone; sometimes, people travel in groups out of a desire to elevate their lives. For some, this is a path of escape. The belief underlying that desire is that their body will no longer recover in any way. Others, despite earning, giving, and receiving abundantly, perceive a certain emptiness in their existence—they set out seeking how this occurred and what caused it. Apart from these, individuals who have sought merit through virtuous deeds journey with a fervent determination to fulfill their lives, or to do and inspire others to do at least a fraction of what they can for the people, contemporaries, and future generations in the concept of God. But we are entirely ignorant of all this. We are unaware that such a realm of spiritual wealth exists and that we have a role to play in this scheme of action. We do all these things without knowing why.

[GV1:P1689] Irrespective of what we do among these, or even if one becomes a perfect ascetic, we are all destined to leave this bodily cage. When we depart from this cage, we do not pause to reflect on what fortune we managed to accumulate within it, what our meritorious deeds were, and what constitutes them. Even if we do think about it, we limit it to a brief reflection. What is the part we can play in this scheme of action? We should become an asset of wealth for ourselves, or for ten, a hundred, or a thousand people. What are the qualifications we need for this? We have never thought in this manner either.

[GV1:P1690] The effort cultivated over numerous ages in accordance with the epochal duty is an endeavor that began from a time when one did not even know how to wear clothes, or how to seek them—or even when one could not contemplate whether clothes were needed or not. That has evolved through food, clothing, and civilization, leading us to reach various states today. Even the material and the spiritual domains have emerged through refinement. This material and spiritual are not two different entities. They are one and the same. They are categorized into two merely for the sake of differentiation through nomenclature, to attain certain realizations.

[GV1:P1691] This very material body serves as the foundation for the spiritual as well. The foundation of the spiritual comes to us, knowingly or unknowingly. Having profoundly contemplated over ages on what occurred in the material realm, what occurred in the spiritual realm, or if something did occur, what is to be derived from it, certain great souls firmly resolved to fulfill their lives and do everything possible to bring about peace and harmony beneficial to the world. A fragment of the ascetic practices they conceived, acted upon, and realized exists in these places. It is for this reason that we select such holy sanctuaries and undertake pilgrimages.

[GV1:P1692] In ancient times, there were individuals who investigated all this and undertook journeys with purity of action. Such rare individuals exist even today. There are those who reside permanently in various places such as Kashi, Rameswaram, Ayodhya, and Vrindavan. These permanent residents are to be considered merely like the numerous crocodiles present in Kashi. Nothing more has emerged from it. However, only a person who contemplates this can reclaim the knowledge of its meritorious essence, and thereby, acquiring courage in action, become one among the countless people who earn it for the sake of humanity, all living beings, and contemporaries.

[GV1:P1693] Guruvani

[GV1:P1694] March 1987

[GV1:P1695] Thus, after wandering around and passing a considerable amount of time, such a soul—some of them—returns for eternity. Thus, someone whom we call a prophet, a deity, or a guru emerges once in an era, in a manner that they cannot even be considered one in a million. That is what is meant by a prophet or a guru.

[GV1:P1696] Everyone can be a guru. Fathers, mothers, husbands, wives, and children are everywhere! Therefore, everyone is a guru, right! Not in that sense. It is a soul that returns after contemplating and recognizing this, having diligently executed the scheme of action in that ascetic power for a long time, and then surrendering. Whatever this returning soul does, people turn out in two ways to either accept or abuse him. There is a kind of people who agree to whatever is said. They are completely useless. There is another group—the impotent ones. Today, they perform actions with stubbornness. They perform actions with immense stubbornness and clamor. Among these, only a very few honor the truth. Perhaps one in ten million people might emerge as virtuous. Most of the aforementioned people would be there to abuse and assault him. It is such people who are born and die among us. However, there is a small section that differs from this. They can be called by any name, whether a devotee, an active worker, or a wise person. We perform our actions by being part of this. When performing actions as part of this, what all thoughts should we possess?

[GV1:P1697] What have we and our ancestors accomplished? What actions have they performed for this world? We turn to this path to learn this. This is a story unknown to most people. When we turn to this path, there is a medium to focus the attention of those with experience—for instance, householders with a minor level of experience, even if they are not spiritualists. There are a few men and women here with vision. There is also a circumstance to learn many things through them. There is a medium to focus their attention too. They must also have a resolve. During my life, during the life of my society, what am I witnessing? What am I hearing? What is my duty towards them? Numerous people like me have been born, lived, died, and perished; what is this provision I have received? For what purpose is what I have seen and what God has shown me? A medium that aids in reflecting upon these. Having instilled monotheistic belief and brought it to you, God is establishing a stage in this medium through you that is capable of bringing change to the people of the world themselves.

[GV1:P1698] In that position, if you act in a manner that evades the duty of seeking action through purity of mind, word, and deed, there are certain dangers. Those with experience must be very careful; the matters stated in this* must be very carefully noted. What all is transpiring in this world? Have you not seen that there are inner-dwellers (antaryamis) and the special qualities mentioned about them in it?
