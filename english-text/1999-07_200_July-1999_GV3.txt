[GV3:P0021] Santhigiri's is the knowledge guided by God. These two thousand four hundred and forty-four are past gurus. All these gurus will be born in this lineage. Along with these gurus, crores of their lineage are also to be born. They all have to be born before this Kali Yuga ends. Those who work for their birth hold the position of those gurus. And then liberation. Another special provision is that the entire clan of those who create the lineage that straightaway carries this ideology will be purified. How many births will you have to take to complete this karma?

[GV3:P0023] Let me describe an illustrative vision received here. What we have seen in the world so far is nothing. There is much to be seen on a much larger scale than what has been seen. There is much to reach. It will come. A notification for that has been received. The primary lineage of this is the Santhigiri lineage.

[GV3:P0024] A stream of light is striking the earth in one or two places in Kerala. [GV3:P0025] People have started saying that what exists today is not enough. The people who discover this light will make public what this inadequate thing is. Along with that, a capability for karma will erupt from here so that the world understands what Santhigiri's mission is. They will be the perfect prophets of this mission, which is God's will. I say truly that every child who acts courageously for this will be praised as holy souls who impart the purity of karma essential for the eternal salvation of the world.

[GV3:P0026] God has given us the opportunity to retrieve the lost parts of the world. If it is not done properly, it will be lost again. Those parts must be grown as a support and shade for God's will. If not grown that way, or if an attempt is made to grow it by holding onto worldly justice, God will not give it again. One person must do the work of a thousand people. We have that much more work to do. Acting for one year like this will yield the result of acting for a thousand years. The thoughts and actions of you poor people will become research materials in universities within fifty years. For that, your humility and eligibility must rise to that extent. The gain from life in this ashram is very great. For that gain to happen, one must live forgetting caste and religion. Even if your beauty is destroyed, wealth is lost, or illness comes, life will not be destroyed. It is enough to be brave. If there is bravery, there is a blessed life. The life that comes like that is the greatest.

[GV3:P0027] This is not the age of Devas. Making Devas appear is not going to achieve anything either. What takes many births to achieve can be achieved in one birth in Kali. Kali is the time that came as a correction in the Rishi tradition. Kali is the time of the Shudra. The Shudra exists in all Varnas and in all times. That correction must come from the Shudra himself. For the correction in the Rishi tradition to come from the Shudra, who must he become? He must become a Rishi, a wise man, and attain liberation. He must become above all worshiped figures.

[GV3:P0028] This eligibility must come to receive the knowledge according to the will of Brahma that there has been an error in the dharma over time. Through states called disembodied voices and direct manifestations, a path of resolving karma is happening at Santhigiri, which is capable of resolving the sorrows of my twenty-nine years of life, and at the same time enabling all beings in the world to act and be made to act benevolently. The Guru path advised at Santhigiri is the greatest path of resolution that makes every human being practice the sections of dharma spanning from atoms to the absolute in the universes, bringing into them a life of dharma that removes sorrow, brings virtue through karma, and carefully guards dharma as the foundation through a life obedient to the will of Brahma. It theorizes solar system evolutions and is able not only to preserve them as theirs in karma, but it has become fully capable of bringing complete gain to the lineage.

[GV3:P0029] The Guru is appointed by the will of Brahma in accordance with the Yuga Dharma. Prophets who come for different time spans like two thousand, three thousand, five thousand years come under this Guru. The Guru is the visionary who foresees and commands what a Yuga needs and doesn't need. Only rarely in the solar system is a Guru born with the worthiness to witness such karmas. That Guru will have no aberrations. He is with the lowliest, he is with the highest, and he will not hate the rogue. Since diversity came from oneness, nothing is forbidden to such a Guru.

[GV3:P0030] An authoritative step for eternal survival throughout the world is being implemented through this ideology. Where Narayana Guru, Buddha, and Krishna left off, that is where our beginning is. [GV3:P0034] The Globally Radiant Path of Dharma

[GV3:P0035] Having known the good and bad through the birth we have received, we are reaching the historical truth of the long periods in which we have been born and raised under the dharmas of Kali Yuga. In this journey across lives, we have also passed by great souls who had visions for the times. But we are unable to retrieve it. Ours is a soul whose operational capacity has been lost in that way. Therefore, ours is a lifestyle of moving forward feeling happy with what is received. There is no point in living like a trapped wolf, employing our body, mind, and intellect without being able to overcome this fate.

[GV3:P0036] Kali is a time where even days, hours, and minutes are conducive to acting with awareness of the soul's operational capacity with the help of the body, which experiences waking, dreaming, and deep sleep states. It is also a time of connecting past lives, current, and future lives. If one attains firmness in present karma and achieves the elevation of dharma, this connection cannot be made. That is, if one gets trapped in contemporary theories that have gone astray, it will not be possible to move forward.

[GV3:P0037] There are many obstacles before us for this. One of them is Shaktism, Shaivism, and Vaishnavism. It may be due to the fortune of some past life that we got human birth. Thus we have had many human births before this. Unable to break away from tradition, such a person acts in various ways and loses his own fortune. As an example, for over five thousand years, great souls have come in many states and many forms of effort, yet where the Trimurti tradition has reached, the soul is spinning from there without finding a way to salvation.

[GV3:P0038] According to our belief system, the goddesses and gods we have been worshiping as deities are our God. Its succession rights belong to severe karmas ranging from ghosts to the city of vampires (Vethalanagaram). This is the biggest mistake we have inherited from our past tradition. We have not been able to realize that this is a mistake. How can we, who have degenerated to the level of having a separate god for each small caste, understand this? But we can understand. We can understand what the long historical truth of the past is by placing the soul in a suitable manner within the body, which is our accumulated karma. The status of a soul is to perceive the Yuga Dharma that has been spoken, sung, and taught about various things. Even a person who stood behind rationalism and worked firmly to acquire emptiness can qualify for this status by knowing certain things.

[GV3:P0039] When a god or goddess is worshiped and made to manifest, another will follow it. Thus, a halo of fortune of thousands of visions will come to us. To achieve this, we need a great soul capable of explaining these visions according to the times. Through that person, one must know what aspects of visions are, understand even demonic natures, and understand the part called the city of vampires before moving forward. However, we have a long history behind us of being satisfied with the gains from these ghosts, spirits, and demons, offering vows and gifts. The knowledge of our ancestors is also the same. Therefore, no one has the virtue to reclaim and act upon the dharma of the times. There is no karma that goes up to its theories. What can change this, how can it be changed - this is the subject we must think about.

[GV3:P0040] As far as we are concerned, when we move forward past the city of vampires, no matter who shows the path that reaches the karma of a Deva, the karma of a prophet, or the karma of a visionary in whichever way, an unmanageable demonic karma resides in everyone. If you look at anyone, you can see them moving forward with a kind of senseless life. We also have traditions in many forms that are unable to experience, respect, and absorb the glory and love of the Supreme Being. Without thinking about this destruction of karma, we have inherited a course of life where we cannot act upon the dharma of the times despite seeing, hearing, and knowing about it from somewhere.

[GV3:P0041] Then what is the course of life suitable for us? We must be told a traditional foundation that is lived out according to the times by conscious actions in the Turiya state, after showing visions in the waking, dreaming, and deep sleep states. This is the world-class karmic dharma. For that, one must be able to completely see (in vision) all past Kali Yugas and all Chaturyugas. One must be able to discern the truths and untruths in it. We need the foundation of knowledge brought to completion by five Gurus who reached the states of gurus across time. We, every child in all religious doctrines of the world, must get this familiarity. What is intended here is to harmoniously see the operational capacity of the past Chaturyugas and the specifics of the present and future truths of dharma. Then, we arrive at a path of knowledge where we directly see and hear what the mistakes of past times were.

[GV3:P0042] What is referred to as states is about the part that perfects Shaktism, Shaivism, and Vaishnavism. The ancestral acharyas have not been able to reach a state perfected in that way. This is also the extent of the meaning of the word 'Kala Dharma' (Dharma of the times). When looking back at the dharmas of acharyas, we can see that some great souls have practiced certain true dharmas suitable for the times. What is intended by Kala Dharma is the timely dharma that shapes their supreme love and the tri-temporal (past, present, future) natures they saw, knew, spoke, and struggled to attain. However, since we have traditionally followed a life that is out of tune with the times and lacks regularity, this timely dharma is like a reflection in the water for us.

[GV3:P0043] Knowledge is said to be like an ocean. Knowledge that reached capacity in which era? Now, by justifying and observing this, can a wise man whose soul is in distress be given guidance that integrates the four eras, acquiring the total meaning of life, seeing various wholes like the attainment of the other world, heaven, hell, or the universe, to complete it? This is a part that must be seen, known, shown, and completed. For that, a globally radiant path of truth must shine before us, showing who we each are through our past lives of mind, word, and deed. Through that, there should be a karma where the knowledge of the past and present is given correctively in parts known as vision, disembodied voice, or light. Only a visionary can give that.

[GV3:P0044] Now, who is a visionary? Is it someone who completes by singing, speaking, and writing about three periods in the Chaturyugas - presenting long lists from Puranas and epics? No. Long-term vision is the knowledge of a consciousness that stands in wisdom, devoid of birth, having seen and known the history of creation, preservation, and destruction from a grain of sand to all forms of characteristics.

[GV3:P0045] Vision is long-term vision. Seven thousand four hundred and forty-four visions is one state. Thus, when reaching a state in the nature of a visionary by seeing visions, through the person with that knowledge, determining its density--knowing how and where our mind and ideology stand in the subject we have seen, known, heard, and taken, we must start as 'one' from there.

[GV3:P0046] Narayana Guru has said in one place, "A man who crosses the boundaries of the three worlds and expands needs no other work to do," and in another place, "The lamp that shines after the triputi (knower, known, knowledge) is destroyed will not fall into the hands of a deceitful ascetic." In this section, there is a subject we must be very careful about. After the entire nature of our mind's primal karma has come into us, we can only grow according to the worthiness of the person who shows and corrects what is to come next. This is why it is commanded not to go without a Guru.

[GV3:P0047] What is said is this: We are seeing and knowing many things. In that, from ghosts, spirits, and demons to Yakshas, Gandharvas, and Kinnaras is one state. [GV3:P0049] We have a familiarity from Puranas where some water is drawn from the sea, kept in various vessels, and we are told that it is seawater and it is pure. If we want to at least somewhat understand the dynamics of the wheel of time that changes periodically, this familiarity is not enough. It is impossible for anyone with this familiarity to reclaim the temporal divisions made of devas, asuras, and demons up to this day in the Trimurti system. If scholars who are commentators on letters think they can clear doubts and rewrite, it won't happen. [GV3:P0050] The result of effort over a long time has become the soul's karmic tendency, like a path of sacrifice. [GV3:P0053] What about the knowledge of ants, cockroaches, and numerous other living beings? Vision is what defines this as a virtue. Many names and many biographies have come into the realm of knowledge. Scholars state it in their terms, and sacrificing soul-knowers state it in their terms. [GV3:P0054] Celestial spheres have nine natures. There is another nature that divides it into four. In which karma of self-knowledge called vision, and in which tendency can this nature be served to each person in different ways? The ability to impart the light of eras is the main thing here.

[GV3:P0055] The diverse historical truth of nine spheres is what is utilized in a soul. It cannot be illuminated by the glory of knowledge measured by the intellect. Then who can illuminate it? [GV3:P0058] The Tapas Practice Entitled to the Yuga Dharma

[GV3:P0059] There was a state when I tried to think about how my past life was. Crores of souls who took human form and reached the state of the Trimurtis stand in the subtle realm. All are form-concepts created by humans. That is, a conceptual form belonging to the artistic sense of an artist who draws outlines. The subtle realm is a procession of crores of conceptual forms that humans have imagined in this way. Rahu and Ketu are formed by the gathering of the light of such conceptual forms. This even covers the sun and the moon. That is how an eclipse occurs. During an eclipse, the rays of the sun hit the moon, and its dust particles falling on the earth are considered poisonous potencies. This is shown to ascetics. They will purify these toxic substances (mercury, sulfur, vermilion, etc.) found from the earth and keep them in their bodies. The practice of alchemy (Rasavada) is purifying and applying the most poisonous of these. Scientists say that even gold can be made from this. This is what is depicted as a peacock, the vehicle of Subramanya. It shines because it has more copper in it. The treatment method of applying peacock oil arose by applying intellect in this. An application of even greater intellect is the Kayakalpa treatment performed by ascetics. If Kayakalpa treatment is done, then food and water are not needed. The eight siddhis and Samadhi by bursting the head are all parts of this.

[GV3:P0060] When an ascetic who has performed Kalpa Seva is made to meditate sitting in a cave with eight faces, changing positions according to zodiacs and stars, the bones in the body will become like cotton, making him unable to stand or walk. Siddha medicine is a treatment system completed by consuming the Navapashanas (nine poisons) in various ways along with Guru Kalpa. The things done by creating caves with seven and nine faces and keeping the body at each cave entrance at every watch (Yama) are part of this treatment. It takes eighteen and a half years to complete this Kalpa Seva system by taking this body and placing it in all nine cave entrances as watches change and turn. The Puranas and Vedic sciences are descriptions of what was completed in this way. The six-faced Subramanya is the one who carried the Kalpa Seva as six sciences. His vehicle is the peacock. It is poison. Subramanya's body had the strength to carry this much poison. The authors of the Puranas called this Subramanya 'Mayilvahanan' (He who rides the peacock).

[GV3:P0061] The state of Subramanya is the power of penance acquired over numerous births. Names like Shubhramaniyan and Arumukhan came because of this. Vyasa, who devised this, was brought in, made a Vaishnava, given the name Krishnadwaipayana, and depicted. Characters like Drona, Bhishma, and Shalya are stories depicted by devising such imaginations. Shaivism, Shaktism, and Vaishnavism are also imaginations devised over various periods. What is described by differentiating this is Ravana's head. His head needs to be equal to the heads of ten Brahmins. That is, he has the intellect of ten Brahmins. That character was changed into a Brahmin, and changed into Ravana. Where he was supposed to attain ten states, he was made Ravana. He couldn't be devised into ten states, could he?

[GV3:P0062] It is by keeping aside the soul that grows like this that it incarnates as a Guru. It is by assigning this meaning that it is said a Brahmin should not be questioned. This meaning of Brahminhood will come up in many places and contexts. When it comes into the imagination of a poet, Shiva's people will write praising Shiva. When crossing and seeing the Puranas, I had to think about the true state and the courageous actions of Ravana.

[GV3:P0063] The Brahmin class only reaches up to a Deva. A Brahmin acting as a Deva, decorating like a Deva, and standing with positions as Goddess and God is the karma called 'Brahmajnaneti Brahmana'. A knower of Brahman exists in every class. A Deva will not let anyone pass into the state of wisdom. That is what chased Sri Krishna around without letting him stay in India. Sri Krishna has crossed the state of Deva and attained siddhi in numerous Yugas. Sri Krishna was born in such capability. All demons were invoked and all witchcraft was done to kill Sri Krishna. But knowledge is not an outline of imaginative fancy. It is a state that stands devoid of form. [GV3:P0072] The Extensive Meaning of a Guru's Birth

[GV3:P0073] The wheel of time or Chaturyuga. How many visions when a Chaturyuga revolves? The total practice of penance of a Chaturyuga traveling around is one state. A soul that does not break the transition of a Yuga becomes the authority of a Yuga. A soul that does not break the transition of a Chaturyuga becomes the authority of a Chaturyuga.

[GV3:P0074] Five Gurus will come as the authority for a Yuga. Along with them, Gurus who have completed 500, 2000, 3000, 5000, 10000 years will come. They are also called Gurus. Those educated people who do not know what they wrote, or what they left out from filling, are the ones who interpret letters and state all Yuga Dharmas wrongly. [GV3:P0075] Many great men have failed to revolve around the penance practice of a Yuga and acquire the state. The wheel of time to be evaluated is not about the wheel of time that passed five hundred or a thousand years ago, but the wheel of time that sees through the coming Yugas.

[GV3:P0076] The Yuga Dharma is described as four periods—Satya Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dwapara Yuga, and Kali Yuga. If one wants to completely know about all these four Yugas, only one who revolves and knows the wheel of time (Chaturyuga) through penance practice, whatever stages have been conceptually brought in, can do so. However, a Guru who has completed from 500 years up to a Yuga is also a Guru. There is a temporal dharma completing one Yuga and a temporal dharma completing a Chaturyuga. Through penance practice, one can cross all wheels of time. Those who fail to achieve this are the ones who practice yoga due to the quality of the soul. Such yoga practices are meant to cross the era of Gods and Goddesses. Sri Ramakrishna's emphatic statement 'Not this, not this' is exactly this. It is also this that a soul that has crossed the position of a God desires to see the Kali Yuga, or desires to be born in it. There are also souls that have requested not to be born in the Treta and Dwapara Yugas. Such followers of Yuga Dharma are specially set aside. It is a great soul who has grown in the order of the sky, broken transformation, and come according to Yuga Dharma who is born as a Guru. Such Gurus will cross conceptual forms and reach the karma of open space mentioned by Agastya. One who reaches seven hundred such open spaces is a Yuga Dharmi. This open space will explain all dharmas beneath it.

[GV3:P0077] Good things do not catch on in all parts of the earth. Similarly, good water will only pool in certain places. When the sun's rays hit due to the progressive pressure of the earth and the moon's dust particles fall on the earth, what conditions those dust particles are the day and night, the new moon and full moon, and the natures of the stars. Crossing all these one by one, crossing the distances one by one through numerous formless open spaces is the soul, whose life is arranged in the concept of a Guru. It is regarding the verses of a Guru whose life has passed like this, forming and emerging, that it is said, 'Word is Truth, Truth is Guru, Guru is God.'

[GV3:P0078] The Dharma of Kali waits to get the birth of a Guru who has been set aside as such a great soul. It is as the time permitted to get such a Guru that the author of the Bhagavatham said, 'Of all the times, Kali is indeed the best.' Not knowing how to improve this time, they explain and praise knowledge and ignorance, standing tied down. Besides, it can be done for the fulfillment of desired matters. It has been revealed that whatever the people wish for, it will yield results.

[GV3:P0720] Such a thing happens only very rarely across eons. It is informed that you will get an opportunity through this to correct it whenever a mistake happens in the divine decree. This will bestow upon you a portion of merit that never perishes in any era and an accumulation of wealth that never decays. Thinking that let whatever needs to come, come, and whatever needs to go, go, you must bravely carry this forward. Everything we do here has been made to be done by making us experience and believe it. All this is for you. You are the ones who need to nurture and sustain this in an organizational nature.

[GV3:P0721] Question: What is the reason for all four yugas having the same repetitive nature?

[GV3:P0722] Guru: This question arises because the tradition of Hinduism has become immeasurably old. Sanatana Dharma means the culture of the great souls who existed in their respective times. History explains that the cultures which came and went in the forms of Shaktheya, Shaiva, and Vaishnava tried to reform mankind to an extent. In between, the way some acharyas followed the religious lineage can also be seen. However, those who came that way do not belong to the lineage of the original Manu. In the lineage of Vaivasvata Manu, the original Manu of this Manvantara, precisely due to some flaws that occurred to an acharya named Sathyathranan who came at the end of the third Chaturyuga, he went towards selfishness, and hence the reign according to God's will did not happen. Sathyathranan's concept was like a theory of evolution. Therefore, in order to implement the will of Brahman, the Trimurti system was brought in during the seventh Chaturyuga.

[GV3:P0723] Question: Did the Trimurti system not exist before that?

[GV3:P0724] Guru: No. Manus are those who fulfill the ideas according to the will of Brahman regarding the growths and declines of the world up to a certain period. Through such fourteen Manvantaras, the origin, creation, sustenance, and dissolution of this world have been brought about.

[GV3:P0725] The nature of the soul according to the will of Brahman has also come about in this way.

[GV3:P0726] Question: Why is it that most people do not know that there is such a Manu lineage?

[GV3:P0727] Guru: As mentioned earlier, the feeling of "I" (Aham Brahmasmi) happened to the Manu lineage. In that ego, it was seen in the state of knowledge that all the acts of knowledge that had occurred in this world until then came through it. If you look at any Vedanta books, you can see this feeling of "I" standing out. You can see this independent feeling of "I" coming to the forefront if you speak to any ordinary person. When coming to the term Brahmachari, such feelings came through the Guru lineage. The deity I hold, or the goddess I hold in the lineage of deities, has more capability and glory. As a result of this coming to each individual, Chamundi, Yakshi, Pey, Marutha, Madan, and Mantramoorthi all became lords. This happened due to the ego and selfishness of each individual.

[GV3:P0728] Question: Why did it happen like this?

[GV3:P0729] Guru: As I said earlier, the Trimurti system was created by the command of the Brahma power when the will of Brahman did not materialize due to the ups and downs that occurred in the Manu lineage. Vedanta ceased to exist for this Trimurti system. Its nature became worship, prayer, and manifestation. When that nature changed to showing tests by making even Marutha dance, mankind was unable to fully see the glory of God or accurately know or study its measure, or the centers of knowledge.

[GV3:P0730] Question: If the Guru says so, doesn't it mean that nothing that has come so far is correct?

[GV3:P0731] Guru: Yes, what was said so far is that it is not correct.

[GV3:P0732] Question: You said that the Trimurti system was formed to execute the will of Brahman because the Manu lineage made a mistake, right?

[GV3:P0733] Guru: By bringing the varna-ashrama to the people who were worshiping in the Trimurti system in such a way that they could not act according to the merit of the time, caste and sub-castes were introduced into it. Prior to that, fabricated stories were created through sruti and smriti and made into authorities. Before that, empirical arguments came and some sannyasins themselves handled the siddhi systems. By the time it reached this point, the Manu system faded away even from memories. The current degradation is the result of not changing the systems that should be changed according to the change of time, and doing it with the vain idea that the position of Brahman should always remain in the hands of the Deva-Brahmins.

[GV3:P0737] Vow and Prayer

[GV3:P0738] It was hinted earlier that doing such a prayer here is according to an instruction. You must remember while doing this prayer, during this prayer time which rectifies the mistakes of a long time, if even a small scratch of ignorance enters your mind, it will spread. It will spread in many ways. We are doing the prayer to rectify the flaws that have occurred in all the actions of the householders, and similarly in all the actions of the sannyasins and brahmacharis. This is not a matter according to the capability of your mind. Therefore, some ignorance and mistakes may occur. There is nothing else to say other than that it is God's will making us do a special thing at a distance that no one can say. You should always think that may God give an opportunity for this Guru and the Guru lineage, which God has given us, to impart double the goodness after removing not only the shortcomings of mine and my ancestors that have occurred over time, but also the overall shortcomings of the people of the world. The children you raise and who are growing, and the children standing here having accepted sannyasa are equally participants in this noble karma. You must also think about how it should be done if a way of redemption is to come through the karma you all do together. You should always think, while doing any work, that if there have been shortcomings through sannyasins and their lineage, or through any parts of this world, all of them should be destroyed, and in that place, the goodness according to the will of Brahman should come through the Guru lineage. When studying children do not get knowledge in their mind, they should pray like this. You should pray that not only the children in this lineage, but wherever there are children who have gone astray, they all get goodness in this way.

[GV3:P0739] This is the word God told us. You should pay special attention to boys and girls separately and pray for them. The karma coming from me, who is a woman, should correct the mistake of the woman in this world. The man should also think the same way. That should be your qualification. This qualification of the man and the woman is advancing together here. At that time, our mind should not become bad or we should not fall into circumstances that make the mind bad. Especially the men and women growing here are the ones to make judgments for the world. We should become great through this sense of dharma. Standing in this dharma, our constant prayer is that all the mistakes that have traditionally occurred not only in this solar system but also in other solar systems should be resolved, and we should be given the knowledge, familiarity, and experience to do what we need when we need it. When we have that knowledge and God tells us and makes us do it, we should be ready to do it. Other than this, thoughts like rubbish lying on the road should not be allowed to enter the mind. If this prayer is done on a full moon day, it will yield full results.
