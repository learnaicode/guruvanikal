[GV4:P1280] June 2006

[GV4:P1281] Our ancestors have already lived through these three epochs. What remains is the great illusion (*Mahamaya*) and the illusion of the Supreme Lord. Thus, confined within the protective veil of illusion, one is placed under the dominion of the Supreme Lord. What else should I tell you other than this? Is it not true? If brought under this Supreme Lord, is the time of the Lord not over? What is time? These three epochs. The *Satyayuga* has passed. It just went by like that. Then, according to the dharma that 'the universe will follow the course of genesis and karma', who are we by birth, who are we by action, who are we by merit, who are we by fortune, and what is the karma to be fulfilled? You yourselves must perform these five tasks. Entrusting them to just anyone will not suffice. You can entrust them; but only to one who is capable. Otherwise, it will result in ruin. Do you understand? 'This is *Gurudharma*'. There is no point in speaking of any other dharma. We must observe what became of our ancestors, our preceptors, or the incarnations who came as upholders of the epochal dharma, in their actions according to the *Yugadharma* that has passed through our many lifetimes. This is *Yugadharma*. This is what must be understood by holding onto the 'Guru of the transition of time' (*Kalantara Guru*). When there is a transition in time, one must know what all that entails. Every epoch has a transition. Likewise, the four-epoch cycle (*Chaturyuga*) has a transition. In this manner, seventy-one *Chaturyugas* have transitions. The preceptors who appear in each of those transitions are the true masters of this. You must witness it yourselves and attain it yourselves. Instead, what use is it to you if the 'Guru has seen it'? Therefore, this is why you attain discipleship; not merely for a commercial transaction. So, starting from demons, or as you often recite out of vanity now—what is it you say?—'Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha, and Vamana'; after these ten incarnations, how many incarnations have there been now? If you include Buddha and the rest, how many incarnations would that make? What is the use if so many incarnation-like beings came, and you and I just played along? This is not what I am saying. Whether it concerns you, me, or the world, without realizing the downfall caused by this singular error, we are fully suffering like a fish fallen into a stream. You must understand this. However many deities, goddesses, sages, ascetics, wise men, or devotees have passed in our lineage, we must liberate those who need liberation, protect and sustain those who need it, and they must ascend through our dharma in a manner befitting their karma. There might also be those among them who need to be reborn in our children. They too must do good for this world. This is not something that chases after anything else. It has not touched a single thing. It has not been involved in anything whatsoever. I am seeing this among the youth and among many others in your homes. By correcting this, it is being contributed to the world through you. If you do not understand this and act accordingly, my doing this will be like washing it down the river. You should only come here if you are prepared for this. Otherwise, there is no point in coming here. What you all have assumed is that when near death, or when all the nerves fail to straighten, one should go and lie in a wayside inn. Is that not what you have learned? But this is not that. Absolutely, for your age, and for any young age, look, even children of that age are experiencing this here. You should not assume that this is a mystical power (*siddhi*) or the continuation of something else. This is the first application. This is the divine resolve (*Brahmanischaya*). You should come and participate only if you have faith in this. Otherwise, I do not wish for you to come. I will never wish to summon you either. Then, having brought you to such an awareness, you will be instructed. That is a matter that satiates both your hunger and mine. It is meant to be consumed in such a way that one never hungers again. You should only consume it if you are willing. Then, try consuming it. We can consume it slowly. So, did you understand what was said? Sorcery, medicine, astrology, demonic possessions, and even the practices of an ascetic (*avadhuta*) were mentioned. It is not about telling other secrets. All of this has fallen within our experience.

[GV4:P1284] The Perversion of Various Forms of Worship

[GV4:P1285] The manner in which you have perceived things is different. We have possessed this traditional knowledge. You are aware that we have incurred certain deficiencies because we have engaged in highly uncultured forms of worship, which have permeated every religion, caste, and class nature. Because it has been passed down traditionally in this manner, we have not been able to recognize it, nor have we been able to comprehend how this occurred or the reasons behind its occurrence. Not just one, two, or four, but numerous exalted souls have come and practiced this for that purpose. If we observe the arrivals since the beginning of this *Kaliyuga*, it is traditionally passed down that there have been two thousand four hundred and forty-four preceptors. Sree Krishna came merely to demonstrate initially what karma entails within these five thousand or five thousand two hundred years. However, we did not understand any of that either. The stories written and taught to us for that purpose, and what we learned, have all led us down an erroneous path. Then, the only way to say there is no error in it is through that familial lineage. What is it? There is a familial lineage called *Yadava*. It emerged within the *Shudra* class. Having originated in a lower stratum, that community still possesses a familial instinct in Northern India and here, surviving alongside cattle and their lineage. But we have not understood any of this. We have not contemplated why kingly status was bestowed upon this *Shudra* class and brought about in this manner. The reason is that these *Yugadharmas* manifested as a completion of the errors that have occurred over myriad ages. Thus, without fulfilling this *Yugadharma*, no one can accomplish anything else. So, if asked how to fulfill this *Yugadharma*, we do not know that either. The greatest blunder we have committed due to this is the massive doctrine of not knowing what we must do in each epoch. Why? The *Satyayuga* is described here as an epoch that speaks everything truthfully, without mentioning caste, religion, or class. Its actions were also the same. But when the *Tretayuga* arrived, we were not taught what the nature of that *Tretayuga* should be. Nor were we taught about the *Dvaparayuga*. The tradition of the karma that humanity initially demonstrated in this *Tretayuga* still exists to a small extent among us. What is that? Retreating to the forest. You have been led to believe that *Sannyasa* means going and residing somewhere in a forest. This is not *Sannyasa*, and we should not perceive it as such. Moreover, it is impossible to do so in accordance with the present times.

[GV4:P1286] The *Yugadharma* is being transformed. However, it is said that when this *Yugadharma* is transformed, a quarter of that epoch will be spent on this transformation itself. Therefore, we lack knowledge regarding this *Yugadharma*. Or, if there is any, it is all attributed to the Supreme Lord. There is no knowledge to articulate anything beyond that. Otherwise, it is the same in other religions as well. There is another religion that believes a group is born into the homes of lowly people and yet must arrive like kings. Thus, we did not even possess the conviction that manifestations from the Divine could appear as kings, as mendicants, or as the uncultured. What happened to us because of this? It is absolute ignorance that has been handed down.

[GV4:P1287] Sri Krishna is one of those who were born in various places to change this ignorance. Before the beginning of Kali, that is, out of the thought that if Kali started, things would go out of order, it was made so. But that too was not successful. Nothing that came later was successful either. Thus, such an ignorance of Dharma exists among all of us, among all human beings. Now, Jesus himself came. Moses came—none of them came to create a caste or a religion. No one understood this. So what happened? When life was lost halfway, our nature of worship became a killing tree (the cross). Whatever they were hanged on, that gallows began to be worshipped. At the same time, some place a cross. But nothing else is placed. In this way, it initially transformed into eighteen types, and now sixty-four churches exist. Then, speaking of its smaller and smaller factions, it is said that there are over two hundred. So, if this has happened to a religious doctrine that emerged in just four thousand years, what else could not happen? It is a lack of original knowledge that has afflicted us. This original knowledge is supposed to be obtained entirely from God. Then one might ask, weren't they all born from God, then why is this? Or you may ask if it is because of their fault. It did not happen because of their fault. As they grow up, it takes time just to understand them. When they are understood, it takes time to disobey them. They are obeyed only after their time. Then, everything seen, heard, spoken, and known will begin to be celebrated as a science. In this manner, no matter whose history you take, the knowledge we have acquired to this day is like this. So, when a man is alive, what is it, or what could we grasp from the karma that a man living like that relied on and desired? We don't know that either. Having gone into the depths of a profound knowledge where we have absolutely no discernment, all this turned into knowledge and began to be spoken through miraculous powers. Thus, in whatever manner a person was born, about that person, in a very clear manner, webs of siddhis and mantric arts came along, and apart from that, the path of tantra as well. When this changed into mantra, tantra, and yantra, we became ignorant of what was in practice at its beginning, what should come in its middle, what the dharma of that Yugadharma is at this end, or how another begins later. What can we impart to our children?

[GV4:P1288] Now, you are all family people. Each of us has a course of karma that enables us to grow in accordance with the family and live in accordance with the land. Can these children live in the world by knowing what danger has befallen this land, family, and country? Apart from living with us according to the prevailing dharma, they get nothing else. Then, some so-called historians write and fill up whatever they can. When all that is written and filled—we don't even have the time to study it. Then what will we study? Thus, what can each of us do when we don't have the time to learn and teach about the respective eras? We fought by speaking of caste, religion, and class, bringing ourselves to a karma where children do not live together, being ignorant of the Yugadharma; what can we do about the denial of God that occurred by not knowing and following it? Think for a moment, if God exists in different ways for each caste, how can you say there is one God? If God is two different things for you and me, then it is nothing special. We all have God. If this God resides in different ways, how can we unite? Similarly, acting contrary to His karma, that contrary karma was diverted for the caste. Thus, karma was created through caste. Caste was created through karma. Thus, things contrary to each other are done. However, there have been some changes now—within these five thousand years. How is it? Now, everyone learns carpentry. The government has ordered to teach it like that. Similarly, the priesthood. It has been made to appear that whatever jobs exist in this world can be done by anyone. Just like this, education can be for everyone. If we see that education can be for all people, it means they are attaining a karma to be independent. So, because we lacked education, the ignorance of calling upon God while speaking of caste and religion often occurred. Believing it to be a karmic capability to manifest God, even the serpent dance (sarppam thullal) that originated in ancient times has become impossible to discard from our hands today. After that, by worshipping various kalari deities and displaying ferocity in various forms of worship, it is said that one attains the manifested power. How will our children understand this tradition acquired through dance, special rituals, and karma? Furthermore, do we have the time to study what our mothers or fathers tell us, or by sifting through the rights and wrongs that have come into our Vedic system? Or if we study, can we grasp anything? Because all kinds of shortcomings had come upon us like this, it took a long time just to follow the Yugadharma. So, when this Yugadharma begins, it will be something else entirely. It is seen through calculations that Kali is the shortest of eras. It has four hundred and thirty-two thousand years. So, can we call upon God only after this Kali era ends? It is said that Kali will not affect us. How did Kali not affect us? It is not like that. Kali is indeed good for all of us. What has been told to us and what our ancestors have taught us to this day is that Kali means destruction, isn't it? If our children themselves act improperly, attributing it entirely to the faults of Kali and explaining our own faults and virtues is the extent of our knowledge to this day. How can we find fulfillment in this knowledge? How can we act by knowing at least the dharma meant for us? It is a karma that we cannot accomplish in any way. However, by bringing forth these great souls here and there, no matter how they come before you, you must at least respect that the people sent by God have come to do that. Otherwise, you won't be able to accept it. For example, we could not understand Sri Krishna, who was born such a wise person. It was covered up with stories, turned into the most corrupted stories, changed into stories and plays, and became Kathakali. How much can we achieve in the karma of showing and informing that depicting all the evils in the land is our current intelligence?

[GV4:P1289] When examining each of these instances, we realize how long God has been working for us! However, we have completely failed to understand this. You may all ask: if God is everything as claimed, why can He not just change everything in a single stroke? That is incorrect. Consider the vast expanses of time, even the very calculation of a 'Kalpa' that you have heard of. It speaks of the history of a Kalpa—whether remaining inactive for that entire duration or functioning throughout it—that has encompassed countless 'Chaturyugas' or countless 'Manvantaras'. Can we proceed on the right path merely by studying all this? We cannot. What, then, should we do? If a prophet suited to the times, a preceptor (Acharya), or any such great soul (Mahatma) arrives, how will we recognize this person? We are utterly incapable of knowing. This is the same across everyone's history. Our shortest measure of time is this Chaturyuga. How so? Because Kali Yuga has only just begun. How can we, who cannot even fully comprehend a span of merely three epochs, grasp the extensive history of this Chaturyuga? Since we lack information regarding any of this, we have merely been reading the records written and preserved by whoever established the calculations, wherever they did so. How, then, could these historians formulate such an extensive historical account? They cannot. That is precisely why, no matter how clearly they stretch or condense the narrative, it remains a story that does not extend beyond ten thousand years. This is evident even if we examine the knowledge we currently possess. There is no need to look at anyone else's accounts. Since the birth of Sri Krishna up to the present day, the great souls who have incarnated have attempted to reform us by speaking in the simplest of terms, through their births and by causing births. Yet, no one understood this. Nor does anyone know who this is. Having reached that state, we have all fallen and decayed into a course of action where no one respects anything; we have drained its vital essence and instead gathered worthless refuse. Consequently, we have become thoroughly corrupted. How can we rectify this? It can only be achieved if a great soul instructs you. No ordinary individual is capable of imparting this. No one can correct you, except for that specific individual who has been granted the absolute authority for these times.

[GV4:P1292] The Path of Dharma Transcending the Stream of Thought

[GV4:P1293] Today, if an incident occurs anywhere in our known world, alternative mediums have emerged to present it before us instantly as news. That is a process that has evolved up to television, taking the place of true knowledge. Thus, you see this before you today. We live in an era where we lack the means to think and comprehend what this is or why it occurs in this manner. It does not readily yield to such thought. If we enter the stream of thought, what can we achieve other than pondering over various histories? We can achieve nothing. Therefore, we possess a trajectory of Dharma that inquires whether there is anything beyond this stream of thought. However, no community or nation has been able to grasp it. The familiarity we have with it today is constructed by intertwining all issues in a manner that merely incites us to fight among ourselves. Today, we are actively choosing the very place where the fortress of destruction—built by playing and manipulating the state machinery—has grown the largest. Every one of us is like that. What can those historical scholars who make these choices convincingly teach us about our true reality? Let each of you reflect upon this. Hence, if we, who are born and raised on a wretched path that is incapable of making us think, incapable of independent thought, and incapable of absorbing us into any real knowledge, try to figure out what we should become, it will prove futile. There is no point in invoking rationalism or theories of liberation (Mukti) for this purpose. If one speaks of the theory of Mukti, it cannot simply be taught. It is Mukti itself that is arguing. How can that be done? One who is bound to Mukti will have no time to tell you. If you hold any such understanding, please state it. You are all educated individuals. I am asking the educated. I possess no formal education. Therefore, you have the right to speak. Please do so. Thus, in this condition, it is not something that can be attained through mere study. Regarding that, as I mentioned earlier, the first university originated in our land; we claimed everything in its name. Is this not how we are taught? Are we not studying a history that claims when its scraps of paper were gathered and set ablaze, it burned for three months? What, then, are educated people like you going to serve here? What will you place into the hands of these children? You cannot offer anything. No historian can. No historical scholar can. Where do we currently stand regarding the actions we must undertake? Reflect on that as well. If we proceed without understanding that, we have no authority to articulate any justice concerning ourselves, our nation, or any part of the world. Let it be an educated or an uneducated person. Can we work and accomplish this to witness it as a reality within our lifetime? Is it an individual matter? No. What is required now? You tell me. What is it that we, who are alive today, need? Is it sufficient to merely patch together the remaining fragments of those stories taught by our parents and pass them on to our children? If so, that is enough. In that case, since the parents have already spoken it, does anyone else have anything further to say? When parents speak, they can only address other worldly matters. This is not the responsibility of the parents. Realizing this one truth, parents should admit, "Children, this is not it; we do not know. At least practice this; practice the acknowledgement that we do not know. As for what is known, practice that." Thus, if each of us—having degenerated to the extent of lacking even the authority to say this—can attain Mukti by highlighting every culmination of this destructive course of fighting among ourselves, then proceed along that path. Take the easy route. If you can accomplish something in this manner, then do it. Otherwise, there is no use in corrupting yourselves by repeating this story. You possess an awareness of Dharma, prompting you to ask whether this can be accomplished or not, and how it might be undertaken. How will you grasp this Dharma? I can only suggest a minor task for this. When I say 'minor task', I mean an easy task. It is not actually a small endeavor. None of you should approach it under the illusion that it is a small task. We, who have not even had the opportunity to hear and understand it, possess no capacity to distinguish between good and bad, or small and great. We can only boast meaninglessly by uttering such hollow words. Therefore, relying on historical truth, this is entirely impossible for us.

[GV4:P1296] The Field of Action Imparting Ideals in Accordance with the Yugadharma

[GV4:P1297] We have learned our contemporary history itself in such a way that we cannot understand it properly. Whether you want it or not, and if you do, what it should be—to practice at least the dharma you have heard about, you must have a realm of action that imparts the idea, mind, and intellect. You should not try to fulfill this through anything else. It is not possible. In your day-to-day life, for the rich man, it is the rich man's loss or the rich man's profit. For the one who works daily, it is about uprooting the working class. Other than this state of affairs, regarding you or us, what has this world imparted to us? What all has been built for us in the name of this caste, religion, and class? You will not think about this either. Then how are you going to think about these good things and tell your children? Or how are we going to mold our children to imbibe a good personality? When all this slipped from our hands, they took it away, and it became an art of argumentation brought back by a certain group. Now this is 'Philo-rubbish' (a play on the word Philosopher). It is not philosopher. It is 'Philo-rubbish'. How are you going to understand things through this philosopher? So, the part where you claim to have reformed it to this level has become this corrupted. Do you know why I can say this? I can say this because I am uneducated. If you are educated, you cannot speak such nonsense. This is 'nonsense', in a way, isn't it? What a deception it is on his part to say it is 'philo-rubbish' in front of you educated people? So what we need to understand is that we, who study this philosopher, did not see what our predecessors had done. We hold the rest of the history spoken by many great men who came to honor that course of action. But today, we are unable to comprehend any of this. That is why I said, if we wander behind those historians, we will get nothing. Through history, you will not get a single word about the path where your contemporary human beings acted and failed, or where our ancestors failed entirely. Then what should be done? People who are your contemporaries must see, know, speak, and practice this, and guide you onto that path. There is no other justification for this. If anyone claims there is, it is contrary to the truth. It will not happen. Because, all the knowledge we have discovered so far has been taught to us by the sorcerers among us, to fight and win against foreigners, or to hand it over to them, take money, and make a living for ourselves. Who else is there? In this history that has been cast so far away, what will you pass into the hands of your children who stand ready to ask, know, learn, and teach who each of us is? If they have the right to it, you must preserve it. It cannot be otherwise. So what you need to understand from this is this: Look at whether the great men born in the history after the beginning of this Kali Yuga experienced happiness or sorrow. Gandhiji is a great man who was born as a mere materialist. Gandhiji had no spiritualism. You should not assume otherwise. Why? The Congress people portray him here as a spiritualist, do they not?

[GV4:P1299] Guru: If it is not working out, why carry it around? If it has no value, can't it just be discarded? Now we can say that it is good. What would be said in those days? We have come a bit further along. Nothing of the sort has happened. We did not study that history either. We could not grasp the magnitude of that sacrifice. We could not walk in the path of dharma either. Nor did we attain the aspect of virtue. This is the truth. Other than that, there is no point in us studying a lot of these things and reciting them somewhere. That is not why I am asking you. The question is whether or not we have fallen into the miserable state of being unable to give contemporaneity to such self-sacrificing people who embraced that way of life, and cultivate it within ourselves.

[GV4:P1300] Disciple: The spiritual aspect has not been imparted.

[GV4:P1301] Guru: There is no spiritual aspect at all. Let that be. We say that Mahatmaji is a person with a spiritual side. Let that remain so. You do not need to take that up anymore. Why? [GV4:P1302] Someone created the Vamana avatar in order to impart goodness to our country. We have been taught that Vamana trampled down an individual who was ruling righteously. We have not been able to grasp this contemporary history. For what purpose did they conduct all this? So, remaining ignorant of any such matters, how can we pluck out the misfortune and falsehood that have crept into the actions we have praised and glorified to this day? If our children were to stand straight and ask us directly, what answer would we give them? This is our greatest misfortune. Let that be. How should we view it then? For that, you must look at Sree Krishna as a historian or a Yogeeshwara. What is a Yogeeshwara? How long will the karma we are going to undertake with this love endure, where will it lead, and how can we successfully execute it? We must be capable of learning at least one such karma. That is possible only through a renouncer (thyagi). What kind of renouncer should that be? It is for this reason that we must view Mahatmaji as a renouncer. He did not come preaching something entirely different. That man is a true renouncer. How were we able to comprehend the richness of sacrifice he demonstrated before us, enduring the kicks of soldiers without making a grand spectacle of it? How did we learn it? This is what we must understand. So, what were we able to learn from Mahatmaji's love? We failed to adopt that steadfastness in detachment. To demonstrate that this has become completely chaotic, what do the administrators of today, who grew up through that lineage, or those who rule, or those who invoke his name, show us today? They showcase Mahatmaji's sacrifice. How can we possibly adopt this sacrifice? It is entirely impossible. If anyone has demonstrated the true nature of sacrifice in our contemporary history, we have not been able to grasp it. Furthermore, if we study the history of exhausted and weary individuals who argued rationally or spoke of salvation, claiming all this is non-existent or false, how far will we progress? These are the two aspects we have retrieved today. Therefore, we cannot accomplish this. Do not speak of salvation, nor utter any form of nonsense. It will not work. We cannot become worthy of worship without at least understanding the dharma we must seek out in our lifetime, the sacrifice inherent in that karma, the hardship within that sacrifice, and how we must endure that hardship. This is history. The one who walks around today with a label hung in front of him, claiming to be the greatest renouncer, has achieved nothing in the position of that duty. What could possibly be achieved? We have failed to adopt anything demonstrated by our ancestors in all such respects. Consequently, whatever karmic paths we have utilized to study about renouncers have all become entirely corrupted. This state is far below our dignity. Today, after arriving at a point of singing, speaking, playing, and making us play, they have a song proclaiming that God is love. "Who knew the love of God, O mind."

[GV4:P1303] Who truly knew? As this question continually intensified, what is the current state? Now it is all about foot-tapping, beating, and jumping around. Is this not how it escalated? If it is claimed that God will manifest from this, what are we supposed to do? Therefore, no evidence is required to prove that we are flawed in every conceivable way. We ourselves stand as the ultimate proof for all of this. I am sharing with you something from my understanding regarding what we must do if we desire a transformation from this state. It is my personal knowledge. I am uncertain how you will interpret it. What is understood is this. That is, within the karma that articulates Sree Krishna's sense of dharma, visibly manifest the dharma referred to as 'Pratyakshavaham Dharmam' (visibly manifest dharma). This is its true dharma. This does not refer to the 'visible' that we perceive today. What have we currently comprehended by the term 'visible' (Pratyaksham)? All these poets, or the scholars of today, term it 'Pratyakshavadam' (empiricism/positivism). Consequently, what do they call the rising of the sun? In contemporary literature, it is described as "bursting forth," is it not? A sun that bursts and falls in such a manner is what we have here. So, if these charlatans could successfully impose all this upon your ignorance, where do you currently stand? Where is it bursting and blossoming? It is not bursting or anything of the sort.
