GURUVANIKAL · April 1996 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P1009 to GV2:P1019
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

April 1996

To be able to see in that form the realms where our ancestors were stuck in the course of life. Here our children are able to see that. That is the directly perceivable dharma (Pratyakshavaham Dharmam). There are no other justifications for it. There was a dangerous reason in what Krishna realized and told Arjuna. That reason is revealed through Krishna's life itself. Krishna falls down, struck by a hunter's arrow. Having heard, known, and studied the part where Krishna says that there is no saving him anymore and asks to take the women and save them, why can't you grasp the truth of those words? What have you understood when you explain these causes and effects? That is, you are unable to grasp as much as you see. Then what should be done?

To us, things are experienced here as directly perceivable dharma in the waking, dream, and deep sleep states. This directly perceivable dharma should not be just like telling a story. If waking, dream, deep sleep, and Turiya have come into your experience, there are some people you see during those times of experience. Who is that? Those are the people in your house, not the people in my house. But the people in your house and your land are the people in my house too.

This closeness is what you have with me. Only this bond can bring you close to me. And this is exactly what you get as a reward for coming close to me. This is how you should study what Krishna is and what Krishna did.

You should grasp what it is that you see manifested before you. That is, what is Shiva, what is Brahma, what is Krishna, what is God? Through what is seen manifested, you must understand well what this is. Otherwise, there is no meaning in saying that you saw Shiva, saw Krishna, etc. This vision (the experience of seeing it manifested) must be brought into the path of action of what these people, who speak of the past state, did. We are not realizing a dharma that settles things through action after putting an end to pretenses. We should not practice such a dharma. God has given us the opportunity to do it completely truthfully.

How do these children perceive it directly here? The great ones who came and went as our ancestors are coming before them. They come and tell themselves what the historical truth about them is. To know if what they say is true, you must tell the Guru. That is how their history can be known as truth. Knowing this in this state, you are not sannyasins, but householders. It is your children who are shown this. There is an action here that can show it like this. These children who see like this are many in this lineage spread across this entire country. To know and grasp this, how should you, who have known this, approach the Guru?

Gold must be rubbed on a touchstone. Grinding it on a normal stone will not reveal its purity. Without shedding the ignorance of grinding gold on a grinding stone, can you know your own purity? There are many in this lineage who know how to test purity like this. What they say is the truth. They may not be able to recount history. But they must know how to speak the truth. Those who do not know should learn through those who do. Having known it, you should desire to attain it through your effort and your love. There is much to say about this. But it shouldn't be said just like that. This manifestation does not discriminate between male and female. It does not discriminate whether one is a drummer, an elephant mahout, or a rogue. Whoever they are, if they are children of God, they will receive it. So, what should one do to become children of God? You must think about that. You should not abandon history or truth. Waking state (Jagrat), dream state (Swapna), deep sleep (Sushupti) - first, it must be seen like this. Just as it is seen in the waking state, it must be seen in the dream and deep sleep states. Similarly, after seeing it in the Turiya state, one must journey to the Turiyatita state. You have faced defeat in the course of that karma's journey. Now you must think about what to do to reclaim this fortune. Realizing this and acting upon it, your resolve should be to strengthen the consciousness of truth, no matter what we lose. That is your karma. That is the truth that must be elevated within you. Prepare for this truth. This is all I have to say to you. If Jesus could be persecuted like this, there will be people to persecute you in any way. Those who run away saying, "I cannot die," are not worthy of taking up this fortune.

So, you must attain the subtle body of the ancestors who have passed away. From where and from which realm does this subtle body control you, what is the shape of that realm, what all happens there, and how will you be taken to the story happening there? The one from whom you receive these answers is the Guru. If you search where you ought to search, you can find yourself and your causes. You must instill this inquiring mind in your children and make them everything for the nation. You should not make them belong only to your home. If they are made to belong only to the home, when everything earned is eaten up, they too will die. They and you will all die. In the end, as the master said, you shouldn't ask for anything other than a single thread, a rosary, and an upper ear ring. In this state, we need to move completely towards the side of knowledge. This is your need today. To fulfill this need, losing your properties, house, or other things is not a loss. This cannot be brought about through money. It means that we cannot travel on this path holding onto the pride of money. Money is needed; I am not saying you do not need money. Do not use this money to act and show off the arrogance of a non-story that writes and completes stories to show that there is no God just because you have money. The rich, the beggar, and the poor must have this knowledge. Being poor does not mean being a great sinner. He is one who has swallowed sin like a bark. Even a local person walks around to reform the poor. That will not happen. Because, you do not know who these poor people are and what to do with them. If you dress up these poor people and make them speak ill of you, a situation will arise where he becomes a great sinner and walks around extending his hand before you. You should not go into that karma. That is the path to your destruction. The poor should not be reformed in this style of yours. Let him have the knowledge to buy and eat. What and how does he buy and eat? If someone sees the one you teach and send out, anyone should feel like giving him what he needs. Having acquired the means to eat and dress, wealth, capability, respect, and humility through you, children who come to this nation to implement the dharma of the nation must come creatively through your hands.

Guruvani
