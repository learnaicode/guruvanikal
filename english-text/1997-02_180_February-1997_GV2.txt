Guruvani February 1997 When we say love of God, we must imagine a form for God in our mind. Because God is indeed a form. Otherwise, if we say God is omnipresent and give some other meanings, we will never understand God. Haven't we been worshipping the form itself for the past two yugas? When that familiarity exists, will you be convinced if it is said that God has no form? Moreover, it will be difficult to grasp.

Form does exist. Just as we see the form of a child or an adult when we take a photo of them, everything in this nature has a name.

That is not our subject. The form must be severed. That is, even if we reach the light of the past times, the subject is how it should be severed. From the time the past yugas (Treta and Dvapara yugas) began, there has been form meditation up to this day. There is also formless meditation. But we can

It is difficult to be convinced. But it is right next to us. We cannot understand it clearly unless we understand this formless form.

How should we understand when we say formless form? We say that God is formless. It is true that it can be seen as formless. If it can be seen, it means it has a form. That is, even an atom has a form, and what is seen in atomic form is also a form. But it has no specific form. But when it is seen in everything, it can be found in its color and qualities. The Guru is the one who recognizes these differences in color and quality. This is what we do not understand.

Placing an idol, placing a lamp, or even lighting an incense stick is done after thinking of something in the mind. This form will also keep changing according to the concept of the thinking mind. That is, the form will keep changing just as the concept of the mind is. But these forms can only be recognized by vision. How can we make the form known by such vision our own? Or how will it become ours? This is the subject. So, whether it is a form or formless, if we move forward believing that it is us, we will not see the form later. All this is one. We do not see anything else separately. For example, suppose we take a photo and see that form as large. But if you look at the size of this picture, it is only enough to be contained in a piece of that form. Then it has a complete form. It has a specific form. Seeing it suddenly like this, when that perspective also runs into it, that form is not seen as large or small. Then the focus is on a journey beyond this. Then everything becomes ours. It should be seen like that - everything as ours.

When we say that everything should be seen as ours, it is not like saying 'our property' by talking about the universe, it is exactly what we have seen. Everything we have seen is ours. But we see many things. From dawn, what all falls in our sight! Will all that become ours? It is not only ours, it belongs to many people. Or it belongs to many natures. How can we take back each of these things that belong to many individuals and are in many natures? A knowledge is imparted for that taking back. That action is what needs to be known. It is formless; at the same time, it has a form.

Whether it is formless or has a form, it is seen by giving it a form, just like seeing a picture of an atom. Similarly, even the absolute can be shown in a picture. Suppose a picture is taken of the solar sphere or the other nine spheres. But we cannot know what is contained in all these pictures. Similarly, we cannot know what is contained in each individual we see, where they have reached - we cannot know this either.

The thing that has been achieved at this end is something that has been implemented since the world was created. Do not say it does not exist, it does exist. But when we bring it up by giving it a form and divinity, because we cannot determine its measure, we give it a meaning in an undeserved extent and we become nothing. For example, can the person who saw Krishna make a mistake? Did they see Krishna as big as Krishna's size, or did they see Krishna as big as the absolute of the person who saw?

What we need to understand here is that every object has an absolute. Every atom has an absolute. In whatever way there is an absolute, there is also an end in that way. Thus the absolute of the earth is the absolute of the solar system known to us. The presence of a Guru is essential to understand in this way. What kind of Guru? Each subject and its completeness and specialty - thus recognizing the completeness, specialty, quality, color, and experience of everything, and recognizing the special quality of Om, that is, the long form of the letter consciousness of Omkara!

By writing 'Om', you see the Omkara. You also see Omkara as the embodiment of consciousness in your vision. You see that form into which the sound of Omkara merges. You attribute the distinction of a complete form to Om. Even when it is a formless sound, it has a form. You also see this complete form of Omkara contained within something that is completely formed. Don't you see that action, which makes many things that are complete forms into complete forms and sustains them, also as a form? Therefore, even though God is formless, He has a form. This is how it should be seen.

Seeing everything in the position of a Guru, and seeing it contained within that Guru, the Guru's perspective should be taken as the author of the Gita says, the Guru is the embodiment of all Dharma. What is this embodiment of Dharma? One should start seeing it from the cause of origin. It is not enough to have an origin revolving only around one earth. It is not enough to state the cause of this solar system, speak of Vedanta, and add wholeness. As Vedanta says, gross, subtle, cause, and effect come, and in the part that says cause and effect, what is in the subtlety? Increasing such subtlety, reaching from the atom to the whole in the nature of light, thus one can see the action in the Dharma called gross, subtle, cause, and effect. That is what we should call karma. How will that karma and the doer unite? Keep a medium, be it a Guru or something else. Realize the Guru as all these coming together in that medium. This is the vision!

March 1997

If you look at human history, everyone passes away confined within a form. The form of a place, the form of an ashram, thus everything is a form itself. Our subject is how this form will end. Triputi

It is said that when the end comes, the light of one's true nature (Swaroopa Deepam) will shine. That means, when the actions (karma) that exist as the triad (Thripudi) end, the light that is the true nature of the soul should shine at the end of life. When we say Thripudi, our understanding is Brahma, Vishnu, and Maheswara. The reality is not like that understanding. There are many types of Thripudi. The light that shines when Thripudi ends is also of many types. That can only be determined from experience.

It is often said that there is a witness deity (Sakshimoorthi) who is formless-form (Aroopaswaroopan) or of the form of Omkara. It is said in connection with Krishna and Rama as well. It is said in connection with all gods and goddesses. Om Tat Savitur Varenyam Bhargo Devasya Dheemahi.......... Here too, the form is added. Some people will explain the meaning of Om Swaroopa and dismiss it. What is needed is not to explain the meaning and dismiss it. How can one explain the meaning and dismiss the formless-form? It was seen that even the Gayatri mantra has a form. Then for what is the meaning explained and the form made non-existent? Therefore, such an imaginary commentary is not what is needed. When devotion grows, when the form grows through devotion, thousands of such states will be reached. In many places during these changes of states, deviations will occur. Someone standing next to me, appearing in my form, tells me to apply Dhanwantharam Kuzhambu on my head, applies it, and moves away. What can be said about this? The belief that 'it was the Guru who said it' is the most necessary thing. But what if one cannot recognize the Guru's words? It will take a long time to recognize it with perfect examples like that. It cannot be recognized by small methods.

One must know, one by one, crossing the changes of states, how this Guru has reached all moving and non-moving things, how he has reached all forms. Then the disciple himself sees a witness deity changing into a formless-form. That is, seeing it as a formless-form, seeing the form in the formless. But it also has a form. Like a mirage - like an illusion. In short, whatever objects exist as formless-forms, the Guru is in all of them! This is the concept of the Guru.

You have a flaw. A tendency to easily dismiss things saying, I have known all this, I have already known it. This complete concept of the Guru is not something you have known, taken, and thrown away. The variations of the Guru's form have changed billions of times. If one sees something that stands beyond time and space according to human capability, and dismisses it saying, Oh, this is not like what was seen in the old days, what can be done? This understanding is due to a lack of knowledge. This lack of knowledge exists in all subjects. Therefore, all knowledge must be acquired from the Guru. In billions of forms from the atom to the infinite, their respective knowledge is contained. How can this be known? It must be known through those who have experienced it. The knowledge seen and known in that way is what is here. From the smallest to the largest, whatever objects exist, whatever reality of form exists, see the Guru in all of them. The Guru seen in that way is the formless-form.

Then the Guru moves and stands as a form within one's own form. The Guru will be shown in the nature of whatever thoughts and imaginations one has. Whatever exists where names and forms merge and stand in the form of sound and point, all of them stand embraced in the Guru.

With us, there are men, women, big, small, father, mother, wife, husband, everything. But we have also given a place to each and kept them apart. Similarly, there is a different imagination for God as well. Everything that comes as the nature of the soul, the nature of the universe, or the goddess of the universe, comes through the Guru. Even the smallest people have experience here in this state. But everyone will not have that knowledge. There are those who do not get it even if they ask the Guru. How many people have cried lying down, saying it is enough if I get it one day. It will not be obtained like that. You must understand that too. For example, there was a person in Kadakkavoor. He used to struggle a lot to get this. He would wake up at 3 in the morning, bathe, and walk up to Mel Kadakkavoor to do poojas and other things. Those who do not struggle and desire in this way, those who do not struggle at all, they are getting it. What answer can be given to this? You should not think about whether someone gets it or not.

Whether you get it or not. Getting it also has a meaning, just like the relief you feel when applying Dhanwantharam Kuzhambu, as mentioned earlier. You can see it in all your worship deities. You will see someone; you will see it in someone. Everything must be given to you by making it formless and with form. It is the Guru who gives. Along with oneself, in one's own form, that is, exactly as one is, so is the Guru. It means the Guru matches one's imagination. The Guru will lift you out of all these imaginations. Moreover, only the Guru knows what you are.

Don't we say we had a dream? We are actually seeing and experiencing the dream as if it were real. But we only realize it after experiencing it. That it was a dream. Another thing is, after seeing the Guru, whatever appears and disappears in a formless state, all of that should become the Guru. We should be fortunate enough to see everything that appears and disappears in the name of the Guru. Bringing one to this fortune is called 'correction'. There are millions of such corrections. Therefore, it is necessary to call everyone and say it generally, and also to explain it individually to isolated people.

The experience of a thousand people is in a thousand ways. Why does God show it like that? Isn't it enough to show a truth in the same way? What is being shown to you is the repetitive power of a life that has come and gone in this world for so long, both formless and with form. That is why it is shown in different ways to different people. That change acquired in life is indescribable. This is how God's love is. God comes as formless and with form, and has transformed everything that sprouted, grew, and perished in this nature into the Guru. That is how everything in God's imagination has been transformed into the Guru. Therefore, the Guru must be present in everything that has come and gone in the universe through the ages. Otherwise, that Guru is not complete. That is why it is said that the seeker must get the Guru in many ways, both formless and with form. You must get this understanding firmly.

When it is said that God is love, we understand it because we have love within us. The form and nature of that power of love will keep changing. No matter how the form and nature of the power of love are changed, the Guru is beyond everything. Then it must be understood that whatever formless and formed natures God has, the Guru is the light of all of them.

What is meant by the repetition of the Guru? Suppose a person meditates in a cave for a long time and then dies. After that, in what form did that great soul come and be born? In what forms and natures did he come over the ages? This is repetition. Then one must know that the Guru is the lightness that recognizes each of these. It is this lightness that has entered as the core of bliss within our minds. It is out of our love that we give it many forms and call it. You cannot even imagine the magnitude of that action which we come to know one by one. It will not be enough whether you see it as one, a hundred, a thousand, a million, or in countless ways. This is where the Guru is needed. The Guru will point out that this is what it is. And then merge you into it. Thus, it will be merged into everything. Then only oneself and the Guru. This is how that lightness should grow. It is growing within our minds; as the bliss of life! Once bliss spreads into everything as love, then there is no form.
