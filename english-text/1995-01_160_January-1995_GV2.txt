GURUVANIKAL · January 1995 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0801 to GV2:P0819
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

January 1995

We only have an acquaintance without the actual knowledge of what Pournami Vrutham is. The sun, moon, and earth - these three spheres are associated with Pournami. The earth is meant for exhausting karma; one enters this field of action (karmabhoomika) through the dynamics of life that has evolved due to the power of the sun. You should be guided by a knowledge that tells you what to do and what not to do in this field of action. You should not let yourself be led into destructive actions by being impressed with mere fragments of Vedanta. I have a request that you should not alter these words of truth which are spoken for your understanding.

Such a prayer is being conducted here according to the instructions. While performing this prayer, you must remember one thing: during this prayer time, which clears the mistakes of many ages, if even a small scratch of ignorance enters within, it will spread. It will spread in many ways. We are praying to rectify the defects that have occurred in all the actions of householders, as well as in all the actions of sannyasins and brahmacharis. What we are doing is not something that matches the capacity of our minds. Therefore, some mistakes and ignorance might occur. There is nothing else to say other than it is God's will that makes us do something so profoundly special that no one can even describe. You should always pray that may God grant this Guru and the Guru lineage, given to us by God, the opportunity to remove the shortcomings not only of myself and my ancestors accumulated over time, but of the entire global population, and impart goodness of double that measure.

The children you raise and nurture, and the children who have taken sannyasa and stand here, are equally participants in this noble karma. You must also think about what should be done so that a path of fulfillment emerges through the karma you all do together. Not only through the sannyasis and their lineage, but wherever shortcomings have occurred in any part of this world, all of them should be destroyed, and in their place, goodness according to the divine will must come through the guru lineage, you should always think while doing any work. When studying children do not get knowledge in their minds, they should pray like this, not only the children of this lineage, but wherever there are children who have gone astray, you should pray for goodness to come to all of them in this way. This is the word God has given us. Boys and girls should be given special attention individually and prayed for. The karma coming from me, a woman, must correct the mistakes of the woman in this world, and the man. This should be your qualification. This qualification of man and woman is advancing together. At that time, our minds should not become bad, nor should we fall into situations that make our minds bad. Especially the men and women growing up here are the ones who have to judge the world. We must become great through this sense of dharma. Standing in this dharma, if there are any other traditional solar systems, our constant prayer is that all the mistakes that have come through them so far be resolved, and that we be given the knowledge, experience, and habit to do what we need when we need it. We got that knowledge, and when God tells us and makes us do it, we must be ready to do it. Other than this, thoughts like rubbish lying on the road should not be allowed to enter the mind.

Question: Guru! When natural disasters occur, what is the remedy or solution to remove them?

Guru: As a result of natural disasters, losses have been occurring for a long time. This will repeat again. Changing the nature of prayer and the nature of worship is the only solution for this. Not only in India, but anywhere on the face of the earth, times are coming when anything can happen. Among the notifications we get, there is one thing that has been received many times. The existence of the government will cease to exist, and the dangers that have occurred at the administrative level will seem very trivial compared to the dangers that are yet to come. We also prayed for about four years to avoid this tragedy. When meteorologists announce that there will be a cyclone, we pray. Then it will be effective. We prayed when there were difficulties in Mexico, which is called the underworld on the other side of the earth, and when there were widespread beatings in Islamic countries. When Indira Gandhi was in danger, a letter was written to her indicating it.* Notices were printed and distributed here. She lived only for two weeks after saying this. When her danger was being received here through vision, those who stood listening misunderstood it in another way and wrongly informed the Manorama office. That Indira Gandhi will not die.

* Letter to India

It was written in the same way to the Indian Express office as well. It also happened in such a way that those who heard this news felt contempt for the ashram. Not everyone, but some had it. Later, the journalists themselves said that it came because of the mistake of hearing and knowing this.

The earthquake in Kalloor was very terrifying. Later, it occurred in a minor way at various times. Even then, we had printed notices and advertised it. Many people in the Kalloor ashram had also prayed. That fear of the earthquake then slowly subsided.

Question: Guru, are you saying that natural disasters are caused by man's misguided forms of worship and various kinds of evil deeds?

Guru: Yes, if we say that, we have to look back a very long time. When talking about periods, we should at least talk from the time of Krishna. The shortcomings of the course of action that have come to our knowledge since that time, only then will we understand at least a little.

Question: Could you explain a little what you said about natural disasters occurring continuously due to misguided and untimely lifestyles?

Guru: If we start explaining it like that, we will have to go beyond the calculation of the Manvantara. When we say beyond the calculation of the Manvantara, we do not mean only the past Manvantara. What is currently in existence is the twenty-eighth Chaturyuga of the seventh Manvantara. Until the third Chaturyuga of this Manvantara, dharma flourished without any highs and lows. After that, Satyathranan of the Manu lineage, who came in accordance with the times, looked at the past three Chaturyugas. Then only the tradition of Manu is seen, nothing else. Wherever he looked and however he looked, how it came in the five senses, how it came in the five koshas, its course of action that took place as Manvantaras and what is currently in existence, that is all that is seen. As each person attains each state, there are many levels of attaining immortality in the divine nature after crossing the demonic, but the truth is that it was seen that the mind system was working in all this. The six Manvantaras that took place according to the Brahma's imagination and the three Chaturyugas in this Manvantara took place truthfully. Along with seeing all this, this sage is also seeing the history of many visionary gurus in his guru lineage. All that has come in accordance with the Yuga dharma. There is a calculation of so many gurus for each Chaturyuga, and everything has been brought according to that calculation. This sage sees all this as coming and joining him. The reason this sage sees it this way is that he somehow missed thinking about the change in Yuga dharmas. Along with that, the four sentences starting with Aham Brahmasmi that he said are still the upadesha mantra given to sannyasins after their initiation name.

Guruvani
