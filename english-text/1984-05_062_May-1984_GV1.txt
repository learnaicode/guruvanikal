[GV1:P1397] May 1984

[GV1:P1398] Today, we hold nothing that aligns with the truth. What stands now is a combination of other gimmicks. We ourselves have evidence here that God had a great desire to change this. There were great men and ascetics here who did not employ any such gimmicks. They are the ones who imparted this kind of knowledge to us.

[GV1:P1399] The lore of 'Parayi Petta Panthirukulam' (the twelve lineages born to the Pariah woman) belongs to that category. Except for demonstrating matters concerning certain deities, they did not exhibit any of these gimmicks. They have stated that this system of worship itself is not like this. A proof for that is the idol consecration at the Cherthala Karthyayani Temple. What is known as the Cherthala Pooram takes place there.

[GV1:P1400] All the women who resided in the Buddhist *viharas* were beaten, brought to a place called Kottappuram near Cherai, verbally abused, hacked to death, and thrown into the sea; subsequently, a temple was built there. That is the Kodungallur Bhagavathy Temple. Instead of hacking them to death as was done then, today, goats and fowls are sacrificed. They continue to do this while hurling abuses. It is women who are worshipped there. You can witness all of this if you visit the place. There are several such Devi temples scattered around.

[GV1:P1401] Seeing this and coming down here to South Travancore, we have the 'Mudippura'. Mudippura means - there is no body, only the head, face, and earrings can be seen. They tie it up, plant an arecanut tree trunk in the middle of a paddy field, and tie it to that. Then, when the festival is over, they dismantle it and take it away. This represents the head of a demon. Haven't you seen the figure of Bhadrakali drawn—with several heads of adults and children? It means all of them were chopped off. She stands there holding them hanging in both hands with a sword. She has hands and legs as needed. One hand to hold the sword, one to support the head while cutting it, another to collect the blood, separate hands to hold other weapons, and then separate hands for playing, bathing, and eating. Thus, there are numerous hands. We would be terrified if we look at each of her nails. Their concept is that she will tear things apart with these nails. These are the concepts given to us to worship. What will those who worship this become? If our method of worship takes this path, it is no wonder we perish. It is because our knowledge has perished in this way that we do not hesitate to sacrifice even our own children. You might have read in the newspapers that such sacrifices were made even recently. So, when such things are happening among us, how far do we have to go to progress culturally!

[GV1:P1402] The governance here fell into the hands of the Shudra who had degraded to that level. That governance also degraded the people entirely. Because one group can acquire education, another group cannot; one group can stand close, another group is not allowed to; one group must stand eight *kathams* away, another group can stand one *katham* away. They have contrived to write all this down as *Rajadharma* (royal duty). We must reflect on what all things the 'Ponnuthampran' (sovereign) here has ruled upon in this manner. Then, amidst this, there are the 'Kudiyanavanmar' (feudal lords). They are capable of doing anything. They are the ones ruling the land. When we look at all this, this culture among us has strayed very far away. Then, it is because foreigners like the British or the Arabs came here and made them do things that we achieved the kind of freedom we see here today. Thus, although we lost one *dharma*, we gained another freedom. We got the freedom to walk on the roads, to let others walk, and to get an education. That is why this knowledge could reach India today; otherwise, it would not have.

[GV1:P1403] They give the depressed classes a good name, 'Harijan', but what is done here is gouging out their eyes and dragging them into the fire to burn. He is only ordained to toil for the one who torments him. He has no right to do anything else. If this is what is happening here even in the present situation, how much the people of those times must have suffered! Today, it is claimed that freedom was obtained by these very same groups, yet what we see are the machinations done to deny them freedom. This is what is happening today. We have great freedom to beat people to death. Such great freedom is kept close at hand. This is what is happening today even after we got freedom. Thus, such a subjugated system of governance still exists among us. The things that have befallen us today as a result of such atrocities spreading everywhere throughout the country are called a part of *Kali* (the Kali Yuga). So, what is the special characteristic of *Kali*—it is the time when we all should get freedom. *Kali* has such a merit. That is, not obeying any of these rules. Then poets will emerge, they will write for freedom, they will repeatedly bring about victory in accordance with the times, and that victory will lead to human freedom.

[GV1:P1404] Here itself, so many poets have written with a thirst for freedom. Asan (Kumaran Asan) has also written, urging to change the rules. So, if they say that if you don't change such rules, you will be beaten and destroyed, it will happen just like that. Even though we had an idea to conduct ourselves in a good manner like this, there was no governance here to implement it. We suffer this disability because of that problem. This situation is changing—if not changed, it will come. It will be changed. It will certainly come to be so, without a doubt. But the reason for saying all this in the midst of this is that a section of the people who suffered like this - a society that committed all sorts of atrocities to degrade and destroy humans was prevalent here. As a result of it being prevalent, it became impossible for us even to live here.

[GV1:P1405] Guruvani
