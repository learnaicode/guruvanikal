GURUVANIKAL · June 1991 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0072 to GV2:P0091
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

June 1991

There is no one who does not wish to be good. If one more is added to the nine poisons (Navapashanams), it becomes gold. If something else is added to gold, it becomes pure gold. Similarly, life should become pure gold. What is your role in this process? Each of you is enthusiastically rushing into various actions. Everyone desires to become good. But in which path should one become good? If I become good in that path, what will I become? What exactly am I? You jump into actions without thinking about any of this.

To accomplish anything, one needs the appropriate understanding, knowledge, experience, and practice. Our land, our home, and the actions of our guardians whom we follow, are all factors that should be useful in our actions. In that same path, our children and their children, who are to prepare for tomorrow, must also become enlightened. Every individual among you must ask yourselves - what have I done for my home, my country, and if needed, for the world? Even if they wish to do something, some will act against their home. Some will act against their country. Some will act against everything. However, there are a rare few who do good for everything. The action of such people is the action that aligns with all good actions.

First, let our home itself be fine. What is the obstacle to fixing it? No one wishes for their home to be bad. Does anyone wish for their wife to be bad or children to be bad? However, there might be at least one in a thousand who wishes so. 'If only my wife would die!' 'If only my husband would die!' There are also those who think, 'Even after I did all this, my son turned out like this, it would have been better if such a person did not exist.' At the same time, there are those who wish, 'I have nine children, if I could just give them one proper meal for at least one day before I die.' We are people who see everything creatively, aren't we? What is your creative perspective on these differing mindsets mentioned?

Today, there exists a situation where such perspectives cannot be corrected. What exactly is the thinking habit of the people? Some reach a state of downfall. Some reach the top layer like a high-level book and stand in a state of not knowing where to go. Is there any possibility for higher study evident in such a book? There is a possibility. The possibilities in this must be thought out. When our daughter is a child, why did she become my child? Why did I get such luck? Why did I get such a husband? Why am I acting out all these roles in this land? What is the purpose of all this? Why was I born? Why did I grow up? Did I come to show all this and cause harm? Did I come to accumulate wealth? Or did I come to teach mischief to all the people? We need a habit of asking ourselves each of these questions. That habit is our...

It should be passed on to the children. In that place, what is the action our ancestors have done to us and to the tradition that has grown like us?

This habit of asking oneself applies to everyone. This applies to everyone without distinction of educated or uneducated, scholar or layman. You are householders. If householders do not live as they truly desire, it is useless to think that its sinful or virtuous power will not come down through generations. The only difference is that the course of action each person has to take is different for each person.

No one is bound by death. We are in the action of intellect and education. We are in a hurry to seize power through a life based on the English model. This eagerness extends from lawyers to judges and beyond to the Supreme Court. The common man does not have such a great inclination. A group of people who work and make others work day by day for a meal, another group who exploits all these hardships, a minority who wants to do at least a little good; if there are people who do good, or if they want to do it, there are others who are ready to destroy them. What is the reason for this world to be so diverse?

This thought should start from the grass. Even an atom is capable of action, so everything is contained in it. If you study atomic matter and make an atom work, you know what all can be done. If we have this education, we do not need to seek the knowledge of others to know what the course of action is. Whose course of action should be redirected according to the nature of the thinking habit.

Disciple: Actually it should be from the parents.

Guru: What if the parents do not have such luck? Or the children do not have the luck to earn it. Even if they had, what is the condition to take it with correction?

Disciple: Good association.

Guru: What is a condition with good association in this manner? All the conditions available today are like a dense scene pulled together. Have we inquired what is the way to distinguish a condition from this, accept it, act on it, bring it closer to Dharma, and bring it from Dharma to the path of virtue? Our goal is good indeed. But it does not reach the action. What should we accept for this? What is the action each person has done so far? What is needed for tomorrow? What was the Dharma of the ancestors who passed away yesterday? Whether they portrayed it wrongly or rightly, should we or someone else have the stubbornness and vengeance on how to take it back?

What kind of characters are we? How will we understand such things? When scientists studied everything and grew up, how have the situations become? If you sit in Italy or Germany and press a button, the whole globe can be destroyed. They are trying and propagating to grow and develop science more and more. How should we take it? Or how many people can take it? In the action of our ordinary life, we stand in an action that we cannot overcome no matter how much we struggle. How should we change from that action? Without virtuous parts with Dharma, how will you survive with the action that each of you thinks out?

Disciple: Think and shape the right action. Stand firm in that action and move forward. It is impossible to say how it will come.

Guru: That action is beyond thought. How should we think about that action? It is not done using anyone's intellect. The knot of knowledge through the application of intellect has entered that action. It is difficult to untie that knot. We do not need any of this for that. No one wants to be bad on their own. Growing above everything that grows for our comfort, convenience, and for the good of the country,

Each of us must be the owners of actions that benefit the world and every living being. Parents should be the first to become like this. Children should also grow up knowing what their parents and their growth are, and whether they are capable of giving them what they need. A husband must know that his wife should be able to understand him, and a wife must know that her husband should be able to understand her.

Disciple:

It doesn't seem like such an easy thing.

Guru:

If you haven't understood this after living for so long, what will little children do? A little girl growing up in front of us is meant to become a wife, a mother, and a grandmother tomorrow. She is the one who has to manage a household in our land. We must know what to instill in her. This is not something she can acquire merely through association. It is only possible through life.
