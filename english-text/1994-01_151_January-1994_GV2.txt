January 1994

It is difficult to determine when Hinduism originated. Hinduism has imparted all rituals to us. The ritualist, the idea of the ritualist; the yogi, the idea of the yogi; the jnani, the idea of the jnani. Thus, there are no ideas that have not been imparted to us. But all these ideas were not handled properly with the required knowledge and experience. Which of these to accept, which to reject - you (householders) do not know this. Vedanta became everything to you. Vedic rituals gained the most respectable position. With the entry of Aryans, their women could perform Vedic rituals. Dravidians were kept away from it. After that, Brahmins introduced an addition - the sacred thread (Poonool). This sacred thread system came about five thousand years ago. Thus, a situation arose where it was not known what is right and what needs to be corrected.

Now, the word sannyasi evokes contempt. If something that was good for a long time becomes corrupt, it happens like that. Recently, many sannyasis came to Kerala - Narayana Guru, Chattampi Swami. They were despised by their contemporaries. But when they showed their spiritual powers, people had great

Significantly. Some will say it is an illusion. It is not an illusion. At times, the power will manifest itself. I have such an experience.

The time when we were preparing to build an ashram in Kallutti. Bharathammavan was sent there a month and a half ago. When there were fifteen days left, I also went there. When I went there, no work had been done there. There is no water. There is nothing in that area, water. Can water be brought from the river to the top of that hill? I felt a little sad. How will this be done. I just took a bucket and put it in the well. There is no water. I put the bucket and came back. After fifteen minutes, a child came running and said that water had come into the well. Sathyan came after that. After that Bharathammavan came and fell at my feet. In an hour, the well was full of water.

Now another incident. Bharathan and four others from Vakkom came to Varkala to see me. I am not there. The neighbors said that I had not been there for two days. When they were sitting on the rock feeling sad, they saw me coming up after taking a bath with soap and a towel.

This is not a power. It happens automatically when a stage is over. Sree Narayana Guru coming out of the anthill is a power. Now its time has passed a lot. It is not that ghosts, yakshas, gandharvas, and kinnaras do not exist. If these are properly practiced systematically for twelve years, power can be shown. But the life of the one who shows this power will stand under it. Near the old kings, sanyasis did some medicines and showed power. Therefore, the kings controlled their food. Gradually eating leaves, then reducing that too, it will reach a stage of eating even nux vomica seeds. After that, there is a gulakalpaseva with navapashanas. After consuming this, one will reach a stage where one can sit in any snow without water and food. Their body will glow at night. It is believed that this is divinity. If this is called divinity, what is the story. But, because of them, poor sanyasis have no salvation. But there is one thing, Narayana Guru and Chattampi Swami all died of illness. There is a Neelambaswami, a disciple of a Manikandaswami near Nilamel. He also shows some tricks. Pambuswami also shows power like this. None of this is divinity, it is a skill.

Yoga is a much higher system than all these. There are many skills called gaunamani, stambhanamani, etc. There are also swamis who appear by getting on the back of many people. None of this is sanyasa. What we need is whatever is needed for peace of mind. There are some things that God can give us. We will also have the same age as God. The child born yesterday will be older than us. You can see all this yourself. You can see in which animals this life has been born before. That is why Hindus believe in reincarnation.

The character of past lives has been described in the history of human beings. Women who believe here, who have no inclination, can see their past lives. It is not because they worked for it, but because of God's mercy. It has also been convinced how to be in the remaining life. That is part of God's mercy. Therefore, it is a blessing to be able to participate in the sankalpas and prayers going on here for your peace of mind and to be able to spend anything for it.

For some time now, the things told by God have been done at various times. In nineteen seventy-three (1973), the thing I desired for ages was fulfilled. It was informed from the light that I am happy from today. Following that, everything informed is being done. You know the happiness and sorrow experienced all this time. You also know that the announcements and the actions according to them are literally correct. There were many happinesses and many sorrows in it. But whenever there was any sorrow, even when there were big cases in its name, God protected without any dangers affecting this.

In Kallutti, a lotus with two thousand four hundred and forty-four (2444) petals was shown in a vision. All these petals fell off. All these petals represent the disciples. All these

It was informed that they are the ones who are meant to arrive. The meaning of this was understood through a message received after several years. These two thousand four hundred and forty-four (2444) people are gurus of the past. All these gurus will be born in this lineage. One of the many karmas we were made to do was for this purpose. God is granting us immense rewards by making us perform very small karmas. Along with these gurus, their millions of disciples are also yet to be born. All of them must be born before the end of this Kali Yuga. Because of what we have done for their birth, we hold the position of those gurus. And then, liberation. Another special provision is that the entire lineage (gotra) of those who create the tradition directly carrying this ideal will be purified. How many births would it take for you to complete this!

Only rarely in the solar system is a Guru born with the qualification to witness such karmas. That Guru will not have any unnatural reactions. However lowly a person who approaches him may be, he is with them; if an exalted person comes, he is with them; he will not even hate a scoundrel. Since multiplicity arose from oneness, nothing is forbidden to such a Guru.

God stands with compassion to impart this glory to the householders. You must be prepared to do this with capability and deep sincerity. Whatever we do, it must be in a way that causes no pain to anyone. One must do anything to protect one's own life - if you realize you cannot escape, you must kill. Krishna himself has done this. If someone attempts to kill us, we must carefully evade them. But if they are adamant about killing, we must have the inner strength to strike back.

Everything should be in moderation. Your interactions with anyone should be moderate. You must always maintain the awareness and vigilance that you are performing these glorious karmas. There must be a firm resolve to behave at home only with a sense of unity.

Guruvani
