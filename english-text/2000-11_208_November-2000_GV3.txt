[GV3:P1063] November 2000

[GV3:P1064] We do nothing here except what God has ordained. What do you see as the greatest goodness here? The nature of the mind. The action of the mind is the greatest action. It is to not do wrongly, that is, as a betrayal, the things we know. All other mistakes are human nature. God placed human-natured mistakes. God Himself has to tolerate those mistakes. We have nothing to do there. Why did God place this? Why consider this a mistake? God placed this mistake to complete the action of creation, sustenance, and destruction. Only humans can complete that action. Animals cannot. Humans were given discretion. Within discretion, dharma was given. From dharma came merit, and from merit came fortune. The soul embedded in dharma within fortune will pave the way for liberation. It will take a lot of time to pass this knowledge on to children.

[GV3:P1065] People assume that if they marry off a child in the house, they are saved. Parents say their suffocation has ended. In reality, that is the beginning of the suffocation. It is after that the fights start between the elder and the younger, the big and the small. Their children will never get along. There is a great danger in South Travancore. It is here that this alliance has happened the most. The filth combining all these sensible and senseless alliances has stuck to our souls. It is this soul that comes back as children and grandchildren. What more is needed for a never-ending trouble? We do not know any dharma. We must understand this and feel the pain. What do we say when we talk about dharma? We say, 'Is all this possible for humans, let it go.' If it just goes like that, where will this action be discarded? Therefore, we must have the determination that we can and must do this. Then we will be able to do this. Anyway, it is very difficult to raise these girls as children. While raising them, if we do not support their whims, they will scold us. I am speaking from the experience of seeing thousands of children. Cutting, stabbing, fighting, and stubbornness are the familiar traits of knowledge among us. We must understand this. This is an experience concerning everyone. There is no point in blaming each other for this. First, it is the mother and father who must understand this. Then they must tell their children with a pained heart. Without this, it cannot be corrected by sheer force. We never received the knowledge from anywhere that a father and mother should be like this. Without any of this knowledge, one simply grew up and became a father, a mother, a husband, a wife. We ourselves have made this animal-like life. Animals do not have the discretion to know all this. They do not need to know either. But what all does this human-animal do knowingly! We must understand this at least a little. Otherwise, just sitting at home and living as one pleases is not enough.

[GV3:P1066] We do not have a home that is useful in that manner. This is the problem of human society. We think our home is good. It is not so; every house has this kind of problem. We have a way to escape from this. That is the Guru's path. In the Guru's path, all troubles will end. But we must grow in the spirit of dedication. When a life ends after fulfilling duties, we usually console ourselves saying everything is over. In reality, it is not so; their mischief has not ended. We must understand that life's struggles are left behind in the soul itself as one departs, not with everything resolved. We get an understanding regarding these mischiefs and struggles from the Guru's path.

[GV3:P1067] Whatever we do, whether good or bad, must be done by uniting it with the Guru. That is Guru-smarana (remembrance of the Guru). That is how one can be saved. Otherwise, life is not going to find any salvation. What will we gain by boasting of greatness while holding on to all the mistakes? Keeping these troubles, we try to explain things to others as if we have understood everything. But first, we must have the realization of who we are, how bad we are, and how good we are. Then we should tell the children that there is such a painful phase in life. Do not tell them that you are perfect. If you say so, neither you nor they will be able to be saved. How much have I told you! But even if you understand it, either you are unable to take it. Or when you take it, mistakes happen. I see such a karma in all of you. If everyone does not try to change that karma, a lot of difficulties will arise.

[GV3:P1068] When saying 'Ashram', you have only heard and assumed it to be a place with a Karmi, Jnani, Yogi, and the magical feats happening there. You have only this much understanding regarding the status of an ashram and deity worship. You don't even have the understanding that none of this has gone beyond a demonic state. We have been afflicted by the ignorance of making everything sound grand through the prowess of magical powers (siddhis), astounding the ignorant, and preaching Advaita completely to others. This is a truth given as God to bring a change to all this. If you do not intend to sanctify the life you have received by standing firm in that belief, I cannot do anything for you. If you act with your intellect and enter a dharma that gets ruined through your actions, no one will be able to correct you.

[GV3:P1069] If you pay attention, you will understand one thing. Whatever anyone has done here, that karma has returned and joined them. And then they were lost in some way. This has happened even to the children who were first selected for the sake of vision. All of them went wrong and ruined themselves, but they couldn't do any good for this cause. Following that, Guru Puja was established here as God.

[GV3:P1070] What is the knowledge of Guru Puja? This is not the system of gods or an isolated sleight of hand in the system of Siddhis. None of that belongs to God. If it is to belong to God, it should not be my sleight of hand or someone else's. This is a karma bestowed upon you for your salvation. From the experience of all this time, I have not been able to see this as anything separate from what is of God. This action is not opening the door to the theory that the material is different and the spiritual is different.

[GV3:P1071] The spiritual and the material are like two sides of a coin. It is the truth contained in all humans. Let those who want the truth take the truth. Let others take the illusion. The worldly way is like that. As far as we are concerned, we must take the truth itself. For that to be possible, both men and women must feel the pain for it.

[GV3:P1072] You yourself are seeing what the lifestyle that has come down through many ages is. Everyone, regardless of caste and religion differences, is seeing it. An example for this is Agastyankunju from Chandiroor. He is a Christian. He grew up playing with me. He has been seeing this for the last four years. He came and told me about seeing the life order of his many past births. A sight that a person cannot even imagine. How many such experiences have become evident here! What did you understand from this? This is not an action that can be shared by one caste or religion. Therefore, you should not ruin this by using your intellect.

[GV3:P1073] I have a sorrow. I have shared with you my hardships and experiences up to this moment. You are not showing sincerity worth that. You have not demonstrated that sincerity in action. You have not acted on your own and subdued yourself, nor have you made someone else join and act in this. Then what is your capacity to act? Some good must come to this world through you. This is my desire. This is the intention behind keeping householders here in this state and nurturing them. Worldly karma must be fulfilled through householders. When permission was received to perform Guru Puja, God informed this. You are being made to do the life karma of many ages that God had set aside. It has also been announced that you will understand it. If you believe, love, and respect this in that manner, you will be able to carry this forward well. Do not assume that this is being done for me. This is a karma given by God. Do not cause flaws in this. If you cause such flaws, the defect of that karma will not leave your soul. Do not raise the boys and girls growing up in front of you in your own ways. You, who have grown up with love for caste, religion, and class, should not raise children like that. It is an injustice to this state.

[GV3:P1074] If a word is received, if its timing is missed, that's it - doing it a second time will not make it right. How many experiences are there in front of you where things were lost by doing them at the wrong time! Do not assume that you can correct anyone by using intellect, deceit, and lies in karma. It will not happen. You must have a sense of karma and a sense of dharma matching that will. That sense of dharma must be turned into merit, and merit into fortune. It is that fortune you need. You should not behave here just to appease someone. If so, all the work you do will be in vain.

[GV3:P1075] Even if a Guru has millions of disciples, that dharma will not last. Because the karmic defect capable of destroying all goodness is hanging above your heads. What will you do if you use your own intellect and deceit while trying to change that karmic defect? We are journeying towards the Yuga of truth by changing the culture of the tribes you were born into.

[GV3:P1076] Satya Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dvapara Yuga. By acting completely well using Satya Yuga, the karma performed through Treta Yuga must be solidified. Just like the karmic path of origin. That karma must be solidified and completed when it reaches the end of Dvapara. This is creation, sustenance, and destruction.

[GV3:P1077] What is creation, what is sustenance? The demonic possession residing in the five elements of this world is being merged into the five senses. The senses transfer it into the five sheaths (panchakoshas). Annamaya kosha is what operates into all cells. That is nature. That means we are seeing this entire nature operating in the annamaya kosha. If we want to change from such a nature, the word of truth given by God for that is to keep the word. Keep the Guru's word. There is no need to think about or keep anything else in this.

[GV3:P1078] Keep the Guru's word as truth. When kept like that, an attribute of truth comes and joins within you. That attribute turns into you, and you become self-luminous (Swayam Jyothi). Setting aside all concepts of worship, it is affirmed:

[GV3:P1079] Antarjyothi Bahirjyothi

[GV3:P1080] Pratyagjyothi Paratpara

[GV3:P1081] Jyothirjyothi Swayam Jyothi

[GV3:P1082] Antarjyothi Shivosmyaham.

[GV3:P1083] The light that is inherent in me is the external light. This external light will become self-luminous. That self-light is the Supreme Being. The self-light has merged into the soul of the Supreme.

[GV3:P1084] Everyone is becoming this in this karma. Is it not ignorance to say that we cannot know, see, or hear this karmic ability that has poured into us? The karma of Santhigiri is to preserve as truth, through you, your children, and their lineage, this blessedness given to us as God by performing Guru Puja.

[GV3:P1085] Guruvani
