July 1994

There may be some shortcomings in those who come and go daily and those who stay permanently in the ashram. The reason for that is - when you get close, some subtle aspects may not be understood. People of many natures will come to show off by showing and doing many things. What if the people here stand like an oracle with a drawn sword in front of them? Those who handle more devotion and those who worship without knowledge will make such mistakes. Some ignorant people traditionally worship with the attitude that I must achieve this, otherwise I will throw you away. It is not his devotion, but his ignorance that is the fault. You cannot say that his (Ezhava's) devotion is not right. As far as Nairs are concerned, even if they make fun of worshipping God, they will not say it bluntly. Because they are accustomed to being humble.

Why do we come to the ashram? What should be done as householders? Even if those standing here are ignorant, those who come should respect them. Because isn't he working from your center of faith? If you give respect, he will do it a little more straight. Gradually a goodness will arise through that mutual love.

This should not be seen as a normal ashram. Because there is a special experience here. The good and the bad all see this with perfect love. The great man will talk about its greatness. The bad man will at least say that there is no discrimination there. You should behave with the middle class in a way that is neither. The people here should behave in a way that does not offend these three types of people.

Since it is a place of experience, priority should be given to experience. Even if they have failed and suffered losses through their actions, they can go somewhere and succeed. If strictly implemented, it will only become like a normal school. It means less sacrifice. When you have to catch and tie a madman, you should do so. Since the common man is struggling for money, special attention should be paid to him. A familiarity that allows working closely is what needs to be developed.

Why is it said that words should be guarded? Jesus only implemented ten words, right? Only if the word is guarded in the same way can it be implemented. If there is a mistake in action, it can be corrected by action.

If a word is spoken wrong, it cannot be corrected. Many mistakes have occurred here because words were taken lightly and handled without thought. Through words, much prosperity and fortune can be earned. You have a lack of experience in this. If a mistake is made in a small matter, do not use big words.

When devotion arises, what takes hold first is demonic nature. How would it be if an ignorant person loves? They will give up to the ocean. What if they oppose? They will pluck the ocean out. One who loves moderately will only stand moderately even if they see a severe mistake. Women possessed by Yakshiyamma will speak with clenched teeth. Yakshiyamma's nature will blend into her and she will become like a demon. When she dies, Yakshiyamma will become a demon. Then she will never rise from that. How would it be if gods and goddesses are worshipped? If gods and goddesses feel love, even the words spoken will come true. A demon does not have that much deceit. Those who worship gods and goddesses will make a mistake. Whatever they think will be accomplished. Immediately, he will attain arrogance. Rising a little higher, when he starts to climb to that position, he will also go wrong. Either he will go mad, or he will become a fool. If you hold onto a god, you should not worship a guru. There is a church that sacrifices roosters. There are many such things in temples too.

How will a guru behave? They will not let you near any of these unwanted things. Whatever you do, there is a benefit. But the guru will not even show it. To one who comes like this, coming very strictly, they will trouble in every way. If you want to improve, improve, or else go - that is the meaning of it. Although they show love, they will also show this. Some gurus will destroy your pride. Even then, you should stand firm. Later, they will reveal the flaws in that pride which is an insult. This experience of the guru should be useful to you.

The goddess has a quality. She will pull you close and create emotion. If his karma does not yield results in the way the goddess intends, she will trouble him. Finally, she will turn him into an emotional creature. If there is also a vision, such a situation will definitely arise. This will continue until the status of the god and goddess.

Those who come to the ashram should pay special attention to one thing. Do not tell those standing here about the things seen on the way. Because, while doing big things, getting caught up in small things will destroy the good. The lack of experience without attaining humility is the reason for all this. There are some intelligent people. They will also insert their intelligence along with what they believe. We should take this forward without getting caught in any of this. By embracing this idea through sacrifice, we can move forward through virtue. You cannot move forward if you hang onto the ends of small words. You should discard the knowledge that ties up knowledge and take the humbled knowledge.

With what thought do you go to a house, temple, or ashram? When going to a temple three or four times, some people will get the thought of going on a pilgrimage. When going many times like that, the thought will come to go to an ashram and stay there. When it becomes many times like that, an ashram experience itself might come. When it becomes like that, when some experiences come, some trivialities will arise. If you live in an ashram for twenty-five years, you should not only study for twenty-five years, but also give what you have studied. If the receiver is not ready for it, then how will you give it?

When you stay here, you should pay attention to those who come and go. Some will come as rogues. Some will come with antics. How will you teach them? Those lower than us, those higher, the sattvic, the rajasic, everyone will come. Some will do what they have to do with the time they get. Some will stand like a potato. What if you study and do this as a guru lineage? You will be able to keep your own model.

A sannyasi who had lived an ashram life for twenty-five years and reached maturity came here. He is a person standing in the Trimurti tradition. Having reached three steps (status), he also knows how to elaborate on what is up to there. Those who come like this without a guru can see more than those who have attained through a guru. Because the nature is a mixture of rajasic and tamasic, humility was lost. What can we learn from that person?

In the Trimurti system, Rama stands supreme. At the beginning of Kali Yuga, Krishna came. Even though Krishna is far ahead, in North India today, Rama and Durga are worshipped. What should be known through temples and what should be known through ashrams cannot be known today. A person who has gone to a temple for many days and done pilgrimage will find it difficult to leave the ashram. A person who comes to the ashram without this familiarity can only find faults.

The story has been taught that monks should eat only green leaves and water. But if it is to be useful to man, that is not enough. Vasistha, Vyasa, and Bhoga are the ones who have contributed something to us. They all lived only for society.

Before going to the ashram, one must think about why one is going. Otherwise, there is no use in going. The movement is formed to make this understood. There are no humans without love for God or some other kind of love. Whatever kind of love it is, isn't it sattvic? Devotion comes when the feeling of love increases and reaches its peak. When love for God comes, other loves will leave. Everyone has love in different measures. When one maintains a love in the guru parampara, one can study each and everyone. Even one who has completed a pilgrimage as mentioned earlier will be convinced about this only if he gets many ashram experiences. What is gained in a hundred lives can be gained in a single life with ashram experience. One can be convinced in ten years whether what one thought was right or wrong. Thus, a lot of knowledge can be gained through ashram life. One can think deeply and know about past stories. Then, will one enjoy hearing the story of Ghatotkacha?

Familiarity with the ashram over time is necessary. No matter which community one is born in or which field one works in, this can only be achieved through the age of the soul. There should be a thought about what an ashram is from the beginning. If there is such a thought, no one can just stand idle in the ashram. The ashram has everything needed for continuous work. One should increase familiarity by doing it at home, in the country, and in the ashram.

That . . . 'effort' (shramam) is the ashram. We do not know that what we do many times stays in our soul. The subject being done must be completed. One will not feel respect even for a monk who has not completed it in that way. Rajas and tamas will be prominent in him. Even if one feels respect for such people at first, one will gradually realize that there is a mistake. How many such people have come and gone from here. Not a single monk who is respected from beginning to end has come and stayed here. It takes time to study a monk. The same goes for a brahmachari. No matter how much penance or effort is made, if one is great by oneself, no one will feel disrespect towards him. Such people are very few. If the father and mother are not capable of experiencing, the children will not be good. The ashram is a combination of all these aspects.

If a strong person speaks, any scholar will have to yield. We have seen everything we need. In the ashram system, not only the perfection of sannyasa, but everything can be seen.
