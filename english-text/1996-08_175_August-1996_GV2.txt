Like the astrological predictions that are sometimes written to make fun of you, I am also making fun of you. But I am not joking, I am speaking the truth.

There is an old saying that if you earn money even if it brings disgrace, that money will erase the disgrace. Both the knowledgeable and the ignorant have no difference of opinion in this matter. I am also one of those who belong to that group. The word 'knowledgeable' came into existence only because there are 'ignorant' people. But the reality of the ignorant is true as far as the knowledgeable is concerned. At the same time, it is taught that the ignorant is the knowledgeable. What kind of nonsense is this? It is certain that if ignorance is let loose, it will drown in the procession of the knowledgeable. Therefore, we need a medium that drives both of these. In this, knowledge should be driven first, then ignorance.

The fact that the Karma Yogi becomes an ignorant practitioner itself makes us understand that both ignorance and knowledge are equally tiring. We have to see the knowledge-ism that brings up the useless here. Like the import-export* system, the incoming politics has burned here. The politics that existed in India before Mahatmaji was created by A.O. Hume. The wealthy people who claimed the succession (Indian National Congress) of that politics taken over by Mahatmaji and reached anarchy should have known one thing first. We must first find the root cause of whatever we are focusing on. There is a reason to say this. Caste, religion,

prophetic religion and many other things have been nurtured, and this politics that has spread like a parasite in all of them will also die when those trees die. Indian politics has reached this state today. We all should be able to recognize what we gain and what we lose from this politics.

In the same way, when a procession serving destruction in the name of spiritual and material things passes before us, we may doubt whether it is our human brothers themselves, or the demons of the past, or the illusory forms of the divine elements that are said to come in the future. There is no doubt that it is an illusory form. But is illusion non-existent or existent? If it is existent, in what form can it be shaped?

If these are only some aspects of the contemporary chain of mistakes, before all this, we have made a mistake somewhere as the cause of all this. What is it?

There is a puzzle called Yugadharma in Hindu history. Even if we study the Ramayana, Bhagavatam, eighteen Puranas, four Vedas, and six Shastras altogether, the complete picture of Yugadharma is not revealed to us. None of those who have tried to correct us by counting and scientifically explaining these Vedas, Puranas, and epics one by one are bad. In addition to all this, many secrets of Yugadharma have been indicated here and there. But nothing can be clearly understood. There have been people among us who talk a lot about the Yugas and those who have stopped by giving accounts of up to five thousand years. A

In a short period of time, India and Asia have a history where Sri Shankara and many other great scholars, sages, prophets, and gurus came and went, attaining scholarship and greatness among Hindus. But whether it is because the succession of what was written in the scriptures went wrong, or because of various karma doshas, or because of bad luck, there is no way to know why - our ambiguities regarding the Yugadharma are very large. But there is a way ordained by God for us to know all this clearly here.

We don't even know what came and went where in the Asian empire. Can we know what the progress of scholars and commoners is just by writing endlessly about this arrival that has happened to date? Can we determine the duration of the influence of the prophets and the small gurus who followed?

Now what is this duration?

The duration refers to the long Manvantaras and the Chaturyugas contained in the Manvantaras. Our effort is to find out which Chaturyuga of which Manvantara this is. Not many people can participate in this effort. There is clear evidence that they cannot. How many prophets came here? How many gurus came with an influence of a thousand or two thousand years? This is the evidence. What can we do by counting such endless numbers? Is it enough to find this out in some way? You must be aware of the effort or endeavor to complete the story of the god and goddess and try to complete everything with endless words like God's Maya or Mayavilasam. The truth is that none of this has influenced us, or rather, it has not taken us anywhere. It is also true that quite a few efforts have been made here as mentioned above. We have also experienced the results of those efforts to some extent. There is no point in arguing about what is there and what is not.

Here, an easy way has been gifted to us from that Brahma's decision. On the cover page of an almanac among us, we can see a female figure blindfolded with a black cloth holding up a weighing scale. How can one know the accuracy of the scale if blindfolded with a black cloth? What should the symbolism of the effort brought through it look like? Its meaning is that even if blindfolded with a black cloth, if there is effort, the accuracy of the scale can be shown. Although we all know its meaning, the misfortune of not getting the fulfillment of the meaning has existed for billions of years. The earth and the moon belong to the orbits of the solar system. Although many people calculate and research the science related to it, no matter how one calculates the nine planetary positions, no one is able to see and say beyond the subtle results. What is said is in the manner of sometimes it works, sometimes it doesn't. One of the many reasons for this is this. If ten people in the same star gather, ten characters, if a hundred people gather, a hundred characters. If a thousand people gather, a thousand characters, there are many reasons to say. Therefore, it is not possible to just say it like that. We have the luck to get the knowledge according to Brahma's decision that there was a mistake in the dharma of the times. Therefore, everyone living in this opportunity is lucky. No one should ever let go of this luck that has come to us. Kaliyuga has the special quality of being the period ordained to be born in Kaliyuga, the fourth quarter of Chaturyuga, and to know what karma and dharma are conducive to liberation. The countless thirty-three crore gods of the Hindus. This earth where we are born and live is one of the lands where millions of Gurus, prophets, and countless sages who came along with them have come and gone. This fortune cannot be postponed by setting a deadline. Therefore, let us all work together. Without perishing by talking about the vanity of caste, religion, and religious belief, without falling into the whirlpool of blindness and rotting away, let us share the fortune given by the divine will with everyone. The vision is what shows all the blessed souls called demons, prophets, great sages, yogis, karmis, jnanis, and bhaktas till today in the five states of waking, dreaming, deep sleep, turiya, and turiyatita with the power of the yugas. It is by seeing through this vision that the Gurus who came according to the laws of the yugadharma have showered their glories. If you want, this is an opportunity for you too to see it with your physical eyes and attain fortune. Kali is the time given to take up and practice this karma ordained for each child, without perishing by babbling about male, female, caste, and religion, and without getting caught in the whirlpool of the learned and the ignorant. You can believe that this is not just a dharma but a procession of dharma. Let no one doubt that this path of dharma is the same for all of us who got the opportunity to be born together. See, know, and make known all the above-mentioned good and evil with your physical eyes by holding onto a Guru. Stop harboring the ignorance of playing by talking about the greatness or smallness of any god. God will come if called in any language or form with the intention of God. No one needs to doubt this.
