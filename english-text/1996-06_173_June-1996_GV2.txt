GURUVANIKAL · June 1996 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P1030 to GV2:P1038
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

June 1996

All those who have come as Hindu religious theorists have behaved in such a way that we cannot know each other. Even today, the situation is no different. Calling it a secular nation and fighting for religion. Today, we have reached this kind of action. If we fight like this, will we ever be able to stand together? We have desired for the good. In reality, what we are doing is not good. My God is greater, others' God is small; behind this attitude, small castes got small Gods. Behind this difference, everyone has been working while staying away from each other. Therefore, if any good action is seen from a person or a society, it is usual to move away from there. It is the perspective of that person or society that has changed. It does not mean that we have changed from where we stood. We cannot change. Moreover, we are only creating scratches in that society and causing it trouble, rather than being able to impart any good from it. Or if anything

If anything has been done, it is merely some inadequate actions. No dharma has been practiced otherwise. As long as the human world does not think about how dharma will and should be practiced, no good will come.

Going after angels, saints, cardinals, and apostles, and believing it to be monotheism. Believing that so much was done under the same Pope for so long. Although it is true that this has managed to unite us to some extent, it has not been able to impart anything good. Similarly, Islam, Jainism, Sikhism, and then the Gurudharma Pracharana Sabha - there have been countless such things in India. Has anyone been able to say a definitive word about what has happened in these states, from devil to god, from god to sage, from sage to ascetic, from ascetic to guru or imam, and what harm has come to us from any of these? Or has anyone imparted the understanding that this is the harm that has happened, and that we, humanity as a whole, must change from this harm?

Many people have wished to see Kerala as a model. Because, unlike other small countries, Kerala has a virtue. The fact that agricultural land is less in Kerala, which has more plows, is not an inadequacy, and there is enough water and organic nature to preserve them. But we have not done that and set it right. The reason for this is that a great tug-of-war is always maintained between the workers, laborers, and the capitalist among us. This is a continuation of the old fight. Even though we are ready to shift conceptually to the path of modern perspective, there is no need for more proof that we are unable to abandon that old tradition of intellect.

Now take the children in our homes. Until what age can we keep girls in our homes? When we send these girls to another house, we do not know what virtues we should send with her. Instead, what do we do? Bargain for money and sell the girl and the boy. This is completely unnecessary. We don't think about what qualifications this girl should have to become a mother. Can they add anything to dharma other than wasting the children's lives by imparting undeserved wealth, lordship, and suitable boasting? Look, how has life been destroyed without giving an opportunity to understand the meaning and depth of life by knowing the hardships of life and the transformative nature of life? Therefore, it is like establishing the foundation of loveless action by adopting the dress and language of foreign countries.

I am not saying that you have no love for your children. You have love, and you have thoughts about what should be in the course of action. But beyond all that, some things need to be changed through the children. That is, through all past religious customs, our ancestors have brought some rituals. There is nothing in it that is sufficient for the completeness of life. Therefore, nothing is complete for us. How are we standing in an incomplete state? Calling upon the god of caste, it crept into religion in another guise. When the god of religion was placed, the fight began. This fight has gathered and stands today in a system that falls and breaks everywhere.

Everyone should think about why we, humans, need this system. Instead, you should not be ready to say that my god, my guru, and my imam have more strength. Purely as humans, as men and women, we

We must live by giving love to the One. Do those who speak of caste and religion use the five elements differently? Is the nature of the five senses different? When we act through the five sheaths (Panchakoshas) and reach that state of love and respect, we will realize that love is the greatest of all. It is this love that is offered to God. Then, having received God's love, humans should share that love among themselves and raise their children in a way that sustains Dharma. There is no greater duty for a human being than this. Only when this happens can we truly love God. That love for God will bring purity to your mind. If this purity of mind is shared for the goodness of humanity, you need not doubt it—you will reach the highest pinnacle of Dharma. Otherwise, if we raise children without imparting anything that contributes to the good of the world, they will bring disgrace not only to their homes but also to the land, lose their Dharma, become devoid of merit, and end up as pathetic beings. You who loudly proclaim that there is no caste, no religion, and no class are the ones who have the tendency to assign castes even to animals. You must understand that this casteism will not only destroy you but will also turn out to be the greatest disaster for life itself. The body will perish anyway. This body, which is scrubbed, bathed, dressed, covered, and carefully carried around every day, has no real greatness to show off. All that truly exists is the life force (Jiva). Yet, nothing is being done for the glory of the life force. It is a special manifestation of love. For the sake of that love, let us forget caste, religion, and class, and unite.

Guruvani
