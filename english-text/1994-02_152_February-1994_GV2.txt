February 1994

Among us, people have a certain perception about the ashram. That it is meant for acquiring occult powers, or that it is a place of fake godmen, or for the accomplishment of an individual's selfish motives, or established to enjoy life by engaging in smuggling or business

Or a place, and many such concepts. Whatever it is, an ashram means one has to follow a certain discipline. As far as a Hindu is concerned, there are three traditions: Shaivism, Shaktism, and Vaishnavism. But they cannot be distinguished from each other. Vaishnavites believe that they emanated from Brahman. Each sect believes the same way.

Through the establishment of the Chaturvarnya system, a lot of ignorance has spread. If we study the history of the last three thousand years, we will understand what kind of difficulties we have overcome to reach here. Caste is the most formidable barrier. In every home, there are people of four castes - Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Shudra. Those who hold power are the Brahmin and the Kshatriya. The Shudra and the Vaishya are oppressed. As times changed, the Shudra also had to be given power. But the Brahmin and the Kshatriya refused to hand over power. With that, the situation became 'might is right'. There were many such kings. Those who claim to be scholars are still trying to do the same today.

Today's most important thought should be how to change the next generation by removing the flaws in the worship system. We must think about the problems in today's politics. Today's rulers are those who have not understood the essence of Indian culture. We must firmly believe that we cannot survive unless our culture and heritage grow and become stronger. When Westerners dressed according to their physical appearance, we blindly imitated them. Even great souls said this. But you refused to accept it. Our anxiety is to accept whatever Westerners show and say. Where were we born, where did we grow up, what is the glory of our nature? We are not ready to think about any of this. We are only investigating the easiest way to make wealth. What will become of the children of those who learn to make wealth like this? Is there any place for culture here? For you who live like this, what will you understand if someone says 'ashram'?

There are many reasons for the corruption of culture. The main one is worship. Towards the north, the deity is Chamundeshwari. What can we impart by worshipping Madan, Marutha, etc.? What is to be achieved by gods and goddesses who have no place in the Kali Yuga? If a Shudra can study the Vedas, why should there be a place for Chaturvarnya? If an Avarna can have the worship rituals of a Savarna, how can a marriage from a Shudra's house be a 'Sambandham' and a marriage from a Brahmin's house be a 'Veli'?

You all must have heard about Ayyappan. Who has Ayyappan been portrayed as! From where is the story told that Ayyappan split and fell? This Ayyappan was a person who lived like a sannyasi. He also had a little vision. He was a perfect 'Sadhu'. What should we understand if it is said that such a man went for tiger's milk for a woman who was immersed in worldly affairs and had a headache? He did penance with the concept of Dharmashastha. Thus Ayyappan's status became great. Today Ayyappan is called 'Hariharasuthanayyanayyappan'. This is the poet's deceit. If asked where Ayyappan came from, what all nonsense is he saying? You who have learned this do not know what an ashram is. What will you understand if someone says a temple or a bhajan mutt? The place where we worship each of our things is a bhajan mutt. The place where they are worshipped is a temple. There are many types of ashrams and Guru traditions. A Guru is not someone who shows miracles. What you need is to be brought through humility and led to fortune. This should grow through children. This is what an ashram is for.

We have a culture that led a very low standard of life. Because knowledge is very small, we have an arrogance that knowledge is power. The arrogance of 'I' is in all human beings. There are those who think, 'Oh, they criticized me'. They don't have the knowledge to think that it was good to be criticized. Ignorance is more in girls. There are shortcomings in the lowest and the highest, regardless of age. Where was I born, why was I born, grew up

These girls mostly grow up without any thought about me. They have a tendency to just drift along without doing anything or getting anything done. Everyone is very far from their duties. Every home has a tradition of living in ignorance for a long time. A tradition of eating and living without ever working. We are the children of this wretchedness. Caste-based struggles can be seen everywhere. No one knows how to think about how to raise girls other than to engage in such struggles.

If this habit is to change, a suitable government must come. For that to happen, one must pray with an aching heart that the ignorance that has come to reside amidst this human society be removed. Do this. It is very painful to think about you. Pray that our emotions do not flare up and ruin this place. Pray that these kinds of nonsense burn and perish, that all this perishes with this world. The first thing you should do is to beg God to give us a way to remove these shortcomings from our offspring and to give us the mind and action for it. You will change.

You have a lack of administrative experience. There are those who have perished by committing fraud in administration. All that can only be changed by the grace of God. If you keep a strict routine for prayer, there will be great results. You are people who cannot love each other by saying good words. No matter who they are or how good they are, you can see a stain deep inside them. You must pray that fraudulent thinking disappears from the world. What great men say does not easily slip away. Hearing Jesus' words, they waited for one thousand five hundred years for the kingdom of heaven to come. Work must be done swiftly. Otherwise, they will be confused in their actions. When doing something, you should think good thoughts and do it. Those who have done so will be seen with ten hands when they are born again. But there will be no such arrogance. Those with vision should imagine that a good way will be formed in everyone. If you imagine with a stain, the imagination will become bad. You must pray that if there are any flaws in what we and our lineage have done, please resolve them and join us to you.

In nineteen seventy-three (1973), there was an announcement that the thing I had desired for ages was completed today. The nature of that desire has been happening here for some time. An opportunity like the one Moses got was also received in India in that same proportion. But a somewhat subordinate worshipful nature that could not take that opportunity existed here earlier. According to Hindu scriptures, there are four yugas (Chaturyuga). There were acharyas as authorities for each Chaturyuga. Krishna came before the beginning of Kali Yuga. He removed the customs that existed until then. But it got buried in myths. Krishna was tied among the gods and goddesses. That should never have happened. Krishna is an avatar. Therefore, he said to discard what existed until then and worship him. Moses was not that pure a soul. When Moses was born, the subjects were slaves of the Pharaoh king. The Pharaoh knew that Moses would be born among these fallen people. Therefore, all the male children born were being killed. The background of Krishna's birth was also similar. The king killed all the male children. Moses' father and mother somehow hid and raised him. They prayed fervently. Then they set him adrift in the water. The Pharaoh king's daughter and her maids saw him, realized he was a slave child, had him picked up, and gave him back to his mother. She raised him until he was four years old and then gave him back to the palace. Moses was not given wisdom on any of these occasions. When he came of age, while hiding in a shepherd's house, he married an honest girl there according to the word he received. It was there that the baptism of wisdom took place. Krishna was a wise man from birth. Even after Krishna's time, although there were many gurus among us to correct things, you could not take it. India is the birthplace of many great men. Therefore, we do not need the Varna system and the Trinity system here. After nineteen seventy-three, we started getting everything completed here.

The Prophet had also received the message. But it was also said that it would be told only when requested. This prayer being conducted here is something that has been revealed.

It is the girls who have got the fortune to do this. Through this, we should be able to remove the evil that caused the downfall of women. What the Prophet said fifteen hundred years ago became true today because we were able to believe and act upon it. Similarly, what we say today might not be understood by today's generation. But God has a purpose in making us pray today. Everything that was revealed here has been preserved here. It cannot be revealed now. It can be revealed after this generation.

Guruvani March 1994 The foundational nature of all matters of our ashram has come through prayer. A state will come where the world will understand what our prayer is for. A ritual called 'Poortheekaranam' (Completion) was conducted twenty-seven times. Of the twenty-seven stars

Those completions were in character. In nineteen seventy-three, twenty-seven were completed. It was on the day the twenty-seventh was completed that the message 'what I desired has been completed' was received.

In this atmosphere, the atoms of perfect emotions are full. Therefore, men and women should handle things carefully here. Every word, every look, and every expression of yours will spread and linger in this atmosphere. It will never leave your life. This is a field where Dayananda Saraswati, Rishikesh Sivanandan, Sankaracharya, and everyone else failed. Then there is no need to talk about your case. If you can use your skills and abilities for this, it is a great fortune.

I love your work, not money. You need the determination and pain to realize that this is the result of a great sacrifice and to respect it by always sacrificing. You must maintain humility, and along with humility, care in words, and a pleading prayer, 'Father! I am doing something, making something happen, always make me do it according to your will.' This will only work if there is a dedicated attitude to dedicate life for this. If those who work today, even a child, carry this out without making a mistake, there is no doubt that their names will be recorded in the pages of history. It may take one or two generations for that.

This is a fortune your ancestors did not get. You have received that fortune. This is not for being enslaved to anything or anyone. The great sinful power of killing the Prophet's children and seizing power is the reason why looting and murder happen wherever Muslims take power. Therefore, your life and the mischief in your life must be untied and resolved here.

If you live a disciplined life, you will be able to understand that good and bad come intermingled. The instinctual bad lying at the bottom of life should come out, untie, and good should come in its place. Those who do more good things will get as much bad in delusion. If one is a seeker or a sattvic, they can stop it themselves.

Our experience is like getting a new earth. Before getting it, we don't know what it is. The word earth refers to the body. Many things are stored in the godown of the heart. It should be kept from spoiling. No matter how hard it is, you should be able to finish it. There should be a state where we do not need both merit and sin. If you earn merit, then you have to be born again to experience its result. If you encourage your mind, no one can defeat you. The men and women standing here are knowingly or unknowingly doing some good things. You cry when pain comes. Even through that crying, you should pray, 'May I be able to do your will.' We do not need siddhi. Siddhi is a specialty of time. It is enough if siddhi acts as a specialty of the respective time. Siddhi is not needed for this time. Krishna showed and stopped the full nature of siddhi. It is not allowed anymore. God is leading us to the Satya Yuga. For that, we need to do it there and take it here. Otherwise, you don't have to do anything for God. You have to do it with a broken heart and take the result of that pain. Instead of working and letting the mind go into mischief again and again. You should never go back even an inch after doing it. Everyone's good qualities should be modeled. There is something to learn even from a small creature. Learn from everything and maintain the determination that at least one person will improve through my work.

The greatest guru of a dull lineage will get ten male and ten female disciples. That will depend on the guru's luck. Of these, three will be excellent, seven will be medium, and the rest will only reach the light of God. They will not merge in the light of Brahman. No matter how many others there are in that dull lineage, they will only attain salvation in the next birth as members or servants of this guru lineage.

Then there is another group. Those who gain insight, rise up to some extent, and then degenerate by not doing anything. Another group consists of householders. They take everything in moderation, lead a family life, and ultimately attain liberation. Or they merge with the Guru. Both these groups should not harbor any doubts about the Guru. If ever such a doubt arises, they can attain liberation by confessing to the Guru that such a doubt occurred in the past and seeking a remedy. Even householders, if they do not err, can reach up to the state of the Trimurti. If they do err, depending on the magnitude of the mistake, they will be born for countless lifetimes, and at some point, by accepting the discipleship of an exalted Guru in this Guru lineage, they can attain liberation through that. For those who come into the fold of this Guru or Guru lineage and then reject it after some time, it is difficult to even get a human birth in subsequent lifetimes. Even if they do get one, they will have absolutely no wisdom. Later, by praying for knowledge, they will experience many lifetimes through some other lineage and eventually merge into the state of the Trimurti. Now, there is yet another group. Those who rise on their own to get close to the Guru and the lineage, reach a level where generally no one makes mistakes, but then fall into error due to jealousy or some other bad actions. The physical condition given to such people will be such that they cannot work in any way towards attaining God. Then, in one lifetime, they will have complete devotion and humility. From there, after being given many lifetimes, meeting many gurus and grieving with a weary mind, in one lifetime God will show mercy, grant a healthy birth again, and give liberation through that. That will be obtained only through severe sacrifice, after becoming exhausted from searching and reaching a helpless state. There is still another group. They, having been born across many lifetimes and ages, struggling and worshipping in the Shakta, Shaiva, and Vaishnava traditions, will in their final birth come under gurus who appear every two thousand or three thousand years, and thus attain elevation according to the arrival of those gurus. This is merely a brief description of a very detailed philosophical vision.
