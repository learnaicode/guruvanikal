[GV1:P1677] January 1987

[GV1:P1678] Many of you are engaged in ashram duties here. However, you have a lack of experience in such activities. We can only act according to the karmic path laid down by our ancestors or contemporaries. Irrespective of the era, we only inherit a few tendencies of our ancestors, our own tendencies, and the knowledge of our contemporaries, whether they are scholars or ordinary folks. The world has a peculiar characteristic today. We can instantly know events happening in any corner of the world through the media. That wasn't the case before. Many events would occur within a five-mile radius that we wouldn't even know about. That does not happen now. However, things happening in remote villages are still often unknown. Even though such awareness has grown significantly within us, we have not completely succeeded. Along with that, each of us needs to grow culturally as well.

[GV1:P1679] There is a kind of knowledge that comes from observing and doing everything. We can only attain that knowledge to a certain extent. To impart knowledge to us, there are various systems like the Bhajana Mutt system, the temple system, and the ashram system. What we receive from all sides is knowledge. The knowledge belonging to the Bhajana Mutt is not what the temple system holds. The ashram system is not the knowledge of the temple system. Only the ashram system will move us towards true knowledge. Until then, they make us believe by preaching the grand aspects of the karma system, convincing us that everything is achieved through those rituals. Massive lies. In Hindu logic and Vedic authority, there are many fire rituals (*havanas*) like *Tila Havanam*, *Mrityunjayam*, *Shuddhi Havanam*, and *Kshetra Pindam*. Then, they attain *Mantra Siddhi* (mastery of mantras). *Mantra Siddhi* is later converted into *Deva Siddhi* (divine attainment). Only after going through this does one realize that none of this is true knowledge (*Jnana*). It is for the sake of true knowledge that one enters an ashram.

[GV1:P1680] For a wise person (*Jnani*), there is no *Karma Kanda* (section on rituals); there is only *Jnana Kanda* (section on knowledge). *Jnana Kanda* can only be obtained from an ashram. A sannyasin or a Vedic scholar wandering in Vedic rituals will not attain it. The ashram is meant to impart this understanding and to practice and internalize it. We have never had such knowledge. We have only acquired knowledge about the blazing miracles that cause overwhelming excitement by witnessing this *Siddhi* (occult power) system. It is exactly as scholars have written it down. We have heard that in Vashistha's ashram, there was a cow that granted whatever was asked of it. We do not realize that all this came from the poet's imagination. We just swallowed it as it is. We completely believed the story of Kanva's ashram and Shakuntala, as well as the miracle-filled history of Buddha. These stories have only managed to corrupt us, not improve us. When ordinary people narrate, sing, and play out these things, it becomes like a *Thiruvathirakali* (a traditional dance). After watching it for a while, one gets bored of all the games. How many such fabricated tales have they made us believe! The *Devadasi* system itself is an example of this. How much ignorance exists in the *Devadasi* system. We are the culmination of such ignorance. We just do not understand that.

[GV1:P1681] The ashram system does not talk about any of these things. It is the realm of spiritual experience (*Anubhuti*). Gods, goddesses, the sages under them, and the wise men standing alongside them will all come before this true *Jnani*. Before the *Jnani*, all of them are merely subordinates. Subordinate means they have absolutely nothing to give to this individual. In front of that person, they possess a much lower level of operational capability. None of the things related to the temple system or similar practices can be performed before this *Jnani*. It is when this happens that we understand reality. So, it is not easy for this knowledge to be transferred to us. Why? We are the remnants of the karma performed by our ancestors. Secondly, that path of karma is commonly practiced among us today. Therefore, we cannot stay away from it. Our belief systems have become like that. Moreover, looking at the world at large, the followers of various gurus and prophets have become corrupted in different ways by misusing their intellect and power. This even spread as a distortion within governments. Thus, religious systems have turned into statecraft and a trick to win in it. All concepts became like that. We do not realize that we are destroying ourselves in this manner. A change must be brought about from this.

[GV1:P1682] It was mentioned earlier that the ashram is the realm of spiritual experience. When you count from demons to gods, and from gods to sages one by one, you realize that their knowledge is limited to a certain extent. Haven't you heard a short poem? It might not have been written with full understanding. Or perhaps it was: *'When you cross the six steps and reach there, you can see Lord Shiva.'* What do you understand from this? The meaning everyone gathers is that Shiva can only be seen after crossing six steps. It actually means that if you cross the six steps, you can see what Shiva possesses and what he does not. It means one can understand that Shiva is limited to this much. Before someone who has crossed five and a half steps, Shiva has nothing to do. Devendra will not approach one who has crossed six steps. Devendra is the lord of all these gods, yet even he won't come. Then comes the state leading to the divine presence. None of these exist there. There is only the gross, the subtle, the effect, and the cause. Then there is the concept of a sage who transcends even that. Haven't you heard, *'Sankalpa mathrena Parameshwara'* (The Supreme Lord exists merely by thought/will)? This is not easily understood. It is impossible to know who transcends this. It is also difficult to cross this state. According to the dharma of the *Yugas*, we cannot cross it in the *Treta Yuga* or *Dvapara Yuga*. That is because those eras belong to the gods. When that time passes, even these gods must take birth as humans and attain liberation. That is what the Puranas celebrate, after all. So, we have taken birth in such a time. That is why it is said that the sages pleaded, 'Please allow us to witness Kali (Yuga).' It means they desired a birth in the Kali Yuga, hoping that at least then, by being at the Lord's feet, it wouldn't be bad if they were saved and liberated.

[GV1:P1683] Thus, without this familiarity, you will not be able to achieve this. It is for this reason you are advised to behave in accordance with the ashram's nature. It is the task of transforming a human into a god, into a sage, and guiding them to the path of liberation. First, solidify your belief system. The worship we perform today—realize that the gods and goddesses are limited to what they are. Then, embrace a form of worship in accordance with the dharma of the Yuga.

[GV1:P1684] Guru Vani
