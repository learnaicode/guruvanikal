[GV1:P2448] December 1989

[GV1:P2449] Guru: It was Bharadwaja who discovered Vimana Shastra (Bharadwaja Samhita). Bharadwaja's actions illustrate that disobeying a Guru's words will lead to destruction. When he was ready for the practical application of his discovery, the Guru warned him not to do it, stating it would lead to destruction. But Bharadwaja did not obey. He went to Ceylon with an aircraft having bulletproof glass and presented it to Kubera. That concept associated with Ravana came through Kubera. What happened eventually? A woman from the discoverer's own country was abducted in this very aircraft. That became the seed of destruction.

[GV1:P2450] Disciple: Is this a vision?

[GV1:P2451] Guru: This is indeed a vision. But it represents another path.

[GV1:P2452] Disciple: Didn't we receive this information here through a vision?

[GV1:P2453] Guru: No. There is history. Even though the Guru was present and had cautioned him, as a result of the disciple trying to exhibit his brilliance, a part of the country submerged into the sea. The very destruction the Guru had indicated earlier transpired. A portion of the country where the disciple, who ignored the Guru's words, operated went into the sea. The small fraction that remained amounted to nothing. Isn't that how Lanka stands even today? Overall, that kingdom perished. This also serves as a warning that such a truth should not be forgotten. From time to time, nature has been providing us with evidence.

[GV1:P2454] Kubera is one of the great emperors in the world. Kubera's kingdom is near Dwaraka. Because it was associated with actions taken without obeying the Guru, this country also faced destruction. Because a woman was abducted, a curse befell here as well. A war ensued between such individuals.

[GV1:P2455] What did they do subsequently? They wrote the story of the Brahmana. They made the other one a Dravidian. They wrote that he possessed ten heads and many such undesirable things. But if an action to destroy him had not occurred, his (the Dravidian's) ignorance would have amplified. Without abandoning this, he continues as a devotee of Shiva. The intelligent Vaishnava examined history. Then he realized that the era had passed. Thus it became everything in the hands of the one who embraced the Vaishnava concept. Because he wouldn't let go, they began creating the aforementioned Acharyas in this manner. Thinking that at least some dharma should prevail among humanity, Gurus were appointed in various places. Since it wasn't possible to travel so far and reach across a vast sphere back then, temples were established in different locations.

[GV1:P2456] Disciple: Then what about the statement that temples are not needed?

[GV1:P2457] Guru: I did not say that temples are not needed. It means the concept should be transformed. How should the temple be changed? The temple of those days is unnecessary. That follows the Vedic method. That is Tantra. (It is referred to as mantras and tantras - not truth.) Because he didn't claim it as the truth, he did not lie. For instance, a Tantri who is a specific Namboothiri. There too, he collected money without lying. I will demonstrate many tantras to you. You must give me money. And you did give it. You yielded to many things as well. Standing on that support, how many things did he manipulate? They even conducted Devadasi dances inside the temple. Thus, the sanctity of the temple was lost. Even if you adopt the method of the one who installed it, you can only progress up to a certain limit--the underlying principle is different, isn't it!

[GV1:P2458] The deity's status and decorations constitute the aforementioned chaos. How is a Devi temple decorated? If you observe the Madurai Meenakshi temple, you will see that it depicts the commotions of the dance of Shakti and Shiva. Where is the spirituality in that? This is a grand temple, not merely in South India, but in the world itself. Are Hindus capable of imparting any knowledge regarding these commotions?

[GV1:P2459] Disciple: All of that is considered a part of our culture.

[GV1:P2460] Guru: It is indeed culture. Each architectural element within it signifies a particular achievement. All of that is true. But look at how the concept of God we have constructed here is!

[GV1:P2461] Disciple: No one contemplates that, Guru!

[GV1:P2462] Guru: That is correct. Because this ignorance is deeply entrenched within us, we have been unable to attain the realm of true knowledge. Consequently, everything perceived was regarded as God. As the Master observed, 'To them, Yakshis and phantoms are deities. For us, Madan, Marutha, and Bhadrakali are deities. An entity clutching skulls and serpents is our god. To what extent will such a deity benefit us? Did this ferocious manifestation arrive to confer blessings upon us?' It is to such entities that ranks from Durga to Chamundeshwari have been assigned. They have been extolled through nine legendary exploits and woven into mythologies (tales of incarnation). What we have inherited is the residue of the karma enacted through such fabricated narratives. We have remained oblivious to this.

[GV1:P2463] What is this? This is the karma revealed in the visionary realm for the preparation of the Treta and Dvapara Yugas. It is not to say that it does not exist. The Brahmin himself has stated that this is not spirituality. Furthermore, it is an essence formulated in accordance with each individual's level of knowledge. To cultivate that essence, a social structure is necessary. Temples and prayer halls were conceptualized as such social structures. They have not understood any of this at all.
