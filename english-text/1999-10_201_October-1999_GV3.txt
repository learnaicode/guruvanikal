[GV3:P0741] October 1999

[GV3:P0742] About forty years ago, a number of people came to know in a visible way about the experiences I had on a small scale. That was at Varkala. When they knew it then, quite a few people came and went during the time they knew it. The people also felt it in a good way. But very soon after that, that is, within three years, ten times the number of people who came with love became opposed. If you ask what it is, I don't know either. Others don't know either. If you say it is like that or like this, and ask why it is so, one doesn't know what to say. But after this continued for four years, I came here after being told not to sit in a way that two people could see. When I came here, this was the acquaintance I had. I know some ten or twenty families here. I called and told them. I intend to stay here doing nothing for two and a half years. That is God's command. I had told those who have faith and love to bring something for me to eat. But as locals, they did not decrease it, they kept increasing it. But when it became three months, it started coming a little more than what was required for this. When it started coming like that, I told them not to bring anymore; what came is already more than needed, so don't bring anymore - so they stopped it. After that, a total anarchy came here in a way I don't know how it happened.

[GV3:P0743] After that, how many incidents some people belonging to this place themselves - shouldn't say everyone - a number of them did in many ways. What I understood is the secret of all this - I saw that all the powers worshiped till today were opposing. So if opposition comes like this, what to do? It is not like that. An order came from Sree Krishna that we should calm down a bit. We have some things to do. If it is not done, they will turn into a group that troubles again. That is not needed, we can do it. So before starting it in that way, let me say one thing. That first started like this. This Prabhakaran - most of you know him now. At that time he was studying. He also participated in that ceremony. Like him, seven boys and seven girls. Unmarried people. They were made to stand together like that. Then it is called a completion.

[GV3:P0744] Even I don't know what this completion is. Then some gods and goddesses would start coming like this and show something and go away. Like that, this completion has happened here thirty-four times. If you ask me what this is, I don't know how to say either. Completion is the name. Then Prabhakaran himself asked, what is this called completion, I don't understand it. He was told it will be said later, it is not time to say it now. A good answer could be given for this only three years after he asked. How is it means, worshiped till today - that is what you need to know - there is a scene here built up and worshiped till today as the Hindu system. But there is an application itself before us misleadingly called Sanatana. We hold it as Sanatana. It is not so.

[GV3:P0745] This is purely special uproar created to build up the Hindu religion. So when we say the deity of worship touching this, it is up to the Trimurti. So how to change this karma that has gone up to the Trimurti - it is impossible by anyone. This is the incident. This is what is called this completion, making us do that karma and completing it. How it is means, even the yakshis, gandharvas, and kinnaras will be finished off with one opportunity. Then what is added with the divine elements above that will be finished. It was completed in thirty-four times like this. But by twenty-seven, it reached this said Trimurti system or Devendra system. That was finished. Then I was told to keep quiet for two and a half years. Sitting like that, I was told to do two or three completions again. After that is when we started in the way seen today.

[GV3:P0746] How it is means, this Sree Krishna himself came and told them like this. I am not the one doing this. The children are here. So, you might ask why I couldn't do it. That's not it, the Guru-disciple relationship means acting by knowing the Guru. It is not enough if someone knows that Guru, the person who is a disciple himself must know. Only then will he get mental firmness. Making those who made their minds firm in that way do it was Sree Krishna himself. Like that, many things. So Sree Shankara and Rishi Saibaba had to be taken as the last ones.

[GV3:P0747] In the meantime, everyone from other religions came and went. Everything came and went in a loving manner. No one denied. At the same time, this Sree Shankara took seven days to change. Do you remember, Prabhakaran? What is it? Coming and standing. Not allowing to do anything. Then what is said, that is the matter you need to be careful about. They are all great people. We will have to oppose them. We should oppose only if seen. If they change on their own, let them change. When the pooja took place at nine o'clock on the seventh day, he changed on his own. In the same way, Rishi Sai was also changed. If you ask why it was changed like this, Sree Shankara is said to have shown siddhi. But a jnani should not show siddhi. That is the scene tried to be changed. Not to change Sree Shankara.

[GV3:P0748] It means no siddhi should be shown in the part of this jnani. Siddhi glories or any other act of service or seva vrithi should not be done. This is what is understood from Sree Shankara. What is it, when Sree Shankara went to the forest; a story has been written. And people have held it as a siddhi. True, it is a siddhi; you all know about entering the king's body and all. But that is the aspect that should not be done like that. Therefore, Sree Shankara could not cross this state.

[GV3:P0749] What is called this state is the full form of the seventh sky. Those who have studied Vedanta know that. But although it is said, nobody knows how they will calculate and take it regarding it. Speaking about this seventh sky. So after this seventh sky, then the second sky. That is what is as completion. No one has done that. Just went to the seventh and looked. So if seven is completed, this karma will not come. The dosha of this siddhi karma will come.

[GV3:P0750] There is a great person unaffected by this dosha, that is the Prophet. That dosha has not affected. Difficult to show siddhi. Then the jinn servant who came after that might have shown something. Other than that, the Prophet never showed it. So there is this person called the last prophet as one who does not show siddhi magic. But this could not be crossed. Going to the seventh, that is when it is seen that this world is entirely ruled by jinns. There - sitting there is what is done. In that land, it is called a graveyard; sat there. Did not even go out to urinate from there. So such a thing happened to a person who did not show this siddhi.

[GV3:P0751] At the same time, this Rishi Sai means someone who showed thousands of siddhis, but that, we have considered those siddhi magics as belonging to God. So there is a karma of deceiving man with yogas and such practices and various such siddhi magics and making it seem that this is God. We should not do that. Till then, whatever destruction comes, will not do anything that should not be done. That it will not be done, the items I had worshipped and all of it, I took it and threw it away, but did not do unwanted things to it. Will not do. Thus siddhi should not be shown. If it is God's will, it should come on its own. That was the desire. But now I am not trying to convince you. But I have a matter to tell you the truth. Because some of you have heard and kept the applications of many sannyasins and many divine elements joined together. You have held all this and kept the uproars of a religious system that you have considered as God's aspect. None of this is work done by God. God will never do so either. If we want, as humans, we have no authority to do anything other than seeking God's compassion.

[GV3:P0752] Guru Vani
