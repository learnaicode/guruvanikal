Guruvani May 1997

When comparing our knowledge with the knowledge of other countries, we can agree, if we want, that it is a very slow knowledge. However, because our ancestors did not handle it properly, we could not truly bring that knowledge to the side of knowledge. Therefore, we have not been able to take the knowledge that is our heritage. Nor have we been able to give it.

There is a knowledge—a vision—in the nature of our ashram that is not usually heard among us. It is often referred to by names like pratyaksham (direct perception) and asareeri (disembodied voice). But for us, vision means what has happened since the creation of this earth, or the solar system itself. What are the causes and effects of such events? Has the true knowledge of the vision been passed down through the lineage of the Trimurti or the lineage of Manu? Or is the vision its true aspect in the division of philosophers and ideological theories? It is a glory granted by God to bring peace to the arguments that have come through these various aspects. Through this glorious visionary aspect, remove the ancestors who were the cause of the birth of individuals from that position, that is, give them compassion in that nature and keep them aside, and then give birth to them when they are to be born. This vision is what God has allowed us to bring such peace. It is the duty of you who are living today to raise children who are worthy of giving birth to the ancestors who are thus kept aside with compassion at the appropriate times. But the living do not seem to have that familiarity. Prior familiarity is the growth of familiarity. There must be an ashram to develop prior familiarity.

To develop this familiarity, God brought many ideological religions and many prophetic religions. Various types of Vedantic programs were introduced at different intervals. But nothing has been able to unite these diverse traditions. You do not even know that such a mistake exists in human life. Therefore, you have not been able to respect each other so far. You have fallen into such a chaotic path.

You cannot unite by recognizing the lack of knowledge that existed in the beginning regarding this and its good and evil. For that, this life itself is not enough; today we have living conditions that are much more developed compared to the past. Despite all the greatness, we are running competitively after education, wealth, and enlightenment. No one is trying or desiring to foster human love among us. What is the reason for this? The same as said before. That is, those living today are the remainder of the mistake of establishing the foundations of life without recognizing ancestors, gods, sages, and ascetics, and without knowing what the state of yoga, the state of education, and the state of wealth are. Therefore, you do not have the knowledge to love, to get close through love, and to handle anyone. What you have is the knowledge to return to the barbarism that has been accumulated under the name of a new culture. What can a language, a country, a religion, or a culture do to resolve this?

We must be able to do that. God has given us such an ashram familiarity. An action to unite your faith, your love, your respect, and your humility. What is faith? There is a faith in mother, father, wife, and husband. Let that faith become straight with knowledge. When that becomes straight, whatever we have in the land, all of that

It can be straightened out. What good is it to learn and live by looking at the hypocritical practices outside, thinking that our house is no good and everything else is fine, and then get ruined? How can we get ashore? What good has the human race gained by worshiping God in many ways and showing off with drums, songs, noise, and respect?

We cannot take one good thing by separating these mistakes one by one. God has shown us an easy way for that from the past. God gave knowledge to the king for the birth of Krishna and the birth of Jesus. But the scholars or leaders of that time did not allow that knowledge to be believed like that. Not only that, they also encouraged to take it in the way it happened before. Therefore, with the life of those great men, their idea went away. Or they fabricate a theory suitable to degrade the people and teach those ideas through it. When that happened, there were neither the ideas of great men nor the fabricated theories. What do we need now?

An idea suitable for this time, which gives the right to all the varnas in the varnashrama dharma, the Brahmin, the Kshatriya, the Vaishya, and the ignorant Shudra born in the fourth quarter, to grow up and reach Brahminhood together and get liberation. No lineage has been able to give an idea based on this knowledge till today. That is not because of the fault of the ascetics who came with the ideas. What do the ascetics know? They think that if God is manifested, God will come here and beat many to death and make some others good.

The idea given by God for the human world to change from this ignorance is what we are developing through this ashram acquaintance. We have received this with all the special qualities according to the time. The ascetics, the rulers, and the people have desired and suffered for a long time for this arrival. There is a big story behind this of desiring and suffering for a long time. We do not have the qualification to work up to its qualification. A situation has happened to our country and house that we cannot go outside our country. All countries have this situation. Because no country has the experience of taking back the idea according to the qualification of the time.

No matter through which time cycles it comes back, an idea reaches its complete form in Kali. Kali is more important than Satyayuga. If anything has gone excessive due to any lifestyle in Satyayuga, it is Kali that straightens it out through realization. The lifestyles of Treta Yuga and Dwapara Yuga are also straightened out in Kali.

This Kali has another special feature. From the beginning of this Kali, God has desired and worked for many times. It may seem why God has this desire. It has been said and convinced that God has no desire. God has the will of what should happen and what should not happen. It is only that God does not need the result of karma. It is enough to understand it like this.

Because the language, dress, lifestyle, and nature of each country are different, each one can only learn to live from a distance from each other in the acquaintance of each other. In this lifestyle of man and woman, many mistakes have been made. That is, when the time of the god and goddess was kept and grown, it was thought that it was God's play. It came to the place where God took it. We could not even recognize whether we are killing God or God is killing us. What else can we do other than fight among ourselves when we have reached like that? Some people should have a desire other than this. That is some ascetics or prophets or people. Otherwise, it cannot be implemented. Because the people as a whole are caught in negative qualities and are looking. Everyone is trying to be stubborn by saying their side. Even if it is like this, we have a favorable situation that this is God's decision. That is, whatever our ancestors worshiped and did and spoiled in whatever way, liberating all of them

We have a task given by God to hand over to the children. We have built such a path.
