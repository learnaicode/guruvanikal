[GV1:P2603] November 1990

[GV1:P2604] There are quite a few people who have experienced and understood the nature of the action taking place in Santhigiri. Even we ourselves have not been able to comprehend why this was made to be done, or to reflect upon the things that have occurred and been done up until this very moment. This is because there is no action in this that resided within us, that we had imagined, or that we had adopted by seeing some other model. It only contains a course of dharma that was continuously modified, instructed, and executed through us at every moment. In short, it contains only those actions that we never had the qualification to perform.

[GV1:P2605] This is an action over which even the Trimurti system, which replaced Manu's dharma that somehow faded away, has no authority. How has this action come to replace the action that denied Vedic rights to the Vaishya and the Shudra? It cannot be explained how. Because this action stands beyond even the laws of Manu. One must remember: the Shudra, who stands lost in the dharmic system constructed by the Vedic religion, is never going to come forward. Why? Wherever there is a festival, Chenda percussion, horse racing, and Padayani, wherever there are traditional dances (Koothu), at the forefront of all these, this Shudra stands ready to dance to the rhythm of the Chenda. Wherever there are Bhadrakali temples, the noisy rituals and administration of them are in his hands. Wherever there is a temple festival (Pooram) with elephants, these elders stand at the front, holding these twenty-four-flamed torches, bringing and spending the oil required for it, eager to maintain their status as elders. When his course of action involves sending his daughters for Thalappoli, exposing that semi-nudity before others, it is not difficult to understand what his intellect is! The justification he gives for encouraging our girls into temple dancing (Dasiyattam) is that it is a dance for the Lord. As if the Lord is that vile! Where has this course of karma led!

[GV1:P2606] To eliminate these obscenities, many great souls have come since the time of Sri Krishna. What did they intellectually turn even Sri Krishna into? The one who demonstrated all the karmas of this world through eighteen chapters and bore all the sorrow within it was depicted as what? He was depicted as the husband of sixteen thousand and eight women. It is the capability of a cunning intellectual stratagem to ensure that the Universal Guru is not accepted as such. As part of that stratagem, even the Gita Govinda has been written and published. We are the masters of a wretched course of karma that proclaims the presence of erudition, Vedanta, and heaven knows what else in all this.

[GV1:P2607] There is a poem by Changampuzha Krishna Pillai. In it, there is a double tragedy. At least four or five men and women will not fail to die in a single day. We are the ones who spread the word that this is good poetry. But what does this poem encourage? The suicide of several individuals. There are numerous cultures around us striving to cultivate such poetic imaginations. Those who watch movies exhibit modernity by killing their grandfathers. We, who depict great souls as evil, are several times worse than evil itself. We are a lineage that has become so corrupted.

[GV1:P2608] Ours is a duty bestowed by God to transform this lineage. We must accomplish it by doing it and making it done. We must understand it, and we must accumulate and provide that understanding for the world. God has given us the message that it is for the children of the world.

[GV1:P2609] There are shortcomings in all the actions we have performed so far. We are always being informed that this is because we haven't accomplished it properly. We are unable to accomplish it. But today, we have been given the courage that we can accomplish it. However, some of us still struggle until dawn and then go to sleep when it dawns. Isn't this the custom of the old Bhadrakali temple festivals? The more you struggle, the more it should remain in your hands. When it is time to reap the benefits, you should not go and fall asleep. We have been informed that if you accomplish it, you will be able to see the fruits of your actions yourself. Whether you do it here or at home, wherever you sit and do it, you must do it by acquiring the necessary merit.

[GV1:P2610] It should not be done with the presumption that one has walked alongside the Guru or interacted closely with him. One should consider that God is making us do it, and that we have been blessed with the fortune to do so. If I have received the opportunity to put a basket of soil, if I have received the opportunity to lay a stone, if I could spend a crore of rupees or even a penny for it, that is my fortune. It is that fortune that should be made useful. We are doing this to eliminate the traditional mistakes made by our ancestors. We respect the greatness that has created an institution for this. Therefore, we should think about what must be done to immerse ourselves in sacrifice as much as possible. Our actions should not be like the backstabbing of politicians.

[GV1:P2611] God has given us the course of action to make sacrifices for this lineage. Every small deed done for it must be performed with determination, strength, and prosperity. Everyone must reflect on how much courage there is in their actions.

[GV1:P2612] This fortune is not something I had known or even thought about; it just came my way as if found by chance from somewhere. What is needed is the determination to gather it up somehow. For that, what is required is not the stubbornness of fighting based on caste, religion, and class, saying "I will show you by winning, I will show you by defeating, I will show you by destroying." Whatever good is done is indeed good. Whether a learned person does it or an unlettered person does it, good is always good. Everyone must strive to undertake actions that are always inherently good.

[GV1:P2613] Guruvani
