[GV4:P0966] February 2004

[GV4:P0967] To evolve out of this familiarity like this, one cannot grow enough in a single lifetime. What is it? Having seen what the origin looks like, without showing in what all ways that origin has occurred, what the light of the times is, in which era I am writing this, for how long it should endure, and what the past times are, one cannot simply move forward. Thus, no one can put in the effort for this. Even if one puts in the effort, the truth cannot be known. When it happens like that, where no one can definitively say anything about anything, the Shaivite, the Shakta, and the Vaishnavite have only completed it from where they reached. Therefore, whatever anyone has said is complete. Then, if one states which of these is conducive to liberation, creating a story there in a confusing way with endless calculations for infinite times, creating a myth there, and behind that myth, having this mentioned king sign it, or having those emperors sign it, writing histories later for however long they ruled at that time, it becomes a practice of writing them down as being everything in this world. Devotees, if necessary, will impart a royal writing tradition to it and thus prepare an action that can be successfully passed off as being everything. So, for this action to be set right for you, ٹ or when our lives, each one of ours, reaches the religious doctrine of some prophet, evaluating where that prophet stands, where other gods and goddesses stand, where those who calculated and pondered over these stars, zodiac signs, and planets stand, where the Saptarshis who thought about it stand, how it is changed from there, identifying that due to this reason this went wrong, and now due to this reason, crafting the matter because of this and maintaining that action as the root of karma is the future liberation. So, at what all times can we correct it, that is the subsequent responsibility. All of those corrections will only happen in the Kali Yuga. Things entitled to the others still remain.

[GV4:P0968] To accomplish all that is mentioned in the sections describing a Goddess, God, Sage, and Ascetic, there needs to be a visionary (deerghadarshi) who can reveal the karma required to unite the soul (jiva) and the supreme (param)—a karma meant for millions of souls who have arrived as the successors of thousands of states, having performed the associated karma and dharma and acquired that merit. Then, who, where, and how will one make such a visionary understand? This is the subsequent essence. Sometimes, individuals other than these renunciates attain all this. When non-renunciates attain this, a sense of triviality often creeps into them. Therefore, since we attained this through the approach of a Guru, what must we show the Guru in return for that compassion? The Guru is one who is born and performs karma across all eras; it is the disciple who must demonstrate how that person should be. That person (the Guru) must not merely state it. Because he (the disciple) must live and demonstrate it according to the words received. Thus, the disciple is the master of that course of karma which is lived and demonstrated. That disciple must similarly impart it to the people and to his contemporaries, and pass it on eternally as the wealth of the disciple. This is one path. Otherwise, there are some who leave, claiming they will reach God directly. The obstacle to reaching God directly is a certain knot that no one knows how to explain.

[GV4:P0969] They are not Gurus who appear in every era. For that, they have been given certain names. By classifying them as Sage, Ascetic, Wise, Devotee, and Actor, and by correcting the rights and wrongs that arise from household duties, with that acquired merit, wherever they stand in the ethereal path, a disciple of the aforementioned Guru must stand there and show the succeeding generations what his Guru and he himself are, and elevate them. Thus, this specific karma cannot be seen at a mere glance. However, it is a state where certain scholars or visionaries have kept speaking, and ultimately, one cannot look back to see what their final karma was. The greatest evidence for this is Christianity, Islam, or the era of Moses. So, by narrating such stories, admitting defeat in all of them, reasoning beyond that, bringing it into elite scholarly logic and complicating it, it stands on a truth that has become so convoluted that no one can extract its essence. Therefore, one cannot even call it the truth. But is it not the truth? Will the elements present in all these karmas not be seen? They will be seen. But it is not like this; Sri Krishna is the greatest among those who came as the rightful heir to this Kali Yuga. After that, the next person who worked for it at least a little was able to approach it only because of a lack of erudition. Various stages were crossed and passed. Having passed them, this person did not know what all these states were. Then, he kept progressing according to what was taught. When he stood in such a formless state, it became impossible to discover the root cause of the many subjects identified as formless. Each of you must contemplate why that is so. If a visionary like Jesus says, "Let the rock split and move," it is definitively stated that it will move. Similarly, there have been times when people followed the doctrines of the God and Goddess and existed only under them. Those times cannot be bypassed either. What will be done then?

[GV4:P0970] It is a specific power that can only be corrected in this Kali. Having demonstrated to the disciple how to implement dharma by performing the karma that is the cause of this Kali, and having observed how the disciple and Guru share it, it is that disciple who reveals the Guru to the world. Therefore, no matter how scholarly such a Guru lineage is, the objective will not be achieved. Vivekananda and numerous others, whoever has come to that plane, none of them will be able to achieve it. This specific karma cannot be accomplished. We require an acquaintance with this karma known as the soul's good deed (Atmasukrutam). In this Guru-disciple relationship, it is not a God and Goddess; where and into what will all these elements transform? Then, what all is contained in this bodily entity existing in the nature of the five senses; in which realm has it formed a base, taken shape, and positioned itself? It cannot be carried forward merely by grasping it as a tool in a karma hidden within the realms of creation, preservation, and destruction. Why is that? There is a place for that creation, preservation, and destruction. Upon reaching there, it changes, and the karma of proceeding into the realm within the Guru belongs to the visionary. That visionary should not speak to you by demonstrating miraculous powers. There should be no illusion or deceit; one must live with the meaning of carrying it forward in every way as the path shown by God. But while living like that, one thing is necessary. We are born today in the era known as Kali. It was mentioned earlier that Kali has a dharma. Not knowing what must happen in that Kali-dharma, we have created a veil using Puranas, epics, and histories to such an extent that we consider Kali to be evil and are unable to grasp what the foundational karma of Kali is. When we say a veil, it also implies the Vedas. So, when reading the Puranas and epics of these Vedas, one wanders into this forest and can never find a way out of it. So, it is not possible here either. Then where is it possible?

[GV4:P0971] There is a guiding principle that says, "Great sages praise what they see on that very day." So, what was done in the dharma of this Kali, what was done in the Chatur Yuga that preceded it, how many karmas have been flawed over countless ages in that Chatur Yuga; in that karma, the foundation of dharma is created there, united with the purusha bearing that karma, by the Guru who says "our lineage and us," or the soul with that personality in the position of the Guru. This can be called creation, preservation, and destruction. But it is impossible to enter and proceed into such matters without someone's help. That is why it is said in many ways that the Guru is Brahma, the Guru is Vishnu, and the Guru is Shiva. So, when this is said in many ways, God is in everything. Then what is the essence! He is in the atom and in the infinite. Thus, the God residing in the atom and the God residing in the infinite are one and the same. Therefore, if seen in the size of an atom, that is enough for me. If seen in the size of a great infinite, that is enough for him. If not, and if seen in the entirety of a sphere, it is complete for him. So, as this passes by, we have degraded to such an extent that we cannot present our own doctrine there, and instead, we act out our karma and perish, confined to the history of an archer-hero of karma who operates by forsaking all love and respect.

[GV4:P0972] Guruvani

[GV4:P0973] March 2004

[GV4:P0974] If this is not to be lost to you, you must attain a Guru before whom your gods, goddesses, sages, ascetics, the wise, devotees, all the seers of the past, and all the prophets who have come thus far, can stand and say, "My work is only up to this point; I entrust it to you, please complete it." Without such a Guru, you will never be able to fulfill this. Once this is understood, the karma that would otherwise take ages to accomplish can be achieved in a single generation—or in two or three generations. However, the guiding Gurus suited for this must demonstrate what karma can be realized through their teachings. One can proceed only after observing the course of karma that has been thoroughly experienced, enriched with love, cultivated into a profound wealth of affection, and entirely dedicated through Ishwara, God, Brahman, and the Supreme Brahman. This karma rests upon the foundational essence of a life that has grasped the complete form of karma through the glory of spiritual austerity (tapas) practiced with enduring dedication, transcending multiple cycles of birth and death across different eras. Thus, if you follow such a Guru, all that has been said will be realized. It is upon the foundation of such a Guru that one can truly say the Guru is Brahma, Shiva, Vishnu, or the entirety of existence. It is then the disciple's duty to reveal to the world how that Guru should be perceived. Therefore, what we need among us is a profound wealth of discipleship that can undertake this karma and impart it accordingly.

[GV4:P0975] In this karma, we are very far away. Therefore, you can ask what is needed. Then, in the period you have received, observing the initial phase, the final phase, and the long duration of a guru-disciple relationship, the state of that long duration must be entrusted to you through words. This is the visionary. If we are fortunate enough to perform actions through such a visionary, it is not just the greatest in the world, but regarding this power known as Brahman, we must understand here a course of action that reaches the authoritative person who can declare whether it exists or not, and demonstrates what is and what is not. A course of action is what needs to be learned here. So this is the dharma of Kali; what that Kalidharma is, that it will be like this, and that these things should be done for it — we are the beginning of a tradition that keeps stating this. We will complete that beginning over several generations. Then for the remaining times, the Guruhood there possesses a personality that must instruct what a human being should do after transforming into a deva, a rishi, a sanyasi, and a jnani. A personality of Guruhood is required. You will be able to pass on such an acquaintance forward. Otherwise, you will never get the opportunity to pass it forward. Then, we must also see this state in our Guru. Only then will you be able to believe and convince others that this Guru lineage is complete. Whoever great person says this, or whichever great people's words you hear, it is fine, not bad, you are just children who say it is good and step aside, but none of you could give birth to children suitable for it. Then, a human being like this cannot step up to any of these aforementioned things. Then who should step up? Coming as a visionary, it is a great remainder of karma that can observe what happened in these three mentioned times. A great karmic remainder it is. Having observed that, it should guide you through it. Guiding you, you must attain a course of action that reaches a state where the entire Grihadharma called Karmakanda, or the entire government, or the entire imperial rule, leaves nothing more to be explained or convinced about. This is what you should get. This is the Guru.

[GV4:P0976] So, such a Guru, if you get one, how will you recognize them, and how will you not recognize them — unless you understand this and move forward, the misfortune of none of you being able to find or accomplish this will remain there like objects sunk in the mud that cannot be lifted up. Then, in some era, by God's will, it must rise up and attain an eternal state in such a way. Therefore, all of this is being explained to you in a way that you can see it with your own eyes. Thus, regarding how we should accomplish this, that path of action is absolutely represented by the Guru. That Guru and how they should be is what the disciple must understand. So, that disciple, having seen these three mentioned times — past, future, and present — that disciple'sٹ wealth of discipleship will turn around. ٹ Having gone like that and returned from it, only an heir of Jivanmukti can show you this path. Otherwise, no matter how much of a Siddha, or how great a Jnani, or how great a Bhakta one claims to be, it is of no use. This right, without obtaining it from Brahman or from one's chosen karma, cannot be exercised by anyone. Therefore, as much as possible, you must understand the importance of this Guru-disciple relationship and what happens when this Guru and disciple unite, and act accordingly. God has given us a medium to do this. Because men and women bear witness to that medium, regarding where and how it should be taken from now on, the largest chain of a beginning is what we have initiated. Now, until that chain is completed, there will be advancements and retreats where no one can do anything. You should think for yourselves whether we need to change this or not. An opportunity to change this, that is Kali. In that Kali, where it is said that everyone can attain Brahmajnana, because this Chandala, Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Shudra do not unite, work together, and complete their lives, everything has been lost through chanting and taking in turns. But in that manner, you cannot proceed anymore. That is why you must hold on to a Guru. When saying Guru — what is it? Through that Guru, whatever God, Goddess, Rishi, Sannyasin, Jnani, Bhakta, and Karmi exist must be obtained through this Guru. Only to the disciple of a Guru who completes it in that way will this karma be useful. So, instead of that, a vicious circle that is of no use to you has gripped you. That is Devas, Goddesses, Madan, Mantramoorthi, and thousands of Yakshis mentioned as such — to cross all this — you will not get anything other than the help of this one Guru. So, for such a Guru to be born near us while we are alive is said to be a great ٹ what we need today is a Guru who seats us nearby and explains, lifting and showing us the antiquity of billions of years.

[GV4:P0977] Therefore, you lack the requisite experience to find such a Guru. What constitutes these obstacles? Consider the caste deity—only individuals belonging to that specific caste will approach that deity. Similarly, only those adhering to specific religious denominations will approach a religious deity. Viewed from this perspective, everything here remains entangled in an incredibly complex labyrinth. Each of you must comprehend this individually; before embarking on your familial life, you must initiate a process to determine what needs to be resolved. To perfect this through repeated practice, and for the sake of our traditional growth and foundational stability, it requires unraveling a vast sphere of action (*karmakanda*) bestowed by God. This alone will suffice to meet the requirements of all of you, as well as these youngsters here who have donned the attire of ascetics.

[GV4:P0978] If you say, "Let it be after this *Kali* (Kaliyuga) passes," you will not get time after *Kali* ends; neither your ancestors got it, nor will the future generations get it. Therefore, a Guru must instruct you to understand the *dharma* (righteous duty) of *Kali* and guide you along that path. That is your greatest wealth. Instead, God is not tied behind the supernatural powers of some other god, goddess, sage, ascetic, or wise man showing off nonsensical asceticism—this is not to say that. The path to attain God must be received from our Guru. This Guru might emerge as the Guru of the world. If four or five such lineages of Gurus are brought together to function unitedly, this will never fail. You should not believe that it will cease to exist either. Therefore, what do we need? What is meant by this path of karma and the path of virtue? Having understood all this, it is said that there is no way for us other than through a soul (*jivatma*) that has acquired this virtue. If all of you do not handle this with this understanding, what I do is completely futile, an absolute waste of labor. It will culminate as the most severe waste of effort. So, that must not happen. You should not present or preach about any such demons, gods, goddesses, outcasts (*chandalas*), or idols to anyone. Only then will this progress. Thus, I have explained this to you as an introduction to conducting the *Guru Puja* (worship of the Guru) here.
