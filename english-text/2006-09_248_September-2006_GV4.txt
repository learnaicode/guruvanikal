[GV4:P1428] September 2006

[GV4:P1429] Right now, theology and all religious doctrines are being taught. Aren't there people who have studied this? Then why are you specifically teaching it now? Is it not because it cannot be put into practice here? How can it be attained by merely speaking of its valor? That is not possible. That great soul must come, grow, and grasp the history of that domain right from its beginning. After doing so, without giving that great soul their due, we cannot move forward. You do not understand this. We are taking responsibility for the errors that have crept into this prophetic religion. Thus, we are taking upon ourselves these two thousand four hundred and forty-four Gurus as well. After taking them, we must make them function. What did you understand from this? This is why seven generations have been mentioned, for them to come and act. Isn't that what I said when speaking about vision (darshan)? The time of all of them has passed. Whose time? That of all the successors. However, in this position, a few years of the Prophet (Nabi) still remain. I do not know if you have understood that. It has been one thousand five hundred years since he arrived. Only a few more days remain for that. The word spoken by that great soul must not be broken. Shouldn't they be capable of ensuring that word is not broken? But if the era of that great soul passes, then you will not be able to grasp it later. What is it? It must be grasped within this time frame itself. When we pray, we will connect with all the traditionally existing constellations of stars for that purpose. Different levels of importance are assigned to each one. After receiving this vision, it is being handed over to a Guru capable of bearing it. We must take it back ourselves. To practically implement it among the people through action, a distinct personality is required. Therefore, we must adopt the karma of this lineage of Gurus who have come and gone until now, right down to the 'Ustad'. Otherwise, if we go the other way, things will get complicated. It is the lack of time to grasp this that stands as a roadblock for Sri Krishna and the aforementioned Prophet. This is not something anyone can deny. It is a historical truth. This is not how the scholars put it. They are talking about some other convoluted theories. We must respect whoever has come and gone, wherever they may have been. We should not deny them. If we are not to deny them, we must embrace their karma. There is no other justification for it. We cannot proceed without clearing the path for them. That operates through the grace of God. So, if we attain a Guru or a guide, only when we reach that point and look from there can we see the rest. Therefore, it is like climbing a mountain. One must climb one, see its end, get down, and likewise climb up and down seven folds to accomplish the task. Narayana Guru gives many examples for this.

[GV4:P1430] 'There is medicine on the mountain, three snakes and

[GV4:P1431] tigers standing guard on both its sides,

[GV4:P1432] an outcaste took and consumed half of it,

[GV4:P1433] it still shines, oh mind, you too take it!'

[GV4:P1434] This is understood. But what to do? It did not come into this doctrine of karma. It is not enough to speak of the doctrine of dharma. The doctrine of karma must come. This is what is called experiential realization. When that realization later enters the realm of utilization, upon attaining it, we can only say that we have attained it. But it becomes true knowledge only when it is utilized for the people. Calling it a vision (darshan), they recite Vedantic hymns—their stance is that the vision is wrong. If this vision is wrong, why did the name 'darshan' even originate? Isn't darshan the dynamic course of action that distinguishes between what is and what is not! Who says it does not exist? What will happen if one speaks all sorts of nonsense, talks of literal knowledge, makes corrections using vowels and consonants, and merely performs intellectual gymnastics within the theory of Yoga? You will feel bad when I say this. It is a painful matter. I am speaking from my knowledge. Hindus have a limitation, Islam has a limitation, and similarly, all these mentioned visions have a limitation. It is when you go there that you see the predicament. No one will understand this even if they hear or see it. This is my experience. A system called fulfillment existed here. The word was received. Certain things must be done. They were done. It is not the Vedantic vision spoken of in logic. Is there any need to conduct mimamsa (critical investigation) here that eliminates dharma by bringing Vedanta into mere alphabetical knowledge? The result of that inner vision is, 'What is this, father, that you have shown me?' If these knowledgeable religious theorists fail to understand this, what can we do? Time is simply being wasted. It is about what needs to be done to prevent that from happening. That is the topic I must tell you along with the beginning of the Kali Yuga. Whether anyone asks or not, I will say this. The reason is that we lack the karma of imparting and absorbing love in the act of communicating face-to-face. Who are we telling this to? Who is being made to hear this? What is being said here? Aren't all these theorists speaking as if they came to praise and glorify a mistake? Do not adopt that theoretical approach. That is all I have to say to the people of this world. I have nothing else to say. Who am I to tell? I am merely conveying to you the sorrow that I have experienced.

[GV4:P1435] Disciple: We have only one religion. The religion of love. We cannot attain salvation through any other religion.

[GV4:P1436] Guru: It will never be possible. I have nothing to say to those who think it is possible. We have insulted the Gurus who showed us what that love is. Isn't this the truth? Now, if anyone has anything to say, let them say it. This is my experience that I am sharing. Then, God assigned a specific time period to each one. That is what we know now. Two thousand for one, five thousand for another, ten thousand for yet another—we do not receive any knowledge beyond what is said in this manner.

[GV4:P1437] Guruvani
