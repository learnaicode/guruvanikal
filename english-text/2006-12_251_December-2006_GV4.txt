[GV4:P1479] December 2006

[GV4:P1480] There is a custom of hanging down south. You haven't heard of it, right? When a king was traveling along the way, a woman was standing there displaying excellent singing, dancing, and performance. Thinking she possessed artistic talent, he called her, took her with him, and taught her everything necessary. He even married her himself. He married her, and eventually, he set out to tour the country. He went out several times. During one of these trips, when they reached a certain place, she asked, "Does that hang without being tied, or is it tied and hanging?" Which one is it? She asked this when they arrived beneath a tree called 'Aatuvanchi', where she used to play. It produces a stem about a foot long. You know the mango stalk? Like that, a very long stalk emerges. It is called Aatuvanchi. At the end of it, there will be a fruit. It was while she was playing beneath it that the king had taken her away. When she came riding an elephant to see the country, she asked the king whether it was tied up or if it naturally grew that way. "Is it tied and hanging or hanging without being tied?" He dropped her off there and departed, telling her to return only after learning whether it hangs tied or untied. Instructing her to observe it properly, he left her there and went away. Isn't our story similar to this?

[GV4:P1481] There is such a profound error residing within us. We must see who is the inheritor of this Yuga Dharma. That is why it was said — we need not pursue any of the others. How many acharyas will it take to resolve this — observe this. The dharma of a yuga, then the dharma of a chatur-yuga, and thus how many dharmas of chatur-yugas — a Manvantara. The reason is that this matter was not clearly stated. Simply tallying numbers and altering letters will not work. It must be resolved right there. Then what is this Yuga Dharma? The author of the Gita has stated, 'Kali is in the Shudra'. Then who is this Shudra? Anyone who is not a Brahma-jnani (knower of Brahman) is indeed a Shudra. So it means this Shudra must become a Brahma-jnani. Isn't it? That is precisely why Krishna was brought and placed in your lap. You did not accept him. You trampled and suppressed him. Therefore, this could not be fulfilled. These devas and deceivers standing together — who are these deceivers? That is, Sri Krishna, having labored so hard and waited for ages for us, is demonstrating all his accomplishments to Arjuna. He bestowed upon Arjuna the mastery of weapons. After that, what is witnessed upon reaching there? "Look at this, Arjuna; instead of ruining things by practicing yoga as preached, look at this," Sri Krishna is saying. He looked and saw, right? He saw, accepted it, and took everyone along. Then the running commenced with them once again. Right? Running to attain the longevity of ages. What is meant by the longevity of ages is, suppose we discovered a time-span of fifty thousand years or a crore years; can we live that long and then depart? Then aren't we living without truly having lived? That is the truth. Until that truth is perceived, is it acceptable to simply proclaim this as the truth? A far-sight (vision) of these fifty thousand years and a lakh years was established: agreed. But can we proceed by claiming there is no such far-sight?

[GV4:P1482] Disciple: No.

[GV4:P1483] Guru: Then shouldn't the remainder of what exists be seen? Even today, the Christians do not perceive that era. Do we not harbor the ignorance that teaches that by reasserting him as 'beyond time', he washed away the sins? That is, they declared this and committed murder. Having killed, all those who orchestrated the killing died. After they passed away, it is being preached, using his very religion, that the Lord washed away all these sins. What kind of teaching is this? Then they bear no sin, do they? Aren't we all perfectly pure? Thus, there is a profound ignorance in teaching religious doctrines in this manner.

[GV4:P1484] Disciple: It exists in all religions.

[GV4:P1485] Guru: Yes. So, after saying this, the people who have started saying Pentecost at the end, what is it - they say Hallelujah, Hallelujah. They are speaking that language. So, the question was whether it is correct to say that they are speaking in tongues. This was not said to insult anyone. Isn't this our situation? As long as we do not understand that it is not like that, we will keep saying the same thing. So, we cannot just talk about these four heavens and seven heavens, and talk only about this solar system and move on. Understood? There are so many solar systems. When we say galaxies, aren't they clusters? Where will the calculation of stars, which is being tallied here based on the calculation of a total of twenty-seven stars, lead to? So, the change of one solar system itself is the change of many solar systems. The issue is that this is not understood this way. We see this revolving like a earth, sun, and moon. But this revolving goes from days to years to ages - no need to go to ages. Don't we say a Jupiter cycle? This Jupiter revolves and comes back in twelve years. Isn't our age being acquired? Do you want to know where we have reached in its acquired karma? This is not to say it doesn't exist. It certainly exists. If everything exists, we should go there and correct even the philosophy. Otherwise, there is no justification for this. It's not about taking a bath after preaching Vedanta. We are empty in the aspect of knowledge. Then, one should not say that what exists does not exist. If you say 'emptiness', you shouldn't say that. That is why it is said 'everything exists, the peak of philosophy'. If it exists like this, where does it stand? Shouldn't we start from where it stands? Without seeing where it stands, from where will we start? This is how we are. So, we haven't even been told how long a solar system, where this dharma of ages and Jupiter cycles has increased, will last. So many ages have been counted; this solar system - shouldn't it burn out? What is said is that the calculation of this burning out over time has not been told. This is where we stand. Everyone became like this. You should not move forward saying that what these great people said does not exist, nor should you go saying that there is only sorrow in your life. There is sorrow in everyone's life. If you abandon the dharma that can take this sorrow as happiness and say that the path of karma based on your whimsical imaginations is right, you will be ruined. This is what you don't understand. Do we need this or not? My father, my father's father - we will see what condition controlled them. If you say this doesn't exist and say it is where mother is, where father is, it won't work; father should be where mother is, and mother should be where father is. Wherever those life-givers stand, that must be broken out. This is 'karma became bindings for man'. Then who is man? The soul. 'Only when bindings are cut will liberation come for sure'. 'Liberation' is spoken of in different times. So, this Guru and the disciple of the Guru must be the inheritors of the dharma that satisfies these times. At least during our lifetime, we should be able to understand what this means. After that, children, I can live seeing you. Who should say it? A father should say it to his children. Then what should the mother say? You should see the karma of the father's clear mind corresponding to the calculation of the sky.
