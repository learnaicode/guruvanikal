English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

May 1992

Since the time people started living in the world, there has been a way of distinguishing between good and bad.

What do we mean by good?

When some people sing, it will be sweet. That is missing in the singing of others. Street children will sing more sweetly than some musicians. If there is sweetness of voice, no one will feel offended even if they get angry. The Tamil language is sweet. Listening to Tamil songs increases devotion.

Since ancient times, attempts have been made to distinguish between good and bad. Compared to the time when this earth and its creatures emerged, human life has only been a very short period. If we examine the course of time, we can see that a much higher culture existed in many periods than what exists now. Culture may change with the change of time. Environment can also be an indicator of culture. A man's qualities change according to the circumstances he finds himself in. But there is a culture that does not change according to time, place, and circumstances. That is the real culture. That culture is capable of transforming the mind in present life. Mental transformation can come with money. It can also come through knowledge and habit.

You should think about why you come and go to the ashram. Sometimes there may be fifty ashrams among fifty lakh people. The people who come and go to these ashrams are only a very small percentage. That too, keeping in mind their needs according to their tendencies. Some come to be leaders, some to steal. Others may come to be fools.

We should think about the reason why each tendency and the matching according to the tendency come together. Only those who are very careful will understand this. Each place has a different type of culture. The whole country desires good. The government also desires good. But good does not arrive sufficiently. However, it can be made good by rubbing and rubbing. Like smoothing a rough stone by rubbing it.

Human life is difficult in every way. Living according to ashram dharma is even more difficult. The five dharmas were mentioned earlier. From where should it become right for ashram life? There are two or four types of ashram life. Those who come to the ashram suffering without health, without even a means for a meal. Those who come to the ashram with food but desiring something else and not being successful. There are those who grow a beard, sit around, and do many times more than what a sanyasi does. But others do not see any of this. They don't see it either.

But we who see and understand all this should have a thought. Due to circumstances, even very great people go wrong. We should be able to overcome the circumstances that lead to such mistakes and accept only what is suitable for ashram life. None of these are blessings that can be achieved so quickly. If there is a perfect sense of purpose in ashram life, if there is a determination to act according to the goal no matter what happens, even if there is nothing to do, one will not act and become empty. It takes very little time to beat something out. But that time is not enough to nurture it.

If hunger is destroyed by detachment, it will become a devil. We must understand where this culture of detachment was passed on from. There is suffering other than all this. The life of lying on the street, suffering, embracing all sorrows, and accepting the earth as one's ancestral home. That is sacrifice. The sacrificer

He considers a life of hardship as comfortable. In ashram life, what one should desire and embrace is sacrifice.

What should be the nature of sacrifice? There are people who endure sacrifice to buy a cow and those who endure sacrifice to buy a bicycle. If so, what a child endures by planting a banana tree and watering it is also a sacrifice. Now another aspect. Haven't you seen slaughtering by saying 'Bismillah'? Haven't you seen the 'Aham Brahmasmi's? Everyone else is a devil! Thus incomplete, arrogant and blind actions. It's all knowledge.

Contrary to all this, a collective effort is required to achieve the perfection of knowledge. A caste, religious or national perspective is not enough for that. A collective effort, life itself, accumulating all ideas, is needed. We can stay in the ashram only if we have the knowledge to think according to our ability. When working sincerely, if four people criticize, there are those who drop it and leave. That is where purity of action should come. Our actions should not cause pain to others. I, who lived for the movement for seventeen years, was thrown out without even being given a meal of rice gruel. But I could not say a word of blame about them. Either my action lacked strength. Or if it has strength, the assurance that it will come.

'Jvaramatri vishrathikondumoo- nnaritam velakal cheythumarthiyo?' as asked in Anuthapadasakam, I had this assurance. I tested whether ash could cure illness. It worked. I realized it at the age of thirty-two. All physical ailments were cured by the ash here.

How to achieve peace with this life? There can be peace for all our emotions. It is then that one becomes ready to serve anyone and has a dedicated attitude. The form of such a person can be seen in everything. The ashram will grow and grow and become only him. This is how it should be targeted. One should come to the ashram with such high thinking. To do what is needed in the ashram, to ensure that no one makes a mistake, many such blessings will come. If one thinks that I am staying in the ashram to practice and make others practice dharma, one will not be able to make a mistake. Even if it is made with difficulty, there should be a vessel to keep it in the heart.

What we have got now is the result of some good deed done over lifetimes. The knowledge to preserve this good deed is what must be acquired. Only by doing something and as a result of some goodness can one reach here. This place can also tear away goodness in a moment. Remembering that the next moment that action is not for us, we must constantly perform actions. No matter how hard it is, if there is an interest in doing good, this place can destroy all people. Loving humans should be with ability.

There should always be a thought about what one can do as far as one understands. If you are attentive, there will always be a dignity in the action you do. There will be a humility in dealing with others. If jealousy cannot be completely abandoned, there will at least be an obstinacy for it. No one can stay here with love for their home and relatives. No matter how many days you stay here, it should be with sincerity and true love. It is wrong to pretend to be sincere and to maintain a cunning love. If there is love for something, it must be innocent. Innocent love is equal to supreme love.

Regarding those with vision, special care is needed in this matter. Even if a slight misfortune affects those with vision, it will affect the entire ashram.

Small children and mothers come here, don't they? It is difficult to reform mothers. One should only get close to mothers as much as necessary. If one gets closer than necessary, bad influences might spread. This is an institution. This is not just a place to accept sannyasa. There is a shortcoming among us that we are unable to sufficiently respect and understand even the spiritual experiences here. What is the tradition of this ignorance?

Shubhavani
