[GV3:P1115] February 2001

[GV3:P1116] This memorandum has been noted many times in various ways. This occasion is used to note this so that someday the people will be convinced of it. This situation, that is, the temporal defect of the world, has been experienced by the people of the earth for many ages. Those who are supposed to handle the masses through governance are acting entirely without the people's knowledge - acting completely like a children's play, just like kids playing with dirt, making mud pies, and walking away fighting when they get angry. As an example of this, these seventeen lakh years can be counted. All these rulers are lying enslaved to this mud-pie making. Specifically, within the last five thousand two hundred years, it is this trumpet of adharma that marked the beginning of India's defeat. It is enough to mention it as belonging to this yuga. Do not say it belongs to before that. The best example is the frenzied antics of the gods. It is unknown how many great souls this destructive action has sacrificed to death. People of the world or governments do not know how many emperors who desired to rule for the sake of truth were killed under the influence of such destructive actions.

[GV3:P1117] Because it is not known that this defeat is in India, the most recent examples are the verbal tricks wielded with the arrogance of always raising and finding comfort in the opportunism of a bygone goodness of the Indians. No one even thinks about the disasters this has brought to the world. Especially since the birth of Sri Krishna, by this divine will, it is a great opportunity for the Shudra class to have some goodness. Here, attempts were made to rule and make others rule, to know and make others know, and to impart exemplary knowledge to the people in many ways. However, without understanding that this is God's mercy, these ruling lords are maintaining a familiarity similar to parents' affection yielding to the bad behavior of children, merely yielding to the childish insistence of kids. And that too, entangled in the thread of deceitful arrogance linked by gods and goddesses and their followers, the whole world is joyously heading towards ruin like the resurrection of winged termites. If all people living today do not understand this, it will be paving the path of violence towards all living beings. Today's various governments, especially those who hold the umbrella for useless knowledge seen today, are rulers of mimicry who submit to the arrogance of American supremacy holding the immortality of being a trickster. It is good for the people to know that this is the underworld job of ignorant governments who give glory to soldiers claiming they attained heroic martyrdom during times of destruction, dancing to the tune of a madness that not even a child in the world should know, make known, or is unable to know.

[GV3:P1118] To inform this, many mediums were used. But like dogs barking with a mouthful when the moon rises, these two-legged dogs bark and stop, but they do not understand. That is why the halls of intellect flow today, giving this award and that award to that hall of intellect and destroying it. This is seeing the fireworks of mindless donkeys, the deaf applauding, unable to hear the sound, winning and playing the game of worthlessness. Destroying it by giving it an award. To bring a change to this, God for our sake within just a hundred years, the ignorant pranksters who tried to make a name as British or other paramount emperors, having played making mud pies as Eastern and Western in various ways, without recognizing the good and bad of living beings, what all have they fought and shown in this world! For this recent downfall, the British who ruled the empire where the sun never sets are first-rate experiential subjects. Following that, the vile people they rejected as useless assumed the role of world police, spread all kinds of vileness, and as the greatest karmic culprits fighting life, have lost even the life essence of seed varieties. The heavenly fools who do not see this are turning into the vile act of feeding the thief, making money by selling the country. All these masquerades shown are strategic assemblages unable to understand that this is purely due to not respecting God's will. The rulers of the world better know that unless you put an end to this, you will not be able to do good to a single living being. All the people trying to hold the helm of the country have tried to inform this. But they pretend not to see. The knowledge they have acquired is such that one could say they neither knew nor thought about it. What can be done with them? Even God has no choice but to accept defeat. They are acting entirely as priests akin to winged termites.

[GV3:P1119] Unless you remove this hardship of the world, a karmic defect will occur that will make it impossible to honor the coming Satya Yuga. Be mindful.

[GV3:P1120] Guruvani

[GV3:P1121] March 2001

[GV3:P1122] None of the ashrams usually have the facility to casually come and go or stay for two to three days. It is available here. If we do not value this, there is no use for it. None of us came here having learned everything. It became like this through the passage of time and familiarity. There was a time when there were no clothes, no languages, and no education. People lived during those times too. Over time, various reforms came about. No one thinks about whether these reforms are flawed or if this is the only thing we need. If someone is seen growing their hair, everyone will do the same. They will not think about why it is done. As movies change, fashion will also change.

[GV3:P1123] Reforms should be in a way that brings us goodness. Otherwise, they will cause harm. There are many reforms in the ashram. Those without knowledge will not understand them. One learns to pray through the Bhajan Math and the temple. It can be understood through the Guru. Muslims and Christians have a gradual system like this. That is, Thaikkavu, Mosque, and Mecca. For Christians, it is the house of devotion (Vanakkamasappura), Church, and Metropolitan. Those who understand this would have thought about all its aspects right when they arrive at the ashram.

[GV3:P1124] If two youths from today's society come here, they will show off the market fashion, overflowing with beauty. If rich people come, they will show the arrogance of wealth. Other ashrams will not allow any of this to be shown. But here, all this is permitted. Isn't this a reform? If such men and women stay here for two days, this behavior will leave them without them even realizing it.

[GV3:P1125] A girl who walks around wearing the clothes provided by her father does not think that if she loses her father, she will not have them. Can girls make a firm decision that "I will not let anyone see my mischief"? Girls should think about this when they come to the ashram. There are facilities here for girls to come and stay without anyone's companionship. That is the greatest reform.

[GV3:P1126] So what should you do? You should think and learn about the fact that a facility, which your lineage did not get, has been established here. No one got the facility to walk on this path. So what could that be? This is a reform. How was an opportunity created for everyone to live like this, regardless of caste, religion, age, or gender? This is a wondrous event that exists nowhere else. It is clearing a new path by changing the old story.

[GV3:P1127] The time when asceticism meant going to the mountaintop is over. Asceticism is needed inside our homes and in our minds. There is a flaw in today's society. Since women are not allowed to enter the mosque, if their children commit any vile act, they will be found there. Women lack culture. They have love. But broad-minded love does not come. The lack of culture in women is another reason for the loss of culture from society. Those without culture will eventually move away from places where there is culture, after a certain point.

[GV3:P1128] Those staying in the ashram should think about how to behave with those coming to the ashram. They should think about how to live in the ashram. It may happen that acquaintances from a time when one lived a bad life might arrive here at some point. The goodness of the ashram should be shown to them.

[GV3:P1129] God has given such an opportunity as a result of penance done over many lifetimes. Even if one cannot do anything good here, there should always be a thought to at least not spread evil. When returning after visiting the ashram, one should accept at least one word and implement it. Then there might be opposition from home. The stance of the house might be totally the opposite. It doesn't matter. It will be helpful to those who support it. Even if nothing works out, there should be a resolution that "if I ever build a house, I will keep this word true."

[GV3:P1130] The discipline of the ashram touches the heart and provides experience. A sorcerer will come here, a wicked person will come, a good person will come, and one with a mix of good and bad will come. Whoever comes, study him. There is a method for that. One should pay attention to that condition. There should be devotion with attention. Otherwise, there is nothing special. Study how he is and how to behave with him. Whether it is a man or a woman, whoever comes should be studied. A ninety-year-old may not have the knowledge of an eight-year-old. An eight-year-old might have the humility of a ninety-year-old. A half-gray-haired mother will show humility and politeness. One must see what they have hidden inside. Those standing here must fully understand the character of people from eight to ninety years old. Not just the bad side, but all sides. Girls should have this memory strongly. Boys, who feel that they must get an opportunity, that they should do things in a certain way, and accomplish at least some things—without this certainty, they cannot do anything.

[GV3:P1131] Those who go outside the house and talk about household matters cannot be kept in the ashram. This is their innate tendency. This tendency must be guarded against. There must always be a determination that this good opportunity obtained will not be wasted, and that our attention will eradicate the flaws of our ancestors. The humility to behave happily with everyone, and the firm resolve not to be enslaved by any other emotion—this much is enough; it will constantly nurture us. There is plenty of proof for this in this ashram.

[GV3:P1132] Guruvani
