Guruvani December 1994

I have not failed to see what the Acharyas have earned for the world. But some of them, at the time of finally leaving the body, told stories of wonder, and introduced practices like jumping into the river and merging in the act of abandoning the body, or wrapping the body tightly in a thousand cubits of cloth and floating it in the river Ganges. Among another caste of sannyasins, there is a practice of committing suicide by jumping into a gorge. Thus, our tradition has operated with many deceptions of wonders hidden in karma. You should think about what can be done to rectify a lack of distinction that has flowed and perished in this lack of story. I am telling you one of my experiences. It took many years to realize that the aforementioned systems are today. What is this that is seen, what is said, where will it reach if it goes on like this - in a state of helplessness. But I did not stop my search. At one point, it was even said that I was beaten and chased away from Varkala. There was also a group to say that I went with two girls and was never seen again. So there was a time when they made me happy by saying such things. I left Varkala according to the notice that I should not sit where two people could see me.

From there I went to a house in Manavoor. The people of that house would always come and call me. Two people I knew also came with me. Do not sit here, it is dangerous if you sit here

It changed when I heard the disembodied voice. The people who came with me dropped me off at Mannamboor. There, someone came with a car to pick me up. I went to Paravur with him.

When I reached there, I saw the celebrations and activities of a death anniversary. A ghost came there. After seeing its activities, I returned to Mannamboor the next day. When I went back, I got a good reception. This house is at the entrance of a fish market. The shops and everything else there belong to this family. The people in those shops were waiting with sticks, logs, and weapons. The head of the house, perhaps feeling something then, told me: 'Swami, don't stay here now. You should leave now. I will explain things later.' He had brought me forward. And I left. At that time, they were standing there, raising their sticks and logs, shouting slogans. Due to God's love, nothing happened there. I didn't know why all these preparations were made. I left without knowing. I understood the matter only later.

Because the mother in that house was cured of her illness and their daughter's danger was averted, they used to come to me often. I wrote them a letter and sent it. 'Child, you are staying in a house where you live respectably. Don't forget that... Whatever the relationship between you two is, let it be, I will talk and set it right.' Seeing this, the mother and father thought that I was interfering in a way that would spoil their child. I had sent this letter four years before I went to that house. It was a letter sent after doing a ritual for them. It was this letter that they received when I went there four years later. That's when they felt love for me - as mentioned earlier, they felt like dealing with me properly.

My experience is always like this. Wherever I have gone, I have had to live as an object of ridicule. Even after six or seven years of coming here to Pothencode, I had such an experience. Many patients came here, were treated (only with holy ash, holy water, and prayer), and were getting cured. Among them, a woman was brought here by her husband for the cure of mental illness, and after being cured, she was taken home. After a few days, that woman came to the ashram with a relative of hers, wishing to stay for two days. The next day, I looked and didn't see her. She hadn't gone anywhere else. She was inside the well.

Thus, incidents have emerged in life and danced a destructive dance. But all this was not something a human being could bear. That it passed is only by the grace of God. That in every state - by state I mean the place a sannyasi or a brahmachari should reach and attain - is being attained state by state. How that is, is that according to the calculations of the scientists who are contained in this world, where atomic science reaches - whether they call it energy, or power, or the glory of nature, or whatever name they call it - the nature of life and soul evolving through the covering of the five elements that we are experiencing daily today, by the knowledge of the five senses, by the recognition of the five sheaths. No other science is adequate for this except the help that has worked for many past lives to put all this together. I am informing you of this because it is a truth I have experienced. There will be an opportunity to know what a large part of the experienced truth is. Otherwise, I cannot say that these are the negative aspects resulting from my following some sannyasi, siddha, or god.

We have a tradition where we have been able to see up to the Trinity system as a visible dharma. You can believe that it exists. Because we cannot just throw away the actions done and caused to be done by our ancestors, can we? That must come. And it must be grasped along with it. That is how we must proceed. But when you grasp it, you must have an understanding.

What is that knowledge? When a god, goddess, sage, ascetic, wise person, devotee, or a person of action comes, if there is no lineage of gurus to teach and make them experience eternal peace, you should not go that way. If you go, you will be destroyed. I have the evidence of thousands of experiences for this. Since that evidence is mine alone, I don't care what anyone says. I am not someone who has accepted the traditions I grew up in. I walked through all of that at first. Then I understood as much as its knowledge, and by God's grace, I got that knowledge. Again, it took years to understand its various aspects. Only after that did I want to say something to the people. It's only been ten or twelve years since I started talking.

How should you maintain this tradition? Whatever devil, god, goddess, sage, ascetic, or wise person has entered your path of action so far, all of them will be given to you in the glory called Guru, in the animate and inanimate, in form and formless, in light. Or you will be made to experience it in the Guru's action. Otherwise, you have received the tradition of a theory that is taught in an orderly manner. When you merge into this truth of experience, you must be careful about one thing. All you have to suffer, love, and respect is sacrifice. Otherwise, you have not been given wealth and other abilities to be happy. May God himself give you wealth and ability. Let the lucky ones experience it. Or let the lucky lineage of children experience it. Wealth and ability should not be lost in ignorance.

If you catch a guru, that guru should be able to tell you from the devil to the city of vampires (Narayana Guru's vision stops here) and beyond that, what is the nature of the trinity, in which sky they stand, which realm is their place. Otherwise, you will fall into trouble by getting caught up in the uproar of miracles and thousands of tricks, and in the action and inaction of royal people. Living as subjects of all this, you do not know that you are being pushed into the deep abyss of action to capture a poor section of people who are the owners of some knowledge and make them fight each other. This is my knowledge experienced. You who believe here have that evidence. Whatever devil, god, or goddess comes before you, if you tell them to get out, they will go. Or if you say 'you will burn' two or three times, it will burn. That is your Guru's power. It will burn in the name of the Guru. With that strength, you will be able to catch up. Then, through the action of this path of dharma, earning merit, a complete richness that you must take together is ready before you. You must view it broadly and pass it on to the children who have reached the action. That should be the ultimate goal of life.

We know thousands of sacrificial methods. Thinking that the Ashwamedha Yaga can be completed by capturing its karmic flaws is ignorance and the path of destruction. It is a path that sows destruction and reaps destruction. When accepting a Guru's path, you must fully believe in it. Do not catch it without substance and put it in your mind. You must be able to change from that way, no matter how hard it is. Sitting in front of the god strengthened by caste and religion, chanting mantras and performing satyagraha, nothing is going to happen. And it hasn't happened either. Those who live according to this must also make others live according to it. This is the path God has given you to get out forever from the abyss of ignorance in which your father and mother were caught. I hope you will be able to capture a great theory by traveling only on that path. For that, it is most appropriate to worship it as Pournami.

Guruvani
