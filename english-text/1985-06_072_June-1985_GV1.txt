[GV1:P1523] June 1985

[GV1:P1524] You have been conducting Pournami prayers here for quite some time. You are doing it even more intensely than the temple traditions. However, you have a shortcoming. That shortcoming is incompatible with our present desire. That shortcoming did not occur by itself; it was brought about. This is due to our fundamental ignorance. I have said before that only ascetics (sanyasis) observed the Pournami vow (vrata). They did this because the course of karma would be completely severed. But you have not understood that. You only possess the knowledge that follows certain poets who have prescribed a specific result for each vow. You must understand three things. First, how should our belief be recorded? Second, is this belief meant to eliminate our course of karma, or is it to ward off some other sinful power? Third, are you doing it merely as an offering (nercha)?

[GV1:P1525] Among us, the worship of God is unknown. We only know offerings (nercha). "I shall offer this to that place for this purpose"—such things are more prominently reflected among us. This was sufficient in the Treta and Dvapara Yugas. This is because, at that time, they had not attained the aspect called liberation for the soul (jivan mukti). No matter how high they rose, humans would only reach the position of a Deva. Therefore, it was not necessary then. It is not like that in Kali Yuga. We have assumed that nothing good will happen in the Kali age. However, we have been told that Kali is the most suitable time for everything. It is said that in Kali Yuga, the Shudra rules. 'Shudra' does not denote caste. Even within a single household, there are people of four castes: Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Shudra. If we have ten members in our family, all four of these temperaments can be seen among them. Today, in our country, it has not been introduced in that manner. Our ancestors or statesmen introduced caste based on Varnashrama Dharma. Believing that there is no existence without caste, each of us is dealing with a situation where we live tied solely to that caste, enslaved by its specific karma or associated emotions. This must change from here. We who live in Kali Yuga must contemplate and understand the Dharma of Kali. The Dharma of Kali may sometimes be mentioned in the Puranas, Vedas, or Upanishads. Someone might have written it down. However, you will not be able to observe any of this. You simply cannot adopt the methods of the authors of these Puranas today. Furthermore, such a highly systemic (sastriya) life will not be practical for you today. While suffering from various such incapacities, you will not be able to complete these tasks. But we have an easier way.

[GV1:P1526] Since the beginning of the world, that is, in this creation of Brahma (Brahmasrushti), a deluge (pralaya) occurs after the period of fourteen Manus. We do not even know what the duration is within that deluge. In the period called fourteen Manvantaras—the time of one Manu is seventy-one Chaturyugas. One Chaturyuga itself is twelve thousand divine years. A single divine year equals three hundred and sixty-five human years. In this, how much of a lifespan do we possess?

[GV1:P1527] Although human lifespan is said to be one hundred and twenty years, is everyone able to reach that? Within this period, we must understand every kind of weight in the totality of our merits and sins. We do not even know what a life is. Our ancestors did not obtain that knowledge either. We have the habit and experience of living under the assumption that the Kali age is good for nothing. It is merely hearsay knowledge. We have no real information about it. In truth, that is not the case. Kali is the most suitable time for us to seek merit (punya). It is also the most beneficial time for women. Poets and others speak about women. Each of you should introspect and see how much truth you can speak in your life. In today's life, there are hardly any who speak the truth. The reason for this is the disorder that has crept into you due to habit. This habit exists because ignorant mothers raising children has continued as a lineage. They have only one thought: one child must be married off, and another must be brought in through marriage. They are raising children in such a state of frenzy. They want to show, "This is who I am." They are satisfied if they have ten children and the respective affairs are taken care of. Other than that, the capacity to think about what merit they need, why they did all this, or what must be done to prevent this wretched behavior of theirs from repeating in their children, is completely absent in their lives. Therefore, even if such knowledge is spoken of, you will not understand it.

[GV1:P1528] I have already told you that there is an easy way for you now. We have been informed that once the world is created, a nature that subverts the will of Brahma could also occur in the world. To explain how that happens, after great Acharyas (teachers) were born and made their lives blessed, their successors came and, like watching a dance without knowing the story, did many things, as a result of which that history had to be rewritten. That is the reality. If a couple of hundred years pass like this, we will drift away from history in a way that we cannot even imagine.

[GV1:P1529] The information is that twenty-five *Chaturyugas* have passed since such a thing happened in the world. A scenario where Brahma desired to bring about a change has been bestowed upon us in accordance with that divine will (*Brahma-nishchayam*). What this means is that, just as we do everything possible to raise our little children, for us who know nothing and lack scientific awareness or anything else, it is as if the will of Brahma has waited and provided this for us. While we receive it, you must understand this subject thoroughly. But you are not understanding this. There are many young people right here. Although they reside here, a conscious action corresponding to this does not arise in them. That does not mean you are committing a mistake. Because you lack the knowledge, you are unable to consciously grasp it and bring it into practice. It is the same with those who come and go here. This is the easiest era for you to change from this inherent nature. A path has been created here for you to receive knowledge from Brahma for this transformation.

[GV1:P1530] This path is not one that has existed before. Through this path, we are growing as a *Guruparampara* (lineage of gurus). This is a great fortune for us. When we got the opportunity to be told this, every Acharya came and said, "Children; you must not go wrong!" This is the greatest thing we can attain in this world. It has been said that if you do not preserve this properly, after two generations have been careful, the third generation will destroy it, if you do not guide it straight.

[GV1:P1531] This is not *Sannyasa* (asceticism). It is not a knowledge that we discovered and brought forth. We did not set out on our own to resolve some other mistake. Unknowingly, we, who were incapable, somehow fell into this light. After falling, when we lay there and flailed our arms and legs, each of these things was taught to us. You more or less know what is meant by flailing arms and legs. The times when I suffered hardships. What all insults I heard; I heard unbearable abuses. Most people know all this. They also know that none of the allegations heard were true. Today, that has become clear. So, it is not just about hearing abuses; the hardship endured for seventeen years is not small. It is the tenth year since understanding this. This has been received in the name of a great fortune. Even after being born and dying for aeons, there is no liberation for this except that husband, wife, children, and wealth will keep turning like a cartwheel. Because life revolves like this along with the world. But today it is not like that; since it is a *Guruparampara* that lays the foundation for that will of Brahma, we can achieve this.

[GV1:P1532] You—as the ones who are to perform the primal duty (*Adikarma*)—Brahma Himself has brought you here. Like a blooming garden in a desert, this fortune has been given to you who possess no knowledge. This is only something that you can actively practice and achieve. You do not have to observe numerous fasts (*vratas*) or rituals for this. Sunday fast, Monday fast, Pradosha fast, Ekadashi fast—after observing numerous such fasts across countless lifetimes, the fast one must finally undertake is the Pournami fast. It is easy for you. With the Pournami fast, all other fasts can be avoided. The Pournami fast is the vow that makes one eligible for liberation (*Mukti*). You have been blessed with the fortune to observe the ultimate fast right at the beginning. It is the will of Brahma indeed that provided an opportunity for this.

[GV1:P1533] Guru Vani
