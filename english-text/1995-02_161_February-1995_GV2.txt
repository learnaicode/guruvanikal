February 1995

Question: Guru! What is the difference between a Deeksha name and the advice given by a Sanyasi?

Guru: After a Brahmachari practices Brahmacharya for twelve years, they will become aligned with either Shaktheyam, Shaivam, or Vaishnavam. Some people mix all three and worship. Once a child is born, among Hindus, the traditions of the Shodasha system are followed. The Shodasha system is a principle where rituals are performed in seventeen stages from birth to death. This involves advising a name in the tradition of some gods or goddesses. This is called Upanayanam. While performing Upanayanam, the Surya Gayatri is chanted.

There is the course of karma conceived as Nanmukhan and the Trimurthis. When Subrahmanya got angry, he pinched off one of Nanmukhan's heads! Subrahmanya is sometimes called 'Shubhramaniyan' in Tamil. Shubhramaniyan is one who has turned his virility into a bead. The premise is that by eating leafy vegetables, using kayakalpas within twelve years, and practicing alchemy, just as mercury is solidified, this virility is made exactly like a bead. Subrahmanya's guru is Bhogar, isn't he? In this same yogic practice, the virility

Bhogar is the one who made the bead. Even today, there are ascetics who believe that through this alchemical practice, the bodily functions reached the five sheaths (Panchakoshas) through the five senses and the five elements (Panchabhootas), and the individual soul (Jivatma) united with the Supreme Soul (Paramatma). Those who find bliss in this by citing a yogic calculation (the calculation of alchemical practice) of seven thousand for Bhogar and twelve thousand for Agastya are the ones standing behind this sensory function. As an illustration of this, there is a story about Bhogar, which explains how he got the name Bhogar by turning his semen into a bead.*

Let's take the aforementioned Satyathmanan. Satyathmanan means the one who follows the truth. Satyathmanan saw that everything that continues through the Manvantaras comes to him. * The story is elsewhere.

When looking at it this way, he sees himself as everything. The tradition of knowledge, which is the five sheaths in the evolution of the five elements and the evolution of the five senses, comes to him. What Satyathmanan saw in this way is not wrong, it is correct. However, because he did not consider the chronology of the Yuga Dharmas, he saw the visionaries, gurus, and prophetic figures who had come from the beginning up to him, and then everything else, coming to him. When he saw in himself the visionary nature of everything in Manu's tradition coming to him and him seeing himself as everything, in the visionary tradition of seeing himself in himself and in everything, he felt 'Aham Brahma' (I am Brahman). Once the nature of this universe, which comes from the cessation of rotations, is seen, most people cannot think beyond it. This is the aspect of how the Jivatma and Paramatma function after the action of the five sheaths. People stop talking at the boundary where they begin to think and understand beyond that. By bringing the functional capacity of the gurus, who came according to the Yuga Dharma through the passing Yugas, to Nambuvan's karma, a conceptual form of Brahma was given using the three parts mentioned. No matter which sage you ask, there will be no revelation about this.

Question: Then how can it be known?

Guru: Through the visionary tradition. What are these sheaths known by reaching the five senses that evolve through the five elements? Annamaya Kosha (food sheath), Pranamaya Kosha (vital air sheath), Jnanamaya Kosha (mind sheath), Vijnanamaya Kosha (intellect sheath), and Anandamaya Kosha (bliss sheath). After forming these and going into the concept of creation, this aspect is about seeing the Guru, the embodiment of karma, who merges and unifies as the soul and the Supreme, which is the unique karma showing what the karma of the solar system is. However, following Satyathmanan, such a mistake has occurred in the theoretical doctrine. Due to this sage's use of the phrase 'Aham Brahmasmi' (I am Brahman), the knowledge we have received from the visionary aspect is that none of the things in the Brahman concept functioned in the subsequent three Chaturyugas (four-yuga cycles). When it came to a stage where scholars or those without an understanding of the visionary aspect were present, in the second and third quarters of some Chaturyuga, a practice of worshiping with the concept of Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva in the path of creation of karma reaching from demons to gods emerged. When the five elements interacted with the five senses, this sensory nature was activated in all living beings. To recognize it, they mentioned Annamaya Kosha, Pranamaya Kosha, Jnanamaya Kosha, and Vijnanamaya Kosha, and stopped at Anandamaya Kosha. Thus, by the imagination of the mind and the proficiency in the path of karma, three Chaturyugas passed without even being able to take a look at the long history of rotations that came in accordance with the Yuga Dharmas. Satyathmanan failed to get the meaning of the continuous karma and the meaning of the Yuga Dharma. That is why phrases like 'Aham Brahmasmi' came to be. When all the eminent scholars and ordinary people who came after this wrote, depicted, and experienced it, as a devotee says, crossing the six steps

It reached the ritual of praising Shiva, where one went to the place they lay to see Shiva. This devotee's praise is repeatedly maintained. It became a system of worship across the entire Asian continent. Thus, even if we consider Treta and Dvapara as the yugas for that dharma, in the concept of names and forms that stand manifested and instruct, each person scholarly depicted everything up to the status of the Trimurti, or the status of Devendra, or the status of the Ganas. In name and form, according to their respective skills, the deity's mastery of all arts and adornment with all ornaments came into existence as dharma. This was affirmed even through the Puranas. They wrote, the great sages who praised what they saw day by day. But there are many examples of occasions when they could not tell or know the greatness of time. They became arrogant, stuck in the name and form, insisting that there is nothing beyond the Trimurti system. There is proof for this. A Yaksha placed a blade of grass and called all the gods, telling them to burn it or destroy it. Despite trying all their tricks, it did not happen. Because Devendra had no arrogance, he was able to recognize the Yaksha. The one who came as a Yaksha was indeed Brahman, who came by the will of Brahman. The word given there is Devendra saying that Brahman itself came as the Yaksha.

Guru Vani March 1995

Boys and girls need to be taught certain things that will be useful at respective times in the various stages of their lives. As far as girls are concerned, Kanyadanam is an important matter in that aspect. By teaching her thus, her mind is made steady. Boys also need a good life just like this. A

When becoming a householder, one must have a clear understanding of who they should be, who they were before that, and what they must do for their home and country. For girls, it is during the Kanyadaan ceremony that they are advised on how they should be when starting this life, the qualities required to become a mother, and what they should be before becoming a mother. This advice must be given in private. It is said that during this stage of instruction, some boys and girls experience visions. Some also receive visions due to their punctuality and discipline. Nothing else needs to be done for this. As proof, many among us have had visions. This vision occurred only after coming into the Guru's presence. Not before that. That is, these things become clear through the Guru. This is Savikalpa Samadhi.

One must attain Savikalpa through the Guru. A child who attains Savikalpa must reach at least the position of a Rishi under a deity. The result of being a Brahmachari during the Brahmacharya stage must be attained. A lineage originating from such a Brahmachari is what benefits the world. You will not understand this immediately. Two or three things come in order here. First, parents must impart the knowledge to the child about what they are meant to do. Then, the child will carefully absorb what the Guru teaches. The Guru must also advise them about their marriage. Then, their parenthood changes automatically, and they rise to the state of a Devi or Deva in that position. Through the Guru, their soul attains this greatness. They realize this themselves through experience. It is not something I teach them. The deities they worshipped or their ancestors worshipped are attained by them after coming here. At the time of marriage, these deities reveal the Guru to the man and woman. Here, what is looked for is not wealth or education, but a wealth of character. A wealth of character means a traditional familiarity. This is what is meant by Rishi Putri (daughter of a Rishi). Rishi Putri means one raised by a Rishi himself. According to her growth, or within the Guru-disciple relationship, there are three or four types. In Savikalpa Samadhi, before this girl gets married and becomes pregnant, she must have a vision of the Guru and her husband. What did you understand? The Guru has given advice to this child. In this child's vision, the Guru, parents, and the entire lineage will stand in a row. Like Rishis sitting in a royal assembly, they give her the wedding garland. You can go and garland whoever you like (Swayamvaram). Her choice will not be wrong. Because she has seen it. It is not doing whatever she pleases, as people assume. When it is said she chooses herself, it is true. It is destined for her soul. That destiny has already happened. Later, it is shown externally in front of people. They know what the Guru gives the garland for. The Guru, however, only smiles. This is Swayamvaram. People have not been made to understand this. All this is necessary for mature men and women.

This is a ceremony. It is by showing this ceremony that people call it Kettukalyanam. But nobody knows what Kettukalyanam is. It later turned into a celebration. This tying is done with celebrations like riding on elephants. Ten girls are seated in a row, and a groom is decided for the Kettukalyanam. This one person ties the knot for all ten girls. Look at our ignorance. Here, Narayana Guru stopped this Kettukalyanam. This is a great deed done by Swami. Thus, the Kettukalyanam that existed among Nairs, Ezhavas, and even Chandalas was stopped. Ten children, from ten years old down to three years old, are seated in a row, and one person is chosen from among them, and he is made to garland them. What kind of nonsense and baseness is this? The greatest baseness created to make their husband the rightful owner.

Before the child is born, whatever shortcomings the father, mother, and family had, are resolved through the aforementioned ceremony. Even before birth, this child is told what they should become. That is why it is said that conducting and arranging a marriage should be according to the Guru's decision.

What has been said so far is about the old Swayamvara. This is not for us. Instead of this, we have something else, the bride and groom do not need to know about our selection. The father, mother, and Guru should decide. Children should only have that relationship. There is a reason for that. Before getting married, the father and mother should instruct them. The child should be born only like this. For that, their deity connections must be cleared. This is Guru Pooja. The real Guru Pooja happening here fulfilled what was needed for that house. From the day of performing that Guru Pooja, the man and woman seeking the Guru lineage should know that they must do only this. For the lineage coming through the relationship decided by the parents and the Guru, a lineage has been molded by transforming the deity-demonic lineage into a Guru lineage. This Guru lineage is waiting for the growth of this child. For that, the child is instructed to do such and such things. The father and mother should tell them that. Or the Guru will tell them. But the father and mother cannot explain it completely. Because they will only accept the father and mother to a certain extent. What they don't listen to when the parents say, they will listen to when the Guru calls and says. It will never belittle them, but only elevate them. That is why it is said not to look at education and wealth, only the qualification of the family should be considered. This is what the Prophet demonstrated. The Prophet married a merchant's daughter to a beggar's son. And a beggar's daughter was given to a merchant's son. The command is like this; Hey, you give her to him, they will have what they need. No one else has said this. They haven't understood it.

The Prophet has said, Hey Pathu, do not do this. What does it mean? Nectar flows from our brain daily. This nectar should not go into the stomach. It should only go inside the intestines. Khechari Mudra is practiced knowing this. Under the tongue, over a long period - by slightly cutting the tongue with Darbha grass, that is what is achieved. After a certain time of cutting the bottom of the tongue, this nectar will ooze and go to another place. Khechari Mudra avoids that and indicates that it is like this. This is a state of Samadhi. The real nature of Khechari Mudra is this itself. What is this Samadhi? Without knowing the worldly steps happening in the universe, one meditates and focuses there. When the tongue is cut, the throat is closed, right? Attention is needed there. That is a Samadhi. One sitting in this state can see rays of light. It is nectar in the body, isn't it? If the Guru is capable of nurturing and developing this, he will specifically instruct. They will attain Savikalpa. How many people here have this. There is also Sanchara Samadhi. Wherever one travels, they see the place and state of travel. That is, seeing the state of travel in front as Savikalpa. Through that, deities and past Gurus come before us. Instead of that, what do those who call themselves scholars do?
