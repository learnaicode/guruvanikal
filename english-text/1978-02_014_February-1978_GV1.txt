[GV1:P0230] February 1978

[GV1:P0231] Question: There is a state where, through devotion, one gains experience and develops a visionary nature, isn't there?

[GV1:P0232] Answer: Yes, it is true that there is a visionary nature. In our own experience, we clearly see it here in both children and adults, don't we? It is our experience that this visionary nature has been seen in followers of all religions to date. In Moses and his followers, in Jesus, in the Prophet and his followers, this nature can be seen reflected more or less—similarly in Buddha and the Indian sages. It is seen reflecting in the sages of the past and the present. In recent times, the same is seen in Chattampi Swami and Narayana Guru. It is clearly reflected in the poems of Narayana Guru Swami. It can be seen throughout the *Subramanya Stuthi* as well. He has spoken in exactly this manner about Shiva and Krishna. Look at how he has concluded in a verse about the Guru of Subramanya.

[GV1:P0233] Turning southwards with compassion, under the banyan tree-

[GV1:P0234] May the deity who sat in the shade protect us.

[GV1:P0235] About Subramanya

[GV1:P0236] He has twelve eyes, ears, and hands correspondingly-

[GV1:P0237] seeing that, is there such compassion-

[GV1:P0238] No one else has it this way, have you not seen the suffering this slave undergoes here?

[GV1:P0239] People who see me scold me, with one ear at least-

[GV1:P0240] haven't you heard it?

[GV1:P0241] Relieving my distress and granting me refuge-

[GV1:P0242] Don't you need just one hand to do so?

[GV1:P0243] In this way, all these mentioned hymns are manifestly evident.

[GV1:P0244] Furthermore, if we examine *Darshanamala* and *Atmopadesa Satakam*, some independent visionary nature can be seen. Such visionary nature will contribute to many good traits and many bad traits.

[GV1:P0245] Question: What is that, if the vision is not good, why is such a visionary nature seen reflected in both good and bad?

[GV1:P0246] Answer: That is to say, vision (*Darshana*) means discerning the bad and the good to realize the Truth (*Sat*). That is exactly what is reflected in the first verse of the Gita. There are many confusions in the interpretations of scholars. By mixing grammatical nuances, ordinary people are confused and perplexed by it. When looking at reasonably accurate recent interpretations of this, some flaws can still be seen. In Vinoba Ji's interpretation, Dr. Radhakrishnan's interpretation, and Dr. Nataraja Guru's interpretation, what emerges is only the nature of having beautifully examined and understood the stage of a play. The nature of the person who wrote, created, and acted in the play is not seen. Similarly, when the art of dance is performed, everyone knows how to say it is good or not bad. The dual meaning applied in the physical grace of the dance art can only be observed closely by those who practice and teach dance. Just as a butcher can preach and train in self-knowledge (*Atmavidya*), or a tribal person making a living by hunting can attain self-realization (*Atmadarshana*), it is clear that by fortune, the attainment of God can be achieved in all virtuous natures.

[GV1:P0247] Question: What is the reason for you speaking in such a roundabout manner? Your answer seems to exceed the bounds of the question asked.

[GV1:P0248] Answer: It is so that you do not confuse a good matter by asking numerous questions.

[GV1:P0249] Question: Do you have a dislike for questions being asked? Should we assume that is why you spoke so harshly?

[GV1:P0250] Answer: No, there is no need to assume that. When numerous questions are raised and asked, it becomes impossible to state the reality clearly. I only intended this much in what I said. There is a misconception that self-knowledge (*Atmavidya*) is useful only if one lines up scholarship and numerous qualifications. Self-knowledge is solely the grace of God.

[GV1:P0251] Question: In your experiential nature, what do you mean by visionary nature?

[GV1:P0252] Answer: I also have a lesson of experience that one should attempt to impart visions (darshana) to people only after carefully understanding their nature and handling them beautifully for twelve years through corrections received directly from a guru. Within two or three years of having a vision, there are situations where we can reflect some experiences through others. At that time, we might feel that we have almost achieved it. Many have thus reached the danger zone. We too have not been immune to such things.

[GV1:P0253] Question: So the vision is true, and you said that this truth should be preserved in its entirety, didn't you?

[GV1:P0254] Answer: Yes.

[GV1:P0255] Question: Then, in your opinion, how should this aspect of vision be brought about without errors?

[GV1:P0256] Answer: The aspect of vision—firstly, it involves describing the macroscopic and microscopic causes that occur in the immense and the infinitesimal, both big and small. It is about the causes of creation and destruction of the past, future, and present. Even as one sees this, one will not get any idea of what it is or how it is. This is because all these are seen in a mixed-up, chaotic state. Such people are prone to making many blunders.

[GV1:P0257] Question: What kind of blunders would these be?

[GV1:P0258] Answer: Recently, about six or seven years ago, there was a warning from somewhere in the Western countries that the world was going to end. Accordingly, during those two months, some people engaged in charity, went to temples, and there was even an incident of mass bell-ringing in churches. These are some of the blunders that happened like that. Secondly, there are some visions that exhibit demonic characteristics. The blunders that occur due to not recognizing it - thus, the main blunder in this is the inability to distinguish between good and bad.

[GV1:P0259] Question: So, are you saying that we can see what is called demonic?

[GV1:P0260] Answer: Certainly, it can be seen. The nature of visions is difficult until that demonic aspect is removed and divinity pervades - this can only be corrected by familiarizing and rectifying it for at least twelve years.

[GV1:P0261] Question: Is it possible to convince the people of what has been described, in any way?

[GV1:P0262] Answer: There is no use in recording it like telling a few stories. No one can examine and understand it. Undisdainful devotion, respect, and knowledge that respects the greatness and smallness of the Guru are its basis. If one has scholarship and devotion, and if he also has faith, it will be a blessing like an extended helping hand.

[GV1:P0263] Guruvani

[GV1:P0264] March 1978

[GV1:P0265] Question: You had said that based on external factors, it would be useful for the practitioners of karma, didn't you? So, I am asking so deeply to know if, even if one cannot experience it, one can at least go forward convinced that it is true.

[GV1:P0266] Answer: Aren't the experiences of all this time and the experiences of the past proof that one can be convinced? What is there to ask with so much doubt in that?

[GV1:P0267] Question: That is clear like that. But what we understood from you is that ordinary people can make use of it. That is how we experienced it too. However, we do not know how to explain it. We believe that if any of those subjects becomes convincing through your words, we can reflect it among the people.

[GV1:P0268] Answer: If so, couldn't you have said that earlier? Alright. I shall record some things here. My experience is that what is called vision can be experienced by us in the respective form in whichever way we worship. For example, some place Madan, some place serpent deities. Some place certain stones based on some concepts. In all these forms, they worship God. Some seek to attain God by worshipping certain deities. Some people seek to attain God through the medium of certain gurus. Some will try to use some of their own techniques by combining those of yogis, wise men, and devotees. Some seek their own devotion alone in everything, seeing it in the form of their favorite deity.

[GV1:P0269] Question: Which of these do you think is the best?

[GV1:P0270] Answer: The best is the path of the Guru. There are gurus themselves who have realized in many ways. Those who have realized and perfected it in some way should control this. Otherwise, causes for blunders will occur frequently. If one is fortunate to find a guru who has achieved perfection in some way, and if that guru is not helpful to him, if pressed hard, he will suggest the path of another guru.

[GV1:P0271] Question: He is a guru, isn't he, then why did you say that he would suggest the path of another guru?

[GV1:P0272] Answer: The aforementioned guru is someone who has perfected a certain subject. The person who has come might not be one who follows that subject. When he is someone who has perfected it like this, he will understand that even if this person goes on this path, he will have some doubts and go astray, and so he will send him on another path. Thus he can find another guru.

[GV1:P0273] There are gurus in four paths. Karma, Yoga, Jnana, and Bhakti. A person who has perfected at least one of these can correct such things. If corrected, if he is fortunate, he will go on that path and achieve it. That is why it is said that the paths of the guru are easy. Whatever path the vision belongs to, at first it will be a vision mixed with the demonic. Secondly, one mixed with divinity. Thirdly, one filled with godliness. If one does not act by recognizing it, or if the guru who makes one act is not such, one should not expect anything but ruin in every way. We can see many people who have been confused by visions like this. We have seen some of them in front of us too. It is because you do not notice it. That is to say, there are some people who lead a very pure life and live with devotion to God according to rules and regulations. This is seen in all religions and in all systems of yoga, devotion, and karma. Such dangers are mostly seen in them.

[GV1:P0274] Question: What could be the reason for that?

[GV1:P0275] Answer: They read and know a little something, and by increasing the weight of goodness in that very model, they learn about the things stated and do them. Then they are doing it with some firm resolve. At that time, they will experience the aforementioned visions. Truly, in a way that cools their minds, what they desired and what they experienced will unite. Because they have learned what was written, they will hold fast to it. But they will be unable to know or inform about the past, future, and present, or about the existing descriptions, or about the details of what is in the subtle visions. Then what agreed at first will no longer agree later. At this point, their minds will reach the verge of breakdown. It might even lead to madness. This is the defect of not having a guru. However, if it is a scholarly guru, he will restrain him to some extent with his scholarship. But if it goes out of hand, the jumping will repeat without stopping anywhere. I have had the opportunity to be acquainted with many such gurus and disciples. Thus, I have been able to bring back some people and even stabilize their family lives. I have also been able to turn some people towards goodness and leave them to their spiritual goals. Isn't that itself complete proof for us?
