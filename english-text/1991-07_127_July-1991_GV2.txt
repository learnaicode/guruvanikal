GuruvaniJuly 1991

It is a fortune in itself to be able to be born in this Kali Yuga, which is the fourth quarter. It is generally believed that Kali Yuga is bad. But it is not so. If Kali Yuga is bad, how can the dharma of Kali take place? Shudra dharma can only happen in Kali. Who is a Shudra? The lowest in society. This dharma must be implemented in everyone from the Vellalachetti to the Chandala. They will only understand if the extensiveness of development is explained to them one by one. What is the use of beautifully presenting and making them hear the Gita verse 'Pratyakshavaham Dharmam' through presentations like Pathakam, Kathaprasangam, or descriptions of the Kali age?

When looking at a human being in terms of caste, there are Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Shudra. No one has come as a Brahmin by birth. They have only come as a Shudra by birth. It is by action that one becomes a twice-born (Dvija). So, in which era can a Shudra become a Dvija? All the circumstances for becoming a Dvija have naturally reached the Shudra by the grace of God. It is just that we do not know about these circumstances. Ten or fifty years ago, on the side of neglect, there was even untouchability. We must observe and know the developments that have taken place within the last five thousand years, going back from the time untouchability existed. If we go without knowing, a difficulty will arise where this dharma cannot be practiced.

Therefore, it is this generation that needs to know the most about social conduct. This generation must become aware of the mistakes that have occurred to them and then think about the reasons for them. We must historically know what mistakes have occurred to each generation in the last five thousand years. The calculation of the Vedas, Puranas, and Epics is five thousand two hundred years. The calculation of the sages and the calculation according to the Yuga dharma are also the same. That is, from the birth of Sri Krishna, the birth of Sri Krishna has been placed at the boundary of the Yuga. We see Sri Krishna as the owner of a clear background. What did we do to work and reach that condition? Instead, keeping a Madan and saying 'Oh Lord'

Aren't we just calling out? As one's own karma, one places Bhadrakali and calls out to God. There are even people who count and place thirty-three crore gods like this. Is the course of karma the same for all these, or is it different?

Karma is the driving force for everything. It is by that driving force that Madan is made a god. If so, what all qualities must that Madan be filled with? What all such disguises are dancing before us. If religion is what dances like this, is it wrong to call it the opium that intoxicates man? That intoxicating opium, which stands as the driving force to become like whatever we depend on, is never reality. You argue that religion has a broad meaning that it is the way to reach the ultimate form. In the four yugas of Satya, Treta, Dwapara, and Kali, just because one argues that the dharma of Satyayuga should be practiced in Kali, it will not happen.

Man's thought that his own land, country, and the world itself should prosper along with his own home - that goodness, that is true dharma. When that is the case, how can one perform a karma that one cannot perform oneself, without being able to perform one's own karma up to the level of Sruti, Smriti, or Purana? We have made a very big mistake.

The dharma of Satyayuga will not be practiced in Tretayuga. From the beginning of Tretayuga, a different system will come. At the end of Tretayuga, another system will come. That will be for Dwapara. The systems that come at the end of Dwapara will be for Kali. The souls who said that molten lead should be poured into the ears if one hears a letter, have today reached a state where they think that if he learns a letter and gets four paise from it, he can live. What is that? That is what I said, Kali is the age of the Shudra. There are about one hundred and eighty castes in the Shudra class. Eighty-three percent of the Indian population is the Shudra class. The remaining minority always had dominance over this majority depressed section. Not just the Vedas, whatever you take, the Vedic authority belongs to him. But due to the special nature of today's times, it has come to the downtrodden at least a little. So it has become even clearer that this is the age of the Shudra.

But the intellectual power of the Westerner, who has a tradition of only four thousand or five thousand years, stands challenging even the Brahmin today. But even though the course of time is favorable, where does the Shudra in India stand today? The culture of the Shudra stands bathed in Shudrahood itself. The elders have a notion that a Shudra will become a Brahmin through association. If so, there must be a suitable way for that. Let me give an example. In the house of Madhavan Muthalali in Pettah, Trivandrum, there was a person who always walked around with a file under his arm. This person is a degree holder. His job is to prepare and post a plan showing how it would be if a road network was easily cut from Trivandrum to Kanyakumari. He does this every day. In the same house, a person is kept by digging a floor, cementing it well, and keeping it clean. This is done to wash away his feces and urine from time to time. He is not left uncared for, he is a person who is constantly suffering from epileptic seizures and has completely lost his mind. This is the house where Narayana Guru Swami used to stay. Not only that, the Sharada idol was cast in that house itself. They used to provide facilities and whatever was needed to all the sanyasis coming from everywhere, not just here. Among those who come, there will be good and bad. It is like that in every dharma. A special room and bed are kept for Narayana Guru Swami. If other guests come, there are special facilities for that too. Adambil Vasudevan's (Judge Vasudevan) house is also one like this. In that house too, there are separate facilities for Narayana Guru Swami to stay when he comes and for others who come with him to stay.

There is a similar house in Kayikkara (Vakkom). A great-uncle of this family was there. The present name of that place in Melkadakkavoor is Guruvihar. I was the one who later fixed that place. It was fixed based on a vision I had while talking about it. This is a vision from my own experience. So, how should this vision be seen?

I have visited many such houses. I have been to the houses of the former Dewan P. N. G. Unnithan, some of his relatives in Pattom and Fort (Kizhakkekotta), and many other houses. I have also visited the houses of Brahmins in Fort (Kizhakkekotta). Back then, I was fair and thin like a wick. Now I sit like a jackfruit, don't I? At that time, there were occasions when all of them had to invite me to their homes. If I start talking about that, I will have to say a lot of things. I will tell that on another occasion.
