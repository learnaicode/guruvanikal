August 1998

The prayer currently offered at Santhigiri brings liberation to all the Mahatmas who have ever wished to follow the path of truth in their worship. People like us may not benefit as much. What is this liberation? It is not like what is described in the Hindu Puranas or Vedic hymns. When did these Mahatmas incur their karmic flaws, and how long will it take to resolve them? Once resolved, they will return to the human world. They will be all-powerful. God is guiding us towards such a karma.

You know that when marriages are conducted here, certain sankalpa karmas (rituals based on intention) are performed by them. In their sankalpa, certain signs are also revealed to them. Only about twenty-five out of a hundred people may experience this. The rest may not. When you hear the word 'darshan' (vision), you have certain preconceived notions. Even someone who previously practiced black magic (Chathan Seva) might exhibit extraordinary powers if they get a vision. What benefit do people get from these extraordinary powers? Amazed by such powers, we follow the path of people with karmic flaws and turn towards adharma. We believe in the karma of ghosts who died in that manner or died in accidents, and we have a practice of worshiping them by lighting lamps. We do not know what the result of worshiping all this is. We also do not know what will befall our children. Even though we don't know any of this, a strong tendency towards sorcery is seen in foreign countries today. Yet, these are the people considered to be highly civilized. That is where this is seen. So, you must think about where this evil spirit resides and what it does.

The prayer performed at Santhigiri is a truth revealed by God to bring about a change from all this. You are the children who have the right to practice this. Children born since 1973 especially have that right. However, that right will not come to you immediately. Every twelve years, there will be a change in the government of this country. The change in all these governments will happen through these children. These children are going to be the ones who demonstrate what dharma is in this world and how to remove karmic flaws. By the time of your third generation, this will have happened. God is making us the guiding light of such a great doctrine.

This is a time when God is making each of us, each family, complete a great dharma.

What have we heard about Sanathana? We have been taught that it is the Hindu dharma. In what way have we been taught this? Just as the Hindu guiding principles speak of Shaktheyam, Shaivam, Vaishnavam, or Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, in other religions too

This is seen in another imagination. Only that it is in their language. But, when it comes to the side of knowledge, this is not correct.

What was previously called Sanathana is the history of four faces. What are these four faces? It is a system that works as the faces of Kritha Yuga, Thretha Yuga, Dwapara Yuga, and Kali Yuga. It shows us how these four faces work. It passes it on to your children. It will not change until it reaches Sathya Yuga. After that, we have to know what will happen in the coming times. We must believe that this will not change if humans change it. If we can remove a major karmic flaw ordained by fate, at least to the extent of a grain of sand, according to the measure of Brahma's decision, that is enough. Thus it will spread to all parts of the world. After spreading, it will be experienced by all religious doctrines in our long history. Everyone will preserve it and contribute it to the world.

Looking at the whole world, there is an announcement that 2144 gurus came for this correction after the time of Krishna. But they could not correct it. When they correct a little, they will be driven away somehow. A brief description of it can be seen from the time of Moses. After the time of Sree Krishna, it starts from there. Those who have studied history know what that great man was made to do after it started. After that, until the time of Jesus, things like this kept coming. Even though all that came, it only shows the guidance for a hundred years, two hundred years, five hundred years, a thousand years, and two thousand years. No guidance beyond this can be said about them. You will think that with this the world will turn to ashes. It's not like that. Who will we be when we think that the guidance done by these great souls is only this much? In between this, some things are coming. It also has many kinds of negative aspects.

At one time, there was a rumor that the world was going to end. Then believers went to temples, churches, and other places of worship and did and made others do many things. Nothing happened that day. Later, many times it was announced here that such and such a thing was going to happen in such and such a place. What was said to happen was seen here in a vision. At all those times, there was an announcement to pray here. About ten or twenty years ago, there was an incident where people on the Kollam beach were relocated. The reason was that a storm would come. It was announced here and asked to pray. The people who came and went here were made to pray. A specific time period was also given for the prayer. That storm left here and reached some other place. But that storm did not go in vain. It went on and on and took away a whole island. On that day, a priest of a church on that island lamented like this: Where is the Lord I believed in? Father! What can they do? After that, it was said that a cyclone would come two or three more times. At all those times, the believers here were called and made to pray. God accepted that prayer too. That cyclone went to the Bay of Bengal.

Recently, you might have read in the newspapers the prediction that a large piece of rock is coming towards the earth. When looked here, it is not a piece of rock, but a comet with four or five eyes. There will be war even on the path it passes. This is also called a comet. In the old days, seeing this was a great fear. Its light will cover other places. It can be seen like a torchlight. After traveling a little, it will disappear. Then it will hit somewhere, and the place where it falls will be destroyed.

What is this comet? This is a great danger that has resulted from yoga systems like Hatha Yoga. They are independent. They are a kind of souls who do not even respect God. Through pranayama, certain other abilities developed by yoga, and by consuming certain types of medicines, they have conditioned their bodies in such a way that they will not perish even if they sit on a clod of earth. Such people can be seen in the Himalayan regions. A doctor named Somervell saw this during a Himalayan expedition and publicized it. He was in Thengapattanam (Neyyoor) for a few days. At that time, he came to treat Narayana Guru. But he said he would not treat him. Because a stronger medicine than what has already been given to that body should not be administered. Saying, "Let us pray for the Guru," he knelt down and prayed for five minutes. He told a story. While walking in the Himalayan regions, he saw an anthill in the shape of a person. He slowly approached, cleared the bushes, and looked, but there were no termites in the anthill. There was barely a single termite. But it was indeed a termite mound. The person did not know that this anthill had grown over him. After a while, he opened his eyes. When he opened his eyes, a person (Somervell) was standing there. The man inside the anthill asked: "Is Henry 4 still ruling the country?" Somervell replied, "No." From the time of Henry 4 to the time of Somervell, this man who sat inside the anthill calling upon God did not know whose reign it was. He had conditioned this body with Navapashanas and regulated it so that it could survive on a clod of earth, equally in rain, wind, and sun, without even a drop of water. If this is the true fact in our history, what have we embraced? What are we carrying around?

Guruvani

September 1998

This is the history of our thought on God; the truth of history is of this error. To resolve such an error that was brought upon humanity, God has sent two thousand one hundred and forty-four gurus and prophets over the past six thousand years. They could not accomplish it. If God has chosen us to correct that error, or if God wishes to fulfill that mission through poor, ignorant people like us, think about how fortunate each of you who are participants in that mission are, how blessed you are. Through this, you are becoming a path to save the world. You should think of yourselves in such an elevated manner. Instead, you shouldn't think that a coconut fell on your back, or that you encountered misfortune while walking on the northern side. Those are just some of the negative aspects that occur in a human's birth. It is the destruction that belongs to the zodiac sign of birth and the zodiac sign of the lineage that was investigated. What decision should you live by to escape from this destruction, how should you correct your children to create such a lineage of life - the action for this is what is advised from here. Sharing that action with you and the world, God is carrying out the action of implementing it through us.

The path in Santhigiri is the determination of Brahma. This path is what is called Sanathana. Four eras - Krita Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dvapara Yuga, Kali Yuga. Kali Yuga is the path by which man reaches wisdom through the accumulated actions up to Dvapara. That path has been wrong for the past twenty-five Chaturyugas. This error is being corrected now, here. Whether you have this understanding or not, whether you carry it with you or not, God has gifted you that knowledge. To complete this action, you pray upon seeing a form and name capable of a special action given by God. What are you seeing when you pray to this? How many karmic flaws the ancestors brought through worship, how much it has spread in this world - that is being seen from this little child to the mothers.

When you see this, you will have various kinds of pain. There will be no one to pamper you when you see this. Sometimes you might even get kicked and beaten. Even then, you must have the certainty and knowledge that 'I will do this'. We, who do not even think about why that father and mother had to sit in prison to give birth to Krishna, said we didn't kill him because we didn't get him in our hands. What did we turn Krishna himself into? This is the knowledge and familiarity we gained from the Hindu Dharma. This knowledge itself has been continuing for so long. If this is not changed, we will keep going towards an action that destroys the world. Due to the mistake in this system, billions of souls are standing corrupted in the atmosphere.

A short while ago, a stone fell from the atmosphere. It was not a stone. It was a meteorite. It exploded and fell into the Jupiter system. At first, scientists thought it was coming to Earth. It is waiting to fall into many other systems just like it fell into the Jupiter system. It is circling around for many miles to cause destruction. A huge scene like this is waiting above us. This can happen in all parts of this solar system. If this happens in all parts of the solar system, it will overturn all the theories of the Yugadharma. Jesus gave us evidence about this destroyer when he said, 'Satan, go away.' It did not go away. It continued until the crucifixion. This devil destroyed all the great souls who came to this day. Seeing this danger, some others have also called out and given evidence. The one who theorized this devil is karma dosha ruling us. It is seeing this karma dosha that the great ones called out.

For example, those who go to holy places die in accidents when they return. The Government of India tried in many ways to prevent this, but it did not work. Last year, at the Kumbh Mela in Allahabad, out of the lakhs of sanyasis who participated, some made claims and fought with each other. Some died. What is the claim of the sanyasis? Such and such a place is such and such a holy place. It belongs only to them. No one else should enter there. Another form of this is throwing stones at Satan. Many people die in that too. This is the specialty of devotion. Many such karma doshas that die and perish happen in the name of our theories. We should think about how to change these karma doshas. It is to change this that God makes us pray here. Therefore, when you pray, you should imagine and pray that whatever karma doshas have come in this world due to the order of worship should change and the souls standing as chitta due to the dosha of worship should get rebirth or be destroyed.

Our daily prayer is such a karma. God is making us do this world service. Brahma Muhurtha is the true dharma that we complete. You are praying here that whatever salvation should be given to us who are working by taking up such a corrupt tradition from that Brahma Nishchaya, whatever mistakes and good things are there in this karma that we do, you should take it all up and this karma should become holy and that holiness should reach fortune and all fortune should be given as a gift for these people.

Religious theories, epics, and various theories have tried in many ways to convince the things that came through the order of worship that went wrong for many ages. All these attempts are their intellectual exercises. If you try to fill God with literal knowledge, will it be useful for the conduct of Brahma Nishchaya? You should think about this.

Guruvani
