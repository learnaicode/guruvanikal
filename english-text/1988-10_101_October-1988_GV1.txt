[GV1:P2286] October 1988

[GV1:P2287] When we talk about the material and the spiritual, aren't we merely speaking of two sides of the same coin? Then why is the material portrayed as something so magnificent? We should not call it entirely bad; nor should we elevate the spiritual and call it perfectly good. One is just the flip side of the other. I do not know what science is. But I have seen it. That is the only way it can be expressed. Only if I were someone who had studied scriptures could I speak about them, right? I can calculate and explain. My focus is not on what is material and what is spiritual. Where do the karmic bonds generated through our way of life stand? Where do they go wrong, and where do they not? What is the reason for it? What lies in the trajectory of karma? Where is its merit (*Punya*)? What is the *Shikhi* (fire/power) that has operated on its ancestral (*Pithru*) side? That is all I know. It will never say anything wrong. There is no point in saying anything else. The scriptural doctrine that the world today functions strictly based on this remains completely true. No matter who says what, no one can claim that this is incorrect.

[GV1:P2288] This is my experience. I am merely stating the qualifications related to my path of karma, my understanding of dharma shastra, and my truthfulness. Many others may have various qualifications. I do not deny that. Nor do I say it is not so. Should we not see what the nature of the soul is, which extends from the elements to the constellations? Otherwise, there is no truth in it. It can be scientifically proven only if we study whether our form ceases to exist at that point. I am simply stating what I have seen and discovered, exactly as it is.

[GV1:P2289] There is a system in which the nine planets, twelve zodiac signs, and twenty-seven stars operate! Our self-realization is the ability to see how it is made to function. Otherwise, there is no point in merely saying that it was like this or like that.

[GV1:P2290] What must be studied first is the special power of the sun that causes the lunar dust particles to fall to the earth, and the karmic path of the earth which is a component of it. How easy is it to just say that it does not exist? I am not speaking from what I have studied; I have seen it. I can only explain it if such a person comes to me. I will speak only if someone asks what evidence exists in a particular part and what should be done with that evidence. Otherwise, I have not acquired the knowledge to compile and expound upon the scriptures.

[GV1:P2291] J. Krishnayyar: Whichever path humans travel, they must ultimately die

[GV1:P2292] —in a sense. They must die partially. What is the state beyond that? Remembering again, remembering again, it goes on like that. Will it go beyond that in such a manner?

[GV1:P2293] Guru: It will only go that way if one works with a goal. Being human,

[GV1:P2294] if one acts like an animal, will it not grow without a goal?

[GV1:P2295] J. Krishnayyar: Is there any research on this subject happening

[GV1:P2296] here?

[GV1:P2297] Guru: It is happening here. It is an experience. That experience

[GV1:P2298] is shared by many people here. They have been able to realize the experience even in their material lives. They have learned to speak about it authoritatively. There have also been quite a few boys like that. If they make a slight mistake, it will deviate. It will not sustain then. No one has truly worked to achieve it according to expectations. I believe it is due to their lack of desire or a deficiency in their karmic capacity. It is this kind of 'research' that is taking place here.

[GV1:P2299] How do the life paths operate in various ways up to the constellations; how should those souls attain liberation, or if they are to be destroyed, how should they be destroyed; if the deity they worshipped needs to be given liberation, how is that done? We have attained the knowledge to do all these things as well. If this knowledge did not exist, we would have had to stand with those who say there is no karmic path and those who say there is no such thing as self-realization.

[GV1:P2300] Everything is according to our conception. Isn't everything a concept! That is why it is said that concept is the karmic mind.

[GV1:P2301] Now, if one was born in a certain lifetime—what was the nature of a snake? What has it become now from that birth of a snake? As an animal! How did it come to be? What was its capacity for action then? What is the reason for those karmas rising like this? Or what is the reason for saying that a human being will return as an animal? This must be recognized. Just one side is not enough; all sides must be known. As one continues to know this, it will become clear what that soul was. This is how it should be understood. What was it? In what form did it manifest? How did that atom operate? By what color is it distinguished? It has been divided into nine, and all that we see is the manifestation of these nine. Just as the same color can be slightly separated and distinguished, our thoughts and feelings themselves can be separated.
