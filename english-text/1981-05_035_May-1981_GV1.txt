[GV1:P0714] May 1981

[GV1:P0715] Question: You had previously indicated that Krishna is the incarnate deity of a specific Yuga!

[GV1:P0716] Guru: That is correct. The era of Krishna signifies the commencement of one Yuga and the culmination of another. However, prior to deliberating upon Krishna, certain other matters necessitate clarification. Numerous great souls were born prior to Krishna and conducted themselves in accordance with the prevailing Yuga. Yet, even before that, certain systemic anomalies persisted. A Shudra sage was assassinated with an arrow. Concurrently, the rigid caste system, internal sub-castes, and a monarchical regime that systematically deprived specific populations even of basic literacy were forcefully instituted.

[GV1:P0717] Under the dominion of a single monarch, one specific group of subjects was permitted to acquire literacy, whereas another group was strictly prohibited from doing so. How did such a paradigm come into existence? Consequently, it is unequivocally clear that these structural anomalies existed long before. The reality of diverse categories of subjects existing under a single monarch is the direct consequence of strategic political maneuvers, whereby a certain demographic was systematically ostracized and expelled to seize control of the governing apparatus. Those who complied were granted everything, while the dissenters were brutally subjugated and driven away. If observed meticulously, one can discern the remnants of such exclusionary tactics in contemporary administrative structures even today.

[GV1:P0718] It is observable that the scholars who authored the history of Kerala during the period of India's independence conspicuously omitted prominent leaders. If such historiographical distortions occur in the present era, one can only hypothesize the magnitude of manipulations orchestrated by the governing bodies of antiquity. During that epoch, an absolute decree was established forbidding a Shudra from listening to the Vedas. It was precisely for this reason that the Shudra sage was systematically denied the fundamental right to pursue asceticism. If subjects residing under a sovereign's rule are utterly deprived of their right to perform penance and worship the divine, one cannot even fathom the depths of degradation they would invariably descend into. This is the calculated handiwork perpetrated by the adherents of such exclusionary religious paradigms. We must critically analyze the multitude of malicious intentions they deliberately executed. We are already cognizant of historical narratives that are not too distant in the past. Have we not recently witnessed the actions of the Pakistani administration as well?

[GV1:P0719] *Continued from the previous issue

[GV1:P0720] What all transpirations occurred when Marthanda Varma assumed control of our Travancore monarchy? Men were systematically annihilated, and women were captured and forcibly handed over to the fisherfolk. Consequently, as they fled to various kingdoms in desperation, it is documented that they sought refuge in regions such as Karunagappally, Karthikappally, and Vaikom. Their inherent aristocratic dignity and aesthetic grace remain undiminished even today. The lineage of Chempil Arayan, renowned for the traditional snake boats, continues to live with immense respectability. One can also observe Brahmins and others partaking in meals while seated in distinctly separate rows. When such overwhelming evidence remains tangibly present, there is absolutely no doubt that history has been fundamentally distorted. In such oppressive circumstances, as a direct consequence of internalizing a corrupted culture devoid of any authoritative access to the Vedas, none of the individuals from the Shudra caste downwards have been able to achieve genuine emancipation or rectitude.

[GV1:P0721] Since the other two Yugas (Treta Yuga and Dwapara Yuga) constituted the epoch of the Devas, it was entirely feasible to conceptualize the deity and perform divine worship. The fourth Yuga constitutes the ultimate seat of authority for all of this. If the other three Yugas have sequentially progressed in strict accordance with their specific Yuga Dharma, and if one possesses faith in that cosmic order, is it not an absolute certainty that the fourth Yuga must execute its own inherent Dharma? If the Puranas emphatically celebrate that gods and goddesses hold no designated position within the Dharma of the fourth Yuga, is it not an absolute imperative that this cosmic mandate be actualized? Stating that Shudras shall wield governance during the Kali Yuga is not merely a concept to be comprehended; one must inevitably concede when it is asserted that the profound authority over the Vedas also fundamentally belongs to them. Furthermore, by meticulously hoarding these privileges and subjecting us to the indignities of polyandrous alliances devoid of recognized paternity, we have been systematically reduced to our current deplorable state. Delving further into this matter will necessitate extensive discourse. Is it not unequivocally evident that by monopolizing such authoritative domains, the marginalized subjects at the absolute bottom of the hierarchy were systematically disenfranchised and displaced?

[GV1:P0722] There has been a change since the time of Krishna. As mentioned earlier, Krishna's period is at the end of one yuga and the beginning of another. That is, at the end of Dvapara and the beginning of Kali Yuga. We celebrate Onam here, don't we? It is grandly celebrated in Kerala. When a good royal administration existed here, they concocted stories of God appearing and coming here, along with tales of sages, passing them around to create a logical justification. We are taught fabricated stories that someone was pushed into the netherworld just by the touch of a foot. We have also enjoyed listening to them. Certainly, that noble king who ruled well was loved by the people. Therefore, doesn't this prove that the intelligent Brahmin fabricated a story to show that even God is hostile to someone who does good, killed him, seized that royal court, and then made the one who wrote stories for the king at that time create this mockery?

[GV1:P0723] Looking at it this way, one can see such an appearance of God in the house of the King of Pandalam as well. God appears and does what is necessary only when calamities befall the women in the king's house. Looking at the history they have written, it seems that God does not appear to any poor people in our homes. Mahabali, who belonged to the Asura race, was stomped upon; as soon as he was touched, he went to the netherworld. That is how God comes to touch. Thus, as soon as a woman in the king's house got a headache, Mahavishnu and Shiva set out together. When they arrived here, since the woman was unwell, both of them engaged in absurdity, and immediately [the offspring] fell out by splitting the thigh. It was not even a direct birth. Immediately, he mounted a leopard and went into the forest. He caught and tied up a leopard, brought it back, milked it endlessly, and cured the disease. There was a time here when royal administration and the worship of God were conducted through such absurdities. Since the people were illiterate, it was not difficult to weave these stories and make them believe them.

[GV1:P0724] There is evidence for this. There is a history of Ayyappan coming and staying in an Ezhava family in the Cheerappanchira house in Cherthala and teaching Kalaripayattu there. During that time, he also gave advice to a girl there. After that, he went from there to a place called Kanjiramattom Mosque. There he taught Kalaripayattu to Muslims and gave advice to a Muslim youth. At the same time, he went and stayed at the place where the Arthunkal Church is situated today, and facilitated various benefits for many people there. It can be seen that Christians still preserve its memory today. Anyone can be convinced of these facts by going to these places and investigating.

[GV1:P0725] After that, he went to Kanjirappally via Kottayam. It was a place where a group of Brahmins lived at that time. All these Brahmins together took Ayyappan to the King of Pandalam. Upon going there, seeing that he was brilliant and an ascetic, he was made to stay there and entrusted with the responsibility of teaching Kalaripayattu. On that occasion, Ayyappan heard the stories of Vavar, Kochukadutha, and Valiyakkadutha. Thus, one day when Ayyappan went to see the hill, he met Vavar, Kochukadutha, and Valiyakkadutha and gave them advice.

[GV1:P0726] Prior to that, there was a temple there consecrated by a Brahmachari from the royal family. A trench was dug to stay upon arriving there, and a temple was built in the middle of it. To see this, he took them along and went to the place of the Sastha idol, which was a conceptual consecration. The Brahmachari from the royal family - named Hariharan - consecrated it with the conception that his family should become like the Buddha's family in the future, and attained Samadhi right there. An idol of Ganapathi was consecrated over the Samadhi. It was during this time that this Ayyappan arrived and performed penance.

[GV1:P0727] Ayyappan gained power by performing penance over this consecration and Samadhi. Then Kochukadutha, Valiyakkadutha, and Vavar all came down to the king. They had taken control of all the properties there. When the king inquired how to get this back, Ayyappan agreed to sort it out by talking to them. Because Ayyappan was convinced that they would listen if he spoke, as they were his disciples. Thus, the king's men, Vavar, and Ayyappan came to an agreement, and they surrendered the forest wealth. They were also given some land.

[GV1:P0728] In this, Vavar emerged prominent. In the settlement, a condition was placed that one should go to see Ayyappan's Samadhi place only after seeing Vavar's Samadhi place. That is how the 'Saranam' chanting originated, which perpetuates the memory of Kochukadutha, Valiyakkadutha, and Vavar.

[GV1:P0729] Through penance, Ayyappan became greater than this Hariharan and the conceptual consecration. Thus, it could be seen that Sabari stood as a support and shade for this Ayyappan. There is a custom where all first-time Ayyappan devotees take an arrow from Vavar's Samadhi place and place it at the spot of the Saramkuthi banyan tree. That banyan tree is not there today. The one that stood very large was destroyed. Since it is the place where Sabari performed penance, ghee and coconut are offered there. It can still be seen like a homa kunda today. Ayyappan attained Samadhi at the temple precincts. The girl from Cheerappanchira, to whom Ayyappan had given advice during his lifetime, went and met another disciple, the Thangal of Kanjiramattom, and the two of them met Vavar, and everyone together went to Ayyappan. They left that girl there and returned. The temple known today as Malikappuram is where this girl stayed and was interred in Samadhi.

[GV1:P0730] The aforementioned Thangal is the one who attained Samadhi at Kanjiramattom. Today, the family members of this Thangal are traditionally known as the Thangals of Kanjiramattom. Muslims are well aware that the Thangal who attained Samadhi at the Kanjiramattom Mosque is a disciple of Ayyappan. At the Kanjiramattom Mosque, the flag hoisting is on the first of Makaram. At the same time, the doors of Ayyappan's shrine are closed after the lamp lighting on that day. After a very long case in the name of the girl from Cheerappanchira, the High Court granted the right to fire ceremonial shots. The upper-caste hegemony seized that as well again. Stating that such a right should not belong to a household like this and that it is inferior, they filed a case in the court. The right to fire shots was canceled. This was done by the Devaswom Board. This is a recent example of upper-caste hegemony.

[GV1:P0731] Who is this Ayyappan? A boy belonging to a carpenter family in the Palakkad district in North Malabar. His father and mother died when he was three years old. Many households adopted and raised him. Eventually, he learned Kalaripayattu and other things, the martial arts deities appeared, and through that, he almost attained Siddhi. All these things happened while he set out on an ascetic path. This is the history of Ayyappan. (Have you heard the Saranam chanting in the name of this Ayyappan? They will not lie. But they will show a trick - they call out 'Harihara Sutan Ayyanayyappaswami'. It means that Ayyappan, who was Ayyan, became Ayyappaswami. Hariharan is the Brahmachari from the royal family, the Sastha, the conceptual consecration that was there.) If this can be distorted and done like this, isn't it the truth that the poets of that time distorted history and handled it in this way? Isn't it proven that such histories were distorted carefully so that their hegemony would not be lost? Most of the poets of that time belonged to Brahmin families and were authorities in the royal family. The other illiterate people - growing like wild grass - do they know this? From so much evidence existing like this, isn't it understood what all destructions this Varnasrama Dharma has caused?

[GV1:P0732] Question: Where did you get this much history from?

[GV1:P0733] Guru: This history is not just this. There is a lot. That cannot be said. It was just indicated for the knowledge of the people. I have said, haven't I - that many things have been told through philosophical visions, which cannot be revealed now. I have also said that after my time, if there are people who believe in this, let them reveal it.
