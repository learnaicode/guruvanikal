[GV4:P0986] May 2004

[GV4:P0987] What is not within India is not going to be obtained from outside India. An example of this is the prophetic religion that came from foreign lands. What we got before us was the Vedic scholar or religious pundit who did not even have the experience to point out the rights and wrongs that have come in this prophetic religion. These religious pundits dissected and interpreted the letters, and in experiencing that, we turned into worm-eaten objects, and in fact, we truly became worms. Because ignorance was propagated in this state, the knowledge of how to acquire love ceased to exist. Why is that? Our ancestors established various Ashramas, and by standing firm on the doctrines shown by each Guru and cultivating them as such, elevating entities like Madan and calling them 'Thampuran' (Lord), or invoking Kuttichathan and continuing in such ways, we have been born as the remnants of numerous degenerate forms of worship.

[GV4:P0988] So we do not even realize that each person born absolutely has this ignorance. The scholar followed the scholar, learned by considering that merit, studied in a way he couldn't respect in his own actions, and ended up declaring bankruptcy. If you ask what this learned bankruptcy is, you today, sometimes people come here and tell me often, that they take this mental illness to this Thaikkattussery to be seen. Isn't he a psychiatrist? Thaikkattussery is what I am saying. If shown to them, if they can mentally figure out the good deeds of past lives, then nothing else is needed in the world. How much bankruptcy is contained within us! So, if this bankruptcy is contained, apart from becoming utter fools, can we achieve anything with this ignorance of ours? Or even if achieved, would we get anything other than time to fight with each other? The god buried in each religion is the greatest. After that, it is the god buried in each caste. If you truly ask how you have respected and confined the god system that has been minimized to this extent, you have become so cunning that no individual can fully explain it. To change this, a path of action that stands apart and fights against that supremely compassionate divine will overflowing with love, the humble karma of behavior, and the personality and punctuality that uphold dharma, is a course of action that stands in us today. That is this Thaikkattussery. What are they going to do - these people of Thaikkattussery! Let them ask themselves. What can they do? Speaking this language - and this language - practitioners of mischief - what is this mischievous system? It is their system of governance. This system of governance is very mischievous. A government where humanity cannot be contained. By giving positions and titles to this government, bearing baseness, each mind is changing as a remainder of a karma that always fights and wins against oneself as its lineage. What good do they have to give for this? What evil have they instilled in them to give us good? Out of the ignorance to think about any of this, we have become completely insignificant, and born as owners of an ignorance that insignificantly called upon God behind it. For this, you have an ununderstood unity. Then, this is the stage for each of us to share a little love. Here, some children of nowadays write to me. When she or he studies and goes to college, the children of the rich, the rowdies, and the mischievous ones holding jobs have assumed something and have become utterly bad, in a way that shows no respect, like enjoying telling the story of some glass palace. These are the ones ruling us. How would it be? Whatever mischief they show, they will take up the action of ruling. If they feel this mischief is a complete failure, they will take up another mischief. Other than that, regarding them, they have no governance or a path of action as an administrator. Someone without that is unlikely to get dharma either. Dharma - we are all the remainder of the ignorance that sits at the bottom of one without dharma and calls out. So, if we unravel the scroll of this course of action standing as this remainder and fate, we have absolutely no courtesy to interact with each other. Then, some have invented a new policy. What it is, is to help others. He is one who does immense help. So, if he earns ten or two hundred dollars working for a month, taking only what he needs to survive, they say he is helping his friends. Seeing and learning this, the children growing up next door have also become this base. Spreading to this country, such help and cooperation are being invented. What won't the children of family people who have taken up these fragments of karma, turning into this hostel and hotel that extract money by manifesting the suitable five-star hotel and the dark rooms attached to it, do in this country! None of this being possible to do, the children of mischievous people who eat away at the country come like this, and not only in this country, but in the world, rejecting whatever human life there is, we fail to understand that the Vedanta outside the culvert and the imagination of the disastrous minions who stand at the door of the paan shop and pose as swamis are completely the full form of a nature devoid of dharma and karma. Without understanding this, a form they build up the biggest form of revenge that each idol of ignorance created by religious groups that create caste, calling every Pillai Swami like Pillai Swami and Damodaran Pillai Swami in a way that makes known it's a certain caste, can do to us while living. Seeing this, if it is enough for all this to grow in your house as well, there is no need for you to come here. That exists all over the land. For you to feel like coming here, there must be a special reason. That is nothing else. Until today, it is not just holding tightly onto the doctrines of the gods and goddesses worshipped or the lineage of sages or the lineage of Manus that have passed by. All this does exist. For this - for this reality we now see before us - what change must be brought about, the householders who show it through their lives and the children who follow them - not only for the world and the home, but wherever humanity or living creatures exist, for each of you to feel that our children should be the ones to adopt a life that earns merit for all of them, or to desire and act upon it, the traditional path to set out to the Satya Yuga by clearing all available paths and removing the disasters for that path, is what is needed in today's people, and not only that, stepping away from today's Vedanta verses, Advaita - is a very good thing, true - but is this Advaita good for you or anyone else to swallow? If swallowed, where will you go and stand? With whom will you stand? Even this is unknown. All are dualities. All are illusions. You must think about what these empty scholars are doing to you by spouting this illusory nonsense and grabbing money and similar positions from you. If said, this is not something that can be said to please you.

[GV4:P0989] The intellectual prowess of these scholars lies in laying a mat of rationalization for this injustice that has grown to this day. How will you come forward in this state? Will this change if some salvationist, rationalist, or hedonist comes along? Knowing specifically that this is a realization to be imbibed into your absolute karmic path, each one of you—or rather, each one of us—is a path of Dharma meant to take up the duty that needs to be undertaken, perform it, maintain righteousness, impart the light of great epochs to your children, and pass on the right to inherit that light to generations yet to be born. The knowledge imparted from here is meant to let you introspect on what you are doing for this. I will not take or use even a single word from any person, or a Swami, or anyone else. I have left them to their own devices. As far as I am concerned, this exists, and it is true. There is truth in what they said too. But how will you accept it practically like alms in your palms? Or how will you imbibe it into your hearts and transform yourselves into individuals with a sense of justice? We have lost our rhythm in this familiarity. What is being imparted from here is a karmic path that every householder and every individual must strive to achieve in order to gain the respect of an action that can be understood and transformed by you. If you do not preserve this, not only is there no practical utility in stating these things, but it also leads us to an action that pawns and destroys ourselves. To prevent this, there is no point in allowing the Trinity system of bygone eras, or the tribal system, or the writings filled with Puranas, Epics, and Aranyakas to spread into you. None of these will result in anything other than leading you into a moral dilemma that ensnares your soul. Due to this lack of knowledge, we could not even completely fulfill the greatest karmic chapter we undertook. And it will not happen that way either. Because we are talking about something that happened ten trillion years ago. Therefore, we cannot go back that far to retrieve it. Hence, you do not need to go and retrieve any of that. Just acknowledge that these things exist. Having seen them, let today's necessity become tomorrow's truth. Every one of you must be able to understand that the purpose and the realization of the goal of Santhigiri, which has been imparted to us as God, is a consciousness of Dharma carried out with the sole desire of familiarizing you with these paths so that you can perfect and practice them, and you must be able to contemplate what needs to be done about this.

[GV4:P0990] Guruvani
