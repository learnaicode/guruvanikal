[GV1:P1575] January 1986

[GV1:P1576] Yagas and yajnas have been initiated to celebrate and bring our Vedic sciences to the forefront. 'Chandika Yajna'—the very mention of the word Chandika makes its nature clear. By conducting this yajna and convincing us of many things, they turn us into its worshippers. Then comes the Soma Yaga. Through this, they intend origin, dissolution, and destruction. It is claimed that prosperity will increase. The prosperity of the offspring is not their goal. They are exhilarating people by saying many other things. It is said that its benefits will last for ten years. By saying so, they make a person perform the act of pleasing the deity for those ten years and keep him tethered to it. Again, linking to it, each yajna is introduced. When doing so, the lineage of offspring falling into this inclination will naturally increase. They are doing it very intelligently. Thus, if his *sattvic* nature is directed towards the deity, since it is an era where the deity has no place, he too will perish like the deity. Observe the sharpness of their intellect! He will assume the authority of a bunch of rabid, ruined people like this. He will perish. Even so, let his foot remain on top. He will also get the reputation of having done all good deeds. At the same time, the poor ignorant Shudras will fight and die. They know how to completely destroy the Shudra class in this manner. It is the result of forgetting the thought of God. They will also prattle that it is the result of not decorating the temple grandly. You must think deeply about this. As long as you do not think, you will not be able to accomplish anything here.

[GV1:P1577] Recently a yajna was held, wasn't it? What is it? Atirudra Yajna. What is achieved by this? What prosperity do we get? Taking a Shiva Purana story from the Treta Yuga, bringing it into the form of a yajna, and bestowing this yajna. Even though they have imparted this masquerade among you who live in such a civilized world, you do not understand. You gained nothing from diplomacy. What you received was only the stratagem, 'Let us fight tomorrow, today we are friends.' At first, knowledge was not obtained. What was received through the alphabet and education is this culture. Thus it became a situation of 'neither did it come from the south, nor did it fall into the trap.' Now if we speak about renunciation (*sannyasa*), nothing has been gained so far. The old history has collapsed. The idol being rebuilt must be rebuilt once more. We received no knowledge beyond this. Meanwhile, some *sannyasins* and prophets appeared here. They have all been driven away from here. In Kerala itself, many great men came saying, 'Brahman alone is truth' (Alathur Brahmanandan, Shivayogi, etc.). The vast majority of people here are ignorant of such things. If told like this, how will they transmit it? Shivananda, Kanhangad Ramananda; many such people have come to Kerala. But some of them just went towards the deity. Then, if we are to say someone actually highlighted this in relation to Kerala, it is Sree Narayana Guru and Chattampi Swamikal. The community in which Chattampi Swamikal was born did not need him even a bit back then. Narayana Guru was brought and born amidst a most ignorant society. None of the capitalists of that time, who paid a tax of five rupees, respected him then. You must possess the knowledge to evaluate all this and act accordingly.

[GV1:P1578] Guruvani

[GV1:P1579] March 1986

[GV1:P1580] Not only is there no knowledge about the path of self-realization (*Atmajnana*), there is not even a notion of it. What exists is merely the knowledge of some chaotic events that occurred either religiously or based on caste and class. However, with the vision that all this should change, by divine decree, everyone from Sree Krishna to the recently arrived Gurus have come as prophets, guides, and visionaries. But because we have not received the knowledge to understand these visionaries, we do not know what their visions are, what their lineage is, or what will happen to the world through them. They demonstrated many things. But we again evaluate them by tying them down under a god or goddess, viewing them as miraculous powers emerging from that. A lack of knowledge about the path of *karma* and the failure to receive guidance for a pure life. We have received a kind of knowledge that makes life itself go in vain. Not only have we not been able to achieve anything through this, but we are also playing around on the path of blunder.

[GV1:P1581] We must be able to bring about a change from this. This is a path that the divine will (*Brahmanischaya*) itself has shown for us. We can do whatever is possible by us in this regard. Looking at the size of the individual, the home, and this world, I have become nothing. I have not even become an instrument. But without a conviction that I must be able to do it and that I can, whatever you hear or know will either be destroyed by despair, bring about destruction through violence, or plunge you into futility due to the arrogance of possessing profound thoughtfulness. If any such situation arises, it is impossible for you to recover. This is the flaw that has persisted to date.

[GV1:P1582] Bringing about a change from this is possible only if we work with utmost dedication. This work means instilling goodness in whichever parts of our mind have functional capacity. Evil is what causes destruction. The means to sustain this must exist in our imagination. That is not a small matter. What is meant by the imagination to sustain it is—when one begins to draw a picture, it is depicted according to the mental prowess of the person illustrating it. It will be as beautiful as the way people imagine that picture. When some people imagine it, it does not turn out right. That is called their mental refinement. Or let it be called culture.

[GV1:P1583] Similarly, we have no knowledge about the cause of origin or its destructive cause. However, by visualizing it through imagination and with the intellect serving as an aid, it is being transformed into action. Thus, this imagination is primarily about depicting the purity of the mind of the person visualizing it, or its inherent sacred state. Therefore, if we are to attain that sacred state, we must first possess humility. This special imaginative faculty that can reside in humility grows in this manner. The attitude that nurtures this, whether in the little children accompanying us, in adults, or in elderly great souls—they are all shaping this mind. But it is pointless to visualize something without keeping any of this in mind. We must have the awareness that we need to raise children with such imagination, and we must possess the sense of responsibility to nurture any child who wishes to grow in that imagination. Then, once that child grows up in that manner, the child itself will know that they are nothing else. Having reached a nature of becoming nothing, if there is any truth beyond this, the child develops the consciousness that they have a duty and obligation to provide guidance to reach there. Be it a boy or a girl, it should be so. Without such an understanding, we cannot grow. We will become exhausted. Seeing everyone's actions, one might feel, "I cannot do anything amidst this vile behavior, I cannot even get a drop of love, there is no action left here anymore to live, nurture, and learn," and out of such despair, one might sink to the very bottom. This is not right. One should not reach a path of such despair. It will disappoint us and drag others down as well. We should not fall into such matters.

[GV1:P1584] For this, what I state first is the knowledge out of respect for the things we have received—ensure what we are capable of adopting. Along with that, after understanding who we are, we must understand what our lineage is. We must establish a true realization within ourselves: "What ability do I have? Nothing at all. Yet, through this special imagination, I can advance by informing, knowing, and acting." This is the primary duty. For this, a state of pure life must emerge within us, and we have not been given any other nature but to demonstrate it through our own lives. Nor is it possible to have one in the future. Now, evaluating the truth that has been imparted, our actions should reach the children in all corners of this world so that they can attain the merit of this action. One must always be vigilant to acquire a fortune that is a blend of the determination to deliver it, that detachment, honesty, humility, and merit. Fix this much firmly in yourselves first. It is your ignorance to think that Vivekananda or people like him did not take that path. Because they did not come for that purpose. They have come for the attainment of life that many people like you desired. They might not need to work for their lives like someone sitting hunched over with crossed legs tied in a string. Their actions may have nurtured the land, the nation, and the world. That will lead to the Satya Yuga. Because we do not attempt to grow like this, we end up adopting the justification given by someone who examines the pages of history. They would be offering it carrying an imitative nature of their scholarship. We cannot grow by utilizing that.

[GV1:P1585] For instance, let us consider the scholars who have studied Sanskrit. It is unheard of that they ever agreed on anything. Bringing in fishermen who languished as mere destitutes without literacy, making them do all this, taking on his attire and language, they nurtured them by giving them something true. He takes what he sees day by day, packs it, and consumes it as he goes. Someone wrote a shloka here and sent it across. If a reply shloka was not received, they were ready to slaughter him right then—such was the method of our ancestors. This was pointed out not to declare whether they were bad or good. We just need to observe this. What has benefited us now is the alphabet of the Westerner, isn't it? Since we recognized and respected it, even though they came to plunder and destroy us, they gave us a language and turned us into merchants. Shouldn't we respect this? Respecting the merchant in the place of a merchant, we must live as an owner ourselves. If we speak Vedanta, none of this will be effective. What is required here is knowledge. If the end of the Veda is not marked, will our affairs proceed here? That is not what I am stating. Good, not bad, fine, we respected all that. But whatever is necessary for the daily conduct here today—we must accomplish that. What follows is wisdom. That will arrive on its own. So, the objective is to perceive our own flaws. Then identify where we went wrong, was it from our father, our wife, or our husband?

[GV1:P1586] We have two paths to escape from this. One is the path of the Devas, and the other is the path of the Rishis. This path of the Devas was appropriate in the Treta Yuga and the Dwapara Yuga. Today is Kali Yuga. In Kali Yuga, even this Deva must take birth as a human. He is an intelligent human being. That intellect belongs to the Deva. His life is that magnificent. It is beneficial if we catch and eradicate this distortion of life. Today, if you affectionately keep close the children whom you consider useless and ask them if what they did was wrong, ninety-eight out of a hundred will agree. Once the mistake is admitted, it can be rectified effortlessly. There is no need to doubt that. Why? Nature is favorable to us. Those who practiced kingship back then have all been ousted. Those who were practicing Vedic rites became Tantrics. Now, it is not the Vedic path that is being practiced. It is Tantra. Tantra, Yantra, Mantra—didn't you comprehend that this is a trick? Now he has departed. The Vaishya and the Shudra together acquired merit through action. Earning fortune through merit, he has been compelled to seek the path to liberation. That is the era we are in. Why doubt here? You should be capable of this. It is sufficient if you do not keep looking at the old things. I am not stating that the old should be completely discarded. Observe the mistake as it is and accept that mistake as ours. Otherwise, do not assume it can be altered. Then, if we just correct that mistake, it will attain purity by the third generation.

[GV1:P1587] Guruvani
