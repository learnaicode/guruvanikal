[GV1:P1476] January 1985

[GV1:P1477] When we reside in an ashram as an ascetic or in any other capacity—whether male or female—we must not stay there with a divided mind. If a divided mind arises, you will be unable to function there. Sometimes, you may have to stand with absolute obstinacy. You might have to act that way even with householders. Why is that said? Householders will sometimes behave obstinately. On such occasions, the institution needs dynamic and courageous young people who can say, 'Whoever you are, keep your obstinacy to yourself.'

[GV1:P1478] There are those who quarrel with each other when entrusted with a task. If that happens, how much will those who stand ready to serve grow? Look at where their minds are stationed. How good will they become if they act like that? All this will spread harm for tomorrow and destroy everything before subsiding. Then it is not a genuine effort (shrama). That effort will later turn into an unwanted effort. Therefore, you must be very careful about this. Those of you who live, come, and go here do not traditionally possess this wisdom. Similarly, you must reflect upon how much humility you should attain, how much good you have done, how much more good you have left to do, how much sacrifice you have made, what all you have been able to achieve through your sacrifice, and how you must live and survive in sacrifice. One who stays without being able to sacrifice will achieve nothing. Only this sacrifice will endure for you.

[GV1:P1479] You lack the culture to maintain an institution. For that, you must establish it, protect the lineage, and use its culture to eradicate your flaws. Thus, you must become a worthy and virtuous lineage. It is ignorant girls who come here. We keep them here for 4 days before letting them go. This is because thoughts of children, wife, and husband will come and cause her mind to ache. In agony, she will even fall ill. Despite whatever happens, the husband, daughter, or money will not come to the rescue. This body cannot even be lifted up. It is then that she thinks she could have gone there that day and lived in a good path, but she ruined everything; if she gets an opportunity now, she will live like that, dying while saying, "Oh God, if only I had realized this back then." She is saved. Because the next time she is born, it will be with this thought. That is the secret of it. It is for that reason they are kept like that.

[GV1:P1480] Whether it is a man or a woman, any thief or rogue, this thought will come to them at the time of death. They will pray with their hands on their chest, thinking, 'I could not achieve this.' Thus, in the next birth, they will be born into hell, and then they will live by crying out in pain, working, and suffering. Amidst that, saying, 'Oh God, if I had obtained this, I could have offered it to God,' they will share a portion of everything they get with God. Thus, they will accumulate merit. Have they not been saved? This will reach *dharma*, and then *karma*. See—*karma* and *dharma* will come together. Even if the husband objects, she will somehow hide a little for God. That is how she will live. Their children will also learn by observing this. They will hide even more for God than she did. In this manner, their lineage will be saved. That is what was said earlier. That one should get an opportunity to cry out and die like that.

[GV1:P1481] As we do each of these things in this manner, our lineage will grow immensely. Pray with great agony for this. That is our weapon. I have only this prayer to give you. Pray, envisioning that God should do everything for this. Here, you—men and women, young and old—are striving very sincerely and spending money according to your abilities. If a small child weaves, the first earning from it is brought and offered here. We know what weaving yields today. There is a realm of such profound affection within this. We must respect it. It is inevitable that this will manifest either in you or at least in your lineage.

[GV1:P1482] Having prayed for such a lineage, we have also gained good knowledge today. This is the will of God, not ours. Doing this prayer here cannot actually be called a prayer. Because it should be done by starving, crying out, and enduring hardship. Here, you do not have to struggle like that. There are all kinds of facilities. God has provided you with that much comfort. God is thus making us do it with the power of command. You, who were able to play a part in it, will have positions and honors; that is certain. But it is enough if you do not develop the tendency, as mentioned earlier, to do half of this and then lose everything in the end. Even if you live for only four days, those four days should be lived virtuously. May you attain that course of *karma*! You must live understanding this.

[GV1:P1483] From today onwards, we will lead a good life. Go forth praying with the determination that we shall strive for a better tomorrow. When you come next time, go forth praying with a little more resolve or a little more strength. Thus, become stronger each time. Ultimately, people will only be able to see you as role models. Conduct yourselves in such a way that others feel that what you say is right and that is the world. Do it, recognizing it as your need. That is what is required.

[GV1:P1484] Guruvani
