GURUVANIKAL · June 1992 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0283 to GV2:P0297
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

June 1992

We have received a new method. Satyayuga, Tretayuga, Dvapara, Kali - there are four eras like this and each era has its own nature. The dharmas in the four eras function in four ways. In Satyayuga, both men and women will act without error. Satyayuga has a duration equal to the other three yugas combined. The change of time and the dharma of time is God's will. It is in the mind. We humans are the children of those who belonged to the lineage of Manu and acted desiring the presence of God.

Hinduism has a very long history and calculation. Compared to Hinduism, Christianity's is a very small calculation. Hinduism is more than just a religion, it is a culture. The invading Brahmins settled on the banks of the five rivers and studied our history. They were people who saw God with their intellect. What they saw here was a group of honest people without deceit. They were people who would sleep anywhere.

They came and settled at a time when the era changed. Because the era changed, it became impossible to see God directly. Therefore, they went to the forests and seashores, looked at the horizon, and did penance in many ways. They saw various kinds of lights and forms. What they saw, they carved on trees and stones. When they thought about it keeping it in their minds, they got words from the light. They got mantras. The great sages discovered each of these things in this way. Many things were carved on stones and given form and color. Westerners called it the mind. The consciousness called mind. The light called consciousness. That is the truth. We must learn to know that it is the truth. We are people who know the truth with our eyes, not with our intellect. The immigrant Brahmins came here and started practicing each thing. They started ruling the country intelligently. They made the bitter rulers kings. They made them gods. They made people believe so. Tretayuga and Dvapara yuga were thus ruled by 'visible gods'. In Dvapara, the real began to be manifested. Finally, Krishna came. With that, it was proved that all this exists and that God can show what exists. This is history.

The Aryans who studied history created caste. Historically speaking, Kali belongs to the Shudra. That is, he is the authority to handle governance. He is entitled to liberation. Then, intelligently, they put a price even on letters. They took the Vedic into their hands. Then they made the Shudra beat the Vaishya. This diverted the course of the dharma of time. Instead, for at least two or three generations, the Vaishya beat the Shudra. Thus, the Shudra did slave labor and became the owner of the synonym 'ignorant'. The Shudra class, which was subjected to untouchability and ostracized for more than ten generations, did slave labor, lacked food, became dark and exhausted. After that, he became arrogant, a great sinner, and a victim of God's wrath.

It is God's decree that there should be a king at the end of Kali. Otherwise, the king should have ruled all this time. Thus, the one who chased and the one who ran all fell into the pit together. Paternity was lost. The one who lost paternity died, decayed, and that 'pitru' wandered, changed signs, and begot fatherless children. In the meantime, many great souls came and said, "You must see this irony of fate." Then these fatherless children called this great man "fatherless" and chased him away beating him. Thus, they made the Muslims rule there.

In Kerala, the Brahmin was accepted as the priest. Because of his tricks, we were unable to know anything. The English who came here were not respectable people. Those who were unfit for any department there became Viceroys here. They came here and created everything - dress, language, education, law, and government.

Today, an era of hybridity has arrived. Hybridity for humans and even for a blade of grass. This is a very serious matter. We must keep the word of God so that this does not spread to our children. God is in your favor. Jesus and the Prophet have made it clear - that messengers will come and mislead. At first, I had doubts about it. Even when an avadhuta repeated this at Varkala Santhigiri, I doubted it. But the scoldings I had to hear to reform the girls were not minor. Finally, when I received the message, I was convinced.

We need an assurance here. This is not for a caste or a community. If Thiruvalluvar and Naranath Bhranthan did this, can't we do it? Think that if my clan (lineage) will improve, I might die for it. If those who come to the institution are worthless, they must be reformed by enduring any sacrifice. We can certainly carry this forward. Because through the Prophet, through Jesus, through Sree Narayana, through Chattampi Swami, God has given us the experience and qualification suited for the times. Even if cut into pieces inch by inch, we can say boldly -- wasn't Jesus crucified...... We will build up the Guru's dignity. Jesus was ready to be crucified. Sankaracharya offered his head. But the disciples opposed.

A sacrificer will instill goodness in another by enduring anything. The disciple must be extremely attentive. Devotion without attention is useless. It is attentive devotion. If there is such a thing, one will know whatever happens anywhere. Anything must be done for those who come to be cured of illness. Even if one has to be cut into pieces inch by inch, it must be done. There are those who speak Vedanta only for the good of their own children. That is not the case for those who come for service. Whatever they need, they will definitely attain from here. A position must be assigned for such people. One must be able to get done what is needed at the right time. Do not wait for a good time to come later. That might not be obtained sometimes. There is a sacrifice of its own for this. If that is understood through action, there is nothing more to be achieved beyond that. If there is sacrifice, one will not have to beg anywhere. I lived in such a way that in humility, all qualifications will be subservient to this, more than you. You do not need a model greater than this. This model can be carried around with any amount of pride. No siddhi or vidya is needed for this. Only the heart's experience you have received is enough. You must pray to get that heart's experience until the end.

Do not oppose those who do good things. Do not disrespect them. It is difficult for the greatness of ashram life to come to you. There are two reasons for that. One, the South Indian kings were Shudras in Dravidian culture. Two, the North Indian culture was not received by Nairs and those below them. Therefore, their character will not come to you. In addition to all this, you saw and learned the rhythmic worship practices of Tamil Nadu. Because importance was given to Shaktheyam, you became ignorant. A section of such ignorant people became Christians. Instead of learning good things, they copied European culture here. Thus, Hindu culture disappeared here.

India is a natural land. Our culture has an unimaginable depth. It is because life was not organized in accordance with it that our knowledge was lost. We know nothing of Upanishads, Vedas, and Puranas. Renewing this situation

Cannot be raised. Understanding this, there should be a change. The little children playing odd and even here are much more knowledgeable than today's parents. They are capable of knowing any events happening in the world sitting here. But the thought in them should be developed. Adults should live understanding this. To love truth, there is no greater achievement than this.

Attention and devotion are the most important. Then, as the author of the Gita said, everything should be done with intellect and the power of yoga. This is a great fortune. This fortune is for those who take care of it. Detachment is essential. The detachment to endure and forgive anything and to dedicate for something with humility. In this hybrid era, that is the only way to rise from our miserable state. We must strive until we ourselves become the reality. Any trivial matter should be viewed seriously. Physical and mental cleanliness is paramount. Our life should not be like that of the capitalists.

We must have a perfect awareness of the example we should set for the world. One should have a clear knowledge about Sandhyavandanam. Three, six, nine, twelve -- of these, six and twelve are important. Pakkanar's life of detachment is an example for that. Everyone should be loved. Behave truthfully. Whoever comes for whatever reason, there should be an eagerness to know it with enthusiasm. That is humility. There should be punctuality. Help each other. Abandoning the detachment caused by mutual enmity, one should maintain the detachment of mutual love, help, and cooperation. Goodness has disappeared from the Shudra culture. One should seek a lifestyle that helps goodness to come fully. Through that, the karma to build Kalidharma should be brought about. That is our way.

Guruvani
