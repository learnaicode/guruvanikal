Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P1111 to GV2:P1121
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

January 1997

Even if one does everything with the complete understanding that God is the core of the truth being revealed and taught, mistakes can happen - errors will occur. There is a reason for this. When we believe in a prophet or a Guru, by taking refuge in them, we are reaching God. As the disciple advances a little further, he feels that he and the Guru are not different. This realization will pave the way to the soul of truth. Even the different phases of life's journey can be seen. The variations in the journey of the soul, seen in the states of waking, dreaming, and deep sleep, and departing from the waking-sleep state to the Turiya state, this journey makes everyone alike. Thus, the Guru, who is the essence of truth situated within the bliss of the soul, encompasses countless formless skies. Whether one is a sectarian, a follower of a prophet, or a follower of a Guru, those who do not believe that there is only one God will have doubts. That is why it was said that mistakes will happen.

Even if one believes in the truth of the Guru, mistakes will occur if a situation arises where there is a lack of awareness about what needs to be observed and known. This is the danger of having a blemish (doubt) in faith. Such people are ignorant of Guru Dharma.

If faith remains unblemished, one will not be deterred by any circumstance. They do not care about wife, husband, children, wealth, power, or education. The theoretical ignorance that has existed in their family until now must change. With love, respect, and humility, it

What he needs is an act of redemption. This is all he requires.

This is the ideal model of the Grihasthashrama (householder stage of life). Such a householder has no need for caste or religion. There is no need for knowledge belonging to any sense of justice within the human system. It will not become alien by gaining strength in the ignorance that speaks and alienates each other. This is the extent of saying that the guru and the disciple are not different, but one. In the end, there will be no one to come along. When there is no one to come along, only that truth comes along.

I have come to tell this truth. I will not accept the guise of any theory. I have not come to gather people. Seeing your right and wrong, if you can correct the wrong in any way, I have come to fulfill that. You should know the Guru in that way. Otherwise, mistakes will happen. It will be difficult to even spend anything in the name of the Guru. Without knowing what the Guru is, that difficulty will not go away. Let me give an example, it is the history of a great royal family. For that, a story has been fabricated that he cleverly left his house. He did not go cleverly like that. He was a person who lived according to the Guru's principles. His own Guru told him - you have everything, but a situation will arise where your children will be of no use. What should be done when there is no fate for children? Karma can be done, all karmas can be done. He often asked his wife - what will you do after I am gone? I will just sit. Therefore, I will also die with you (it is a matrilineal system, isn't it). Alright, let it be so. He decided to test this. He went to Bhartrihari and practiced yoga. Through that, he trained to hold his breath from one yama to another. Thus, one day, after eating food and folding betel leaves, he sat like that and said again - the Guru has said that you have no fate. He is a king. Being a king, when he had no children or anything like that, he did a trick to know if what his wife said about dying with him was true. Because he had practiced yoga, he held his breath and lay down as if unconscious. Then the wife thought, he is gone. They did not beat their chests and wail. Because the husband's sister is nearby. The sister and children will come and take all the savings. Therefore, deciding that there is no need for fighting and grabbing, holding back the crying, they loaded gold coins and valuables into a bullock cart and sent it away. A gold rope was hung above where he was lying. It was impossible to take this. She found a way for that. She took a thick blanket, covered this body, stood on it, untied this gold rope, and put it in the cart. After that, she came back and started beating and crying. Then he also regained consciousness.

Later he took a piece of cloth, tore it into two, made one piece a loincloth, wore one piece around his waist and walked away. He came back again and sold everything he had for whatever price he could get. He gave the gold coins and other things to his sister. He gave to other relatives as well. He gathered the rest and gave it as alms. Giving alms means giving to the poor. Alms should not be given to the poor, but to the mendicants (sadhus). He does not know. Then he walked away. He wandered around like that for a long time. In the meantime, he would also go to his sister's house for alms. It was a shame for them that he came to the door as a beggar like this. Therefore, deciding that he should not live, they made an ada (a type of sweet) and gave it in his hand. It is said that he bought it happily and said this. The bronze ada will burn the house. Thus, it is said that this sister's house also burned down in the end. There is such a history.

What should be understood from this is that no matter who says they will die with you, or whatever else is said, things will only turn out like this. When there is no one to come along, only that truth comes along. My job is to say that whatever anyone can do for that truth, do it without any difficulty. Only in this way can I resolve your bad things.

One's own parents and ancestors are wandering without any means for birth, karma, or liberation. Giving karma, birth, or liberation to these individual souls

Self-action is what needs to be fulfilled. There is no point in saying that the Milky Way flowed for this fulfilling action. No matter who, what, or how it is made to flow, it will not flow. Because each soul is the remainder of the course of karma that has been acquired in many births. The effort of a householder is to describe the completion of dharma while standing in the attainment of such a birth. It is through that effort that one must reach the action to liberate this world.

This is the dharma of Kali. Will this change if you cry out in pity, holding on to the bottom of some story told by someone, after destroying a household dharma that is to be fulfilled one by one? You will not be able to extract anything from this story. What you need is to become the owner of a completely perfect color (varna). Become a soul that brings blessedness to it. Your lifetime should be the holy days of the soul developed as father, mother, children, husband, and wife, who are destined to bring blessedness to this soul. If you do not have the determination that the aspect of that color can be completely attained by you, such a life itself is in vain. The purpose of life is not to clear the doubts of the mind. What will you gain by clearing doubts like that?

Where does my soul stand in the action of attainment, how should the soul merge in Turiya after passing through the waking, dreaming, and deep sleep states, and how should that course of karma be broken later? This is the dharma called our field of action, which is completed by the soul. No one can break the course of karma except through the dharma of the Guru-disciple relationship. What is the Guru-disciple relationship? The Guru's sense of dharma that liberates whatever deity traditions have come and gone in the disciple's lineage. This relationship should not evolve for money and power, for the sake of the world's verbal state. The success of this relationship should be to acquire the state of color completion in the merit of the action that travels with difficulty and pain through anything. What should be copied into your children who take this up? Seeing the completion of the three states of waking, dreaming, and deep sleep, the responsibility of untying the knot to Turiya is handed over to the Guru. When the Guru unties that knot, the disciple's soul becomes completely free. This is freedom.
