GURUVANIKAL · April 1993 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0483 to GV2:P0494
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

April 1993

Our duty is to transform the Kali of Kaliyuga into a human being. That is very difficult. Seeing the attire of today's doctors, the patient would die instantly. The wisdom of our ancestors is such that they praised the foreigners when the Zamorin was defeated. Westerners came here and spread their attire and culture. They ruled us and taught us how to rule. The poor can only imitate, right? The king had to obey what the Viceroy said. The people became slaves too. Not only our culture, religion, and customs, but even our heritage became alien to us. To the one who studied philosophy, history is theirs, and our culture is merely a story.

There is one thing we need to understand clearly. In Kali, there is no place for God. In Kali, the place is for the Guru. Many great souls came and said this in this fourth quarter. But we did not accept it. When Moses said he would receive the word from God, his contemporaries mocked him.

When Jesus came and spoke after two thousand years, he was crucified. But Jesus appeared to the one who believed. Even if they were poor fishermen, they worked passionately. They even had to endure severe crusades for twenty-one years. But they multiplied many times over. They even overthrew the government. Among them, they also did a favor by coming to India. If one adopted the name Madan or Mathai, a situation arose where they could walk along the road.

In the Varnasrama Dharma, the Brahmin had supreme comfort. The Kshatriya had governance and the Vaishya had trade. The Shudra was excluded from everything and kept outside. He has no knowledge or sense of dharma. He only knows how to imitate. Despite being comfortable in every way, without finding peace, when Westerners came here and grew their hair, the beedi roller and the boatman here started doing the same. The reason for this culture to degrade like this is the mother's lack of knowledge. The Pitru (ancestor) is not pure. A child will be born in our house only according to the zodiac sign the Pitru stands in. An intelligent and rational child will only be born in a family that has performed Pitru Shuddhi (ancestral purification). The Brahmin is ahead in this matter. Because of Pitru Shuddhi, only intelligent ones will be born in his family. But everyone born in a Shudra's family will become a drunkard. Even if they have knowledge and intelligence, this drunkard will only walk astray.

The Brahmin has done another big trick. Out of four marriages, three are Sambandham and one is Veli. Three women from lower castes for Sambandham and a Veli from a Brahmin Illam. If there is no woman from the Brahmin Illam, their karma is incomplete. Or if there is no woman, they will build a Yoni-Kandam. That will also be shown as a mudra in Tantra. You are satisfied if you see Mantra and Tantra, aren't you? In this degraded state, what knowledge have you been able to impart to your children?

Not a single Acharya has performed Pitru Shuddhi. Shankaracharya did Vedic rituals. Buddha said, just believe in me, everyone. There are plenty of Pitrus who have reached the position of Buddha. There are elevated Pitrus standing everywhere and in all positions. Not just Buddha's, there are Pitrus in the positions of all those who have risen.

Buddha saw the light. In the Quran, it is called rays of light. Christians have called it tongues of fire. We have here all the things said by gurus of all religious sects. The children here can see all this. I said that no one has done Pitru Shuddhi. We are doing that karma here with complete purity. We catch all these Pitrus and merge them into the Guru's light. After that, you have no Pitru. That is, you will not have to suffer the curse of the Pitru anymore. After Pitru Shuddhi, you should look to grow the lineage. The result of the karma you do today will be produced after twenty-five years. We should not insult anyone. Everyone is right as far as their matters are concerned. But we need the knowledge to ask and know about our matters. At least our home should be united. That is why you come here. Not to become a sanyasi. If you impart this culture, sanyasis will be born in your home. So, what and how should you, who are the basis for creating such a culture, accept?

The culture in this world itself is such that any great person bows down before their own children. The karma done here is capable of recreating this culture. That is the Pitru Shuddhi here. If you have only two children, let both be united. If there are ten, all ten. There is no ability or knowledge of mine in this. But it is one step ahead of all abilities and all knowledge. No matter which rich or worthy person comes, there is something to learn, know, follow, and be a model here. No one can ever say that this is not worthy.

There is no point in throwing it away in the smoke just because it is good. It will grow only through you. If it was just for me to survive, there is no need to struggle this much. I could just wear a loincloth and sit quietly as a Swami. Everyone would take care of my needs. But I prefer to live as one among you. During my time at Sivagiri, I could go to the Guru by lying that I was going to Muttakkadu. I spent ten years lying in Kadappathu. Then the Guru said, 'You go to the Sannidhanam, you have something to get there.' He meant for me to go to Kalladi Masthan. So I went one day. Met him. Bought him tea. When I returned after seeing him, the Guru said, 'He has given you everything he had to give. He will not let you near him anymore.' How great is the Guru and what the Guru gave. One day I went to see Puthura Swami. When I went, he was performing a homam in the scorching heat. Everyone who came was bringing firewood. I said, 'You should not trouble this poor man like this.' When I said that, someone came to hit me. I said that Puthura Swami would leave that place within four months. It happened exactly like that. Seeing that a bundle of old clothes Kalladi Masthan gave someone to keep would cause harm in the future, I threw it from the Poovampara bridge. Soon after, he said he saw a flood in a vision. After a month, the Poovampara bridge was washed away.

What can you learn from all this? You cannot learn anything by following such people without understanding anything. You will not get any culture. You are the ones who have to impart a lasting culture. For that, God has given us an idea that has never been given before. It is your daughter herself who performs this karma (Pitrushuddhi) looking into the light and speaks in front of you.

You must be vigilant to grow this lineage. You must make this a traditional knowledge. For that, you must be the most honest, detached, humble, and mutually loving. When you cook food, serve it, or do anything, you should pray that this ego be destroyed. Then the blood, flesh, and marrow will change. Thus you can change everything. Those living with you are living honestly. The thought will come that I cannot live a life without them. It is enough not to dress up the mind anymore. Wear whatever clothes you want on the body. Only if we adopt a determination and struggle harder than the determination the fishermen adopted for Jesus's ideology, will this be useful to the home, the land, and the country to that extent.

Guru Vani
