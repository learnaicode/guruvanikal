Guruvani October 1995

You do not know an action to earn merit through birth. Therefore, children without dharma are born to you. Children without dharma are not eligible to earn merit. Children who are not eligible for merit never become heirs to fortune. We have all fallen completely into such karma. The intended action with your children is to liberate them from this condition.

The earth has a magnetic field. Similarly, the nine planets have magnetic fields. It stands as the solar system. How have we understood this? Very few people have understood this properly. It is not good to apply knowledge in this. There is a set of people who do not have the innate tendency to believe whether God exists or not. They are the ones who apply yoga, rituals, and knowledge in this. The state of light inherent in the body is brought about by bodily exercise according to the rules of yoga. That is something that only suits oneself. It is of no use to anyone else. Some words in that rule will match. Some words will not match. That is their own shortcoming.

Some people get thousands of visions. That is, they come in a way that cannot be expressed in words. It can be seen whether the eyes are open or closed. It is an action where the mind ceases to function. This

Karma comes to the purity of man. There are those who get visions at every border without continuous vision. This nature as a whole, that is, the vision that shows what this worldly nature is. For that, he only needs faith. Faith in a word. The word of a clear living soul, like Jesus, like the Prophet, or one who has moved away from sectarian ways like Shaivism, Shaktism, and Vaishnavism - faith in that word. This should be a soul who has known at least up to the state of the Trinity. Or else, they will be taken to some corner and left there. The vision will become a mad vision. That is one. Another is seeing karma standing in a subtle state and how it is done and made to be done in the gross state. There are many such ways of vision. Whatever the way, one should proceed without thinking for oneself, holding on to the Guru and taking refuge in the Guru.

Visions will go on without limit. It will go on in a way that cannot be expressed in words. For example, if we start on a journey, what all will we see until we reach that destination. So, what all would one see if they were to travel around this whole world. There is such a vision. Having seen it like that, one will meet the Guru again. That is its theory. If we set out on a path, we will focus only on the main path. Other things will keep passing by. Another thing is, even if that person is a visionary, Guru, or prophet, for how long and who all can use it. Who all will get this kind of vision and how. This is the vision that shows madness in return - mad vision.

There is a vision that shows many past lives. A vision where everything done in whatever period in the relationship between disciple and Guru comes in sequence. If the disciple is not that knowledgeable, he will be given a vision in a way of lesser knowledge and brought up. An knowledge will be given showing what the disciple's soul has been purified of through many births. For man, there are colors that are knowledge and non-knowledge within the five senses themselves. Standing on that path that goes through the five senses, five sheaths, and five elements, one must go to another path. Whatever the disciple is entitled to will come. The Guru will not ask if you did this or saw that. That is the Guru's qualification. The disciple must also have a qualification. Otherwise, it will stop after seeing one, two, or ten visions. Even if the disciple comes as one with no qualification, if he takes refuge only in the Guru, he will get the prescribed knowledge of what the movements of the soul that has lived and passed as many signs with his life are, knowing that, what the karma following that knowledge is, and how the hymns of karma are. Even in this, there are variations. One variation is a way where one sees all the attributes of nature, sees the nature of this earth, and sorts out how the action of all these souls is. When speaking of special rules, it is the evolutionary difference of the soul growing from an ant (an ant is a soul that has learned to observe according to its knowledge for its own life) up to an elephant, lion, or rhinoceros. The nature that each must receive for the action of all these souls will be contained in that soul in every way. For example, a theater prop. How many props, big and small, can there be for the need of a play. Similarly in the cinema system. All these are courses of karma captured from this nature. If Vedantins say that all these courses of karma are non-existent, will they cease to exist? Should Vedantic words make them non-existent? Will the good and bad karma experienced cease to exist? There is a capacity for action where the body, mind, and intellect work, will it happen if one says it doesn't exist?

Similarly, what kind of conditions this body has accepted can only be known through the evolution of this soul. One must see the activities of one's own soul, as well as those of many other souls. We see male, female, small, large, and so on. How these bodies change roles according to age - father, mother, husband, wife. Is all this an illusion? Or else a soul should be able to rise to the level of that karma. That is, the soul's capacity for transformation came about like this because of a master plan. It is the special transformation of the soul, taking thousands of births over many periods, that is contained in them. As one grows up like this, a vision of a demon comes, and the thousands of activities of many kinds of demons - one becomes convinced of it oneself. All this is coming before us. Or else those concepts that we and our ancestors worshipped, those roles, their creations, their light, all this comes together before us. Then this karma, which is imagined by Brahma, this cause which is gross, subtle, and causal, is known by the soul. When we close our eyes, we see a model of thousands of births.

We often say, I slept well yesterday, slept without any movement, and so on. Because of sleeping, the body gets rest. Keeping away the karma of the many natures of the soul, its activities, or doing nothing. The soul only knows that it just lay down and slept comfortably like this. That is rest for the body. What if it is a subconscious sleep? Went there, went here, that came, this came, saw like that, saw like this, I became a slave to the police, a slave to the king, many mischievous people harassed me, they all caught me, tied me up and threw me in the forest. Some were received very happily. After all this, then only oneself and the Guru. This is the drama. The special rules within this drama are all these things seen.

Farming in one place, many kinds of farming in one place, a desert with nothing in one place, thus travelled and saw many lands. Saw south, north, east, and west. In some places, somehow - getting on vehicles and not getting on. Travelled even in parts where there are no vehicles. How it was when there were vehicles, how it was when there were none, how it was when there were no objects..... Seeing that it is a karma that one's soul has passed through like this, again only the Guru and oneself.

One might have done many good and bad things with one's life. Seeing a karma that cannot be told to each other, even to oneself and to those who can be said to be the most dear, again only oneself and the Guru. What was the evolutionary state of one's soul in. The soul acted in what has life and what does not, the soul has experience acting in moving and non-moving things. We have also seen that subject of moving the non-moving. Saw that it is like this, its life is in this order. Saw it acting in everything from ants to elephants, reptiles, many birds, thousands of flying creatures. Saw why these reptiles, flying creatures, and birds lived that life.

Saw thousands of patients and many karmas nursing the disease, many karmas finding medicine for it, physicians bringing treatments. Saw that in the field where poison should not be given, that poison itself is prepared and given, and similarly everything is prepared and given. Then, from these leaves that are prepared and given, to whatever is in nature, utilizing all of it, it is about the virtuous aspects of that karma that is experienced and finished as one's body and one's soul, this is, in short, what can be called vision.

Guruvani
