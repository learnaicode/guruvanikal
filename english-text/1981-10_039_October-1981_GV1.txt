[GV1:P1047] October 1981

[GV1:P1048] Disciple: What medium do you suggest to stop those who are running today in the name of modernism?

[GV1:P1049] Guru: What is needed is not to stop them. A situation will arise where both the one who runs and the one who makes them run will stop running. There is a proverb that the leg pain is the same for the one who runs and the one who chases. He made the other run because he had more health. The other is running to escape from death. This one's is the frantic run of arrogance. Due to excessive panting, this one will also become like the other. The knowledge given by God that certain things will happen in the world is here. If this is the period when we got the notification, religious scholars and prophets celebrate that great souls have said this even before this. Jesus and the Prophet have indicated this in various parts in the culture of Sanatana.

[GV1:P1050] Disciple: I wish to hear a brief description of what that is.

[GV1:P1051] Guru: What you describe as modern includes machinery, war equipment, and rocket launches discovered through science, right? Very soon, within twenty-five years, some minor untoward incidents will occur in India itself in a way that it will come to a halt. Along with that, there will be numerous opinions and opportunities to carefully study whether this is needed or not. The notification also indicates that a stage might come where India will abandon this along with that. Even if it doesn't happen then, if it is delayed a bit, within three hundred and fifty years, an experi

[GV1:P1054] Disciple: Why do you say that India discovered this? Wasn't it the Westerners who discovered all this?

[GV1:P1055] Guru: No. Take aeronautics itself as an example. It was a sage named Bharadwaja who wrote it. But when this was discovered, the reason for proving that its capability is very huge is that today we have only one thing to say - that glass which does not break even if hit by arrows or bullets was fitted in the Pushpaka Vimana. Ignoring the Guru's command that no matter what this is here, it will lead to unwanted destruction, that disciple went to Lanka with the stubbornness that he would implement it somewhere. It was created there. Then he reached India with it. He also abducted a girl who was in India. History says that because of that, India suffered disgrace, and the country called Lanka became very degraded and its regime was destroyed.

[GV1:P1056] However, a truth has been revealed in this meanwhile. People do not understand it in that way. This was not done for that woman. Because only if she is abducted, the strength can be known. It was to show that. Proof of this is the history that she was kept in the most special places beautifully and purely without being insulted in any way, and Mandodari herself was particular about keeping her so. From this, if we observe carefully, we can understand that it was not the enmity between kings, but to show the power of this Pushpaka Vimana technology, it was a fight between a guru and a disciple. To show the magnitude and weight of the knowledge, another king was caught. He was astonished to see this. When that happened, destruction became its by-product. Therefore, isn't it clear that this was invented in India itself and that India faced a nature of its destruction even back then? With this much evidence, it would not be correct to say that Westerners invented all this. It is true that we did not venture into any of this later due to the destruction of those times. Thus, after ages and epochs passed, this fell into the hands of a foreigner who arrived here. They translated it into their language and gave it back to the world as their own. Evidence for this are the then universities in India such as Nalanda and Takshashila. While it still stands as evidence today, it cannot be said that the Westerners invented this. The present science - worldٹ indications are that within twenty-five years, the destruction caused by this is likely to happen in other countries even more than in India. Muslims are a group that shows nobility religiously, racially, and administratively. One reason is that they will not let others into their administration and religious solidarity for good or bad. Therefore, infighting occurred among them. It can be seen that some countries will move forward for some more time, create conflicts, and step aside. Similarly, within thirty years in India, some prominent political figures and people of sattvic nature will join together and raise a flag of peace. As if in its shade, two great men will come forward with idealism in the Arabian countries. To accept and act upon it, giving it existence and giving its value and position so as to achieve it, two children are being born in the Arabian countries at this opportunity. They will replicate there the perfect form of peace seen in India then. Similarly, in Western countries, there will be clashes between powerful nations and otherwise. Likewise, communist countries will split into two and fight fiercely. They will face destruction from foreign countries as well. When that happens, not only will all countries fight each other, but the desire for peace will also grow similarly. Then it will be India that has to come to the forefront for that. Because it was India that invented all this. Therefore, even during its time of destruction, India itself will have to come forward and act.

[GV1:P1057] Disciple: Does India or you yourself have any suggestions or concepts to give regarding this?

[GV1:P1058] Guru: Yes. One of them is happening here now. The Akhandanama conducted as per the notification.

[GV1:P1059] The notification that some great souls are being born in this continent for peace - it has not been clearly obtained where. The message is that it will originate within three months. It could be in any corner of the world. But to the God who gave us an opportunity to struggle mentally and physically for that, not just the children of India, but all the children of the world should be happy, and by continuously praying for giving us such an opportunity, we must achieve fulfillment.

[GV1:P1060] Disciple: What is required to attain such a capability?

[GV1:P1061] Guru: For that, all of us believers, without any distinction of class or religion, and without being entangled in the evils of the Varna-Ashrama Dharma seen today, must unite as intelligent children, standing shoulder to shoulder, and raise a national organization imbued with true devotion to God and humility. Through this, the intellect and strength of the people will become even more vigorous and resolute. Today, the community in India that is most appropriately positioned, combining intellect, ability, capability, and shrewdness, belongs to the Brahmin class. Discarding the Varna-Ashrama Dharma, which is their foundation stone, a stone suitable for the era of mixture described by the author of the Gita as Kali Yuga must be firmly established there. They must be able to build a complete society devoid of class or caste distinctions by standing united with others. Otherwise, they must not wallow in the class distinctions seen today. First, step away from the system where there is a special form of worship for them and another for the rest. Instead of anchoring everyone in the Tantric practices seen today, anchor them in true worship of God. Alongside them, who employ all their intellect and ability for this purpose, hold the hands of others and lift them up to the forefront. If they are unable to do this, ideal individuals will emerge from among other classes and implement it. No one will be able to stop them. Then, others will drain away all the assets they have built up, and they will have to suffer the consequences of the power of sin. It would be best if they could collectively undertake both the good and bad deeds they have committed.

[GV1:P1062] Disciple: Based on what you have said, as an intimation received through divine will, shouldn't we consider that God has blessed this to become a lineage of Gurus, established at an appropriate time in the divine conceptualization, much like what was appointed for various eras in the past?

[GV1:P1063] Guru: Yes, I have no doubt about that. That is exactly why I repeatedly and emphatically stated this in everything I said earlier.

[GV1:P1064] Disciple: Is it because of such certainty that you have said many times—"This came without my knowledge; I did not expect to receive this much; I have done absolutely nothing for this, nor have I done anything within this framework"?

[GV1:P1065] Guru: Yes, exactly. To bestow this upon me—someone with no education, wealth, or enlightenment, possessing not even a shred of knowledge—is, in my understanding, akin to the manifestation of fertile natural beauty in the parched soil at the very peak of a desert. This is similar to the word received by Moses in recent times, the revelation received by the Prophet, and the enlightenment received by Buddha. There is no need to doubt it. When we look at their sufferings and their times, we realize how much sacrifice they made. We can see that they even sacrificed their bodies for it.

[GV1:P1066] Disciple: Is that why you say that what contemporaries have said and done is insufficient, and considering its magnitude, what we have experienced is extremely minuscule?

[GV1:P1067] Guru: Yes, that is precisely the reason. Consider how much suffering there was during the time of Jesus. After that, people who believed in Jesus were rounded up every three to six months, tied to whipping posts, beaten, and tortured in various ways, before being thrown bound into lion enclosures, where the contemporaries of that great soul found amusement in watching them being torn apart and having their blood drunk, urging the spectators to cheer. It was only after this went on for twenty-one years that some form of permission was obtained from the government. Similarly, the Prophet was whipped, chased with brooms, and exiled from his country. Look at the miseries Buddha had to endure despite being a prince! The scene where Dayananda Saraswati was even pelted with sandals. Even the experiences Narayana Guru faced recently, or for that matter, the fate Mahatma Gandhi met immediately after a prayer meeting as a result of his efforts to unite a society. When we look at all these without exception and compare them with the ideals we have received, absolutely nothing has happened to us. A certain degree of ignorance among contemporaries is a common occurrence at all times. It is enough to understand ours as the most minor among them. You only have the challenge of cultivating the habit of knowing and imparting this knowledge. Be prepared to secure all kinds of blessings from God for this purpose. That shall be your duty.

[GV1:P1068] Guruvani
