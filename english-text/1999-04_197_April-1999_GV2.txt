English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

April 1999

What we have seen so far is that if a Mahatma is born in a deserving or undeserving home, a decision is taken to destroy his life immediately. Theories insist that a Mahatma must be born as a king of kings. But if asked what the logic behind this theory is, we mostly see an inability to answer.

Many Mahatmas have come after Sree Krishna. All of them have come with the emergence of another theory. In short, Vyasa and many prophets born as world teachers, as well as the widespread scholarly clan that originated similarly, have determined this emergence. When studying the dharma theory built by Hindu dharma to confine everything within the divine form, it seems as if God himself has given a deceptive method. Krishna's birth itself is one such thing.

Before Krishna's birth, the seven children born to that mother were beaten to death. The eighth one was not found. Therefore, it was not killed. Although Poothana was brought for that purpose, she was destroyed, according to the storytellers. Looking at it that way, whether born as a king of kings, in a cowshed, or without distinction between a hut and a palace, poets have sung the karma story these Mahatmas have experienced in a decorative manner, disconnected from their lives.

If we look at the Mahatmas who came after Sree Krishna, we can see that their lives themselves have been played with. The ignorance of the scholar who presented this as a special story is not small. When looking at this on the path of Yuga Dharma, the truth has been hidden by praising all their lives in a way that time calculation cannot be brought into history. This is what is understood if we look at the long list of poetic imagination decorated in praise and blame. The first example of this is Sree Krishna's life itself.

Hiding the evidence that Sree Krishna came as a Guru, millions of stories depicting him as a god have been fabricated. Keeping the Dharma of being a Guru intact, making Krishna a god and the Gopis as Devadasis, the reality has been hidden through acquired stories without Dharma in the decorative style of great poets, leaving no place for Dharma and losing Karma. To create the story that Vyasa lived when Krishna was alive, Vyasa was specially given the name Krishna Dwaipayana.

Dwaraka sank. When Arjuna gathered everyone and left there as Krishna said, forest robbers looted everything from Arjuna. It is recorded that many were killed and Arjuna was let go. Then it is said that Vyasa said, the boons of the gods and the bow and arrows obtained from it were for you to use when the Lord was present.

It's over. Now we can go to renounce the world. Everyone knows that all this is said to have happened in the place where Gujarat is situated now.

Looking from then to now, the birth of all great souls after Krishna's time has been like a hidden arrow. Because Buddha was born in a royal family, they did nothing then and kept quiet. Finally, when he became a Bodhisattva, they killed him by giving him pork. Similarly, it is said that Jyotiramalingam went with his body. Then only a lamp remained. It is said that those who wrote the story of Rama many ages ago left his body in the river. The poet's imagination is that he was taken somewhere in a chariot. The same is the story of Ramatirtha attaining Jalasamadhi.

Thus, whoever became a Shudra by birth and a Dvija by action, all of them, even mere poets, have been destroyed in this way. What is the history of Kabir Das himself? Although it was written and managed that he was a disciple of Ramadas Swami, no one knows what that hardship was for. Here too, the story has been written and defeated. What is Kabir's devotion to his guru? Even if all the trees that have grown so far and will grow in the world are made into pens, and all the oceans are made into ink, the greatness of the guru will remain - this is Kabir's devotion to his guru.

When looking at the historical presence of great souls, the stories written about them rarely, have destroyed all their experiential truths in the waterless sea (incomprehensible) by putting them in the karma dosha of the ignorant. We must also carefully understand the purpose of writing and keeping the stories of Moses, Buddha, Jesus, and the Prophet who came after Krishna. These miraculous deeds written like this have been made to be done by poor intellectuals. When talking about realization or vision, except for recording it as the light of the world, the form of light, and the flood of light, there too the gods and goddesses have been glorified.

The truth of the birth of Moses and Jesus has been covered up so that it cannot be recognized. Even the part where karma was given to tell the sacrifice of Jesus has been wrong. If there is evidence that the Prophet who came after Jesus was beaten and driven away by the people, that the mountain was set on fire, and when the fire went out, he came down and ate roasted skin - and shouted that the jinn are ruling the world - why couldn't the place of Samadhi of that great man be fully revealed? In fact, as soon as he saw the demonic power of these jinn, he sat in that cemetery itself. Once you sit in one place, you will see urination and defecation for seven or eight days. Finally, he passed away there itself. Even with all this, where was that body taken and laid to rest? Without even keeping that historical truth straight to remind us of this, they are trying to maintain love in a harsher language than Hindus today. By pouring blessedness into the life of that great man in the way of this kind of love, the seekers of sacrifice have kept it as a land of ignorance in such a way that we cannot take it or handle it mutually.

What will happen to our children who take up such karma doshas?

From Sri Krishna till today, no one has been spared. Who can cure the karma dosha that keeps them as a miracle after the time of the great souls? Isn't this the evil desire of the demonic forces that do not agree to take up the karma of the land of sacrifice?

The extraordinary and decisive dharma that shows the endless excellence of yoga and karma, divided into four as yoga, bhakti, karma, and jnana, has been attempted to be illustrated through Sri Ramakrishna and Vivekananda. What can be done about the brave defenders of dharma who cause intellectual destruction by taking up arms in the names of Sri Shankara, Chattampi Swami, and Narayana Guru, who were born in Kerala? They have left the people as complete scarecrows and pushed them into the veil of blindness. Caste, religion, and class hatred have grown in them to such an extent that even if God started forgiving, it would be impossible.

The blue sky is a colorless color that stands beyond the seven stages called jnanabhumika. The path of karma that has walked into the growth of science, which has written the theoretical lines of the nine planets in the language of scholars and laymen, is what exists today. Science should not become miserable and countless. Today's aeronautics is like this. Scientists should think whether these inventions, which can destroy the entire earth like madmen by giving all these names, are needed or not. What is to be achieved by taking the size and weight of each planet? What part of knowledge is going to emerge from it? What can be taken from it other than the path of karma that makes a goat a dog, as the old people say? What to say to these utter fools who travel in such a way that they never get consciousness in life, dress up in silk, leave the language called the crest jewel of scholars, and maintain a culture by dressing up as neither man nor woman, serving karma that burns with intellect! We have nothing to do with this baseless story that cultivates such a sense of humor. We also know that this is the failure of our daily life. Don't we need at least a good imagination?

Will the stage of karma come if the karma to be realized through life is lost by singing, talking, and writing? Is there anything else in this beyond the intellect that obeys or makes one obey by law? Aren't all these seen the playhouses of good and evil that bring God's words into the intellect by singing? Who doesn't know that all this is growing and spoiling the human masses who do not know how to behave knowing that it has come due to the mistake of the yugadharma.

Who doesn't know that only what the court creates by telling the truth in the name of God will happen? What has the legacy of ignorant creators who try to choose good and evil through people like Mugalalyam and Brigadier made us, the human figures who have created so much misery, as warmongers? Dharma has been presented by making us believe that this is the rule of the world. I ask you again, what else is there to say whether the burden of sin that came due to the mistake of the yugadharma is to test the ability of each person? What is that? Know it truly, think.

Guruvani
