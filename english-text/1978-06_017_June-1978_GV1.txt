[GV1:P0321] June 1978

[GV1:P0322] Question: So, should we consider that the medium you intend is a householder (Grihasthashrami)?

[GV1:P0323] Answer: Yes, certainly, that is it. From now on, it is not easy to correct through any other path than this one.

[GV1:P0324] Question: What do you see as such a great obstacle in other paths?

[GV1:P0325] Answer: The good aspects of all other paths are gradually deteriorating. Not because they are not good. There is no shortage of philosophers in India. There is also no shortage of spiritual thinkers. But among them, many families and individuals who worship deities, even among spiritual thinkers in this state, get isolated from society. Not only that, but a nature where only sorrow remains, without even being the basis for spiritual happiness, is seen in everyone. Don't we need at least spiritual happiness, not just external happiness? That is the distortion of these times. Therefore, wherever its flaws are concentrated, let it sprout there, reap its harvest there, and try to improve through it. This was the intention of all the previous Manu lineages. But it went wrong for some reason.

[GV1:P0326] Question: But if that happens, is your intention that the nature of today's government should change?

[GV1:P0327] Answer: What is the doubt in that? Haven't you understood that the ascetic, the yogi, the worker (karmi), the wise (jnani), and the devotee all come from the home? Then what are you doubting? Don't you understand that if administrators come from such homes, all of today's faces of administration will be purified? When it reaches that point, the people will have confidence that good things can be implemented here. The intention of all public servants and all philosophers is more or less the same. Those who believe and worship God or the Supreme Being must have a complete assurance.

[GV1:P0328] Once you believe, there is no such thing as "I". If there is, it is only that which I believe in. Then I became that. It means that became me. Otherwise, if we go by the way seen even in people who believe they have known it today, we will never be able to see and know or experience it at any time. Let what I intend happen, then I will believe. So, if God does not wait for him and make it happen, he does not believe. Then we can see what the depth and weight of their belief really is.

[GV1:P0329] Question: You mentioned in a word that some gurus are also seen, didn't you?

[GV1:P0330] Answer: Yes, that is true. There are many such gurus. Such gurus will convince them that some experiences in the early days are true, and seeing that they are true, they will fully believe the guru. Growing like that, he will reach the level of the guru. The growth that happens after that will reach a state where the guru cannot correct it or the disciple cannot bear it. Danger will happen in such places. Then they will abandon him saying, "My son has grown beyond me." I personally have one or two such experiences.

[GV1:P0331] Question: What are the measures to be adopted to prevent that from happening?

[GV1:P0332] Answer: Devotion—we have been talking about the story of the one who is complete. But for those whose devotion is not complete, preceptors have shown certain paths according to the times. The matter of those who changed according to the times is what was mentioned in between. There are three types of devotion: blind devotion (Mudha Bhakti), pure devotion (Sattvic Bhakti), and devotion of knowledge (Jnana Bhakti).

[GV1:P0333] Question: What is meant by this blind devotion (Mudha Bhakti)?

[GV1:P0334] Answer: There are certain practices that ask if they can show some results by dancing in a trance using ghosts, spirits, and demons. All this is referred to as blind devotion (superstition). We might feel that some human beings themselves are incapable of manifesting some of its minor experiences. For example, if we go to an unfamiliar place ڊ if we visit a place where there is trance-dancing and such ڊ they often reveal details about the vehicles we arrived in, the characteristics of our home and parents, and some minor incidents that have occurred until then. They describe things like the house experiencing joys and sorrows due to such and such reasons ڊ they detail all of this.

[GV1:P0335] These are matters that are difficult to explain even for a true rationalist, or even for psychologists. Despite all this, because of these distortions, it is classified under foolishness. A path to self-realization is not seen through this either. The special devotion above this is called Satvic thought, Sakara thought (thought with form), and Nirakara thought (thought without form).

[GV1:P0336] Sakara thought makes humans Satvic through direct inference by means of certain manifestations and the awakenings resulting from them. Thirdly, by means of the nature of luminous spheres and the resonances arising from them, the nature of creation and dissolution is described and understood, and the state of complete eternity is experienced. It is the description of these three in manifold ways that is referred to as Puranas, Upanishads, and Vedas. The light of experience thus far is that not everyone is equally capable of understanding all these in detail and reaching the state of experience. These matters are seen shining in their complete form only in a very few blessed souls.

[GV1:P0337] Guruvani
