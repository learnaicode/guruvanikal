[GV4:P1268] April 2006

[GV4:P1269] This must originate from these householders. However, you have completely uprooted and discarded this, adopting instead a traditional knowledge scavenged by someone else—a knowledge which, collectively, does not extend beyond ten thousand years. How can you reconcile this knowledge with the aforementioned calculations? While the temporal span of Kali Yuga is stated to be four hundred and thirty-two thousand years, this knowledge is merely five thousand two hundred years old. Presently, our understanding of Kali is such that we expect it to end tomorrow. How can it end? If we are born in this Kali, and if it is assumed that all born within it are inherently worthless, then who is left to inherit this legacy? The truth is contrary to this. The opportunity to be born in Kali Yuga is significant for you. Why? We are born into a specific state of Kali that possesses a momentum suited to these times and the inherent dharma to learn, act, and practice accordingly. We have been entrusted with the task of aligning this specific state of Kali with dharma. This applies universally, irrespective of religion, caste, or class. Consequently, we remain unaware of the inherent virtues of this Kali. Therefore, regardless of religion or caste, one must reflect upon this. Kali represents the authoritative force of a karma that identifies the errors of the past and present, purifying them into truth. What can we achieve through a dharma that diminishes such a Kali, claiming absolute ignorance? Consequently, we failed to facilitate the transmission of this wisdom to our children or the youth in any manner. What, then, is currently being demonstrated? Imparting religious dogma and fostering mutual conflict through it. What is the purpose of this? To which deity is this being offered? No one reflects upon this. What can we achieve by engaging in such aggressive actions? Wherein lies the error that afflicts our children and ourselves today? If our ancestors or others engaged in action, what have we transformed that dharma into? What have we attained within that dharma? Even this remains unknown. Consequently, due to such ignorance, gods, goddesses, and even malevolent entities have been elevated to the status of the Supreme, and oblivious to the wheel of time, each of us children—the root cause being the parents' inability to impart wisdom to their offspring. There might not have been parents who endured hardships purely for the betterment of their children. Even if there were, let that be. But upon observing that this is how they have struggled to cultivate this lineage, what benefit is derived from it for themselves, this land, or this household? What is it that we believe in?

[GV4:P1270] It is not that one should deny the existence of prophetic religions. They do exist. What we must analyze is the underlying reason for their emergence. Rather than asserting that one event caused another in a circular fashion, we must ask: why did they emerge? The dharma of the Hindu was irrevocably packaged and marginalized, and as a consequence of adopting transgressive practices, mutual conflict ensued. The individual could not approach the Supreme Deity; their only recourse was to approach lesser deities. What, then, could they do? It is a fact that they gravitated towards a space where they found liberty. It is necessary to do so. Had they not, they would have been denied liberty, education, wealth, and authority. Consequently, the only karmic trajectory present within India is one that has deviated due to the deprivation of these fundamental elements. However, if anyone presumes that isolating oneself and engaging in conflict will rectify this, let them attempt it. Otherwise, whether one resides within India or any other nation, it is inhabited solely by human beings. God has not descended to physically reside there. Therefore, whatever human beings require, they will require regardless of location; we can only exist in harmony with nature. It is impossible to impart dharma to individuals who live as spouses and children acting devoid of rational discernment, failing to comprehend or transmit dharma, and unable to reclaim the inherent goodness of nature. When one or two generations pass in this manner, these children will remain ignorant of the profound concept of liberty. Their understanding of liberty is merely what they arbitrarily perceive it to be. This is the genesis of our rationalism and liberationism. If we accept the truth that none of these ideologies will reform us, what is the remedy? How do we define those we term visionaries? A visionary spanning two, three, or five millennia will prophesy only up to their respective epoch. From that point, to elevate it, refine it, and rectify historical errors, the succeeding generation must possess requisite knowledge. Otherwise, it resembles a tree germinated from a decaying stump. Such a tree possesses another characteristic: it will topple in a strong gale. Similarly, devoid of true knowledge, we are merely running along the karmic assumption that science will unilaterally progress. This is incorrect. I am sharing my empirical experience with you. I have not adopted the logic or impulsiveness of anyone else. Given the opportunity to be born designated as a Hindu, I once presumed that adhering to Hindu behavioral norms would be beneficial. There were times I harbored such assumptions. What does this entail? What our mothers instill in us is: 'Children, God will strike you down.' Can one then escape the fear of a wrathful deity? Fear is inevitable. As one matures, the concept of God morphs into something subjected to trials and observations, eventually leading to the conclusion that none of this represents God. Consequently, the subsequent iteration of God is merely the residue of frantic, oracle-like possessions and utterances. When such a concept is carried forward over ages under the guise of faith, love, and reverence, it becomes an entity unfit for true belief. Subsequently, under the mere pretexts of rationalism and liberationism, one is presented with a deity tailored to their intellect, and one ultimately becomes lost within it. Thus, every stage of human existence, male and female, is being misdirected in this manner. Within this karmic progression, originating from the householder stage, you categorize and invoke individuals by various titles such as yogi, karmi, jnani, bhakta, fakir, saint, or apostle.
