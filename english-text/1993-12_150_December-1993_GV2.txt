December 1993

Through what did the existence of the world come about? The truth will be shown only after the complete form of untruth has been shown. One can ask the subtle Guru or the functional Guru through word, deed, and intellect. The Guru can change the residual karma only by making the disciple perform actions repeatedly. A vision is beyond thought and intellect. It should not be doubted. In this world, whether it is great men or other living beings, no two are alike. That is the glory of God. Experience (realization) is glory. Acting from a place of experience must be done carefully. Acting where there is no experience will not yield meaning. There is no question of stirring up baseness there. But where there is experience, the baseness within us will be stirred up without our knowledge. If one is experienced, one will quarrel with the Guru. If one is inexperienced, one should not get confused.

It will only go away. There are so many examples of this here. If this is the case, one might feel that it is better not to act. Even if it is out of madness, bringing devotion and advertising it should not be seen as a flaw. It should come like that. Life is the remainder of having taken a body many times. Not everyone will get this fortune. Even the things experienced by the greatest sages are obtained by children here. If devotion is carried forward with discipline for three months, then it is enough to carry it in the mind always. It is enough if children pray for a short time. Otherwise, the inclination will be lost. One cannot eat good things in large quantities, right? Can one eat ghee like tapioca?

Some people only see the silk dress on the outside. How should love be carried forward? There is a secret in spirituality that we do not know. If you want to take it, it will hurt. Foresight is something that has thousands of spiritual visions and spiritual paths. The path of devotion, the path of yoga, the path of karma, the path of dharma - this is the misfortune of this path that it cannot be recognized even in the Yugadharma Siddhanti through a god or a goddess to explain and understand this.

Now what we need to see is the great ones. Because we do not know what the path of karma according to the Yugadharma is, it remains distant even for those who are called visionaries. It is like what happened during the time of Rama or Ravana, and after that, what was the direction of the vision that came and went many ages ago, it is of no use to say slowly that the multitude of people who do not even have the knowledge to correct it will not understand anything.

But there is an easy way. We need to take the great ones as examples to know and cross the steps of a great Yugadharma, which is not known how long karma and dharma have been flowing astray, by looking at the course of karma from Svayambhuva Manu, who got the foresight for Manutaramkalpas, to Vaivasvata Manu, and by looking at what a god, a sage, an ascetic, a wise man, and a devotee are.

Let us take the shortest time. The time we mean is Satyayuga, Tretayuga, Dwapara, and Kali. How many thousands of years is the foresight of a wise man - called wise men or karmis - who turns a chatur yuga into a karma creation with these four times! How many such wise men make up the length of an age! Who corrects the dharma of that age? Then how great will be the pain experienced when entering the inner chamber of spirituality? Here, the big pillars of the fateful degradation caused by past karmas are being untied. In that place, the good is being practiced and made into our lives. It must be done without any mistakes. Then one will wander with many difficulties. If you pray with the house, you will wander with the house.

Devotion has the nature of getting agitated quickly. If one gets very used to sattvic, then one will act knowingly. One who has not got used to it like that will get agitated like a brute. Such agitation will lead to ruin. The character that should come over a long period of time comes all at once. Whatever good thing is done, there will be a difficulty at first.

Pray for five minutes while working for punctuality. Then always fix it in the mind. When this happens, we will be played with bad things. Disease will also come. Similarly, sorrow will come along with the vision. Whoever we see, the sorrow they experienced will come to us too. The vision should not be told for everyone to hear. If you do good, an opposition to that good will arise. When doing good, foolishness will come. When doing something to prevent foolishness, one will also get confused. Any trick might be used here. Thinking that this is the result of serving, an opportunity should be given to correct it. When unbearable pain comes, one should not back away from there but stand firm with diplomacy. If you back away, everything is gone.

For example, how did R. Shankar become among the community? How did Mannam become? Mannam stood firm until the end. What is the reason for being able to stand firm? He has a diplomacy among the common people. Shankar is not like that. He only thinks about big things. He doesn't get things done either. He doesn't say things lovingly. Humility is very less. Administrative policy

Because it was not there, Shankar applied statecraft. Statecraft alone cannot correct the one at the bottom. The mind must be recognized directly. Ezhavas and Nairs have the same structure. They don't get crazy about spiritual matters. Nairs are a little lower. What is the reason for that? Because they stood under the Brahmins, they could not bring it to their own homes. An old mother in the house would call 'Narayana' and put a bhasma mark. Peons, document writers, village officers, subordinates - all of them will draw a bhasma mark. And a dot on top of it. The attitude is that they are devotees. Such devotion will not cause any trouble. But they don't know how to call God crazy. There was a time when things were achieved even by frightening God. These poor people think that God is tied to the string of fear.

It is believed that if you play the battle between Bhadra and Bharika, ghosts will not enter the house for sixteen years! With a face made of silver, burnt rice flour and charcoal, Bhadrakali's dress, headgear and sword. One gets scared just by seeing this. We have none of that here. Then there is no test and siddhi at all. But there is a difference that ours is spiritual and the other is material. All we have here is prayer. It is for the resurrection and survival of the world. First pray that the evils that have been in our minds till today will go away and that the result of that action will belong to all of us, then put it into practice. If you pray strongly, there will definitely be results. In some child born now, there will be an origin suitable for the resurrection of the world. There are many families who believe here. Almost all families have experiences. You who have such experience should take this forward very carefully. There is a reason for saying that you should be very careful. Because the varna caste system came into the varnashrama dharma, the Shudra did not get an opportunity to understand anything spiritually. Therefore, a clear caste came into existence. We have these four castes in our house itself. The one who does Vedic work is a Brahmin. The one who rules is a Kshatriya. The one who does business is a Vaishya. The Shudra stood without touching anywhere. This continued for a long time. This is the determination of Manu. But Manu also has a determination that the Shudra will rule in Kali. But he was not given an opportunity to worship God. The Brahmin showed a trick to keep the authority for worshiping God in his hands and rule. That is their creation of varnashrama dharma according to their will. It was decreed that the Shudra should not utter the sound 'Om'. The Vaishya was suppressed. Having established such a foundation, the rule was given to the Shudra. Under the rule of the Shudra, man became like wild grass. The people became a mad race with no knowledge at all. Therefore, unable to acquire self-knowledge to the point of quenching his thirst, the position he got was also useless. What is the story if there is no freedom to offer bali even if the father dies?

The life of women has completely degenerated. In addition, the world of women suffered harm from the culture of Westerners and Arabians. To change that, all the sorrows that women can bring must be experienced and finished. A woman has an ability. A man must also see a capable woman completely. The freedom of women is not like what is envisioned today. A freedom different from all this needs to be brought. God has given us a mercy to correct the mistakes so that it can be brought like that. Its karma has been completed here. Now you just have to do it. A woman has nine arts. It must be fully put into practice. The lives of those who went wrong in varnashrama dharmas and others have been purified and released. They will now be born in this lineage.
