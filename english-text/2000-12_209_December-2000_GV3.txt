[GV3:P1086] December 2000

[GV3:P1087] The Guru is described in four ways - Brahmavit, Brahmavaryan, Brahmavarishtan, Brahmavareeshtan. The history and karmic ability that got altered when many wrote about it stand entangled around us like a great danger. We must take and see each one. Everywhere, the term 'egg' can be seen. An egg has a round shape, right? That is, from the initial stage of origin, it has taken the shape of the universe (Andakadaham). It reaches up to the amphibian, the turtle. From the pig, it has reached the evolution of the birth canal. There needs to be no better example than this to see that mistakes occurred right from the origin in the evolutionary path. It takes such a long time for a life to evolve into a state of giving birth. If we take and look at any object in the world, its evolutionary path is exactly like this.

[GV3:P1088] If we think about the antiquity of time, we have the misfortune of not even being able to reach a few miles back. Therefore, it is appropriate to take the beginning of time from the period of Sri Krishna. Due to the twisting of stories regarding the tale of evolution, not a single Acharya could come forward and act. Some poets like Kunchan Nambiar and Ezhuthachan, who saw that this twisting of stories was not the truth, have spoken some truths. Since they couldn't state that openly either, they said it according to their abilities. Looking back, even if one studies many great Vedantins, theorists, karmis, jnanis, and devotees, nothing can be understood. While each wrote and highlighted that part of their capability, deviations in the path over time occurred. If a Guru lineage is not attained in the state change of this clan, the mistakes cannot be corrected. Even if a new thought originates, it will have no connection with spirituality. It is the same when looking at the position of education. A lifetime might pass well due to the goodness of knowledge. The story of Kalidasa is also one such twisted story. Stories like the shepherd boy marrying the princess, and when asked 'who is inside' replying 'who is outside', got twisted, creating a situation where no one could speak the truth. Story writing across India is just like this. Hanuman, a great devotee, and Sri Rama, a great noble soul who lived at that time, got caught in this twisting of stories.

[GV3:P1089] Seeing all this, it feels as if they embraced it just to avoid dying by making mistakes in the karma that completely shook the dharma of India. In whichever yugas and through whichever yuga dharmas however many great men came, all poets and religious believers together have twisted and preserved stories according to the capacity of their language. Due to this reason, performing worship of God truthfully became impossible. Conforming to what was written in these stories, they tried to bring it into action to gain karmic capability, and kept it grasped and imparted in the intellect. This led to the destruction of karma. These scholars also have the responsibility to say whether this karma that led to destruction is true or false. There are some scholars included in this. They are the four types of Gurus, starting with Brahmavit, mentioned earlier.

[GV3:P1090] These four groups shrank to three. This means the fourth face of knowledge (Nanmukhatvam) disappeared, and consequently, the dharma of Kali did not occur. This loss of dharma grows alongside the world. As a result, philosophies became intellectual brawls decorated with language and embellishments. If one starts calling God in this path, from where would one have to call, and where would it lead? Intellectually saying that this negative aspect in the worship of God is neither old nor new does not make it disappear.

[GV3:P1091] This has become nature. Nature never disappears. Even if intellect is poured completely, or even if the administration of a country is obtained, this cannot be fixed. The parts in philosophies that say what exists does not exist and what does not exist exists have hindered the growth of great souls. Thus, scholars wrote and established their respective parts, and all poets who came to this day growing it in a ruined way have kept the world as a concubine. The stories of the shepherd boy marrying the princess and attaching a tail to Hanuman despite him being Rama's greatest devotee are examples of this. Dharma cannot be completed by holding onto these foolish stories. There is no point in holding onto them either.

[GV3:P1092] Saying it shouldn't be held onto does not mean it doesn't exist. Whatever is uttered by name does exist. When we say 'us', do not assume it means only Indians. This mistake has afflicted the entire world. Hence, all rulers of the world have erred. When the children of each of these individuals are born and grow, their task is to become frenzied entities of mischief. In the end, the leg pain will be the same for the one who ran and the one who made others run.

[GV3:P1093] When some scholars who lacked God's grace and their followers wrote stories and turned them into Kathakali, their drama composition was in such a way that they used the Guru or Prophet to enlarge the stage of the play. Vyasa was the one who came to correct this method. But what did Vyasa write down? He divided it into four faces and made four Vedas. Even before that, it had been made into six shastras and eighteen puranas. Vyasa came to correct that. They stand as hymns of praise, but is it easy to rewrite and reclaim all of them? Therefore, we do not need to look in that direction.

[GV3:P1094] This knowledge we have received was shown by God. What we have found today is a karma that needs to be taken up by fully believing, respecting, and loving it. The Sun, Moon, and Earth continue to rotate without losing balance. Two phases, the dark phase and the bright phase, have been declaring results based on twenty-seven stars. They also tried to liberate the physics of the earth by putting it in the calculation of yamas and telling the story of the global sphere. Such mistakes have been made regarding all celestial bodies. This cannot be attained by thinking. These scholars themselves have said that it is beyond thought, beyond intellect. Their principles are seen theorized in many ways as darshana and yoga. No one should even touch any of this. Gurus are needed to handle this. The Guru path intended here is not the one that has come in our concepts and knowledge.

[GV3:P1095] The curse of Brahma exists from whichever time Manu dharma was violated. It is not possible for an educated person or an uneducated person to fulfill it. However, there are many great souls who come by divine will. Twenty-one such Gurus will come in one Chaturyuga. They will not write down anything to convince anyone. The dharma of these Gurus is to see the rights and wrongs of the past times and correct them. At the same time, it is not possible for him to change the structural attributes that have come through the mistakes. How many Ramas, how many Buddhas, how many Jesuses and Prophets have come! Have they been able to do anything for the human world other than bear witness with pain? When the tongue and mouth did not yield to their knowledge, they rejected it and strengthened the three faces. They also wrote down that the face of knowledge was plucked off. Even if written like that, karma is not going to leave the realm where it was performed. Whether seen with the mind or the physical eye, it exists as atmospheric impurities.

[GV3:P1096] Whenever great souls come, God gives them the necessary intellect and ability. But we have only troubled and ruined all these great ones.

[GV3:P1097] The simplicity of a disciple is seeing how the gross and subtle, and the causes and effects, which stand beyond thought, occurred in all times. If it goes through the twisting of stories, this disciple will fall into a pit. But if he goes through the Guru who actually came, this mistake will not happen. This Guru will not show any superiority. This Guru will be capable of giving the account of twenty-five chaturyugas. One can travel through all karmas by holding onto this Guru. The disciple with vision sees both the Guru and himself present in all these karmas. Upon seeing it, he will tell the Guru and receive corrections.

[GV3:P1098] The lineage of the Trinity and the lineage of Manu are not non-existent. Let that truth remain there. What we have to see is something else. What all types of eggs are there in the universe (Andakadaham) which is in the shape of an egg! What all will be included in that egg; all the previously mentioned lights will come into it. Whether we see that light or not, believers and loving people together must respect Sri Narayana Guru's word that 'the deity should be placed in the service of sages'. In that position, there is no difference of male or female, small or big. Correct the mistakes of the world and move forward. This is the duty of a true disciple. This disciple is what can be called discipleship wealth. I am informing you of the truth that it is utilized in this manner in Santhigiri.

[GV3:P1099] Guruvani
