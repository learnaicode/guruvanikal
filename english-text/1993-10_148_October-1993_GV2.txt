GURUVANIKAL · October 1993 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0548 to GV2:P0562
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

October 1993

Some people here have a habit of turning a blind eye to certain things while doing some good things. I have worked hard and done this much so that you can manage. Do not destroy it. It is easy to ruin. It is difficult to build. All this is the result of the efforts of many people. Those who treat everything lightly are not fit to stay here. Even if you can only stay here for one day, your sweat must fall here. To that extent, your shortcomings will fade away. The attitude that 'only what I do is right' is not good for anyone.

Those with devotion will know whatever happens anywhere. Devotion without attention is useless. It must be done with attentive devotion and the strength of yoga. One must strive hard (even those who are ill) and do what is necessary, inch by inch. Some can do agriculture as a service; some can plant and nurture saplings. One can also serve by giving a portion of their own harvest. Those who work from here should be given a position. At the same time, they must do what is needed at the right time. That good opportunity which comes in handy may not occur again. If you feel the sacrifice involved in what you do, there is nothing more to gain. Sacrifice does not need to bow down anywhere. All merit belongs to sacrifice. Even without any special qualification or attainment, the words of a person of sacrifice will create a heartfelt experience. You should pray that this blessedness serves you until the end. Mocking fellow beings, opposing those who do good deeds - these must be avoided. All this is contrary to ashram life.

Kerala mainly has two shortcomings. Kerala will not have the character of North India. This is because South Indian kings fell into Dravidian culture and became Shudras. Tamil Nadu, on the other hand, accepted lower-level worship. Giving importance to Shakteyam, even the prominent people there were ruined. Many among them became Christians. Christians imparted English speech here instead of good things. Thus, a culture unconnected to Indian culture grew in Tamil Nadu and Kerala. The kings here were degraded in every way. We, being the subjects of degraded kings, naturally became degraded as well.

We do not think about the depth of our culture. Humility is the hallmark of our culture. India is blessed by nature. We have everything here. The cornerstone of our cultural heritage is the Upanishads, Vedas, and Puranas. But because we stood apart pretending to have knowledge, we do not know its meaning. Now it is difficult to elevate the old state. Understanding this, a plan itself must be created and brought forward.

We have children here who can sit here and tell about any event in the world. There is no point in saying that they got that ability as a divine gift. If this knowledge is to be brought forward, one must understand and live within them. God has not given us any flaw that requires us to bend our backs before anyone. If one loves truth, there is no greater achievement to come. The fortune brought by attention, devotion, and the strength of yoga must be carefully preserved.

Detachment, the strength to forgive and endure anything, and the humility to dedicate all detachments for one purpose should be adornments. With this skill, we can become good. We must strive until the Kali age becomes a reality. For that, attention is absolutely essential. We should not live here the way capitalists and their children live. Special attention is needed for that. Attention must be cultivated through exemplary discipline.

As far as detachment is concerned, the story of Pakkanar is a model. God has given us permission to have love for everyone and thus behave truthfully. We must help each other. Mutual enmity is evil. That must change and another kind of detachment must come. Then evil will leave you - and goodness will come instead.

Although the trinity nature is mentioned as Brahma, Vishnu, and Maheshwara, Shaivism is prominent in practice. It is the same all over the world. Based on this, Sree Narayana Guru gave priority to Shiva. The Vaishnavite cleverly gained dominance. Its origin happened right here in India. The origin of human history itself is here.

There is a history of Parvati worshipping Shiva. In the nature of linga worship, this cannot be mistaken. Because Parvati is a creation. Having had the vision from birth, she could see the connection with Shiva across births. That is the basis of the origin of karma. Linga worship came into existence due to the scent of desire that spread from the origin of man. The history of Christ and the history of Islam begin from Adam. Parvati also adopted this very nature (Adam's peak is the Garden of Eden in Arabia. Shiva's footprint is in Ceylon.) The Indian concept is that Shiva is not born of a womb. The concept is that he was born by will, that is, by the will of God. 'Sankalpamatrena Parameshwara:' has two meanings. One is that he originated from the will of God. 'Aaseedagrelasabhadevada' means he originated from light. The Vedas also say the same. Sree Narayana Guru also thought in the same way. But there are very few who have thought about its true nature.

Good, evil - only if you know both can you know the sum of good. Evil has the upper hand. Because the origin is from evil. The origin is from the Annamaya Kosha. There is no bad without good, and no good without bad. Like the left hand and the right hand. Why give respect to the right hand? The fruit of karma is individual. Nothing can be called right or wrong. One must know what the qualification for it is. Luck must also be considered. One must know how karma is purified. One must discriminate and understand who I am, what my action is, what I was before, and what I should become. From human discrimination, one can say what is wrong. As far as we are concerned, it has become very easy in that matter. Think about the culture of the time when people did not know how to say paddy. That time and today's time cannot be reconciled at all. The difference evolved from the habit of human desire. At a certain time, this will not suit the body. The atmosphere will be polluted. Then what will we become? This is a greatness of creation.

Our willpower is a physical inquiry. Scientific progress is not accepting what is not suitable for human destruction. There is also a way to find the truth through science. When that is found, science is over. There will be a doubt whether what was discovered is wrong. There will be fear of the world. How many times has the world progressed and struck back like this!

The presence of God can be seen in the seventh state. Until then, it is demonic. Although it is said that the demonic nature was mistaken by doing right and wrong for God's purpose, it is not demonic. They are two states, like Asura and Deva. Disobeying God's word is demonic. That demonic nature is what made Adam go wrong.

What is shown in the vision cannot be said exactly as it is. It does not become evidence for the people. What to do about it? Even those who have seen it here cannot say it. At the age of twenty-five, the second layer of the brain will begin to clear. Because it is shown in the state of the three times. They have had many births with willpower mixed with right and wrong. In between this, there may be a silver lining of compassion. It may be the compassion that came from a poor man when he was given a meal. Even when trying to hit with a pestle, if a grain of rice flies off that pestle and falls into this poor man's pot, even that is a virtue. He will take it and offer it to God as an offering. It is because of the fruit of that poor man's prayer that he gets to see God's service. There are many who pray, seeing that service, wishing they had an opportunity. Because it is not given knowingly, what is received cannot be respected knowingly. A consciousness must then arise to think why I came here. No one has the pleasure to think like that. It does not join through luck.

Adam had someone to walk with him as a helper and tell him things. The Prophet did not get that either. He held on tightly to whatever he received and took from it.

Those who do not know at least one language do not get the opportunity to learn anything else. One thing will be given - vision. Through it, everything will be known. The knowledge of any action will be obtained from it. All the lowly karmic paths lying in this world will end through this karma. It will correct the wrong, make it a tool, and create another value. It is not enough if it is only in the subtle; the karmic path must also reach the people. From the subtle to the waking state, dream, deep sleep, Turiya, and Turiyatita - thus, up to the presence of God, crossing the position of Devendra and the state of Ganapathi, the lord of the Ganas, it has the right up to creation, preservation, and destruction.
