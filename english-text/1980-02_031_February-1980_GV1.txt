[GV1:P0525] February 1980

[GV1:P0526] Question: What is the purpose of this Kumbha Mela? It is not our custom to worship gods and goddesses. Then with what intention is the Kumbha Mela conducted?

[GV1:P0527] Guruji: It is called Kumbha Mela; it is not called Kumbhabhishekam, is it?

[GV1:P0528] Question: No. We often hear the term Kumbha Mela in temples. I am asking assuming it is similar to that.

[GV1:P0529] Guruji: This is not like that. You will understand it naturally when you become familiar with it. Nevertheless, since you asked, I will tell you. I was trained and brought to realization among those who believe in these gods and goddesses. You clearly know that experiential nature exists here, don't you?

[GV1:P0530] Question: I know. I do not have much knowledge about that experiential nature. I do not know whether it is an experience derived from gods and goddesses or an experience from the Guru lineage.

[GV1:P0531] Guruji: I had one or two experiences before leaving home. After leaving home, I had a visionary experience in Aluva. That too was not complete. Following that, I came to Sivagiri because of that experience. I had two or four experiences at Aruvippuram. After that, until the age of twenty-seven and a half, I had no notable experiences. However, following that, I found a Guru, and in connection with that, although there was no order, discipline, or method, the experience had to be truly realized. Thus, it was first experienced on the veranda of the Thuckalay Munsiff Court. It was after two and a half years that I was able to experience it extensively in the right path. By then, it had been a year since I had moved away from Sivagiri. Initially, I saw the aforementioned gods, goddesses, and evil spirits, and believed in them itself. Thus, I was able to see with my physical eyes gods and goddesses, the subordinate gods and goddesses associated with them, and subsequently evil spirits, and even the subtle forces of this world.

[GV1:P0532] Question: How can you reflect in this world that you saw this as the truth?

[GV1:P0533] Guru: Why do you ask like this? Are you not seeing it here now? You hear it being said that the children here have this vision, and through them, certain disease traits, things that doctors cannot diagnose, they subtly discover and declare that it happened for a specific reason—that you have this particular trait, or your house has it, or your inauspicious time is such and such. Have you been able to see that they believe it to be true?

[GV1:P0534] Question: Yes, I have. I understood that it was true in my own case, didn't I?

[GV1:P0535] Guruji: Then why did you ask how this will be reflected among the people?

[GV1:P0536] Question: I had not thought this far.

[GV1:P0537] Guruji: Following this, I will answer what you asked. Thus, I have been able to convince not only gods and goddesses but also the sages who believed in gods and goddesses and their lineage.

[GV1:P0538] Question: After that, what is the reason for saying that the worship of gods and goddesses is wrong and abandoning it?

[GV1:P0539] Guruji: I did not say it is wrong. I did not say that gods and goddesses do not exist. I only said that today, a form of worship suitable for these times is needed.

[GV1:P0540] Question: By "these times," you meant the Kali Yuga, didn't you?

[GV1:P0541] Guruji: Yes. Haven't you heard through some Puranas that gods and goddesses have no place in Kali Yuga, and that they too will take birth in Kali Yuga to attain liberation?

[GV1:P0542] Question: Yes. So, we should understand from what you said that even gods and goddesses have births.

[GV1:P0543] Guruji: It is not that you should understand what I said. The previous authors of the Puranas, as well as the authors of the Upanishads, are seen recording about their births through some histories. For example, we have heard about the story of *Parayi Petta Panthirukulam*. The great figures in it are Nandanar, Pakkanar, Thiruvalluvar, Naranathu Bhranthan, Vayillakunnilappan, and Avvaiyar. In times like these when all this has even come out as cinema, what is the difficulty for us to know?

[GV1:P0544] Question: We clearly understood what you said. We understood that there is birth, that even gods and goddesses can only exist accordingly, and that beyond this, there exists divine power, God, and Brahman.

[GV1:P0545] Guruji: Yes, so you completely believe this now, don't you?

[GV1:P0546] Question: Yes, I believe it.

[GV1:P0547] Guruji: During the time these experiences were occurring, several people approached me with faith; some viewed me in the tradition of a Guru and began having experiences themselves. As these experiences commenced, they started sensing not only deities but also a whole lineage of Gurus within themselves. After about three years had passed in this manner, we began to notice some minor errors—errors even in the visions they were experiencing.

[GV1:P0548] To eliminate those errors, we engaged in divine worship to the best of our abilities. Furthermore, since God had granted us the opportunity to meet each of those Gurus and express our grievances, we conducted all possible trials and experiments within our capacity at that time. None of these efforts yielded any fruitful results. The struggle itself became the only outcome.

[GV1:P0549] During that period, I was in Varkala. At that time, some of the very individuals who had encountered these experiences began speaking ill of me, completely disregarding their own experiences. Through them, this attitude permeated among the locals, leading to numerous defamatory campaigns and various attempts to obstruct our path. Suffice it to say that we somehow barely managed to escape from this predicament.

[GV1:P0550] Question: Why is it that despite having such a progression of experiences, such adversities came to pass?

[GV1:P0551] Guruji: It is precisely for you to understand this in detail that I began speaking in this manner. A testament to this can be found in the story where Gandharvas and Kinnaras came to disrupt Vashistha's yagna and subsequently halted it, following which Vishwamitra brought Sri Rama and Lakshmana, stationed them as guards with the king's permission, drove the disruptors away, and completed the yagna.

[GV1:P0552] Question: Yes.

[GV1:P0553] Guru: History states that many great souls have been made to experience similar situations.

[GV1:P0554] Question: What is the reason for experiencing it this way?

[GV1:P0555] Guruji: This is not the era of deities. When we worship them and reach a state of fruition, some pure souls—not everyone, but as hundreds of thousands and millions of devotees pass by—some emerge like Vyasa and Vashistha. The attempt is to obstruct them. Similarly, anyone who ascends to that level will be obstructed.

[GV1:P0556] Since it is not their era, if a human being manages to ascend through the righteous path, the deities create these entanglements in this state to prevent the individual from suddenly surpassing the realm of the deities and ascending directly to the state of the Supreme God.

[GV1:P0557] Question: Was it in that state that they brought disrepute to you at Varkala and attempted to obstruct the righteous path?

[GV1:P0558] Guru: Yes, it was then that I received a notification, not from these deities but from some light, to leave that place and come sit here. That is how I came to be here.

[GV1:P0559] Thus, nine months after arriving here, I was able to receive a correction for this and overcome this state. Through that correction, we were made to perform what was called a completion twenty-seven times. We only understood that a stage had been crossed. We did not comprehend what it was. For the twenty-eighth time, we were asked to conduct a prayer, calling it a completion. We conducted the prayer. On the ninth day after starting that prayer, we were told to place a Kalasham. Placing the Kalasham, an order was given to perform rituals in specific ways for seven days. We did so. An instruction was given to take the Kalasham and perform it in specific ways. That was also done. The reason for this Kumbhamela was received through a revelation on that day.

[GV1:P0560] This will be beneficial annually for believers and faithful vow-makers. For both children and adults, to address any illness or to achieve any other objective, conveying a prayer—or performing it with all kinds of traditional instrumental music—can be undertaken by those making vows.

[GV1:P0561] The revelation stated that it would serve as both a festive event and a means to fulfill the objectives of the believers. It declared that this would become immensely significant in the world. The Kumbhamela is conducted exactly as received in the revelation regarding this movement.

[GV1:P0562] Question: So you are saying that this is in accordance with the divine will.

[GV1:P0563] Guruji: Yes, this is the divine will. The revelation states that it will grow larger as time passes, and in due course, it will manifest as the greatest meritorious act in the world. This is the brief overview of it.

[GV1:P0564] Guruvani
