Guruvani September 1991 Birthday Message

On birthdays, most people usually write something that people like and publish it in special editions. As far as I am concerned, I have felt that it is best to write and publish about the hardships and losses in my life - it is good to experience such hardships and losses in this Kali Yuga. The Puranas also say the same thing.

The word 'experience' has a very broad meaning. We have the knowledge of experience starting from calling Madan as 'Thampuran' (Lord). If one places Madan or any other such deities in the position of God and calls them 'Thampuran', the one who does so will never reach the position of God. Moreover, even if a person who dances like a demon and performs intense poojas and worships attains certain direct experiences and special powers that can baffle people and lead them to blindness, his soul will become more despicable than that of a non-believer who considers saying he has no faith as a matter of prestige. I have been able to see this as the greatest proof in my belief. Another kind

There are also devoted people. Those who offer vows, perform trance dances, buy and dedicate elephants, conduct Kathiredppu, Pathakam, Harikatha, and Kathaprasangam for the sake of wealth and achieving their goals. There is another group of devotees. Those who experience some minor visions due to intense devotion, and when it increases, a Yogeeswaran, Manthramoorthi, or Bhadrakali who died and remained in the family stands strong in them, and they do something to make people believe until their death. The family later turns him into a favorite Manthramoorthi or Yogeeswaran and makes him the family deity. Due to such practices, that family itself will wander and become bankrupt after one or two generations. Not only that, they will also become enslaved to great sins. There is a reason for writing and revealing this. Of the thousands of such families who come to me for the expiation of sins, I am only able to resolve the sinful power of ninety-nine percent. The sinful power of the remaining few cases that cannot be resolved is either the greatest among terrible diseases or madness in a never-ending Kiriyam nature. It is something that never ends and is hereditary in the family.

If we just say it like this, one might wonder if there is anything real. There is. There is a God who transcends the scope of what was mentioned earlier. A God who cannot be confined to a temple or an idol.

In some families, there are some ancestors like sacrificing great souls who have maintained honesty throughout their lives and have managed to reach at least the position of Brahmarakshas. In later times, some children will be born in that family in the zodiac sign where such ancestors stand. Even if that child does not become a self-realized soul, he will have acquired the maturity to liberate those ancestors by doing good deeds without keeping the aforementioned artificialities. If it is a woman, there will be a lot of goodness. If it is a woman, she just needs some help in the act of giving liberation. Ancestors who attain liberation in this way can show some paths through visions to a woman or a man and make them a good devotee or a wise person. Otherwise, they will at least make them a Yogabhrashta or a wealthy person.

The best way of all this is to find a wise Guru. If a Guru is found, one will be able to attain spiritual merit at least up to the fortune of his life. All these things mentioned may perhaps seem negative to those who are not self-realized. Therefore, I wish that this auspicious day may be useful for the people to get a clear idea for a good path. In this situation where our land, not only our land but the world itself, is filled with ignorance arising from a lack of love and some foolishness of enjoying making money through barbarism, I wish that reading the messages published on such auspicious days may lead to seeking a good path that will help to bring firmness in virtue and the fortune acquired in action.

Question: Guru! If you just summarize this topic like this, we who are ignorant do not know how to take it. And we have no idea how to take it through the words of a wise person. Guru: To take this topic through the words of a wise person, the very antiquity of life is required.

Question :

It has been felt in many ways and contexts that the antiquity of life is necessary. However, we have a strong desire to know about it. Therefore, please be kind enough to explain it in detail.

Guru :

What was said earlier is all about the antiquity of life itself. What can be done if doubts still arise? Now let us talk about what is meant by the antiquity of life. Biology itself is the principle of evolution that is consolidated and shown as the whole in the whole and the atom in the atom. The Hindu belief system is that it is an eternal theory. This is the very basis of the Puranas and epics. However, some scholars, whether experienced or not, have doubts about some aspects of this belief system that do not fit logic. Therefore, they claim that what is authoritative is what the sage Badarayana has written, what the four Vedas, the six Shastras, and the eighteen Puranas have praised wholeheartedly, and then the merit of the research study of the scholars themselves. They will use it in a logical language. Such scholars, who think only with their own logic, will not be able to see the truth in biology. Then they will seek refuge in Mayavada (the doctrine of illusion). But when Mayavada comes as logic, it says that there is no second other than the one. Later, holding fast to the truth that there is no second other than the one, they will bring in logic. This is their logical distinction of studying and teaching such logic for as long as they want. Using ordinary Puranas and logical words, they will also illustrate by stating the inference of mistaking a rope for a snake. If the one who illustrates this sees a snake (let's say an eight-foot cobra) in front of him, even if he dares to chase it away with courage and arrogance without fear, if cornered, the snake will turn around and bite. The truth is that one cannot escape from that snake bite without a mantra, a resolve, or knowledge. If one holds fast to Mayavada with the force of his scholarship and logic, will he not be affected by the poison? If it were a snake instead of a rope, wouldn't Mayavada become hollow if one adopts any method to move away without getting bitten or to destroy it? The one who has studied logic, the scholar, and the ignorant are equal in this matter. However, the sannyasi has some techniques (vidyas) by which poison does not affect the body, no matter which venomous snake bites, let alone an eight-foot cobra.

Question :

What is it that you call a technique (vidya)? Isn't the sannyasi's path one of knowledge (jnana)?

Guru :

Yes, the sannyasi's path is indeed knowledge. But that knowledge does not have the method of destroying or otherwise eliminating what is destined by God. The wise one (jnani) will know such things in advance and adopt the method of escaping from that incident.

Question : Guru :

There is history of huge snakes acting as umbrellas for sannyasis and of some sannyasis having them as companions, right? There is some truth in that too. The reason for this is that most sannyasis accept the conditions in the path of worship (upasana). For example, they are those who have attained perfection (siddhi) through actions in mind, word, and deed in the paths of worship like Shaiva, Shakta, and Vaishnava, and have attained the eightfold divine powers (ashtaiswarya siddhi) as a manifest dharma. Some others have adopted the path of Kalpaseva. This is a siddhi that involves consuming everything from leaves to the leaves of the nux vomica tree. Then there is another siddhi of cooking and consuming the nine poisons (navapashanas) in various ways. When performing such a seva, they will build a cave with nine faces and sit inside it. The medicine

While practicing this, the person will not be able to stand up or walk. Even urination and defecation may stop. There is a calculation that one should face a specific direction during a specific watch (yamam). The disciples accompanying them only change their facing direction to the specified one. The disciples would have been taught in advance about the care to be given at that time. By the time the eight siddhis (attainments) are achieved, the body will return to its original state. Once this care is systematically given to the body, old age and graying will not affect it. There is also a justification for this that death will only occur when they desire it. Serpents can recognize those who have attained two or four of the Ashtasiddhis. Serpents with this recognition will turn back without staying if they see them. Or else, snakes belonging to the bad category without this recognition might bite them. But the snake's venom will turn back and affect it, and only the snake will lose its life. All this is not achieved in one birth. These are paths taken according to the vasana (tendencies) accumulated over many births. Those who have attained siddhi in this way will perform many miraculous deeds. Such siddhas are often mistakenly considered as jnanis (wise ones). Haven't you heard people asking, 'Is he a siddha?' There are karma paths that enjoy telling various stories and details about sanyasis who come after attaining such siddhis. Especially in South Travancore, such stories can be heard. One such story goes like this.

In one place, there were two brothers, an elder and a younger one. One was educated and a scholar. The other was uneducated and a fool. Both of them would take turns going to guard the banana orchard. It was the uneducated one who went to guard one day. It is said that the elder brother went there after hearing hymns being sung here and there. He asked his younger brother how he learned this. He replied that someone gave him two bananas, and when he ate the bananas, the hymns emerged. The elder brother, who took the banana peels from the base of the banana tree and ate them, also became a poet. There are people who believe even this. I have heard people say that a swami seen in one place was seen in another place at the very same time. These are all tricks brought about by the use of gaunamani, swarnamani, and rasamani. Mistaking all this for jnana, the paths we have adopted are the flaws that occur in karma.

Haven't you seen some people playing with snakes around their necks? If he hasn't extracted the fangs or kept medicine in hand, he has definitely jumped into danger. There is an incident in Koorkenchery, Thrissur, where a swami known as Pambuswami walked around with a snake around his neck. None of this should be mistaken for jnana. A true jnani will not display special siddhis. Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa has said that siddhi should be discarded like dog feces.

Thus, through the habit of being born and dying over many periods, the vasana of that jiva (soul) will become that. Then, whatever karma path and dharma path the jiva residing in the body made of the five elements was made to practice, that jiva can return and fall into it, becoming the owner of a jiva that shows many antics. None of this is jnana.

Question: So, are you also talking about the theory of evolution?

Guru: Yes, this is evolution itself. We must remember that if the combination of the lights of the earth, the sun, and the moon does not happen, not only animals, reptiles, termites, and other living beings, but also the creative marvels ranging from the aforementioned navapashanas to the medicinal categories that cannot be distinguished by name or number, will not be able to exist or live in their respective forms, natures, and efficacies. Even electricity, which claims to stand at the peak of growth and progress, is significantly affected by the changes in nature. Therefore, anymore of such karma theories like siddhi, vidya, and mayavada

I only wish that one shouldn't become the owner of actions that desire to chase and threaten humans.

Question: By saying this much, how will people learn about our path and the actions to be performed; or will they not learn?

Guru: For people with faith, that is, those who pray believing in a Guru lineage, there is indeed a realization of results.

Question: The realization of results also happens for those who believe in other paths. Then why is our path specifically highlighted?

Guru: Our path is not one that has come by reading or following any specific path categorized as Yoga, Bhakti, Karma, or Jnana. This is my own experience. I have experienced with absolute certainty that everything that is named exists and nothing is non-existent.

Question: Others also say that they have experienced and realized it.

Guru: What we call our experience is what has been shown in the vision regarding the greatness and great souls in the Manvantaras, the origin of the Trimurti tradition, and its realization of results in the Treta Yuga and Dvapara. Treta Yuga and Dvapara are times when one can reach as far as possible by worshiping, praying, and practicing Yoga in the Deva tradition. But in Kali Yuga, that tradition will not bear fruit - not only that, those who follow that tradition have no salvation in Kali. That is why it is said that they have reached and are standing in that path. The path that Shiva advises Parvati in the Skanda Purana is the 'Guru Gita' which confirms this experiential truth.

In the Guru Gita, Parvati asks Shiva, even though you exist as the life of all living beings and are the greatest ascetic and the most eminent among the Trimurtis, whom do you meditate upon? The subject matter of the Guru Gita is Shiva's reply in the form of advice to this question of Parvati. Even the Trimurtis have no salvation. The Guru Gita discusses how the karma and knowledge of that Supreme Being are, how the disciple understands it, and how it helps in salvation.

All human souls have been caught up in the actions of the Trimurti tradition or the Manu lineage for many ages. It has been proven beyond doubt that no matter what action one is involved in, his action will not go beyond what he does to anything else. This is believed to belong to the original path of Manu.

The children of men have believed in one of these paths - the path of the Devas, the path of the Acharyas, or the path of the Prophets - until today. We have been shown all these paths, the actions that have come according to the order of gross, subtle, causal, and effect in everything, the sum of good and evil existing in each, the deviations that caused errors, and their corrections. Having seen in experience what is meant by chanting names in the Kali Yuga, and having taken and seen the matter which is its gross, subtle, and causal, whatever exists in the form of nouns - Deva, Acharya, Prophet, Sage, Sanyasi - and its action and what connects those actions

Our path is to correct the disciple in the fields of experience and action, having seen and understood the past karmas that are the gross and subtle causes.

The path that corrects the gross, subtle, causal, and effect to this extent is rare. That is our path. The method of the gurus and wise men who have come so far, out of their compassion and greatness, correcting the mistakes that come to us. Our path is the guru's path of correcting it again without error. Here, some may see and hear certain things in the states of waking, dreaming, and deep sleep. In the two states of turiya and turiyatita, when the waking-deep sleep states occur, the guru-disciple relationship here, or the greatness called guru's compassion, consists of distinguishing and making them understand what it is, and elevating the disciple to the height of a wise man.

Kali is the age when liberation is ordained equally for the Shudra, who does not understand the meaning of the distinction of the true varna-ashrama dharma but is eligible for complete self-knowledge in the Kali age, the Brahmin, the Kshatriya, and the Vaishya, who always wants to be only a karma-doer, a merchant, and a wealthy person. That is why even the sages have prayed, "O Lord, grant me a birth and allow me to see the Kali age at least once." And the birth must be in India itself. India is the fertile ground of knowledge of all kinds of sage lineages. The section on knowledge mentioned in texts like the Guru Gita is something that man must acquire through karma.

This greatness of the guru has come as evidence revealed through the words of the subtle-sighted sages that man can reach a much higher level than the knowledge that has come so far. That is why our path provides this through knowledge in thought, word, and deed, without any magical powers, in the form of a dialogue between guru and disciple.

Praying to that ocean of compassion who has shown and told this lineage the path to untie the knots of karma and dharma that have been entangled in the soul for lifetimes through the acquired karmas that are the gross, subtle, causal, and effect of not only this lineage but the entire human world, to guide them to liberation, and to complete the dharma of the next age by coming in this age, that it is indeed the right of the human world to be freed from births and deaths forever,

Your Swami

Guruvani
