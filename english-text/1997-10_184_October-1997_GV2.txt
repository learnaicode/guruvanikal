Guruvani October 1997

There are quite a few people here who have got the opportunity to come and stay in this ashram. All of them have sincerity, love, and respect. Those who stay in the ashram and those who stay in houses outside must remember some things very seriously. Our mind should be full of conflict until we can complete the action that comes through our imagination. That is, how long has it been since we came? What did we come for? What were we before we came? The mind should always be full of conflict with the thought of what we have become now.

Most of the activities here are done by householders. Through the action done here, a part of a new knowledge is being brought. What is this new knowledge? Before this, two eras have passed - Treta Yuga and Dvapara Yuga. Like this, how many Treta Yugas and Dvapara Yugas have passed. Similarly, how many Satya Yugas have passed. But we do not need to make such big evaluations. We have heard that each era happened in a special way earlier. Crores of people came and went to settle these two eras. The authority to conduct these Yuga Dharmas belonged to India. The reason for that is that it became the birthplace of sages. The sages came and worked not to fight and win against anything. It is to develop our happiness, mutual respect, and sense of cooperation. That desire of the sages and the sense of dharma they developed through us existed in Satya Yuga. But Treta Yuga and Dvapara Yuga have gone wrong. It has been wrong for the last 25 Chaturyugas. That mistake that has come through the system of worship has affected not only India but the whole world. It has brought people to a false sense of freedom.

When talking about freedom, we need freedom. Our country was captured by people from other countries. This has happened many times. We do not know where the flaw that caused this lies. Even if we might know, we do not have an understanding that clearly shows what the knowledge of the Yugadharmas that have come down to this day was like. Telling stories about the miracles of some princes and ascetics, and about yogis who lived emotionally without needing food, and finally dancing in front of a devil sitting in our courtyard is the familiarity with the knowledge we have gained. Then they have shown knowledge by showing tests. Will the God who shows this test make us right?

Because such a familiarity came and went among us, we could not bring a true life to men and women. Many kings ruled in a way that no language could express completely. Because their language changed and returned, India lost a common language to speak together. As far as India is concerned today, we are unable to express our desires to each other, develop them, correct them, and share them. Even if we try for that, it is not easy.

Before detailing all these flaws in this manner, I have something to inform you. I have had many opportunities in my life to say what I have said to many people many times. During those opportunities, many pieces of knowledge and many pains as a prelude to them have occurred. The cause of those pains has also been explained. But you have not wished to evaluate it. I had not prepared anything for that knowledge. What I had in my mind was only the imagination of love. When this love was shared and shared among ordinary people, experiences occurred that made it seem whether this worldly life is so painful. This excess of pain occurred through the guru-disciple relationship. Many people talk about the greatness of the guru-disciple relationship. There is some truth in it. But we have not been able to understand that its good and evil are only up to a certain part in the completion of truth. One can plant and grow a plant and see its goodness and experience it. When each such growing thing is acquired as property and grows and spreads as education, wealth, and lordship, we are unable to know for what period this growth and existence is. This is the truth regarding our life. That is, the shortcoming that has come in us is a course of the tradition in which we lived and grew up. That course is the knowledge that knows that I am the tradition that includes all the pains lived as I am big and I am small, and wealth, and whatever can be acquired along with wealth.

Therefore, what we have gained is a capital that cannot love anyone. That lack of love has come through the familiarity of a land and the familiarity of a country. We can never correct it. You should know that this realization is the light of a knowledge that has come over many periods.

What is the condition of every child in our land? We should be able to bring a change to this condition. That is what you get from the life in this ashram. I have informed you of this truth in many forms. I have also made you experience this truth at least in a small way. Due to some familiarity with which you grew up, you are not even able to bring this experience together and show it in your own home. This is what I have been able to understand from living with you. You can only show outside what you can show at home. You have become father, mother, children, land, country, and thus the world in such a way that you cannot be united. How can this be changed suddenly? Because of all this, it is a familiarity that came and went being worshipped and respected ancestrally. The result of that familiarity changing into many over many periods is a familiarity shared by capitalists, kings, emperors, and all such governments. All these children are the ones who shared it thinking it was the knowledge of some truth. None of this is what we have given today.

If freedom is achieved as if by capturing and tying it up, that freedom is not conducive to living without fear. We are not the ones who achieved freedom that allows us to live without fear. What we achieved was freedom gained by a people lacking knowledge, wealth, valor, and enlightenment. If some valor and wealth are tied behind the ignorant, what will we become? Therefore, we have become a silenced race that plays and grows. With the way of life we are accustomed to, we can only live without a sense of purpose about what we should become. We have only had governments that do not know how to behave with whom and in what manner. No matter how big a government has come, they have all committed their own thefts. Which model will we look up to and live by?

I am not saying this is anyone's fault. It cannot be said that it is due to a lack of education, enlightenment, or wealth. What our ancestors worshipped must be changed in every era according to the dharma of the time. The failure to practice Yuga Dharma is the reason for all shortcomings and faults. When we compare the knowledge of our country with the knowledge of other countries, we can agree that ours is a very leisurely knowledge. But our ancestors did not reach a point of knowledge that could handle that knowledge. Therefore, we are unable to take and give the knowledge of our country.

Since coming here, you have understood a knowledge that you have never heard before. That is Darshana. Some have called this direct perception and disembodied voice. But what is called Darshana has very large dimensions. What all happened since the time this earth or the solar system was formed? What is the reason for such things to happen? Are these causes and effects in the lineage of Manu or the lineage of the Trinity, or is it the result of many people coming together? Darshana is the spreading light of this knowledge. How can what came through many be brought together? But God has given us permission to bring a stability to this. What is that? Our ancestors, the ancestors of our individuals, are changed, given compassion in their character, and set aside. Then they can be born at the time they need to be born. The sense of duty of the people living today is to raise children with the qualification to give birth to those glorified ancestors. What experience have these people gained to raise such qualified children? Past experience is the growth of experience. The Ashram is the bridge that connects past experience and the growth of experience. That is, the place that radiates the true light that determines and nurtures the course of past and future eras!

Guruvani
