[GV1:P0391] December 1978

[GV1:P0392] If we say we must become one, as long as this order of worship remains uninterrupted, it cannot be removed from the human community. At the same time, as long as the workers and institutions standing under the shade of these organizations do not stand with respect and devotion, its profound knowledge cannot be reclaimed. Even the society that carries the essence has not been able to do this. Denying and understanding anything from a distance will only be an understanding rooted in meaninglessness.

[GV1:P0393] Question: What is the reason for you to say so?

[GV1:P0394] Answer: After the Swami's samadhi, his fortieth-day bathing ceremony (Nalpathukuli Adiyanthiram) was not conducted. (There is such a ritual after the samadhi of a sanyasi). Why was it not conducted? That cannot be stated in this context. It will only help in creating agitation among the people. It was mentioned merely for the sake of knowing.

[GV1:P0395] Another thing: In Sivagiri, known as the Swami's samadhi place, innocent sanyasis and innocent brahmacharis have had to experience situations for ten years in a manner that no one else could know how they happened. Although many among them are still alive today, a person who lives with the knowledge of having experienced it is the Retd. Ayurveda College Principal, Sri. V. Bhargavan Vaidyar.

[GV1:P0396] All this originates from a culture that lacks the ability to respect and understand. Because of this, righteous sanyasis and brahmacharis vanished from the field of action. That continues even today. Apart from a few changes, it has not clearly resolved, and the disputes among the institutions caused by it have not ended even today. I merely mentioned these things as they are part of my daily experience. We, along with the people, still need to awaken and think so that this can deeply penetrate the very depths of the hearts of today's little children of the coming generations and be absorbed by them. I have a lot to say about all this.

[GV1:P0397] Guruvani
