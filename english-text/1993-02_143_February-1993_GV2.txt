February 1993

God has provided us this path to fulfill the efforts of great men. This is experienced even among householders and children. Your own siblings can be shown as proof of this experience. This cannot be taken in hand and shown. What was taught word by word for seventeen years has been noted down here. It cannot be taken out now. If there is anyone after my time, they will do it.

The ancestors of your** family must be purified. That is what the Gurupooja here is for. Your fatherhood was lost and the offspring went astray. The children have intelligence and ability. But because of their ugliness and meanness, the parents are in a situation where they can neither swallow nor spit. It is difficult to change them from this state. We can advise them. We can pray for them. That is all that can be done. This flaw will not be seen in children born hereafter.

This system of removing the flaws of ancestors and merging them in the light is not just in India, but nowhere in the world. By seeing what gods and goddesses are, catch hold of the deity of worship, and catch that light. This is an action that no practitioner inside or outside India can do. To grasp this, one needs God's grace.

There is something called Yogagni. In that place, the flaws of your house are burnt in the divine light. The subsequent light is dedicated to God and instead, the Guru is given for worship. When you start accumulating merit by worshipping God in the name of the Guru, you might even be turned upside down. Because you are a fallen offspring. When accumulating merit, the body and mind will ache and there will be many difficulties. Blood, marrow, and flesh will heat up. Humility and truth

How can we correct the children who have degraded without merit, knowledge, and freedom of worship? Whatever we do, they will reject and destroy it. Children born from an uninterrupted birth cycle after Guru Pooja will develop a special character by the time they reach sixteen years of age. At the age of twenty-five, they will rewrite everything. They will find what is needed. This is an attempt for that. Children born after this obstacle is removed will think and act intelligently. Thus, they will reclaim the country. We only need to give them some knowledge for that.

Peace and harmony must be fostered in the country. These children will be the ones who work stubbornly for that, regardless of caste and religion. These children should be raised with the determination of being an Indian, without the difference of Hindu, Christian, or Muslim. For this, education centers and employment centers are essential. Through these centers, wealth, enlightenment, governance of the country, and everything else can be acquired. This is a matter of such foresight.

For this, God has given us a visionary nature. First, gods and goddesses and sage ascetics came. Many things were done through the gods and goddesses. They obeyed what was said. It worked like green leaves and scissors. Those who knew nothing called me a sorcerer. No one bothered to investigate what this was. After about three years like this, some contemptuous behavior started to appear. Some things worked out, some did not. Usually, chronic diseases were cured ten days before the predicted day. Only holy ash and water were given. ◌^ But later it stopped curing.

According to the word I received that children should be made to do this, children are doing this. I was told that this is for the children of the world. I am not doing any fraud. I am not doing anything of my own. I have no beard, hair, or matted locks, no mantras or tantras, and no other trickery. It is done with the holy ash given by my God, with the tongue in my mouth. You can accept this if you want. Now it is you who have to implement this.

There is a way for both men and women to become good here. Within that is Guru Pooja. Purifying the corrupted ancestors, teaching to call upon God in the place of worshiping demons, giving new blood, marrow, and flesh with the help of that God, and making the children born from it the basis for ruling the world. What is needed for asceticism is here. Asceticism is the light of God. There are householders here who have received it. They are householders who look after all household matters just like you. There are hundreds of people to introduce here who receive light and form among them. I am not showing this. This is not mine. It belongs to God. Then I have suffered a lot with my body. I wish to see the will of God working in your children. I also have a desire to sustain this. It is for these desires that this language is spoken.

There are people of all castes and religions here. None of them came to become ascetics. I didn't place any of this. This is the work God does to show that God has glory among you. The experience happened to them on their own after coming here and believing. Both those with knowledge and those without any knowledge have this experience.

Because children remove the evils by seeing my form, my body has pain and suffering. I endure that. The goodness residing in the children is being copied to the world. Therefore, those who do Guru Pooja should not accept other modes of worship. Because the demon will block everything again. Your old disease will return. This is an inexhaustible wealth received from God. How you can sustain it and bring it before the world

All that must be done. This is the aspect that should control the administration of the world. Or if you violate this, it will be stopped for the third generation. For example, as a result of the violation during the time of Moses, Jesus had to come. A qualified Moses was given but not accepted. Therefore, after two thousand years, Jesus, who had no significant paternity to claim, was given. When the king Buddha was given and not accepted, Shankara, who had no paternity to claim, was given. Even after that, Gurus were born. All this is God's work, to see if you will accept it. Now, you can only raise elephants and howdahs of what has passed. You cannot cultivate the idea. All this is God's will.

If you reject this idea, there will be danger to the world. If a natural disaster occurs, where will you end up? All this is trivial for nature. You can see its signs here and there. If you do not respect God's will, danger lurks in front and behind. If arrogance comes when you get some money from abroad, God will teach you. This is a land where everything grows. But there is starvation here. Arabia is a land where not even grass grows. But there is prosperity there that attracts you to do slave labor. Because no one lives there without praying five times a day. You have no discipline here. That is why this danger has come upon you.

God gives guidance. You implement it for your children, for your home, your land, and your country. If you do not see this in its proper measure and weight, you will have difficulties. Guru Pooja is done here as a foundation stone for that.

Most of the people who have come here are communists and Naxalites. But the experience here is that all isms and all arguments collapse on the basis of faith. No one should think that this is just asceticism. There is a way of life here. Not only that, except for nonsense, everything else is here. You must have the determination to impart what is here to the children of the world, no matter how hard it is. We do no other mischief here except prayer. Your goodness and fortune must come through your actions. If an ascetic acts, he will deviate from the custom. But if karma is not carefully maintained, everything will be lost. You must pray to impart this prayer, which you have been told to do eight times, to each child and to the children of the world. That is the primal duty. If fortune is to be helpful, first to God

You must pray. Then comes the work for our survival. After that, the intellect for the survival of life. We must develop it and reach a determination for a personality to repeatedly fulfill the actions of a hundred thousand people. Through that, the discipline and order to benefit the world. Only if we act in this way will it be useful. There should be a permanent existence in faith. Do not just go by what someone says, but go to the Guru and know it. Along with that, there should be the consciousness to surrender everything to God. What we are doing today is for how many chaturyugas or manvantaras. God gave us a medium for that. We need a special quality for that. Forgetting male, female, big, small, caste, and religion, we must spread the love of siblings born of the same womb. If you see a flaw in anyone here, you should privately feel the pain and tell them. Similarly, in your siblings' home, if they are unable to get a girl married, or if a family is on the verge of being lost due to the bad habits of a boy, you must be ready to resolve all that.

Never blame a sannyasi. Even if he has nothing, he has endured a lot of sacrifice. Because of the sannyasi's sacrifice, there is a complete benefit for any matter. If you have an understanding of this, luck and prosperity will come to your children. That is, if the sacrifice of a sannyasi is respected, its benefit goes to the householders. The situation where one can go to the land where kerosene is produced and earn money came about because of the religious consciousness of a section of the people.

I heard a lot of abuse for this, endured it, suffered pain - and God also broke a flaw. Although the method of an ashram is adopted here, it is different from the general concept of an ashram. What is the existing concept of an ashram? There are four types of understanding for this - yoga, karma, bhakti, and jnana. The existence and system of this earth are mixed with many of the various aspects of these four. When we say householder, his course of action is what fills this earth. Because householders and their various actions are more in number. Brahmacharis and devotees are very few. And there should only be that many of them. They are guides. The sannyasi and the devotee all originate from the householders. Therefore, it is the householders who need to improve. They are the main ones. Our ashram system is designed with an emphasis on this idea.

All religious principles are said to be harmonious. However, customs and attire are different, aren't they? The essence is the same. But it can only be understood if there is the tolerance to respect everything without rejecting anything. Some people reject the temple system. But that should be with complete knowledge. Rejecting it otherwise is intolerance. The Christians and Muslims here separated from the Hindus here. They happened to separate due to the excess of the hardships they suffered. But the ancestral basis of all these sections is the same. Therefore, everyone is brothers. Hindu religious principles or culture is very great and broad. But everyone has the obligation to think that a mistake happened somewhere. It may be due to the passage of time, it may be due to a mistake in the practice of the dharma of the time, it can be many things like that - we must know that it happened like this according to God's will. Very few people have this knowledge. At least those who know should understand this and act. People in a single house are unable to live without jealousy. Then what about in society? In the country? You must have at least a rough understanding of the varnashrama dharma. We must change according to the times. In the Satya Yuga, man

They lived like gods. In those days, one could pray to God by seeing Him directly. It should be understood that when the era changed, everything changed. The period of a Manu is seventy-one Chaturyugas. Looking at it that way, a human being has only the lifespan of a worm. Within this short period, we can only acquire a little knowledge. And that knowledge itself is experienced from the evils of the Varnasrama Dharma.

There is no place for a Shudra in Varnasrama Dharma. But in which house is there no Shudra? The Pulayan is in all our houses. There will also be a qualified woman. Can we ignore all this together as a family? A Shudra is a Shudra. He worked for everyone. The Brahmin knows this. He also knew that Kali is rightfully the Shudra's. Whatever is built and brought up is the concept of God and the purity in the words of the sages. When we say sage, there is a sage according to each time. According to that time - it is the dharma of the yuga. The dharma of that yuga stands as a part of humanity. The mistake lies in handling it by keeping it as varnas and the mistake that occurred in not doing so. That is why leadership was given to him. The Vaishya, on the other hand, was kept outside. Even the Nair army was created. The rule of the kingdom was divided and scattered. This is due to the duality of the kings. The entire power of sin struck by this duality and others fell on the Shudra's head. Without knowing that duality, without respecting them, those who recited the Vedas at that time did not know the dharma of the time. Knowing it, the kings should be advised by a guru who knows the dharma of the time by calculating the time in those respective times. Although there is plenty of evidence that this happened before, we all know how to say, 'Isn't Kali the dharma....isn't it the destruction of Kali'. When the great sages who did not fully know what Kali dharma is described Kali, they forgot to talk about the dharma of the Kaliyuga. Perhaps if there is any mistake in the time of the sage who knew it then, or if the guru who stated the dharma of the time is denied, this is likely to happen. We see some evidence for this here and there. First, those who made mistakes in the unknown royal dharma or sage dharma made it written that Kali is bad. It must be assumed that there were many sages who saw that the dharma of Kaliyuga was right. Vishwamitra is an example of this, as stated in the description of Kaliyuga that one can see rocks crumbling and flying due to the beating of the sheaves. And the act of Devendra who sent Menaka to disrupt Vishwamitra's penance. It is said in the same story that Vishwamitra created Trishanku Swarga itself with his power of penance. Then my life is an example. I will try to tell it as best as I can. You should try to take it as you can. By looking at it by including karma and dharma with life, those who determine your Kali or determine the time, Kali is the time we have been allowed to finish it properly. Here the dharma is the same for the Brahmin and the Chandala. The same goes for the Kshatriya. Sree Krishna is the great soul who showed through his life that this dharma is right. Similarly, Kali dharma is a subject that is equally possible for a householder, a sannyasi, and a sage. When we hear or know about Janaka's assembly, the decisive imagination that could be said completely about truth and untruth was going on through two women, Gargi and Maitreyi. Even today, at one end of the almanac, the figure of a blindfolded woman holding a weighing scale is given. We still respect them as the words of women showing what the truth and dharmas in Janaka's assembly are. But let there be no doubt that this is a land that has been taken by saying, seeing, doing, and making others do all this. However, because the sannyasis or sages who could show such a true situation slipped into error knowingly or unknowingly, they could not tell how to handle the dharma of the time. Because of this, it has become clear that each of us is the owner of the fortune of a true householder like Janaka, who maintains vision through advice, truth through vision, and dharma through truth.
