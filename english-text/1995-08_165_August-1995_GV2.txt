August 1995

All human beings are continuously receiving knowledge and familiarity from other humans and from all corners of the world. Parts of this knowledge have traveled across different times, lands, and countries. Everyone gives their own account for each part of knowledge. According to their respective ways, each is correct. Mainly, religious theorists record them by studying and teaching about a lineage of gurus, or a lineage of prophets, or an ancient lineage. Followers of the guru path also record it in the same way. If one has to bear witness to all this, or express it in a new language, there are still thinkers today who would say that it is one of the wonders. Even if this wonder is formulated or expressed in words, without the masses practicing and following it, they go about neither obeying nor making others obey through mere words. Can anyone do anything here just with language? A term for this can be stated like this: a slow denier. Nothing can be learned or taught from this.

In the world, the influence of human history belongs to Asia, especially India. It is India that gives the complete account of the Chaturyugas and Manvantaras. It still exists today in such a way that it cannot be proven wrong.

The Yuga system - Krita Yuga (Satya Yuga), Treta Yuga, Dvapara Yuga, Kali Yuga. Its duration has been determined by five gurus known as visionaries. After one Yuga and before the beginning of the next Yuga, a transition period is mentioned. The transition period which is the end and the beginning. It is not possible to determine the time of this transition period entering from one to another. But the Manu tradition determines the time for this. Manusmriti says this; the duration of entering from Krita Yuga to the beginning of Treta Yuga is as long as Krita Yuga. Just as it has been stated and confirmed for Satya Yuga, the duration of the other three Yugas has also been shown. For the Chaturyuga comprising the four Yugas, twelve thousand divine years are mentioned. This is the calculation originating from the Manu tradition.

But this calculation does not mention the period with the special rule of the transition. That is the time when many great men come and work. There are gurus with a vision of five hundred to five thousand, ten thousand years for a Yuga. When taking that in the total calculation of a tradition, it goes to ten thousand years and beyond that, some religious principles, old and new, have regulated it to twenty thousand years and a maximum of up to two lakh years.

According to the calculation of fourteen Manvantaras of Hindus, the time of fourteen Manus together is depicted as one day in the life of Brahma. After that day, then the deluge. Thus, the period of fourteen Manvantaras is depicted as the period of the deluge. But somewhere in between, it got confused and the karma called Nanmukhan (four-faced) went wrong. When that story passes through the Puranas, through some artists, this term Nanmukhan has changed and instead depicted as the face of Brahma. Telling the story through another way, many organs of Brahma are depicted like this. Thus, the imaginary story of Brahma is what completes the Trimurti system. Subramanya, who is of the knowledge tradition, is known as Arumukhan (six-faced). When it came to talking to these faces, the story of Nanmukhan imagined by Brahma itself disappeared. When imagination and poetic fancies mixed and the Yuga dharma and the Trimurti system went wrong, the face of knowledge stopped speaking. In the history of Nanmukhan, which got caught in poetic imagination or the fall of the Puranas, the face of knowledge, which is called the fourth face, was depicted as being pinched off when angry. After that, when it was depicted stopping at the Trimurtis, that face disappeared. That is, the face of knowledge disappeared. In another Puranic saying, when Arumukhan got angry, he pinched off the fourth face. This is the history. Thus, the conceptual deities Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva were depicted and completed in the place of error.

Coming that way, the concept of the Saptarishis or the history of Manusmriti or the Manu tradition is lost without being seen. Coming back here, wherever the Vedas were written as four, the Shastras as six, and the Puranas as eighteen, the boundary of the six (river) was written and completed, and the sea was created there. Even dividing the boundary of the stream and the lake, it has been tried to be told like the knowledge of the sea. Finally, a word liked by the assembly of scholars was completed by the scholars sitting in the royal assembly as the sea of knowledge!

But this is not enough for us. When a mistake occurred in human duty, which should act with light and righteousness for ages, the historians mentioned earlier tried to complete it by calling it the Stone Age and so on. All these efforts have become like trying to untangle a tangled thread thrown into the sea when it gets unraveled.

Be that as it may, can we utilize the opportunity we have received or not? If so, how? The time received means the birth time of great men. That is, as mentioned earlier, the great men who had vision from five hundred years ago, and recently Moses who came after Krishna and the lineage that came with him, and then Buddha who came to India (there are accounts of many Buddhas - among them Gautama Buddha) - there is an account for all this. In addition, the Siddha principles that came at certain times in between, the various principles transmitted through them, such as Yoga principle, Karma Yoga, Hatha Yoga, Chandrika Yoga, Hamsa Yoga, also caused confusion. They did works creating various miracles. When Dr. Somervell traveled in the outskirts of the Himalayas, he saw a person sitting like a skeleton. When he went near, this figure asked: Is Henry IV ruling England now? The karma that completes that penance is amazing, having lived for centuries through rejuvenation therapy and so on, is also here. The karma that states the qualification of penance, that completes the act of penance, has been brought and stopped there. This is a kind of Yoga Siddhi.

If such a miracle is told and diverted, will the face of human knowledge turn? Or if the sutra point in Patanjali Yoga is completed and the karma difference of Yoga Vidya is completed, will it become human duty? In the story of Kabir Das, a disciple of Ramananda, a story of a Gorakh (Gorakhnath) is told. But he could not win against Kabir Das. Not only that, history says he left. Then what is the benefit of this miracle?

But let me tell you what we have been told here. The truth that what should happen according to Brahma's determination is there in the form of the Trinity exactly as it should happen has been experienced. No matter how it is elaborated, it only stands where these three faces are mentioned. The face of knowledge is not seen in it. There is no way to tell the developments when visionaries come there. For that, we need to understand something. If we study about the author of the Gita during the time of Sri Krishna, we will understand some things. History has proven that Krishna was brought deliberately by God's determination. How is that, the history that Kamsa killed the seven children born to that mother even before Krishna was born. Some stories have also been written about it. Whatever it is, a truth is evident. Believing the words spoken by the Gods who appeared, Kamsa could not know the side of the disembodied voice because he did not know the side of the face of knowledge. He could not even know whose disembodied voice spoke to Kamsa saying, "If you do not rule properly, I will send someone to your house."

This is proof that Kamsa could not grasp the truth even after crores of gods in the Trinity system came and told him. He tried mantras, tantras, and Putana to kill Krishna, who came according to Brahma's determination. But nothing worked. The truth that Hindus, who do not fail to tell the history of the Yadava clan where Sri Krishna was born and brought up even today, also failed to respect Krishna along with it cannot be forgotten.

History says that Krishna ran to various parts of India and created many war situations. It details the application of the eighteenth war by the author of the Gita and Arjuna's test of strength. The proof that the people or kings of India did not respect Krishna is that Krishna had to run away to another shore and build Dwaraka from there. Another history says that Krishna, while in deep thought there, was struck by an arrow and fell dead. It was on that occasion that Krishna instructed Arjuna, saying there is no other way to save them, at least take these women and children and cross the dense forests. Vyasa himself says the history that when they went there, in the battle with Jarasandha's men, not even one was left to be saved. Vyasa says thus: Arjuna! When Krishna, the Lord of Yoga, was alive, your physical strength and the strength of your boons bore fruit. But Krishna has passed away. Now none of this will work. Therefore, let us go for penance. Thus, history says they went for penance. Can it be said that the scholars here or anyone else do not know this?

Krishna was born as the eighth son in the eighth state. He also proved that it is the face of wisdom. It is this face of wisdom that reveals the truth to Arjuna in the eighteenth war. Although Vedanta completed many things, the truth is revealed to Arjuna, who cries aloud saying that he finds it difficult to kill great men like Drona and Bhishma and many others. Look at how the poet completes this: Krishna gave his own eyes to Arjuna. But by grasping Krishna's advice, Arjuna sees directly who these great men really are; after that, Arjuna bowed to Krishna and subdued all this. When one remembers that such a Krishna and Arjuna made a mistake, the heart cannot bear or forgive it. How can the sorrowful truth standing in this part be completed?

But look at the evidence we have for this again. The same thing that happened to Krishna has happened to great men of many justifications. The same thing happened to Moses who followed Krishna, Buddha in India, and many great men who came with and without caste and religion.

If we take about twenty-five historical figures, the Prophet is one great man among them. The same thing happened to the Prophet who came in the exact same path that Jesus came. Look at what that great man, who thought that there is nothing but You, has told us; what is this that has been shown to me, who came believing that God and Truth are only one! Jinn are ruling the world, alas!

What does anyone have to say to this? This great man did not say this for one caste or religion, he said it for the human race as a whole. What could we, who came believing and not believing, do about this? It was the Prophet, who attained Samadhi in the seventh state, who said that Jinn are ruling the world. Did he tell a lie, or did he say it for the sake of the world? What else could we do till today other than worship these Jinn? We, who worship gods, goddesses, yogis, sages, ascetics, wise men, and saints in the language of each religion, are actually worshipping Jinn. After that, how many unrespected great men have we seen till today. Radhaswami is one of them. But there is no Guru-disciple relationship.

What is the Guru-disciple relationship?

The Guru-disciple relationship is taking the waking state, dream, and vision to the Turiya state and correcting the final vision in many ways. For one state (one step), there are seven thousand four hundred and forty-four visions. The one who completes seven steps in that path is the one who has seen the presence of God. Then comes the vision of God, the vision of Brahman, and the vision of Parabrahman. A special state that cannot be described about this

It is because they exist that the sorrow of these great men cannot be removed. First, they must be liberated. After that, their lineage must be liberated. Thus, only a Guru who comes through the divine will can recognize whether the aforementioned disembodied voice is true or not, without using sleight of hand, occult powers, or knowledge. The one who rejects knowledge and ignorance, completes duality and non-duality, and makes things look good does not know this. Only a soul who is born completing the eighth state by birth, who has taken countless millions of births thirsting only for knowledge, can realize the word of Brahman.

Another proof for this: Showing in a vision who all these mentioned great men were and how they came, and realizing who stands where in the different states of those times. Our destined duty is to find the self-sacrifice that each of such great men has done for us and knock on the door. Karma is not a clown who argues and wins by saying whether it exists or not.

Vision is when the ascetic and the wise man who have put the soul on the path of virtue come together, show the mistakes and good deeds that have happened, and correct them. This vision joined with seven thousand four hundred and forty-four is one state. The completion of the seven states (steps) - that cannot be achieved by Vedanta. This is the difference in states where, having reached the state of offering the soul by the soul, the stream of light is classified by seven, seven thousand, and seven thousand four hundred and forty-four.

Believing without a doubt that we all owe a debt to the divine will that proved this to be true, one should practice as a Guru the one who corrects by reaching the self-vision of 'Jagrat-svapna-sushupti-natham sakshipoorthi namo namah'. This should not be completed through the words of the best of scholars. This is the calculation of the stream of light completed by self-vision. The language of the Vedic scholar for this is:

Guru Vani

September 1995

We fought each other caught in the system of special rules of all actions—we cannot touch this, we cannot go there, we cannot take that, we cannot see that. In that fighting action, the Varna-Ashrama dharma was lost. The caste system came. Castes cannot touch each other. In this practice, all actions were corrupted. We are reclaiming that corrupted action. Even though many tried, they could not reclaim it. The action in Shantigiri is a return to a casteless state. The goal is not just to develop a monastic order. Nor is it to search for and lift up the knees of Indian culture. There is no need to lift it up anymore. Because this is Kali. The dharma of Kali is not to uplift by standing on the shoulders of the wrongs that are standing high all over the world. Our action is to destroy the wrong part. That is also the dharma of Kali.

In this action, men and women who are householders must acquire a great position. Grihasthashrama is a great principle where a householder rises and, through his children and lineage, takes up the intelligence to rule the world and the action to impart goodness to the world, becoming world protectors or even world motherhood itself. Without knowing this, you are living as opportunists and perishing.

You do not know what worship of God is. You do not even know how to distinguish and act upon what a householder, a sanyasi, a yogi, or a worker is. None of these actions are in our possession now. Our life's quest is to bring this back. This is the first action in the world. This action has not happened anywhere else. If it comes anywhere else in the future, let it come; we have reached the humble action of God's will.

When I first came here to reach this action, there was an announcement not to sit at a junction where two people are not seen together. Something happened here then. The locals also know a little bit. But even though they knew, nothing special happened. When I first built a hut here, I entrusted some of the families here to look after it. You came after seeing the downfall of those families. What is that downfall? They have come here and seen all this. Even though many things happened in front of them, the lack of desire to take it back gripped them and alienated them. Alienated them in such a way that they could never come back or take it back at any time. Some of them say today that it was a bad action. There is no use in them saying so.

The action being done here today is to mold the children who are to give birth to the class of God, Goddess, Sage, Sanyasi, and Sage, whom our ancestors worshipped, loved, and respected. No one who has come so far has done this action. They did not allow it to be God for them. That action has been allowed to us as God. You should know that. But whatever is said, you do not understand it. Even though more than ten thousand families have done Guru Pooja in this institution, there are not even a handful of people who know and live in that greatness. Then how will you take back this action? Many children of this land who were associated with this forgot it. I haven't told them anything. When they come to me, I sometimes try to tell it like a story. But they don't feel the pain. Why? Our ancestors are the ones who made epics and legends into stories, turned those stories into plays, and turned those plays into nonsense. Each of us is the children of these ancestors.

When we say that our subject is to correct this ignorance, what is needed is your children. We are not even able to bring these children forward. Because the dharma that we worshipped and made others worship, and offered a lot of wealth of love, is surrounding our lives. That is, if a girl is born, she is made bad in such a way that her goodness cannot be known. If a boy is born, he is made a drunkard in such a way that he cannot know what action he has to do for this world. It is very difficult to change these bad things. This difficulty is also good in a way. Because at least in the name of that, you will work hard.

There is a saying among us: Husband is God! So is this scoundrel who has completed it by stealing and has never shown justice - is this the meaning of that word? Does it mean a nonsense person who lives by losing humility and virtue without learning to walk in justice? We are creating all these. How are they running this country? What all will this mischief not do?

The first action I am doing for you for this is the Guru Pooja here. This is not about my greatness. I don't know greatness like that. I am not an educated person. I am not great by wealth, ability, or education; God has not brought me to any of that. But there is one thing, God has given a field that anyone can reach. Through this

Here are the children who are meant to give birth to many great souls capable of doing what is needed not just for this country but for the whole world, passing it down through you, your children, and your lineage. We do not even know how to create this. Seeing the gods and goddesses and everything that has come according to the law until today, giving them what they need (not rejecting them), and seeing them in the path shown by God to claim victory or liberation, we are doing it merely by the resolve of 'Parameshwara'. To do this, we are handling the divine voice we received from God exactly as it is.

This is not, as some people think, done by chanting with a lit fire and capturing some ghost to do the work. Nor is it done by bringing a root from the places where I have lived. I have not taken anyone's root. I have not found any situation suitable for taking this root. There are no suitable roots for it either. By hollowing out all these stumps, setting fire to them, filling them with good soil, planting a sapling there, and nurturing it to grow. For that, suitable children are needed. You do not know how I am doing this, and that is my sorrow.

I am making you perform the karma that elevates them to a great level by rectifying the flaws in that tradition, so that the karma in the lineage of gods and sages that has come from the beginning until today does not break. Then what should be your greatness? It is an acquaintance that humbles by bringing into you the right to be born for ancestors, gods, goddesses, sages, and even some ghosts standing as eunuchs. Maintain the truth in that acquaintance and transform yourselves into karma combined with merit originating from that truth. This is the goal. I have no other logic or theory. I have done this for fifteen thousand families in this manner. They are unable to take it. Moreover, its adverse effect is built into their karma, causing it to become a reason for destruction. About fifteen houses in this area, which made prayers and resolves and sacrificed a lot for this, have been destroyed in that state; and the remaining houses have left this area.

There is right or wrong in the ancestors. The difficulty encountered when trying to remove this is very great. To know that, you will understand if you look at the great souls who have passed away. But the biographies fabricated about them are not suitable to be examined in that way. An example is Gita Govindam. Why are children made to perform Krishna Leela? To impart more emotionalism than what is in them, children are decorated in this manner, degraded, raised, and the completion of that karma is added. This is what the Shakta, Shaiva, and Vaishnava here have done. You will never be able to change this. This is the matter behind the arrival of so many great men. We do not have the experience to change this within a lifetime. But there is luck.

You will not be able to see such a thing in any part of the world. Such a karma has never happened in the world until today. Your stories talk about the Bhagiratha effort. Bhagiratha was a king. He did many things. But even in that, this was not achieved. Here, knowledge is being given to you in such a way that you can see and understand it yourselves. That is what God has destined for you. It is in this knowledge that the god, goddess, sage, ascetic, wise man, devotee, and father are corrected of their mistakes and placed in a good position. Your children are living truly and entering into that position. Even if you could not become like that, you should at least be able to make your children do it. This is the great karma you do to the world. I am telling you how you should be from the time I do this karma until the children are born. You do not understand that.

Instead, by telling them something else as a big deal and making them listen and obey, you do not allow your children to come forward in dharma.
