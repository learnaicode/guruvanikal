[GV4:P0991] June 2004

[GV4:P0992] I mentioned a few things about the greatness of sacrifice last time. However, what I have to say does not end with that. There are many things I have gained from experience and understood by seeing and hearing. I possess an immense enthusiasm to impart all these to you, one by one. Only then do I feel you will gain at least some understanding of its true essence.

[GV4:P0993] No matter how agonizing the problems I faced were, I attempted to resolve them while maintaining utmost equanimity. As a result, my problems were resolved. I can definitively state that there has never been an occasion where they were not resolved. To be more precise, I grew up in a desert. I did not collapse in its terrible heat. Gathering strength, I moved forward moment by moment. If I had felt a slight mental distress and abandoned everything back then, I would have reached nowhere today. I would have been destroyed, entangled in the complexities of my own problems. Regardless, I do not know how I was able to gather such internal strength during those days.

[GV4:P0994] Many well-wishers, observing my actions, have offered various pieces of advice. They have tried to dissuade me from many endeavors. However, while listening to all their advice, I proceeded strictly along my own path. I wish to clarify one thing here. We should not alienate anyone. They might be saying such things with good intentions. However, it is absolutely essential for success in life that we accept the positive aspects of what they say and strive to find our own path. We may not achieve its results very quickly. Even so, there is no doubt that the results will be attained in due course.

[GV4:P0995] I began by speaking about sacrifice. All the righteous individuals who have existed in the world have achieved sacrifice through their actions. Only they have been able to accomplish anything eternal here. Look at the lives of great souls like Jesus Christ, Prophet Muhammad, and Buddha. One can find a great mountain of sacrifice there. Moreover, were those lives not the very embodiment of sacrifice? You do not need to look elsewhere. Just look at Mahatma Gandhi, who shines as the apple of our India's eye; how magnificent is the message imparted by that life, which evolved through the greatness of sacrifice. Was he not able to bring a great power, which ruled over a vast nation, to its knees with the mere message of peace? I do not think anything like that has happened anywhere else in the world. I also do not think such greatness could be achieved by anyone other than someone from India. Indians are people who have traversed through that much tolerance and tradition. Their life is one of friendship and peace. No power in the world has been able to surpass that.

[GV4:P0996] In every era, great souls have taken birth in this land of India. Through their powerful sacrifices, they have bestowed vitality and life upon the entire human race. We must understand this clearly. Only that has survived. And only that will continue to survive. Another great soul who created change to a certain extent through sacrifice is Sree Narayana Guru. After Sree Sankara Bhagavatpada, it is difficult to find another Karma Yogi who has brought about such a massive social revolution in our India. Appropriate value must be assigned to his life, which was elevated by sacrifice. If we practice such sacrifice, or if we cultivate a discipline rich in sacrifice, there is no greater service than that. It is not mandatory that we should rise to the level of those great souls from that. Their sacrifice attained a great stature. Therefore, we should not be bewildered by the thought that we cannot even peek into that realm. They reached that supreme position through their relentless effort and readiness. We do not have to turn back thinking about that. If we try to move at least a little within that richness of sacrifice, we will receive its respective results. The magnitude of it will only be received in proportion to that sacrifice. Even so, there is no doubt that it will indeed result in a successful life.

[GV4:P0997] Then there is another side to it. Let it be said that it is somewhat spiritual. It concerns the course of our karma. Some people do not advance no matter how hard they try, while others reach their desired positions with very little effort. What is the reason for this? We can see many people feeling distressed contemplating this. One thing can be pointed out in this regard: the variations in karma. One group of people strives in a state where the karmas or sacrifices performed in past lives are eager for completion. Consequently, they achieve results very quickly. For others, the course of karma may not have been completed in their previous lives. There, this course of karma can only be fulfilled through sacrifice. If we consider the reason for someone rising to great heights, it is because that person's current life is destined for it. They might have completed all its aspects by making sacrifices in their past lives. For such individuals, only a little effort is required to achieve their goals. Therefore, one must remember that the elevated position currently attained is the aftermath of good deeds practiced over multiple lifetimes.

[GV4:P0998] The courses of karma across lifetimes manifest in various ways. Many do not seem to believe in this. It is good for us to remember one thing: if we isolate and analyze the various trajectories of life, we will become convinced of that absolute truth. Many are forced to lead different kinds of lives. A blind child is born into the home of a wealthy person, while in some places, children are born with deformed limbs. We must observe and pay attention to all of this. What are these phenomena? Are they not the resurrections of the cursed aspects of past lives? How can we choose not to believe these things? Thus, it follows that the base actions and good deeds of past lives continue to follow us. There are many reasons why we emphasize this, and it is certainly something others should ponder.

[GV4:P0999] Now, let me say a little about the general life of human beings!

[GV4:P1000] When a human being becomes sorrowful, forgetting everything, he wanders in search of refuge. In prosperity, he forgets everything. That is a general characteristic. However, he must be able to cultivate a presence of mind that remains unshaken and unwavering in the face of anything. To achieve this, he needs to remember the controller of this universe and act with that awareness. Only then will sacrifice emerge within him, and only then will it attain a permanent state!

[GV4:P1001] All the great sacrificers the world has seen today are those who have worked incessantly, standing behind that power. We can all clearly understand what they have achieved as a result. People continue to follow their path and find solace in it. Thus, their words inspire us to lead a fulfilling life.

[GV4:P1002] I have mentioned the life of Jesus Christ earlier. Did he not sacrifice his very life for the sake of sacrifice? What could be greater than that? Even in his final moments, he said, "Father! Forgive them, for they know not what they do." Do not the words of that great figure, who highlighted such greatness by offering his own life, resonate everywhere even today? The case of the great Buddha is no different. Is he not the noble soul who renounced all the royal comforts of this world and set forth for the welfare of the people? Who can claim the magnificence of that sacrifice? How monumental his achievements have become! Thus, it can be seen that all the great souls who achieved immortality in life were the very embodiments of sacrifice.

[GV4:P1003] Here, such sacrifice is also necessary for the foundation of our material life. If we wish to initiate any movement, we must make sacrifices for it as well. The success of that institution will correspond to the extent of those sacrifices. If we examine the lives of many who have risen to prominence today, we can understand this fact. But if the seeds of ego take root in their thought process, we witness the destruction not only of the individual but also of the movement they have built. Therefore, as we progress towards a goal through sacrifice, we must acquire all the relevant knowledge. What is that knowledge? What purpose does it serve? Ego dwells within the human mind. Hence, the primary requirement is the eradication of ignorance, which alone will lead to success. What is required for this? We must understand the life perspectives of great figures, reflect upon them, and subsequently strive to put them into practice. If we proceed step-by-step in this manner, we can reach our intended destination. Such a systematic life is, in itself, a form of sacrifice, and it is for this that we must strive. Perhaps many of you would like to know the benefits of doing so. First is the liberation from our karmic bonds, which facilitates merging with the Divine. The second is contentment in life. We shall attain both of these, leading us to the unearthly, supreme state. What more does a human life require than this?

[GV4:P1007] But in reality, the wealth of knowledge of this city is very immense. Agreed. Suppose it is the greatest thing for our country or otherwise. How do we understand this? Because it is said that the one who married and brought this woman built temples in many ways. Thus, when it came to the act concerning their beloved deity, this Pandyan king built the Madurai Meenakshi temple, and when it was properly completed, he asked this woman, "Now, who is greater: your father or me?" She replied that he was not even as big as one pillar of her father's. He cut that down, sculpted the woman and him together, and placed it there. How do we know history through this? Because so many such fabricated stories and lack of historical truths have misled us, we have no knowledge about anything. With that happening, a discourse arose for our caste. How did it first originate? If we need to cut and dig the earth, we have to invent an iron tool, and only after that can we do all this. So, when our people look at it, how long ago was this iron tool invented? They talk as if it was found somewhere just yesterday. If so, how did cutting and digging the earth and all this happen? Therefore, they talked us into this, deceived us, and ruined us. This is not the story of one Chaturyuga or two Chaturyugas. The story we see today is the remnant of thousands of Chaturyugas. It has been covered up, hidden, and fallen apart at times. All that will undergo many changes according to the coming times. But this is not what we need to understand about ourselves. We must understand exactly how our ancestors lived, know the sad stories of their lives, and then we must definitely find a special dharma to work for their welfare and ours. Because we lack this special sense of dharma, we have no knowledge about karma. Then what happened? We all jump around together in all the frenzy shown by these caste oracles, and you and I compete to show that your frenzy is greater than mine. Other than this, is there any truth to be told in this? If anyone has, they can say it. Some stories were also written fabricating this as God. And now, they are writing stories for some family temples! They are writing about this Devi temple. They write and bring a Devi Purana and fabricate it. [GV4:P1008] We celebrated the magnitude of the malpractices we each engage in, trapped in the mistakes that occurred due to our ignorant methods of worship. What if this ignorance, which is shown as a special rule, is written down and taught! It is the fault that each of our children is not growing up in that proper manner. What happened because of that? Caste came and became the lord, class hatred arose, and in every way, talking about lord, boss, and slave, what we hold in our hands is the harvest of the fortress of destruction that was sown. If we say that all this happened by God's will, how can we deal with it? Why is this said in brief? It is about where we stand in the act of worshiping and making others worship today. So, when everyone talks about the worship of God in their religion, their God stands as the greatest ancient creator. Similarly, if we speak of each caste, our caste is the greatest. And these lower castes do not even have a God. What to do? As a result of building slavery in this way, we do not know how to worship God or worship the greatest entity. This is the truth. Therefore, if we want to worship God, this is not enough. If we don't even have the interest to learn this, what will we do? So, having understood this, we need an introduction to know what God is, what a Deva is, what a Devi is, what a Rishi is, what a Sanyasi is, what a Jnani is, what a Bhakta is, and what a Karmi is.
