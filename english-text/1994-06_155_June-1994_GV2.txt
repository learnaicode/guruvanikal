June 1994

When I started this** here, I got a message; you should think about your life before starting this. I didn't understand what it meant then. I asked my Guru, Padaniswami. He said; I have only received two things. You will get the rest. One, it is not enough to seek only the spiritual. Two, you will have to change your ascetic garb.

Here, under the name of completion, a prayer and ritual were performed twenty-seven times. The twenty-seventh time, on the seventh day after the prayer started, I was told to place a Kumbham (pot). The first intimation about this was received two years prior to that. I also received a message that its efficacy would increase as the years go by. For up to four years, there was no ability to do this. The next time, it was done on a small scale. This has shown us a nature that works and flows for humanity, from the five elements, through the five senses, and through the five sheaths. It is being done with a systematic change from what has been happening to date. This is not something everyone gets.

Every religious nature has had something like a vision or prophecy. Siddhi is not knowledge. This is a path where Siddhi matters cannot be handled at all. Those with me have insisted that I show some things. If I ask Brahman whether to do it, it will say to do it. But after a while, it will say it is not good. Thus, it has to be stopped there.

Detailed information about placing the Kumbham was received recently. It should be done with the determination that our lineage must have the strong ability to carry this. Among Hindus, the Trinity worship system is strongly prevalent. There was a time when this was indispensable. When it was hidden under the Varnasrama Dharma, it failed to take root in the soil of Kerala. It was with the advent of British rule that Varnasrama Dharma suffered a setback and waned. This has not completely changed even today. Political scientists have kept this from being known outside. Although independence was achieved through that, good things have not been touched. That will now only be passed on through children with aptitude. It should be understood thoroughly and taught to the land and the children. The impurity of the land's ways is hidden in the people.

Each worship showed the past Acharyas and their deeds. From the early days of Kali, Gurus were born for this purpose. The twenty-seven resolves mentioned earlier were performed to give birth to children free from baseness at a certain stage of Kali. After twenty-seven rounds, it reached inside. If I were to understand it directly myself, arrogance would arise. Therefore, I was not informed.

Gross, subtle, effect, cause - nothing happens without these. Those who should become the most qualified are householders. They have become bad today. Whether truth exists or not is becoming evident in householders. Those here are able to understand this completely. Those who have sight also get the word. It is because they lack aptitude that they cannot do this with determination. Determination will only come to Brahmacharins.

Although it was said years ago that Karma should be placed, it was not done due to lack of circumstances. This can be done as an offering for your incurable diseases, and those who are mentally broken can also do this. This can also be done to change bad character. In addition, this can be done for the fulfillment of desires. We have received that whatever we think, it will have an effect.

Something like this is something that happens occasionally in the world across epochs. If there is ever a mistake in this, you will definitely get a chance to correct it. It will provide you with a virtue that will never end in any era and an earning that will never be destroyed in any era. You should bravely take each one, saying let whatever comes come, and let whatever has to go go. We have seen, experienced, and believed each of these. All this is for you. You are the ones who have to grow and maintain this in an organizational nature.

Question:

What is the reason for a repetitive nature to occur for the four Yugas?

Guru:

The very reason for this question is because the tradition in Hinduism has become old in countless ways. Sanatana Dharma means the culture of the great souls that existed in those times. It is explained in history that the culture that came and went as Shaktheya, Shaiva, and Vaishnava tried to improve man to a certain extent. Meanwhile, the way some Acharyas have come following the lineage of Manu is also seen. But those who have come like that are not in the lineage of the first Manu. In the lineage of Vaivasvata Manu, the first Manu of the current Manvantara - to be precise, because some flaws that happened to the Acharya named Sathyathranan in the third Chaturyuga went into selfishness, the rule according to God's will did not happen. Sathyathranan's was like a theory of evolution. Therefore, in order to implement the Brahma's will, the Trimurti system was brought

in the seventh Chaturyuga.

Question:

Was there no Trimurti system before that?

Guru:

No. The Manus are the ones who fulfill the ideas according to the Brahma's will about the growth and decline of the world for a period of time. The origin, existence, and dissolution of this world have been created with such fourteen Manvantaras. The nature of the soul according to the Brahma's will has come like that.

Question:

What is the reason why most people do not know that there is such a lineage of Manus?

Guru:

As said before, the feeling of 'I' (the feeling of Aham Brahmasmi) came to the lineage of Manus. In that ego, they happened to see in a state of knowledge that all the knowledge activities that have existed in this world till then have come through them. Even if you take and look at the Vedanta books, you can see this feeling of 'I' standing out. Even if you talk to any ordinary person, you can see this independent feeling of 'I' (my party, my family) standing out. When it comes to the term Brahmachari, such feelings became through the lineage of Gurus. In the lineage of Devas, the Deva I caught or the Devi I caught has more ability (glory). As a result of this coming in everyone, Chamundi, Yakshi, Pei, Marutha, Madan, and Mantramurthi all became lords due to their own selfishness.

Question:

Why did this happen like this?

Guru:

As said earlier, when the Brahma's will did not happen due to the ups and downs in the lineage of Manus, the Trimurti system was created according to the command of that Brahma power. In this Trimurti system, what Vedanta is became non-existent. Its nature became pooja, prayer, and manifestation. When that nature also changed and came to the pride of having Marutha inside and whether to show a test, mankind became unable to fully see the glory of God or to carefully know or study its measure or centers of knowledge.

Question:

If you say like that, won't it mean that the things that have happened so far are not right?

Guru: Yes, that it is not right is what has been said so far.

Question: You yourself said that the Trinity system came into being to implement the Brahmic resolve because the Manu lineage made a mistake!

Guru: The Varnasrama was introduced in such a way that the people who worshipped in the Trinity system could not change according to the time and eligibility, and in that caste and sub-caste were introduced. Prior to that, myths were created through the study of Sruti and Smriti and made into authorities. Before that, empirical arguments came and some sannyasins themselves handled the Siddhi systems. When it reached this extent, the Manu system faded even from memories. This present degradation is the result of not changing the systems that needed to be changed according to the change of time and the futile idea that the Brahma position should be in the hands of the Deva Brahmins at any time.
