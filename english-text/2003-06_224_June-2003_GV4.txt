[GV4:P0835] June 2003

[GV4:P0836] What you must note is that when I refer to the ancients, through our interactions and loving associations, we have formed many acquaintances where some, using their limited intellect, trivialize these matters while considering themselves highly intelligent. We cannot even comprehend the nature of this intellect. It is impossible. It is only at the very end that we realize this capability is entirely beyond our grasp. Therefore, we will not be able to assimilate anything. It will result in a situation where we end up losing the very things we have acquired. Once that happens, whatever we do thereafter will be of no avail. Thus, there are certain individuals who have loved and respected me alike. This has happened even among them. The reason it happened to them is that a vast community of our so-called ancestors has died and perished. For these departed souls, we perform certain rituals according to whichever religion or caste we currently believe in. Under the guise of this, followers of each religion perform these in their own ways, yet some find it inadequate. Not just some; the majority are like this. Perhaps one in a hundred might exhibit a change. We remain unaware that such a scenario is enveloping us. The reason for this ignorance is the wealth of experience we have accumulated over a very long period of traversing with our ancestors. It remains embedded within our very life force. The moment we exhibit a vulnerability, that entity infiltrates. Once it enters, it begins to operate. After it becomes active, even if they are one's own children, father, or mother, they will apply that intellect in a manner that suits their own nature. By applying their intellect in such a way, the detrimental effects of this intellect are inflicted upon this life force. I have reminded you of this many times. However, using this intellect, many people perform sorcery and similar petty miracles, and numerous families where such departed spirits stagnate will nurture this in the manner of 'nurturing the wicked like a palm tree.' Having nurtured it, it brings about a completely alienated way of life in them. When this happens, as an example, the ones who fail the most in this regard—not a failure of intellectual prowess, but a failure in life itself—are those from the Vedic religion. Wherever, whoever, and in whatever manner they have worshipped within this Vedic religion, if they acquire numerous virtues through these packaged and structured rituals of worship, all of it will go and accumulate in this deity. This residing deity will then seize it. I do not know if you understand this. You must understand it. After seizing it, all these virtues are drained, and this ancestral spirit is taken and positioned within the karmic sphere of these family members. All of you must pay attention to this. Do not assume this is a trivial matter being discussed. Ultimately, these family members, having supposedly evolved with intellect, capability, and competence, will completely ruin it. I have realized that this has happened to more than half of the families who have approached me. Because the misconception that they are intelligent and knowledgeable sets in, causing them to lean towards that karmic path. Even if they are father and children, or husband and wife, they will unknowingly operate from that intellect. No matter how much they try to suppress and preserve it, they will operate it and it will ultimately recoil upon themselves. It is a path of great destruction. The reason for stating this now is that there are many who have performed Gurupooja.

[GV4:P0837] Therefore, even though this has been told to you many times, and you might understand it to a great extent, a slight misunderstanding will still remain. Through that slight misunderstanding, you will make compromises. When you make such compromises, it transforms entirely into a compromise. I am mentioning a significant issue that has occurred to those standing close to me. By the closest people, I mean the nearest families. However, despite being close, they could not grasp it; moreover, they have altered it in some manner. Nothing can be done. They still will not understand this.

[GV4:P0838] As far as we are concerned, we possess knowledge that has lived on for billions of years. Even if that knowledge is minuscule, akin to the saying 'many drops', it can become a 'great flood'. Such a scenario has arisen. It is among those standing close. Around twenty families from Pothencode itself form this circle. If one considers the sincerity they showed back then, it is immense. They had no knowledge. They did everything that was told. Thus, they completed the tasks. In Varkala, it was difficult to conduct it. Saying that it shouldn't be visible to others, it was brought here, and even here, it was only after two and a half or three years that those matters were finally completed. Those tasks were completed. Then it took five years for it. Later, to make it practical, it took about ten years to put it into practice. It is not possible to gather all this together and present it before you. What is the reason? It is simply that it will not be understood. Did they do it out of any enmity? No. Small words will be slightly misconstrued. But they ended up stalling there. This is to remind you about the families that performed Gurupooja. Hence, even after saying this, you lack the ability to preserve it. This must be done in a specific manner. That is, 'Word is Truth'. You must not overthink its logical aspects, whether less or more. If you think, it is lost. When the intellect is applied, those matters simply wither away. Some operate solely on intellect. That will lead to peril. I am telling this to all of you because I can only speak of it. God is bestowing upon you a karma that needs to be done, caused to be done, and undertaken. That portion given by God, you risk losing due to negligence, or due to a perceived indifference, or under the pretext that intellect is of no use; we are the remnants of countless generations where everyone grew up in such a manner. Some people stood aside, uttered various words, and lost it entirely. Shouldn't I say this? Should I just speak to appease you? Through their actions, everything went downhill. What is this that I am doing? You must think. I am not doing it because I am capable of it. I am acting merely as an instrument. This is the divine will. To impart that divine will to you, it has been brought to you across many epochs over billions of years. However, in all these coming epochs, Kali is the path of salvation. You have been told and made to believe that this Kali Yuga is evil. Let us all ponder this together—is Kali evil? Thus, when the attributes of Kali are described, people are made to act vilely in accordance with this Kali. But Kali is not like that. If one does not behave truthfully, Kali will lead to destruction. You simply do not understand that scenario. How can I make you understand? That has become a great difficulty. In that state, those from Pothencode brought pots, pans, winnows, and all such things, cooked, and at the time of washing them, there wouldn't even be water to drink. There are those among them who washed the vessels and drank that water. Despite undertaking this with such hardship, they could not assimilate it. Because they would trivialize certain words—thinking 'he just says things like that'. With that, it is entirely lost. Observe every household. This is why 'Word is Truth'. Back then, when things didn't work out for two or three people who were observing and speaking here, I had a deep doubt whether I had made a mistake, and that is when this Avadhuta came to Varkala and said: No, 'Word is Truth, Truth is Guru, Guru is God'.

[GV4:P0839] That is to say, the state of the *Trimurti* was attained. It is being imparted from there. That is where the error occurs. Consequently, about ten to fifteen households there fell into this predicament. However, they were unable to discern it. Back then, an instruction was received that two individuals should not be seen together. That is how the arrival here happened. Having arrived here, a massive undertaking spanning six to seven years was executed without anyone's knowledge. Subsequent to its execution, it was gradually initiated. All of these were administered in the form of *Guru Pooja* and various other practices. However, since even the families who performed it, and those who facilitated it, failed to exercise caution in the *karma* of this word, they are all departing one by one. What can I do with you? I can never do anything for you. This is a sorrowful *karma* of mine.

[GV4:P0840] Guruvani
