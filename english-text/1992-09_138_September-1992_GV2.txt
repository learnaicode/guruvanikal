Disciple: Aurobindo Ghosh has said that Lord Sri Krishna is not the supramental light. It is not clearly understood what that is. There should be some light on this from the Guru's mouth.

Guru: When talking about that, we have to go far back and see. Manvantara -- or Chatur Yuga, how many Chatur Yugas make a Manvantara, we have reached a miserable state of not knowing all this clearly, and much time has passed. It is a mistake that happened through another right.

Disciple: Two words, right and wrong, have come from the Guru's mouth. I have a desire to know what that right is.

Guru: When talking about the origin of our India or the world, it takes so much time for the change of Yugas and floods to come. After that, when entering another change of Yuga, the Manvantara comes. It is said that the world changes and floods come with fourteen Manvantaras. But many events happen in between.

Disciple: Although the events that happen in between are recorded in history, it was not understood that they are related to this.

Guru: It was not understood that it was related to this because there was a flaw on the part of a Jnani's knowledge.

That is, in the third Chatur Yuga of Vaivasvata Manu, when the sage Satyavrata was ruling as the lord of the world, he looked back. He should be seen as a person who was born and brought up in the intervals of Yugas and floods. Only then will we understand.

But no matter in which intervals one is born, if any word or deed happens forgetting the original source, it is difficult to correct it later. Especially when looking at the causes and effects of the origin of the Sanatana religion. Causes -- it is talking about the causes of the seven Saptarshis. What the concept of those Saptarshis is, should be revealed by a Jnani. It was when it became an opportunity where it was covered with stories, sung, told, and reached a karma made into a Kathakali, that Krishna's birth became necessary.

Disciple: Is the Guru suggesting that trouble arose in the Sanatana religion due to Satyavrata's flaw and Krishna had to come to resolve it? If so

It has not been revealed whether Sree Krishna, who came according to the divine will, was able to complete his mission perfectly.

Guru:

Even before Krishna was born, the Yadava clan had a situation where there were kings for each clan at different times - for each Varna. So the Shudra varna had started before the beginning of this Kali. Kamsa belonged to this Shudra varna. Since Kamsa was a wise man, a devotee, and a scholar, he had the fortune to become a king. But the birth of Kamsa and Krishna happened before the end of Dwapara. Since he was knowledgeable after giving birth to Kamsa, it was thought that it could be brought through him step by step.

Disciple:

Should it be considered that Kamsa came as a condition of the divine will?

That divine will brought that royal power to the Shudra class in many times in a way that gave them royal power. In the part of Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, Shudra, we are all Brahmins alike in Satya Yuga. But when it changes from there, it changes according to Yuga Dharma.

Yuga Dharma means Satya Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dwapara, Kali. In Satya Yuga, all people are Brahmins. Everything happens truthfully. They only do truth. Beyond that, they do not think or make trouble by thinking about things that do not matter. Human nature was like that in Satya Yuga.

Disciple:

Human nature was like that. Humans have changed from that now. The reason for such a change should be revealed.

Guru:

That was said earlier. None of us were forgetful in Satya Yuga. After that, the Brahmins - those who are meant by Brahmins are wise men. The karma revealed by Satya Yuga is that the time of such wise men is over. There is another reason for that: Treta Yuga is the time when Kali and Dwapara join together. Satya Yuga is the time when Kali Yuga, Dwapara and Treta Yuga join together. In such an era, man - the early man of Treta Yuga - lived up to one hundred and fifty years. A man less than forty feet or thirty feet is said to be rare. But as Treta Yuga as a whole comes across as Yuga Dharma, an idea does not stand beyond two thousand, three thousand, four thousand, five thousand, ten thousand years at that time. Due to antiquity, it will have many falls. Great men will be born here and there. It will go on like that under those great men. Thus, there was a cover from the beginning of Treta Yuga. It happened as if human intelligence was completely covered. For example, after this truth disappeared, some intelligent, knowledgeable and wise people meditated and found some lights through that meditation and found out what exists in that light and acted as a witness, and that is why India's philosophy lies as an atomic science. Today is the time to talk about the atomic science of the atoms in a fly's leg. The administration was carried out according to the guidance of spiritual men who found out how many crores of precision more than that.

To carefully understand what that karma is and to take it together

Disciple: We can understand that the knowledge of the wise was not widely available to the people because of a misfortune that it was more on one side and could not be spread.

Guru: The words that came from the mouths of the women who are called Veda Vahinis were kept as Smritis and Shrutis. That Karma is what is meant when it is said that Vyasa came and divided it into four. Dividing into four means the Dharma of Satya Yuga, the Dharma of Treta Yuga, the Dharma of Dwapara Yuga, and the Dharma of Kali Yuga. Many wise men could not understand what the Dharma of this Kali Yuga is.

Disciple: Why is it that even the wise cannot understand it?

Guru: As mentioned earlier, wise men will come as authorities for two thousand, three thousand, five thousand, or ten thousand years. If we look at our great country of India itself, it is known that more than six hundred princely states were ruling. Then this rule was controlled by a sage or royal envoys. The one who comes for two thousand will preach for two thousand and go. Or if he is the one who comes for ten thousand, he will make a vision for ten thousand and go. They are called visionaries. The ordinary man of that time did not have the knowledge to think about the karma in their sub-divisions. Why?

Disciple: Is it because ordinary people cannot think about all this?

Guru: One who lives truthfully has no karma to think about anything. He is living truthfully. But that does not mean he will leave love, respect, and humility. But he does not have a high thinking habit. He does not look at its theory of evolution. He has no thought about the past and the future. He evaluates the contemporary action, makes it work, and gives it energy, words, and amount. A mistake happened from this.

Disciple: I want to know why such a mistake happened.

Guru: There have been times when South Indian culture and North Indian culture were going on very differently. In fact, Satya Yuga is a period when North Indian culture and South Indian culture went together. Many events happened due to the change of that Yuga.

This Yuga has the duration of the other three Yugas. In this Satya Yuga, an Acharya or what is said first about it is not what is said later and comes here. Because the end of truth must come. The people who come at the end will say something and go. It will go into darkness as people cannot take it. The increase of people will also be like that. Ordinary people will be plenty. Then it will go into that darkness and be like that for a long time.

Again ascetics or visionaries will come. An example of that is the penance of the Saptarshis. Their concept is what is called the seven lands. That karma is there. Then the special Tantra that formulates the nine and the special Navagraha, along with that, the calculations starting with Rasi, Porutham, Nakshatra, Nazhika, Vinazhika, etc., were linked together. Then a guru left these calculations divided like this. The verse in Guru Gita is proof that those who imparted those calculations had come at different times.

'Anekajanmasamprapta karmabandha vidahine'

That is, the Gita says that those who came at different times starting with Krishna Dwaipayana divided the four Vedas from the words of this Vyasa. These Vedas have been divided over many periods. It is not possible to divide it at one time. It is said how many Vyasas there are. So they came through the history of many Vyasas. Thus in all those times

came as a guru, a king, and an emperor. Thus, there were many minor gurus of different eras and many major gurus of different eras.

Disciple: Gurus who guide for a specific period and gurus for an even longer period appear. The reason for this should be clarified.

Guru: The reason is that every soul has accumulated karma. The Puranas say that someone did penance for a thousand years. Another person did penance for twenty-five thousand years. Yet another did penance for a Manvantara. This can go into the stories of the seers who say this. There are people among them ranging from a thousand to five thousand. There are also those who speak for five hundred years and leave. Moses, Jesus, and the Prophet are examples of those who came for two thousand years. They came as prophets. They did not come to the path mentioned above. They came as self-prophesying ones. They created something else through their own creation. Because, over time, due to the widespread layout of the country, making it impossible to bring any of this straight, they performed their duties in each country according to the language and dress they got at that time, changing and quoting the language, and it became diverse. Thus, in the early days of the Treta Yuga, many gurus had come. By the end of the period, many gurus had come. They spoke in one place for a thousand years, in another place for two thousand years, and in another place for ten thousand years. People lined up under the gurus who went like this, and kings created royal assemblies to show that my guru is greater, my guru is greater. There was a time when decisions were made in it. If we look from one end of a ten thousand acre land, the other end will seem to touch the sky. The one who ruled the land within this limit became the king called Vanavaramban. Inside such kings, there was a royal assembly that praised and glorified their king and their guru. When it came to be so, because these scholars spoke in many ways, it became impossible for anyone to guess what the position of this scholarship was. There was a time when, in the same royal assembly in some periods, due to the honesty of some women, they could sit with their eyes closed and tell the truth. It was also there in men. Such times kept coming as truth itself. But the story told for ten thousand years is not the one told for two thousand years. The story of the one told for ten thousand years is not the story of the one told for twenty-five thousand years. Then they - not only scholars but also kings - became obstinate among themselves. Some of those kings ruled by considering what was said with a gap of two thousand years, some ruled by considering what was said for five thousand years. Some others ruled by considering ten thousand years. Then the defect of not being able to think and know what is right and what is wrong happened here by itself.

Disciple: What would be the path given by God to overcome these limitations and go to the ultimate truth?

Guru: I told you, it is Manvantara. All six Manvantaras went well. It went exactly the same way, there was no difference. But the sage named Satyavratan who came in the third Chaturyuga of the seventh Manvantara tried this, backwards. What have they done in each period? Which lineage is that?

When looking at the matter of that lineage, everything is contained in the Manvantara. From the Saptarshis onwards to the time of Manu, they saw what a Manvantara is. Each one merged into that Manvantara and due to the inevitability of that self-illumination, began to think towards that vital point.

Disciple: I pray to clarify what that vital point intended by the Guru is.

Guru: The sage named Satyavratan came and looked back. What is human effort, how is it. This is what is called the vital point. No matter which way you look, everything has operated under this Manu. Everything goes there and joins Manu. The Guru was conceived as Brahman.

How does the Guru become Brahma? That has not been looked into. The Guru is Brahma, that is the truth, it is the action of the ages - when it came to this, the authors of the Puranas who came in each era wrote each of the Puranas by linking them together. It became unknown to anyone which Purana to hold on to to get its truth. It went into the action of this Aham Brahmasmi. Each of us is Brahma - that is also good in the path of philosophy.

But the rulers and royal preceptors of that time did not act with the knowledge to think about what would not happen here if each of us ignorant people became Brahma. That's where the mistake started.

Disciple: Can you advise a way to overcome this mistake and escape?

Guru: No matter what era the mistake occurs, it is corrected in the Kali Yuga. The Brahma resolution waited for ages for such a Kali Yuga. So, when the end of each era comes, the dharma of the next era must be implemented by taking the last knot of the era. For that, a preceptor who blends the dharma of the past era and the coming era must come. Only five such preceptors will be born in an era. Others will say ten thousand, five thousand, five hundred, and so on. But here and there, a little bit of truth will be seen. Everyone will think that this is a matter for the whole world. Then the rulers and statesmen of that time together wrote and filled the Puranas, epics, Aranyakas, and Upanishads, saying this, and left in their respective times.

Disciple: The Guru said that there are preceptors who come with ideas for short periods of time. But the people who cannot distinguish between the preceptor who comes to correct what has come so far and implement the dharma for the future and those who come for this short period of time fall into error. How can we escape from this and understand the preceptor who truly predicts the dharma of the time?

Guru: For this, one has had to be troubled for two or three reasons. The periods called the scientific age seen today existed even then. For example, who is said to have invented aeronautics - Bharadwaja. When Bharadwaja invented it, the Guru said - 'This is not to be implemented now, it is to be implemented in the coming time. If implemented now, it will do harm.' Because the disciple did not understand that - because he could not see as far as the size of what he had invented - he went straight to the kingdom of Kubera.

He went to the kingdom of Kubera, finished building it there, and returned during the time of Rama. He returned and took Sita away. It was Mandodari herself who kept it very beautifully, without losing honesty and without losing the merit of women's dharma. The Puranas say that she acquired the merit of a mature woman, nursed this Sita or did what she needed, and kept her without any difficulty. At the same time, it also became the cause of the destruction of India and the loss of prosperity of the kingdom of Kubera. Later, visionaries and prophets have come at various times.

Disciple: Although we have heard about one Prophet and one Christ, the knowledge is that many preceptors have come at different times.

Guru: Yes. Many preceptors have come. But the names are many. How many Yogeshwara Krishnas, how many Buddhas, is proof of that. Coming like this, people called Gurus of the ages or visionaries come to do the things needed for the end of one era and the beginning of another era.

But there are two or three things to say about this. The words of the visionaries are to stop in a few words without losing the scope of meaning in philosophy. But if contemporaries are not capable of understanding it, the intelligence and ability to completely see the Gurus or prophets coming for a thousand years, two thousand years, ten thousand years, and a Manvantara in many countries at the same time, and the action part of the knowledge of the vision, become unrecognized.

Not only us, but the rulers of those times and the gurus who advised them would look back at the past, impart foresight in that manner, adorn the royal administration, and the most cunning and intelligent rulers would try to handle it in such a way that their greatness remained intact. If we examine history, we can see that an arrangement of preserving and causing to preserve an administrative machinery molded in the crucible of intellect, hiding the truth over time, is still going on among us today. It is still referred to by both scholars and commoners. Its meaning is Chanakya's strategy. It means hiding the truth with intellect.

In this situation, many instances have passed where statecraft, sleight of hand, supernatural powers, and the tradition of servitude have been misunderstood as wisdom. We can see this even today if we look. Not only in this country, but in many countries, we can see some actions where a certain amount of intellectual prowess, sleight of hand, and the possibilities of using medicinal substances have created wonders, and as a result, the aforementioned intellect, increased by scholars and commoners, has been used to belittle the will of God, making people forget the firmness of God's will. For example, we can see the intellectual strategy of avatars operating by adding the title of Hamsa, calling themselves the final prophet, the final Christ, and recently among Hindus, sage and wise man.

We can see everyone who believes in the aspects of the countless gurus who lived and worked from Sri Shankara to Sri Narayana in the past, praising and glorifying them. Disciple: Could you please tell me why this mission could not be implemented even after so many great souls came?

Guru: As mentioned earlier, we have pointed out the mistakes we made. It is known that the sage Sathyathranan took up to ten Chaturyugas of the four yugas of this Vaivasvata Manu to know what the causal karma is. Then he looked at all the seven Chaturyugas of the last phase of the previous Manu, that is, the sixth Manu, and the three Chaturyugas of the Vaivasvata Manu. One reason for this is that he could not find out from what and how the creative willpower, which is the dharma and karma to ask and know the honesty to distinguish the time from time to time. Secondly, for some reason, the honesty acquired by the great men of those times through the power of penance was lost. For that, the universal 'Aham Brahmasmi' and the four sentences of the Hindus came out, formulated the Vedas, Puranas, and epics, attained Advaita in a single soul, and it became practical. From here, a capacity for action will be reflected in a way that the world can understand what our mission is. This courage of action—to humbly take with devotion, love, and respect, and to give with sacrifice—is a special intelligence that not only our movement, not only those who love our movement, but all those who love this world must possess. Understanding this and acting upon it, people described as writers or scholars, literary figures, and monks and celibates who are meant to make people understand the glories of the visionaries, must come forward. All of them have a right to complete fulfillment. Because the third generation of householders—men and women, wherever they are in the world—who value this completeness of knowledge by paying the full price, making it their way of life, goal, and mission, will be those who handle true skill in action. They will be the perfect prophets of this mission, which is the will of God. I truly say that every child who acts bravely for this will be praised as holy figures who provide the purity of action essential for the eternal salvation of the world.
