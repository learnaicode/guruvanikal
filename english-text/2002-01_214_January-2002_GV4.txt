[GV4:P0001] Guruvani

[GV4:P0002] January 2002

[GV4:P0003] The path that we are currently implementing in Santhigiri is given by God. This is not something that has come in the past or will come in the future. We are only entitled to fulfill our duty of completing this path. There are many here who have come seeing me without even knowing me. I have not introduced myself by displaying miracles or performing magic tricks before you. If you do not understand this with your life, or rather your consciousness, I will not be able to do anything; this is the absolute truth.

[GV4:P0004] God has given us, that is, the human race, only what is absolutely necessary. What is our need? Only what is required for this Guru concept of ours. Everything else must depart from the world. Or, if there is anything that does not depart, it must be assimilated and worked out in alignment with this ideology. This is how it should be implemented. Our prayers and intentions must reach the end of the aeons. Billions of generations have come and gone in a traditional manner. The absurdities contained within them must completely end, and whatever needs to be absorbed into our Guru lineage must be sustained, shaped as God, and fulfilled according to God's will. Therefore, we should not create unnecessary noise in this.

[GV4:P0005] We must clearly understand what the formless and the formed entities are, and what the color of the form is. Suppose the color of the form is given a conceptualization in the Trimurti system. That conceptualization will only reach up to that point. In order to extend the concept to further distances beyond that, the position of Brahma in the Trimurti system lies in total confusion. It has been transformed into one with four Vedas and thus taken through various such platforms. It is not possible to study beyond this.

[GV4:P0006] Question: Even though he has been made into the master of the four Vedas, Brahma still stands as one of the Trimurtis, doesn't he?

[GV4:P0007] Guru: Yes. As the four-faced one. But that face does not exist. Instead, merely a figure has been drawn and kept.

[GV4:P0008] Question: Then what about saying that Brahma is the authority over all four Vedas, having been given the four Vedas?

[GV4:P0009] Guru: They have merely concocted all that. It is just like the concept of Ravana's ten heads. The one who drew it brought it into sound form. What can those who drew this and those who repeat it do about its temporal significance or karmic significance? Because he is four-faced, there is no duality. Everything is an illusion. It implies the absence of two, doesn't it? As long as there is no duality, what can humans do? So this is a lie. If there is something called non-duality, duality must also certainly exist.

[GV4:P0010] What all does duality apply to? The present situation is that it has been included only within the sequences of sound forms. It is the artists who studied colors and played within the sixty-four arts that have brought about these circumstances. And then, everyone has tried to demonstrate it through logic. For example, there is no use in competitively arguing that such and such a preceptor has said it this way or that way. What have they done for the preceptor or for those who competitively argue? What do they have to offer other than the beauty of colors and the beauty of pictures? They can neither see it nor show it. This is just like the prancing of a good bovine. If one merely stands carefully and enjoys this bovine, which shows its ferocity and fluffs up its fur before taking a sudden leap, will that become the karma of the preceptor? Anyone can spout rationalism. How much of it can we actually grasp—this is a subject beyond our comprehension.

[GV4:P0011] As long as one does not know the course of karma that stands as the formless-and-formed, there is no point in offering any justification for it. The blunder that has occurred here stems from drifting away from the karma of the conceptual forms of the god and goddess. When it is said that the deity is both formless and formed, how can this formless position and formed position be discovered? The realm of our knowledge and experience has been convoluted in such a way that it cannot be understood even if seen. What we hold is a muddled education which assumes that everything is achieved with two or four visions of the Lord. What this education does is present the Lord and the Lord's Lord according to a vocabulary of sounds, giving them a form, and then sacrificing them to 'Swaha'. Everything is the Lord's illusion!

[GV4:P0012] To give shape to a sound pattern, the color of the plane from which that sound originates must be shaped. As that color is shaped, the diversity of hues must be formed. It is when this color is shaped that the absorptive dharma becomes complete. Instead of accomplishing such monumental tasks, our knowledge revels in merely speaking about the karma of some Treta Yuga, claiming it is complete, and standing in the position of that Yuga's perfection. This is the aspect that the people are unable to comprehend. If such a profound subject is tossed around like a ball in this manner, what can anyone do?

[GV4:P0013] Here, the loss of an entire Yuga occurs. If one Yuga is lost, nothing from the next Yuga can be grasped.

[GV4:P0014] Question: Can't it be corrected?

[GV4:P0015] Guru: Who will correct it?

[GV4:P0016] Question: Can't it be corrected by coming as a divine mandate?

[GV4:P0017] Guru: When it comes as a divine mandate, a complication also arises along with it. We have an understanding about interstellar spheres, don't we? There is the Earth, the changes in the solar system, and the specific karma brought about by time. This remains within the atmosphere itself. It is just that this nature of karma differs for different spheres.

[GV4:P0018] The nature of changing times stands formless. We must see what the karma that merges into the formless is. This is what is called Varna Dharma. We must know whether this color merges or not. If it merges, we must know with what and at which stage it will be merged. Will it do to merely chant 'Swaha' instead of that?

[GV4:P0019] Here, both men and women have vision. They see and perform all the things mentioned above. This is our subject. These children are seeing where the form ends, what all types of formed and formless entities exist in the world, in which color they all merge and stand as dharma, whose dharma it is, and what its extent is. These formless-forms and color-dharmas are all contained within a single form. Everything is just that. This is a Yoga.

[GV4:P0020] Speaking of Yoga, there are so many yogas mentioned by physicians. There are so many yogas mentioned by Yogis. To put it another way, 'Arimedadi Oil' is a yoga. Thus, every oil is a yoga. Every ghee is a yoga. Every decoction is a yoga. By combining which of these yogas can a special medicine be given for nerve pulsation? For that, the nerve blockage must be accurately determined. That nerve will subsist only in the measure of that life force. It will not stand otherwise. In the part called the nerve, it is maintained by being connected through veins and brought to dissolution through hair follicles. In this state, the rules of Yoga and the rules of doctrine have intermingled and gone awry, standing in a condition where nothing can be retrieved. The formless-form and the color-dharma have played out in this manner and become totally convoluted. The performer of karma who comes by divine mandate must be one who can rectify this.

[GV4:P0021] Missions always exist, do they not? They are present in all eras. There are missions spanning a century, and those that transcend it, allowing one to attain liberation by witnessing the rise and fall of this world. Therefore, simply concluding the mission of a century by asserting that both the manifest and the unmanifest are one and the same will not suffice. For instance, although doctors from various disciplines speak from within their respective systems, the core matter remains the same. However, none of them can control the trajectory of life. This is not something that doctors, or even thinkers or theoreticians, can accomplish. After doing everything possible, they can only step aside saying that they have done all that is humanly possible and that one should now pray to God. As for Vedantins, merely contriving various explanations does not mean they can define what 'Varna Dharma' is, or what the unmanifest and the manifest are. What is the 'Varna' of the deity or Guru spoken of in this manner? What is their 'Dharma'? This is where the aforementioned problem lies. Another aspect is that wherever one is ensnared by a demon, they remain stuck there. Similarly, wherever their disciple reaches a deity, they remain there. What a disciple must understand is in which of the manifest and unmanifest forms the Guru or the deity is absorbed. It is insufficient to merely state that they are absorbed in everything. Where the Guru is absorbed, how the Guru and oneself are unified, what the light of the Guru is, where all the light of the Guru exists, how many manifest and unmanifest forms are within it, and how many 'Varna Dharmas' exist—all these must be declared by the disciple upon seeing them. There are no other justifications for this.

[GV4:P0022] Among the nine planets, we observe three spheres. The rest are seen through the telescopic nature of physics. Seeing these spheres in this manner does not mean we have traveled to all of them. In reality, it is the unmanifest and manifest nature inherent in the planetary bodies that transports us to each sphere. Where does the Guru stand in that unmanifest form; where do they stand in the manifest form? Where does this Guru stand in the tri-guna karma of Tamasic, Rajasic, and Sattvic qualities—this constitutes the state of the Guru. This state must be realized by the disciple themselves. Offering other rationalizations for this will not suffice.
