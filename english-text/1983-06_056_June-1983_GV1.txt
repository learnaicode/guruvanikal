[GV1:P1292] June 1983

[GV1:P1293] When brought up to the Chaturyuga, they do not move forward. They learn from the stories written thus far up to the heavenly realm (Swargaloka), celebrate that alone, walk exactly through that, and will not change. From that day onwards, for God or for Brahman, a lineage must emerge again in accordance with that Brahmic resolve. But how will it come to be? Only those born and raised here with intelligence and capability will reach this path. When moving along this path, those standing on the route taken by their ancestors can only proceed by looking at the concepts written down as yoga, karma, jnana, and bhakti. Thus, one's time will pass simply by studying the manifestation of a deity or their mantric and tantric instruments. He will not be able to advance beyond that. After this, another person attempts the journey, and his situation remains the same. It is said that many such instances were observed. Therefore, the time and state capable of stirring and clarifying this will arrive only in the Kali Yuga. This is because everything else has transpired according to destiny. Denying that, the Kali Yuga is an era where all of these can be suppressed, activated, and united into one. For this purpose, Kali is chosen repeatedly. In such a Kali Yuga, numerous such great souls appeared. That is why some great souls have mentioned in the Puranas and elsewhere: "I must take a birth in the Kali Yuga just to witness the manifestation of Kali, and the Lord must permit it."

[GV1:P1294] What is this Kali—do they not know it? They do know. It is because they are experiencing it. That is the essence of what was said. Thus, many of them took birth in our present Kali Yuga itself. An example of this is the vision Suma received at the ashram in Kallar. A lotus was shown with two thousand four hundred and forty-four petals; when asked how it was counted, it was explained—each petal fell off one by one. When asked what they were, it was said that each represented a Mahatma (great soul). Nothing else was said. It stopped at that much on that day. The following year, an explanation for this was given here. Until then, each event had been unfolding. I am summarizing it. Giving the explanation, it was stated that the Mahatmas seen that day are to take birth and work within our lineage. The first Mahatmas were unable to function within it. They went and referred to the ancient texts. If one refers to those texts, they would revert to the old ways. Because of such an error, it is said that many were born without knowledge, wealth, or enlightenment. Examples of this are Parayi Petta Panthirukulam, Jesus, and the Prophet. Everything they have said in this manner is completely the opposite. They have spoken contrary to these Trimurtis (the Trinity). We did not have the practice to examine what this was. When we take the old texts, we go back into that. That is not needed. Gunangudi Masthan, who came in recent times, had asked, "Will a planted stone speak?" Though various great souls came and spoke for us, we lacked the wisdom to grasp it. However, apart from these Trimurti traditions, because the two great figures who appeared in Western countries came as prophets in a minor capacity and conveyed the exact words they received, they gained significant prominence. They came and ruled here as well. Both these groups ruled our India, which is known as a spiritual nation. Yet, our religious consciousness did not even allow us to ponder the reason why those ordinary people came here and did all this.

[GV1:P1295] Looking at it from this perspective, from how far away we have received this! These two thousand four hundred and forty-four Mahatmas are yet to come and function. This is the first lineage of its kind. You will not find any other familiarity to recover for this. We cannot learn it by looking into those ancient texts. The texts are flawed. These are texts that have originated from prior experiences. It is in this context that ascetics had to employ occult powers (siddhis). Ascetics should not use occult powers. If they do, they will incur their own karma. If they function with humility for the public good and spiritual purity, the weight of their karmic progression should naturally transfer to the life forces merged in the world or the atmosphere, leading them to that ultimate goal. This must be their karma. That has been our principle since ancient times. But having been caught up in this—under this deity—by giving the deity the right to do many things, who knows what that deity has done! It is said that by standing as a god to humans, they would exhibit many mischievous deeds. That was given from Brahman. To remove that, after making a mistake themselves, they must realize it, act again to rectify that error, and humans must rise above the deity. That is the true karma. What we have been told today is a part of such karma. How should that come about?

[GV1:P1296] Certain aspects of your past lives and the devotion you practiced with dedication during those times will merge within your life, bringing about certain feelings and changes. But if you wish to act with a sense of dedication toward the Guru, you must remove the latent tendencies (vasanas) before you. You have a tendency to rationalize and speak without dedication. You do not possess the consciousness to do anything on your own. Yet, you will act upon it bearing witness to your past experiences. If we proceed like this, what will happen to us? We are not to merely quote an ancient text and move on. However, this does not mean we should completely avoid mentioning the texts. What we have to learn from them is this—there have been many errors and many correct actions in the karmic path of the world. What is the reason for this? The karma of a Sage (Rishi) and the karma of the Wise (Jnani)—here, the Rishi and the Jnani have arrived at the same karma. But what distinguishes the Rishi? They have functioned across various levels. If we do not understand the karma of the Rishi, we might think, "Someone else did this in that position, so we can do it too." But we are not of that lineage; that lineage has committed many errors. If we undertake that karma, we will incur harm from it. Therefore, we should not practice it. Moreover, a complete karma intended for our own lineage has not yet come into this. Now, we have a karmic path that we must perform ourselves and fulfill for tomorrow. How will we take it up? We have no experience, no capability, no wisdom—then what is it? It is an acceptance. Without violating that acceptance—we have been taught that "the word is truth." We must believe the word of a great soul. When we apply, understand, and act upon that word, it will become the truth. Then we will feel a deeper reverence. It transforms into the Guru, then into truth, and then into God. Thus, the Guru transforms into God and into Brahman. This is the karmic path we are meant to practice. Now, rather than standing apart as individuals, if you were all born together in this atmosphere, everyone has a rightful share in this karmic progression. It is said that even if we are born as a blade of grass, it is part of this karmic progression. So, what must we, who are bound by this karmic progression, practice and realize? What are the errors pointed out in this? The Guru may commit that error. Only then can that error be rectified. Everyone has committed errors. One progresses by working through those errors. Otherwise, if you operate solely through correctness, you will have to flee when you encounter the operational capacity of both right and wrong combined. Think of the blunders the Guru who first bore this has committed in their life! After returning from a long period of making mistakes, they instruct you, "Do this." When a mistake occurs after doing it, they correct it by saying, "You must do that only after doing this"—do it. It is by believing the word in this manner that they have become Rishis. Then you must accept the word the Rishi speaks. They know that if this is not done in a particular area, this person's karma will not be resolved. They will make you do it. Performing that karma, they might wander and struggle, and perhaps their very life will be thrown into turmoil. It will feel as if the life they had built up to that point is completely battered, like beating clothes on a rock in the water until everything falls apart, when experiencing this beating. This beating is meant to remove the fish bones and impurities stuck to it. They will batter it away just like that. They will wash and straighten out life, but they will not let it tear apart. Even if it tears somewhere, it will be stitched back together. Whatever flaws exist in human life are what get washed. It is washed using the words of the Guru. You might think, "The Guru made me do a mistake." He does not know what this mistake is. He cannot be rectified without this washing. Everyone says, "I was made to perform the wrong karma; I was not given the most correct karma to perform." It cannot happen any other way. He must first err. He must learn from this error. Otherwise, the karmic trajectory within him must be eradicated through that error. Then, the mistakes of the karmas he committed in his previous births will be resolved when he performs this. Once this is accomplished, he will either be guided forward, or he will walk forward on his own.

[GV1:P1297] Guruvani
