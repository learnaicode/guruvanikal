[GV1:P0757] July 1981

[GV1:P0758] Question: From what you have just concluded, it would appear to those with some intellect that this is being stated with immense resentment.

[GV1:P0759] Guru: There is resentment, unequivocally. Would I appreciate my contemporaries deteriorating in any manner? If I possessed a home and a family, would I remain a passive observer if they committed an error? I would endeavor to rectify it to the best of my abilities. If there were a mother, a sister, or close relatives, and it was understood that their actions were detrimental to them, to me, and to the populace, I would alter it. Currently, none of that exists. Presently, devotion to God is not juxtaposed with malevolence. It aligns with benevolence and the elements that sustain it. Consequently, one naturally gravitates toward realms where goodness prevails.

[GV1:P0760] Simultaneously, is it feasible to comprehensively reprimand those who exhibit malevolence? A peculiar perversion that diverts everyone toward malevolence is permeating us. It is observable in everyone, irrespective of distinctions such as scholar or ignoramus, ascetic or yogi, sage, devotee, or man of action.

[GV1:P0761] Just because it is the truth, is it possible to approach everyone and state it directly? Even if articulated, there would scarcely be anyone to heed it. Observing certain things induces agony. Observing others evokes sympathy. There are numerous instances where one is distressed by the sheer obstinacy of certain individuals. In the context of enduring such agony over an extended period by witnessing myriad such occurrences, all these individuals possess distinct capabilities within this world. None of them were deficient in exercising their intellect. If, by divine grace, their intellect could be redirected even marginally toward a virtuous path, is it possible to articulate the immense asset it would be, not solely for us, but for the entire world? Is not the existence of such souls in this world a profound truth that perpetually disseminates eternal peace?

[GV1:P0762] Individuals who harbor the notion that it is sufficient for themselves or for you need not entertain such thoughts. There are countless multitudes in the world. Would it not suffice for ten or twenty-five of them? The nature of devotion to God, or God's love, cannot be comprehensively delineated in any measure. Consequently, the capabilities born of such love manifest in myriad forms. Love resides in everyone. Elevating that love to the pinnacle of its realization can only be accomplished through unceasing vigilance. I do not believe there exists anything comparable to that divine love. It might be owing to the fact that I have become such a believer that these sentiments have emerged. Otherwise, it is my conviction that my words would never exhibit a vindictive nature toward anyone. Perceiving everything through the love that constitutes true knowledge, resolving the deficiencies of existence through actions rooted in that knowledge, acquiring merit in its stead, and ultimately transcending both merit and demerit—if there is an 'I', I perceive it merely as one among billions of offspring, one among billions of living entities. All of this is encompassed within Your glory; You are the truth and the untruth of the world; You Yourself are Sat, Chit, and Ananda—I have merely articulated words aspiring to merge into that state. My sole aspiration is that God may bless all His children to attain this.

[GV1:P0763] Question: The world today appears to be in a precipitous rush with such a revolutionary disposition. What might be the underlying cause? Wherever one observes, religiously and politically, there is a conspicuous absence of tranquility, and actions seem to have devolved into a state of profound uncertainty.

[GV1:P0764] Guru: We should lament only if the populace had not evolved into this state. Observing the progression and regression of the world, this is absolutely imperative for the present epoch. If we remain complacent, assuming that what has been discovered thus far holds some validity, it will prove more detrimental than the preceding conditions. If we concede that what existed previously was valid to an extent, it would culminate in a trajectory that plunges the entire world into obscurity. This is because, oppressed by the tyranny of monarchies, feudal hegemonies, and tenant servitude, caste and religion intensified to their utmost extremes, rendering life entirely bestial. Thus, an ideological quest for liberty fostered a political tension here. None of these aforementioned political enthusiasts emerged by discovering a traditional foundation and seeking to sustain it. Their selflessness, born of witnessing the myriad afflictions and losses of the people, engendered a desire to enact some form of liberation. That is the zenith of their capabilities. Within that paradigm of thought, they progressed—stumbling and recovering—achieving some commendable outcomes while inadvertently precipitating subsequent chaos.

[GV1:P0765] This is an absolute distortion unconsciously assimilated within them, fueled by their egoistic assertions of supremacy, comprising a faction that rejects everything unequivocally and another that accepts everything indiscriminately. When disparate types of leaders were embraced within this matrix, numerous followers blindly enlisted and began unconsciously spouting ideologies. They are ostensibly valorous individuals who remain utterly ignorant of the essence of sacrifice or its fundamental nature.

[GV1:P0766] There is a contingent of individuals who endorse them—destitute people lacking both education and wealth. They implicitly believe whatever is articulated by those they harbor affection for. By aggregating all such vulnerable individuals, a numerical dominance is manufactured. Observing this, the leaders who initially instigated the movement reigned as idealists and leaders for a significant duration. Embellishments and altercations continue to proliferate, creating the illusion that this constitutes the absolute reality of the world. This is the unbridled manifestation of the resentment against the tyranny of the feudal lords and tenants we alluded to earlier. Such distortions will inevitably permeate this process. It is inconsequential. In that frenetic race, even if contemporaries advocate for righteousness, it may not be implemented. Because in an unrestrained surge, this too shall dissipate. Such a pursuit is merely akin to arriving at the seashore and reveling in the sight of the horizon. A state of stagnation will inevitably ensue. A predicament will befall them wherein they can neither retreat nor maneuver from their current position.

[GV1:P0767] Such a juncture will precipitate a phase that rectifies the world's antecedent deficiencies. Thus, as a consequence of this multifaceted and suffocating chaotic trajectory, analogous to the populace heaving a sigh of relief, the temporal manifestation of divine will shall promptly arrive at that precise moment. Then, these aberrations will autonomously recede or be eradicated from religious leaders and mass leaders. Much like the dissolution of numerous monarchies and feudal regimes, it will transpire imperceptibly. Concurrently, an embodiment of the divine will shall emerge. Subsequently, a period characterized by conflicts and disputes over its respective ideologies will persist. For a duration, an intense fervor to implement it will be exhibited. Akin to insatiable desires, it will necessitate a considerable epoch to achieve consummation. When it is thus realized over an extended period, the intrinsic worth of this era will be unequivocally affirmed.

[GV1:P0768] Question: Does this period refer to the Kali Yuga?

[GV1:P0769] Guru: Yes, it refers to the Kali Yuga.

[GV1:P0770] Question: Then, what is meant by this Kali?

[GV1:P0771] Guru: We mean that there will be absolute goodness at the end of Kali.

[GV1:P0772] Question: Certainly, goodness. What is the reason for calling it absolute goodness?

[GV1:P0773] Guru: That has already been stated. As you know, Kali Yuga is the end of the Chatur Yuga. The early period of Kali is an evaluation of the good and evil of the other three past eras. Moreover, it could be that this evaluation is reached over ten or twenty-five thousand years.

[GV1:P0774] Question: Does this evaluation refer to previous births?

[GV1:P0775] Guru: Absolutely, it does.

[GV1:P0776] Question: Then we can firmly believe that there are numerous births, right?

[GV1:P0777] Guru: Yes, you can believe exactly that.

[GV1:P0778] Question: How can this be made believable to the people?

[GV1:P0779] Guru: The people can be completely convinced of this. It is just that today, very few people are interested in thinking about and understanding such subjects.

[GV1:P0780] Question: Then how is this fully implemented?

[GV1:P0781] Guru: Did we not say earlier that there are instances in history where, after running aimlessly for a long time and hitting a dead end, a certain nature gained some acceptance? Similar to that, this will also have its turn. It will be considered like a firm resolve that has grown and risen through fluctuations. Thus, I am telling you in the light of experience that this will be brought to a good path. It will also attain a complete form, just like what exists politically and religiously now. Consequently, in humans who discover this, honesty, humility, and the competence to practice them will reach their full potential. It will be utilized for the peace and survival of humanity. However, that will last for a long time. The era known as Satya Yuga will be shifted to the next Chatur Yuga.

[GV1:P0782] Question: The essence of what you are saying is that it will grow in this manner and complete the era of each Manu, isn't it?

[GV1:P0783] Guru: Yes.

[GV1:P0784] Question: But a certain group of people will not even take a peek at the independent nature you mentioned, right? It is true that you said ordinary people will run after it. However, a specific group does not usually even look at it.

[GV1:P0785] Guru: It would be good if you explained a little more. I do not understand who you mean by "a certain group of people."

[GV1:P0786] Question: Although everyone here talks about progress today, it is shown that there is no progress even in their names. A sweeping term like 'Harijans'. Another approach like 'backward Christians'. We only see one community here that cannot be described in any other way. A class called Islam. If the downtrodden person who is called Thevar or Kali today becomes an Islamic convert, he can immediately sit and pray in the front row of the mosque, at the place of worship, near the tomb. He can perform Namaz. Thus, there is an absence of obstacles to his rituals.

[GV1:P0787] Guru: There are other reasons for this position of Islam you mentioned. Likewise, only two great men have been born here. As a result of Hindus twisting and manipulating it, the term Hindu has been debilitated to a state where it cannot command respect. One is Sri Krishna. He was born in the Yadava clan, which can be called a downtrodden section.

[GV1:P0788] It must be determined that at a time when there was no notable capability or skill, a royal family existed on the basis that might is right. The proof of this is that all the children born before Krishna were killed. Yet, through the stories, Vasudeva was highly praised and glorified in later times.

[GV1:P0789] So it must be believed that except for those belonging to that royal lineage, the rest lived as poor people, as Shudras. The proof of this is that there is a group called Krishnavargakar in the Nair community today. They are just poor people. Cattle grazing is their occupation. Seeing them, one feels that they are not leading an extravagant life. Just as how many sections are encompassed within the single word 'Harijan', similarly, when we say Nair, there are several sections within it. Is it not proof that all of them have been divided and marginalized? The term Nair came down and reached even the Vilakkithala Nair. The pot-maker is also called Pillai and Nair. But what benefit has this aforementioned group given to all of them? Likewise, by giving them names like Mooppakkudi and Velakkudi, they have tried to complete their marginalization. Although the membership here appears as a large number when mentioning the name Nair, when it is subdivided, it reaches a state where we ourselves find it hard to believe how it all stands. Thus, many people belonging to this class and caste have gone back here.

[GV1:P0790] Among the Ezhavas of the south, four illams (lineages) are mentioned: Mootillam, Choyillam, Pallichayillam, and Mayyanattillam. Among the Ezhavas of the north, four are mentioned there as well: Thandan, Channar, Ponamban, and Panikkar. Then it spread and became Vaduvar. Looking at it this way, it is impossible to express in words how much of a mockery this is. This is said about those who have earned the name of a prominent community based on numerical strength. Below this, how many countless castes are there. A high standard has been set regarding these two communities, hasn't it? Even these so-called high communities have not been able to play any significant role here.

[GV1:P0791] In recent times, because the Shudras here had the royal rule in their hands, they did slightly better in Kerala; otherwise, Shudras still have no position in North India. While saying that, people of various kinds are lumped together under the designation of Harijan. A new name has been given to it: Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe. When it comes to that, the people called Harijans today merely face a disadvantage. All these mentioned groups were lying in such a state. There is no doubt that within the next eighty years, this group will reach the position where all the others are currently standing.

[GV1:P0792] The reason for this is that the government, consciously or unconsciously, in every sector under the name of democracy, has established that education and wealth can belong to everyone, and that a position in the government can belong to anyone. If we look into history, we will understand that the prominent communities here did not have much of an advantage. Isn't it clear that this marginalization occurred because the governments in their respective times viewed people with different perspectives? Similarly, today the government gives equal freedom everywhere to the backward Christians and Harijans. Moreover, it provides all the necessary financial assistance for them. At this rate, if we look back after eighty years, the upcoming eighty years will bring them to the level of those who are called the greatest prominent communities today. There is no doubt. Therefore, in any era, there is no doubt that people will be marginalized or prosper depending on the manner in which the state handles and leads them.

[GV1:P0793] However, due to the magnitude of time or for some other reason, Islam has incurred certain shortcomings. They will do many things to show greatness through their rule. Because of that zeal, the stories most commonly seen in Arabian history are those of imprisoning one's father and ruling by oneself. A proof of this is Pakistan and Bangladesh. Even though the Prophet was accepted as a messenger, there is a history of even his children being murdered. It is not customary to kill the priestly class. Yet, we see it being done. Because of the Prophet's steadfastness, wealth and popularity came to them. Now, they are unable to adapt and practice it properly according to the times. If merely one thousand five hundred years of the Prophet's era have been made like this, we see an inextricable knot when we consider what the countless historical treasures of the Hindus are, which cannot even be fully enumerated.

[GV1:P0794] Therefore, even if the question you asked is correct now, it must be inferred that some children of God would have been born among that group as well to remove their marginalization. We must believe that a great man like Ayyankali is a seed of sacrifice. As a result of it, we can believe that today, even in Kerala itself, there are many children in the government, just like powerful people such as Dr. Ambedkar, Devaraj Urs, and Ram, who were even capable of drafting our constitution. All these are sufficient grounds to dispel our doubts.

[GV1:P0795] That is why I stated that, having progressed correctly for eighty years, God has established a state of affairs here such that no government, whether affiliated with a party or not, can discard any of this. Therefore, there is no room for doubt in this matter. Another point, and the only thing I repeatedly urge caution about, is this. We have a society here where even the ascetic monasteries, established by cunning scholars who deceived people in the name of Sanatana, Advaita, and Hinduism, have not lifted a finger to eradicate its evil practices.

[GV1:P0796] It should not be inferred from this that the intention is to condemn them or to banish them in order to move forward. We must also believe that it is perhaps by divine design that they attained certain positions of status, and that through their cunning intellect, they have been able to control and govern the masses to this day. However, we can affirm with confidence that the wise will not reject this era—an era most conducive for them to unite with all human beings, exercising compassion while remaining steadfast in the philosophical principles of Sanatana. This can be achieved if even these capable and cunning individuals strive to impart cooperation and good sense to others in accordance with the present times, thereby demonstrating the unity of human society. Let us conclude this with a prayer that the greatest good sense may prevail.

[GV1:P0797] Guruvani
