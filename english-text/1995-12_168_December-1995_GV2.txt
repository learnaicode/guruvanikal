Guruvani December 1995 We have received a traditional knowledge. That knowledge is different from the way we have traditionally maintained it. Our shortcoming is the ignorance that occurred as a result of our highly uncultured worship, separated by caste, religion, and class. Because it came traditionally, we have not been able to have any realization about it or know the reason why it happened.

Many great souls came among us and tried to retrieve this by practicing and doing karma. We have been told that two thousand four hundred and forty-four (2444) gurus came for this purpose in this Kaliyuga itself. This five thousand two hundred

Even though so many masters, including Sree Krishna, have come within these years, we have not understood what this karma is. It was also a great mistake that they wrote stories and taught us in a way that concealed that great karma. Then there is only one family tradition to say that there is no mistake. The Yadava clan.

There is a family tradition called the Yadava clan. It came from the Shudra class. Coming from its lower strata, even now that group has a family instinct to live with the cow and its kind, not only in North India but here as well. We do not think about why this Shudra class was given royal status. The reason we cannot think like that is because it came in the nature of the completion of the mistake that has occurred over a long period of time.

Without completing this Yuga Dharma, no other classes can do anything. How will the Yuga Dharma be completed? Not being able to know this is the biggest mistake that has happened to us. Satya Yuga (Krita Yuga) is spoken of as an era where everything happened truthfully, without caste, religion, or class. But when Treta Yuga came, we were not told how the nature of Treta Yuga should be. A little of the tradition of the karma done by man in the beginning of Treta Yuga is still among us today. The very practice of going to the forest as sanyasa is the greatest example of that. We have not been told anything about the nature of Dwapara either. Going and sitting in the forest is not sanyasa at all. And one should not assume so. Sanyasa is something that should be done in a way that suits the times.

We are reclaiming the Yuga Dharma. It is said that a quarter of that era will be spent just for reclaiming the Yuga Dharma. But we have no information about it, or if there is anything, everything is God! We do not have the knowledge to say anything beyond God's illusion. This is the current state of Hinduism today. The situation of other religions is not different either. People of other religions also believe that they were born in the homes of small people, or that they should come like a king. We do not have a sense of certainty about it. The clarity in the belief that God's arrangement will come in a lower class, or like a king, or in Indian culture, or in other cultures, is the sense of certainty.

Because we did not have this sense of certainty, we have passed on absolute ignorance. Sree Krishna is one of those born in various positions to remove this ignorance. Krishna was born even before the beginning of Kali Yuga. He was born like that thinking that if Kali starts, the order will go wrong. But it was not possible. Nothing that came after that could achieve it either.

So the ignorance of the Dharma of the era exists among all humanity. Moses came. Jesus came. None of them came to create caste or religion. But what do we do in the nature of worship? First, the church nature came in eighteen forms. Now it has split into sixty-four and exists. Some worship with a cross. It is said that this itself has split into small parts and now there are more than two hundred forms. If this happened in a religious theory that came within just four thousand years, what would not happen with the passage of time!

Whichever way you look at it, this has come as a flaw that happened in the beginning itself. Therefore, we must get the original knowledge entirely from God Himself. Then one might ask, were none of these who came born from God? Or was that knowledge hidden because of their mistake? It is not because of any of that, when they grow up, they

It takes us time to understand. Once we understand, it takes time to obey them. As for those who have obeyed, they begin to think scientifically about what they have known and seen after some time. Even if we look at the history of great souls, the knowledge we have acquired about them is like this.

What is needed to bring about a change from this knowledge? This is what is called Pratyakshavaham Dharmam. But our theoreticians will say that getting the vision of God is enough, everything is done. It is wrong to assume so. For that, completing the Deva tradition entirely, then completing the Rishi tradition, and only then comes the arrangement that shows what Sanyasa is. That is its path. When such a great soul is alive, or in the actions that have come by relying on and rooting in that reliance, what all were we able to take? We could not know any of this. So, going into the profound depths of knowledge that we have absolutely no realization of, we portray it all as miraculous powers. Without reaching a clear understanding of a great soul's life, turning magical powers and mantras - then tantra and yantra - into tools; what happened at the beginning of that life, what happened in the middle, what happened at the end, and how this life duty should be repeated to obey the Yugadharma - we have become ignorant of all this.

In this situation, what can we impart to our children? You are householders. Each of you has a course of action that enables you to grow with the home and live with the land. Can children grow up knowing the danger that has befallen this home, land, and country? Apart from them and us living according to the course of action prevailing in the world, what do they get? Then all they get is only what those called historians have written and piled up. There is no time to study everything written. Then what will they study? What can we, who do not have enough time to study and teach about each era like this, do? Fighting over caste, religion, and class, standing in actions where we do not even live together with our children, not knowing the Yugadharma, not knowing and following it, standing in the denial of God, what can we do?

If each caste has a different God, how can you say that God is one? If you and I have two Gods, what is the significance? Or we all have God, but all these Gods are on different paths. Then how can we unite? Learning the actions of each caste as opposite to each other, turning opposite actions into caste, creating actions through caste, thus everything is done in opposition to each other. However, there has been some change today.

Guruvani
