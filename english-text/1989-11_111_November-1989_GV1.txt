[GV1:P2433] November 1989

[GV1:P2434] The Aryans were migrants from the Middle East. Upon arriving here and beholding this beautiful land, they established their settlements. Arriving here, they drove away the Konkanis who had previously propagated culture in this region. Consequently, the Konkanis were left with nowhere to reside. Their language was also eradicated. To this day, they lack such a distinct language. The language they do possess lacks a script. Fleeing from here, they migrated to Goa. Unable to sustain themselves there either, they fled from Goa. Thus, they scattered across various locations. In reality, is there a favorable perception of them even today? Are they not still subjected to mockery—through stories and plays? They are unanchored individuals. They continue to wander aimlessly, untouched by stability. Even today, they can only be described as a destitute community. Although they fled once, they returned here, aligned with the Brahmins, constructed the Vedic system, and reverted to becoming Brahmins. Their era has concluded. They failed to respect the changing times. The present epoch belongs to the Shudra.

[GV1:P2435] "Shudra" signifies the ignorant one. The era of the ignorant has arrived. The entirety of these emergent prophets originates from the Shudra class. For instance, is Thiruvalluvar not a member of the *Parayi petta panthirukulam* (the twelve clans born to the Paraiyar woman)? It is such prophets or sages who endure here today. The others have all faded away. Similarly, within the prophetic tradition in the Western world, it was the Shudra from the lowest echelon who emerged from among the fishermen. This serves as yet another testament that the contemporary era belongs to the Shudra.

[GV1:P2436] There is ample evidence that the era of the Brahmanas and Kshatriyas has ended. Who has stated this? It is a description by Manu. The Kshatriyas conquered and ruled here and there, and then abandoned it. Later, it became a system of ruling through plunder. After that, it was handed over to the Shudra - the Pulaya. Who is the Yadava himself? He is from a backward class. The story of such a backward person is spread all over India. Krishna belonged to the avarna class. Krishna is indeed dark-skinned. Isn't the avarna who lives comfortably today very handsome? Thus, good times came for the avarna.

[GV1:P2437] Knowledge was imparted through an ethereal voice that Krishna would be born. The entire capability of this action is the sanctuary of the sages who discover such things. Even though the production nature of medicines discovered without any modern laboratory systems was found and recorded, in which laboratory did the sages discover all this?

[GV1:P2438] Disciple: The growth of their own soul.

[GV1:P2439] Guru: Yes. That growth is what we have lost today. Why was it lost? The Brahmana concealed the truth of Brahmajnana, that knowledge, and placed his Vedic religion before us. That has transformed into today's singing, dancing, and the torch-bearing uncle.

[GV1:P2440] Disciple: Today, a conceptual installation has been established here. The Omkara blooming in a lotus. A human figure standing in position embracing it. I understand this as the form of knowledge awakening in a human being. It means the knowledge that can be awakened in a human. Even so, isn't this an installation? Isn't this also like a temple system? Could you explain the difference between the old temple concept and this?

[GV1:P2441] Guru: A deity has a status and a time. The deity's time is the Treta Yuga and the Dvapara Yuga. At that time, even the sages stood subordinate to the deity. Not enslaved. They would not cross that limit. 'Sreevatsankitavakshasu' - the mark called Sreevatsam is where Sage Bhrigu kicked Mahavishnu. If you ask the reason for kicking, that is another subject. It is understood that this human being possesses so much knowledge, right!

[GV1:P2442] Disciple: Understood.

[GV1:P2443] Guru: So, shouldn't such guides be the ones guiding us? The tradition of a Hindu is like this. There is no such thing as a Hindu. This continent represents the tradition of such a guide, or prophet, or Guru. The aforementioned is proof of that. But he can only communicate up to a certain extent. Why? If you look at the facilities a hundred years ago, how would it be? How far could we travel a hundred years ago? Horses, horse-drawn carriages, and bullock carts. Then one had to walk. Another example - take the names of ancient kings. Their name was 'Vanavaramban'. For a king of that era, traveling that much was sufficient. But today? The distance traveled in an entire lifetime can now be covered in four hours.

[GV1:P2444] Whatever that may be, today we have suffered the misfortune of having to understand the state of the world through intellectuals. In which era are we living? In an era of such immense speed, we have reached a pace where ignorance is proclaimed as knowledge. Two things - one, flying very high in modern terms. We have reached a point where we can travel to another planet. At the same time, scientists are now discovering that traveling so fast between these planets is certainly not beneficial. Why?

[GV1:P2445] Disciple: The speed of creation and the speed of discoveries do not align.

[GV1:P2446] Guru: Yes. There is a sphere existing between the earth, the sun, and the moon, isn't there! That sphere is being ruptured. Because of the desire to travel in that planetary expanse, when traveling there, it goes by piercing this sphere. Through it, a ray will spread here. It should only extend up to a certain point. Or it should remain as it is; that is the concept of the sages. Do not go there. Going there will lead to destruction. Did you observe the distance of the discoveries?
