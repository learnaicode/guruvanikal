GURUVANIKAL · December 1996 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P1101 to GV2:P1110
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

December 1996

It is the great men who have praised the glories of the era. Only these great men (sages) can speak about the glories of the eras by realizing them in the dimensions of waking, dreaming, deep sleep, Turiya, and Turiyatita. The foresight of the sages is true. Only they can tell. How can you choose what you need from the untimeliness of their foresight? The sages have said that the greatness of the Kali Yuga is not the greatness of the Treta Yuga and Dvapara Yuga. Sri Krishna clearly saw the greatness of the Kali Yuga. You could not see what Sri Krishna prepared to show you after seeing this. Because your belief is brimming with foolishness.

Standing at a karma where the heavenly world can be seen, you are acting stubbornly after seeing the heaven of great fools. You should not desire such a foolish liking. Then what should you desire? A worthy karma that both you and I can like equally. You and your children should be able to take up that karma. Whatever theories come, man can only act and make others act by imbibing this karma. If acting otherwise, it is not good either. You have got an opportunity to handle a theory of this level. It is said that by fulfilling this dharma of Kali and sanctifying this Kali Yuga, each of you will become like a god. Beyond that, you will become eligible for liberation. It was your ignorance that you could not become entitled to this dharma of liberation.

How did you get this ignorance? By fabricating Puranas and epics, hiding tricks behind them, and worshiping and making others worship based on them, you not only distanced yourselves from each other in the sin of karma, but also became unable to ever get close to the virtues of humanity. This incorrectness is completely present in Hindu philosophy. We ourselves have spoiled this. Due to our wrong understanding, when we followed the theory by adding unnecessary things like untouchability, we had to speak by changing the nature of God. It is through this difference in nature attributed to God that you drifted apart. You should think that this will only serve a karma of making you kill each other.

We know that it is Guru Dharma that gives light to religious theories. People belonging to all religious theories stand included in Guru Dharma. For all these theories...

Guru refers to the Guru Dharma, which is capable of spreading light, as the soul of all dharmas. That is, if you examine this Guru, you will see that all the gods and goddesses, acharyas, and prophets who have come so far are united in this Guru. There is no other logic to it. If that is the Guru's spiritual merit, it is meant to be received by the disciple. However, not everyone will get it; it is the destined disciple who gets it. This destiny is not limited to one caste or one religion. Destiny is the time of birth. That is, creation will happen in the world according to the course of karma! The theory of destiny is the theory of the time of creation. Householders should act knowing this theory. Only then will they have good offspring. Offspring who are pure in karma and holy souls qualified to rule the country. That is how any karma gets its power. But we have not been trained in any of this. It is as if we are determined not to learn. Whatever the special feature of the Yuga Dharma is, a karma has been brought in that makes even a Chandala rule. We cannot step aside saying that a Chandala is ruling the country. This is how God punishes. Because of this rule, since it is impossible to keep people away by citing caste, a complete independence was brought about and this Chandala himself was made to rule. Is there a need to lose oneself by talking, getting acquainted, and getting used to those ignorant theories?

The dharma of Kali is exactly the same for the Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Shudra. But here the Shudra has come with eighty-four types of karma in eighty-four castes. If the Shudra is kept away like this, the Kali Dharma itself will be in danger.

Where did it go wrong? The mistake of denying Guru Dharma in the name of Vedic Dharma. Or the ignorance of behaving in an unconscious state, with dharma covered up without knowing the glory of the prophetic dharma. The masterminds of this ignorance are trying to escape by performing tantras and mantras. This is my experience. This is what I saw.

I had the experience even when I was a very small child. But I understood how to realize the experience through an experienced Guru. At first, I traveled along the path of Advaita. But that path did not help me reach the karma of realization.

How should realization be seen? Vision - vision in the waking state, dream state, and deep sleep state. Later it becomes transcendent. What is meant by transcendent? When meditation and chanting continue, we can see the light of life without our knowledge. We will see all the ways the grandfather lived in this world, all the ways the sky exists. Seeing that, he will see his Guru and himself. This will be seen without any difference between male and female. After that, the deity, or the path of the acharya that has been practiced, will be shown. This is the truth of vision. After seeing all this, one can bring oneself to the state of talking. Once the state of talking is reached, then doubts can be cleared. This is the dharma of direct perception. The core of the truth that is spoken as a word after being manifested like this is God! The one who reaches the form of sound is a Vedantin. The coming together of such a Vedantin and a prophet is the door of God. If we do not work and submit under a Guru who knows this, the sense of difference will always remain among us.

Guruvani
