GURUVANIKAL · November 1996 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P1085 to GV2:P1100
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

November 1996

Jesus said, I have much to tell you, but you will not understand it. That is exactly what I have to tell you too. If you look at each one of you, one thing becomes clear. None of you are able to take this. You may ask why carry around things that you cannot handle. You, who have understood through theory and tradition that if you see something, you can just go with it completely, will never be able to take this. Not just you, not even a child in the world can do it. You must be able to insist that even if you lose your wealth, intellect, and education - whatever you lose, you will save your life. You, who are under the illusion that life can be saved with money, ability, wealth, and all their decorations, are not attaining salvation, but rather the complete form of failure. What would be the mental fortress of yours that is overflowing with these special decorations? Then how and with what will you seek a solution for this? You have a head or a brain, you have thought, you have everything. Then why can't you think it out? Having learned, spoken, babbled, and traditionally practiced everything in a contradictory manner one by one, what can you hold onto to move forward?

There is faith. But you do not know how to believe. This is where the problem lies. All that you have is not for trading in front of God. You may earn for one generation, for your father, mother, and children. You should not earn beyond that. If you earn like that, your children will become the escorts of the aforementioned education, wealth, and intellect. Thus they will perish. This is the logic of the Westerner or the capitalist. When you placed this logic inside your faith, you did not see divine justice.

You must understand one thing. Nature has a far-reaching knowledge. From this knowledge that stands near and far, you can understand everything. It is not that easy to take up this subject. But it can be taken, how? We are applying here that knowledge which says your faith will save you. What exactly is that word faith? When that faith is believed in such a way, it becomes useful for the home and the land.

We must look after our home, look after our land, look after our children, look after everything. When looking after and nurturing like this, husband and wife will not get along, father and son will not get along, mother and daughter will not get along. What can anyone do about the karma that comes without anything getting along like that? Is this contradictory quality suitable for the land? Is it suitable for the home?

You must note what the great souls who came and worked on this earth had to say in the end before they left. What was the last word Jesus said -

Father, have you forsaken me? At the same time, what are we being taught? That the sins of the world are completely washed away by the blood of Jesus. What answer does the world have for this?

The Prophet said that this world is ruled by Jinn. Sitting in the very same graveyard where he saw this, that great man attained Samadhi. For the last three months, he did not get up from where he sat. Can any religion or ideology provide an answer to all this?

So, how will you take all this? Therefore, what should we do? We should only take the path given by God. Do not take any other path. If you take another path, it will be harmful.

Sri Krishna is the great soul born at the beginning of this Kali Yuga. Even before Krishna's birth, that family and the government had received knowledge. The ruler had received knowledge. He was being instructed on how to rule. When he did not obey, he was told, "I am sending someone to your house," speaking to Kamsa. Then what did the priests advise? If so, none of the children born should live. Thus, seven of them were killed. How were these children, who came by God's will, destroyed? Who has the authority to destroy these children who came by God's will? But then, how will we believe this truth that is being told? - You have the right to ask anything like that.

But this is not what I am saying. We can believe or not believe. That is not anyone's property. But it belongs to everyone. We are the owners of karma, taking up the leadership of the house as father, mother, children, husband, and wife. This is completed by both man and woman. Children are the completion of karma for man and woman. To complete that, you should not go after karma that wanders aimlessly somewhere. How do you think you will achieve it by completing it through business and various other ways, learning and teaching deceit? Therefore, that is not how it happens.

Who are our ancestors, our prophets, who are our gurus, what are the different types of conditions, organizations - all these must be clearly understood by us, or rather, in an individual character. It is not possible for us to understand each of them in an individual character like that. All these are indeed great. Everything should be respected. But one thing. Belief will keep coming. But where should belief begin? Where these mentioned ancestors, prophets, and acharyas believed and ended up in a mess - that is where belief should begin.

Sri Krishna did not worship anyone. He worshipped only one thing. Krishna is the owner of such a personality. Moses, Jesus, and the Prophet, among such prophets, twenty-five have come. Hindus are those who believe that God exists in every pillar and speck of rust. Standing in that course of karma, what right do they have to speak of Advaita? How can one agree with a philosophical prophecy that says it exists in every pillar and speck of rust, and in the same breath asserts that none of this exists? No one can agree with that. There is no point in going and talking about this philosophical logic and the meaning of vowels and consonants. It should not be said like that. That is why it is said, "Anubhavamadhilonnilikkille/tanamathilliyu moonnamakshiyale". Can you tell the name of a thing or a person just by seeing it with your eyes, without asking? What if one thing has four names? A person with a name in Malayalam gets another name when he becomes a person in Tamil Nadu. If four names from four places are combined like that

How can one recognize a person? These are all various contradictory qualities of the world. You cannot reclaim these paths.

Again - what is faith? To what extent have great men spoken about faith? To what extent did Sri Shankara speak? Many such great souls came. But no one was able to illuminate our path till the end. After the last five thousand years, no one has come until today. If Advaita is preached and established, will we all become Advaitins? If asked to complete the dualities, can you?

Even if counted one by one, what is that One which remains countless? What do you mean by that? Can you ever take up the karma that stands exhausted after counting one by one again? Yes, when it reaches its defeat. The great sages who praise what they see day by day. You must stand there. After becoming a great sage, what is the next thought? So, across what periods of time have these great sages been born? They have been born across eons. Those born in such a way have spoken about the glories of the age. You have not been able to learn this. Because there has been no opportunity for it. If you are to grow in that field, you must have ancestors who grew up and attained liberation in that field.

Guruvani
