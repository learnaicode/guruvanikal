[GV1:P1588] April 1986

[GV1:P1589] Since the dawn of creation, among the humans born and raised in this world, some give thought to life and leave behind a record according to their knowledge. Some live doing nothing, lacking the knowledge to do so. Only when countless people pass by in such a manner does someone make such a record. Among them, some emerge artistically. Arts can be seen making the effort needed to reach completion by incorporating various means. Such arts themselves have been attributed to the name of God, shaping the knowledge about it into various art forms—thus attempting to replicate life itself. The history of numerous great souls has been replicated in this manner. In this, the majority of artists are those who employ intellect and logic. There are artists who sing, enjoy the moment, and let it pass. Some are contemplative, possessing the endurance to last for ten, three hundred, or four hundred years, having attained karma through faith in God. Over such a long period, the practice seen among people today is writing poetry by reflecting on the acceptance or rejection of this. If numerous people over such a long period come and handle things in this manner, it will reach an extent beyond our imagination regarding how many absurdities and profound truths have accumulated within it. Meanwhile, after all these individuals depart from earthly existence, having attained the results of good and bad karma, they become subtle bodies in the realms of ancestors (Pitriloka) and gods (Devaloka); thus, belonging to this solar and interstellar division, according to the results of constellations and planetary positions, each of these individual souls (Jivatmas) remains in the atmosphere indefinitely, and it must be observed how they act during the events of birth and death pertaining to their own families and other families. How can we, who blindly believe that this courageous execution of karma entering not only humans but all living beings is ordained by fate and willed by God, obtain a correction from this? From here, cursing birth itself, they vanish from the face of this earth.

[GV1:P1590] Guruvani
