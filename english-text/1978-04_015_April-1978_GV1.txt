[GV1:P0277] April 1978

[GV1:P0278] Question: What is your message regarding what people should know and practice in accordance with the present times?

[GV1:P0279] Answer: In what sense have you mentioned the present times?

[GV1:P0280] Question: It is in the sense of what is said regarding the four yugas.

[GV1:P0281] Answer: When talking about yugas, you know that it is Kali yuga now, don't you? Most Hindus today are accepting and following things without knowing the nature of this Kali yuga. Be it religious practices or other social activities.

[GV1:P0282] Question: Why do you say that there is a change among Hindus? It would be good if you could advise on how it should be taken forward then.

[GV1:P0283] Answer: Merely informing and causing to be informed will not fulfill the purpose. The matter lies in putting it into practice and realizing it. Eradicate the distortions seen in people today, and in its place, help them realize the essential moral lessons.

[GV1:P0284] Question: What are the paths you have to suggest towards that?

[GV1:P0285] Answer: Why did you only ask about Hindus? Are people of other religions not human?

[GV1:P0286] Question: I didn't ask intentionally. I know that all people will receive encouragement through you. That is why I didn't ask.

[GV1:P0287] Answer: Just like in Hindus, certain flaws have crept into some other religions as well. Although the nature of all religions is good in principle, it is understood that the current decentralization of rituals is not good.

[GV1:P0288] One. The main change that needs to happen among Hindus is to have a definite unified nature in the methods of worship. For that, India's own dress code and language should be incorporated into the code of conduct.

[GV1:P0289] Two. The attention of the Government deserves the utmost importance in this. Because, if anyone tries to implement a unified nature, monotheism, etc., the Government must examine it and understand whether it is beneficial to the country and the people or not, and if it is beneficial, provide the necessary help and cooperation.

[GV1:P0290] Three. Today, some changes have unknowingly occurred in the existing writers, poets, artists, puranas, and vedas in certain special natures and circumstances. To change this, all kinds of art lovers today, in accordance with the Upanishadic verses - certain special people by God's will are born and raised in this world as a foundation for the times to come. We are unable to see, know, and inform about its prosperity due to the lack of the aforementioned help and cooperation. If help and cooperation take root at least in those who accept this, even if it is a minority seen in India today, evidence can be collected. Raja Ram Mohan Roy who founded Brahmo Samaj, Dayananda Saraswati the founder of Arya religion, even without knowing who the founder of Sikh religion is - although it is worldly, Sikh religion cannot be called spiritual because something else is needed in spirituality. After that, Christianity and Islam exist in India today. We can carefully observe some small changes in all these. Even if it is worldly, because the organizers of the aforementioned Arya religion, Sikh religion, and Brahmo Samaj are able to maintain their zeal, animosity, and discipline at least at a small level (especially those belonging to the Sikh religion), whichever government it is, they are seen to allow some of their own disciplines. The turban, dagger, beard, not having to wear a cap even in the army - didn't all this come from their sense of duty? But didn't you see some existence, at least financially, in Christianity and Islam, which have a little government inclination? Some flaws have started coming in that too. I'm not saying no.

[GV1:P0291] Question: Could you please indicate what that is?

[GV1:P0292] Answer: Because there is money to bring about some changes like new and old, it has turned into mere rituals and lost sincerity. Through that, religious conflicts and lack of love for God, and trying to maintain their own goals by using any violence against fellow beings. Everything is felt as decentralizing those founders or their ideologies.

[GV1:P0293] Question: I didn't understand what you used as new and old.

[GV1:P0294] Answer: A justification of new and old has crept into the people who have been believing in the Quran and the Bible. Buddhism is right - however, it needs to be examined whether the religion of God is right.

[GV1:P0295] Question: What do you mean by religion of God?

[GV1:P0296] Answer: The birth of Christ and the birth of the Prophet - their births are not from a family carefully built on sciences and evolved from it. That birth itself - is to change. The cultural work intended by this new and old. Their words are not taken by looking at where they were born or originated. Their own growth, sacrifice, eagerness to spread love - the full form of that sacrifice may not be possible in Buddhism. Then the result from God will decrease. That can be seen if you look at those religions today - followed by everything. If this is the case for these recent religions, how much change must have come to the very old Hinduism. To change into this, look at the great men born within Hindus themselves.

[GV1:P0297] Guruvani
