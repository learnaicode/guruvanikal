Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0495 to GV2:P0511
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

May 1993

Love for one must be pure. Cunning love and love pretending to be sincere are wrong. Because you are experienced in the spiritual side. Experience must be respected. If those with vision develop insincere and impure behavior, it will spread widely.

It has been said many times that ours is a new method. This age is Kali. Kaliyuga has its own nature; just like Satyayuga, Tretayuga, and Dvaparayuga. The change of ages is God's will. There are references to it in Manusmriti. Hinduism has a very long history. There is a calculation. Compared to it, the history and calculation of Christianity is very small. Hinduism is a culture rather than a religion. We belong to a lineage that worked desiring the presence of God in Satyayuga.

At the end of Satyayuga, some Aryans (Brahmins) migrated and settled on the river banks (four). They studied our history and culture. They understood in their minds that we are without deceit, honest, seeing God directly, and good people. The people here had no hesitation in sleeping anywhere. Moreover, they were of the thought that whatever is God's will, that alone is enough for them.

Time changed. With the change of time, God was no longer seen directly. In distress, they went to the forest and did penance in many ways. Gradually, they began to see light and form. What they saw in penance was drawn on a tree with a green leaf. When they thought about it in their minds, they got words from the light. They got a mantra. Great men started discovering such things one by one. What was discovered was carved on stones. They were given form and color. Westerners call this the mind. How did this mind come? That is consciousness. Consciousness, light. We must learn to say that is the truth.

It was during this transition period that the Aryans migrated and settled. They started practicing one by one. The practice grew in many ways and reached up to the rule of the country. They ruled intelligently. They made others kings. They made people believe that the king was God. Thus, the king ruled Tretayuga and Dvaparayuga. In Dvapara, they started manifesting. It came to be that all this is true and if needed, God can show all this.

Studying this history, the Aryans created caste. He knows that Kali belongs to the Shudra. That is, the right to rule. Intelligently, even a price was put on letters. The Vaishya was beaten by the Shudra. Time changed this. Later, the Vaishya beat the Shudra for about three generations. The Shudra was made to do slave labor. Doing slave labor, the Shudra became a Shudra. By making them do slave labor for ten generations, they were made lower castes. They worked hard without food and in distress and got tired. When doing slave labor, there was no arrogance. After that, arrogance increased. With arrogance, the whole great sin fell on his head. Because of ego, God hated him. It was said that there would be no king at the end of Kali. The whole of Kali was to be ruled by the king. Thus, the one who chased and the one who ran fell into the pit. Paternity was also lost. When the father died, there was no way even to offer bali. Paternity was destroyed, ancestors wandered, and when children were born with changed signs, the clan itself was destroyed. Meanwhile, great souls came and said that you should become one. But they were tortured, chased away, and killed in many ways. Finally, Muslims were brought and made to rule.

In Bengal, a Brahmin eats fish. Not in Kerala. In Kerala, the Brahmin came only for Vedic rituals. Because of the Vedic's lies, the Shudra became someone who knew nothing. Now, for some time, there has been a light again. Although the British came as a precursor to it, they are not decent people. A good-for-nothing there came here and became the Viceroy. The British came here and created dress, language, education, administration, and law. Before that, although there was no law here, one could live thinking of God.

Today, the era of hybridization has arrived. There is hybridization in all living beings. Do not take this lightly. You must heed the word of God. God is favorable to us today. Jesus and the Prophet have said, 'Messengers of God will come and lead you astray'. I had doubts about those words. Even when the Avadhuta came and told me, I was convinced only when I received the revelation.

This is not meant for just one caste, religion, or society. If it didn't work when Thiruvalluvar and Naranath Bhranthan did it, wouldn't it work if we do it today? One should think that if my lineage will improve, I might as well die for it. We should try to accommodate everyone who comes to the institution by any means. If they are good for nothing, keeping them here and making them sacrifice will bring good. This is the time of this lineage. Therefore, even if you are cut into pieces, you must stand firm in God. You do not have to experience the sacrifice of Jesus being crucified or the persecutions suffered by the disciples. Because God has given you the freedom and social status to resist and glorify the Guru. Although Shankaracharya offered his head, the tradition of opposing the Kapalika is before you as a lesson.

But you have shortcomings. None of you came with the desire to go and stay in an ashram for at least one day. There is no inclination for that. The whole land lacks that familiarity. Nor have you learned to obey at least one person at home. Ashram life in this manner will bring danger. If karma goes wrong, one will have to take another birth just to suffer it. Even after suffering for this throughout one's life, the pain has only increased; nevertheless, there is no limit to endurance. Those who cannot do it should tell themselves that they cannot and withdraw.

Once you decide to stand firm in a belief, you must obey what the Guru says. Ashram life is about untying every knot of past lives. When you wander, seeing and hearing things, a habit of thinking will develop. One must respect the sense of duty. Householders and visitors must respect those staying in the ashram. For householders to be able to respect and love like that, those staying here must have good discipline. If one experiences and understands hardships from the movement, what can that personality not become! Even if you come to the ashram, ask for some service, and do it, you should consider it a fortune.

If a human birth is obtained from a bull, the nature of the bull will be mixed in it. If a human birth is obtained from a sloth bear, deceit and treachery will be more. If it is from a monkey, fickleness will be more. The softest are the deer and the rabbit. Most women have a heart like a rabbit. If you look closely, you can see about all births.

My effort is to destroy the past inclinations of human birth and nurture the present inclinations. I will only ask if you can just do this today. Because the sannyasi knows that due to the inclinations of past lives, one cannot obey directly. Only the lowest and the highest will be made to obey. The lowest will be beaten to death. The highest will be told to stop.

Only if you think about what the course of karma in me is and who I am, can you go along with this. Since all inclinations are intertwined, it is difficult to dismantle and rebuild karmic capacity. You can only be saved if you obey and keep your word.

Some women smile with jealousy. No one will understand that. She herself can also act in reverse. If a woman lies, we cannot prove it. A Brahmin woman does not have the knowledge of a Nair woman. The Brahmin who says that a woman should not be included in anything, [includes] her in Vedic

Why was it taught? Giving women freedom for worshiping God and for education is very good. In Hinduism, freedom is given to women who progress by worshiping God well. Rising above all this, they are now reaching towards wisdom. Position must indeed be given to environment and qualification. That is the special rule of the time.

Guruvani
