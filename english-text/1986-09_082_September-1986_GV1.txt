[GV1:P1606] September 1986

[GV1:P1607] Question: I often visit many ashrams. Similarly, I came here too. I have heard many people saying various things. I have a doubt.

[GV1:P1608] Guru: Listen to what I am going to say. Then the doubt will be cleared.

[GV1:P1609] Question: I have worked in many ashrams and institutions. What I see here is a method different from those. That itself is my doubt.

[GV1:P1610] Guru: That is not your doubt. It is indeed the truth.

[GV1:P1611] Question: That is what I need to know.

[GV1:P1612] Guru: That knowledge cannot be obtained just by being told; it must be known through belief over a period of time. The Trimurti system and the Shaiva, Shaktheya, and Vaishnava methods of worship seen today have not only misled the people but also impoverished and alienated Bharath (India) itself. Even today, we accept the rule of others and call it our own.

[GV1:P1613] Question: Why did you say it belongs to others?

[GV1:P1614] Guru: The English language is not ours. The attire is not ours. In short, everything is an imitation. We have lost everything of our own. Exhausted as we are, we have not yet been able to see this as the truth. As a result of arguments between early priests and kings, the Puranas were rewritten by a few immigrant scholars known as Brahmins, altering the knowledge that was in the hands of the kings. When further arguments arose through that, the Upanishads were created. They contain little reality. A few poems written by scholars and the realities written by the wise are mixed together in them. Initially, there were only the Dasha Upanishads (ten Upanishads). That became eighteen, then one hundred and eight. Now they are countless. The same goes for the Gita. Initially, there were only three hundred shlokas (verses). The rest were added over time according to the circumstances.

[GV1:P1615] Question: How did you get this evidence?

[GV1:P1616] Guru: Haven't you understood that this evidence is a vision (darshan)?

[GV1:P1617] Question: You yourself say that visions can contain falsehoods.

[GV1:P1618] Guru: I told you, I got this when I tried to correct that.

[GV1:P1619] Question: How do you handle this knowledge?

[GV1:P1620] Guru: There is a command to carry this knowledge forward like a Guru-disciple relationship. That is the path being followed now.

[GV1:P1621] Question: Another doubt. It is about this Akhandanama (continuous chanting) which is chanted using your own name.

[GV1:P1622] Guru: It is not an Akhandanama. It is a mantra. If needed, it can also be a stotra (hymn). In that sense, it can be called an Akhandanama. While receiving visions, two disciples received these lines as an ethereal voice (ashariri). 'You must strengthen this by chanting it. You will need it someday'—they came and told me that they received it this way. I did not let them do it then. The reason being, my name was in it. But those who received it used to chant it without my knowledge. The reason for that was they had received an ethereal voice saying, 'If you do not strengthen this by chanting, danger will befall you.'

[GV1:P1623] Question: There is no way they could chant it without Swami knowing, right?

[GV1:P1624] Guru: They would chant it loudly when I was not around. When I was present, they would do it mentally. Because of that, I was spared.

[GV1:P1625] Question: Could you explain a bit about that?

[GV1:P1626] Guru: It should not be explained. I can say a few things. I mentioned earlier that our attire and language are not ours. We had to adopt them because there was a lack of knowledge and action in our country. A person without knowledge cannot expand any action, can they?

[GV1:P1627] Disciple: How can you say there is no knowledge—isn't our land the one with all the knowledge?

[GV1:P1628] Guru: Then why did we take what belongs to others?

[GV1:P1629] Question: In the past, we had universities like Nalanda and Takshashila, didn't we?

[GV1:P1630] Guru: I too am saying that they existed. That language disappeared from among us because our merit (punya) diminished. When our merit diminished, we had to become slaves to the infiltration of others. They had knowledge and capability. A language like Hindi or Pali existed here. I do not know if it came about as a mixture of everything. As a renewal of that, Sanskrit was introduced. Through Sanskrit, the Puranas, Upanishads, and Vedas were written. In them, they wrote 'Amaranirjjaradeva' and turned the Brahmin into a god. Consequently, the vast majority of people came to be known as Asuras. Through some of them, the Shaktheya system was developed. Through some others, Shaivism was developed. And through yet others, Vaishnavism was developed. None of this is bad. Each is better than the other. How will you find out which of these is right and which is wrong?

[GV1:P1631] Question: It cannot be found out.

[GV1:P1632] Guru: That is the exact experience I had. It led me astray to a great extent. For us who are living, learning about the past is fine, but it is not something to be practiced in this era. Knowing that, what is needed is the sense of dharma to unite and work for the present time. Assimilating the bygone Vedic dharmas and karmas, mixing them in the Puranas, giving undue importance to gods and goddesses, and thereby diluting human potential—this is the result of poets and artists working together. This led to a surrender to miracles, the occult powers derived from miracles, and all kinds of activities taking refuge in them. I am a person who has experienced and known such surrenders. Bearing witness to the direct inferences I had and have experienced through that, I tell you—I am trying to present myself before you to instill courage in you, the courageous souls who have attained a truly worthy birth. May you be convinced.

[GV1:P1633] Perceptual inferences (*Pratyakshanumanangal*) refer to our ancestral beliefs and their guiding principles. Direct perception (*Pratyaksham*) is a part of this. The disembodied voice, the stream of light—the only witness to recognizing them is the grace-filled, luminous streak of compassion. What and how to testify to it is solidified over time by the experiential record of that moment. There is a reason for saying 'over time'. Otherwise, a Guru is needed to explain it in that very moment. If lacking complete qualification, not only will the Guru be unable to do so, but the disciple will also fall into disarray. Because of this, many people wander astray and reach danger zones. There is evidence that they have suffered thus. Our nation was full of goodness and had reached great heights in all respects. All forms of nature here are favorable for the way of life. Nature and its elements intertwine, and humans experience its complete bliss. Other countries have not received such favorable nature. They have climates that are partially favorable, partially unfavorable, and even uninhabitable. Mahabali, the Asura emperor, is proof that our land was completely filled with goodness, blending devotion and humility, and possessed a governance system that imparted a good method of protecting the weak. The hypocritical policy that branded one who does not consume *Sura* (liquor) as an Asura insulted noble kings, transformed the one who consumes Sura into a Deva, overturned knowledge through Homa (fire rituals) and Tantric practices, diverted Dharma from virtue towards Adharma, manifested this course of action here, redirected all charities to Brahmins, thereby fostering Adharma and causing the loss of the people's merit. In the Kali Yuga, this Dharma is an absolutely harsh Adharma. The Vedic system, which did not even hesitate to write down '*Pratyakshavaham Dharmam*' by portraying all the virtuous souls as Asuras and Asura lineages while realizing the one who poured Sura as a Deva, fabricated the Puranas and Vedas and became the authority of the Upanishads. Consequently, the Asuras degraded into a state without literacy, not even feeling that the land and country belonged to them. Then, divine worship became trances, lamps, ululations, and joyous shouts at the edge of the forest, along with the consecration of ancestors under the shade of trees. Over the years, this tutelary deity system evolved into global worship. It includes Yantramoorthi, Tantramoorthi, Yogeeshwaran, Marutha, Kali, Kooli, and even the Brahmarakshas who protects Brahman. If needed, it becomes Dakshinamoorthi. Once it becomes Dakshinamoorthi, it is a wait to consecrate Uttaramoorthi. The boons received by Bhasmasura are a deception created by writing such stories. Just like the verse in the Gita, '*Pratyakshavaham Dharmam*', I too could see everything directly. Not only did it become direct, but I also received the word, and through it, illnesses were cured. Not in a trivial way—a patient treated in Singapore for eight years was cured in forty-one days. A patient in a family where the father, daughter, and daughter-in-law were doctors was cured. Their names are not disclosed as they might feel slighted. Only holy ash (Bhasma) and holy water (Theertha) were given. Even bladder cancer of that nature has been cured. Similarly, there are many direct experiences considered as spiritual realization. It was then that terrifying forms of tutelary deities and gods appeared ceremoniously with salutations in an instant, informing through those with visions that performing certain methods would yield quicker results, and we acted accordingly. After a month of this, the influx of people increased exponentially. During that time, all this began to fall apart. Not only that, all the people who had loved until then resorted to abuse, fighting, and quarrels. It was only after about seven and a half years that a correction was received for this.

[GV1:P1634] During this period, an opportunity arose to uplift the place currently known as Guruvihar in Melkadakkavoor. Its rightful heirs—Sharada, Bhargavi, Lalitha, Bhargavi's husband Gopalan Master, and son Ramadas—all arrived at Varkala Santhigiri to resolve their family curse. They inquired about this. The descriptions were explained to them. Among them, madness and incurable diseases were persisting. When asked among that group, "A curse of Narayana Guru is seen. How did that happen?", they told the truth.

[GV1:P1635] "There were two mothers in our group. One of them had hurt Narayana Guru. One was favorable to the Swami. We four are the children belonging to one of those branches. We had an unmarried uncle. He and the Swami were close friends. In those days, the Swami was known as Nanu Ashan. The Swami used to come and go at Kuzhivila. An artificial cave was created there. It had enough space to accommodate a table, a chair, and a sofa. One day, when the Swami arrived, the sofa and chair on which the Swami used to lie had been chopped up and thrown into the nearby deep water. This was done by the mother and her accomplices who disliked the Swami. The mirror was thrown on the floor and shattered. The heartbroken uncle said to the Swami, 'It is no longer suitable for us to sit here, let's go.' Then, he took the Swami to the house of their relative, Mundayil Kunjikrishnan Chattampi in Varkala. Walking from there through the Thoduve bridge along the canal, he sat under a jackfruit tree and worked towards what is today's Sivagiri." All this was narrated by the late Gopalan Master. He was an Anchal Master; the father of Ramadas, who is currently the Junior Health Inspector at Anchuthengu. Only a small remnant of that dilapidated artificial cave had survived. He sorrowfully told me that women now use the spot to sit out of the sun and beat coconut husks, and children use it for defecation and urination.

[GV1:P1636] Then, what I felt like saying was this: "It doesn't matter, lean some long palm fronds against it, tie palm leaves to it, clean the place, and light a lamp there for six months. Then you will have some experience. After that, we can do what is necessary." Accordingly, they cleaned it and did what was required. Before the completion of six months, it was time for the Sivagiri pilgrimage. At 4:00 AM, while the milkman living there was milking the cow, light reflected on the vessel, and when he turned to look, a ball-like light was rising from the cave, from the spot where the lamp was lit. When it rose and ascended upward, dogs, cats, and cows in nearby houses created a commotion, and cows broke their ropes; this incident was experienced within a four-mile radius. This can be understood by asking the locals there.

[GV1:P1637] After this, as per my suggestion that it could be renovated, they repaired it, resumed the rituals, and began to see results, at which point the old adversaries reared their heads again. When I went there, a quarrel took place. While Gopalan Master and I were hastily boarding the train from Kadakkavoor railway station, a member of this family came, struck me, snatched away my towel, and left.

[GV1:P1638] Today, they are averse to recounting this history of Guru Vihar because I am its primary agent. Even now, madness and other incurable diseases continue to haunt that family, and these afflictions have not been completely eradicated. This serves as evidential proof that if spiritually blind souls lacking ancestral purity remain within a family merely to conceal the truth, that family can never be truly reformed. Consequently, those who sincerely desire reformation will be unable to attain it through their own efforts, and others will be ready to misinterpret the situation. The opponents concealed the truth, relayed false information to Chirayinkeezhu Ramakrishnan, and consequently had him write about Guru Vihar in a distorted manner. As I have stated earlier, it took me seven and a half years to discover the underlying cause of this opposition, and it took another seven years to rectify it. What I am addressing today through my own experience is the profound realization gained from that rectification. I have consequently discerned how this Trinity system originated, what anomalies exist within it, what befell the lineage of Manu, and why the memory of Manu has vanished from the hearts of the people. I shall not expound upon these matters at present.

[GV1:P1639] Question: It is said that Manu is the son of Brahma, is it not?

[GV1:P1640] Guru: That is exactly what I said has been corrected. It will take a considerable amount of time for you to be convinced of it. After you read some literature and become somewhat convinced, I shall explain it to you. It is a spiritual revelation, after all! Did you not observe that the very people who were granted the experience eventually harbored enmity toward me? To understand this to some extent, read the section titled '*Daivachinthanam*' authored by Narayana Guru. Subsequently, read the *Guru Gita*. It contains the spiritual instructions imparted by Lord Shiva to Goddess Parvathi. Let at least some of your doubts be resolved through that text. The remainder will naturally follow through direct experience. This Kaliyuga possesses a highly unique characteristic. We have been conditioned to believe that Kali is inherently evil. Satyayuga, Tretayuga, Dwaparayuga, and Kaliyuga—these four epochs collectively constitute a Chaturyuga. All human beings born in the fourth quarter of this Chaturyuga—Brahmins, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas, and Shudras—must experience both good and evil equally and attain liberation in their progressive journey toward the ultimate truth. This principle is not stated differently for anyone. The Brahmin, whose supremacy was established by the Brahminical religion through Vedic tenets and Tantric practices, holds no legitimate place here. The Kshatriyas, who executed royal governance utilizing their education, wealth, and enlightenment, also have no place. If the era of the Devas was culminated through magical and Tantric methodologies during the Tretayuga and Dwaparayuga, the epoch of Kali must be fulfilled through profound penance (Tapas) and supreme wisdom (Jnana). Penance, in this context, is the formidable ascetic power attained through righteous action; it is not acquired by retreating into a forest. That comprehensive action—action encompassing all dimensions such as thought, word, and deed—will systematically engender both fundamental purity and supreme sanctity. Through this transformative process, spiritual merit (Punya) will invariably manifest as an inherent trait. Undoubtedly, when one accrues spiritual merit, divine fortune will perpetually accompany it. It is proclaimed that this very fortune can empower one to claim the right to ultimate liberation (Mukti). One may designate it as 'Yoga' if so desired. This is precisely the doctrine articulated in the *Bhagavad Gita*. Act by thoroughly assimilating the profound words of the Supreme Soul Sri Krishna: "Buddhyayukto Yoga balena" (Act with the formidable power of Yoga seamlessly united with the intellect). This will subsequently pave the definitive way for liberation. This is the paramount Dharma of Kali. It is fundamentally because the Tantric dominance of the Brahmin and the sovereign administrative rule of the Kshatriya have been irrevocably dismantled that the priestly scholars deceptively propagate the notion that Kaliyuga is entirely worthless. That is not the factual truth. On this auspicious day, I am formally recording these profound words for you, seamlessly incorporating my own spiritual experience.

[GV1:P1641] I am reminding you once again to ensure the absolute culmination of the essential duties that were previously brought to your attention and which you have only partially executed. Everyone must undoubtedly remember how we desperately wandered in search of drinking water two years ago. There were even individuals who deeply pondered whether Kerala would perish entirely due to a devastating drought unprecedented in recent times. During that critical period, prayer assemblies were conducted continuously from Santhigiri across various locations, actively involving both believers and non-believers. You are thoroughly convinced that everything stated back then was the absolute truth. In the geological history of this Earth, which remains a relatively small sphere revolving within the orbital path of the solar system, India, situated prominently along the equator, inherently possesses an almost temperate climatic system. I am articulating this without forgetting the empirical fact that extreme heat and cold are occasionally experienced in certain regions here as well. However, when objectively compared to other nations, we primarily experience a highly temperate climate. This is exceptionally true concerning Kerala. We are all thoroughly cognizant of the historical condition wherein Kerala was virtually a terrestrial paradise when compared to states like Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and Madhya Pradesh. Yet today, systematically reversing that idyllic state, various specific committees and assemblies have been strategically established here to firmly entrench Tantric practices and the orthodox Brahminical religion, functioning in a destructive manner that eradicates intrinsic spiritual merit and leads the very nation toward its ruin. You are well aware that as a direct historical consequence of their past actions, immediately subsequent to the onset of Kaliyuga, our nation was tragically lost into the subjugating hands of numerous foreign invaders. The frenzied oracles of Tantric practices are once again aggressively roaring with rhythmic percussion, dance, and song, deliberately obscuring the grim historical reality that we not only lost India's vast economic wealth but were also stripped of our indigenous modes of worship, traditional attire, and native language, thereby being universally reduced to abject slavery. Alongside this chaotic scenario, several individuals superficially labeling themselves as believers and non-believers are wandering aimlessly in utter intellectual confusion. Those who ostentatiously claim to be non-believers have readily adopted the alien attire and language of foreigners, overflowing with the arrogant delusion that the absolute core of all knowledge resides exclusively within them; they have tragically transformed into sterile intellects, much like the barren arrogance of a mature yet entirely leafless tree. Nothing whatsoever emanates from their literary endeavors except a profoundly putrid stench. The piercing arrows of so-called 'modernity' have been conveniently provided to them as ideological weapons for this destructive purpose. They completely fail to realize that they and this aggressively roaring mob of pseudo-believers are not two distinct entities, but fundamentally one and the same. It is merely akin to the myriad waves of the ocean roaring and crashing individually. Every single wave violently strikes the shore driven by the forceful ego of asserting its own supremacy. The exhausted and systematically impoverished masses, who innocently believe all this spectacle to be the ultimate truth, must consciously avoid falling into these deceptive traps and losing their spiritual bearing; instead, they must meticulously investigate and comprehend the inherent Dharma of this specific era. As long as we physically exist on this earth, if consuming food and drinking water is an absolute biological necessity for us, we must concurrently cultivate profound spiritual merit in a harmonious manner that does not violate or alter the fundamental, immutable laws of nature. In this noble pursuit, all human beings are fundamentally equal. There is absolutely no distinction between the erudite scholar and the unlettered commoner. There is no discriminatory differentiation between a Kshatriya and a Vaishya.

[GV1:P1642] From the Santhigiri Ashram, you have been repeatedly and emphatically reminded of this in the following manner. In this earthly realm, the present epoch is unequivocally the Kaliyuga. The Satyayuga, Tretayuga, and Dwaparayuga each possessed their own distinct and specific Dharmas. During those respective historical periods, those designated principles were duly and systematically executed. If such was indeed the historical precedent, we must firmly and logically believe that Kaliyuga also possesses a specific, inherent Dharma. What was scripturally documented in the specific context of Devas and Devis has already been comprehensively practiced until the historical culmination of the Dwaparayuga. The Supreme Soul Sri Krishna has conclusively proven this fundamental truth, visibly demonstrating it by appropriately acting and facilitating designated actions. Critically examine the eminent Yugapurushas (epochal spiritual figures) who have emerged since that era. In the Western nations, numerous divine mandates have been successfully established through the profound ministries of Jesus, Moses, and the Prophet. Concurrently, within that same historical timeframe in our own land, from Sri Krishna to Buddha, Vardhamana Mahavira, Dayananda Saraswati, the profound preceptors belonging to the illustrious lineage of the *Parayi Petta Panthirukulam*, countless solitary Mahatmas, and within Kerala itself, spiritual luminaries like Sri Narayana Guru, Chattampi Swamikal, and Sri Shankaracharya—all these great souls have unequivocally and repeatedly exhorted us to live harmoniously and fraternally like the children of a single father; deeply contemplate upon the absurdities and contradictions we are currently perpetrating in the sacred presence of their monumental spiritual legacies.

[GV1:P1643] I am reminding you once again. If we are to successfully avoid wandering endlessly in a desperate search for essential drinking water and food, we must sincerely and exclusively engage in monotheistic prayer directed solely to the One Supreme God, rather than blindly and futilely chasing after numerous demigods and subordinate deities. It is precisely to impart this profound realization that these great souls have been prominently highlighted. We are not intellectually bankrupt people who fail to comprehend any of this. Instead of finding false, momentary solace in witnessing aimlessly wandering spectacles—such as the bizarre ritualistic aberrations in Tamil Nadu, the extreme worship practices resembling cannibalistic savagery in Rajasthan, or the demonic, self-destructive savagery of leaping into blazing fire pits in Lanka—we must consciously awaken with rational wisdom and spiritual clarity. By artificially categorizing spiritual paths into Mahayana and Hinayana, we must decisively and permanently escape from the deceptive, binding strings of the regressive Tantric practices that have once again thrust us downward into a lesser spiritual trajectory. Emphasizing once again the undeniable and absolute truth that the 'Path of the Guru' (Gurumargam) is unconditionally essential for this ultimate salvation, I solemnly dedicate these words to you.

[GV1:P1644] Your Swami,

[GV1:P1645] Guruvani
