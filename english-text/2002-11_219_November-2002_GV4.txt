[GV4:P0692] November 2002

[GV4:P0693] While I was staying at Shantigiri in Varkala, a mother came to me and was cured of her illness. Thus, they had faith in me. During their harvest season, she would bring a portion of it to me. She would also bring enough hay to feed two or three cows for a year. A girl in that house faced a severe crisis. Then, this mother and her husband came to me and pleaded with tears that I should visit their home. I went, did what was necessary, and their distress was alleviated. Because a major affliction was resolved in this manner, they all developed great affection for me. I advised them on certain matters. The mutual misunderstandings, quarrels, and lack of prosperity in their home were resolved. A situation arose where the father, mother, and siblings lived together in mutual love. With that, they began to obey whatever I said. One day, a boy from that house sent me a letter stating that from now on, they would only listen to what the Swami says.

[GV4:P0694] Three years passed. One day, an incorporeal voice (*ashariri*) spoke—saying that I should not stay in a manner visible to two people. (These things occur in a certain spiritual state. Whenever such revelations and experiences occur, major events will happen within three years.) Since the instruction was received not to stay there, the person accompanying me suggested that we could go to that family (mentioned earlier), as they had been inviting us for a long time. Then I too thought that it would serve the purpose of relocating as instructed and accepting their invitation, and so decided to go.

[GV4:P0695] Thus, we went there. Upon arriving, another person had come with a car; it was his father's death anniversary ritual (*aandubali*), and I needed to go there. Saying that I would return from there later, I went with the person who came with the car. After I left, the mother found a letter that had fallen from the girl's book in that house. She gave it to the father. That letter was written to her by a boy. It was written in the letter that I was their enemy. When I counselled them and set them on the right path, their illicit relationship was severed. Therefore, I allegedly spoke in order to make enemies of them! Naturally, this girl developed an enmity towards me. The mother too felt distressed by me. This distress had befallen a family who had visited me ten to twelve times within a span of six months. That does not matter; it is a reality that can occur in human beings.

[GV4:P0696] But that is not the main issue. In front of their house, there are small-scale industries and a market. All of this is situated on their property. The one associated with this girl went to these shops and had all the youths there well-prepared and stationed. They were standing with sticks, wooden clubs, and machetes. He stood hidden without coming near. As soon as the car arrived there, all of them rose up together. I could not understand what was happening. Then, the girl's father approached me and said softly, "Swami should not stay here now. I will explain the rest later." He then blocked them by spreading both his arms. Therefore, nothing happened to me. Otherwise, it would have been the end right then.

[GV4:P0697] The very next day, a journalist made a speech:

[GV4:P0698] "Sometimes the Swami will appear, only to aggravate the flaws of the young girls. And then he will extract as much as possible and leave." He delivered this 'journalistic speech' in many places. This is my experience.

[GV4:P0699] Another similar case is that of the woman who jumped into the well at the Pothencode ashram and died. That woman had a mental illness. Her illness was a convenience for her husband. When her illness was cured, she became an inconvenience to him. Therefore, he forcibly brought her back and left her here again. In that despair, she jumped into the well. This case too earned me a good reputation and status. Around five thousand people gathered and came here to perform 'Pushpanjali' (floral offering). By Pushpanjali, I mean an offering of abuses similar to the Cherthala Pooram songs. It even became a Crime Branch case.

[GV4:P0700] Numerous such truths that have been experienced are before me. My entire life consists of numerous experiences like this. It is these experiences that I am presenting before you. Why am I telling you this?

[GV4:P0701] Where do the demon, god, goddess, sage, ascetic, the wise, the devotee, and numerous such great souls stand? In which zodiac and in which sky do they stand? What are the qualities of that sky? What are the properties of that light? Similarly, what are the characteristics of the nine worlds, and what are the effects produced when these nine worlds revolve? The factual aspects of having experienced all these are before me. We must also understand that this has the nature of revolving through epochs. Then, we must experience those epochs themselves. Along with that, we must also reflect upon the evolution of our soul.

[GV4:P0702] The tendency (*vasana*) is what contemplates the evolution of the soul. This *vasana* is equally essential for the father, the mother, and the children. If you are convinced that this is necessary, you should not speak of arguments, doctrines, or individuality. If you waste time talking like that, the children will become sinners to such an extent that you will be unable to do anything. When they become sinners, their arrogance will inflate, and they will cease to respect anything. No weapon will have the power to cut through the core of this arrogance. What follows will be a wrestling match involving wealth, power, and education. Furthermore, there is caste, and upon that, a religion. You are hiding behind such ideologies.

[GV4:P0703] Another thing to understand is the state of your ancestors who existed in the karmic path of your past, and the state of the karmic course that has evolved over the subsequent ten thousand years. There is an experiential aspect of perceiving this here. I wish to share what I see with you. When I say this, those who are receptive will accept it, and others will not. Let them accept or discard it. That is not my concern. Even if only one person attains goodness, that is sufficient. That one person is of great significance to me. I am certain that it will eventually multiply into six, thousands, hundreds of thousands, and tens of millions. When this occurs, the karmic course that encompasses those tens of millions will enter your home. This is my objective. The primary source of this is my own experience.

[GV4:P0704] We must thoroughly comprehend the paths of the deity, goddess, sage, and ascetic, and traverse through them. Similarly, ranging from malevolent spirits to the king of jinns, or entities referred to in doctrines as Brahmarakshas and Gandharvas, one must identify their abodes and understand what must be done to intercept and alter that karmic course. I shall impart no other reasoning to you but this. I am conveying to you the logic derived from the experience of perceiving this karmic path.

[GV4:P0705] I am finding solutions to certain incurable diseases caused by this karmic course. This is the mission entrusted to me by God. No one else can perform this task. To execute this duty, permission is required. Furthermore, that permission must be granted by that supremely authorized entity itself. Otherwise, there will be no enduring resolution to this.

[GV4:P0706] It is inadequate to merely term this as the purification of ancestors or deities. Regarding these ancestors, if they are meant to evolve, they must be nurtured; if they are to be sustained, they must be maintained; or if they are destined for destruction, they must be eliminated. Having done so, whatever is necessary must be infused into your life. Likewise, whatever needs to be imparted to the deity, sage, ascetic, enlightened being, and devotee must be transmitted. Consequently, each of your homes will become a receptacle for this transmission. However, when it is infused into a thousand homes in this manner, only one or two may prove suitable for such a state. Yet, from those two, it can be transmitted to hundreds of thousands. That will require generations. When a hundred such generations have passed, the dharma of this Kali Yuga will be fulfilled.

[GV4:P0707] What is the dharma of Kali Yuga? It is the dharma of direct manifestation. It involves witnessing the manifestation of spirits, gods, goddesses, sages, and ascetics; observing their variations in form; perceiving their colors and attributes; identifying the causes behind these colors and attributes; and thereby envisioning the solar system intricately linked with the twenty-seven stars, twelve zodiac signs, and nine planets. None of these elements we discover were created by us. If the calculations of every solar system positioned in the corners of this universe—indeed, of countless thousands of such solar systems—reside with you and me, then we are inevitably bound to preserve them. If we fail to preserve this, there is no purpose in discoursing in this manner.

[GV4:P0708] Your children must be capable of preserving this. Studying Vedanta will not suffice for this, nor will the practice of sorcery achieve it. It cannot be attained merely by purifying a deity or adorning one's chosen deity with a full coating of sandalwood paste. Even the complete manifestation of Shakta, Shaiva, and Vaishnava traditions will be of no avail.

[GV4:P0709] Let us weigh the profound words spoken by the Prophet in his final days: 'Jinns rule the world.' If you believe that his personality transcends you all, you must respect the gravity of those words. Similarly, figures like Sree Narayana Guru, Swami Rama Tirtha, and Jesus—why do we bear the burden of unrighteous actions conjured by mere intellect, instead of acknowledging the powerful karmic deeds of such supreme renunciates? If it is out of your own sheer ignorance that you bear the weight of this supposed salvation and logic, at the very least, refrain from dragging your children into it. If this affliction strikes the children, it will grow within them to an extent where no one can do anything about it. Thereafter, even if one despairs, weeps helplessly, and abandons life itself in anguish, that wrongful karmic course cannot be reversed.

[GV4:P0710] This very issue resides within each of your homes. Through which doctrine are you going to convince whom? First, introduce this to your children and convince them. To achieve this, fully believe in the words of Jesus, who urged us to seek and to knock so that the door may be opened. The words he uttered in anguish, 'Father, have you too forsaken me?', are absolutely true. In the heavens he witnessed, that karmic action remains fully pervasive. However, no one comprehends that heaven or that karma. Standing within that realm, in the parlance of those times, bestow your love upon deities, spirits, or whatever you prefer. Yet, the fact that the people crucified Jesus remains an enduring truth.

[GV4:P0711] According to the divine will, we must attain a resolution. Santhigiri has been bestowed with a path to instruct our children, the generations to come, and their lineage on what must be done to fulfill this resolution. However, you are not acquainted with this path. Therefore, if explained, you will not grasp it easily. You will only understand it through the familiarity gained over a period of time. It is enough to proceed with faith and love for this. Nothing else is required. Those who rely on pure logic will only feel inclined to dismiss it. They will not be inspired to connect this with their lives. Convincing someone that a matter disconnected from life can be accomplished is futile. Nevertheless, it might be possible to bring them into faith by revealing certain aspects rooted in their past lives.

[GV4:P0712] Some individuals, during their childhood, may witness certain lights and forms. Some might consider this experience to be demonic. Others may believe it to be some extraordinary divine attribute. There are also those who dismiss it as mere ignorance. None of these assumptions are correct. Regarding this, there are specific causes and effects involved. What we need is a karmic process to elucidate these causes and effects. If one can comprehend these causes and seek a resolution, there is no need to depend on anything else.

[GV4:P0713] What we have accumulated through the merits of our past lives has grasped us as both good and evil. When it takes hold of the mind, it manifests as disease. Therefore, one can only contemplate a remedy after identifying the malevolent thoughts or errors associated with the karmic course that have entered the mind's resolutions and hesitations. It cannot be resolved by a single word or even a hundred words. This has occurred through life. It can only be transformed through life itself. For that, one must understand what each individual needs to do.

[GV4:P0714] For some, certain issues are resolved through faith. That is not my doing. It is entirely their own faith. There is no necessity here for me to instruct and make them believe. However, there are numerous cases that have been resolved through my counsel. Certain cases that I thought would never be resolved have indeed been resolved. There are ample examples demonstrating that if an opportunity is created to guide someone by saying, 'Doing this will remove this specific deficiency,' a solution can be found for any case. If asked how this happens, I would say it is your faith itself.
