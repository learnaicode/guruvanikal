[GV1:P0064] June 1977

[GV1:P0065] Dear Ones,

[GV1:P0066] We live in a highly advanced era. However, we face a multitude of problems. Overcoming them is an absolute necessity; otherwise, we will face failure in life. Thus, we must find a way to escape from all these difficulties and hardships. That is precisely what I intend to discuss now.

[GV1:P0067] There exists a large segment of the population that remains steeped in ignorance. It would be inappropriate to leave them unguided in such a state. We must be capable of instilling in them a clear understanding of this world and of life itself. I am not referring to those who display reluctance and prefer to remain secluded. Rather, I speak of those who yearn to comprehend the essence of this magnificent life. If we can provide them with a guiding light, they will be able to build a fulfilling life. This is what we must strive to achieve.

[GV1:P0068] Imparting knowledge to this group is the need of the hour. What methods can be adopted for this? Merely memorizing a few books does not seem to serve the purpose. Nor should one assume that blindly reciting our *Puranas* will achieve this goal. However, if the stories and teachings presented in these *Puranas* and other texts are categorized and analyzed with discerning intellect, much can be grasped from them. When such a mindset of rational discrimination becomes deeply rooted, we will be able to acquire the strength to think and act pragmatically concerning other problems as well.

[GV1:P0069] It is my opinion that the youth of today show reluctance towards this. They do not seem to have much affinity for our ancient culture. That is incorrect. We can evaluate today's problems only if we study the events of yesterday. Therefore, our youth must understand a little about our cultural heritage with a sense of dedication. Subsequently, they should pass on some aspects of it to the next generation. They will accept what is good and discard what needs to be discarded. Leave that to the hands of time.

[GV1:P0070] It is already evident that our *Puranas* have survived through all ages. How much strength have the *Mahabharata*, the *Ramayana*, and the *Bhagavata* bestowed upon us! We are well aware of the fact that their concepts are so profound that they can never be exhaustively interpreted. How did those great sages achieve this? In our conception, what an immense revolution these gems of thought, formulated by them while dwelling deep within the forests, have brought about for mankind! Is this all? Are there any realms they have not traversed? Are there any subjects they have not spoken of? When we reflect upon this, the immense power of that tradition astonishes us. We are driven not by the question of how they accomplished it, but by a deep yearning to understand what that power was.

[GV1:P0071] That remarkable power was attained through sacrifice. If we wish to understand what that sacrifice entailed, or what that specific power was, we must cast a glance into the past. What was the power achieved by Lord Buddha, Sankaracharya, the Prophet, and Jesus Christ? Can any political philosopher stand before those supreme powers? Can they even approach their capabilities? What profound changes have been brought about in human society by Buddha, the very embodiment of compassion, and by Sri Sankara, who provided guidance by evaluating the scriptures and the *Upanishads*? Has anyone else here been able to accomplish even a small fraction of their work? For that matter, could any prominent political figure ever surpass the path laid down by Sree Narayana Guru—who, having mastered the essence of Vedanta, guided the masses around him onto the right path through the avenue of faith—or by Sree Chattampi Swamikal, who awakened the people by exposing the Vedic practices that had lost their foundation over the course of time? Everyone knows that the answer is no.

[GV1:P0072] What guided those noble sages there was a unique power rooted in tradition. Our people were ready to perceive it and accept them. Initially, they all had to face hardships. They had to practice immense sacrifice. Yet, with unfaltering steps, those great souls advanced, bearing witness to that unearthly radiance.

[GV1:P0073] The essence, therefore, is that a great power guides us. We are moving along the path that has been illuminated for us. By treading this path rooted in tradition, we achieve only elevation. What we must possess is an inner thirst to discover it. It is for this that the youth of today must strive. Only then will your next generation step into a life of happiness and contentment.

[GV1:P0074] This should not be interpreted to mean that I reject progressive ideologies. Why cannot we aspire for progress while remaining firmly rooted in our tradition? It appears that many today harbor misconceptions—believing that our ancient *Arsha Bharata* culture consists merely of outdated systems and must be entirely replaced. That is the mindset we need to change. If we change it, we can build a novel stream of thought in our cultural sphere. You can realize this even without my stating it. It is imperative that the cultural realm achieves elevation. Only then can a large section of the people be guided onto the right path. We have already seen that such modernization has occurred across various eras. Its movements continue to evolve. It is just that when it becomes outdated, there is a need to create a sense of novelty once again.

[GV1:P0075] As previously mentioned, the problems of materialistic life constantly afflict us. How can a solution be found for this? How can we confront this calamity that stares everyone in the face? This is a thought that many might raise. To this, there is only one thing to say. Provide peace to your mind, which is the epicenter of all problems. Grant it tranquility. If you can provide that peace, the problems are resolved. No matter how severe they may be, you will realize that their intensity is gradually diminishing. Thus, the problems will be solved in due course.

[GV1:P0076] How does one provide peace to the mind? That is where the contradictions between concepts reside. How can a mind that is boiling over and ready to explode be restrained? There have been numerous instances where it has been successfully restrained. We observe that there is a power in this world that transcends our human strength. We must believe in that power. Only if we believe can we truly discover it. The radiance of that power has been visible since the dawn of humanity. Since then, humans have begun to worship that power. That worship continues today. And it will endure as long as humanity exists.

[GV1:P0077] We are moving forward through the light shown by those lives. Even though humans have progressed immensely, they are unable to distance themselves from that power—such is the magnitude of that power. That is God. We visualize that governing force in our minds. We present our problems to it. It compels us to live as rational and wise beings.

[GV1:P0078] It is true that this power has been divided into various factions. They were named religions. The essence of all these religions is fundamentally the same: to achieve peace, harmony, and fulfillment in life through them. That is what all the *Vedas* and *Puranas* urge us to do.

[GV1:P0079] As stated in the *Rig Veda*, let noble thoughts come to us from every side. Through them, lead a peaceful life. To achieve this, concentrate the mind. To attain concentration, worship that power consistently. It will instill courage in life. It will show the right path. Live according to that path. Let that be our ultimate goal.

[GV1:P0080] Guru Vani
