[GV1:P1775] It was from there that the residence at Santhigiri in Pothencode began. Nine months after coming and residing there, this became clear again. Instruction was given to act. It was done. Hatred based on caste, religion, and class has grown within them to such an extent that even God's forgiveness would be inadequate.

[GV1:P1851] The blue sky is a colorless color that transcends the seven realms known as the realms of knowledge (Jnanabhumika) or ignorance. Today's path of action has been reached through the advancement of science, which has documented the theoretical trajectories of the nine planets in a language accessible to both scholars and laymen. Science has become fragmented, reaching a stage where it can neither be fully quantified nor defined. Modern aeronautical science is in this state. Scientists must contemplate whether we truly need these discoveries, given such names, which possess the potential to destroy the entire earth like the acts of lunatics. What is achieved by measuring the size and weight of every planet? What branch of wisdom is going to emanate from that? What else can be derived from it, other than the path of action that turns a goat into a dog, as the ancients used to say? Having traveled in a manner that never attains true consciousness in life, wearing absurd disguises, abandoning the language of true scholarly eminence, and fostering a culture dressed in ambiguous forms that serves up intellect-killing actions—what can be said to these utter fools? We have nothing to do with such baseless tales that cultivate this kind of absurd humor. We also know that this is a failure of our daily lives. Do we not need at least one good vision...?

[GV1:P1852] Will a realm of action (karmabhumika) emerge if the karma to be realized through life is lost by merely singing, speaking, and writing about it excessively? Is there anything else to this beyond an intellect that obeys or forces obedience through rules? Are all these spectacles we see merely stages for the play of good and evil, where the words of God are intellectualized? Who does not know that this nurtures and corrupts the multitudes of people who do not know how to behave, realizing that all this is a consequence of the errors of the Yugadharma (the dharma of the age)?

[GV1:P1853] Who does not know that only what the courts construct by swearing in the name of God will prevail? What has the legacy of those ignorant creators made of us—these wretched human figures they have created, who behave like war-mongering beasts, trying to choose good and evil through figures like brigadiers? It is by making us believe that this is global governance that dharma is being presented to us. I ask you again, what else is there in the world other than each person bearing the burden of sins, born from the errors of the Yugadharma, according to their own capacity and strength? Understand this dynamically and reflect!

[GV1:P1854] Guruvani
