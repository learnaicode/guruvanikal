Guruvani June 1997 What mistakes have crept into this worldly philosophy, how those mistakes have manifested in action, how they have become mixed up in the paths of the avatars and their followers - it is very difficult to retrieve and correct all this. But God has given us a way to retrieve and correct all this. We also have a practical method in that regard. This is being truly experienced in our lives. We see it in the states of waking, dreaming, and deep sleep. God has also given you an experienced Guru to distinguish the truth and falsehood that may arise in this. But you are unable to take it. There are many children here who have grown up in that path. None of them have been able to take it. Nor have they been able to train someone else. Or if they have somehow grasped it, they have only destroyed it by overthinking it with their intellect. This situation is extremely regrettable.

To change this situation, you must sit together as father, mother, and children and think. You must have a determination that this subject, which I received as a gift from God, will be shared and developed together and returned as God's own. How will you give it as God's own? You understand and practice it to some extent. But you are unable to practice it with its true value. For that, this is what you must do. No matter how hard it is, unite the home. Do not try to reform the land without uniting the home. That will never work. Home, land, then country - the nature of unification should be in this order. Otherwise, we will always be running after someone - politicians, looters in any kind of administrative field, the aggressors we see and hear every day. The entire Shudra class is ending their lives running like this.

Shudra does not mean a specific caste. In a world where actions are performed without the right to Brahmajnana, whatever classes exist, those classes are called Shudras. We believe that karma is completed with drumming, singing, noise, and taking a vow on a specific day. Not only can karma not be completed with all this, but these are also thorns on that path. The worldly philosophies that humans have adopted for crores of years have gone wrong like this.

As a remedy for this, God has given us this subject and path. But the world is so fierce that it cannot be desired and taken. Our birth is in such a time. Yet we have nothing to fear. Because this time we are born in, Kali, is better than the Dwapara Yuga in which Sri Krishna was born. Since Sri Krishna was born at the end of Dwapara, which came after the period when Treta Yuga had grown, he knew how to fulfill the parts of Treta Yuga and Dwapara together. But Krishna

Despite his efforts, he could not get the lineage to do this. There is a reason for that. There is a truth that when a person is born, the part of raising that person, making them reach a certain level, and making them act is developed through their successors. Krishna also struggled a lot to bring about that truth. But he couldn't. Even after having to sacrifice his own children, he couldn't. What is the value of Sree Krishna? You are seeing what God has taught us about the value of Sree Krishna. That alone is enough to know who Sree Krishna is. The situation was such that he could not even live in his own house after he was born. Whatever the truth or falsehood of the stories circulated in connection with Krishna's life may be. Today's need is not to evaluate it. Krishna came after announcing in advance that he would come at this time. Unable to cope with the house where the children born before him were beaten to death en masse, he ran somewhere. Even after running so much, he could not survive. Forgetting all the actions done by directly confronting, clashing, and establishing alliances with all the kings, it is imagined that he goes into everything by singing hymns of praise to God. How can one imagine without action? No one knows this.

What is karma? It is the familiarity with karma, which is a mixture of good and bad, past, present, and future. It is the guru, the king, or the government that should prepare this familiarity with karma for the world. It is the contemporaries who should do this. Do we and the rulers think so deeply? Only a person who thinks and acts on an idea at this level becomes a believer. Belief does not mean my belief, your belief - it is not like that. Do not taint belief. Do not taint belief for any reason, not even to gain someone else's love. Because we must value our love for our lives and our love for our lineage.

What all sorrows will follow us when we start acting. Let it be happiness or sorrow, good or bad, whatever it is, it is ours. We have not thought of rising to the position of such knowledge. Or if anyone has done so, we have only destroyed them so far. Not only that, by destroying homes and lands so that neither men nor women can grow, the human race has reached the position of ignorance and is testing each other's strength. What will anyone get from this test of strength? Suppose there can be achievements according to each of our desires. But what will those achievements become when they come to our children? We can see that fighting, murder, and robbery are sitting right in our house without going anywhere looking for them. The misfortune arises that we can never raise a good girl, or when she becomes a mother, she cannot impart the father's knowledge and the mother's love and affection to the children, and thus give birth to offspring who bring light to the world. Because the mother says, the children should be like what the mother does. The father says, the children should be like what the father does. Thus all mothers and all fathers are fighting.

What will we do here? If we end up in an action that only fights and wins, we will not be able to do anything. To change this, see what happened behind us, and move forward without tying ourselves behind it. Father, mother, and children should try to move forward together. If each of us does not take care, we will never be able to escape. We should not take together the good and bad of our past miseries, the course of action done by governments like the imperial position, but rather think and know what the existence of good should be without evil coming into our hands. For that, it is not just necessary, but essential to have a care about what should be when becoming parents. As a basic element of that, you should know what the action done in this ashram is. By removing your deity, ancestors, and sage from the influence of your action, your action is brought forward in that place. But you do not know that. If you have ancestors who corrupt your tendencies, from that the ancestors and thereby you

It is liberating. Is this something that you, your home, your government, or the worship you have been following can do for anyone? This ashram dharma permanently changes you from the course of action that destroys you, and blesses each life by placing it in virtue, fortune, and dharma.

You know nothing about a sannyasi. Not everyone has grown up to be a wise person. But if they grow according to their own knowledge, we are unable to recognize them. This is the truth of it. If we sit around just to find fault with them, how will they correct their mistakes? Let them correct their own mistakes. This is the aspect of it. Otherwise, the mistakes of the world will not be corrected. If said in the language of Vedanta, it is not something that is going to happen in any other way.

When we lead a life, there should be a caution. Two things are required to maintain that caution. The ashram experience we have had so far is modeled on the Treta Yuga and Dwapara Yuga. Such a model will not work in Kali. Kali has its own dharma. The dharma of Kali is to act knowing the time of the Deva and the time of the Rishi. For that, one must cross the state of the Deva and attain the state of the Rishi above it. Regarding India, that achievement is never right. It cannot be said that it is because of India's fault. But it is a fault. We are unable to cross the state of the Deva and attain the state of the Rishi in the Indian way. Or if any ashram character has worked well in that way, it will be brought to some bad system. Most people have not understood this.

Then the prophetic religion. We have not practiced the prophetic religion to date. What is ours is also not ours. Because we don't have it, what have we become? An inability to act on the side of goodness has come. This inability has come as if making us all ignorant.

This must be changed. No one understands how to change this. You must have the ability to understand this and live. If you do not have that ability, even if you live with me, you will become bad. That is, you will have a misfortune where you cannot know yourself. Therefore, there must be a decision that we will live in this way. Only if there is that decision can you know each other. Otherwise, no one should have the idea that this can be brought from somewhere and fixed.

My desire is that you should have a determination that you will achieve something with a life. If you do not have that determination when living with me, then I should move from here. But because it is not suitable for a life, I cannot do that. You must make a decision that you will live in harmony with this divine will. Otherwise, you must take a precaution to live on your own. Otherwise, you will be neither in the ashram nor at home. If you feel that what I am saying is not for your need, you should do it at least thinking it is for my need. Either something good for you, or for me.

You should never lead an ashram life looking only at the good of the ashram. You must also see your own good. If you do not prioritize your own good, none of this is going to happen.

Why is it that not even one person among you has risen up as an exemplary model? I have often told you, if you wish to live associated with me, either you live as belonging to the ashram. Or stay away from it. When I say stay away, it doesn't mean there is a hindrance to coming and going. I have told you a lot, and have a lot more to tell. But why do I say it in vain? Say it and cause pain? This is my pain. You don't understand that. No matter how much is said to you, you prefer to go along with the old habits of your life.

You do not have the knowledge about the greatness of this ashram and this idea given by God. That is not your fault. In terms of an ashram system, this is an ashram system of a kind we have never seen before. An ashram system that follows the Yugadharma has never come before. Therefore, no one has been able to explain it back to anyone until today. No one even knows what Yugadharma is. Other than the fact that it is written about in the Puranas and such, no one has any other knowledge about it.

In the Chatur Yuga, the nature of Satya Yuga is of a special kind. Kali is the time that resolves the negative aspects that have been repeating for three periods. If any flaw has occurred even in Satya Yuga, Kali is the time to resolve it. Otherwise, Kali is not bad. Saying Kali is bad is meant for the Deva, not for humans. What is this flaw? Now, when looking up to the time of Sri Rama, it is seen as a long period - eighteen lakh years. Taking into account the heavenly pleasures of the Devas, worshipping them and living in that state, their merit has been exhausted. Since the merit has been exhausted, it means they have nothing left to do. That is the truth of it. If they want, they can take birth. Or if they do not want birth, they must have received the teaching conducive to liberation. Without receiving that teaching, they cannot do anything. When a Guru of that stature comes, they want to get a birth. Otherwise, do not assume that the Deva has any other ability or knowledge. It is not right for us to get any such notion. Another thing, what the Deva has is a demonic quality. However, the Deva's life is one that has stood in many ways, danced around in many ways and perished. So what that shows would be the quality of a demon. Similarly, Yakshi and Gandharva (Akasha Gandharva) and so on. What harm do they all do? Haven't we heard that if this possesses, it won't leave. So what good is such a demon going to do for us? How many such entities have been seen right here? What all nonsense have they come and stood and said during Darshan here. You all have seen and experienced all this. Therefore, you should discuss all this among yourselves and decide that we should not go that way anymore. We must tell each other and realize that this is why we are doing all this. We must live and learn in that way. If the continuation of the old is what is destined for you, then what connection do you have with this? Without knowing this, how will many people, using their knowledge and maintaining the ashram and temple practices they have seen, and speaking Vedanta, become acquainted with this? The situation is such that nothing can be clearly told to you. Because your knowledge is knowledge from the Deva, the Devi, and other such demonic forces.

If science is false

Look at the eclipse

There is a saying in Tamil. The scientific conclusion on how an eclipse occurs is not completely correct. Purana scholars, Veda scholars, and science scholars in the sense of Indian scientists have a misfortune of not being able to fully grasp anything that has been discovered scientifically to date. The reason for that is for many crores of years

is happening. Only if we can see the reason for this, can we understand what the matter is.

The calculation of Kaliyuga is stated in different ways not just by many scholars, but even by many visionaries. The reason for this is that the calculation of Manvantara was mistaken. The calculation of Manusmriti and the current astronomical calculation, which works by comparing Indian science and Western science, cannot be reconciled. As long as we do not see what mistake has happened not just in India or the Earth's orbit, but in the solar system itself, any knowledge can only be stated for a short time.

This knowledge is not our knowledge according to God's will here. We should be able to live by submitting to this knowledge. Otherwise, each of you should not be seeking one thing after another. Those who are interested in this, do this. I mentioned earlier, it cannot be explained and convinced. There is no question of whether there is authority for this action or not. God has given you the fortune to come here, obey, and make others obey. If you throw it away and show something else, it is not possible for me. You should live by deciding what you are going to do next, after each of you has understood it.
