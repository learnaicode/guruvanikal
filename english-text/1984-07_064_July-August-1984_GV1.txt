[GV1:P1416] July-August 1984

[GV1:P1417] (The purpose of sending this letter is not merely for the Indian; it is written and sent to integrate the human life that currently exists, has passed, and is yet to come across the entire world, serving as an ultimate example or life, for the continuous effort towards the foundational step. Regardless of who among you opens this, if you feel that you are not entitled to pass it on, you should not tear it up or throw it in the dustbin. You must at least keep it with yourself. Alternatively, if you feel inclined to deliver it happily, it is desired that you execute this with the conviction that you and others will achieve through individual karma. Following this, a letter will be sent every week.)

[GV1:P1418] With love,

[GV1:P1419] Your Swami

[GV1:P1420] To the attention of the loving Mother,

[GV1:P1421] To fully justify addressing you as 'Mother', one's karma must indeed be pure. This letter is written with the aspiration that by earning complete merit to become not only a model for us Indians but for the entire world, and an inexhaustible wealth for the nation, it may serve as the foundation for us, the Mother who is with us, and the world.

[GV1:P1422] According to our Hindu logic, the origin of humanity is considered to be through the lineage of Manu. That is also our knowledge. The Trinity (Trimurti) has also entered our worship in the meantime. Today, we can observe that the entire world is eagerly striving for something. If observed closely, it is evident that not only humans but all living beings are yearning for something. Everything is intended for the good. If we think with a little intelligence, we can understand without anyone telling us how Manu and his lineage, concerning India, have operated and culminated in the rightful state of renunciation (sannyasa), effectively demonstrating the sum total of such an intention. The evidence for this is that India established better universities (Nalanda, Takshashila) than anywhere else in the world. Due to their karmic proficiency, we are still accustomed to boasting about them by name. However, the purpose of this is to create an opportunity to think together about how much it is possible for us to experience this today, rather than it merely shining like the Milky Way of poets.

[GV1:P1423] The purpose of these brief words is to instill the enthusiasm to firmly reflect upon the successes and failures we confront today, and to act with foresight, bestowing a purity upon our successors, the younger generation, with love and respect. The awareness that we are functioning in accordance with the times is contained within us for this purpose. The opportunity to state here that believers, as children of the world, must know things in accordance with the times was created for a specific understanding. Today, the entire world believes this to be the Kali Yuga.

[GV1:P1424] In this Kali Yuga, most people hold the assumption that the world will be destroyed. Even if we merely consult the calculations of our epics regarding the existence of the world, or the calculations of other nations, upon realizing the reality, the calculation is that this is the seventh Manvantara since the origin of the world, and seven more Manvantaras are yet to come. Currently, the Vaivasvata Manvantara is progressing. Within it, the Kali Yuga, which is the fourth quarter of the twenty-eighth Chaturyuga, is taking place. In this Kali Yuga, human life and the objective of governance should harmonize with a steadfast detachment to act and direct actions towards the impending Satya Yuga. This should be done with the Shudras, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas, and Brahmanas working together, and all humans acting with the thought of one faith, one love, and one religion, ensuring that by the time the Satya Yuga arrives, the absolute wisdom of the sages, the absolute karmic proficiency of the deities, and the absolute prophets or the absolute eminent renunciates—with the conviction that their pure lives have perpetually materialized in karma among the people. We must also possess the firm conviction that governance can, to a certain extent, participate in shaping an ideal life.

[GV1:P1425] In this regard, the government must understand and act in accordance with the times. To comprehend this, we must know the successes and failures of the yuga dharmas that our predecessors repeatedly engaged in. If one does not act with this knowledge, there is a proverb among sannyasis: "If it comes, let it come, otherwise only a thread is lost." If one can tie a thread to a mountain and pull it close, that is a victory. Otherwise, only the tied thread and time are lost. The meaning of the phrase "if it comes, let it come" implies acting in sync with time. If it aligns with time, even this thread is unnecessary; one merely needs to push it along with the current. The reason it is advised to tie the thread of karma is that if it is merely pushed, when the flow ceases, it will sink here and there in the water. The thread is meant to keep it afloat and steady without hitting obstacles. Today, we have completely obtained the flow. All that remains for us is to swim upwards in that flow, aiming at the goal. How fortunate we are to have received the opportunity for the swim of karma to fully succeed. It is the Mother's fortune—or rather, our fortune—that an opportunity has arisen to act as the central pillar of the government, to act by honoring, observing, and understanding with absolute love and respect that great power, Ishvara, God, Brahman, Parabrahman, that truth which represents any invocation based on respective religions, which provided the opportunity for our birth and our fortune. Saluting with silent, profound nectar our fortune of getting the opportunity to act by glorifying, praising, and extolling with perfect encomiums, and offering salutations to the Mother, who is the first step in acquiring the courage to act eternally by accepting the medium of prostration with a bowed head, and to a capacity for action like that of the Mother,

[GV1:P1426] Your Swami

[GV1:P1427] To the attention of the loving Mother,

[GV1:P1428] Let us believe that the purpose and objective have been understood from what was previously stated. There is ample evidence here regarding where we experienced shortcomings and vulnerabilities in properly understanding and executing these actions. The truth is that we have not been able to examine even one of these and rectify the flaw. Many great souls have pointed out the medium through which we should act for this. Some are also seen acting upon it. Only those who acted in accordance with the times are seen advancing among them. At the forefront of this are Moses, Jesus, and the Prophet, and their lineage. Long before that, it was Krishna who proved certain matters to us with guidance. Sri Krishna Paramatman proved through his own life that he had to incarnate in that state because numerous rights and wrongs occurred not only to India but to the world due to the actions of many great souls even before that. There is absolutely no doubt that this was demonstrated for our sake, for the sake of foresight. That divine resolve desires to be implemented through various means like this. Pertaining to certain aspects that compel us towards this, those who first erred and caused errors effectively destroyed their own *karma*. *Karma* here means the body. The proof that the divine resolve had foreseen this in some way long before is the very fact that the Yadava family possessed royal status. The valor of Kamsa, who was depicted as an aggressor by history regardless of whether he was actually one or not, is illustrated. By being born as a small child in it, showcasing a long period of aggressive tendencies through the child's biography, destroying it through life or *karma*, and demonstrating through the eighteen divisions of war *karma* what appropriate and practical efficiency should exist in that place, it serves as a supreme example for us to proceed as the successors of that *karma*. Krishna's life showed what is needed and what is not needed in that position by completely annihilating the eighteen *Akshauhini* army, which included Bhishma, Shalya, and others known as gurus and elders until then. Not only did we fail to attain it, but we have also experienced the consequences of tearing apart and destroying that life. We are well aware that the consequence of not obeying it, or the consequence of not having that path proven to us, is that the Kohinoor diamond, which was the greatest adornment for a royal title at that time, adorned the imperial title of the British. Even before that, the Mughals came and took the Shiva Linga gem, which we believe to be one of the greatest in our worship, and kept it as a stepping stone in their place of worship. If it appears that we are believers in God, because our ancestors' undertaking was incorrect, one diverted worship; the other diverted the government. As a result of those who grew up in the ice through history becoming ready for a struggle capable of overturning the world itself through effort, we carry out their activities today under the name of black sahibs. Let us not forget the reality that we can learn certain disciplines only if we use their language itself.

[GV1:P1429] It has been proven so far that it has not been possible religiously, politically, or administratively to advance by taking the past systems, whether right or wrong, in this manner. There is absolutely no doubt that if we cannot change or rewrite this calamity that the world is facing today, our country will also reach the brink of a massive catastrophe. Like Krishna and the Prophet, the *Parayi Petta Panthirukulam* (the twelve clans born of a Pariah woman) here have demonstrated many things. Many things have been shown in such a way as to make the position of a deity seem like a mere farce. It does not mean that there is no deity. It is not meant to say that there is no god, goddess, or subordinate deities in the Trinity worship. For the epoch of *Kali*, the deity itself took birth as a human, displaying the functions of a deity, sage, and ascetic, and to make the intrinsic values of it useful to humanity through a lineage of gurus or prophets, the government, the Vedic system, and as religious believers trusting in it, the Vedic scholar, the pundit, and the associated skilled practitioners must unite, reflect on what the guidance for it is, find guidance suitable for these times, and learn the lacking matters from a wise one (Jnani). Today, we have at our disposal a medium that is possible through a wise one who knows the past, future, and present through realization. We can know it through such a path. Just as in the instance where Krishna is said to have revealed it to Arjuna, and like the medium spoken of by the Prophet—it is said that I received it through words, light, and form proven to us—which became experiential for us in later times; the Prophet's words back then that deserts would be seen as cities and that endless gems and various minable resources could be excavated from this soil have been fulfilled today. Similarly, as long as this remains experiential for us, the time has long passed for us to act with the conviction that our country and our government can only survive through knowledgeable people. Towards this, create an opportunity for the government, believers, devotees, and the renunciates to work together. Attempt to unify the followers of all religions. Call upon the wise ones who can perfectly explain the three dimensions of time in this, or go to them, ask and learn how to handle it, understand both the past and the present, and believe and make others believe in a path that guides towards what is to come. In this, we can only trust the words of the wise ones. There is absolutely no doubt that scholars and the government, by trusting the words of such wise ones and acting upon them, adopting it as a medium, can bring about a correction for the world. As a witness or knowledge to this, the notification that we, as a lineage, can correct it behind the word of God, conveys that word to you. That word is as noted below.

[GV1:P1430] The knowledge or celestial voice we received in 1973—the word from the light—the notification from the form—

[GV1:P1431] 'The matter I had desired for epochs has been fulfilled today.' Following that, we are convinced beyond doubt that the notification received until 1984 enables us to know and communicate what the destiny of this world is. We wish to inform you of this in a manner that you can verify and understand using science—the modern science seen today—or through experiments and observations. To utilize your capabilities, ours, and the knowledge of scholars together in a way beneficial to the world, for rulers comparable to a mother, philanthropists, and wise individuals to work unitedly to avert the impending dangerous juncture in the world, and for the children of the world to find and live with peace and harmony behind it, allow us to convey these words.

[GV1:P1432] Today, there are two groups of people in the world: believers and non-believers. Regardless of which group one belongs to, we are all convinced that, apart from certain routines of life, human life is essentially the same for everyone. Furthermore, apart from the fact that life is led according to the nature of each country and human intellect, no other difference is seen. In this regard, concerning India, most people in the world tend to believe that it is a symbol of peace. The reason for this is the diligence and efficiency of our past ancestors. This has mostly come through the lineage of preceptors (Acharyas) and intellectuals. Although some excellent words are highlighted in it, it can only be said that a traditional mistake has occurred, which we have not been able to practice and maintain. But if asked, we can explain it. We possess that knowledge. The government must be completely attentive to imparting such knowledge into human life, especially into the lives of young children, both boys and girls. The government should prepare a scheme that can be implemented by some public servants without any religious discrimination, and execute it in every home to make parents aware of combining education and culture to make life value-based. This will make the motherhood and fatherhood of the children growing up in it valuable. They will never have the tendency seen today of destroying universities. The heinous act of quarreling by citing a long list of sects in the name of religion or caste will not be seen. The true path to achieving this would be to work behind the ideas of the wise, guided by their blessings and instructions, which will act as a support and shade for the good intentions formed by the unity of political and religious followers. Merely citing past myths and epics will not be enough for this. Only a wise person (Jnani) who knows the present and the future can handle this. The government must have the resolve to believe this and make people believe it. For this, we have now achieved an excellent time for the government to bring together all good and efficient people to work. Looking forward to drawing the attention of mother-like administrators and efficient workers towards this,

[GV1:P1433] Your Swami

[GV1:P1434] Guruvani

[GV1:P1435] September 1984

[GV1:P1436] Today, in this Kali Yuga, the one called a Brahmin has become a Chandala, and the one called a Shudra has become a Brahmin. However, even today, the Shudra has no position in Tamil Nadu. Yet, the descendants of those who were Shudras here possess great abilities. But despite attaining that ability, since they did not embrace Brahmanhood, that lineage has suffered degradation today. If you examine each instance like this, you will see that they are not taking it up.

[GV1:P1437] It was the one at the lowest rung who was given it first. From the 'Parayi Petta Panthirukulam' (the twelve tribes born to a Pariah woman) to the eighteen Siddhars, they were brought and tested. Most of these eighteen Siddhars belonged to the Shudra class. That is how God will reveal it to you. In Western countries too, it was brought through a Shudra. Through Jesus—who came from perfect fishermen.

[GV1:P1438] Similarly, among the Arabian people, the birth of the Prophet was as the son of a carpenter belonging to the lowest stratum—one who made and sold idols. You can ponder why God maintained such a contradictory attitude. It is proof that this is the era of the Shudra. Not only here, but everywhere God has brought it this way. You do not understand that. You are again trying to search upwards. You are not reflecting on the fact that it has come down from above.

[GV1:P1439] In recent times, Sree Narayana Guru and Chattampi Swami were born in two communities here. You did not imbibe anything they preached. Each of you has deviated from the path of becoming a Brahmin and is in a rush to show off your strength through wealth, education, and power. None of this aligns with God's desire. Therefore, it is despised by God.

[GV1:P1440] Today in Kerala, the Nair community is considered to be one of the most logical and administratively skilled. Observe if anything else remains in them other than this initial bravado. You need to think very deeply about this. If anything happens, they will exaggerate it and show it as something huge. They will do whatever it takes for that. But they will not do anything with true inner understanding. If you look at their temple traditions, they will do everything externally—building it, performing poojas, coming to pray, and showing rituals. After showing all this, when it comes to the matter of the inner pooja, everyone steps back. They do not take up the Vedic practices either. They are also unable to personally call upon God to eradicate the afflictions in their lineage.

[GV1:P1441] But in today's times, do not think that God has brought all this to test and know us. It is not testing that is being done. Five thousand years have passed since the Kali Yuga began for us now. Although it seems like a long duration, it is only a short period. Dvapara Yuga is double the duration of Kali Yuga. Treta Yuga is double that of Dvapara Yuga. But Satya Yuga is as long as all these three combined. Thus, after the passage of such a long period, God caused the birth of individuals to bring about a transformation here. Even then, improvements were made through individuals. It was them we called prophets or gurus. Apart from through them, we have achieved nothing here to this day. The mediums that have come through their path are temples, churches, and ashrams. The one called a prophet is, in truth, a guru.

[GV1:P1442] As I have said before, in ancient times, when there was no knowledge regarding art, if some scribble was drawn, it was considered art. As it evolved, people learned to draw the human figure with complete beauty. Adding colors and decorations to it, they began carving it on stone and earth. Later, they also started worshiping it, calling it a deity. When a little more beauty was added, it felt like God. After that, the figures of men and women were decorated and sculpted. I am talking about the early humans of the Stone Age. In those days, no one in the forest used to wear clothes. They did not know about clothing. That is why when the pictures of people of that time (men and women) were carved, they were nude figures. As time passed, they started covering just a few parts. This is because the changes brought into their art reflected how they gradually started wearing clothes according to their civilization. You can see all this if you look.

[GV1:P1443] In recent times, all the gods in Tamil Nadu and Karnataka got mustaches. Similarly, if you look at the depictions of women referred to as Menaka, Rambha, and Tilottama, these nylon sarees flow over them. They are painted in such a way that it seems they wore all this back then. In this manner, all forms of knowledge that God has given us to this day have been through humans.
