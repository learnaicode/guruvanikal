Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0641 to GV2:P0657
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

April 1994

The result of the Pournami prayer is supremely excellent and cannot be replaced by anything else. Knowing the purity of intention across many births, it is on Pournami that one can act upon and fulfill it. The Pournami vow yields the benefits of all other vows combined. By observing all these vow benefits, one can become a Guru. It is also on Pournami that the Guru attains liberation. This is spiritual. For worldly matters too, Pournami is something that helps attain perfection in the same way. The specialty of Pournami is that darkness does not spread even a bit. On one side, there is joy and coolness. On the other side, the quality of being able to capture anything. Seeds acquire more quality in the coolness. The quality is capable of generating and sustaining it.

If one attains Samadhi after accumulating the essence of merit through countless births, that Samadhi has perfection. The atmosphere is calm on Pournami. There is no distance between sunrise and sunset. When one rises, the other sets. When one sets, the other rises. Wherever the rays of the sun and the moon fall, all the above-mentioned qualities enter. The Pournami vow helps the Guru to reach perfection as God and Brahman. Whether one thinks about the benefits of the Pournami prayer or not, one should imagine that all our sins are destroyed, good and evil are separated, and our prayer will yield benefits in a way that contributes to world peace, so that all moving and non-moving beings receive the benefit. The planets will achieve balance, and through that, a special power that contributes to the welfare of the world will emerge. Pournami is the most suitable day for creation and dissolution. The creation, moreover, will be beneficial to the whole world. Just as this state is envisioned in a noble person, it acts in humans as well. Although the spiritual and the material are mutually complementary, whatever we give priority to will grow. A mother who gives birth to a child on Pournami must be conscious. She must know that this is for the world and that it has been ordained by God in this way.

We have proceeded by respecting what already exists. When coming through the path of the ancestors, some obstacles arose. After that, a deviation was made. One might ask how this was obtained despite respecting what exists. It is difficult to answer that. We do not think about how those who came before were. But we adopted such a path. How this happened is a subject to be thought about. Sivananda called Hatha Yoga as Raja Yoga. But Raja Yoga is none of this. True Raja Yoga is falling into it unknowingly due to the good deeds of previous births. For example, Krishna. He did not reject anything. He solidified what existed until then and showed that he is none of this. Krishna acted with the desire that actions suitable for the times should come. What has been done so far has value. But he is one who stands stepping on it. Krishna is a perfect sage. It was as a result of reading the Gita that Karl Marx said to behead. But the essence of the soul was lost. The intellect of an ordinary person should not interfere in the things done by visionaries.

I have learned one thing from the great souls who have come and gone. Most of them have rejected the past paths. If we want to be able to see what the past is, we must come through the past itself. As far as we are concerned, we do not need the past. Because we got a new path. We got this through an incorporeal voice. It will only tell us to do this at that time. No one has been able to know everything from the beginning. It is this breath that redirects us at times. It redirects us through notifications. Mistakes are corrected in Kali Yuga. Then it will let us into Satya Yuga. What order can we give to this correction? It could only be done on a small scale. More is to be done from now on. I have said a little bit. Even so, I have been seen as a denier. A character for this has arisen from that imagination itself. From the words that the thing I had desired for ages has been fulfilled today, it will be understood that this is an imagination from earlier.

Now the question is how to take this forward. That is, the system of worship. Those who created each system of worship did it after asking and knowing how it should be. We also do the same. We have another special feature. This was given to us without us asking for it. I cannot do it myself. Those who ask for this, those who do it, and those who make others do it have a responsibility. That is why it is said that householders cannot do this. But householders who live like sages can. That householder must be a person who is determined to take it to a certain stage and then change.

How and who should implement this idea? I am not the one to implement this. Even if one person asks, many people know, and some people think about it, it can only be implemented according to the working capacity of the person who is going to implement it. Is it because they did not believe in Narayana Guru that the lineage became like this? Then how should the belief be?

This cannot be implemented by mere belief alone. What should be included in what is called belief? There is a need for an awareness about belief. We utter a word because it arises in the mind. Just saying it is not enough. It must be put into practice. Belief will end up in the eight egoisms. The feeling of 'I' will always stand out. Only the one who turns these eight egoisms into eight prosperities can put this into practice. We must imbibe the love and affection of one who is pure like this. You can work by standing together with his working capacity. Alami yaluma sadarthamallaham sa- chchidamythamennu thelinju dheeranayi sadasadithi prathipathiyattasantho- mithimyduvayi myduvayamarnnidenam Narayana Guru has said. Only one who acquires these eight prosperities, turns around, thinks deeply about what is needed, asks, and acts can do this. Anyway, the course of action for this must be pure.

What if we think we can't do it? The mind should not be tired. Predecessors have achieved many things like this. So humans can do this. For that, the mind is needed. There is a story for this. Two people are working for liberation. One person does all the actions he gets with a firm mind. He laughs and plays. But he does not think about the fruits of his actions. One person is a severe ascetic. He has no thought about anything else. When asked a monk about their eligibility for liberation, the ascetic still has one more birth. Knowing this, he beat his head and cried. When the other person came to know that he has as many births as there are leaves on a tamarind tree, no matter how many births pass

"I too have liberation," he said, clapping his hands in joy and laughing. It is said that he attained liberation right then. That is the firmness of the mind.

You have not yet attained the courage, righteousness, and compassion to achieve this. Those who have the vision will not enslave their own merit to another. They will not do anything devoid of humility. They will not let firmness bring about arrogance. No matter who they see, their mind will not wander towards them. No matter who comes, they will not be subservient to them. They will maintain their merit in all fields. Even if one acquires the eight siddhis, one must be able to maintain honesty. For that, determination, dispassion, richness of humility, courage, and happiness are all needed. One should not feel hesitant or inferior in front of anyone. One must have the firm conviction that "I will achieve this." Disregarding honor and dishonor, one must stand firm as a seeker of truth.

For those with vision, their qualification is nothing else; they will only have the position of a watchman of a public property. An owner-like caretaker! Only if we have the firm conviction of why we have come here, can we do all this. Whatever you do, there must be a resolution to it. Pray for a few days that the discipline, order, and method for this should be instructed from the Divine itself. If mere trickery has this much discipline, then surely it will be instructed from this which has come as ordained by destiny. Once received, at least ten people should attain the completion of action with strict observance. Everyone should implement this with perfection.

Guruvani
