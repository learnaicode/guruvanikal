GURUVANIKAL · October 1991 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0140 to GV2:P0153
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

October 1991

A few good people have come to me from the institutions of Narayana Guru and the institutions under the Ramakrishna Mission. They have told me about their lives. They have tried to ask and understand the causes of the ups and downs in life. Among them was a man named Gopalan Mash. Gopalan Mash and a few others used to come regularly when I was living in a hut in Varkala. When they came there, they all had many different kinds of diseases. Gopalan Mash used to say that it always felt like some kind of evil was hanging over him. He was also looking for a solution to it. I said that if he did as I said, a solution could be found. He agreed.

When Sree Narayana Guru sat there and asked if there was anything left in that area, they said that it was stretching like a cave. The rest was leveled and lost. I told them to tie a good arecanut leaf base at the bottom, tie palm leaves on top of it, and place a lamp inside. They did so. When six months were completed, an incident occurred. When the milkman started milking after cleaning the vessel, he saw a light shining in the vessel. When he turned around, he saw a ball of light like a ball coming out of the cave. That ball of light then went up. They themselves said that when it went up, the dogs, goats, and cats in the area of a four-mile radius started crying and making a noise.

Later I went there and looked at this. What was tied with arecanut leaf base and palm leaves was made like a small ashram. Later, whenever I went there, a few people would come there. A few people turned against it and said that this person should be stopped from coming here. Not only that, they also came to beat me. When they came to beat me, this is the man who saved me from being beaten. Otherwise they would have beaten me. They waited again. But I didn't go there again.

All this cave area was fixed, and a statue of Narayana Guru was also placed. The people there cooperated enough in it. A vessel shopkeeper there - his name is Madhavan Pillai or Chellappan Pillai or something - first brought a lamp there. That then increased a lot. It was when people started coming and believing a lot that opposition came. When opposition came, I looked at what the reason for it was.** When I told Gopalan Mash the reason for the opposition that I got from the vision, he said it was true. It is like this.

In this mentioned place, there were two girls who were older and younger sisters. One of their uncles lived as a brahmachari like a sanyasi. Even before Narayana Guru Swami went there, he made an artificial cave there, made space for a sofa, a bed, a table, and two chairs, and was running it like that. If he saw Narayana Guru Swami anywhere, he would call him and make him stay there. At that time, Swami was known as Nanu Asan. Whenever this Nanu Asan stayed there, one of the girls would clean the uruli and prepare dried rice, chutney, and curd. Then the other girl would say, 'Hey, your man has come'. This girl was not favorable to Swami. There was a mirror there then. The people of this unfavorable girl took this mirror and smashed it on the floor. Then they chopped up this bed, sofa, and chairs and threw them in a deep pool that was there.

One day when Swami came, this was the situation. Then that uncle said. It won't be right for us here anymore. I have a relative in Varkala Mundayil, I will take you there, he said, and took Swami there. From there, Swami would go somewhere during the day. When you cross the Thoduve bridge, there is a place called Pullanjimoodu. When you walk there, there is a jackfruit tree and a spring pit on one side. People come there and bathe and all. A little further away from that, there is another jackfruit tree. If you go and sit there, you can hear the roar of the water. It seemed good to meditate sitting where you can hear this roar. That is the reason for choosing Aruvappuram. It is the sound of water crashing down there. What will you feel when you reach the final stage of this sound?

You can see the water falling down in several stages, finally producing the sound 'Om'. That place where the Swami used to sit normally also had such a greatness. He used to go and sit there because of that greatness. But there were four thugs there. Thugs ruling a place is everywhere, right. These thugs were special people. Narayana Guru Swami is someone who says do not make toddy, do not give it, do not drink it. But these special thugs belonged to the group that said toddy should be made, given, and drunk. They saw the Swami there - at Nadaya. They would call the Swami and say, Swami has a sense of difference, we do not have a sense of difference. Swami would agree to whatever they say. He would also go behind them. Otherwise they would beat him. They would not let the Swami go until they made him agree that they do not have a sense of difference. One day they called the Swami like this. There was a toddy shop there that day. They entered there and bought toddy for the four of them and then for the Swami. This is their lack of sense of difference.

After this incident, he went and sat under a jackfruit tree at a place called Kattuvilakam. When he sat there, a mother came and said, I will give Swami a place to sit. That place was a hill. Like an inverted palm jaggery. When he sat there and looked around, everywhere was like this. He sat there. That is how Sivagiri was formed.

Even before Sivagiri was formed, Aruvippuram, Muttakkad, and Aluva were formed. When I described this place like this, Gopalan Mash said. It is true Swami. I told them a way to do karma. I also told them that if they do it like that for twelve years, they can achieve it. They started doing it and it was after about a year that they came to beat me. Then I did not go there. That place is today's Guruvihar.

It was after that I came here. I took this Gopalan Mash with me to find a place to tie four palm leaves. In the meantime, an announcement had been received that one should sit in such a way that no one sees for fifteen days. Therefore, I went and hid in a house in the said place. Thinking that fifteen days were over, without remembering, I went to the railway station to go to a place. When the train came and I was about to catch it, the son of a sister of the mother of the house where I was hiding pulled my towel and hit me.

The formation of Guruvihar is not in history. I made it. Did you see the experience I had when I tried to fix it? Did Narayana Guru Swami do anything wrong that the sofa, bed, and table were chopped up and he had to submit to the thugs?

From there I had a thought that I should come straight here and make a place. But there was no way to come from there. Many patients were lying there. Varkala has a terrible kind of stone. These patients are lying on that stone. Therefore, I cannot go anywhere. The family members of these patients were coming there. Then a hut-like thing was made. So it passed there. So after passing, in the twelfth year, when I went there and tried to fix it all, this beating happened. Then I did not go there. After that I came here.

Rithavani
