[GV1:P2584] October 1990

[GV1:P2585] Disciple: We know that it was revealed as a divine voice (*ashareeri*) that Santhigiri's is a religion of liberation.

[GV1:P2586] Guru: Yes. It is a religion of liberation. A religion that liberates all *karmas* and brings forth the reality. In bringing it forth, we are not casting it away. It is liberating itself. We only need to perform this particular *karma*. We do not need to do anything else. Even if this *karma* is driven away, it will not leave. Suppose an affectionate dog is raised in a house. If someone beats and chases it away without any reason, will it leave the house? Similarly, we are performing this *karma* with authority.

[GV1:P2587] Disciple: What is the reason for taking a dog as an example?

[GV1:P2588] Guru: We grew up like dogs, adopting the attire and language of others. Now, if we try to chase away that attire, language, *karma*, and custom by saying we don't want them, will they disappear? Aren't we merely fighting among ourselves like a pack of brawling dogs? Do you not realize that we lack even the sense of a dog? At least the dog knows it should not leave the house. But we do not have this understanding. We only have the mentality of "If you hit me, I'll show you." Even if it's a father, he shouldn't hit me unnecessarily. Just because one is a father doesn't mean he has the right to do anything. One should act with that understanding. This is our sense of freedom and sense of democracy.

[GV1:P2589] This is the direction our modernity has taken. But is there liberation from slavery through modern thinking? Liberation will not happen so suddenly. True liberation is possible only through spirituality, or the subtle realm. We are solidifying that *dharma* which is designated as *karma*. It will operate at the subtle levels of human existence.

[GV1:P2590] Disciple: How should those who join this lineage uphold this *dharma*?

[GV1:P2591] Guru: First of all, a disciplined way of life must be imparted to the family. Speaking of a disciplined way of life, hasn't the Vedic scholar provided a discipline of his own? A sorcerer has his own discipline. Similarly, we too need a discipline of our own. This does not mean completely discarding what currently exists. Let all that is old remain in its place. Without destroying any of it, we will extract its spiritual essence. However, we will certainly destroy what is base and vile. This is our modernity.

[GV1:P2592] Disciple: Does that mean the external appearance of temples and churches will remain exactly as they are?

[GV1:P2593] Guru: Yes. But, over the course of time, new principles will be introduced into them. It will only be achieved over time. As stated earlier, it will operate at subtle levels. Sudden changes will not be made in external rituals. It is also not right to attempt such changes. This is because people will become bewildered. If done so, they will be left in a state of not knowing what to do. One must seek spiritual merit through actions, and fortune through spiritual merit. That fortune will become the foundation of everything. Such fulfillment of action is our discipline and objective.

[GV1:P2594] Disciple: Could you elucidate a basic duty to be performed as a believer here? Beyond believing and gaining strength in that belief, as an individual in life, how should his behavior, love, and humility towards others be? What should be his code of conduct?

[GV1:P2595] Guru: A family consisting of his father, mother, wife, and children. In this family, there exists the course of karma performed by the ancestors. Unify them by explaining and making them understand what that course of karma is. He must also have a steadfast belief regarding knowledge, wealth, and mastery. Based on that belief and understanding, he should be guided to act by telling him that doing a particular thing is sufficient. Thus, through the Guru, the course of his karma from birth to death will be explained to him and put into practice. Then his resolve is in the Guru, his capacity for action is in the Guru, his moral strength is in the Guru; his knowledge, wealth, and mastery are in the Guru—in this way, the Guru principle has already influenced him like the Brahmasutra.

[GV1:P2596] Disciple: For that, shouldn't consciousness and action become self-evident to this individual?

[GV1:P2597] Guru: It is required, and that is exactly the level at which it is operated. That itself is what is called a Guru lineage. The Sikhs are such a steadfast lineage. Now they commit violence. The reason for that is the tenure of that Guru's path has ended. Before they started the violence, they were indeed followers of a virtuous path. Now they act in a way that makes it difficult even to say they are followers of that path. One could say violence is an instrumental cause, as the era of that Guru's path has concluded.

[GV1:P2598] People will accept our traditional growth as something beneficial to the world. To make it so, we only need to remember one thing: act with humility. It is enough not to think about destroying everything else. Whatever else exists, let it remain in its place. We hold no animosity towards anyone.

[GV1:P2599] We have our path, and our path is not one meant for conflict. If we engage in conflict, another conflict will grip us. Therefore, we should only act according to our own principles. Then we ourselves will become endowed with intellect, action, righteousness, humility, and capability. No one else will be able to destroy that. If we proceed to act peacefully, everything will come to us. This is our foundation.

[GV1:P2600] Disciple: What is the Guru's message for us?

[GV1:P2601] Guru: We lack the capacity for action. Whatever action you do, foresee it beforehand. There is no rule that only a specific action must be done; any action should be accomplished. The word received in the vision is that you have a lack of capability. Therefore, perform every action with resolve and prayer. If so, by the grace of God, everything will become complete, and our effort to build an action that can impart peace to the land, its people, and the world itself will bear fruit. Dedicate yourselves by telling, understanding, and resolving among yourselves that what we have been doing is a universal action. Otherwise, do not waste time gossiping and boasting wherever you go. The Divine Word is that prayer will yield very auspicious results. You just need to act with humility towards that word.

[GV1:P2602] Guruvani
