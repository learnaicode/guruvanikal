[GV1:P1485] February 1985

[GV1:P1486] Everything we do with penance and other rituals has a result. It has often been informed that this is not a lineage with the corresponding knowledge. In the experience up to this day, it feels exactly like that. The reason is that such spiritual knowledge does not exist among us. We do not largely accept the principles of psychic powers (*siddhi*) or other principles of *karma*. However, there is belief in *karma*. The wise can untie that *karma* through faith. That is why it is said that self-realized souls do not accept it. We will only see one or two such self-realized souls in our entire lifetime. We might sometimes see plenty of others. Although one may find a yogi, a performer of *karma*, and a devotee, the self-realized soul will be rare.

[GV1:P1487] Those with some experience explain and inform us about the merits of this yoga, *karma*, and the like. However, self-realization begins only beyond Devendra. When speaking of the position of Devendra, it stands at the sixth state. So, it begins from the point where the Indra of the devas and the lord of the *ganas* stand. The stance of the wise is that whatever exists up to that point cannot be called self-realization. But here, we often proclaim those who practice devotion for ten or twenty years as great self-realized souls and knowledgeable beings. This is something that remains confined merely to the knowledge of past incarnations. Such knowledge refers to an assembly consisting of all demons, all deities, sages, ascetics, and the wise. That is why it was said that not many such knowledgeable self-realized souls appear among us. Not all of us can reach that level of knowledge either. Furthermore, it is only three hundred years after their time that people begin to praise and acknowledge their glories.

[GV1:P1488] If such a great soul arrives, this is how we can understand their nature: they will always show us affection in a very profound manner. It will also be accompanied by a majesty that makes it impossible for us to ever forget them. Yet, within that, the disciplinary nature will not be absent. They might discipline us in every way. But we lack the knowledge to humbly listen to or understand all of that. The reason for that is the previously mentioned lack of acquaintance. Even if we fall into the ignorance of not being able to implement what we received in our time, we should at least have the wisdom to see, hear, and accept it. There is a tendency among us to reject and dismiss everything. There are many who assume that if they mock and dismiss everything with contempt, others will believe them to be very great.

[GV1:P1489] It is not that kind of knowledge which is being discussed here. This is extremely profound. It has been thirty-three years since I had the experience. However, we happened to receive this in return through its aspect of wisdom only from 1973 onwards. Prior to that, the aspects of the Trimurti were underway. Numerous difficulties and hardships had to be endured for that. Its other aspects are still not concluded today. That is, a deity, a goddess, or even a minor deity is proclaimed with immense significance in our Vedas, Puranas, and even the Upanishads in the position of visibly manifest *dharma*. When Vedantins come here, they speak of all this as eclipsed matters. Similarly, when we had the experience, it seemed like a great wonder to those who were with me at the time. Then, it became very troubling for them—wondering whether all that had happened was false or not.

[GV1:P1490] The words given by those under the Trimurti, and by the Trimurti themselves, later came to a state where we could not use them. We did not know what it was. It was a condition where we could not comprehend whether it was our fault or some other deficiency on our part. We ourselves did not know what everything that occurred prior to eleven years ago actually was. It was from the eleventh year onwards that each of these things began to be understood in this manner. By the fifth year, we were able to firmly establish the conviction that we would definitely make no mistakes hereafter. Yet, we have not been able to implement it among the people. Very few people have understood this even today. Even among the believers, those who have comprehended this fully would not amount to even two percent. It is a scenario of that nature. We are instructed to acquire this through our *karma*. It has been informed that a conviction should be established in all of you who come here, and by acting according to that conviction, through your progeny, proportional to the magnitude of our present deeds, they are to become great souls subsequently. This occurs for the third generation.

[GV1:P1491] However, the children being born at present possess merely a fraction of knowledge. We know from our experience that the children born today are more intelligent than those of past eras. Yet, even they possess only a very limited extent of knowledge. A distinct birth is ordained for the third generation—they are none other than our ancestors (*Pitrus*). The ancestors, which means the great souls existing in various realms such as *Devas*, *Rishis*, *Yogis*, and *Sanyasis*, will relinquish this ancestral state. Upon relinquishing this state, in accordance with the divine resolve (*Brahmanishchaya*), they stand poised as the rightful claimants to incarnation. Now, among the current believers, the second generation will attain a state of absolute faith. It is to them that these ancestors, possessing the rightful claim to incarnation, are waiting to be born. Therefore, you who are householders (*Grihasthashramis*) must exercise immense caution. The immense magnitude of the deeds we perform today defies verbal articulation.

[GV1:P1492] The purpose behind the incarnation of these children has been revealed. That purpose will commence here after a span of twenty years. After these years, a substantial transformation will occur even within our present system of governance; this will manifest through the children born after 1973. However, it is the children born twenty years later who will ascend to this position of knowledge. It is for this very reason that you were advised to exercise such profound caution.

[GV1:P1493] Among the revelations received, millions of ancestors—*Rishis*, as well as those under the dominion of the *Trimurti* and *Manu*—came forward and declared that this must be attained through *Karma* (action). *Karma* signifies that even our slightest movement constitutes an action. This is precisely why it has been stated that these ancestors will incarnate only within those of the second generation who are capable of attaining absolute faith.

[GV1:P1494] Guruvani

[GV1:P1495] March 1985

[GV1:P1496] You possess certain deficiencies. What are they? The *Karma* accumulated from the deeds of our ancestors is on one side. The second factor stems from the circumstances in which we are born and raised. Thus, both these elements have taken hold of us. They have manifested within the individual soul (*Jeeva*). However, when this soul begins to function, our inherent tendencies (*Vasanas*) merge with the *Karma* of our ancestors, rendering us unable to accomplish tasks to completion. This is the underlying cause. How much time is required for you merely to comprehend this concept? Consequently, you lack the inherent inclination to undertake a *Karma* of such vast magnitude. To provide a brief illustration: right here amongst us, there are those who exhibit the utmost devotion, as well as those who claim to believe but waver intermittently. None among them hesitates to assert that their own actions are unequivocally correct. We even have individuals among us who harbor the notion that if a task cannot be accomplished through them, its ultimate failure is of no consequence. Can this be considered appropriate? 'I am insignificant in this grand scheme; what remains is merely an entity called "I." I am oblivious even to the extent of what can be accomplished through it. Let me attempt an effort at least akin to that of an ant'—this tone of supplication is absent within you. Instead, there exists an egoistic sense of 'I.' Entangled within that ego, clinging to the accumulated conditioning (*Samskaras*) of myriad past lives, manifesting as a primal instinct clinging desperately to existence like a biting ant facing death, how can we possibly internalize and attain this? We harbor a pervasive arrogance that brazenly claims, 'My ancestors, my associates, or my contemporaries have accomplished everything and bequeathed it to me, and I am fully aware of it.' Therefore, it is practically impossible for us to undertake this collectively. Nevertheless, if we proceed with steadfast faith, we can achieve a certain degree of comprehension. 'O Divine, I lack the capacity to execute this. May my lineage, at the very least, be endowed with that capability'—you must cultivate this attitude of absolute surrender.

[GV1:P1497] Revelations have been provided regarding the progression of this lineage. Thus, amidst the multitudes of *Rishis* and entities belonging to various other classifications, a select group of Brahmin ancestors—encompassing Brahmin *Rishis*, *Sanyasis*, and *Yogis*—was observed. The specific reason they were distinguished is that they represent a lineage that historically wielded Vedic authority within the *Varnashrama Dharma*. Naturally, the remainder consists of ancestors to whom such authority, erudition, or supreme wisdom had not been transmitted. It is for this reason that the former group was explicitly emphasized. On the very day this spiritual endeavor (*Karma*) was initiated here, they arrived and pleaded, 'You must salvage us.' The response they received was, 'So be it, but let it be for a later time.' However, upon their subsequent return, twenty-five percent of them were assimilated into the Absolute (*Brahman*). The remainder continue to wait. Their aspiration is to attain assimilation through the merit generated by the *Karma* we are poised to undertake. Those who remain number approximately two to three hundred thousand. You must comprehend the statistical magnitude: these two to three hundred thousand exist amidst hundreds of millions of individuals.

[GV1:P1498] From the standpoint of true knowledge, one must ponder how a *Karma* of such immense magnitude has been entrusted to us—individuals who lack even the rudimentary awareness of the most diabolical and debased demonic entity. There is but a singular explanation we can offer for this. It is unequivocally nothing other than the divine resolve (*Brahmanishchaya*). There exists a profound rationale behind the divine resolve manifesting in this manner. One contributing factor is the cyclical progression of the four *Yugas* (epochs). The other pertains to the lineage of *Manu*. Subsequent to an undetermined disruption within *Manu's* lineage, the *Trimurtis* were instituted. However, the *Trimurtis* found themselves unable to advance further. The underlying cause for this stagnation culminates with *Devendra*. Consequently, there remains no inherent right to ultimate liberation (*Mukti*). Furthermore, *Devendra* himself languishes beneath the affliction of a mortal's curse. We must therefore comprehend that even *Devendra* occupies a state vulnerable to human malediction. This serves as compelling evidence of the exalted heights humanity can attain through righteous living. And this holds true even during this era of the *Shudra*. The term *Shudra* denotes one bereft of true knowledge. The epoch of the *Shudra* is the *Kali Yuga*. Operating under the delusion of possessing supreme wisdom, hastily intervening in all matters without a definitive resolve and acting devoid of righteous *Karma*—such is the nature of this era. It is precisely within such a tumultuous historical juncture that we have been incarnated. Yet, to ensure that this divine purpose manifests within humanity, the Supreme Being has been meticulously arranging the sequence of events since antiquity.

[GV1:P1499] The epoch of the *Shudra* commences with the advent of Sri Krishna. Within our southern Indian region, particularly in Kerala, aside from the selective elevation of a few individuals from the *Shudra* strata, the broader populace has remained devoid of intellectual enlightenment. Furthermore, this elevation largely occurred under the auspices of brute force, categorizing them akin to brigands—pertaining to instances where political authority was usurped through mere physical might. A trajectory that initiated with the *Ettuveetil Pillamar* ultimately met its definitive conclusion during the reign of Chithira Thirunal. Yet, in this societal framework, the privilege of communal dining (*Panthibhojanam*) was strictly prohibited to all save the royal lineage. Such was the prevailing interpretation of *Vedic Dharma* in this region. What profound knowledge could possibly be derived from such practices? Are these selectively elevated *Shudras*, or indeed anyone else, cognizant of higher truths? Their existence is restricted to base consumptive acts, sartorial displays, superficial pageantry, musical recitations, and ritualistic processions (*Thalappoli*). Consequently, the collective intellect of our society—the knowledge inherent to the *Shudra* state—has descended to abysmally low levels. Correspondingly, scriptural exegesis was reduced to the mere recitation of mythological texts (*Puranas*), casually appended with a superficial gloss of *Advaita* philosophy. We are the very progeny of such profound ignorance. Ergo, it is improbable that individuals such as ourselves will attain immediate spiritual realization. Nevertheless, through an act of divine grace, you have been granted incarnation. This grace lies precisely in the fact that we have been incarnated during this very *Kali Yuga*. The *Kali Yuga* is fundamentally an era of hybridization and syncretism. The state itself is characterized by syncretism. Consequently, every consumable commodity is a hybrid. This constitutes the ultimate triumph of the *Bhagavad Gita* and its author. In contemporary times, whether among humanity, the avian and animal kingdoms, the botanical world, or even our sustenance, nothing remains free from hybridization. This hybridization extends even to parental lineages. To rationalize this, individuals construct the apologetic argument: 'If Panchali was permitted such liberties, why should we be denied?' This rationalization is applied to figures we traditionally venerate and revere. Yet, their comprehension is strictly limited to the numerical aspects of the narrative; they remain entirely oblivious to the profound esoteric significance of Panchali. We constitute a hybrid demographic, executing our *Karma* in a state of disarray and leading profoundly impure lives. Regardless of the counsel offered, an individual in this state may not spontaneously embrace a purified existence. Sage advice may entirely fail to penetrate their intellect. Alternatively, we are burdened by a pathological tendency to pursue perceived 'goodness' while simultaneously deriding and humiliating the very sources of wisdom. This paradigm must undergo a radical transformation. Even if no other grand objective is realized, this detrimental disposition must be eradicated from within us, allowing this very hybridization to serve as the catalyst for the purification of our existence. This process is entirely analogous to the horticultural technique whereby a grafted tree, capable of yielding exquisite fruit, is cultivated from the rootstock of an ordinary indigenous mango sapling.

[GV1:P1500] This is a path bestowed by the divine will of Brahman to attain purity in this state. It is a path of action (Karma) that enables one to reach, through their own deeds, the realm inhabited by ancestors, deities, sages, ascetics, yogis, and the enlightened. We have been granted the authority to bestow birth, to impart the karma necessary to grant liberation to others and unify them with Brahman, and to perform actions for the pure karma of the children yet to be born into this lineage. This is its path. It currently remains in its primordial form. We have not progressed significantly forward. Henceforth, the authority over this rests with you.

[GV1:P1501] The reason it was stated that a lineage is essential for those who believe in this tradition and pass through it is that your ancestral identity must be dissolved from there. How should it be dissolved? It must be dissolved through liberation. The karma to grant liberation to our chosen deities whom we have worshipped—whether they are referred to as Devas or Brahmarakshas—will eventually come into our hands. This is our appointed karma.
