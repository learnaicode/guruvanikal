[GV1:P1700] April 1987

[GV1:P1701] During a pilgrimage, just as our mind becomes corrupt, they** will enter. Just as it becomes good, they will also enter. When performing actions without being either of the two, one will go there.

[GV1:P1702] Only God is above this. Each one of us needs to see something. When we speak of a guru lineage, when travelling with gurus, when praying and imagining along with the person who prays considering them as a guru, this will enter according to our strength, our line of thought, our karma, and our thinking habits. You do not know this. It can enter even without this. You should be careful at such times. Most of you will not be able to handle this. The reason for not being able to is our own ignorance. We belong to the Shudra class, don't we! Even if it is today's Brahmins, they too know nothing. Other than knowing some mantras and tantras, some properties of yantras, and some rituals, they stop when it comes to the path of manifest dharma. There, they change to a state of not knowing anything.

[GV1:P1703] These inner dwellers, who exist in this atmosphere in microscopic form and have the ability to do anything anywhere and go anywhere, have the karmic capacity to do and make others do everything. That is why the Prophet said that this world is ruled by Jinn. In their language, they are called Jinn, Hindus call them Brahmarakshasas, and Christians call them saints and angels. It is in this position that you are going with the guru.

[GV1:P1704] You need to have caution when going with a holy soul. This holy soul brings benefits and leads to attaining liberation up to the position of karma. To go beyond this, a truth must be carried. Without a guru lineage, one cannot go there; no matter how truthfully it is done - where to? One cannot go past that state. For example - suppose we go to a place looking for a great person. We do not know this person directly. We do not know anyone there either. If we ask, they won't let us in. In this situation, wouldn't we just get stuck there? Similarly, in this position, there is a block. Without knowing the way. There is no one there to inquire and find out. There is no one there to find. There is no one to tell us either. There will come a state, a plight, where we have to go and knock there. The magnitude of this plight will cause us to wander there and become nothing. That is what the path of the guru is for. The guru will bring the quality of our life to whatever position it stands in and keep it there. How much? To get a certificate from the government, a certain qualification is required, isn't it? There is such a government there. This government according to the law will be a power. That power will not let us pass. Such powers are turning us hither and thither. Those authorized for that are standing there.

[GV1:P1705] If people with corrupted minds during self-reflection go and handle things there, it will take advantage of that convenience and get inside. And then mislead this person. At the same time, what if it is merit that is found? It will enter looking at that context too. Just a slight slump is enough, it will enter then. That is its job. There are many such tricksters in the atmosphere. It will make us into its nature. Then it won't let go of it either.

[GV1:P1706] Billions of such indwelling entities densely populate the atmosphere. They enter us from the atmosphere at the velocity of wind. In the presence of a Guru, billions of these souls (jeevatmas) arrive, depart, and linger there. Having arrived, they stand observing closely at the threshold of merit and sin. Whatever actions you perform at this juncture, they will enter you assuming that very nature. While in the presence of the Guru, if your disposition is contrary to the Guru, they enter in that state, and if contrary to the Guru's ideology, they enter in that respective state during that time. Alternatively, if your disposition is aligned with something else, they enter bearing that ideology, and if it stems from an act of defiance, they permeate as a defiant ideology. Subsequently, it becomes impossible for them to attain goodness. You must be mindful of this during that time. Once it permeates, you will be unable to progress further. If you do not remain highly vigilant and aware while engaged in this action, it would be tantamount to inviting one's own destruction. It becomes exceedingly distorted. The majority of you are unaware of this.

[GV1:P1707] An example of this was observed during our previous journey*—these were experiences of indescribable helplessness. This is evident through experience. Similarly, regardless of your endeavors, such occurrences do not manifest at all times or in everyone. These souls arrive with the conviction that they will attain something through spiritual merit (atma-sukrutham).

[GV1:P1708] Conversely, when you embark on an endeavor for the sake of karma, what myriad virtues might you acquire unawares? You might elevate the people themselves, or transcend the prevailing impurities of character. Failing that, you might at least tread a path that imparts goodness even through a single word. That higher functioning aspect might transition toward goodness through us. Our vital force (jeevan) is the master of such a conscientious act. The vital force maintains such vigilance. Consequently, the mind gravitates toward whatever captures its focus.

[GV1:P1709] Guruvani
