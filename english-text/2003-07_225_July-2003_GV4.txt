[GV4:P0841] July 2003

[GV4:P0842] We have spoken about the matter of prayer several times. I am reminding you of it once more. It has been a full twenty-five years since we began offering this prayer uninterruptedly. Yet, most of those who have been practicing it since then do not understand it at all. If we ask what it is, our general understanding of prayer among us, or what most have heard and assumed, is that the grace of a deity is sufficient. Then, with offerings and vows, some of us have heard about a one-day *pooja*, a one-day inner *pooja*, or a full-day comprehensive *pooja* (*adakkipooja*)—this is the extent of knowledge we possess. When we speak of a one-day comprehensive *pooja*, looking at the most prominent ones in Kerala today, there are only two or three such temples. One is Thiruvananthapuram, or Vaikom, Ettumanoor; these belong to Travancore. Similarly, moving northwards, there are several temples from Kodungallur up to Kasaragod. That extends almost up to the Parasurama temple. Why is it that we build these temples—especially as grand temples in our Kerala—constructing the *Nalambalam*, followed by the *Pattupura*, *Oottupura*, *Poottupura*, and the *Aanakottil*, filling them with immense embellishments?

[GV4:P0843] We have not been able to question and understand what this is until today. The reason for this inability is that for our ancestors, or consider them to be Kshatriyas—except for imparting the authority of the Vedas to these Kshatriyas here, it is established or ordained that they hold no authority. They are referred to as Rajagurus and the like. Beyond that, if an attempt is made to unite us using the Vaishnava tradition, the Shaiva tradition, and the Shaktheya tradition, we will not unite. Standing in three distinct natures, they became highly embellished and degraded into mere songs, dances, games, clamor, and pageantry, making it impossible for even a child to gain any sense of realization from them. Or whatever is imparted is taken from elsewhere—like Kathakali, which is now carried forward as Kerala's unique identity. Observing this itself, the knowledge of addressing each entity differently is being copied from there. It was transformed into such a state even up to the *Theyyamthullal*, and today the government also highlights it, giving it importance as a folk art. It cannot be said that no one has had the opportunity to think about how much we have grown through this, how much we have absorbed, or how much we have been able to perceive; there have been opportunities. However, despite having these opportunities across various eras, a grave misfortune of time (*kaladosham*) that prevents us from uniting has bound us all together, steering us towards the path of destruction.

[GV4:P0844] We have heard of and known the *karma* called *Sanatana*. But today we are bewildered, unable to distinguish between what is *Sanatana* and what is Hindu (*Haindavam*). The uniqueness of this *Sanatana* is to act in accordance with the knowledge of the times. But we neither know what this time is, nor how to carry it forward. Its nature—whether it is a truth or an untruth practiced by our ancestors, as we have confined our actions following the trajectory of *karma* originating from the *Treta Yuga* after the passing of the *Satya Yuga*—it is a matter of jurisprudence. How did we behave in this; the fourth era is *Kali*. When we speak of *Kali*, what we have assumed is that it belongs to the ignorant or that it is an era of the ignorant. That is completely erroneous; there is a concept they have divided for the four eras, represented as the four Vedas. It is stated that it is Vyasa, known as Krishna Dwaipayana, and so forth. Whatever it may be, this knowledge of ours has left us perplexed. How can we celebrate or proceed hereafter in the *dharma* of this *Kali*?

[GV4:P0845] Stating '*Kali Shudrankal*' (*Kali* resides in the Shudra), *Kali* has operated in a manner that can never be altered. Therefore, when it was proclaimed that *Kali* belongs to the Shudra, many of us felt 'we are great people,' while many others felt 'we are the worst people.' What did those who were neither of these comprehend? It is a collective of a caste hierarchy enslaved because they could not adapt and assimilate according to the times. Regardless, if it is said that *Kali* is Shudra, even we do not know how we understood it. The *dharma* of *Kali*—these three eras have already functioned and passed. If any deficiency occurred in them, to rectify that flaw, the goodness of *Kali* and the Shudra must jointly attain wisdom and emerge as the authority of the *Satya Yuga*. One cannot become the authority of this *Satya Yuga* effortlessly. The intellect, capability, and competence bestowed upon us are based on an understanding of divine providence concerning us. Because we are completely unaware of it, we assume that *Kali* is evil, and it has somehow spread throughout the world as a derailed *karma*. It has been flawed over many eras. To express it in our knowledge, twenty-five *Chaturyugas* have passed since this arrived. Hence, we cannot calculate or comprehend this at all. The duration of a *Chaturyuga* is twelve thousand divine years. One divine year equates to three hundred and sixty-five human years. How can we exhaust ourselves counting this? Thus, having traversed through so many such eras, how much time do we have, at most? What are we to learn in the meantime? It is not a subject that can be completely learned. Then what must be imparted? It is the *dharma* of *Kali*. This *dharma* of *Kali* is not meant to emerge divided by caste or religion; it is a positive aspect that must manifest globally in human *dharma*. Whether you call it freedom—whatever you may designate it, no matter how much suppression was attempted, there is a transformation over the last fifty years; those up to sixty years of age can observe what that change is. Subsequent to this, the intellect of children from the last twenty years—each of you older individuals, whether it is good or bad, their intellect surpasses ours. We can discern this by observing children up to two or three years of age now. Despite such profound changes, the entire world has presented an opportunity to convince us that *Kali* is evil and *Kali* is destruction. How can this be changed? Most people presume that we cannot change it. We can change it. Therefore, this is what you must labor for. If we cannot change it, what is the benefit for our generation? We should not consider age, whether young or old. Do we need to change this or not? It absolutely must be changed. How is that possible? Within so many *Chaturyugas*, we cannot exhaustively chronicle how many millions of Mahatmas have appeared. Then how can the common populace be transformed?

Shouldn't you have the wisdom to respect and accept it? There is no one ready to take this up. My body - even lying down like this, I am speaking. What should each of you do? These people standing near me right now, each one of them, if they are like this, then what can I do? Realizing it as a memory, you must understand and act according to your knowledge as needed. This should traditionally reach the world as your acquired karma. You must understand and act accordingly. What can I do? I am not putting on any pretense before you. You would have shown greater respect than this. I know a trick. Do you know what it is? Forcefully pushing it upon you. And then lining up ten or a hundred people, praising them as 'he' and 'him' and parading them around, just ordinary people. Hearing this, you would tremble and accept it. But that is deceit. Nothing can be gained through that deceit. What can be done if those without devotion are caught in this blindness? So, this is entirely a provision given to you by God. If you harbor jealousy, darkness, or ignorance towards this, the same will happen to your entire household. We do not belong to any caste or religion. This holds the highest and most formidable position in the human world. You should understand and do this. You must know this. Grieving with our minds, whatever needs to exist in this world is bestowed upon us through that supreme cosmic power. What should we do? I do not know, dear. You must submit this request and take it up. Otherwise, there is no point in us merely repeating this as a model.

[GV4:P0937] If our boys are not capable of taking this up, it is to be attained through women. Women must fully reflect that image of the primordial mother. The coming generationٹ from them, along with their husbands and children, are meant for serving the world. For this, you must see one thing: whatever exists in the world will be shown. Then each of the spheres will be shown. In a way that proves that the Guru stands with you, or else, Asan says like this, "He sees without eyes, hears without ears" -- this is the part which says that. Kumaran Asan says this. Even though all these things were said, it was said as a continuation of learning due to the Guru's approach. But no one was able to grasp it. Then for that characteristic, many ways have been mentioned. Such a person walks devoid of illusions and desires, as an Atmayogi. These illusions and deceptions are of no consequence to him. What else? Like a madman. What is madness? Craziness. Being mad means those who lack sense in their heads. Like a madman, like an absolute simpleton - like someone who knows nothing, walking devoid of illusions, desires, and magic, an Atmayogi - this is what Asan said. That is the practice of an Avadhuta. But it is fulfilled exactly as you see it. So wherever the Guru's light exists, in that light, this path of karma, as a mother, where do you take it -- that is what is seen. So, given to you by God is a great light of truth! You were called to be told this. Even if we didn't know it, we were able to come this way and understand that this is the truth. Now you are the ones who must think about what to do with it. Not me. I should not tell you anything more for this. Because based on what you see, you are the ones who must decide to change these deceptions of the world. For that, is there any need to think about things caused by our ignorance and sit behind them grieving about it?

[GV4:P0938] Is our life not one born under the influence of the twenty-seven constellations? It should align with the calculation of three zodiac signs. Three times nine is twenty-seven. Over how many tens of millions of lifetimes might we have been connected to those who fall under these? There will be wicked individuals, malicious ones, and those who have harmed us in every conceivable manner. Encountering them evokes feelings of affection, resentment, and goodness within us. This is the inherent play of it. I shall limit my words to this for the time being.

[GV4:P0939] This is the contribution imparted to the world through you and your lineage. In the capacity of a mother, this is sequentially inherited by the mother, the children, and so forth. Where else does such a phenomenon occur? Having received this, our duty is to preserve and dignify it, thereby conferring dignity upon the world and inspiring its adoption. It is not something to be merely observed and discarded; it is not akin to visiting a museum and returning. We are earnestly seeking this through our very existence. This is not an instruction for you to embrace asceticism (*sannyasa*). Asceticism is an entirely distinct path. What is being expounded here transcends asceticism profoundly. It is not mere renunciation. It is a divine manifestation brought forth by the will of God for the entire world. That is precisely what must be comprehended.

[GV4:P0940] Consider the elderly mothers of our past—those who frequented the markets. They are unique to this region of South Travancore. Have you observed them placing another basket inside a torn fish basket and carrying it upon their heads? Similarly, this is an inadvertent error that has occurred. It is a provision graciously bestowed upon us by God. It must not be discarded. This is the only counsel I have. Through the actions you perform and inspire here, and through your words imbued with greatness, all those who come and go must be uplifted. Everyone who visits this place and departs must attain a profound sense of peace and tranquility.
