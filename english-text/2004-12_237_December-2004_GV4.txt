[GV4:P1026] We must observe the suffering starting from Sri Krishna. In the karma that is yet to come, God has given us a karma where we reduce it to ashes and, in a way, preserve it as *Vibhuti* (sacred ash) and prosperity. We are capable of undertaking this. We must undertake it. I have said this for you numerous times, or perhaps I said it for myself numerous times. But you are unable to take it up with that level of determination. Nor am I able to make you do it. If both these kinds of pain reach God, it is the greatest injustice we inflict upon ourselves. That is why I am telling you, and why these ancestors, yogis, and karmis have been kept aside. Giving them a chance to be born in their zodiac will not happen through anything other than our good deeds. At a time when we possess such an inheritance, along the path imparted with the determination, detachment, and love that we must become fully capable of accomplishing it, we must all work in unison and place our karma before the world; even if not everyone can understand the course of this life, we must develop faith in the merit that our Guru lineage will purify and humble all of us, and we must translate that faith into action. So, when it is said that the faith should be translated into action, it refers to the past faith. Not the one existing today. In that karma given by God as an opportunity to observe and articulate the greatest complete error or truth of the past faith and the one existing today—all of us—it is called Gurupooja here. If asked what this Gurupooja is, we do not have the time to explain it. During that Gurupooja, God reveals it to us. What is it? How your ancestors are. Where their souls stand, under which deity they are, what has happened in the karma of which gods and goddesses. To change this, if we just call out "Oh God," it becomes clear. Once it becomes clear, it dissolves into the light shown by God. That is neither our greatness nor the greatness of the world. All this is a profound knowledge we received according to that cosmic will. Having reached the realm of this knowledge, we must not run mindlessly behind the greedy people who turn into wealthy and clever individuals in the future generations, performing karma for wealth accumulation and showing deceit through commerce, thereby imparting the greatest baseness. Let us have the wisdom to live with what we have received, and let us not go into a karma that imparts an emotional form into the future children by showing them the greatest illusions. This requires some effort. Because we do not have the ability to take up and act upon this encyclopedia of knowledge that has been passed down through the epochs. Then what is it? Preserve the word received from God in that manner and walk according to this word. That is how we were told with the greatest evidence by an *Avadhuta* that "Word is Truth, Truth is Guru, Guru is God". I had doubts back then. What is it? It is neither what I did nor any mistake committed by our people. So, the karma proven by that *Avadhuta* is that the disability we have suffered to this day is due to not safeguarding this word. It was also the truth. It remains the truth to this day. And it will always be the truth. All of you must be able to act from that conviction. For that, each of us is growing into the dharma of imparting the determination to attain it, not only in the lineage we steadfastly see, hear, and know, but in each of the children we love. Pray to God for that.

It's not like that; not only can all this be changed, but you must also live according to the dharma of this era (Yugadharma). This is the greatest fortune we have received today. Simply changing it without purpose won't work. We can only achieve it if we have such karmic capacity. When we do this, we face a lot of difficulties and hardships. I can only speak about the information concerning me. Whatever else has happened elsewhere, must have happened. And it will be conducted. Regarding me, since my childhood, I had a thought: what is it that we need to attain, and how will we accept it? Whatever it is, there was divine grace for that. As a guide to that, if the people who get it and handle it don't become householders and their lineage, we are taking upon ourselves the greatest sin of destruction that can come upon the world. At the same time, there is no need to suffer. People will bring whatever is needed for two, four, or ten people to survive. That will be enough. But that is not our goal. Seeing the right and wrong that have come in the tradition, we must pass it on to our children and whatever generations and lineages are yet to come. When we say lineages, it refers to the three types of worship orders among Hindus called Shaktheyam, Shaivam, and Vaishnavam. The path of our doctrine is to understand the karmic movements standing in this divine presence or star constellations beyond that, act upon it, and give it to our lineage. If this path is not clear to you, you won't be able to do it in any way. During the different transitional states of this, I have suffered some physical ailments. That can happen to anyone. But for others, it has usually led them to death. Except for the fact that death did not occur, all other pains have come. You had a perception before. We have a tradition behind us that has seen, heard, known, practiced, and observed the karmic systems of thousands of yogishwaras who practiced yoga, and some paths known through wisdom. There are certain special rules to know how to remove it and bring it before us, and through whom it should be brought forward. But I am not in a position to explain all that today. You will be told about all this. Since my physical condition today doesn't allow me to summarize and explain all this, someone among them will tell you about it. Because of passing through various states, the body has been damaged. Now, my soul has no damage, but the body is damaged. I cannot describe what it is right now. Therefore, please understand it at your own pace. Then, they will explain the reason and significance of this event called prayer now. Whatever it is, do it properly. Now, most people among us are not capable of making us understand in the way we need. However, it can be achieved through our effort and through karmas we undertake with repentance. What is it? If we do not regret past actions, we cannot take this up. You should be told about that too. There are several such subjects, many additional subjects. From them, we will listen briefly to one or two subjects and then go for prayer. If one person has come from our house, if it is not understood by everyone in that house and, if necessary, by the neighbors, and if it doesn't reach the people, it will become a great sin we commit against ourselves. [GV4:P1048] He who speaks has not seen, he who has seen does not speak.

[GV4:P1049] The fourth face is a special manifestation of the imagination of knowledge. It cannot be corrected or modified. It is regarding such a face that the term 'Nanmukhan' (four-faced) is used. If this passes through the Trinity or the three faces, it will only be creative. A dissolutive special imagination that the disciple earns is also the essence of the Guru. In that path, the Guru-disciple relationship does not differentiate between male and female here. That itself is the Supreme Brahman. It is a truth that can only be repeated for the Guru and the disciple. It will seem like nothing on that path is learned or acquired. It will feel like in that Guru's life and growth, nothing is learned or acquired. Only the disciple will know that everything emanates from within himself without his conscious knowledge. Only the disciple can know the Guru. No one else can know. Such disciples are of thousands of types. Just as these seven notes are expanded into sixty-four musical notes, wherever the note ultimately settles, that is the Guru. The one who understands that is the disciple. That is why it is said that only the disciple speaks, and the Guru does not. When a scholar applied intellect to this and brought in their knowledge system, they wrote that the one who speaks has not seen, and the one who has seen does not speak. That is an act merely fit to be written by imitating the style of these vowels and consonants. How can one know the truth through this? The true word of the Guru is experienced by the disciple without it being spoken. Then one must come into the realization that the Guru and the disciple are one. There is no physical manifestation there. What can be said about the stupidity of writing a commentary just because Advaita was written down! They turn this very stupidity into their scholarship. And then, by speaking of it somewhere, they proclaim themselves as scholars. It means there is no experience. This is the stupidity of the scholar. All those who learned by hearing this repeat the same thing. Remaining here without male or female distinction, they named it Parashakti—simply because they had nothing else to say. Just like going to retrieve a coconut fallen in a mud pit, both the one who took it and the one who didn't got stuck there. Or like going to retrieve a seed fallen in a mud pit. They kept this hidden in the dark. But then, like the Englishman's tale, it sprouted inside. Pitch darkness, new moon. So, blackness. Complete blackness. It is a lid made of black. Inside it, a black cat. The cat understands that there is darkness only when it opens its eyes. This is a story of an Englishman. Will it open if covered with a story like this? It shouldn't be a story, it must become an experience. Now, our people who practice yoga, through asana demonstrations, assign it various other languages and narrate tales of an eight-span body. That will be correct according to their respective hand spans. Will it be correct for a little boy? If it is correct for him to that extent, it simply means it corresponds to whatever his size is. So, yoga is often discussed by incorporating such span measurements. Will things work if this is merely written or spoken about? This is not something that can be measured or written down like that. It is hastily spoken and hastily concluded. About this, Narayana Guru also says: 'One must fall, bow repeatedly, and recite'. When a scholar says this, the word 'repeatedly' loses its meaning. How can he fall repeatedly? Before one thing breaks, another bursts forth. Thus, it turned into an untrue trick constructed within the scholar's hidden strategy. Who can fulfill this? How can it be fulfilled? This is where it becomes a state where the disciple has nothing to share and the Guru has nothing to share, and the consciousness of changing states and differences disappears. It is a chain-like structure of non-existent disguises that teaches names like Dvaita and Advaita. It will seem like everything is there. But there is nothing there. That chain form is of karma. If that is gone, with what will you bind it? For this, saying Hatha Akasha, Hoda Akasha—thus citing Akasha in everything, ending up in a logician's language, speaking so hastily, and moving forward without caring for anything—how does one stand? For a person who goes without caring for anything, it is easy to speak about Advaita. When examining the karma and dharma paths of the commentator, it is like they built and built, made it dark, and closed their eyes. It is because they cannot explain the meaning of this 'repeatedly' that they close their eyes and create darkness. This love, respect, humility, and richness that grew in the light of time were all nurtured without a divisive intellect. The discrimination between the self and non-self mentioned by the author of the Gita, and the dedication of the self and non-self—whoever can do that is the Guru. The ability to say this, aside from the author of the Gita, has not been seen to this day. Or if it has been said, it is merely the iron fist of Vedanta's haste. It will not reveal the truth. Dvaita and Advaita are attained by climbing the steps of the Guru-disciple relationship in many ways. What is needed is not carefully explaining its meaning. Vowels and consonants are not required for this. In dissolutive love, glory, and truth, without the intrusion of disrespect, it becomes a merging of fortune and merit—the unadorned steps of the Guru-disciple relationship. This is the Guru-disciple bond. This is not something to be accomplished by pretending to be scholars or non-scholars using vowels and consonants. The result of people writing endlessly and posing as scholars in various ways is the gathering of literal meanings, thereby sowing the seeds of a difficult impasse. This is a crop that can never be harvested.

[GV4:P1052] A Message for the Welfare of the People

[GV4:P1053] When we acquire a god, a goddess, and a lineage, it is spoken of highly. We have become incapable of knowing where the history of the *Nanmukhan* (four-faced Brahma) has gone, which was formulated by blending the positions of the Trinity (*Trimurti*) with the concepts of the Siddhars and the enlightened ones (*Jnanis*). It is said that many people asked Narayana Guru Swami about these matters. He gave no reply. It was Velutheri Kesavan Asan and Thirunelli Krishnan Vaidyar who posed this question. None of the learned scholars to date have been able to explain what the status of this Trinity and the status of the *Nanmukhan* actually entail. This represents the history of a specific era in India. It is also said that many have logically dismissed its core. Certain other aspects have reached us through individuals who tore pages from historical truth to rewrite them. The reason for this is that it is the lineage of Manu that interprets everything, encompassing all worldly nature. However, to those who have studied extensively, certain elements may appear correct while others seem erroneous. These concepts of right and wrong can be observed if one meticulously examines the history of our Supreme Lord Sree Krishna. But what we have come to know is a situation where poets and their fundamental nature fled in defeat, arrived, and vanished, merely to achieve historical milestones. When examining the timeframe of all our religious doctrines, what we observe is a minor detail. In your own life, you are born, you grow, and you die. Within this span, what is displayed is the extraordinary valor of great souls who went about fabricating tales when the scholar, the ignoramus, and the rogue fled in defeat. It has been written that an era wherein we fight among ourselves and somehow evaluate and reflect upon this is not one we are entitled to. This is what has been written and referred to as the *Manusmriti*. Through this, which foundation do we possess? Is it the foundation of the Trinity? Or is it the passage where Narayana Guru states that the lamp which illuminates upon the cessation of the triad (*Triputi*) cannot be attained by a hypocritical ascetic? Similarly, the Brahmins have also been deceived in numerous ways. When we speak of these Brahmins, what we have heard about is *Karma* (action). How much of this *Karma* can we possibly witness and write about while we are alive!

[GV4:P1054] If this is brought before a great scholar, they will merely read out the names of several books to those who approach them expressing their genuine sorrow. Every book elaborates upon miraculous powers, the disciplines of Yoga, and the protocols of *Karma*. Along with this, one is expected to assume the role of an ignorant believer or devotee. How can this possibly provide an elucidation concerning the *Dharma* of transitional epochs (*Yugantara Dharma*)?

[GV4:P1055] After Krishna, two thousand four hundred and forty-four followers of the Guru's path have appeared. The same applies to Christians. It is said that Islam burst forth from this. Be that as it may. Let us not argue over it. This is because a period of one thousand five hundred years has been attributed to its functioning here. If one clearly and affectionately asks them about this, they will have nothing to say. It involves all the gods, goddesses, and the visionaries who came and went during that interim. From their perspective, all of this is indeed the truth. Why is it proceeding in this manner? No one will be able to provide an answer to that. The reason is simply the ignorance regarding what the *Yuga Dharma* (the law of the epoch) truly is. Numerous branches, which such great men have investigated and stumbled upon, constitute the fundamental wealth of our nation. Multiple attempts have been made to make you understand this in the context of a Guru lineage (*Guru Parampara*). However, consider whether we have not inherited from our predecessors a *Karma* that has stumbled and collapsed under the weight of your intellectual prowess, familial prestige, and academic sophistry! I am telling you this merely to clarify it. You will not understand this easily—that there is an inability to guide your children onto that path. It is the knowledge of a mother who survives merely by straining her vocal cords that resides within the child. To whom, how, and in what manner will this knowledge be questioned? If you yourselves openly discuss these matters within your homes, there is no need to generate such a massive uproar about it. You may examine the calculations of the *Chaturyugas* that have come and gone over these twenty-five *Chaturyugas*—you will find it. It should not be sought through mere scholarly study. Where and how did your Guru act? In what manner were you a witness to that at the time? God has created a lineage to enable you to reclaim this. Men and women must not corrupt this by committing errors. It is not that the aspect of the Trinity and the *Manusmriti* do not exist. Similar is the lineage of the prophets. When they stand before a God brimming with compassion, possessing occult powers (*Siddhi*), abilities, and mastery over mantras, they will render all their capabilities entirely useless before departing. Do not question how we have received this now. Because foundational sciences divided epochs into four and prescribed them as Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Shudra. How long do we think its duration might have been? Unless learned directly from the lips of a Guru, no one can explain these matters. Everyone knows that, as far as you are concerned, all these matters are true. How many great souls have appeared before us to live and learn for the sake of this truth!

[GV4:P1056] Similarly, demonic actions (*Paishachika Karma*) are described in numerous ways. If we keep completing all of these, across how many epochs have we been returning with destructive actions that lead to death and ruin! You have compiled and documented various pieces of evidence for this with the help of those who hold affection. I have also heard some people mentioning this to children. In all those times, I felt that what they were saying was indeed correct. Our governance system represents the acquisition of an ability for India to embrace such a fortune. If they and you contemplate and ask how this should be approached, asking them will yield no results. Consequently, the entire human world, having arrived at this demonic *Karma*, is incapable of discerning what the *Karma* of the father is, what the *Karma* of the mother is, or what the *Karma* of the child is. Therefore, do not proceed by squandering your time. Divine retribution is not merely limited to our nature; our ancestors have beautifully and with solid evidence documented world affairs and the global sphere. However, these scholars will not acknowledge it. When we speak of scholars, we have two types, do we not? If one attempts to resolve one, there is neither root nor branch to be found anywhere.

[GV4:P1057] The divine causality (*Ishwara Nidanam*) was first demonstrated here by the lineage of the Buddha. Although the truth was spoken, it could not be practically lived out. This is being stated so that all of you may comprehend. Here, one can travel by bus, by bicycle, by bullock cart, or on foot. Consider this as an analogy. This is not the primary subject. I shall elucidate all of that subsequently. Thus, everyone, all of you, must safeguard your lives in a manner that leads to true knowledge. It is for this very purpose that you performed *Gurupooja* (worship of the Guru). However, you have not grasped the true essence of *Gurupooja*. That will be explained later. If you do not fiercely protect it, it will transform into an affliction bestowed by God. Each aspect lies behind a truth that you can perceive in detail. Most people generally refuse to accept this.

[GV4:P1059] March 2005. We must first examine the causes of our nation's degradation. It is here that we have committed our most significant errors. The primary mistake concerning our nation stems from initially adhering to certain ways of life—resulting either in its inherent deficiencies or in a form of governance tainted with wickedness. This has been our predicament. In the context of such governance and the ensuing wickedness, our ignorance has led to profound failures that are entirely unjustifiable. This phenomenon is not confined merely to our region or to India; it has occurred globally. However, regarding our capacity to rectify this, our developed critical thinking remains ambiguous. We lack clear guidance on what actions to take today or how to approach these issues, resulting in individuals merely acting according to their own limited understanding without achieving anything substantial. We can briefly examine the root of this failure. It is not minor, but rather profound, though expressing it thus might be difficult for you to accept. Sri Krishna was born prior to the *Kali Yuga*, or more precisely, at the juncture of the *Kali* and *Dwapara Yugas* (the conclusion of one and the commencement of another). In a sense, this represented a profound burden of sorrow, the magnitude of which we have yet to fully comprehend. However, by misinterpreting this, teaching it inappropriately, and attributing everything to the author of the *Gita*, both scholars and the laity fall into an innumerable series of traps. How can one even begin to quantify them? Therefore, this is not what we require today. What the author of the *Gita* stated is accurate. Have we truly accepted the author of the *Gita*? If so, in what manner? This is where our error lies. When asked if we have anything meaningful to say about the long history ordained by divine will across ages, the answer is negative. We consider the pedantic parsing of words to be the highest intellectual achievement, and we each hide behind this superficial cleverness. However, will such pedantic analysis bring any spiritual benefit to your soul, my soul, or any soul in this world? Such exercises merely serve as intellectual entertainment. You will find yourself incapable of performing actions that are genuinely beneficial to your life. Consequently, while existing in a state where you are told that the era of your birth is the *Kali Yuga*, and that *Kali* is detrimental, how can we possibly rectify our situation? We stand in opposition to the divine will that provided the *Shudra* with the opportunity to attain the knowledge of *Brahman*. Sri Krishna serves as a prime example of this.

[GV4:P1060] The history of Kamsa demonstrates the anomalies that existed here even prior to the birth of Sri Krishna. We have been taught that Kamsa was inherently evil. This is fundamentally incorrect. Kamsa was a king. He fulfilled the duties incumbent upon a king (*Raja Dharma*). However, we have failed to comprehend the extent and manner in which this *Raja Dharma* should be executed, or our position within the conceptual framework of the *Yuga Dharma* (duties of the era). Consequently, what has happened to us? As the poet Asan noted, "the mistakes committed yesterday become the established doctrines of today," and so it has come to pass. We remain unaware that we are committing errors. Consider the profound sorrow of parents whose seven infants were ruthlessly killed prior to Sri Krishna's birth. Our historical understanding suggests that he killed all seven and spared the eighth solely because it evaded his grasp. If this account is accurate, what were the consequences of failing to cultivate knowledge about Sri Krishna or failing to generate any benefit or awareness for the subjects? Alternatively, in a region governed by numerous rulers—over six hundred provincial kings—the virtues of one king were absent in another. It is as if they were trapped in a vortex of conflict and conquest, or rather, languishing and suffering within it. Thus, we have merely wasted time observing the ignorance propagated by these kings day by day, or analyzing the achievements of our scholarly lineages, without ever truly understanding or acting upon this knowledge. We still remain incapable of doing so. The circumstances surrounding Sri Krishna's birth evolved in this manner. Yet, the populace of that time lacked the understanding necessary to protect Sri Krishna. Or perhaps, the kings lacked this understanding. Consequently, despite being shown the reality of this king, or the true nature of Kamsa's life, we failed to comprehend it. Kamsa was never an evil person. What then is the principle? It is '*Pratyakshavaham Dharmam*' (Dharma is that which is directly perceived or commanded).

[GV4:P1061] A king is one who listens to whoever explicitly reveals the truth to him. Thus, God stood manifest before him. Standing in that manifestation, what did God do? He dictated *Dharma* to him. Therefore, aside from the fact that he steadfastly acted upon that perceived *Dharma*, Kamsa committed no crime. He killed each infant in accordance with the command he received. The greatest tragedy, however, is that the contemporary kings, scholars, or the wise lacked the intellectual tradition to analyze why he acted in this manner, or to correct and guide him from a position of true knowledge. Why were they unable to offer this corrective guidance? That is another aspect. Our primary occupation consists of recalibrating the magnitude of our ritual offerings, operating under the framework of the currently popular *Trimurti* system, or the sectarian paths of *Shakta*, *Shaiva*, and *Vaishnava* traditions that we individually celebrate. We have not been instructed on the role of temple traditions within the *Dharma* of this *Kali Yuga*. But we have not acquired the knowledge to explain the underlying reasons for this. What is the cause? We lack detailed information concerning this *Manvantara*. Or rather, we are ignorant of several critical matters. A *Manvantara* refers to the epoch of a specific *Manu*. The duration of a *Manu's* epoch is said to be seventy-one *Chatur Yugas*. Within these seventy-one *Chatur Yugas*, this *Kali Yuga* represents the smallest measurable fraction.
