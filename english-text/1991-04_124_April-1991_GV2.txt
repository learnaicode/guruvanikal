April 1991

How far is the possibility of experienceability?

All the actions of the past ages must be combined and given a new dimension. To be more precise, it is not that we bring it, but God makes us do it. Everything happens according to time. However, it is true that some things change due to our ignorance. The anomalies that have arisen because we are unable to do what is necessary at the right time are huge. The ignorance that takes place in places that are touted as civilized is terrifying. There are people in this civilized world who believe very much in witchcraft. Reports show that even in America, witchcraft is feared.

What Vedic and occult practices can be seen among those who are considered the greatest in culture. Even ugly practices like jumping and shouting 'let me show the test' are taking place. A man who is ashamed to say that he is a believer will not be allowed to deny it by his family. Or if allowed, the neighbors will not agree. Even if there are believers, it is like today's political epidemic. In a house, one is a devotee of Vishnu, one is a devotee of Shiva, one is a devotee of Subrahmanya, and one is a devotee of Devi. Everyone's belief is right and great for them. If you hear their logic in it, those who hear it will also think it is right. That argument will increase and become the feeling of 'I'.

From a grain of sand to whatever things are born and destroyed in this world, everything has its own absurdity. This world exists because of the support of those absurdities. Looking at it that way, it is clear that even absurdity cannot stand without supporting another absurdity. Humans are seeing greatness in everything like a frog in a well, supporting here and there without knowing the mutual support of absurdities. When living in this world understanding this, a 'one' is needed to lean on. The truth is that if you work leaning on that one, the work will be in everything. Otherwise, the work will become empty in such a way that it cannot be felt that anything has been done.

When the work that was stuck here and there in the frenzy of the theory of indivisibility strengthened over time, it became a big pile of anomalies. This effort of ours can reduce it a bit. It is also a nihilism to say that all these anomalies can be completely wiped out from this world. Therefore, all we can do is stand behind that divine determination, combine our intelligence and ability with that determination, and pray to give us the knowledge and ability to do this. If you think about it, we don't even have the position of an atom. If you look closely at this atmosphere, you can see how many atoms are flashing in a single shape. Just think that we are only one of those countless atoms.

It is not that there is nothing. Even if you think so, it will go to emptiness. All atoms have mutual access. It should also be understood that the nature of access reaches everywhere like gas.

Isn't the nature of the mind exactly like this? Whatever our desire is, the mind enters into all those desires like a gas. Whether good or bad, the mind enters in that manner. Some ascetics are an example of this. Through certain worship practices, they believe in gods and goddesses as God and worship them. It becomes worship according to their desires. Among many such people, some will feel a desire to show off their skills in it. Then the desire to use 'Siddhi' (mystic powers) will arise. This Siddhi will be to attract people or achieve selfish things. Whatever it is, the desire itself is the goal. One person's desire may simultaneously cause harm to many.

Since the time of creation, the world has existed with good and bad. Thus, we, who are a mixture of good and bad, and many like us, must think and understand what this world is before showing miracles and miracles among miracles. Otherwise, to change the destruction caused by these miracles, one will have to constantly show miracles and make efforts. Thus, the efforts of both these groups become zero. One created and another destroyed. No one could achieve anything. This is how the one called an ascetic strives and fails. None of them did this with the desire to become bad. They slipped in their actions. If we lack the inherent tendency to act with the will of God as the goal, keeping our mind in the presence of God, this flaw will unknowingly grip us. Such flaws are what come upon us who act in many things externally and internally.

As far as a devotee is concerned, there is an inherent special quality by birth. The Yogi has it through Yogavidya, the Karmi through Karma, and the Jnani has it spontaneously. Although the goal is the same for everyone, the devotee has the ease to do it with a sense of dedication, the Yogi has an attitude born of Yoga strength, the Karmi has the attitude of "see what karma I have done", and the Jnani has the ability to make it appear that whatever anyone does is right on their part and that he himself is that. Experience has proven that only the devotee and the Jnani can achieve it easily.

Since the devotee, the yogi, and the jnani have a sattvic nature, the realm of karma is self-experiential. But because there is also something called 'Sattvika Aham' (Sattvic ego) along with this, the feeling of 'experience of this' may come and cause one to distance oneself from the observance of principles. One will unknowingly take pride in the greatness of the things one has done. That sense of pride has affected everyone. The proof that this sense of pride is mixed in everyone is the nature of people who say, "Oh! It's somewhat okay," even when they see the best thing. They didn't say it was useless, did they? This flaw is most prevalent in the greatest intellectuals. There is only one way to prevent this flaw. Have a sense of dedication to the Guru. The devotee especially must have it. The Jnani, on the other hand, having the goodness of doing right in its respective place, will not boast in goodness but will always keep his pure karma only in goodness. And he will complete the karma as if it were ordinary.

To reclaim the goodness in karma, guidance appropriate to the age (Yuga) is essential. When it is in the Kali Yuga, guidance must be given by seeing its sum and substance. For that, there must be a clear understanding of what Kali is. Kali is present in people and others. The concept of Kali that it is a time when man's goodness is completely destroyed, unable to do good, is not correct. We ourselves say that we have arrived on this earth according to the results of our past actions. If so, a little result of what was done in Treta Yuga and Dwapara Yuga will also come in this Kali, won't it? If it comes, shouldn't the good done then also come? That is, evaluating the good and bad of the past times, the fourth...

When acting in time, assessing the balance of virtues and sins, the result of the action leads to a state of liberation devoid of good and evil, which is a state of dissolution. There is no time like the Kali Yuga where any goal can be achieved through hard work. That is why even our ancestral sages desired to be born in the Kali Yuga to attain liberation.

What is happening here is an effort to evaluate and understand everything that has occurred so far, rectify its flaws, organize it according to the times, give shape to the upcoming situation, and bring it forward. This is the knowledge given to us as Brahman. It will take a long time to put the knowledge we have received into practice.

When organized in the nature of an ashram, this ashram developed some differences from the traditional ashram natures that have existed so far. The customary practice of completely following the path shown by the predecessors is what is organized here. God has entrusted us to organize the worship systems of Vaishnavism, Shaivism, and Shaktism according to Hindu principles. This is an effort to bring a 'One' in place of all three. That is how far the experiential validity of this effort extends.

Guruvani
