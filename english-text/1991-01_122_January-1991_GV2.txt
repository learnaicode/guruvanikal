English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

Guruvani

January 1991

What is the greatest duty vested in a householder? A householder should live a disciplined life and develop the experience and effort to raise their own children in the best way possible. Even if nothing else, they should at least develop an understanding to live together in harmony as father, mother, and children, and impart this to the children. Let them grow in that understanding if they can. For that, an appropriate model must be created. If they are to be capable of that, they need a self-developed culture.

Now people are growing up divided into various castes. Therefore, what else is there other than an eagerness to prove that one's own caste is superior? When people of each caste grow up in this manner, there is nothing else left to grow in the land (world)! By developing such a very despicable system and destroying themselves, it has become the cause of global destruction.

The era of the Ettuveetil Pillamars in Travancore lasted for more than a century. When they got such a long period of time together in their hands, if they had utilized it with the knowledge that it was a right obtained for the Shudra class, our land would not have suffered such a great blunder. They did nothing in accordance with the Yugadharma (duty of the age). Can anyone say that they have done anything in that regard? Therefore, those who think even a little cannot even accept their model. Some are rationalists, some are rationalists who speak Vedanta. You, who are adept at both, are being driven out by others. You will not be helpful in anything. The one who speaks of salvation does not obey even a single word of it. Other than speaking Vedanta, what connection does he have with the essence of Vedanta? Take the Gita Yajna itself. Other than reciting a few slokas from the Gita, what heritage of the wisdom of the Gita is there in that recitation? If one studies medicine, it only has meaning if one lives as a physician.

Vedanta - it is only good for unnecessarily troubling people. In rationalism, there is no argument about anything. What argument can one who knows nothing about this world have? For the one who came, it ended up being merely positivism (pratyakshavada). All these are blunders that occurred due to our lack of intelligence. If we are to change from this degraded state of action, the woman in the house must become good. Otherwise, no action can be accomplished. Isn't it a total difficulty if a girl grows up in the house? The thought is just to somehow get her off their heads. If there are two or three girls, the mother will fight with herself. If they argue with each other, she will beat her chest - 'Are these grown-up girls filling the house only mine?' Isn't it true? That was the pain of a mother speaking. But if they had been raised the way they should have been, if they had stood the way they should have, would there be any difficulty no matter where they are? We have not been able to impart this culture.

Due to the flaws in how we grew up, we did not get the knowledge to take care of our children from anywhere. If the father can lead a respectable life, the children will more or less follow it. If the mother cannot obey, manage, and make things run here, everything will be destroyed. It is not enough if only the father is good. Only if the mother imparts all the aspects of goodness will there be room for the children's complete knowledge and humility. Or, if a woman is good, the husband will come drunk and do all sorts of unwanted things. If he wants, he will make her drink too. To my knowledge itself, there are many such people. It is their children who need to be made good. This is the subject that householders should mainly pay attention to. There is no need for any Vedanta here. If Vedanta is served before an ignorant person, it will only be like a dog getting a whole coconut. Vedanta is fine. But

More important than that is life. What is meant by life, how should one live - we must know this.

We have made many mistakes with life. We must try to rectify this. It must be brought about through children. What we need first is the sense of dharma that leads to it. I have told many people that this is your disease, it came because of these reasons, and you should do this for it. I still say it. But no one has the ability to obey it. I am sad about that. What to do? I have tried to convince thousands of people. No one is able to take it. After two or four years of leaving without being able to obey, they will come back with the same disability. Then there is no remedy. Hasn't the time passed! This karma cannot be rectified through adults. It is only possible through small children. We can bring it about to some extent through them. Everything will be fine after three generations. That is the only way it can be done.

Is there any knowledge to impart to children? No. No matter how ignorant parents are, they will have some care for their children. But that's all there is to their care. What to do? Our contemporaneity has become like this. Understanding this, we must sorrowfully think about how we should be and what we need from now on. There must be a willingness to put that thought into action at any cost. This is what you have to do, and what you must do. There is no point in just listening and saying it is good. What is good? Whatever is appropriate, I will do it as my life experience dictates, must be decided and done by the whole household. If the women in the house say they won't do any of this, the house will be lost. It means the house itself will not exist. That is certain. The house is not going to improve with other people's answers.

Do I ever tell you any Vedanta? What is needed is knowledge worthy of being connected to life. The main component of a house is the woman. The woman, who is the main component, must have some greatness in her hands. Otherwise, when they reach ten or sixteen years of age, children - whether boys or girls - cannot be kept under control. They will go according to their wishes. If these guardians cannot keep the children under control until they become knowledgeable themselves, what is their use?

Do you want life or not? If you want life, it is not enough to live somehow. Even families who have tried very hard have gone down a lot. Because they can only apply the knowledge they deserve.

I have developed this idea by hearing a lot of abuse. Not only abuse, but there were even cases and quarrels. It hurt a lot. They stopped the abuse feeling themselves that it is not right to say more. Now they don't abuse much. That's the only change to speak of. There is a reason to suffer this much. If a Vedantic karma is given, people will not be able to do it. It is giving an introduction to a life dharma. A right has been obtained from God for that. By standing firm in that dharma, it is easy to earn merit and fortune from merit. This is the basis for the survival of life itself.

Among us, karmas like Thilahavanam, Mrityunjayam, Kshetrapindam etc. are performed, aren't they? Instead of that, Pithrunivarana karma is done here. Giving the deity what they want, the deity will also be liberated if they ask for it. Then the ancestors do not have to stand under the deity. You know there is a calculation of twelve zodiac signs and twenty-seven stars. Because the god and goddess in this stand and are born in their zodiac sign, all those evils will be transferred to us. This is the evil of the karma residing in us. This is what is called the result of birth. Explaining this to the people and comparing it with their experience

It will be said. And it will be completely correct. Once convinced that it is correct, it will be said that such and such a thing should be done. Those who want to will do it. This is the correction being given from here now. Those who believe here are able to understand this. But they lack the capability to fully accept and implement it. That is not their fault. The environment in which they grew up and the present circumstances are like that. The culture required for it is being taught. But the situation is such that it cannot be accepted and implemented. Even a small child is not free from the influence of this situation.

Guruvani
