[GV4:P0980] April 2004

[GV4:P0981] Therefore, having understood this, you must perform *Guru Puja* to the best of your abilities—achieving purity of the deities (*deva shuddhi*), sages (*rishi shuddhi*), and ancestors (*pitru shuddhi*)—as this Guru lineage transforms and brings about a paradigm shift in the traditional methods of worship that have existed hitherto. There is no trace of the path of any other previously existing Guru lineages in this. Neither does it contain the path of any other preceptor (*acharya*). This is an independent endeavor that absorbs the entirety of the objectives of the preceptors up to this point, transfers all these rituals into our midst, and ensures that these children or that lineage become instrumental in a manner beneficial to the knowers of Brahman (*Brahmajnanis*). This is the underlying vision. In alignment with this vision, even if only mentally, you should invoke God, or if all of you are physically able and willing, everyone should go and circumambulate the place of worship to the right and chant the continuous divine name (*akhandanama*) for a while. Everyone should chant internally. Having informed that everyone must do this: on one hand, there are ritualistic injunctions that are unbearably burdensome. On the other hand, there is a situation where there is absolutely no one to properly explain the practices that can be adopted. Thus, after the *Yuga Dharmas* (duties of the epochs) themselves have passed in myriad ways, the epoch that provides us with the correction for all this, affording us an opportunity to act by recognizing the mistakes or validities that occurred in all previous epochs—the *Satyayuga*, *Tretayuga*, and *Dvaparayuga*—is the *Kaliyuga*. However, we have been conditioned to believe that this *Kaliyuga* is worthless. Since *Kali* is considered worthless, there are those who even think there is no point in paying much attention to it. We must not evaluate it as worthless. By critically examining the rights and wrongs that have occurred up to this day concerning *Kali*, this *Kaliyuga* serves as a preparatory phase that imparts the knowledge of what must and must not be done to usher the departure toward the next *Satyayuga* into the midst of the masses. We require an awareness of such a *Kaliyuga*, where we are informed even of the errors and granted the opportunity to rectify them. Otherwise, our experience is one of clinging to some mythologies or epics in some way, wasting our lives without any attainment in this field of action, and living a life discarding action, oblivious to its true nature. Why is this so?

[GV4:P0982] The advent of an enlightened being (*jnani*) like Sri Krishna shortly before the commencement of this *Kali* is the greatest testament for this *Shudra* class. Because, prior to that, there was a time when kings ruled as our spiritual preceptors (*Desikas*). Consequently, in accordance with the *dharma* of that epoch, as the author of the Gita states, "Pratyakshavagamam dharmam" (dharma directly experienced), these kings would stand manifest and instruct upon each of these dharmas. However, moving forward from the era of Sri Krishna, that divine resolve (*Brahmanischaya*) had been prepared for us long before, sending many individuals in multiple phases. We failed to discover this within the dharma of our India. The reason for this is our karmic trajectory, wherein we cling to obsolete customs and act upon them, rather than rectifying and internalizing the worship practices of the aforementioned four epochs (*Chaturyugas*). Although it continued in that manner until Sri Krishna's birth, it is said that an ethereal voice (*ashariri*) was given to Kamsa even before the birth. Yet, the greatness of this ethereal voice was never articulated or grasped. What happened as a result? Kamsa was portrayed and presented in a way that makes us declare him evil. But Kamsa was not evil: How? He was merely abiding by his own *dharma*. What was the nature of that *dharma*? This directly perceivable *dharma*, where gods and goddesses manifest and instruct on what must and must not be done, constitutes that matter. So, under these circumstances, upon receiving this ethereal voice, it becomes unbelievable. This is because none of the knowledge of the past ancestors aligns with the dictates of this ethereal voice. So, what does this ethereal voice proclaim? "Rule by looking downwards, not upwards." Since this system of governance did not operate in a single language, the tragedy wherein individuals governed and facilitated governance according to their own convenience in numerous languages across India—lacking the ability to comprehend and act according to the *Yuga Dharmas*, or a situation where the king attains a sage-like status but fails to transmit that wisdom back to the populace—has been persisting across numerous cycles of *Chaturyugas*.

[GV4:P0983] But with the determination to resolve this, this mistake ending, and at the same time people coming together and growing to become knowing what the dharma of the next Satyayuga is, it was to transform into such people that Sri Krishna was made to take birth. But Sri Krishna was born a Jnani (wise one) from birth itself. But this action of being a born Jnani remained unknown to this Shudra class. Now, when saying Shudra class, you might assume it is a caste; it is not a caste. It is a Varna. We all belong to the Shudra class. A prescribed karma is arranged here regarding how a Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Shudra should behave. But converting all those karmas as each one's own entitlement, that karma went on in a way that Dharma could not sustain. We got caught in a trap where it could never be practiced, and each of us moved behind those scrambling to copy the baseness that shows the pride of inter-yuga dharma. What happened to us because of this? We lack self-knowledge. We did not know that we went towards information where others' knowledge destroys us ourselves. Then again from the time of Sri Krishna, ٹ despite Sri Krishna being such a great person, the status attained by that birth, unable to cross over from it due to the ignorance of our people, for a long time, ڊ that acquaintance from the day of birth - is a news of sorrow. Because, the royal family had the misfortune of having to beat to death all seven babies born before him. All this is due to the inability to follow the word received from the divine voice (Ashariri). What is this Ashariri? It means 'do not look upwards'. So, do not rule by looking at emperors, do not rule by looking at past kings either, look at what your Dharma is today - that is what the Ashariri meant. But since this was not a familiar subject as far as Kamsa was concerned, he maintained the scenario that was heard and made to be heard from these gods and goddesses. That is why we did not get good information about Sri Krishna. However, even after this, many gurus or prophets and their subsequent successors who grew up, all of them had the misfortune of having to hide behind this mistake. Those who tried to correct this were Nabi and Sri Krishna. But it was not corrected. The reason for that is, since it was not an elevated society, what they said could not be corrected and understood. For this reason, all of us, having been caught up across ages and known as the remainder of the fault in actions, we are taking birth in Kali Yuga to rectify it. But we do not know this. So what happened?

[GV4:P0984] Can we proceed by speaking a truth that belonged to the Satya Yuga? This is the Kali Yuga. Similarly, can we step down and act by speaking of the karmas of the Treta Yuga and Dwapara Yuga? That is also not possible. However, what we need are multitudes of Gurus who can show and explain to us the course of karma that has been worshipped up to this day. But we do not know this either. What have we heard and kept in mind? Rejecting everything, absolutely everything. We only have the experience of hiding behind numerous Tantric and Yantric procedures that show 'Swaha' by saying either 'Shivoham' or 'Swaha'. But the advent of Sri Krishna was to bring a change to this. After that, in India itself, Buddha and his lineage, the Sikhs, the Jains, and similarly numerous Gurus have come. But since we lacked the knowledge to reclaim any of this, we went back to the old ways themselves. We did not get the path corrected by Krishna either. We are just standing in the ignorance shown by the authors of the Gita or those who preach the Gita, who merely speak of the power of penance of the long history of the Treta Yuga. Then, can we actually acquire this knowledge? No. Then what is it? Can this enter into the householders? Nor that. Then what is it? Tangled in these demonstrated Tantric and Yantric procedures, we have only been able to create humans capable of sowing the destruction of this country. Then you may ask in a way. What is it? Is God that bad? The meaning of what has been said is that He is not bad. Why? The things known about the past times should not be denied. The first step to bring you to an experience of knowing it, having denied it, and then acquiring knowledge, was already laid at the beginning of this Kali Yuga. After this, Moses, as well as Jesus and the Prophet, came. Although they also spoke of the prophetic path, it would be correct to say that even today, we do not actually know what this prophethood is. Because we, the people at the very bottom, desperately changed religions and followed unfamiliar paths, adopting an ignorant path that showed great arrogance, and thus the path of truth was closed. So in this state, we are in a position where we cannot do anything at all. We just need to look from the beginning of this Satya Yuga. If we look beyond that, there is an incomprehensible karma. Then, only a hint of it can be given to you. Otherwise, it is not a karma that you should look at, mechanize, and formulate. Having completely understood the sense of defeat, how can we overcome this defeat? This is our thought today. To give us the authority for this thought, God's grace—as if brought along and given to all of us—gave birth to Mahatmas in all parts and all countries in this way. But none of this made us give up the customary rituals of our ancestors. Not only that, we neither saw nor knew what these ancestors practiced and what flaws existed in them. We too merely performed what someone else had shown. We had nothing beyond this. Because of this, even though numerous Mahatmas like Sri Krishna came, we could not revere and accept them. After it came to be like that, what this aforementioned prophetic religion demonstrated was whether a custom could be changed in any way. But even there, only the ignorant poor mingled and melted into it, with tantras, yantras, and literal knowledge of that nature. Having gone there and taken over the monopolistic nature of this foreign path, becoming fragments of an action we perform, what are a bunch of directionless and stubbornly ignorant people showing today? Siddhi; when that Siddhi is mentioned, you think it means Jnana (wisdom). If asked, "Isn't he a Siddha?", the meaning of your question is that he is a Jnani. Since even the knowledge that Siddhi and Jnana are two different things is lacking among us, we ask like this: "Is he a Siddha?" So, if we are led to a karma that brings achieved powers (Siddhis) to astound and instruct us, how will this old karma go away? Or is it that what our people practiced was nothing; and if it was something, how can it be changed? After this, having changed it, in what should we act to move forward? Until today, none of those who spoke within India have mentioned this path.
