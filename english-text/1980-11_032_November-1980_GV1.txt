[GV1:P0565] November 1980

[GV1:P0566] Question: Now, through what do you intend to establish this?

[GV1:P0567] Guru: I told you earlier itself, that this must be established through a Guru.

[GV1:P0568] Question: I understood that. You yourself say that it should not be through a Guru who lacks experience.

[GV1:P0569] Guru: Merely having experience is not enough. There are certain states and paths in this. A Guru who has not reached at least the seventh state through that path cannot establish and bring this to perfection.

[GV1:P0570] Question: Then how do you currently opine that this can be brought about?

[GV1:P0571] Guru: For that, we have words regarding the states that were directly communicated to us from the light itself. It is my absolute conviction that it can be brought about in that manner.

[GV1:P0572] Question: Could you please tell us a little about that?

[GV1:P0573] Guru: That is not something that can be simply told. However, I cannot help but say a little. Everything else will be revealed to the public by those who believe in this after my time. For now, I will tell you just a little for your understanding. When I speak, you will only understand if I explain from its original nature. Therefore, I will tell it that way. I will recall and talk about the place and house where I was born.

[GV1:P0574] Recalling the place and house where I was born, I had a family lacking in knowledge and a mother lacking in knowledge. I have no memory of seeing my father. He had passed away. I know from the locals that my father was a great devotee. My mother was someone without devotion. My maternal grandfather's family was also the same. From a very young age, I was aware of certain experiences. I did not mention them at home. I did not even tell my mother. There is a family temple at home. The divine worship of the people in this house meant going to the temple once or twice a month, that's it. My maternal grandmother used to take me to that temple. Once there, after the prayers were over, I would not feel like leaving.

[GV1:P0575] One day, after the prayers, when my grandmother held my hand and stepped onto the path, I was about to say that I am not coming. It was then that a voice came from there. I do not know from where. My assumption is that the Goddess spoke. The voice was like this: 'These people are fools and savages. Do not speak to them about this matter.' Thus, I would not do any such things while they were watching or listening. Nor did I inform them.

[GV1:P0576] During all that time, as far back as I can remember, I could see a form inside my chest. It is densely filled in there. It is from that form that the words emerge. I thought that all people had this. My body is like a microphone. I know that this lasted until I was about nine years old. Later, it disappeared. It was only after that I played or conversed. Until then, I wouldn't speak. Not because I didn't know how to. Assuming it was because my tongue couldn't twist properly, the family used to offer vows and rituals. Due to this, I couldn't even attend school at that age. Until about twelve years of age, I went to a traditional village school (Ashan Pallikkoodam) and acquired a basic education.

[GV1:P0577] I spent two years at the place where my mother was married for the second time. There, my stepfather used to drink and cause quarrels. Thus, within those two years, I internally felt an end to the life there. Since the desire to go to some ashram was within me, I wore a garland around my neck under the pretext of going to Palani.

[GV1:P0578] One day at dusk, my stepfather came home drunk, made excessive noise, and used inappropriate words. Because of that, I went and slept on the veranda of a nearby Bhajan Mutt. The next morning, I woke up, went to the teacher who taught me the alphabet, and told him that I wanted to go to an ashram. The teacher thought for a while, came with me, and enquired about where an ashram could be found. We met a person. He said that there is the ashram of Agamananda Swami in Kalady, but he didn't know if they had the practice of taking in and teaching children there. Regardless, the teacher took me there itself.

[GV1:P0579] Upon arriving there, I was informed that they were not enrolling children for education at that time due to a lack of facilities. Consequently, I walked from there and reached the Aluva bus stand by dusk. Hearing two individuals, who were oiled and ready, conversing about bathing at the ashram ghat, I followed them. Upon seeing the place, it indeed appeared to be an ashram. The head of the Sivagiri Mutt was present there on that day. I approached him and explained my situation. He replied, "Alright, let the morning come; go and sit over there for now."

[GV1:P0580] The following morning, he himself asked me to stay there. He then called the local secretary and entrusted me to his care. From that day forward, my life in the ashram continued there without interruption for two years. Subsequently, my life associated with Sivagiri extended for seventeen years, both at the main Sivagiri center and its branches. During this period, many things that people perceive as negative attributes came upon me without my awareness. I had committed no wrong to warrant this. I had to endure profound sorrow because of it. Having lived for so long without attaining any spiritual realization, my desire to find an enlightened soul intensified immensely. Fortuitously, that situation enabled me to find a Guru.

[GV1:P0581] Through him, I attained spiritual realization within two and a half years. Thus, two more years were spent residing in Sivagiri and its affiliated branches. In the interim, I faced significant opposition from my co-residents. They would initiate conflicts by levelling baseless accusations against me, of which I was entirely ignorant. I was compelled to endure such adversities in various forms while at Aruvippuram. I eventually relocated from there and returned to Sivagiri.

[GV1:P0582] Despite possessing this spiritual experience, my lack of formal education presented a significant hardship, as I was unable to read and comprehend other matters. Although they attempted to educate me on several occasions, an inherent inability to learn prevented me from acquiring literacy by any means. Upon my return to Sivagiri, I grew weary of an existence fraught with these deficiencies. Surrendering all the keys to the authorities, I resolved to seek out my Guru and reside with him. I informed him that I had grown exhausted of my life at Sivagiri, had relinquished the keys to the management, and had thus returned. The Guru remained silent for a considerable time before placing one and a quarter rupees in my hand. He then instructed me, "You must return to Sivagiri this very instant. There is no one present there. In those days, a sum of six annas would afford a meal at the Grand Hotel in Thampanoor. You should dine there and proceed to Varkala."

[GV1:P0583] I perceived this directive as exceedingly distressing. The predicament arose from the fact that I had already surrendered the keys and departed with the declaration that I would not return. However, guided by the conviction that a Guru's command must never be disobeyed, I resolved that I would only embark on any further journey after first returning there. The thought of my household also crossed my mind. They suffered from a distinct lack of cultural refinement. My guardians were descendants of those who had led an unrefined existence in the past. Consequently, the prospect of returning home felt utterly abhorrent to my way of life.

[GV1:P0584] Regardless, I decided to first proceed to Sivagiri before venturing anywhere else. Thus, I arrived there. Upon hearing the sound of my washing my hands and feet, Swami Kaivalyananda, who was serving as the treasurer at the time, called out, "Karunakaran, please come here for a moment."

[GV1:P0585] He informed me, "The Samadhi shrine has not yet been opened. Go, open it, light the lamp, perform the necessary duties, and return." Accordingly, I fulfilled the required tasks. At night, after our meal of gruel, he summoned me to sit at the pavilion in front of the Sharada Mutt and advised, "Do you understand? When you departed, there were many people here. Now, it is deserted. It has become a deplorable state of affairs. When individuals who have dedicated themselves through hardships, such as yourself, leave, this is the inevitable outcome. Many arrive here for various disparate reasons. None of them truly lead a life of spiritual contemplation. You must not depart in this manner again. Is this clear to you now? Therefore, you must never harbor such intentions or leave in the future."

[GV1:P0586] He narrated all his experiences following his participation in the Vaikom Satyagraha and meticulously detailed the tenets of the Sivagiri way of life, omitting nothing. Consequently, I firmly resolved not to leave Sivagiri again. Despite all these subsequent events, what captivated me the most was my Guru's prescient declaration that Sivagiri was deserted and all activities had ground to a halt. From that day onward, my reverence and devotion toward the Guru deepened profoundly, cementing my conviction that his commands must remain inviolable. Thus, as some more time passed, the Guru who had imparted these teachings to me arrived, sat near the Sivagiri culvert, and summoned me.

[GV1:P0587] He informed me, "You will soon experience a transition from this place, but do not depart abruptly," and promptly placed a one-rupee coin in my hand. True to his word, the prophecy materialized, and I was subsequently relocated from Sivagiri. Regardless, witnessing the fulfillment of his assurance instilled in me an even greater measure of faith and unwavering resolve. I knew I had to remain there until his return. He was on a journey, and seeking him out would be a futile endeavor. Following my relocation from Sivagiri, an individual named Gangadharan Muthalali, who harbored a personal fondness for me, facilitated the construction of a modest hut, where I subsequently took up residence. Even after more than six months had elapsed, the Guru remained entirely untraceable. There was a specific location near the beach where he occasionally stayed. Upon investigating that area, I eventually found him there. He remarked to me, "You have not yet attained spiritual realization, have you? You may depart only after that realization has been awakened within you."

[GV1:P0588] Consequently, I resided with him at the coastal area for an extended period. From there, he took me along to Thuckalay. During the daylight hours, we would wander through various mountainous terrains, marshlands, and forests. As the Thuckalay court convened and adjourned, he would frequent the court premises. I remained entirely oblivious to the purpose of these actions. The Guru would proclaim to the disciples congregated there, "Do you know him? Do you realize who he is?" Having seated me beside him, he would shower me with profound adulation, declaring, "He is an exalted soul. You fail to comprehend his true stature."

[GV1:P0589] I am well aware that he spoke such words extensively. However, I remained entirely detached from his assertions. A lingering curiosity did persist within me regarding his motive for bestowing such immense praise.

[GV1:P0590] Thus, on the fourth day at three in the morning, while nine of us were lying in a row, I suddenly experienced a sensation as though the entire world was collapsing and my body was being crushed into the size of a mustard seed. The Guru struck me lightly and inquired, "Are you frightened, child?" I replied, "My inner being is not afraid." "Ah ٹ ! You are saved."

[GV1:P0591] However, I lacked the sense to walk on the road. I could see vehicles coming, but I did not know to move aside. I had the traits of a madman at that time. He called one of his disciples, a man named Parameswaran Pillai, and said, "You must take leave for a few days and look after him." Thus, he took leave and took care of me. During the daytime, he would take me to all the holy places around there. When we went there, I could see all the conceptual deities and gods present there. There is a hill called 'Kattuva Sahib Mala' there. One has to pass the Padmanabhapuram Palace to go there. On the way, we rested at two places. There is a conceptual entity called Kalliyankattu Neeli there. Upon reaching there, the Guru made us all sit down and asked us to meditate. While I was meditating, Parameswaran Pillai called the Guru and said, "Swami, she has come!" "Tell her to go," the Guru replied. When I woke up from meditation, I understood. They were talking about the entity called Neeli. Then he asked me, "Did you see anything?"

[GV1:P0592] "No," I replied.

[GV1:P0593] "Alright, nothing with a demonic form will come before you. That is good. I need to make him do a task now."

[GV1:P0594] I did not understand what it was. Then, he praised me quite a bit and started walking along the way. He asked me to pick up a tile seen on the road. I picked it up. After walking a while, we saw a fairly decent temple with an outer wall where daily worship was held. The time must have been around three o'clock. The Guru sat near that temple. He made all of us sit down as well.

[GV1:P0595] "Open the temple and show everything to him alone," the Guru instructed. The sanctum sanctorum was locked. The rest were a few idols cast within the compound wall. Ablutions had been performed, flower garlands had been offered, and worship had been conducted on all the idols. When I returned, the Guru asked.

[GV1:P0596] "Did you see everything?"

[GV1:P0597] "I saw."

[GV1:P0598] "It is a clean place, isn't it?"

[GV1:P0599] "Yes."

[GV1:P0600] "There are few such clean places in Tamil Nadu, right?"

[GV1:P0601] "Yes."

[GV1:P0602] "Did you like everything?"

[GV1:P0603] "I liked it,"

[GV1:P0604] I replied. He got up from there and said,

[GV1:P0605] "Go inside, spit thoroughly on this idol, and come back."

[GV1:P0606] I felt a little hesitant and troubled. I am a Hindu believer, and moreover, how could I spit on this, which I had worshipped since childhood? However, the Guru's word could not be disobeyed. He asked me to step on its feet and the idol's chest. I obeyed that too.

[GV1:P0607] "Bring that tile here; take it, urinate in it, and bring it back," he said. I did so.

[GV1:P0608] "Pour a little of it on the crown of all these idols"—I did that too. After that, I had a fear inside me. I was afraid of whether I could do such a thing in the realm of faith. Then he came back, sat down, and gave us all an explanation about all this. It pertained to the details regarding the remnants of the ascetics, the remnants of what was offered to the deity, and what humans use. All these entities were suffering here. He convinced us that all of them had become eligible for liberation from this day onwards.

[GV1:P0609] We walked again and reached Kattuva Sahib Mala. We had visions there. He provided descriptions about it. We consumed the fruits and water we had brought with us and descended. Throughout this time, he explained the stories of the ascetics and their greatness to us. It was then that I realized there was a large part of what we had merely heard that was yet to be fully known. After returning, before coming back from the road, he stripped me of the shirt and dhoti I was wearing, handed me a piece of torn cloth, and asked me to wear it. After I wore it, he gave me a slap and told me to run.

[GV1:P0610] Saying "Where am I to go?" I ran.

[GV1:P0611] The experiences encountered on that journey were profound. In places like Suchindram and Kanyakumari, I had indescribable experiences. By this point, I developed an obstinate desire to reach the Guru as quickly as possible. I arrived. On my way, I received money for clothes without asking anyone. Moreover, I had attained profound internal experiences that could not be expressed to anyone. Upon hearing all this, he embraced me, wept, and bestowed a blessing, saying, "Now you may go anywhere, you will get everything you need." Immediately, I became adamant about going to Thiruvananthapuram. I boarded a train from Thiruvananthapuram to Varkala. He went to Vallakkadavu.

[GV1:P0612] Subsequently, for six and a half years, many such experiences were observed and corrected. After five and a half years, those who approached me began to have certain experiences. Thus, after seven years and three months (on the 1st day of the month of Vrischikam), three individuals arrived as if they were coming for morning prayers after a bath.

[GV1:P0613] Within that month itself, a sudden popularity surged among the people, reaching a level where hundreds of individuals were arriving daily. They began coming and reporting that they experienced relief simply by mentioning their ailments and leaving. I did not do anything in particular; I only made mental resolves for them. After seven months, the men and women who came began to experience a phenomenon of having visions. It was experienced by about fifty people to an extent recognizable as visions. However, they did not know how to correct it or guide it properly. Nevertheless, they began to attain such a state. They would say that all the matters they came with intentions for were being fulfilled. I did not do anything specifically.

[GV1:P0614] It continued in that manner for about three years. People developed a great deal of love and respect. During this time, one woman and two men emerged and began handling other matters themselves. The woman and one man were older, and the other was a child. After proceeding like that for a while, things would work out sometimes and fail at other times. After a while, I had a feeling that they had made mistakes, and I beat and reprimanded them extensively. None of this yielded any results. Time and again, things failed to work out, and difficulties kept escalating. Various types of difficulties began to emerge in different ways. Previously, if it was said that an issue would be resolved in a certain number of days, it would have been resolved ten days prior. Later on, even after the specified date passed, the illness could be seen partially lingering. Gradually, it reached a state where three-quarters of the time, things did not work out. For all this, I would reprimand them.

[GV1:P0615] During those times, an inexplicable slight hostility began to spread among those who came. The construction of Varkala College was completed. Classes also commenced. When boys went in groups, it was common for them to tease the girls. When they arrived there, these girls would go inside. Perhaps from hearing what was said to many while standing there, some felt an affinity towards this and made it a habit to receive prasadam. Many boys also came. Among them, those who walked around passing comments at girls noticed certain changes in the girls' behavior when they came there, leading the boys to feel that this was a deception or sorcery. As this spread in the college, it gained a facility to spread simultaneously to all the market shops and about four thousand homes. Along with this, some changes and behaviors that deviated from the same faith were observed in the children who had visions. Thus, an earnest investigation began to find out what this was. How could one know? Our Hindu rituals begin by standing in the position of the Trinity. Despite doing all the necessary work through this, I struggled without gaining liberation from it. Consequently, I had to face difficulties in that state for about a year. The children with these visions themselves continued to experience tremendous hardships. Finally, a disembodied voice was heard saying that two people should not sit together in a manner visible to others. Following that, we remained withdrawn for another two months. Again, the same message came: you must not sit like this.

[GV1:P0616] Today, in the place known as Santhigiri Ashram in Pothencode, a small hut-like structure was built for an ashram. A person was appointed to light a lamp there. When another intimation was received, feeling it was no longer appropriate to stay here, I moved there. In those days, it was not a place where people would frequent. One could stay peacefully. About seven months passed like that. During this time, there is a cave in Melkadakkavoor in Vakkom where Narayana Guru had sat. Its owner came and said that it was in a dilapidated state and asked if something should be done. I said, "Light a lamp. Let six months pass." After six months, people living even four miles away could see a great light emerging from that cave. Following this incident, the owner said it should be converted into a place of worship. While its construction was going on, the intimation to move to Pothencode was received. Along with it, a couple of words were given: "You should chant this. Chant and become strong." Two lines were given for two people. Before completing this, while sitting at Kadakkavoor railway station at dawn to go to Chirayinkeezhu with Kadalikkad Gopalan Master, some boys returning from a festival came that way. Some of them booed and made noises, and when I held on and got into the train, someone came, struck me a blow, grabbed me by the towel, and dragged me. However, on that day, I had suffered a lapse in memory. The words received through the divine voice had instructed me not to step outside for three days. For the first two days, I did not step outside. On that day, forgetting this, I went out. It was three days after this that I happened to come here, based on the intimation not to stay in a place where two people gather. When I left Varkala and came here, a rumor was spread that boys who were hostile to me had beaten and chased me away. When I was not seen, people believed it. They were never convinced of the truth either. Nine months after coming here, a word was received from the light that anything should be done only after two and a half years. However, after nine months, an intimation was received from the Trinity and the Rishis under the Trinity stating that treatment for illnesses could be done. It was started. The results started appearing double than before. Suddenly, a situation arose where about three hundred people would come and go daily. Within three weeks, the situation was such that sixty patients could lie inside and get cured. There were no buildings or anything else here then. The patients and visitors used to lie on the bare grass. They were exposed to both dew and sun alike. Out of dedication, they disregarded all this. Suddenly, like before, it became difficult to conclude all this within three months.

[GV1:P0617] In the meantime, an intimation was received to chant the name that was given to us earlier. When that name was prayed and resolutions were made with it, all the undertaken diseases were cured. Again, an intimation came to remain motionless for three and a half years. In accordance with this intimation, I remained silent. I was informed to undertake a journey, and I travelled. There were four of us at that time. We also took a child along. It is known that this child had received a vision earlier. We stayed here for a few days. Until then, none of those who were communicating through visions were taken along. The reason for not taking them was the feeling that, like before, some errors were occurring due to the obstruction of certain Devas. Meanwhile, it became evident that what was coming from these Devas was wrong. A feeling arose that this was no longer needed! What is received from the Light is sufficient. I firmly decided not to accept anything of this sort. This was also spoken through a divine voice. Finally, a correction was received over three weeks in the nature of a vision of Krishna. That has remained true in experience to this day. Before reaching all the temples during the journey, this ten-year-old child would visibly receive the deities and signs present there. Thus, we went up to Madras. We accomplished more than intended within three months and returned. Along with returning like that, the location for the Kallar ashram was found and it was established. Many things happening today were foretold right there. Since it is impossible to reveal any of that now, it is not being done.

[GV1:P0618] After that, it is understood that the intimation received from 1973 to 1979 is almost entirely filled with truth and greatness. It can be stated here that a few things in that intimation need to be clarified for this human world. There are two paths for the worship practices of Hindus. One is the path of Devas and Devis (deities) and the other is the path of the lineage of Rishis. The status of Devas and Devis belongs to the Treta and Dwapara Yugas, and the path of the Guru is for humans to experience and exhaust through births all the mistakes they have committed in various periods and attain liberation. The other is that the souls who have lived with the path of merging with God, having taken birth in this Kali Yuga, standing as a Guru to human beings and indeed all living creatures engulfed in Kali, will grant liberation to those who need it; and other pious souls will get rebirth in the approaching Satya Yuga, living on earth like Devas, attaining the fulfillment of birth in a way that allows change according to other yugas—it can be understood that this is the path maintained by the Guru lineage according to the law of Manu.

[GV1:P0619] Therefore, the future intention for the remaining period of Kali Yuga is to advise humans according to the directions of the Gurus belonging to this Manu lineage, convince them about the luminous matters beyond this Trinity system, and guide them on that path to liberation or transform them into souls eligible for the positions of the aforementioned Acharyas. What has been said is only a few brief portions of the intimation received from 1973 to 1979. The parts of karma performed accordingly and the experiential parts realized through it will be discussed later. Those are matters with certain directions and intentions. They cannot be stated hastily like this.
