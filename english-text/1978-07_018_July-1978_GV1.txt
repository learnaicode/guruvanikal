[GV1:P0338] July 1978

[GV1:P0339] Question: What do you describe as Sakara thought?

[GV1:P0340] Answer: When we speak of a deity of worship, it is not deities like Madan or Yakshi. They are deities belonging to the nature of the Trinity, or Gurus who have imparted some experiential nature in the same manner. If one holds on to any one form of the Trinity, the devotee's desire—its physical beauty, its glorious power, and its compassionate glance overflowing with devotion—will entirely come together, and we will personally experience the mind thrilling with overflowing, joyful devotion. All these harmonious imaginations are creatively formed in the devotee's own mind. Thus, with the manifestation of any of these, its primordial experience can be seen in dreams, deep sleep (sushupti), and the fourth state (turiya). Following this, it will continue in the waking state as well. When this happens, the devotee's mind will begin to overflow with joy. The sub-deities of such gods, as well as certain souls who are devotees believing in these gods, will also manifest along with it.

[GV1:P0341] Question: Once that manifestation has occurred, then there is no need to have an aversion towards other things, is there?

[GV1:P0342] Answer: I did not understand what you meant by other things.

[GV1:P0343] Question: By other things, I mean that once the deity manifests to him, he will be able to understand more or less everything.

[GV1:P0344] Answer: Some minor things about the deity will be understood. It will also be understood that this is a manifestation. However, not much help will be gained from it to enter into spirituality.

[GV1:P0345] Question: Why is that, isn't this spiritual?

[GV1:P0346] Answer: If you wish, you can call this the stepping stone to spirituality. Beyond that, this cannot be called spirituality.

[GV1:P0347] Question: Then what can we achieve with this Sakara thought?

[GV1:P0348] Answer: We often say in ordinary words that we attained a sort of liberation through this. First of all, this can make one experience it like a perfect, obedient servant who has believed and served. Secondly, it is difficult for the nature of atheism to reach him later. At the same time, he becomes firmly convinced that faith has a complete theistic existence (foundation). Other than these and similar things, it does not reach the true nature of spirituality.

[GV1:P0349] Question: Then what would be the other results if these gods are believed and worshipped so earnestly?

[GV1:P0350] Answer: The believers in the world will be able to receive some minor things from this person who made them experience it, like words that seem to have been suddenly experienced, and they will accept and act upon them. People will also enthusiastically start making minor offerings and the like. This can be carried forward up to a certain point. After that, both the one who believed and acted, and the one who made them act, may end up stagnating like water in a pond that never dries up.

[GV1:P0351] Question: I didn't understand that, it would be good if you could explain it once more.

[GV1:P0352] Answer: If gods and conditions are accepted, a human can test it and show results only up to a certain limit. After a long period like this, he may reach the position of that god. Thus, that ability of the god may not fully manifest in him, and even if it does, it will result in dissatisfaction. When that happens, the god himself will create adversities against him. Then he will hold on even tighter, pray, and perform even more rituals. The results might then increase slightly. But the results will turn adverse, making it seem as if the actions have become entirely futile. At that point, due to his mental distress, he will lose his mental balance entirely and turn into an incompetent person who causes more danger than an atheist.

[GV1:P0353] Question: Why does it happen like that? What is the reason for such a thing happening to him despite being so great?

[GV1:P0354] Answer: We have known from the Puranas that even for a deity, it is about living happily in the concept of heaven for a certain period of time. That is also what is known from wise sages. That is our experience as well.

[GV1:P0355] Question: Then, isn't it said in these Puranas and elsewhere that in the past, these deities were made to manifest, and great things were accomplished?

[GV1:P0356] Answer: That is more or less true. When the knowledge, qualifications, and intellect of the writers are mixed into it, errors occur in the Puranas. Those who study such errors, accept them as truth, and practice them will face the same blunder. They are the ones who are said to commit worse mistakes than atheists. It will require a very difficult phase for their souls to attain liberation.

[GV1:P0357] Question: What do you state as the reason for that?

[GV1:P0358] Answer: Because this soul learns to act in this manner and remains somewhat aware and unaware of the results of its actions, it may fail to live a respectable life while alive. The reason for this is the arrogance of feeling that one has experienced and known many things. Beyond that, there is an unwillingness to acknowledge anyone else. With this, all their actions become wrong, and at the same time, they lead a life unsuitable for ordinary people. They will be unable to speak about highly spiritual people and will even reject them.

[GV1:P0359] Many people of this kind can be seen in various places during the same period. Leaving their physical bodies, their souls will remain suspended in the atmosphere. They will not get a rebirth immediately. Standing there, they will gather souls lower than themselves to join them. Thus, they will form a group. They will trouble ordinary people and lead noble devotees astray.
