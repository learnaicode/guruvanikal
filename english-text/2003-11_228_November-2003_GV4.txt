[GV4:P0950] November 2003

[GV4:P0951] Therefore, he can never grow and arrive with wealth and prosperity. Buddha is an evidence of this. Another evidence is Sri Shankara. Observe where these two reached by taking two different paths. The numerous gurus who came after Sri Krishna are the evidence. All of them acted with the desire to speak the truth in various things. But they did not reach it. This is what you must see. The prophetic religions and they too have arrived at this same point by saying various things. So, if this prophetic religion is stated to be wrong, how can another person's religion be right? It is fine if it changes by being proven wrong. Alternatively, if other paths of the gurus are stated to be wrong, how can one say that any other guru's path is right? So, whatever rights and wrongs have occurred to date, the first question is: what is the cause for this? We must thoroughly understand and describe those effects that originated from that cause. By describing and understanding them thus, making the children investigate what this mentioned path is, and as parents, knowing and understanding that path ourselves, how can we bring about a change from the flaw of karma where one has transformed into a deva, a devi, a rishi, and a sanyasi? That is not possible if only a man changes or if only a woman changes. For this, one must reach at least the position of Vashistha or Vyasa, who advised the devas. If you stay away saying that this is a very lengthy matter, you will not attain it. This is an era where either a woman or a man can utilize the dharma of Kali. You should never consider it as a caste or a religion.

[GV4:P0952] We have been born in the dharma of Kali in order to take up and execute a dharma that entirely belongs to the human world. We must inevitably reach this. Even if we happen to be born out of this time, it will not be possible. Every era has its own special mandate. Having been born in Kali, how can we proceed without respecting it by saying that Kali is bad? Therefore, we can only proceed by respecting Kali. How should it be respected? What should we be like in order to respect it? How are the chronicles of these past times narrated? Such matters have been stated to some extent through Puranas or epics. But when it comes to reality, they mislead. This has been our experience till date. To deviate from this, many gurus and prophets have been brought into this dharma of Kali Yuga over various times since Sri Krishna. Thus, in between, great sages have also been brought to provide guidance to this deva and devi. But we do not know how this guidance is provided to the deva and devi. Then, all we know is that going somewhere according to this Amavasya—most people do not do it, but when a few go for the bali ritual—various phrases are recited there. "Anya janmane bandhavah" is a word uttered at the very end. So, will everything be resolved just by saying this?

[GV4:P0953] Will it be resolved if we repeatedly say all these things starting with 'Jambu Vivekanandavarshe'? Therefore, this is not it; regarding us, where did this flaw of karma occur—this is what needs to be known. But it will not work even if a man and a woman know it separately. This is because, as long as the father, mother, and children do not unite, you cannot do anything. This is the result of your united actions as father, mother, children, husband, and wife.

[GV4:P0954] You must start your ascent from wherever you attain the grace of a Guru. Otherwise, when we speak of working together, is anyone acting without cooperation? No. Each of us is the creator of an idea that remains unsatisfied no matter how much wealth, power, and knowledge we acquire. Where can we find a change from this? Change cannot be obtained from anywhere. So, the knowledge we have been led to believe is that one should embrace renunciation after wandering in pursuit of this until three-quarters of life is over and the strength of the nerves has waned. When these nerves are in a state where they cannot even be lifted, what can possibly be accomplished here? We are drowning in a sense of Dharma that has fallen into the ocean, merely trying to discover in which era of the epics and Puranas some knowledge from an unknown time was preserved. We must step away from this. To step away from this, a proof is needed. I am trying to explain it briefly here. It is impossible to study and teach the glories of all the Gurus and prophets who have appeared to date, the apostles who came as their followers, or the disciples of the Gurus who belong to these four realms. Instead, we are discovering it. Discovering it means recovering it from the great sin of a Guru's sacrifice—what is meant by the great sin of sacrifice? When one goes to immerse oneself in sacrifice, everyone rejects him like a great sinner. People will never be found to love him. When he remains unloved in this way, his mind will wander in the tragic state of being unable to connect with and love anyone. It is the same whether it is a man or a woman. Furthermore, if one receives one, two, ten, or a hundred visions, they will keep diverting us by showing the conditions of these visions. So, if the conditions of the vision keep diverting us, how will we search for and find it? Which is right and which is wrong—there are no two paths for men and women for this. There is only one path. Then on this path, in the characteristic of the Yogi who said that women—even one's mother—should not be in the house, in this conditioned karma where one attains proficiency in action, who can remove the karmic flaws of a human life that has fallen and struggled, and how? Or if someone is called a perfect devotee, how can one know what that devotee truly is? It is never possible. Because, to hasten toward the realm called the Guru, one must know what a Deva is, what a Devi is, what a demon is, or what is the significance of the epochs that have evolved from a worm or even an atom? This is impossible for anyone other than a Guru who can reveal this. So how do we know what kind of a Guru that is?

[GV4:P0955] There is a meaning that we have been taught. What is it? How will we survive in an era that dictates even mothers, who are women, are restricted? Or suppose someone goes out alone and achieves something. Isn't it an act of destroying people by reaching the realm of magical powers and performing miracles? Thus, attaining all kinds of occult powers and letting them overflow as if they were true wisdom is the karmic flaw that has afflicted us. When we hear 'Siddhi' (occult power), we think of a wise person. There are many Siddhis. Even manifesting a lower spirit (Chathan) is said to require eighteen siddhis. Man calls even this Chathan or Madan 'Lord'. And then this sorcerer or the so-called Vaidika tells us: Madan, Nadan, Nadesan. Meaning, Madan grew up to become Nadan and transformed into Nadesan. If we are made to believe that this Nadan has such abilities, where will we fall following this path? Where will we end up? How will we proceed? We have fallen into the midst of such a karmic affliction. So, having spoken about Sri Krishna and brought him through two paths, why wasn't this Sri Krishna kept in our land? What is the reason for him going outside India? When he went somewhere, gathered everything, and built a life there, what caused it to sink in the water? Such a habit of thinking does not even occur to us - or even if we did think, there is no way to find out regardless of whom we ask. Then, although some things have been agreed upon in certain places, implementing them is difficult. What is it? Now, Sree Narayana Guru, Chattampi Swami, or Sree Sankara - these three great men were born in Kerala. Saying it is Adi Sankara whom they knew and reached, what is the path we have arrived at? Does our nature only have the measure of these thousand years? If we rely on Adi Sankara, who did not understand any of this, as a reporter, how can we move forward? We shouldn't seek understanding through that path. How has it completely changed for us over the preceding ages? What all happened due to the progression of those times? Where do we and our ancestors stand after living through such a long period - this is the grace of the Guru. We cannot move forward from where those ancestors stand. We cannot abandon them on the way either. We can only move forward after performing ancestral purification, divine purification, or even the sage-level purification associated with them. If asked how to purify them, it is through the colors of light. So, having perceived this light-color, how is the Guru-essence that has taken form in it, what paths did the Guru who took that form travel, was it a Guru who lived by blending Karma, Yoga, Jnana, and Bhakti to attain karmic fitness, or someone who came as an heir, established the Guru Dharma and departed, or someone who reached the level of qualities that should exist in a Guru but couldn't act - when such a state of ignorance has pervaded our scientific understanding and epics, and we say we are completely lost, it is not merely the state of the place and time, but the state of the space and time of the ages. For a certain period, we completely cannot act. Why? Where it is calculated that a Kali Yuga itself spans four hundred and thirty-two thousand years, our subject is to understand by analyzing the Kali eras that have been estimated at five thousand years, a special rule. That is what we must understand. How will we perceive this? We can never see it. But then again, we can. There is no point in insulting anyone by speaking about something that doesn't exist. Or there is no point in saying it even if one's intellect has developed. For that, a soul suited for your attainment of a Guru must be a Guru who reveals what you and your lineage are. The karmic capacity to bond with that soul is called our inherent tendency (vasana), and it serves that purpose. That is the vasana of the soul. How that soul operates within these universal elements and the five senses and culminates in the five sheaths. The Guru is the one who integrates the dharma of karma and vikarma acting as the living being and the soul through these five sheaths and reveals it here. Otherwise, that Guru cannot be attained for trivial matters. So, if you are told this, where will you end up standing? You should view it like this. I told you that I arrived here by mistake. Because once we have become fully capable up to the Trinity system, what do we think? That if we mention the Trinity, we don't need to seek anything else. There is indeed such a time. I won't deny it. That period is after completing the Treta Yuga and up to the end of the Dvapara Yuga. After that, there is no point looking there. This culminates in Krishna. That story of Krishna is what I narrated earlier. When it stops at Krishna, how will we know the nature of the Gurus who came later? How will we understand what exists within those Gurus? That is something shared mutually by the disciple and the Guru. The author of the Gita describes it as the discrimination between the Self and the non-Self, cultivated by men and women, young and old, according to their respective merits. It is discerning the Self through the Self. Then how much merit from past lives is required? This is it. It is the Guru who must account for it. Thus, counting and reaching the karma where the counted number becomes meaningful, we are observing this. What all is it? It involves showing us the time from which these elemental forces began to move and act. There should never be a deviation from that. If it changes, it is lost. If we introduce a change and reach there, it will be akin to trying to extract the core of a banyan tree. It will be like searching for the core of a banyan tree that has been spreading branch after branch for thousands of years. That is not how a meaning is assigned to this banyan tree. How its veins flow, how it draws nourishment from the tip of the leaf, that is the only cause. So, just as a tree does not grow without leaves or its other characteristics, man has grown and become an entity devoid of a core. But there is a core. Where does it merge? It merges when the karma to attain Advaita dissolves upon realizing what exists in the eighth realm above these seven realms. That is how karma ceases to remain there. Otherwise, denying the existence of karma will bring us harm. Thus, through how many variations in states has the guidance of those renunciates, who have embraced this secret of the universe to eliminate karmic flaws, reached us.
