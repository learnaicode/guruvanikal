[GV4:P0946] October 2003

[GV4:P0947] I have come many times. During all those times, I would come up to Olassery. And then return. I haven't traveled to other nearby areas. I have only visited two or four places. I haven't come here during this summer season. It was said that this is a special place. An old and new knowledge of the land has somehow merged into this. But we haven't been able to embrace it in the appropriate way. We haven't had the opportunity for that either. If asked whether it is better anywhere else in other aspects, that is not the case either. The reason for this is that we have not been corrected in the knowledge regarding what our outdated understanding and familiarity are, the difference between the Trimurti lineage and the Manu lineage, what Manu is, and how Manu came into being. Another reason to state for that is this: we come to fully know about the divine resolve from some Kalanthara Guru (guru across times) and the contemporary gurus associated with them. But the knowledge is that this slipped out of our hands approximately twenty-five Chaturyugas (four-epoch cycles) ago. This cannot be comprehended by humans speaking of it. It cannot be comprehended by a Deva speaking of it either. I came to know of it just as one realizes something by getting into an accident. If asked how, we possessed a certain knowledge; a knowledge belonging to the Trimurti lineage. The custom is to state the matters of Parashakti and stop right there. Or, to articulate it in a slightly more refined manner, the only application we have witnessed is narrating the story of Devendra and dismissing it by saying it is the play of the Lord. Everywhere there, a karma that creates wonder is what has been presented to us. We can only believe it. Why? It is because we reached a point of not knowing where the error occurred and where it needs to be corrected. I indicated to you that this was realized by falling into this blunder. What is referred to as this blunder is that we observe a familiarity of having worshipped most powerfully the karmas up to the Trimurti status. But this also strongly narrates the story of the heavenly world and halts. To justify that to us, they mention 'Pathalanagaram' (the netherworld city) and narrate its special features. That never aligns correctly when juxtaposing certain things with the dharma of Kali. When they come around to stating this, we have learned by being told and made to recite matters of countries that can be passed off as being on the far side of this earth. But because of this, we do not reach the threshold of knowledge either. This does not transcend beyond a nationwide knowledge. The reason it does not cross over is the karma written and linked by mentioning the Devendra status and Parashakti status. It does not evolve beyond that either. But it is not possible to arrive here either. Then this is how we perceive it. We mention states of transition (avasthantharabhdham) in some Puranas. But they haven't been able to articulate it conclusively. What is it? When we speak of states of transition, it is a vision. The resolution of this vision is stated entirely as seven thousand, seven thousand four hundred and forty-four, adding seventy like that, to seventy thousand seven hundred and forty-three. So, it implies there are this many states of transition in a specific culmination. How will we demonstrate this? In what manner can it be explained? But this vision is attained by a common man only as a rarity. Even if attained, we will not be permitted to enter into it. The reason for that—the gurus who have transcended this fourth stage are exceedingly few. Hamsapadam (the state of the swan) is appended to everything, many gurus are mentioned by associating it like that—we haven't been informed what all categories of Hamsapadam it entails. Everything is stated by appending Hamsa. To provide a summary of this Hamsapadam, we started articulating it and, if necessary, all animals are designated as the vehicles of these avatars. How many people can believe this? Similarly, we do not reach anywhere except a position glossed over, such as the vehicle of Subrahmanya or the Garuda vehicle. This is a realm they have carefully navigated by intertwining the material and the spiritual. But upon reaching there, they have nothing to impart. This is an experience of mine. The lack of anything to impart arose; some of us have heard to what extent this Devendra status reaches. Not everyone has heard. The individual named Devendra is one who has transcended this Deva status. By concluding it with the story of a Yaksha, and stating this is not it, we wouldn't comprehend it either. Because we haven't witnessed it, nor has it been conveyed to us. But if asked what has been conveyed, we do not know that either. In the karma where the Puranas and Itihasas have arrived in this manner, after revolving within it for so long, an opportunity is obtained to emerge from it. For this, the times mentioned by the author of the Gita are what is accurate in it.

[GV4:P0948] In that position known as 'Kali in Shudra', it is the Shudra class. Thus, when we speak of the Shudra class, even in our Kerala, they have been divided into seventy-four castes. These seventy-four castes become more than a hundred when we go to North India. We cannot even imagine where this sense of dharma—which has later turned into a deity of caste and rendered actionless—will take us, how it will end, or how we will be able to proceed. The reality is that neither a Purana, nor an epic, nor a science has arrived to articulate how we should redeem ourselves when caught in such a flaw of karma. Why did it not arrive? It is because they do not function with the knowledge of times. What we consider as 'times' refers to the four epochs: Satya Yuga or Krita Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dvapara, and Kali. We are now standing at the juncture where the author of the Gita mentions 'this Kali in Shudra'. Thus standing here, like those who lack absolute theoretical knowledge or who cannot comprehend interpretations without studying a language, what we do not know is: who are the gurus? Who is the guru of these times? Why are the primeval gurus merely invoked for the sake of it? Who should become the followers of this guru? In which lineage should it culminate, and which part should it reach? What, then, is the dharma of Kali? We ourselves must observe the rights and wrongs that have occurred to date. Having seen that, we must fulfill it within the dharma of Kali, rather than running away from Kali. We have been made to believe that the dharma of Kali is entirely flawed. Because Kali has been depicted as an evil, we do not become thoughtful in every aspect. Furthermore, one should not think about it. It describes what state is experienced by incorporating atomic science into atmospheric revolution. But beyond that, no one can do anything there. Knowing how this atom originated is the way. Regarding this way, how does the vibration of an atom exist within us? How many billions of vibrations exist together? It means that we ourselves should perceive this. There is no other logic. After discovering this in such a way, we only need to view men and women merely as an origin. It is impossible for a man and a woman, or any prophetic religion, or any guru of the era for that matter, to simply depart after acquiring only the vibrations corresponding to that era. But acquiring it does not make one a guru of the era. The description of an era is illustrated through twenty-one gurus. However, we have not actually been able to see such gurus anywhere recently. Even if they exist, it is a misfortune that one cannot reach out to them. That is what is called a yogi. So, what has the yogi done? He touches no one. Going along a path without touching anyone, he mastered the laws of this nature in whatever ways possible, considered these laws as his life, and preserved it as yoga.
