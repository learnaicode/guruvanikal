[GV4:P1091] December 2005

[GV4:P1092] Through what did the existence of the world come about? The truth will be revealed only after the complete form of untruth has been shown. One can ask the subtle Guru (Sukshma Guru) or the practical Guru (Karya Guru) using word, deed, and intellect. The Guru can change the karmic capacity only by making the disciple perform karma repeatedly. Vision (Darshan) is beyond thought and beyond intellect. It should not be doubted.

[GV4:P1093] In this world, whether it is great beings or other living creatures, no two are alike. That is the glory of God. Experience (realization) is a glory. Acting from a place of experience should be done carefully. Acting from a place without experience will not cause disasters. There is no question of baseness being stirred up there. But where there is experience, the baseness within us will be stirred up without our knowledge. If one has experience, one will clash with the Guru. If one has no experience, one will simply get confused. There are so many examples of this here. Given this, it might seem better not to act at all. Bringing about devotion and publicizing it, even if done in a frenzy, should not be seen as a fault. It should come about like that. This birth is the remainder of taking bodies numerous times. Not everyone might get this fortune. Even the things experienced by the greatest sages are obtained by the children here. If devotion is maintained with discipline for three months, then it is enough to constantly carry it in the mind. It is enough for children to pray for a little time. Otherwise, the inclination (vasana) will be lost. One cannot consume too much of a good thing, can they? Can one eat ghee like one eats tapioca?

[GV4:P1094] Some people only see the external garb. How should love be carried forward? There is a secret in spirituality that we do not know. To grasp it, one must suffer pain. Foresight encompasses thousands of spiritual visions and spiritual paths. Bhakti Darshana, Yoga Darshana, Karma Darshana, Dharma Darshana—the misfortune of this path is that even through a god or goddess, or even by a propounder of Yuga Dharma, these cannot be identified or described. Now, we need to highlight the great souls. Since we do not know what the Karma Darshana according to Yuga Dharma is, it remains distant even to those called seers. It is like asking what happened during the time of Rama or Ravana. There is no point in softly stating that the multitude of humans, who lack the knowledge to even correct and describe the worldview of the many ages that passed before, will not understand anything. However, there is an easy way. We need to cite the great ones as examples to understand, overcome, and clear the grand step of Yuga Dharma, wherein we cannot comprehend how long Karma and Dharma have been flowing astray. This is done by examining the course of Karma from Swayambhuva Manu to Vaivasvata Manu, who obtained foresight across Manvantaras and Kalpas, and by analyzing what a god, sage, ascetic, wise person, and devotee truly are. Let us take the shortest period. Time, as we describe it, consists of Satya Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dvapara Yuga, and Kali Yuga. A Chatur Yuga is formed by these four periods, transformed into an act of Karma by wise ones or practitioners—how many thousands of years does a wise person's foresight span! The length of a Yuga is made up of how many such wise ones. Who corrects and writes the Dharma of that Yuga? Then, how immensely profound will be the pain one has to experience when entering the inner chambers of spirituality? Here, the heavy burdens of destined baseness caused by past karmas are being untied. In its place, we are infusing goodness into our lives through action. This must be accomplished flawlessly. Consequently, one will wander due to numerous difficulties. If one prays along with the household, the whole household will wander.

[GV4:P1095] Devotion has a tendency to become easily agitated. Once a person becomes thoroughly seasoned in Sattvic qualities, they will act with proper understanding. One who has not attained such maturity will get agitated like a fool. Such agitation leads to ruin. The character traits that are supposed to develop over many periods emerge all at once. Whatever good deed is done, a difficulty will always arise first.

[GV4:P1096] For the sake of punctuality, sit for five minutes and pray through your work. Then, constantly fix it in your mind. When this happens, we will be showered with abuses. Illness will also come. Similarly, along with the spiritual vision, sorrow will also arrive. Whoever we see, the sorrow they experienced will affect us as well. Visions should not be spoken aloud for everyone to hear.

[GV4:P1097] If a good deed is done, an opposition will arise against that good. When doing good, resistance will come. When taking steps to prevent the resistance, one becomes exhausted. Any kind of falsehood might be employed here. Considering this as the consequence of the service rendered, an opportunity should be given for correction. When unbearable pain arrives, instead of retreating from there, one must hold on with diplomacy. If one retreats, everything is lost. For example, how did R. Sankar fare among the community? How did Mannam fare? Mannam held on until the end. What was the reason he could hold on? He had a diplomatic approach among the common people. Sankar was not like that. He only thought about monumental things. He would not manage to get things done. He would not speak matters affectionately. Humility was significantly lacking in him. Because he lacked administrative diplomacy, Sankar employed statecraft. Statecraft alone cannot correct those at the grassroots level. Mannam was exactly the opposite.

[GV4:P1098] Ezhavas and Nairs share the same predicament. They do not endure pain in spiritual matters. Nairs are a bit lower in this regard. What is the reason for this? Because they stood under the Brahmins, they could not bring it to their own homes. An elderly woman in the house chanting 'Narayana' with a mark of holy ash. Peons, document writers, village officers, subordinates—all of them apply the holy ash mark. And a dot above it. The pretense is that they are devotees. With this kind of devotion, there will be no hardship. But they do not know how to call out to God with a pained heart. There was a time when things were achieved even by frightening God. These poor people think that God is tied up with the string of fear. The belief is that if the battle between Bhadrakali and Darika is enacted, evil spirits will not enter the house for sixteen years! A face crafted in silver, dotted with rice flour and charcoal, dressed in Bhadrakali's attire with headgear and a sword. Seeing this itself induces fear. We do not have any of that here. Furthermore, there are no tests or miraculous powers here at all. However, the difference is that ours is spiritual, while the other is material.

[GV4:P1099] All we have here is prayer. It is intended for the revival and sustenance of the world. To eradicate the impurities that have existed in our minds until today, and for the fruits of those actions to become ours, first pray, and then put it into practice. If you pray intensely, there will certainly be results. In some child born now, there will be the genesis that contributes to the revival of the world.

[GV4:P1102] The joyful operational capacity to convey the divine message

[GV4:P1103] I speak to you with such authority to express my sorrow. You must understand the core of the grief I experienced when I began reforming so many people. Do not undertake this without realizing it. You have some kind of spiritual bond with me from the past. How is that? You might have assisted in penance, or perhaps your merit is that you poured a glass of water when I was walking by, utterly exhausted and hungry. Or it could have been some very small help offered during a time of hunger. When millions of people do this, there might be a hundred or so who gave it knowingly. They are the ones who experience this. You must perceive it in that way. You need to understand this. Otherwise, it is a love that humans cannot withstand. If you do not understand it this way, what can I do with you? We traveled to so many places and walked in so many ways. Somehow, we arrived here. In some small way, it might be lingering in some soul. That is what has come. Otherwise, it would not come. This is not something that is divided and distributed. The evolutionary theory of life is truly manifesting in all of us. When it is said that Buddha himself took 5,000 births, how many millions must we have taken! You will not understand this. Thus, through you, you are standing in a karma ordained for you by God. You must not waste it in vain. If you discard it, I cannot do anything. Those who stood by me, for ten, twenty, or twenty-five years, have fallen away over a single word. You will find yourself unable to do anything. Understanding this, let us take care not to corrupt our mind, words, and actions. That is the truth of the word. When that word becomes truth for us, it transforms into the Guru, into God. So, if you do not understand at least this much, there is no way for us to be saved. I have just told you about the situations of so many people. This is only one or two in a thousand. Most have gone in a way that they cannot be spoken of, touched, or stood by. Milk is good. But if it spoils, what use is it? All of you must understand this. It is good. This aspect of being good is an experience for everyone. There will be quite a few children who have this experience. By what I said, I do not mean that everyone will shine brightly. They will receive what they are entitled to. Then, if they make an effort and proceed without breaking their word, it will be fine. Otherwise, nothing can be done. Do not say you want this or do not want it. Why? Because if you say you do not want it, it will move far away. And it will not return. That is why I pointed out our Karnataka Swami. It is purely out of grace that the spirit of truth spoken of by Christ, or the light of that truth spoken of by Aurobindo, happened to you. There is nothing else to say about this. That is why, if you do not carry it with appropriate gravity, it is equally dangerous. You might take this lightly, and like boys and girls, you might just talk about some things for the sake of fun and entertainment. That is not permissible. It will lead to danger. They do it without understanding. It will push them to the brink of destruction. Therefore, all of you must keep this matter very true, and now a fulfillment has come to us in this manner. What is this the fulfillment of? Of nothing else. I said recently that there is a calculation mentioning up to the seven heavens. The calculation of Vedanta. The difficulties that arise when crossing beyond this cannot be described in words. It is after reaching this point that the Prophet, Moses, Aurobindo—and there are four or five other great souls like them—say, 'We truly do not know who this is.' This is the stage we must understand. This is a blissful functional energy that truly comes to you. Its name is bliss. When this bliss comes, we should not go and tell anyone. If you tell, it will not stay within you. It will be shaken off. That is why it is said that this should not be spoken of. Therefore, if you are fortunate enough to find this useful anywhere in your lifetime, you must perceive it truly and preserve it.

[GV4:P1104] I am telling you, do not throw it away like this, dear ones; this is an opportunity. None of us will attain this merely by praying nominally. Then why are you being made to do this? It is to show you a fragment of the truth and then reclaim it. By reclaiming it, it is to pass on those karmic principles that went wrong, through us and through our children, to our future generations, however many may come. Today, not even half the time has passed since the world was created. It is said that it will be gone tomorrow. If asked why this Asia is fragmented into so many parts, it is because ice melted from various places and turned into water. Scholars among you say that the ocean covers more than half, or three-quarters, of the earth. It happened like this. This was diverted from the land. This is the melting of a massive iceberg. From time to time, it will melt and submerge things in this manner. This danger is not just for our country. It threatens the entire world. You must understand that the realm known as 'Patala' (the netherworld) actually exists on earth. Thus, the people in this Patala can also see this. Do not ruin this opportunity given by God to reclaim it. I will not be able to do anything. I am expressing this to you with deep regret. If those who come and stay here, those who come and go, and those who continually visit do not pay attention to this, what can you achieve? I am not sitting here to perform miracles. I have nothing to gain by making you believe in something else. The reason I am saying this is that this is a divine message that must be delivered through you and your children to this country, or wherever human beings exist in the world. This cannot happen except through your children. Guide your children properly and raise them to the best of your ability. After that, this is a recurring fortune for your country and simultaneously for all our lives. Do not try to cast this away. I cannot do anything. No one who has seen me will speak of sleight of hand, magic tricks, or any such displays. I have never even announced that I possess such a thing. You must understand this. Why did I not announce it? Why wasn't it made known that such a thing exists?

[GV4:P1105] Disciple: The Guru himself did not know it initially, nor did those who stood beside him. It was later accomplished through disciples endowed with vision. In history, the only such incident we have known is regarding Kabir Das's Guru. He was unaware of his own greatness. If the greatness had not been brought to light in that manner, or if it had been known from the beginning, or if the Guru had pursued the path of scholarship, the error committed by the Acharyas of the past would have been repeated; he would have deviated from the path and failed to reach the state attained today.

[GV4:P1106] Guru: Right now, you are only this many people. If we were to step into any given place, crores of people are waiting to come. But none of that is necessary. This is not a necessary matter at all. If you do not understand, act faithfully, step forward according to your abilities, and spread it across our country and to every part of the world, then I cannot do anything unless you handle it understanding that it is the guidance of the Word of God. "Oh, we are not even aware of a truth that made us feel like rendering even a rust-mote worth of service, spending for it, struggling, and suffering for it. Whenever we are finally able to realize and adopt it, may we be able to share it with everyone with love and respect" - with this prayer, I implore all of you to safeguard it and engage with it knowingly, and with that, I shall conclude.
