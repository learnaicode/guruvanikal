[GV1:P1351] November & December 1983

[GV1:P1352] When we begin to learn and teach each aspect of our lives for the first time, we must comprehend its inherent difficulties through experience or through life itself. When it is said that one must know through life, it implies that knowledge is essential for our individual lives or for the existence of every subject.

[GV1:P1353] In today's circumstances, where everything is available, we forget even to contemplate the state of the era when people did not know how to cook food or weave clothes. Initially starting with leaves, they progressed to tree bark, and subsequently invented clothing. How much hardship and sacrifice must have been behind the invention of alphabets and medicines for diseases! In the beginning, humanity suffered from diseases for an extended period before discovering medicinal remedies. Countless lives must have been lost in the process of testing them. Even today, the discovery of medicines continues. In this manner, we continue to discover various things.

[GV1:P1354] From that time until today, as each new development emerges, we refine it under the guise of 'modernization'. Thus, using our intellect, we prepare whatever is necessary for this life in myriad ways. If we reflect upon the aforementioned hardships, everything we witness today is the culmination of immense suffering endured in the past. Similarly, we initiated practices of worship. In the beginning, we possessed no such knowledge. Thunder, lightning, rain, the sun, the moon, and the stars were all marvels to the people of that era. This gradual sense of wonder transformed into worship, and eventually, the concept of God unknowingly permeated human consciousness.

[GV1:P1355] Primarily, all living beings desire food. Following that comes the need for clothing, and then the management of bodily pain and diseases—surpassing each of these stages, we have now arrived at the era of nuclear science. Science will undoubtedly make many more discoveries. However, all of these are merely for material existence. Similarly, a substantial effort is required to cultivate our inner consciousness. Beyond all these factors, there is a profound element behind humanity acquiring such capabilities and intellect. We must recognize that.

[GV1:P1356] An unseen regulatory power is controlling not just us, but the entire universe. After contemplating this for ages, intellectual individuals began to worship it in a manner that seemed appropriate to them at the time. Subsequently, guided by their prevailing intellect, they might have retreated to isolated places and engaged in practices akin to penance or meditation. This could have been performed on a seashore, beneath a tree, on a mountain, or in any solitary location they could find. Later, they practiced this in various forms, such as collective hymns, songs, and chants.

[GV1:P1357] Among those who practiced in this manner, certain great souls began to have specific experiences. They reported seeing a particular color, a flash of lightning, a circular light in a certain location, a beam of light resembling that from a torch, or a radiance akin to the rising sun or moon. Such experiences have been described in various ways. Following these visions, they repeatedly beheld a human form.

[GV1:P1358] Attributing various interpretations and characteristics to that human form, they progressively taught society to worship and promote worship in its current state. When referring to the current state, idol worship holds primary significance. There are those who argue that there is no substance in this, claiming it is merely an entanglement rooted in human thought processes. Be that as it may. Regardless, we must entrust someone as a witness for every painful circumstance we endure.

[GV1:P1359] During childhood, parents are the most significant figures. As individuals mature, they develop the understanding that there are figures greater than their parents in the world. As more time passes, they realize the existence of a governing authority above them, and ultimately, a God who transcends everything. Consequently, as we undertake various practices to comprehend the glory of this God, we may receive revelations in forms other than the previously mentioned luminous apparitions. Certain great souls possess words of correction for this. It is through such processes that we too have been corrected and guided.

[GV1:P1360] Evaluating each of these aspects, we do not have to suffer even a fraction of the hardships endured by our predecessors. Considering the magnitude of their sacrifices, they suffered immensely on our behalf. However, we have lacked the vision or the conscious effort to evaluate and appreciate these contributions. Nor are we developing such an awareness. Perhaps a few individuals might be doing so, and society reaps the benefits. Thus, when we examine every matter, it becomes evident that we must fulfill our individual roles as well as the roles of every individual within our society.

[GV1:P1361] This knowledge consists of understanding what we must do in this regard. Every branch of knowledge must be practiced, experienced, and understood; its deficiencies must be eliminated, and it must be demonstrated—as an individual—in a manner that is beneficial and accessible to humanity. For this, a person is required. You may call him a Guru or by any other name. The nomenclature does not matter. It is sufficient that we acquire the knowledge.

[GV1:P1362] Ever since the inception of the world, humanity has possessed this spirit of inquiry. Within this realm of inquiry, there are those who remain completely ignorant. Furthermore, there are individuals incapable of acting with the requisite knowledge for it. Such an ability is inherently attained only through birth. For instance, a plant grows and blossoms solely because it possesses life. Subsequently, that life-force transitions into various other organisms, then evolves into reptiles and animals, ultimately culminating in the human form. Humans thus acquired intellect and cognitive discernment. We have progressed to our current state through the harmonious interplay of this intellectual brilliance and inherent tendencies.

[GV1:P1363] We must believe that all this is either a divine play or the glory of God meant to be revealed to us. Understanding this, why have we gathered here today? What is our duty? Who should we become tomorrow? Our ancestors are the ones who provided each of us with the guidance to contemplate such questions and pursue that path of inquiry. What is the era we are living in now? Our current quest is to determine how to proceed in accordance with these times. The present era is *Kali Yuga*. Prior to this, three eras—*Satya Yuga*, *Treta Yuga*, and *Dvapara Yuga*—have passed. It is the knowledge that existed in the *Treta Yuga* that we have been slowly unearthing and comprehending piece by piece. When *Dvapara Yuga* arrived, it evolved into a state of knowledge where we could explicitly experience it and facilitate its experience for others. In *Kali Yuga*, the knowledge we must henceforth apply and realize is that which allows us to discern its right and wrong, thereby embarking once again towards the *Satya Yuga*. We must be endowed with the fortune and knowledge that amalgamate the residual wisdom of the Gurus who appeared across various epochs with the divine grace that illuminates through our present endeavors.

[GV1:P1364] Does everyone attain this fortune? No. Divine grace exists, but that specific fortune is not always received. Today, it is said that Westerners are at the forefront of discoveries. Yet, wherever one goes in their lands, one can find both the ignorant and the knowledgeable. Refined knowledge exists everywhere and at all times. However, the reason for all this, as stated by our religious followers or spiritual Gurus, is that what has presently manifested is the innate derivation of that life-force, shaped by the accumulated *karmic* tendencies of one's birth and upbringing. It is this life-force that operates in such a manner within our bodies. This is precisely why the true nature of that divine grace cannot be fully comprehended. Nevertheless, there are indeed those who fully comprehend it and act upon that realization; we currently fall into this latter category.

[GV1:P1365] Guru Vani
