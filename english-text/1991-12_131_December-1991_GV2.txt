Guruvani December 1991

I only know how to say what I see. I don't know anything else. I will do what I am told. Even though the people who were with me here at that time were ignorant, they would come and pray at the time they were told, just as I told them. Large stones are lying loose on all four sides of this. We sleep on top of those stones. We pray while walking over them. Thus, walking, lying down, and suffering, all this was completed. After that, everything started to happen one by one.

It was shown how the Trimurti system came into existence and what caused it to be cursed. This Trimurti system will not change except in a Kali. That change is for humans. Or for the world. Even in India today, whose story is celebrated and told? Krishna is considered the greatest among the avatars in Hinduism, isn't he? Why did the Dwarka of Krishna, who did so much good when he was alive, sink into the sea? Did that great man do deeds that deserved to sink like that? Moreover, our mothers also pray by addressing Krishna, who saved sixteen thousand and eight families, in obscene language, 'You who stole the clothes.........come running.......Krishna......' This is the nature of the devotion of our Hindus.

After Krishna, a lineage of countless gurus came. Buddha, Vardhamana Mahavira, Shankaracharya, Parayi Petta Panthirukulam, Guru Nanak - thus India is a great sanctuary of sages. Not only in Kali but even in the time of the Devas, the sages had the power. We know the story of Gautama who cursed Indra of the Devas. It was Anasuya, the wife of a sage, who freed him from that curse. How great is the power of that woman.

If we have a girl in our house, what we should teach her is not the story of Krishna running away with clothes. In reality, we do not know how to take the knowledge we got in this era. Did we take what so many great souls came and practiced, did actions worthy of an acharya, and taught us to take merit from that action and fortune from that merit? Did we take what Chattampi Swami came and said that Shudras have the right to Vedas, what Narayana Guru Swami came and said not to ask, say, or think about caste? Then how will we take the benefit of association with great souls?

None of us want to do an action that causes harm to ourselves. Then how did we get so much harm? I have already told you many experiences of how the very actions I have done for each person have been stolen by their own people. I have never said anything that is not in my experience. The people standing with me here are not even my family or locals. They are children of various castes and religions. Why do I make them do all this? I used to do all this myself at first. One day the plate I was worshiping with slipped from my hand. That day I was told, 'You should not worship, you must not worship, you do not know who you are or what you are. The power you worship will suffer harm.' Instead, one should develop others in that position and guide them as a Guru, rather than doing the action oneself.

At that time, one family used to give me food. They would also give food to those with me. There was a day when they gave food to forty-two people. When I went and asked, that mother said that they were not doing anything special for the Swami. We haven't put a handful of extra rice for the Swami. We haven't bought coffee or tea.

I did not know any of this. How should I understand this? I came to know about this only after seven years. It seems that two or three families like this set aside food for me and then eat. Even if they have to feed a small child in their house, they first set aside a portion for me before giving it.

While living like that, I got a message not to sit where two people could see me. On the twenty-first day, due to a lapse in memory, the incident I mentioned earlier happened. The incident at the railway station where I was grabbed by the towel, shaken, and beaten.

It was after that, without me saying anything, I made these children say it. After that, I haven't even picked up and placed a single flower. Then, all I do is give a garland or something for a wedding, that's all. Learn the lesson through me that if you do good, even if it is delayed, you will surely receive good.

I now remember something I said twelve years ago. That this place where I sit will become a densely populated town with shops and markets, surpassing other nearby places. That happened. Today, Pothencode surpasses places like Sreekariyam, Kanyakulangara, Venjaramoodu, and Mangalapuram. I have said that after three hundred and fifty years, this will become a world pilgrimage center. It is written down here.

The time of gods and goddesses is the Dwapara Yuga and Treta Yuga. Kali is not the time of gods. We have not been able to change the practice of worshipping gods and goddesses to this day. When Vedic rituals failed to yield results, they did it in the form of 'Tantra'. They have taught us to call the great priests 'Tantri'. So, they admitted that they are not doing Pooja, but Tantra. Doesn't that mean what they do is Tantra and then Mantra, not the truth? When even the one doing this says it and we cannot understand, when will we be able to properly carry forward the Dharma of Kali?

Until now, we have acted and shown results not through the quality of association or knowledge, but through the aspect of science. Because of that, we have been unable to achieve anything. We cannot even explain how the changes from our childhood to the present day have come about. Such huge changes have occurred. This is the quality of Kali. We should be able to admit at least that much. But our ancestors have said that Kali is not good. We have also assumed the same. If so, who will take the goodness of Kali? We have assumed that the same practitioners who call themselves Tantriks are performing the duties we need, keeping God at the forefront. But what have those Vedic priests, whom we thought were doing Dharma for us, become today? Learning a little English, they are half-starving people wandering in search of some business or job - even a peon's job is enough - for a living. But wherever they are, they have their Tantra. Whether in an office, a factory, or anywhere else, no matter how many people are there, only he is able to win everyone's love even today. Why doesn't this intelligence come to the rest? It is the remnant of the karma done by their ancestors as staunch believers without letting go of rituals. Our ancestors do not have such a karma practiced and done in that way for us to follow. Therefore, whatever you say or show to this lineage, that tendency will remain deep within them without leaving. I am speaking from my experience, which is the cause of the hardships I had to face in my life from such people.

When you go to such places, it should be to acquire merit from good company. It should not be with the expectation of gaining wealth, power, or any other similar positions and honors. Whatever God gives you, let that be enough for you. You will get exactly what you deserve according to your karma. I am never a hindrance to what you receive in that way.

I am one who has seen and experienced karma in the five states of waking, dreaming, deep sleep, turiya, and turiyatita. I am one who has realized what the four yugas are, what mistakes have occurred in these yugas, and how we, having reached the fourth quarter, Kali, following these dharmas of time, should realize that dharma and what good it will do for us. From a state where a Shudra was not allowed to hear a letter, we have become worthy of the karma to enter anywhere. But if we firmly resolve to remain suppressed within the old fortress of varnashrama dharma, how can we achieve anything? How can true karma and dharma be brought about? Varnashrama dharma has been brought about in such a believable manner that it seems like a reality. But we must reclaim its true nature. To reclaim it, a lineage of acharyas or a lineage of prophets, or at least a perspective of that level is necessary. But we are born into a lineage that lacks the karmic qualification to work for it or make others work for it. This is the subject we need to understand. Based on which place will you take this? It can be any place you believe in. I am not specifying a place. You must be told this. You must know if there is truth in it.

It is said that liberation is the goal of karma. How can one attain liberation without severing past karma? I have told you only a billionth of my experience. You must be convinced of it through experience. Otherwise, you can only proceed by believing what someone else has experienced and told you. We ourselves must think about how to become clear in this karma, which is progressing with the fat of knowledge from scriptures and Puranic epics. Foreigners like the British and Arabs brought their karma and propagated it among us, but what could we give them in return? It is said that India is the seat of dharma. But we prefer theirs. But he says this is what is good. Because he, who doesn't even know how to wear a dhoti, manages to wrap it somehow and walks barefoot here. He is one who has made money, bought cars, played, and enjoyed life. He did not come here to beg. He comes after depositing the necessary money in the bank. But he walks around here like a beggar.

Our people do not even know how to wander around like beggars. They do not even know why they wander around like beggars. He has understood everything here. Some people come here too. They come and tell me their experiences** here. Otherwise, I have not gone anywhere or known anything. I have not even studied a single letter of this. My only wealth is my experience.

Having known the course of dharma that has come through the three times, we have received a pure time to become an authority in a way that sustains merit by reclaiming that dharma. What do you do for that? That is what Sri Krishna said, 'Mamekam sharanam vraja'. Believe in me. Abandon everything else - what does that mean? It is said that arrows pierced the body of Bhishma lying on the bed of arrows, let the blood drain, let the sins I committed end like that, he said. Why don't you understand all this? Is it because it doesn't align with the scriptures? Unfamiliar things never

Do not say that. We must go along with science. Otherwise, we will have to say that they were created for nothing. That is not so. A lot of myths have crept into science. They must be discarded. There are plenty of truthful things. We should be able to accept them as truth. We must be able to realize that mistakes have occurred in such and such ways, find the light from those mistakes, see that light in children, and reclaim it.
