[GV1:P1761] November 1987

[GV1:P1762] When left with no other choice*, I moved away, built a hut, and stayed there for seven years and four months. In the fourth month, on the first day of the *Vrischikam* month, seven people arrived. They came carrying all kinds of food items in their hands. They said, "We are observing a vow here today." Based on what they went and told others, eight to ten people arrived the next day. In this manner, within that month itself, the situation became such that nearly a hundred people were arriving daily. They claimed that their diseases and troubles would be cured. This was the knowledge gained from what they said. Furthermore, peace was also visible in all those who came. After continuing like this for about two and a half years, it ceased to yield results.

[GV1:P1763] The spiritual experience occurred at the Thuckalay Munsiff Court. It took approximately three years to complete. All the demons associated with the completion of the Trimurti system—whatever names are spoken—they are all present. What we have heard of in this world is very little. What comes before us is in the crores. All these do indeed exist. But what spiritual strength will this impart to us? Apart from obtaining a result when offering certain vows and oblations, when one transforms into a human being with spiritual experiences, what realization is gained from it? For its spiritual peace. This became clear to me. It is said in the Gita itself—Dharma is that which is directly perceivable, is it not! Standing materialized and imparting knowledge, and having received guidance in such a manner, I became convinced that this is the truth. After becoming convinced, the question arose as to how to manage this. When it was managed, it was completely fruitful. Not a single syllable failed to bear fruit. This was effective for hundreds of people. It was not managed by me alone. Regarding the ailments, those that were given up by the Medical College came and were completely cured. Even bladder cancer was cured. If this is spoken of, people will naturally come! Following this, it became ineffective. People suddenly began to hurl abuses. There was no information as to what it was. Investigations were made through all possible means of inquiry. It did not become clear. At that time, a word was received. The two lines currently chanted, "Om Sri Karunakaraguru Parabrahmanane Namaha / Om Sri Karunakaraguru Satyapradaya Namaha" were stated, and it was said: "You must chant this and strengthen yourself. Otherwise, harm will befall you." It was not understood. My name comes within this. Because the name appeared, I told the children who received it not to chant it. Since they had received it through revelation, they would chant it without my knowledge—without making a sound.

[GV1:P1764] After three years, there was a relocation from this place to another place—the place currently occupied. It was only after being told three times not to stay here that the move there occurred. It was instructed that one should not reside there in a manner visible to two people. Today, there is nothing there. It has simply become barren land. It lies like a withered, grass-covered expanse on the sloped area of the hill.

[GV1:P1765] A few days after arriving here, a large number of people started coming. Normally, such gatherings do not occur at Hindu places of worship. But this was indeed a massive crowd. People just began arriving in that manner. Suddenly, it reverted to the old state. The reason for this was unknown. What was instructed had not been violated. It was said that an error had occurred. If an error had occurred, why could it not be pointed out? The number of people who arrived was overwhelmingly large. If necessary, it was enough to cause harm to the Ashram itself. There were prominent merchants. There were those who came from high social standings.

[GV1:P1766] They were residing on this rocky ground, enduring the dew, rain, sun, and wind. It was for their ailments to be cured. There were no other buildings anywhere here. There was something akin to a small marquee. How many could sleep inside it? Yet, all of them kept coming like this. However, they had faith in me—in this work of mine. Regardless, I told them one thing: I will only come out after understanding this. You must take care of these patients. There were mentally ill individuals, those with stomach pain, and those with various types of diseases. Having said this, I went inside and sat down. All these patients became violently agitated. Those with stomach pain started screaming terribly. The mentally ill, since there were no trees here, went to climb and hang themselves on the trees in the adjacent property. In this manner, some fifty or sixty individuals became agitated in this state. Those who ran away were chased down and brought back.

[GV1:P1767] That evening at five o'clock, an announcement was made that food should not be consumed. It was said that even water should not be drunk. There were two merchants. Saying, "Now water cannot be drunk, such a rule has come," they went to the well, drew water, and drank it. Five minutes later, they vomited. What they vomited was poison—like something mixed with green color being poured out. Swelling also developed. When two others went outside, they received two blows—from where, it was unknown. Terrified, everyone remained inside. This was the incident of that day.

[GV1:P1768] That evening, permission was given to consume food. The announcement came: "I will come when it is five minutes to six o'clock. You must all be ready for prayer." It was done accordingly. On that very day, three patients were instructed to act in a specific manner. They were cured. Thus, within fifteen days, all these ailments were completely cured.

[GV1:P1769] Then, a journey was instructed. Among the journeys undertaken in that manner, one proceeded up to Chidambaram, the largest temple in South India, but it was not continued thereafter. Walking continuously from Kanyakumari through this region, Chidambaram was reached. Even after arriving there, there was nothing to impart. It was decided, "I will not go anywhere anymore."

[GV1:P1770] Guruvani
