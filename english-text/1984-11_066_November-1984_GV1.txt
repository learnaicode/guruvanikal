[GV1:P1453] November 1984

[GV1:P1454] Generally, there is an ignorance seen among those leading a householder's life (*Grihasthashrama*), being unable to know how to conduct their lives. By introducing all sorts of 'absurdities' into our worship practices, we have degraded ourselves to an extent where we do not even desire the survival of the soul. It was mentioned earlier that being born as a human itself is for the achievement of the soul, isn't it? The Kali Yuga is an apt time to rectify all this. The task God has undertaken by causing Mahatmas to be born for that purpose, and correcting all this through their lineage, is no small feat.

[GV1:P1455] Today, there is a competition among householders—between those who go to Arab countries to earn money to live, and those who live here with a small job. One group thinks, "My children, with this small job, must become greater than the others." Another group arrogantly thinks, "I was a mere spade-worker, but look at my children—isn't my money greater than his job?" In this manner, they exhibit arrogance. Remember that if you run competitively like this, you will fall somewhere. It is because of our ignorance that we are running frantically like this. We have not been able to impart any such knowledge to the women in our homes. One reason for that is our very lack of tradition.

[GV1:P1456] It was mentioned earlier that this is the era of the Shudra. It is among them that this commotion has arisen. Ignorance is the reason for its occurrence. Today is a time when the Brahmin has become a Chandala (outcaste), and in that position, the Shudra has been able to become a Brahmin. The *Parayi Petta Panthirukulam* (the twelve clans born to the Pariah woman) and the Eighteen Siddhars are proof of that. After that, distinct Mahatmas were caused to be born among each caste. The reason for that is, only if born in such a way would culture emerge among them. Otherwise, they would be rejected citing their caste. We can attain merit from the birth of a Mahatma only if there is an absence of arrogance. Narayana Guru Swami and Chattampi Swami belong to the group of Mahatmas who came in that manner. At the time of their birth—for both these groups—anyone with a few pennies did not want them. It was only poor and uneducated people who accepted them. Because of that, they improved to a certain extent.

[GV1:P1457] Sri Narayana Guru Swami contracted a marriage from Anchuthengu. Adjacent to it, in Kayikkara, to the north of Asannagar, there is a household named Perunthara. They were exceedingly wealthy. There was even a colloquial saying among the locals that if the wealth of the Perunthara family were cast into the sea, it would fill the ocean. However, it is innumerable how many destitute children have emerged from such a household. We must reflect upon the underlying reasons for such an occurrence. During those times, they did not accept Narayana Guru Swami. Kakkakuzhi Padmanabhan Vaidyar belonged to this lineage. He was the one who, in opposition to Narayana Guru Swami, appeared before the Travancore Praja Sabha (Legislative Assembly) and argued against 'Temple Entry'. This is being articulated so that you yourselves may reflect upon the extent of degradation and the decline of cultural refinement amidst yourselves. When spiritual masters manifest among you, they are seldom revered. We must cultivate the wisdom and cultural refinement necessary to honor them. To achieve this, virtuous and meritorious children must be born in our midst. Virtuous progeny will be conceived only if there is '*Pithrushuddhi*' (ancestral purification). Accordingly, to rectify these circumstances and to forge a lineage endowed with wisdom and cultural refinement, the Divine has bestowed upon us a profound knowledge. It is towards this noble objective that we are striving here today.

[GV1:P1458] Not merely Sankaracharya, Jesus, or the Prophet, but none of the great souls who have incarnated hitherto have been vested with the authority to perform '*Pithrushuddhi*'. All of them accomplished '*Pithrushuddhi*' merely on a minuscule scale, and that too through immense effort. For this purpose, the followers of Jesus solely engage in rituals such as chanting 'Anatha' for '*Pithrushuddhi*'. Within the tradition of the Prophet, Muslims facilitate sacred rituals such as 'Ratib'. Hindus orchestrate rituals like *Balitharpanam* or *Homam*, and in the temple tradition, '*Kshetrapindam*'. Beyond these practices, a comprehensive methodology for ancestral liberation has not existed until now. That is precisely what we have been granted here. It is impossible to articulate the profound reverence that an individual who truly comprehends the magnitude of this blessing would exhibit.

[GV1:P1459] You might have heard of the term *Bhagiratha Prayathnam* (Herculean effort). It is narrated that Bhagiratha, who had attained such an exalted royal status, renounced it all and undertook penance for an extensive period solely to purify his ancestors. However, in stark contrast, we have been bestowed with a remarkably effortless path here. By merely mandating the performance of a *Gurupooja*, one gazes into the divine light, visualizes the ancestor situated in that realm, and grasps them spiritually. Once grasped, the ancestor is either liberated, retained in the ancestral state, or granted ultimate salvation (*Mukthi*). Liberation in this context implies the granting of a new birth. Consequently, ancestors who receive this rebirth will reincarnate amidst us. Since these ancestors have attained liberation through this profound process, those progeny will be born with extraordinary merit. This spiritual undertaking transcends any caste or religion. Unprecedented in global history, no preceptor has hitherto attained such a divine methodology. The wisdom that manifests through the offspring born in this manner will unequivocally surpass any knowledge we currently exercise. It will exist on a remarkably elevated plane. However, we shall only bear witness to this manifestation when they attain at least thirty years of age.

[GV1:P1460] It has been merely eleven years since we were granted the divine sanction to perform *Pithrushuddhi* in this manner. From that juncture onwards, we have been executing this sacred duty. It is explicitly to acquire such sublime merit within this lineage of descendants that you are instructed to abstain from extraneous modes of worship. If you nurture the ensuing generation that emerges in this manner, your own spiritual deficiencies will be expiated through them. As long as this *Guruparampara* (Guru lineage) endures, your soul shall continue to accrue spiritual merit. Through your divine supplication, your life force and physical vessel accrue sanctity, while simultaneously, your lineage of descendants ascends to an exalted state of merit. Through this profound qualification, what we have ensured is the very preservation of the world. Such an exceptionally monumental spiritual endeavor is what we are undertaking here. While engaging in an action that accumulates such profound spiritual merit, we will inevitably have to endure manifold hardships, tribulations, and sacrifices. If such trials were absent, we would fail to comprehend its intrinsic value.

[GV1:P1461] If one examines the histories of Jesus or the Prophet—what immense sacrifices they endured! Those who placed their faith in them were battered to death! They were crucified! That is precisely what the Crusades exemplify. Hardships accompany only virtuous pursuits; malevolence encounters no such resistance. However, we are not necessitated to endure such monumental sacrifices. Upon conducting a comparative analysis of these two realities, one recognizes how infinitely superior our path is. Therefore, we who are obligated to live revering this path, must cultivate a lifestyle perfectly aligned with its sanctity. That is the imperative duty before us.

[GV1:P1462] Guru Vani
