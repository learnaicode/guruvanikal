Guruvani October 1992

I am doing and making others do this work here not from my own ability. I have no education, no wealth. But the knowledge here is one step ahead of all the knowledge in the world. Let any rich or worthy person come. They have much to learn from here. No one in the world can ever say this is bad, but it will only grow through you. I live here humbler than you. I don't have to struggle this much just to survive. It is enough to wear a loincloth and sit quietly as a Swami. Everyone together will take care of all my needs.

In the morning from Sivagiri, lying that I was going to Muttakkadu, I used to go to the beach. I spent ten years lying on the beach. Then the Guru said, 'You go to the sanctum, you have some things to get.' I went to Kalladimastan, bought him tea. Ate the leftovers, and came back. Then the Guru said, 'He gave you everything he had to give you. And a little more. He won't let you near him anymore.' How great the Guru is! And what the Guru gave. After that, I went to see Puthuraswami. What I saw was him making a fire in the sun and performing a homam. Everyone who came brought firewood. I told them not to trouble this poor man like this. Then one of them came to hit me. I said Puthuraswami would leave here within four months. It happened exactly like that.

What will you get from such actions of monks? What can you learn? People follow each one without thinking about any of this. You are the ones who should seek a permanent culture. You should follow everything keeping that in mind.

He gave us an idea that no one has ever received until today. Your own children are doing this looking at the light. They are telling it in front of you and making you understand. Therefore, you must be vigilant to grow this lineage. This is a way for us to know this traditionally and to be saved. For that, utmost honesty, humility, and mutual love must be maintained.

Whatever you do, you should do it praying for this ego to be destroyed. If you give food with such a thought, the blood, marrow, and flesh will change. This way, you can change all qualities. The sister, daughter, or wife is living honestly. The thought will come that I cannot live without her.

Wear whatever clothes you want on the body. Do not clothe the mind. You must learn to live with the thought that my mind and body should be useful to the home and the country. We have to struggle harder for our goal than the fishermen did for Jesus.

How and why is the Pournami fast observed? The Pournami fast is a single meal. On the day before Pournami, you should start the fast without using meat and fish. The next day, eat one meal after five o'clock in the evening. Those who take a single meal the day before can eat lunch the next day.

Pournami is a glorious day in every way. Between the dark fortnight and the bright fortnight, Pournami is more important. The dark moon (Karuthavavu) gained importance because of the ancestral offerings (Pithrubali). Brahmins celebrate the full moon (Veluthavavu). They made us do the opposite. Even that was made to be done in reverse.

Observing the fast on Pournami is mainly for the purification of ancestors. The offering on the dark moon day will not be effective for an ancestor who passed away as a devotee from among us. If there is a Guru, there is no need for any of these offerings.

In our lineage, more than two thousand families have performed ancestral purification. All of them should be considered as ashram families. All their other duties have been exempted with this - duties to ancestors, duties to gods, and the duty of charity. Now, what is meant by the duty of charity? No matter how good things you give to children sleeping on the porch by keeping them in the house, it is of no use. Just by giving charity, his karma will not be corrected. That will not be the duty of charity.

'Ottar' only likes to walk along the way. If we help such people, the result will be taking on their sinful power. Then, giving a share of what we cook is not charity. Some old families call it doing charity. It is not right to say that there is poverty today. But the fact is that there is no poverty today. It is hard to think that there is even a single house today where gruel is not made.

As mentioned earlier, more than two thousand families have performed ancestral purification. Out of that, at least five hundred families were unable to make gruel. But that is not the situation today. A lot of changes have taken place. Despite all this, our previous experience - worship in the Trimurti system - has not changed. The drumbeating of the Varnasrama Dharma has not changed. The caste thought that followed it has not changed. Now caste thought is retreating inward. Caste has now become political. What is the reason for this caste to come?

I was born in the fortress of caste. I have experienced that difficulty to some extent since my young age. Why did the Vaikom Satyagraha take place? It was for the freedom to walk on the road outside the temple. This caste is that harsh. Even today in North India, Harijans are burned to death. Fearing this, people became Christians and Muslims. Someone who could not bear to see this came to power. C.P. Ramaswami Iyer.

The Congress was formed around that time. Because of that, there was a slight benefit. The Vaikom Satyagraha gained strength. That is the achievement of Gandhiji's Congress. C.P. did a trick to create communal solidarity. Not out of love for the poor, but out of fear that the Hindu community would perish. Here, the NSS and SNDP were strengthened. Shankar and Mannam were called and advised. Some Ezhavas were told to make Shankar their leader. All this was done to break the Congress. C.P. was certain that when the community became strong, the Congress would collapse. By making speeches at a place near the church in Thalayolaparambu (through K.R. Narayanan), Nairs and Ezhavas were turned back. Although I was very young, it was not difficult to understand all this. None of this was done to improve the community.

How much is the Congress today? Everything is in the name of caste. If you look closely at all this, it is not difficult to see that caste has not changed, but has turned in a different way. Every party is a fortress of caste. As a result, the culture has degraded. This is why politicians oppose spirituality. Mahatmaji had both. But his successors lacked that culture. Independence was achieved through Gandhiji's sacrifice. What did these successors achieve after independence? Leaders without self-culture brought anarchy to the culture. Politicians will not allow self-culture to develop. Because once self-culture comes, politics cannot survive. The revolutionary faction in politics are those who foster anarchy.

What is our path? There are good people in every section. In Hindus, Christians, Muslims, in politics, everywhere. Bring all of them together and lead them to this culture. That is the wealth of the country and the world. We can understand where we have failed historically and traditionally only when we reach this richness. Everyone who comes here should strive for that. When you give food to your children, when you send them to school, they should be convinced of this. The shortcomings that have come to us traditionally can only be resolved through generations. Cultural knowledge should be ready to be received in whatever way it is imparted. That knowledge should not be used for any personal gain. There are great intentions behind this. What is happening here is something none of you have seen or heard. It can be fully brought about through your children. This is something no acharya has ever said or done before. The foundation of that is the Guru Pooja conducted here. Do you know what is actually being done here in the name of Guru Pooja?

Guruvani
