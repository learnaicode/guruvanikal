July 1992

What is the general understanding of an ashram? In the past and present general understanding, ashrams are of four types -- Yoga, Bhakti, Karma, and Jnana. These four form the basis of the earth's culture itself. There might have been many other related characteristics to this.

The course of action of householders should be viewed with great importance. Because they are the ones who should be helpful in the Karma Kanda. Brahmacharis and devotees are very few. They should only be that much. They are guides. Therefore, it is enough if their number is small. But householders are not like that. Sannyasins and devotees all have to emerge from householders. Therefore, it is the householders who need to become good.

A center is essential to become good. Religious principles are said to be uniform. But customs and rituals are different. Nothing should be denied. Only by respecting everything can the essence of each be understood. The essence of various religious principles is the same. This is what I have learned without a doubt through my experienced life.

Some people deny the temple system. They can deny it. We also do that, don't we? Blind denial will do more harm than good. Denial should be with cause-and-effect discrimination; it should be by compiling and summarizing the scientific awareness and essences in religious principles. Sage Charvaka rejected many things taught by the Vedas with logic. It is not enough to say that observing and experimenting through self-experience are the methods suitable for scientific examination. The essences known through examination must be compiled, summarized, and established through action.

The Hindu religious code or culture is very vast. Christians and Muslims in India, and almost all religious groups, separated from Hinduism. They separated due to the excess of hardship they had to suffer from it. That means Hinduism went wrong somewhere. But the mistake that happened to Hinduism is God's will. Very few people know this. But even those who know do not act with determination. We know about the Varna Ashrama Dharma. Since the beginning of the world, we have been undergoing changes according to the times. The people of the Satya Yuga were like gods who could see and pray to God in person. When the Satya Yuga passed, the times also changed. How many Chatur Yugas and Manvantaras have passed! One Manvantara is seventy-one Chatur Yugas. Looking at this, a human's time is so insignificant. Within this insignificant period, we can acquire only a very small amount of knowledge.

Although a human's time is very short, we should at least be able to know the harm caused by the Varna Ashrama Dharma during that time. A Shudra has no place in the Varna Ashrama Dharma. But there is a Shudra in every home. There is no point in denying it. He worked innocently for everyone. What reward has God kept for his innocence?

The Brahmin knew. His was the right of Kali, the ruling authority. That is why he even gave the position of army commander. Because of the kings' punishment, the Shudra could not escape from the bottom. By beating, the entire power of sin was placed on his head. Even the Shudra becoming a king is according to the Brahmin's rules. Even the king who is passed under the belly of a golden cow, tied with a thread, and raised to the status of a Brahmin is not prescribed the Vedas. Only Murajapam could be heard. Can the subjects of such a king hear the Vedas? In the Varna Ashrama Dharma, the Shudra was caught and made a king. But he has no knowledge. This ignorant king is the visible god! Thus the people became ignorant and bound. Buddha came to change this. Before that, at the beginning of Kali, Krishna came. Opposed everything that existed. Said Sambhavami Yuge Yuge.

Now how difficult is it to make the images of Kali in Kali Yuga into brilliant people who are Yuga Karma Yogis. The situation is that there are only dark-skinned white men molded by the British in India. These poor people completely imitated the British. Not only dress and language, but even governance is their imitation. Ours is a tradition where there were kings who only obeyed what the Viceroys said. We have neither our culture nor our religion. Even for one who has studied philosophy, our culture is just stories. And the Westerner's is history. Thus we are slaves in every way.

In Kali, there is no place for God. In Kali, the place is for the Guru. How many great souls came and told this truth! First Moses came. When he said that he was speaking the word received from God, the world mocked him. Two thousand years later Jesus came. When he was crucified, he showed his form to the one who believed. Even though they were stubborn and had to suffer severe punishments, they worked with determination. Those who worked multiplied doubly. The Jews were exhausted in front of their sacrifice. Christians have that tradition even today. They were able to establish the world's government. Among them, they came to India and did a favor. If Madan becomes Mathai, he can walk on the main road. We swallowed his good and bad alike. We imitate them in every way. When he came here and grew his hair without getting peace, every beedi roller and boatman here started growing their hair.

Our country has come to such a bad state because our mothers became ignorant. Our Pitru has no purity. Children were born in the family according to the zodiac sign in which the Pitru stands. Children with intelligence and logic will be born in a family that has purified the Pitru. In that field, Brahmins are always at the forefront. The entire Shudra lineage became that of drunkards. Even if he is knowledgeable, he will be perverse. But the Brahmin did another extreme thing. Three are Sambandham. One is Veli. The one in the Brahmin's house is Veli. All others are Sambandham. To complete the karma he does, he needs the woman he married through Veli. Or if not, he will show Yonimudra and do Tantra. We are spit in his Mantra and Tantra, right. What knowledge can be imparted in a lineage that has degraded to this level?

No Acharya has come who has done Pitru Shuddhi in its completion of Karma. Shankaracharya did Vedic. Even Buddha said that if you just believe, everything will change. We should know that there are many Pitrus who have reached the position of Buddha. Pitrus are filled in all subtle realms. These inner dwellers are the ones who killed Jesus. Pitru domination has been established in that position. Pitrus rule the position of all the Acharyas who have risen.

The light that Buddha saw, the ray of light mentioned in the Quran, the tongue of fire in the language of Christians - whatever religions have described that consciousness as, all of it is here. The children here see that conscious energy in all these ways. Ancestral purification (Pithrushudhi), which no one has done with complete purity so far, is being done here. The ritual performed here involves catching all the ancestors and bringing them into the Guru's light. If you do ancestral purification here, then you will have no ancestors. There will be no ancestral curse either. Then what you need to look into is how to grow the lineage. The result of what you do today will be useful after twenty-five years.

We do not deny anything. Each is right up to its own extent. But what is desirable for us today? Good children should be born in our families. Our children should have the knowledge to self-fulfill whatever we are running after. There should be good Karma Yogis, Jnanis, and Sanyasis in your home. You, who are the cause of such an excellent creation, should have a clear understanding of the ashram.

Guruvani
