[GV1:P1718] June 1987

[GV1:P1719] There exists a lack of familiarity among us regarding the reclamation of the *Trimurti* and *Rishi* traditions. We predominantly presume that *Rishi*s are merely worshippers of deities. We too subscribe to this same belief. Have we not relegated our women even to the level of servitude? We have become so debilitated by blind faith that we remain ignorant of this reality. How can we, operating under the misconception that this approach will benefit our nation, redirect it towards an alternative path? How can we transform individuals who harbor such convictions? You yourselves possess an intuition that this is insufficient. But how can it be transformed? Do you not require a definitive approach for that? It is due to our lack of inherent aptitude that we have failed to assimilate this.

[GV1:P1720] Many individuals have emerged among us. These include both those who exhibited mystical powers and those who did not. The majority comprise individuals who demonstrate occult powers invoking deities; those who prophesy through trance and frantic movements. Our prevailing notion is that blessings and curses are the only outcomes we can attain as rewards. Have we not been indoctrinated to believe that a mere vision of God is sufficient, signifying ultimate fulfillment? Is that not the rationale behind recounting the divine visions of Poonthanam and Bhattathiri? Even if one were to experience thousands of such visions, they hold no substantial value. This is because such phenomena can contribute nothing to the pursuit of Self-knowledge (*Atmavidya*). I am conveying a realization I attained during a period of profound distress. I was instructed to discard all these practices and proceed in a specific manner. It was only subsequent to acting upon that guidance that I attained salvation.

[GV1:P1721] If the *Treta Yuga* and *Dvapara Yuga* existed before the advent of the *Kali Yuga*, does not the *Kali Yuga* have its own *Dharma*? If *Satya Yuga*, *Treta Yuga*, and *Dvapara Yuga* possess it, *Kali Yuga* must also have a *Dharma*! How, then, do we practice the *Dharma* specific to *Kali Yuga*? Our flaw lies in not discovering the path and acting upon it with love and sacrifice. It is not about claiming to have discovered it and sitting somewhere for five or ten years. We have not learned how to impart this to our children through sacrifice. We remain contented with blessings, curses, vows, and ritual offerings.

[GV1:P1722] Experience is what is required first. If someone discovers it, we must hold onto that discovery and progress. It may not be possible for everyone to discover the truth. Such a path does not reach us. This is due to the historical errors of the *Shudra* class from time immemorial. Should not this error be rectified, thereby generating merit, leading to fortune, and ultimately culminating in fruition? Do you assume this can merely be plucked effortlessly? This realization must manifest within us. This is its designated path. It cannot be attained by citing any other justification. Furthermore, it cannot materialize except through you, the householders. Regarding the roles of wife, husband, and children, as long as self-knowledge does not awaken within them, what is the meaning in labeling their children as bad if they grow up aimlessly? We are unable to take this up with absolute determination. This must somehow be integrated into our being.

[GV1:P1723] There are two paths - the *Rishi* path should be understood as the path of sacrifice. Instead, one should not adopt the mystical paths of deities or the paths of ascetics who perform miracles. What is to be done with that? Do not try to alter it yourselves. The individual you accept must possess the capability to transform it. Otherwise, it will be like holding the leg of someone who is hanging onto a palm frond. One must grasp it with conviction. One must be convinced that this person is experienced. There is no use in saying things like 'I saw Sri Krishna' or 'I saw all the deities'. This is not spiritual truth. All this may be true on one level. But it is not spirituality. Why did I say that? Did I not tell you earlier that I traversed the path where all this was seen and guided? This is not suitable ٹ. And then I stated that it became correct only after I took an alternative route and reclaimed it, did I not? That is the path of sacrifice. This is the *Rishi* path. This *Rishi*, this guiding force is not subordinate to any god, goddess, or the trinity known as Brahma, Vishnu, and Maheshwara; they are referred to as the *Kalantara Guru* (Guru across time). They highlight the rights and wrongs that occur over time and make us the inheritors of it. Thus, are you not the rightful heirs? Through your life, you are conveying this to your wife and children. It is that wife and children who will accomplish something meaningful for this world.

[GV1:P1724] Who all emerge from your household? Have not all the paths that exist—be it that of the *Yogi*, the *Karmi*, the *Jnani*, or the *Bhakta*—originated from the hands of this householder (*Grihastha*)? Once goodness emanates from them, is there a need for another individual to come and do something for this land or country? This path is not in our hands; we claim we lost everything because 'they' invaded or 'others' invaded—but who paved the way for 'them' and 'others' to invade? Was it not our own ancestors? Because they invaded, we at least attained literacy. Otherwise, even this would not have happened. Having understood at least this much, you must develop a steadfast determination for this cause.

[GV1:P1725] Today, there are more than a hundred families capable of speaking the truth openly. There are householders who can even explain what these Trimurti are and what is within them. There are indeed people who can explain who a *Jnani* (the enlightened) is and what the greatness of the Gurus is.

[GV1:P1726] Today is Kali Yuga. Kali has a specific dharma. Can we, who are born in Kali Yuga, live by abandoning it? We must adopt an approach wherein we accomplish everything while remaining within Kali. Do our own people not say so? In Kali Yuga, the chanting of the Lord's name (*Namajapa*) is sufficient. Whose name? What kind of name? They do not know how to answer this. If we consider the current practices, have they not chanted millions of times? Are they not chanting billions of times? And what is the result? Whoever embodies fortune in Kali Yuga; there is another expression for it — having done all these...

[GV1:P1727] Residing at the foot of a tree, performing penance,

[GV1:P1728] One must dedicate their idol in the service of the sages.

[GV1:P1729] In such service to the sages, it means that one should minister to them and follow their path. If we do this, it will bear fruit. We might lack self-realization. That does not matter. We must at least be capable of doing this.
