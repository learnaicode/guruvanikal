[GV1:P0133] August 1977

[GV1:P0134] Dear ones,

[GV1:P0135] I had mentioned a few things about the greatness of sacrifice last time. However, what I have to say does not end there. There are many things I have gained from experience and understood by seeing and hearing. I have a strong desire to share them with you one by one. I feel that only then will you be able to understand its essence at least to some extent.

[GV1:P0136] No matter how agonizing the problems I faced were, I tried to handle things with great equanimity. As a result, my problems were resolved. I can assert with certainty that there has never been a situation where they remained unresolved. To be more specific, I grew up in a desert. I did not collapse in its terrible heat. I moved forward every moment by gathering strength. Had I felt a little mental distress back then and given up everything, I would not have reached anywhere today. I would have been destroyed, entangled in the web of my own problems. Regardless, I do not know how I was able to gather such inner strength during those times.

[GV1:P0137] Seeing this approach of mine, many well-wishers have given me various pieces of advice. They have tried to dissuade me from many of my actions. But even as I listened to all their advice, I continued to forge ahead on my own path. I want to clarify one thing here. We should not displease anyone. They might be saying such things with good intentions. However, accepting the good aspects of what they say while striving to find our own path is absolutely essential for success in life. We may perhaps not get its results very quickly. Even so, there is no doubt that the results will definitely be attained over time.

[GV1:P0138] I started speaking about sacrifice. All the noble souls who have existed in the world have achieved sacrifice through their actions. Only they have been able to do something eternally lasting here. Look at the lives of great figures like Jesus Christ, Prophet Muhammad, and Buddha. You can find a vast abundance of sacrifice there. Why, were those lives not the very embodiment of sacrifice itself? We need not look elsewhere. Just take a look at Mahatma Gandhiji, who shines brightly as the apple of our India's eye. How incredibly magnificent is the message imparted by that life, which evolved through the greatness of sacrifice.

[GV1:P0139] Was he not able to bring a superpower, which ruled over a vast nation, to its knees with a mere message of peace? I do not think anything like that has ever happened anywhere else in the world. Nor do I think such greatness could be achieved by anyone other than someone from India. Indians are people who have evolved through such a great deal of tolerance and tradition. Their life is one of friendship and peace. No power in the world has been able to surpass that.

[GV1:P0140] Great souls have taken birth in this land of India in every era. Through their powerful sacrifices, they have bestowed vitality and life upon the entire human race. We must understand this clearly. That alone has endured, and that alone will endure in the future. Sree Narayana Guru is another great soul who brought about change to a certain extent through sacrifice. After Sree Sankara Bhagavatpada, it is difficult to find another Karma Yogi who brought about such a massive social revolution in our India. His supremely sacrificial life must be given its due value. If we can perform such a sacrifice or cultivate a discipline rich in sacrifice, there is no greater service than that.

[GV1:P0141] This does not necessitate that we must rise to the level of those great souls. Their sacrifices attained a grand stature. Therefore, we need not be bewildered by the thought that we cannot even peek into that realm. It is through their relentless effort and readiness that they reached that supreme position. We should not retreat just by thinking about it. If we try to advance at least a little in that richness of sacrifice, we will receive its corresponding reward. The only thing is that its measure will be strictly in accordance with that sacrifice. Even so, there is no doubt that it will indeed culminate in success in life.

[GV1:P0142] Then there is another aspect as well. Let me say that it is somewhat spiritual. It concerns the trajectory of our karma. Some people will not move forward no matter how hard they try, whereas some only need to try a little to reach their intended position. What is the reason for this? We can see many people feeling mentally pained thinking about it. One thing can be pointed out there. It is the variations in karma. One group of people might be striving in a state where the karmas or sacrifices performed in past lives are eagerly waiting for their completion. Thus, they achieve results very quickly. For others, the trajectory of karma might not have been completed in their past lives. There, this trajectory of karma can be completed only through sacrifice. Moreover, if we state the reason for soaring to great heights, it is because that person's very birth in this life is destined for it. They would have fulfilled all its aspects by performing sacrifices in their past lives itself. For such people, just a little effort is enough to attain it. So, one thing to remember is that the elevated position currently attained is the consequence of those good deeds practiced over multiple lifetimes.

[GV1:P0143] The karmic trajectories of lifetimes take various forms. Many people do not seem to believe in it. It is good for us to remember one thing. If we take the different kinds of vicissitudes of life and analyze them, we will be convinced of that absolute truth. Many have to lead different kinds of lives. A blind child is born in the house of a rich man. In some places, children are born with stunted limbs. In other places, deformed births occur. We need to see and pay attention to all these things. What is all this? Is this not the resurrection of the cursed elements from past lives? How can we not believe these things? Thus, it implies that the baseness and good deeds of past lives follow us. There are many reasons why we emphasize this. And this is indeed something that others should reflect upon.

[GV1:P0144] Now, let me say a little about the general life of human beings!

[GV1:P0145] When a human being becomes sorrowful, they forget everything and wander around seeking refuge. In prosperity, they forget everything. That is a common trait. However, one must be able to cultivate a presence of mind that remains unshaken and unwavering under any circumstance. For that, they need to remember the Controller of this universe. They must act knowing that. Only then will sacrifice emerge in them. And only that will attain a permanent state.

[GV1:P0146] All the great sacrificers the world has seen today are those who have worked unceasingly while standing behind that power. We can all clearly know what they have achieved as a result of it. People continue to follow their path. They find solace in it. Thus, their words inspire us to lead a contented life.

[GV1:P0147] I spoke about the life of Jesus Christ earlier, didn't I? Was that life itself not offered up for the sake of sacrifice? What could be greater than that? Even in those final moments, he said, "Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do." Do the words of that great man, who showcased that greatness by sacrificing even his own life, not echo everywhere even today? The case of the great Buddha is no different from this. Is he not the magnanimous soul who renounced all the royal pleasures of this world and set out for the welfare of the people? Who can lay claim to the magnificence of that sacrifice? How incredibly great have its achievements become! Thus, we can see that all the great souls who achieved immortality in life were the very embodiments of sacrifice.

[GV1:P0148] Here, the foundation of our material life also requires such a sacrifice. If we wish to start any movement, that too requires sacrifice. The success of that institution will depend on the degree of this sacrifice. If we examine the lives of many people who have risen to prominence here today, we can understand this fact. But if the seeds of ego fall into their path of thought, we can see that not only the person but also the movement they built gets destroyed. So, when moving forward towards a goal through sacrifice, we must acquire all the knowledge related to it. What is that knowledge? What is it for? Ego dwells in the human mind. Therefore, the first thing needed is the removal of ignorance. Only that will lead to success. What is needed for that? We must understand the life perspectives of great people. We should think about it. Then, we must try to put it into practice. If we try step by step like this, we can reach our intended destination. That systematic life is, in a way, a sacrifice. We should strive for that. Perhaps many of you would like to know what its benefits are. Firstly, liberation from our karmic bonds. That leads to merging with God. Another is life's satisfaction. We will attain both of these. Through that, we will be led to the ethereal supreme state. What more does a human birth need than that?

[GV1:P0149] Guruvani
