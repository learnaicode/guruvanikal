[GV1:P2404] July 1989

[GV1:P2405] The visionary first contemplates the means by which this earth should be made suitable for human habitation. Instead of traveling to various other planets or engaging in actions that degrade humanity, the visionary initially discovers the measure and weight of a karma within this solar system. That is, they assigned a specific duration regarding the epochs. Based on the knowledge obtained then, a determination was made in the language of that time. How? Manvantara! By assigning a duration of fourteen Manvantaras, specifying that a deluge happens in such a manner within it; this is our calculation.

[GV1:P2406] Disciple: Do others not possess this calculation?

[GV1:P2407] Guru: Others do not have the calculations of the planetary spheres, do they? Then how will they determine the calculations? That is why it was said earlier that this is a sacrifice. There is no one who has made sacrifices like Jesus. It is true that he sacrificed. However, after contemplating and understanding the merits of the actions taking place in various parts of the world, there was no time to think about what was required for them. Otherwise, one would have to be an innate sage like Krishna. Krishna is a sage by birth.

[GV1:P2408] Why was Kamsa, who knew in advance that Krishna would come, prepared to kill his own sister's children one by one? He possessed knowledge about this world to a certain extent. He too had some wisdom. Why were you born? You were born to do such things: because you are a Shudra born in the Yadava clan. Kamsa, who received a disembodied voice declaring that authority in the Yadava clan was given to him to rule in such a manner, was not an ordinary man. He was someone who had achieved foresight to a small degree. What is the meaning of the disembodied voice? I have to impart some guidance through you, who has ascended to the position of an emperor. What is that guidance? This Shudra path must be cleared. For that, an administrative system must first be prepared.

[GV1:P2409] A Shudra has the right to rule. It was there in all eras. The claim that they have no right was written down later. In those days, there were kings from all castes. In every era, everyone had the authority to rule and to have governance conducted. Krishna's arrival was to correct all of this. But what happened when that Krishna was tied to the dharma of the Hindu, or rather, the Deva? It turned into a path of war between the Dravidians and the Aryans. Because the old worship was not changed, the power of the Dravidian concept weakened; he intelligently introduced the concept of the Vaishnava and studied the sciences that existed here. The Aryan knew that the era of the Dravidian, whose conceptual power had diminished, was over. The Dravidian remained entangled in Shaivism and Shaktism. In this context, the Vaishnava applied a cunning intellect, studied all these things from here, and made it seem as if the Sanskrit language belonged exclusively to him. He even specifically wrote and created a Samhita. When the Prakrit language was refined into the Sanskrit language, that language became exclusively his own.

[GV1:P2410] Where did this Brahmin come from? A hymn was even composed to establish that they settled here because a celestial body akin to the sun guided their path. That precisely constitutes his prayer on the day of the Avani festival: "We bow to you who granted us the opportunity to reside here"—this is the essence of the Surya Gayatri mantra. From this very fact, one can deduce that he is not indigenous to this land, but rather migrated from the Middle East.

[GV1:P2411] Disciple: You mentioned Shaivism! Was Shiva a guru?

[GV1:P2412] Guru: That is how Shiva is understood. Shiva was not born of a womb (*yoniputra*); rather, it denotes a position. He was a conceptual son (*sankalpaputra*). His origin is not articulated anywhere, whereas the origins of all other entities are explicitly stated. There is no mention of Shiva's genesis. Only Shiva and Parvati existed here. By studying this and recognizing it as belonging to the Treta and Dvapara yugas, he adopted the philosophical path. Thus, the Vaishnava concept was embraced.

[GV1:P2413] Disciple: Is Vishnu a different concept?

[GV1:P2414] Guru: Yes.

[GV1:P2415] Disciple: Is he a conceptual son as well?

[GV1:P2416] Guru: Not a conceptual son in essence, but rather contrived to become one. His conceptualization portrays him resting his arm in the Ocean of Milk. Consequently, he too was transformed into a conceptual son. Everything was uniformly depicted as being permeated by Vishnu (*Vaishnavamayam*). Amidst such conceptualizations, the ignorant must inevitably attain knowledge at some juncture! When might that occur? That realization will unfold in the Kali Yuga. From the onset of the Kali Yuga, this knowledge reached the Shudra as well. The doctrine imparted was that the Vaishya and Shudra are destined to labor, while the other two classes reap the benefits. This is the very history that has been fabricated and inscribed. Furthermore, there is an Advaita philosophy that proclaims all this to be one. This is fundamentally a deception. We must now examine how this was introduced through such conceptualization.

[GV1:P2417] Guruvani
