Guruvani January 1992

When devotion arises, a special joy is attained. There is a specific way to increase that joy. At that time, if those without devotion come near, not only will we be unable to increase the devotion growing within us, but it will also fade away. Their tendencies will also enter us. To carry forward the growing devotion without interruption, many sacrifices have to be endured. People who have endured such sacrifices are generally rare in our lineage. Without the inherited nature of devotion increased through sacrifice, it is impossible to achieve material gains. Thus, we have become lacking in both devotion and material gains.

When true devotion comes, there will be no hesitation to do any work. Action is a special quality that stands alongside devotion. Whatever is done, there will be no laziness. There is a special joy in working at that time. This is because one sweats through the work. That is good. But along with it, some kind of emotion or agitation might arise. Agitation should not occur there. Great humility must also be shown. Only through that will our karma cycle be broken. A great love for everything will also be felt.

When true devotion develops, one rises slowly. One behaves only gently. Along with that, many unusual intellects will blossom. It is an intellect capable of doing everything necessary. The karma created in devotion, if not exhausted by performing it, cannot be untied even after many births. It will also follow like a divine curse.

When devotion arises, one should pray to God, surrendering, 'You gave me this much devotion, I could forget everything and get closer to you. But the next moment you also give me vile natures capable of destroying all that; what should I do about it?'. Along with praying like this, if one sincerely desires the defects to change, there will be a lot of crying. If one cries like this ten times, we will have very big changes, and along with it, a defect.

Once there was a sannyasi. A sannyasi whose logic and intellect blossomed through devotion. He had a defect. A prostitute lived next door. Even though she was a prostitute, she had a virtue. She wouldn't speak ill of anyone. She only thought that she was worse than everyone else. She is one who does wrong. The sannyasi had a thought, I will drive her away from here. He would speak ill of this prostitute to everyone. But because of this hatred of the sannyasi, he was destroyed.

To know the reason why he was destroyed like this, this sannyasi approached a guru. The guru said, 'Hatred is black magic. Your power of penance was lost because of black magic. But that prostitute is not like that. Except that such a karma happened to her in some course of karma, she is one who thinks that the mind should always be kept good. God will have mercy on those who harbor a thought of repentance about it if a mistake is committed.'

When devotion comes, even the voice will acquire a pleasant soft rhythm. Avoiding noises, it will become calm and slow. When devotion comes, many imaginations will awaken and rise in us without our knowledge. In the initial stages, one can only cry and speak. After passing many such stages, all emotional agitations will die and a state of knowing nothing will arrive. These changes in state must be recognized with great purity. Otherwise, it will lead to the destruction of devotion itself.

Girls should always take special care to keep the other members in the house satisfied. Moderation should be maintained in everything - 'Mitamavyaya'. Along with the increase in devotion, the passion for action will also increase. Even so, it should be done moderately without making a fuss. If moderation is maintained, it can be done forever. Understanding this principle, always keep doing the good that we can do. It was mentioned earlier that many defects will also rise along with devotion. This is an opportunity to work while praying meltingly, 'Please remove these defects that occur to me.' If you want to make someone obey, you should speak while doing the action. Only if you do, you can make them do. If a fellow being makes a mistake, do not speak without tact. Approach them gently and speak with humility adding greatness, that is the best.

That is how I used to do it. Perhaps if someone did not obey, I would do their work along with my work. If there is any love in them, I must grab it. That is why I did this. Then there would be a love and along with it a grudge from them. Because of that grudge, I got plenty of opportunities to cry. That is my success.

I have always done everything with a smile to everyone. You can smile at boys and girls. Then they will only speak politely. I have experienced that smiling at boys of the same age is not so good. Because I treated everyone equally, those older than me disliked me. I have suffered many disadvantages from that.

As far as boys are concerned, they have no limitations to act. You don't need to show off in front of someone who is experienced in acting, he will see everything.

When devotion comes, a certain state without any feelings of hatred will also come. That is when bliss occurs. If you get to experience that bliss, it will never leave you. If you experience that ecstasy for at least two seconds, it is a great thing, it is worth that much.

Devotion is a special merit. Not everyone gets it. Devotion that arises out of sorrow for a wife or husband is not devotion. Devotion that brings ecstasy - that is a blessing. If that devotion is attained for three and three-quarters of an hour, it means a Samadhi has been attained. If a sensible person gets this, he will try to make it experienced. Devotion is a special thing that should be experienced. There are three states of experience of devotion: waking, dreaming, and deep sleep. Everyone has it. In this state of mind, the past, present, and future will be experienced intertwined. When experienced in this way, the feeling of having seen before, the feeling that is currently existing and working, and the feeling of merging into something will occur at the same time. After waking up, a habit of all this being imprinted in the mind will also come. Later, a state will come where words are obtained from it by making it manifest. Then it will start to be obtained through the respective lights. With that, the movements of life will be shown.

The waking-deep sleep state will lead to a state beyond consciousness. That state can be called Turiya. The state commonly referred to as Sanchara Samadhi is Turiya. Along with seeing the movements of life, Sanchara Samadhi in the Turiya state can also be awakened. But for that, a Guru is needed. The Guru must be an authorized person according to Brahmanishchaya to awaken Sanchara Samadhi. This is the realization of life mentioned above.

Where is the seat of our Prana? Prana resides in the Annamaya Kosha. The body is made of Annamaya Kosha. That is, Prana resides in the body. There is a space inside the heart. It is spread in the layers of the brain. That is where Prana is located. You who see and experience where Prana is located and what its course of action is, do not pay attention to the movements of Prana. Whatever your thought is engaged in, you do not think by giving it its complete form. Even the meat trade has its own form. Even if it is a tailor, after sewing, if there is any defect in it, he will understand what it is.

It was mentioned that a great person will get ecstatic bliss for three and three-quarters of an hour. The children here** get it like that for days. Yet they do not keep it. Because they did not fast for this. The children of the boss have a habit of not wanting whatever is given to them. But try giving it to a poor man's child. He will receive it with both hands extended. That is not the situation here. There is a desire to use this. But they do not know how much benefit there is.

This should be used by giving importance to one's vasanas (inherent tendencies). One must think and understand the defects of each person's karmic path. What all were experienced in previous births, how one lived, what all actions were performed - all these must be seen, thought about, and understood. The knowledge you get is to remove these karmic paths.

Light, its different colors, the frequency of each, how it merges - to show each of these. Along with that, you can also know about your karmic path.

Didn't I mention earlier that when devotion comes, various emotions will arise. If he is a renunciate, no matter what he has to face, he will stick to the subject of devotion. If not a renunciate, sometimes it might be lost. To prevent such loss, the Guru must be present. Once this state is reached, a medium is no longer necessary.

If you look at the sky, it is empty. What all exist there? Let the mind be as vast as the sky. Let it be as deep as the ocean. Whatever comes into the mind, it cannot maintain a permanent thought process on one thing. Wherever we sit, past mischiefs tend to come. Future mischiefs also come. But in this, the one you like the most will remain permanent. But through the medium you have accepted, even that can be overcome. It can only be overcome through that. Therefore, a medium is essential.

Guruvani
