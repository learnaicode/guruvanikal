GURUVANIKAL · December 1992 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0432 to GV2:P0447
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

December 1992

What is done in Santhigiri under the name of Gurupooja is a karma that removes all ancestral doshas and merges them with God. Until today, no one has performed such a Gurupooja. Muslims perform this karma under the name of Ratheeb and Christians under the name of Anatha Chollikkuka. What Hindus do in this regard is Pithrubali. What Buddha did for this is not clearly recorded. What Sankaracharya and Narayana Guru did is a karma in the Hindu model. But no one has been able to perform ancestral purification in a way that catches the devil beyond heaven.

Will you believe if I say there is a devil beyond heaven? This is a very big subject. There is a section known as Yogabhrashtanmaar. Those who have attained power through penance and stand apart. These are the ones who harm the world. No one has been able to see them through any medium until today. Their position is between the devil and God. They have practiced Hamsayoga, strengthened the air in the body, and mastered the nature of flying. Therefore, it is impossible for anyone to cross them and reach Brahmaloka.

These Yogabhrashtanmaar are those who have conquered even Brahma. They will harm anyone who tries to enter Brahmaloka.

Gandharvan is such a Yogabhrashtan. Sree Rama could not conquer him. It is a historical truth that Sree Rama had to retreat saying that a Guru coming in Kaliyuga will destroy you. It was he who gave Jesus to death. The Prophet, looking at him, cried out and said, Alas! Jinn rule the world. Even if he comes in a body, no one has been able to recognize him so far. But we know the history of this Gandharvan clearly here.

All the karmas happening here are beyond intellect and thought. That is, whatever the divine will is, it happens according to the will of that great power. Everything happening here was received here as a disembodied voice years ago. This can only be called the glory of that great power. Everything that happens in this world is in that concept. Anything will happen only according to that will.

When Moses said that words would be received from God, the people opposed. Giving the same idea, God gave Jesus, who had no qualification or dignity to speak of among the people, to the world as a guide, born two thousand years later. God caused Buddha and the Prophet to be born in the same way. But the world did not accept them. Even after that, the world did not accept anyone who came according to God's will. The result? Giving place to the Vedic, caste and class differences came and everything was destroyed. This is the cause of the greatest tragedy of the world's human society.

This great dosha has been following humanity for twenty-five Chaturyugas. To free humanity from this dosha in this Kaliyuga itself, two thousand four hundred and forty-four

gave birth to gurus. All these gurus had a special rule based on experience. What Christians call 'tongue of fire' and Muslims call 'Noorhan' is this special rule. Hindus call it a voice from heaven, vision, light, etc. This is generally the special rule we have, but ours has another special feature added to it. Whatever we ask, we will get an answer from God.

You are the ones who know, see and hear this. If you have a determination to live thinking at least a little about this movement (idea), you can remove the devil that torments the world. You can put an end to the killing and murder happening in the world.

There is one thing that the family members who perform Guru Pooja must fully know. Along with purifying the ancestors, the fallen yogis between the devil and God are also being caught and removed. Therefore, the grandfather will not mislead your lineage. It is the harm done to you from your zodiac signs that is being removed with this. After twenty-five years, the result of this will begin to be seen. Can anyone destroy the coming generation like this?

The souls of the liberated ancestors will be born in your lineage for good. Thus, the children born after purifying the ancestors are those born embracing this change. By the time the children born embracing this change after nineteen seventy-three put this idea into practice, the torment of the devil mentioned earlier will disappear from the world itself. Those who pay attention can see that every child born after purifying the ancestors has special characteristics in character from birth.

Once women come here, they will stop bad practices. Men too. Its goodness will spread to the neighboring house as well. When that goodness begins to come, there will be opposition in every way. I have imparted to you as knowledge a determination to endure it. The harm of not imparting such knowledge has happened in the world along with other torments.

It has been mentioned many times that our ashram has some special features from other ashrams. It is not just sannyasa that happens here. But as far as sannyasa is concerned, its subtle nature is here. Anyone can achieve it. It does not require any special birth qualification. If there is the determination, dispassion, goodness and along with it sacrifice of a sannyasi, anyone can achieve it. A householder needs not only that, but something more. Is it available from the hidden cover you have traditionally embraced? If we evaluate Hindu customs themselves, aren't there diversities? What should be done to bring from diversities to unity, or even if thought about, nothing has happened.

Now let me say something about myself. I am not a person who desired this much. Everything came without my knowing. Although I have tried to make you understand this truth I have known in many ways, you have still not understood it. But it cannot be said that there is no slight difference in those who believe here. Isn't it comforting that thoughts of religion, class and caste have decreased a little? Along with that, a desire to be united has also come. Before this, Shankaracharya, Chattampi Swami and Narayana Guru were born in Kerala itself and worked for this. (Because of the scandal about paternity and the lack of reins of Brahmin hegemony, Shankaracharya had to work in North India.) But unity could not be established. Because followers

Instead of making a fuss over it, they did not bother to accept the idea, nor did they make any sacrifices. In that place, they gave importance to miracles and tricks.

We have started the effort to take up the sacrifice in the idea here. God gave us a way for that. That way should be useful. Anyway, the world definitely needs a correction. Even if born for ages, one may not have the fortune to see and know this. But this is a fortune for you. The fortune that gives the acquired karma that is useful to remove the devil that harms the world. Even if education, wealth, and lordship are lost, this fortune will always be with you without ever fading. If three generations do not make a mistake, this will become the total idea of the children of the world. If you make a mistake in this, God's will will have to be implemented through another birth in the fourth generation. This is what I have to inform you.

Guruvani
