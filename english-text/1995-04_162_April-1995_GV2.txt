April 1995

Question: I have often heard you speak about the Pournami Vrata. Can you explain it once more?

Answer: There are many vratas (vows/fasts) among Hindus. Among them, the Pournami Vrata is for the goodness of the soul. It is not for achieving great financial status. However, when goodness of the soul comes, financial status may follow. Without the goodness of the soul, whatever status one achieves, none of it is permanent. Moreover, it will turn into distortion.

For a long time, we have been talking about births and acquiring the merit of births. There are those who ask what births are. There is no need to ask whether there are births or not. Because the very existence of a visionary proves it. A visionary foresees for a long time, doesn't he? Then is there even a need for the question of whether there are births?

The karma of Hindus stands in the Trimurti system. For a long time, there has been no karma in this path to know and act whether there is anything beyond that. But earlier it existed. It is not in the Hindu doctrine, it is in the doctrine of the Manu lineage. But what Manu is, what the lineage of Manu is, or what each of us belonging to the lineage of Manu is, no such information is found among Hindus to know. Then scholars or similar other people say that there is mental stability and then do some storytelling. What do we need from this, or what does someone else need, has not been seen and told in the light of the times. Nor has it been told what has come and gone. The times referred to are about the Chaturyuga - Krita, Treta, Dwapara, Kali. The very last period is Kali. We have been taught that Kali is bad and that it will lead to destruction. Kali should not be taken lightly. It has special qualities and can qualify for liberation. We have known such a Kali to be bad. How will we change this perception of ours about Kali, how will we grow? How will we take it up to the doctrine? None of this has been received from ancestors or Vedic scholars. It is not that they do not know, or rather, they can only give what has reached them. We have passed millions of years. History reaches a maximum of five thousand, six thousand, seven thousand, eight thousand years. Can anything be known from an account that reaches the vision of such a short period?

But according to the Hindu calculation, a Chaturyuga itself is twelve thousand divine years. One divine year is three hundred and sixty-five human years. In such a long Chaturyuga, what all did we do, what all did we not do? Who will we ask this? Which Mahatma will tell us? No one could tell us. Because the attempt has been to know by standing within the Trimurti system.

Question: What is the shortcoming of the Trimurti system?

Answer: Five thousand two hundred years ago, Krishna came. A birth greater than Krishna's has not occurred on earth. Such a Krishna has been hidden behind curious stories and dirty stories, making it impossible to truly know him. We have not been introduced to the greatness of Krishna.

Another example of our ignorance is the story of Kamsa. We have been taught that Kamsa is wicked. But Kamsa is not wicked. Kamsa stood in the situation where the light of the times spread. The author of the Gita says that dharma is directly perceivable.

The king was present during wartime in the very manner of how a king should be. In Kali Yuga, we see this in the Yadava clan. This Yadava is a Shudra, belonging to the fourth class among the four Varnas. The Vedanta that teaches us to keep the Shudra at a distance is what we have been taught. Shudra does not mean a Shudra by caste. There is a Shudra in the house of a Brahmin, a Kshatriya, a Vaishya, and a Shudra. Similarly, there is a Kshatriya, a Brahmin, and a Vaishya in every house. We do not know this. Because we do not know about this Shudra class and Shudra Dharma, we also suffer the misfortune of not knowing about Kali Dharma.

Many great souls have struggled to remove this misfortune. But because we are ignorant of where our ancestors or we went wrong or made a mistake, the Dharma in the special actions performed by men, women, young, and old has been lost to the point where we do not know what it is. This loss will never become irreparable for anyone. This is because we are not ready to go beyond the Trinity system. Without going beyond the Trinity system, or at least reaching the state of the Lord of the Ganas, one cannot attain liberation. This is what the mind system says. We have no information about this mind system.

This is the time of Vaivasvata Manu. Vaivasvata Manu is the seventh Manu. The current time is the Kali Yuga of the twenty-eighth Chaturyuga in the seventh Manvantara. Which one should we take? The Trinity system or the mind system? This is a subject that must be understood without any confusion between the classes. What work will we do for this? Whom will we ask?

Except for our ancestors saying many things and leaving, no one knows anything clearly. No scholar or Vedic priest has taught us anything. As a result of an action that has deteriorated due to these reasons, natural disasters occur and cause frequent destruction somewhere, but we have not been able to bring any good out of this. You must always think about this. Always think about what you should become through the actions that you and your children must perform. Otherwise, this is the destruction that each of you is building for your children. You will competitively develop science and technology more than necessary, and you yourselves will be destroyed one by one. You must also think that the world itself will shine and be destroyed by a flood of food. Let us give up this leap towards destruction and be ready to work for a better tomorrow.

God has given us a path to know all this through an action. This is not a work that tells you it is like this or it is not like that.

Question: How is the path given for us?

Answer:

The Dharma of Kali is good for all classes - Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Shudra. Brahmins should not be taken in the sense of those who perform Vedic rituals. It should be taken in the sense of 'Brahmajnaneti Brahmana'. We should abandon the myth that Brahmajnana will not shine in Kali Yuga and see Kali as a special period of time when the best time to make it shine and the opportunity to do so have come. This path of Dharma to be realized in worldly actions is given from Brahman. That is what is being told to you.

I have experienced a lot of pain and difficulties for this. When I say this, I can only say it briefly. Those of you who are interested, please inquire and find out about this.

Guruvani
