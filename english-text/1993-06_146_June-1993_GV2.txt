GURUVANIKAL · June 1993 · Volume 2
Malayalam source paragraphs GV2:P0512 to GV2:P0522
English of the whole month, in the translator's own paragraphs.

June 1993

Those who believe here will understand the secret and reality of human life, karma, and the reality of the form of karma. If one keeps their word without failing, the course of karma will become pure. This society does not have the nature of inquiring into and respecting anything. Generations to change this nature

You will have to. You must do at least a part of the purpose here. That is sacrifice. That sacrifice must come in every action. Only if there is a greatness will it be respecting this divine decree. The Guru's desire is to improve everyone. With a sense of duty, what the Guru commands, the disciple must obey. The dialogue between Krishna and Arjuna can be taken as an example.

You must have a realization that there are only perversions in the world, and we will change that. Those who are here are not knowledgeable about Hindu culture. Their assumption is that they have general knowledge. But that knowledge cannot be applied anywhere. That is why they are unable to accept and respect anything. They came from a land covered with snow blocks and faded glory and ruled us. The generations that came in between with the ignorant people who were here have gone completely wrong. A few devotees and monks who thought and suffered about the future and generations of this land - a few yogis who saw all this and cried out and laughed - who among these can we accept? This is also the reason why no one understands the things happening in this ashram.

When I received a message from God to do the prayer happening here, I did not understand then that there was such a big purpose behind it. It was only after asking and understanding the visions of fifty people that I began to understand its total sum. Each person's vision in each way according to the broadness of their minds, when evaluated together, I understood that God is enjoying watching a group of machine-like people with no knowledge operate to fully implement world justice. It will cause danger if you take such a big thing without giving it this kind of dignity. If not kept in that state, there is nothing more dangerous than this. These girls who got the opportunity to live here must protect this even by sacrificing their lives. Otherwise, its destruction will happen through them.

The existing perversion must be completely destroyed. You must have the ability to trample and muddy this perversion and dig a trench for it to flow away. Each resolution made here goes to the respective zodiac signs and is changing the snow accumulated there. But what has happened in the hearts of the people cannot be changed. What is in the subtle can be changed by the power of resolution. This process is happening in nine planets, twelve zodiac signs, and twenty-seven stars. All these changed things are being vomited on earth - like the dust particles of the moon falling on earth. If it continues as it is happening in this atmosphere today, you cannot even think about the danger that is going to happen to the earth in the future. Only the one with eyes can see. You must do this karma without failing in punctuality. Otherwise, the one who comes next will destroy it.

You have no knowledge about the Guru lineage. It is out of fear of the Siddhas that some contemporaries have done certain things. But it is not like that for you. You have got the opportunity to do it with a full heart. Yet you are unable to do it. Whatever changes are made in the atmosphere, you must act for that change to be useful to the people. The karma you do must be held in the atmosphere and come back as the nature of nature.

Until recently, there were poor people who worked for the lord, got the result, and stood submissively saying this is your rice. Even now there are such people in some places.

But there is another side to this today. The incidents of cutting down these lords also arise from nature. Every action has a reaction, doesn't it? Every action is merging into nature. The lotus grows in the mud. Nothing else will come out of something pure. It will only merge away. It cannot be taken and applied. This is the nature of creation since the beginning of the world.

Eras are changing. People do not have the knowledge to change according to the change of eras. When that happens, there will be some kind of natural disaster. Seeing this much, you could not see this. Then how will this idea be brought forward? One group says that everything will change if the bonds of caste are untied. Another group says that the trinity system is good. Yet another group says that everything exists within their religion. We have a consensus through experiences. Our chanting will fly and through the imagination in it, all baseness will catch fire and be destroyed from the subtle power in the universe. God's insistence is that this should be done by a group of poor people who cannot do this with value and knowledge. To cut the severity of the spiritual landlord, the trinity system, a revolutionary force in line with the idea of the Naxalite Guru lineage must awaken. You should not apply your own intelligence and cunning here and spoil this. This is a field where Buddha and Sri Shankara worked and lost. We should only think about how to act in that merger of whatever we see. The great souls are those who bathed at four o'clock when the snow was drinking and flowing in the river Ganges, and did penance by chanting in pain. Then it changed to mantras and tantras. The capacity of an ordinary person should work in the place fixed by doing penance and come down from there. Only if it comes like that, it will be right.

The word of the discoverer is logic. Someone should do that word with sincerity. Because it was ruined by doing action. It must be purified by doing action itself. The scoundrel has made mistakes through the scoundrel, the yogi through the yogi, and the sannyasi through the sannyasi. It is not easy to correct it. If the action is not through calmness, it will go astray. This is not a subject that any scientist will yield to.

Our asset is experience. The beginning will be difficult. According to the degree of sincerity, the difficulty will change. When praying, the first thing that happens is difficulty. You may not even feel like praying. Again interest, again fading - that's how it is. Just as one does not like to eat the same food every day, prayer also has changes. The reason is the deformities in one's own past lives. Other religions do not say this. Because it only has an elevation up to the status of a deity. The Quran says a lot about liberation. It is not complete. Because none of these religions have a history and antiquity of the length of eras. The Bible has a history of at most six thousand years. This is not enough to reach the secret of human life and the reality of the form of action. The field of complete knowledge is very, very far from all this. There are no artificialities there. There is no severity, purdah, and other forms.
