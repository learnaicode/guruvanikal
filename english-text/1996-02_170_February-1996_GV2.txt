February 1996

The mistake that has occurred due to our system of worship is very great. The malpractices that each of us is caught up in - celebrating these malpractices, showing them as special, our ignorance written, studied, and passed down through generations - the harm cultivated is even greater than this. What happened because of that? Class hatred emerged, and in everything, sowing this destruction as lord and slave, reaping it has become our lot. How can it be right to say that all this happened by the will of God? How can this be handled?

Today, we must think about where we stand in the act of worshipping and making others worship. For each religion, their own God stands great as the author of the Vedas. For each caste, the God belonging to their caste is great. The lower caste has no God. As a result of building slavery in this way, we lost the knowledge to worship God. This is the truth. So, we should at least have the knowledge that worshipping God like this is not enough. Understanding this, we need an introduction to recognize what God is, what a god and goddess are, what a sage is, what a sanyasi is, what a wise man is, what a karma yogi is, and what a devotee is. Can you be led straight by introducing all these one by one? In a way that it cannot be recognized why we were corrupted by being taught like this, we acquired vile knowledge by studying and teaching grammar and logic. Therefore, something came here that makes you unable to believe that the good knowledge was not what existed in our country.

We say that the first university in the world was in India (Nalanda, Takshashila). Thus, we must take a look at the state of the country. We have a wretched habit of not being able to think and understand all things like that. Therefore, if there is a familiarity among us of thinking and understanding or being liberated and understanding, we must try to understand by keeping it in the forefront. Otherwise, we will not be able to understand anything. Because you did not do so, you fell behind in the act of preserving knowledge. A historian or a person born only for the dharma of an era cannot preserve this knowledge.

In the act of preventing Kali, who is each of us, what is the reason for this knowledge, and how do we stand with the effect in that cause - we must reach this sense of action. If we speak highly while supporting the fallen historical objects, where will we end up? If we insist that the history since the time writing began is the truth, when will we be able to know about the Yuga Dharma and the Chatur Yuga? To know about the Chatur Yuga, it is impossible without the knowledge of the Yuga Dharma. First, one must know the dharma of the Chatur Yuga. After that, one must know the dharma of each Yuga.

How should the dharma of Kali Yuga, which stands at the fourth quarter of this Chaturyuga, be imparted? We have no information about this. Because we have lined up behind the custodian who lived by following someone else and made it into a science. The technologies that create today's war materials were learned from the logic called Nyayashastra. If aviation science was first developed in India, why couldn't India take it? Indian science has thus gone to many places. They are selling it for money. India, however, could not make anything, not even a pin. We have only had a history and a government that could only degrade us. What else have we been able to do here other than fight with each other? Have parents been able to give at least one truth that our future generations can respect? In this state, everyone has become one born in the lap of dead and decayed ignorance.

God has given you an opportunity to think about what is needed to change this. Today we have many good insights of modern science. We do not think about why it came about in this era. Or even if a habit of thinking develops, it is only capable of thinking about some histories and nothing else.

We have a path of dharma to inquire whether anything has extended beyond this stream of thought. Neither the people of this country nor the people of any country are able to take it. The result of this is the unrest seen today. The government has sown the seeds of destruction everywhere by playing and making others play. The people reap them. Historians also travel along that path but are unable to convince the people about its ravages. Moving forward in this state, we have no right to speak a truth about the world, the country, or ourselves. Whether educated or uneducated, a personality is one who can complete an action to see the truth during their lifetime.

What should those who are alive now do? Is it enough to pass on the rest of the story woven by our ancestors to our children? If so, we have nothing more to say than what our father and mother said. It is not in history either. Then where can we find it? Will we get it if the great uncle comes dancing?

The people of Ambalappuzha and Cherthala have a little more of this kind of work. There is a Satyagraha Ashram there. I think it is a school now. Why was it built? Why did your ancestors conduct satyagraha there? If they fought so much for the freedom to walk outside the walls of that temple, for the freedom to walk on the road, we can think how great we are. Instead, big stories have been fabricated. If we cut open the root of this ignorance we are holding onto, will we get any knowledge from it? This is what our contemporary history has shown us. We have studied that history without knowing its reality. You must think whether we need this history or not. If you feel you need an alternative, you must have a dharma for it, if not directly, at least by hearsay. And an idea to act on it. There must be a purpose of action that imparts mind and intellect. It will not work if you try to complete your life with any idea you get.

Each of you is a worker of day-to-day life. His loss, profit, digging up the roots of the working class - digging up the roots of the capitalist by making them pay wages without working - this is all the knowledge this world has given us. Similarly, what all works have been done as caste, religion, and class. Standing in this familiarity, any

Having learned about good things, what will you pass on to your children? How do you make children owners of a good personality? Have you ever thought about the reason for this? They took away all the good aspects from here. They developed it, made it into a creative science, and gave it back to you. You are parading it around as philosophy. Through this philosophy, what will you achieve for the fulfillment of your karma and life?

Guruvani

March 1996

You who study and teach philosophy have not seen what the ancestors have done. Many great men came and spoke to honor the path of karma mentioned by the ancestors. We have the rest of that history in our hands. But today it is not possible to know any of this. If you follow historians, you will not get any of it at all. You will not get a single word through history about the path in which the great ancestors and your contemporary great men have worked and failed. Contemporary great men are leading you to the path of knowledge by seeing, knowing, and acting. There is no other justification for it. If another justification is attributed to it, it is contrary to the truth. Without exception, the knowledge we have discovered so far has been handed over to people of other countries. The sorcerers who taught us to give it, get money, fight, win, and live are with us in the guise of guides. What else do we have, who do we have other than karma that has pushed so much tragedy? What do you have to give into the hands of your children who are waiting to know, study, and teach by following your path? So this is how you should understand it. Have the great men born since the beginning of this Kali Yuga experienced happiness or sorrow? Gandhiji was a great man who worked in materialism. Gandhiji did not have any spiritualism, did he? Yet how much sacrifice he has experienced. But not only us, even his followers could not grasp the magnitude of that sacrifice. We could not walk on the path of dharma and grasp the aspect of virtue. This subject should be understood in this way. We have come to a predicament where we cannot give contemporaneity and nurture people who have kept such a sacrifice-rich lifestyle.

Personally looking at it, Gandhiji is a spiritual person. But there is a flaw that it is not suitable for the times. Because he has advocated for Ramarajya. Which is this era of Rama? How many Ramas have come after that? Where do we stand in the karma that teaches Ramo Ramacha Krishna Kalki Janardhana? If so, where can we reach if Ramarajya is brought? There is a history that Gandhiji participated in the Vaikom Satyagraha. Vaikom Satyagraha in the temple

It was not possible to enter. It was to walk along the road around the temple to reach the boat jetty on the western side of the temple. How have you understood this history itself? If this is the distance of our contemporary history, is it easy to learn the truth from ancient history and move forward? Now let's take another history. A Vamana avatar came and trampled down a king who was ruling the country well! Why trample down someone who rules well? Because he is an Asura. If he is an Asura, what imparted Asura-nature to one who rules exemplarily? Does history have any other information to tell us other than the complexity of such myths?

This is the state of our history. How can we detach the contradiction of action that is praised and glorified without being able to know the true state of anything? What do we have to tell our future generations if they stand up straight and ask? Let that be.

How can we see Sree Krishna, as a historian or as Yogeshwara? When we praise 'Yatra yatra Yogeshwara Krishna', this author of the Gita, who is Yogeshwara, came and we could not know him. Is there any meaning in such people saying that Krishna is Yogeshwara? If we speak bluntly, you will say that Krishna has not died, he is merged in the fire of Yoga. Then we all got salvation, right? Each one blabbers such justifying misdeeds, telling stories, enjoying and playing around. Let us change from this storytelling. Let us try to know about Krishna. We cannot move forward without thinking, knowing, acting and suffering as much as that man's sacrifice. To know about that sacrifice, there must be desire, determination, dispassion and love to know about the state of any action that aligns with truth. Dispassion is the respect of love.

There are some things within ourselves that show us how the dispassion of love is. A sacrificer can show us those things within ourselves. It takes a sacrificer to show the sacrifice of Mahatmaji. Even after being kicked by soldiers, without taking it as a big deal, that man is showing what the richness of sacrifice is. How have we seen even that richness of sacrifice? A lineage did not grow to a level where we could take up his steadfastness in dispassion. That became a mess. An unjust society or government did not come forward and show us the history of the contemporary era. How far will we go if we study the history chewed and spat out by a handful of people who waste time talking about some rationalism and liberation without even the slightest connection to sacrifice?

Therefore, we do not need to talk about liberation and sacrifice, nor any nonsense. What is the action suitable for the Dharma that we need to achieve in our lifetime? How much weight of sacrifice is there in that action? What hardship does that sacrifice have? How should we experience that hardship? At least we should know this much. This is the aspect that should make great men worthy of worship. This itself should be the history regarding great men. What have the sacrificers we have celebrated and presented as the greatest sacrificers today been able to achieve? What has the Prophet's last prayer in that graveyard shown? True Muslims cannot deny that. We have denied all the actions that have occurred to recognize such a sacrifice. This is the reason for the downfall of the people.

I am saying what I know about what should be done to bring a change from all this. That is my knowledge. Saying Krishna's sense of Dharma

In Karma, Prathyakshavaham Dharmam is mentioned. That means seeing Dharma manifested. Manifesting does not mean the manifestation that today's poets and scholars talk about. What is their theory of manifestation? For example, how do they talk about the sun rising? It bursts and blooms. Where is the sun that bursts and falls like this? If these mischief-makers can lean another ignorance on top of your ignorance, where do you stand? Where is the sun supposed to burst and bloom? If you are taught like this about this special rule that revolves within the paths of the sky, witnessing a dharma of time, who do they think you are when they say this?

What is Krishna's Prathyakshavaham Dharmam? Krishna is asking, why don't you kill them? I don't know any of that, I can't fight them this way and kill them. I only feel this way about the act of killing them. Do you believe what I say? Definitely. Then why don't you obey that word? Now, I only have doubts about killing here. But you just think about this. Then it is said that Arjuna sat on that chariot with his eyes closed. When he closed his eyes, Arjuna himself sees. There is no harm in killing them. That is how God appears before him. God teaches to kill teachers and relatives. How might God come in your logic? How can you, who deserve compassion saying that not even an ant should be harmed, feel like killing? I am asking the historians. How can you feel like killing? Didn't you see the logic of the Jain theorists, covering their mouths. That is, even germs should not be killed. If germs cannot be killed, how will this earth survive? Then are you and I great sinners before God? How many creatures have we knowingly boiled and eaten? If we can plant and sprout all these seeds, cut them and eat their leaves and roots, isn't that loss of life? What dignity are you establishing with this Advaita word?

We must be convinced of one thing Arjuna saw. That will be your goodness. Where did you see it to say that you are not killing anyone, nor are you causing anyone to be killed? Arjuna then bowed and took up his bow. Let it be true or good, right or wrong, whatever it is, it is manifest. The Karma-vadi Krishna asks Bhishma, who fought and lay on the bed of arrows. You could have gone before this, then why are you lying like this? Then Bhishma says, let this go, Krishna, this will go. When looking at this historical truth said by Bhishma, who are Bhishma and Drona? What was the reason for them to fall like that? Thinking about why Krishna himself stood in this path of Karma, standing as Guru and disciple, and caused them to be killed, we get the meaning of the word Prathyakshavaham Dharmam of the events that happened then in human dharma and those that happened contemporaneously.

Guruvani
