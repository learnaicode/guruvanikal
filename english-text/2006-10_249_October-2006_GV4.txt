[GV4:P1438] October 2006

[GV4:P1439] It is the Yuga Dharma (the duty of the age) that we must understand. There is no point in presenting something without observing what must be observed in that particular era. Is this not our tradition? Have we not been born? Where will you go to correct yourselves now? We only have a very short lifespan. When looking at the grand vision of time, we do not exist even as a mere atom. What will we do here? This is where the truth lies. The knowledge of this doctrine, which pervades both the atomic and the infinite, must begin from the point it has reached. Otherwise, things will not be right. There is nothing to be gained by simply wandering here and there. That is just teaching people foolishness. It is not honesty. We have pushed out onto the veranda the standard of love established by our ancestors. This is the question the Prophet asked. They do not know this. None of us knows it either. All the Mahatmas who have come before and after have strayed from this Yuga Dharma and blocked the path. I do not know if one can study this deeply and say that fish and meat can be consumed together. You are all educated people. I am uneducated. Each of us is left out of the corridor of this sectarian trend, which makes us very sad. What will happen if we do not all feel that this is regrettable? If understanding that is our goal, love must be at the forefront. The respect for that love is required. You also need the wisdom to receive it with humility. Otherwise, it will not be Dharma; it will become Adharma. I respected that experience because, at one time, Mahatmas with all visions spoke of the greatness of scholars in that manner. However, scholars have also come. I am not saying they haven't. Within these five thousand years, many known Mahatmas have come. But the demonic vision they speak of is the remnant of the wicked deeds standing in their practical realm. Do not feel bad when I say this. It is a mistake that can happen to them. It is indeed a wicked deed. We understand this when these Mahatmas come one by one; I will not stand by my knowledge or acquaintance. This is where this calculation is told. We must see all these mentioned Mahatmas without exception. We must grasp that aspect of where they went wrong in their practical realm. Regardless, we cannot reject them and put something else in their place. How it is, we can speak in terms of the countless crores of people who believed and obeyed them, but a crore is not a magnitude here. If we do not accept their decadence, there is no other way. We cannot say it. We can say it, but we should not. You can only proceed by rejecting them. When we accept them, we are taking on that part. We have to experience it. That is one reason you asked. The youths who have come to me so far do not understand it at all.

[GV4:P1440] Among them, only this Pai spoke a truth. Dr. Pai. How many years ago? Eight years ago. This is not a disease worth mentioning. Then I will give you medicine. If I do not experience this, there is another flaw. You will not be able to move forward. You can discard it as unwanted. Shouldn't we see it in the practical action combined with blood, flesh, and marrow? It is not there. I haven't told any of this to anyone here until now. So, seeing what happened to that Mahatma, we should not intellectualize that action but explain it. Are we to intellectualize it and hand it over to the scientists? When I say this, it becomes too much. They cannot do it. The illustrative experience for that is the very science you speak of. Where science hits a dead end, there stands God. If I tell them all this now, won't they mock me? Since you have no way to proceed, I said this to clear a path. The most difficult thing in this world is the action that our ancestors worshipped and made others worship. There is also no knowledge to sustain it. So, it is for this reason that the words spoken are indicated. Seeing this in detail is why it was asked, 'What are you doing to me, man?'

[GV4:P1441] Disciple: The Prophet had to lament like that in the end.

[GV4:P1442] Guru: That is what we must take into account. It is exactly this. The question asked, 'Father, have you forsaken me?' Why did you throw me among these demons? That is the sorrow. If there is no solution to this sorrow, there will be no solution to anything. When I say this, some will like it. If they like it, let them. Anyway, things won't work out just because you like it. Because my lifetime is just a fraction of an atom, isn't it? If I discard even this atom and leave, where will I go? We cannot take anything with us without this. The ultimate truth of a human being is love. If that love is without respect, it is not love. This is where it was lost. We need a person to figure out the path of that love. That is what was said. That is, as far as we are concerned, we should be able to carry this forward. But we can show it to them. Everyone in every religion has said that it will happen. None of them said it merely for the literal meaning of the words. Then what is so special about saying they have said it? It was said after completely gathering all this. This is the benefit of working in this intellectual realm. What will we do here? Once you understand this, there is no need to worry about us at all. That is the only proof I can give you.

[GV4:P1443] Guruvani
