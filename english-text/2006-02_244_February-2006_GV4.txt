[GV4:P1259] February 2006

[GV4:P1260] After your time in this world is over, what thoughts can each of you possibly have? Or what can you pass on to your children? If you have a grandchild in your home, what will you—as a father and mother—tell them? You try to show them God by telling tales of miraculous creations. Is God someone who performs miracles? Tell me if any of you hold such a belief. Are these miracles God itself? These are nothing but some ignorant notions that have clouded the human intellect. As far as we are concerned, regarding human beings—when we say humans, do not segregate them by caste, religion, or class—everyone is equally human. Then what is it? There are disparities. Everyone, whether fortunate or unfortunate, has made their efforts. And that effort is right. What is meant by that 'right'? It is the Chaturvarnya—Brahmana, Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Shudra—according to the Yuga Dharma. We all think that caste originated from this established system of Chaturvarnya. Do not assume so. That history is untrue. The nature of Chaturvarnya merely defines the Dharma of each Yuga. Therefore, out of our ignorance, each person interpreted it in their own different way, resulting in a mistake that came into being.

[GV4:P1261] For us to correct this mistake, it has been billions of years - not just one or two. ٹ What is it? Isn't it what you are learning and teaching now? Not two thousand, four thousand, five thousand, or at most not going beyond ten thousand, you are stating a historical truth. Is this all there is to the origin of this world? So many billions of years ago - now, this Kali Yuga itself, as far as we are concerned, how long is it said to be? It is said to be four hundred and thirty-two thousand years. This four hundred and thirty-two thousand years, only the Kali Yuga that came after Sri Krishna is here. So we don't even know about Sri Krishna. What is it? Such a great a person! But what did we turn him into? For one group of people, he is the author of the Gita; for another group, he is the one who stole the clothes of the Gopis; for another group, he is a true womanizer. What all this womanizer has done, we don't even have the knowledge to see, do not forget the truth of that. But none of this is Sri Krishna's deed that we must believe. Why? These sixteen thousand and eight families, when they were in jail, if he gave them a vision there, you have not observed what it means. These sixteen thousand and eight families, and the later generation of the poet who created it came, then it just became sixteen thousand and eight wives. What a negative deed this is! How can you accept this? At least something that aligns with logic a thing shouldn't be said! So, going after the poet's imagination, abandoning dharma and dancing like an oracle, on top of that knowledge, one is seeing God. How will you see this? In what way will you see God? Then you didn't feel a thing. When talking about this Shudra class, in our homes, there are Shudras, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas, and thus all the Chandalas gathered together people as well. There is a story told here about that. I don't know if you know it. I don't know if the people of this land know it. When Narayana Guru came here, a big rich man here said, it seems: caste and all will change. Okay. Saying this, he said a Chandala resides in you. This man immediately went and the Chandala who lived in his compound was pulled out and removed, it is said. I don't know if you have heard this. So he didn't understand it at all. Our ancestors and those revolving in the sphere of influence of rich people are like this. What are they passing on to us? But a householder shouldn't be like that.

[GV4:P1262] In reality, what constitutes the knowledge of a householder (*Grihasthashrami*)? Their true knowledge lies in preserving their educated intellect without letting it degrade, while possessing children, a spouse, wealth, and authority capable of serving this land, home, and nation. Instead of this, what is currently happening? Everyone is solely focused on becoming highly educated. More specifically, God has bestowed a particular blessing upon you. The very first college in Kerala was established here (Kannur). However, what was the outcome of this college's blessing? You simply became like a westward-looking machine. Whatever occurs there, you indiscriminately gather and bring here. Merely observing and accumulating from the West has become your primary task. As a consequence of such accumulation, what have the North Indians done now? They have reduced the South Indian to the status of a monkey. Consequently, they consider themselves perfectly beautiful and superior in every way, while depicting us as monkeys. Furthermore, they did another thing. They proclaimed that the Vaishnava tradition is supreme. And what of the Shaiva tradition? They designated that as Dakshinamurthy. What are we to understand from this? Shiva is considered a guru of the people of these southern regions, encompassing these four states. By labeling him as Dakshinamurthy and composing texts like the *Dakshinamurthy Stotram* to convince us, they made us fall for this narrative. What did they do next? Along with that, they introduced Shaivism. And alongside this Shaivism, they brought in Shaktism. By continuously associating and combining these concepts, what did this amalgamation result in? It resulted in a state where we gained absolutely no true knowledge. It is a fact that we fell into this trap, whether knowingly or unknowingly. Therefore, even if someone approaches us to explain the truth, we remain incapable of understanding. There is a prevailing belief that devotion to God is exclusively for ascetics (*sanyasis*), and that householders have absolutely no need for it. But what happens if your child or a loved one faces adversity? Or if some form of ruin befalls them? At that moment, they call out to a God. Which God is that? Ultimately, it is the mother—representing nature in accordance with this Shakteya and Shaiva knowledge—who cries out, "My God, please let nothing bad happen." Thus, they pray that nothing untoward happens to the children who are about to be delivered after completing the ten-month gestation period following the menstrual cycle. Consequently, they desire neither the good nor the bad. Our mothers constitute a large community speaking from such profound ignorance. Why have our mothers become this way? Or consider our fathers, who travel elsewhere to work, struggling immensely to support their children, wives, and others—though this might be slightly less prevalent in these parts, it still exists here. If you visit South Travancore, they refer to it in two ways. One is stating that the 'elder is arriving' (*Mooppil varunnu*). The other is 'Father Comrade' (*Achan Sakhavu*). They use these material terms. In this context, there is another group: the materialists (*padartha visheshakkar*). They are the ones who blindly consume concepts of logic and liberation.

[GV4:P1263] Guruvani

[GV4:P1264] March 2006

[GV4:P1265] Therefore, apart from the consequence of having wayward children through these various paths, what else have we achieved? What is the evidence that we have become incapable of upholding the *dharma* (duty) of our home, society, and nation? Today, you must observe the downfall of the Nehru family, who were historically asked to sell this country and leave. I do not believe any of you will attain a position within a system greater than theirs. What is the reason for this? They were generally born into the Vaishnava community. Subsequently, they grew into figures of authority regarding knowledge, wealth, and various fields of action. However, because they solely relied on Western education, they failed to identify and adopt practices that would genuinely benefit the nation's welfare. Yet, they did not intentionally attempt to ruin the country either. The truth is that they inadvertently fell into a trap. Why did they fall into this pit? That question remains unasked. The reason for this downfall lies in the Western culture they imbibed. Here, when we Hindus pray, they might gesture in a certain way. What does it signify? It is an expression of their devotion. What is this? Instead of folding hands in prayer, they make the sign of the cross. Consequently, when you adopted their teachings, your practices also changed accordingly. Through such blind imitation, not only did we lose the path to align ourselves with the ultimate truth, but we have all been reduced to mere scavengers of foreign cultures. You completely fail to comprehend this. If this is the only thing our rulers could provide us, where do we go from here? Because we drifted in that direction, we reached a state where this imperialistic nature seemed to have completely consumed us. Thus, if India initially held this imperial status globally, it eventually shifted outward and later returned. When it returned in this manner, what occurred? Every country began developing its own distinct models, and certain religious adherents became individuals who would not hesitate to commit any atrocity to seize power. We have heard of historical eras where rulers imprisoned their own fathers to reign. And you study this history. Is this what we truly desire? Consequently, we lag behind in all such aspects. Furthermore, this is not a recent phenomenon. You might assume this began yesterday or the day before. No. This is a profound distortion that has been occurring for eons. The root cause is our profound ignorance regarding the path of action established by Manu and his lineage. Even now, there is a path of action that we acknowledge. It manifests as Hindus following the Trinity (*Trimurti*) system, others adhering to the prophetic system, and the presence of isolated spiritual teachers referred to as gurus or the practice of various yogic disciplines.

[GV4:P1266] So, having said Karma, Yoga, Jnana, and Bhakti, their job is to unfold these into four and teach this Karma. But if this is taught, what will this ordinary man do? He will not be able to grasp this. He can only grasp it if he is told what his Karma is. But such an acquaintance is rare as far as we are concerned. When this rarity came into practice, we now see our growing children as the people who tomorrow must teach their own children what this mother or father needs. Therefore, what ٹ is it for these children - what all has that slavery in which this father and mother are trapped done for the children. What are these raised children turning into, by doing and making others do this slave-like labor? You all know that. So, it is in this state that you are raising a family, in the present situation. Then they will start looking for something else for you. What is it? 'Old age home'. They have already searched and kept it ready. For you to go and sit there. What earth can we, who have only learned to become bad in this manner, possibly take up? Does this belong to one religion or one caste, or does it exist for all castes and human beings, and if so, what is this, and what should we do? As an example of this, if we are to have the courage to say that this did not happen to us just today or yesterday, there is a right and wrong based on this religion. Only if you see what that mistake is, can you pick it up and rectify it. One is that, from the day it came to be written in stories as history that the Manvantaras originated under Brahma, straying from the lineage of this Manu, the foundation has been lost. Then what is the other? Various kinds of religious theories came here. What is it? - That God is walking behind us. Saying 'You take this'. And then we don't have to take it. Even when that comes, we have two ways there. Like that, whichever prophet comes, there are two ways there as well. Thus, what we have in our hands is traditional knowledge that stands divided into thousands of sects. Where will we take and give this? Is this how God should be? When each of you shares like this, is God something to be placed there? You don't think about any of this. Everything is like this. No one's God is like that. You should only do this if you understand it that way. The current system of Hindus is not right at all. But what about others? A spirit of the times (Kaladharma) has been stated for theirs. If they can at least survive that much, it is good. But none of us knows what that Kaladharma is. They will say it there. What? 'Antichrist', 'Final Prophet'. Then, whether it is of this Manu or this Brahma himself, that calculation has gone wrong. Then what should happen in this world? Which calculation should we take? The one in the lineage of Manu, or the one in the lineage of Brahma, or the one in the lineage of the prophets? Or the one in the lineage of the visionary? What is this visionary? So, because we do not know any of this, we are fighting each other in such a way that we do not hesitate to do anything to one another. What are you going to do with this? Where are your fighting hands going to be placed? Or where is my God going to stand after fighting? If it is said that we have only been able to work under miscreants who have been unable to think about this so far, who is each one of us? The reason for such degradation is the traditional ignorance that does not impart religious consciousness. Is it to fight each other as you have heard? God is not this. Our path is a course of action that stands firm in the life truth of our minds. Let it be whatever it is. You have the right to affirm that and then think about what is needed beyond that.
