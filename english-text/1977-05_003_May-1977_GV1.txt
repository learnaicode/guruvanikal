[GV1:P0044] May 1977

[GV1:P0045] Dear ones,

[GV1:P0046] The religion I believe in is one of friendship, brotherhood, and truth. Neither I nor my mission desire to move toward a path of self-destruction by blindly believing in anything and standing divided. There are rays of love in everyone, and I wish for everyone to live in harmony and happiness. For this purpose, I perform my duties. I desire to bring you to my path, to the way that has evolved from my penance. I consider that as one of my goals.

[GV1:P0047] Let me now clarify what my paths are. Today, our entire society is polluted. Standing divided, it is moving towards the abyss of destruction every moment. No one has any certainty about where to go or in which direction. What we see is everyone staring at each other blankly. What is the solution to this? That is what needs to be found. Many face various hardships and destroy themselves. They are reeling under incurable, fatal diseases. Economic instability stares many in the face. Thus, I see a large multitude of a deeply disturbed human society around me. They have no idea what to do. Brothers and sisters who consider this human birth a curse, I have only one thing to say to you: I am willing to bestow my strength upon you.

[GV1:P0048] What is the power within me? I know many of you will ask this very question. I shall explain it in detail. My power has been acquired through ceaseless penance. For years, I performed austerities for it. I endured sacrifices. Those sacrifices made me who I am, and they dissolved my very self. You might wish to know where it comes from and what it is. That is natural. Before clarifying that, I wish to take you to our heritage, to the spiritual essence of India.

[GV1:P0049] If one looks into the spiritual eminence of India, it can be seen that a small group of great sages worked with the welfare of humanity as their goal. Through their penance, they elevated our Hindu culture across various stages. Swami Vivekananda and Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa were all resplendent luminaries of that tradition. Not many have been able to walk their path. Leaving that aside, let us come to our Kerala.

[GV1:P0050] Three eminent sages were born here. The first one who rushes to our memory among them is Sankaracharya. The other two are Sri Narayana Guru and Chattampi Swamikal. Their blessings have forever enriched our little Kerala. We continue to see its movements. However, as eras pass, many changes are occurring in the minds of the people. A situation has been created where it is difficult for generations to come forward holding firm to those directives. There is no point in blaming anyone for that. It is something that happens, and it is liable to happen.

[GV1:P0051] The necessity to provide guidance to the people at various periods is increasing immensely today. Only that will bring them peace. Behind the survival of Christianity and Islam, there has been such guidance. Many interpret it in various ways and present it in new forms and expressions. People find it impossible in the present times to blindly cling to old doctrines. The resurrection of these splinter groups is the ultimate consequence of this.

[GV1:P0052] This does not end here. As an unending process, it will continue to exist as long as humans are on the face of this earth. Each religion undergoes different kinds of changes. But its fundamental tenets may not necessarily change. Even so, what I am indicating is that it will take shape in accordance with the era.

[GV1:P0053] In this context, I invite your attention to the problems of Hinduism. Hinduism stands like a massive tree. It has numerous branches. Are there not further branches within those branches? Thus, all of it stands distorted and mutually disconnected. There is a lot to be said about the problems of Hinduism. I believe that what has crystallized within me today is a form derived from years of contemplation regarding this. I have been able to see, hear, and experience many things.

[GV1:P0054] All those are heart-melting incidents of prolonged suffering. My conscience ached at the thought of our society's degradation. From that pain and sorrow, I was able to generate a special vitality. Even as a very young child, I was suppressed by its power. I approached many places for the clarification of doubts, and I understood many things. Finally, the journey towards my goal began. The sacrifice I undertook to reach that goal has made me who I am today.

[GV1:P0055] A time when caste-based sectarianism reigned supreme. I was not imagining that era; I was witnessing it directly. Seeing that, a fire blazed in my mind. There were many instances when I felt that I myself would be consumed by that burning fire. There, I found someone. I became his disciple. I was able to learn a lot from that Guru. When I say that it was solely due to my endurance, do not misunderstand. I had to exercise immense patience. I had to wander aimlessly. I had no thought whatsoever of whether I would succeed there or not. Just one goal. The effort for it. Finally, I found it.

[GV1:P0056] I spoke about the corrupt practices of society. I have a few things to say there. It is about the Aryans who arrived here. They were giving instructions. According to those instructions, those poor folks fought. They were made to believe that they were strong. The poor souls were mesmerized by it. Directives were given to view the remaining sections as despicable creatures and to destroy them upon sight. Following that, that group kept performing those valiant acts. All the other sections were tolerating it. What could they do?

[GV1:P0057] In these circumstances, the section designated as the downtrodden could not organize themselves. Suppressing their pain, those people were cursing. Do you know who bore the brunt of that curse? Those who orchestrated these evil deeds and those who executed them. With that, our communities began to degenerate. When various factions within Hinduism fought each other, all sections were being destroyed. These poor humans were unaware of it. We see in the political arena a process of ascending to power by making people fight and destroy each other, don't we? It means the same existed in our Hindu community as well. As a result of it, there is a large section here that has slipped into ruin.

[GV1:P0058] That group which received the curse, and another group which was branded as downtrodden and marginalized. They are the two groups I see around me here. Both of them have begun to realize that they committed wrong actions. What they need now is upliftment. An ascent from the lowest step. What is required for that?

[GV1:P0059] That is where I firmly assert that a path must be cleared. Only by traversing that path can you attain salvation from these sins. I know numerous families steeped in misery. I am ready to offer my strength to them. Many have already approached me. To all of them, I have only one piece of advice. Move forward with righteous deeds. Seek atonement for your sins. Seek the counsel of Gurus for that.

[GV1:P0060] When speaking of Gurus, I have another matter to remind you of. Today, the Marwaris, Gujaratis, and Sikhs are living prosperously in North India. Many among them are industrialists. They lead an affluent life. They have Gurus to guide them. It is in accordance with the directives of those Gurus that they make their moves in life. You can understand without my saying that they achieve tremendous success in their lives due to this.

[GV1:P0061] Along that path, Gurus are ready to provide guidance in our Hindu community as well. But our people must be willing to recognize and honor them. If so, I firmly believe that we can secure a bright future.

[GV1:P0062] - Santhigiri Karunakara Guru
