[GV1:P2418] October 1989

[GV1:P2419] A functional capacity that ascended within the human intellect! Through this capacity, he subsequently investigated the deeds of primitive humanity. Having studied this, the intellectual pondered how his contemporaneity could be utilized for the benefit of others. Consequently, he contemplated the phenomena within the sphere of the nine planets; he reflected upon its existence, as well as the evolution, sustenance, and dissolution of the solar system. Initially, he assigned positions to the nine planets. All nine planets revolve. The axis of one is positioned lower, such that all others revolve beneath it. That is the Sun. Why do they revolve in this manner? It is not merely to create day and night; evolution must also occur. Evolution must be catalyzed by the power of the Sun. The lunar cold and atmospheric dust descended upon the Earth, transforming into the essence of seeds. The functional capacity that operates from the five elements (*Panchabhoota*), reaching the five senses (*Panchendriya*), and penetrating the five sheaths (*Panchakosha*), must manifest in this manner. Is there any alternative pathway for this?

[GV1:P2420] Disciple: How, then, does creation take place?

[GV1:P2421] Guru: Exactly. Having studied the nine planets in this manner, he imparts this knowledge to humanity. It manifested as Yugas. Which Yugas could they be? The Treta Yuga is calculated by subtracting the duration of Kali Yuga from the Satya Yuga. The Dvapara Yuga is derived by subtracting the duration of Kali from the Treta Yuga. The Kali Yuga comprises four lakh thirty-two thousand (4,32,000) years. This epoch belongs to the Shudra. The prophetic tradition had never attempted to perceive things in such a manner.

[GV1:P2422] Disciple: Did they not attempt to perceive it, or were they unable to attain it?

[GV1:P2423] Guru: They were indeed unable to attain it. When actions unaligned with truth are observed, a conflict arises between the laborer and the capitalist—claiming, "They do not yield all that they acquire. They seek to destroy us." There exists an exploitative class that merely instigates people to fight by propagating such narratives! A class that incites both groups against each other, suggesting that if the capitalist is ruined, the workers will inherit everything! The result: both will be annihilated. The ownership of the karma to destroy both belongs to this class. This is not what is genuinely required. How long is our lifespan? A hundred years at most. Within this span, one must assess the proportion of merit (*punya*) and sin (*papa*) holistically, determining what actions a human should perform at specific times. There is a karma born within the framework of the nine planets, twelve zodiac signs, and twenty-seven constellations. A trajectory was formulated incorporating these twenty-seven constellations, akin to the equator delineated for the Earth. Similar to that imaginary line, this was constructed, and a mathematical system was devised for it. This calculation was conceptualized in India. India's calculation is the most comprehensive. The aforementioned prophets lacked this mathematical insight. Why? Their thirst for inquiry had not reached those depths. This can only be attained by contemplating the underlying causes for the creation of the Earth, the Moon, and so forth. India possesses such a guiding vision derived through this contemplation. There, as previously mentioned, even an insect is accorded a place (compassion). What was the consequence of proceeding with such compassion? The Aryan religion emerged. Various religions were sequentially established. Subsequently, all of this was consolidated to establish Advaita. Madhva attempted to establish Dvaita. All these individuals articulated what they perceived. No matter how long one argues about these matters or emerges victorious in debates, foresight will not simply materialize. Therefore, it is prudent to set that aside. Based on the nine planets, twelve zodiac signs, and twenty-seven constellations, the *Yama* calculation determines what transpires every two and a half to three hours. A certain number of *Yamas* constitute a day; as that calculation becomes infinitesimally smaller, it reaches the fraction of time it takes a healthy human to pierce two stacked tender leaves of a bael tree or lotus with a stylus, penetrating one leaf to reach the other. Identifying that precise moment is the challenge. Then comes the *Mahani* calculation. One thousand *Mahanis* equate to sixty-two and a half *Vinazhikas*. I have studied all this through writing. India possesses a metric of time far more precise than the calculation of a second.

[GV1:P2424] Disciple: Is this not a branch of astrology?

[GV1:P2425] Guru: Astrology is precisely this. Is astrology not entirely based on calculations?

[GV1:P2426] Disciple: However, when calculations are performed currently, they do not seem accurate!

[GV1:P2427] Guru: That is because sufficient effort is not invested to compute them meticulously. One has to dance to the tunes of the world, after all!

[GV1:P2428] Disciple: It is said that such profound calculations were attained! Yet, they are referred to as visionaries who lacked access to such grand metrics. Nevertheless, they did establish a culture here, did they not?

[GV1:P2429] Guru: That is undeniably our own fault. This intellectual alienated us, preventing the Dravidians from uniting, precluding the Shudras from coming together, and thereby keeping people estranged by denying them education. He appropriated it for himself.

[GV1:P2430] Disciple: Who?

[GV1:P2431] Guru: These immigrants. Are they not referred to as those who migrated from the banks of the Sarasvati River?

[GV1:P2432] Guruvani
