Sivagiri Liberation March at Santhigiri

What the Guru spoke to them when they arrived

The story we know about Sivagiri and Narayana Guru is, to some extent, complete ignorance. The reason for this is that we have fallen into a miserable state where we cannot recognize and act upon what Sanathana or Hindu Dharma is. This misfortune did not start today or yesterday. We have no realization of when Sanathana was abandoned, what caused it to be abandoned, or how the Trimurthis came into being. When the Trimurthis arrived, there is a predicament where the face of wisdom does not speak. When we say Sanathana, it is a course of action that utilizes four periods. The four periods are Krita Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dwapara Yuga, and Kali Yuga. We have not understood the concept of 'Kali Shudrakal' mentioned by the author of the Gita or those who respect and know about it. That matter of Shudrakal is what we do not understand. It remains the same even today.

We must consider at least from the time of Sree Krishna. The ignorance of not even knowing that Sree Krishna was born as such a great soul is evident as far as we are concerned. That is, the opportunistic conditioning that taught 'Kali is destruction'. Therefore, spreading a notion among us that Kali is useless, as far as the family members are concerned, the entire knowledge of the house became Kali destruction. Coming along that path, the knowledge of the house, the knowledge of the land, and the knowledge of the country became like that. We have not been able to lead a course of action that respects the great souls who have come until today. Proof of this is that Sree Rama descended into the Sarayu river, or took Jalasamadhi

It is used in the sense of having attained. In truth, we have never been able to investigate the reason why he had to attain Jalasamadhi (water burial). Since then, how many great souls have come in this Kali Yuga. How many crores of great souls have come in the other two yugas. There are many sorrowful stories of us not being able to understand what anything is and act accordingly. We have never searched for this, known it, and acted upon it. We don't even know it. We turned towards the path that the Puranas and epics led us.

Three periods are repeatedly mentioned. It is also said that the dharma of Kali is the Shudra era. What is a Shudra? In the karma practiced as four varnas, the dharma is that the Shudra varna is fully entitled to knowledge. What is called Shudra was changed to 64 Nairs in Tamil Nadu and 84 in Kerala. Even these 84 castes do not know what they have done. This has not appeared in the history of the great country of India to date. Isolated gurus and some people who practiced yoga, jnana, karma, and bhakti went their separate ways. Therefore, caught in a dilemma of not being able to distinguish what karma and justice are, we became completely like strangers. This is an ignorance that cannot be corrected in any way.

Narayana Guru worked seeing all this. He is not talking about caste and religion. Many people say that Narayana Guru's ideology is socialism and so on. He behaved with the desire to give a karma similar to knowledge to the Shudra class as a whole, to these 84 castes, and through that karma, to attain knowledge. But there was not even a single eye with him to see it in that way. Therefore, the destruction of Kali, which acted without recognizing this Yuga Dharma, is being sown. This has been going on for so many Kali Yugas.

According to the teaching that Kali is destruction, how many people have seen Kali as destruction. Kali is not destruction. The karma given to the Shudra clan is the knowledge that was mixed with the Vaishya, Kshatriya, and Brahmin over three periods, and this belongs to the fourth quarter. So, an inability to act, not knowing what to do in this fourth quarter, stands bewildered before us. This is due to the misfortune that occurred because the dharma of the time was not known. However, some people say with some words here and there. In Jnanappana also, these words are highlighted, asking if Kali is not the best among the times.

The ignorance that sannyasins are mere beggars, or mere mendicants, has placed a completely great flaw among the people. Sannyasins are neither beggars nor mendicants. Besides, I am speaking in the sense you call a sannyasin. Our people often ask if he is a Siddha. But Siddhi does not mean sannyasa or jnana. By worshipping each god and goddess and showing some of their respective feats, we see a mark of magical powers. We assume this is sannyasa. No, this is not sannyasa. A sannyasin is one who has passed through the Satya Yuga, Treta Yuga, and Dvapara Yuga over many ages and goes on completing the dharma of Kali. If such a great person is born and comes among these people, what we see today is the remainder of those who traveled with many evil desires, unable to recognize it, and many householders who thought that no one should be greater than me. This ignorance has not affected one caste or religion; all castes and all religions have fallen into this path. So, an understanding about this did not fully come into the guru's paths. Nor was it recognized what the guru's path is.

A Guru is not one who comes and seeks knowledge. A Guru is one who has seen and acted upon what the evolutionary function of life is over many ages. No individual knows about such a Guru. Since Sree Krishna, that is, at least within the last five thousand years, not a single scholar or layman has said what the ignorance that happened to India is.
