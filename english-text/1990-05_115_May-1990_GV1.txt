[GV1:P2511] May 1990

[GV1:P2512] Disciple: Did knowledge exist on earth before the Vedic order arrived here? Is the Guru saying that this is not their knowledge?

[GV1:P2513] Guru: Brahmins are those who came from East Asia. They themselves state this in a mantra portion of the Vedas. They came here, established centers of intellect here, made the local Vaishyas and Shudras do the work, and became virtuous themselves, or rather, souls who imparted virtue. By becoming laborers, the Shudras lost their knowledge. Numerous attempts were made to rectify and clarify this. Sree Krishna was born in the Yadava clan. Krishna’s uncle, Kamsa, was also born for this purpose. Kamsa looked up to imperial rule, believed that to be correct, and tried to destroy all this, didn't he? But because it was God's will that Kamsa's model was not right, Krishna emerged. Through a clan called the Yadava clan, this action was transformed into an action of wisdom. But it did not succeed. Therefore, they defeated us by even introducing the Devadasi system (dasiyattam) in the temple tradition. Because of this degradation, they were certain that our ancestors would only stand on this particular side. They had a firm conviction that only as much as the concept in which consciousness is fixed while performing an action will be of use to us. If you go to a plastic company and ask for gold ornaments, will you get them? Just like that, we unknowingly fell into an order. When we saw the color and attire of the object they showed us, we assigned it the value of gold. Not only that, we also desired to realize it and become it. Thus, the intelligent ones globally subdued and ruled the majority of the people within their system. Because we had this shortcoming, the British and the Mughals, who came in the prophetic tradition, were able to subjugate us. And what about those belonging to the society forged by this Vedic religion? If you look at Kerala itself, you will know; in Kozhikode, Thalassery, and Kannur, most people have become Muslims. Even today, they proudly say that they belonged to the Vedic religion, and that their ancestors were Brahmins.

[GV1:P2514] When the Mughals came here, the Vedic people became helpless. The poor people also accepted Islam. When the poor joined the Mughals, there was an anxiety that they would come to the forefront. The king, before this conversion was complete, had someone belonging to the Brahmin clan converted, and imposed ostracization (bhrashtu) on the one who went across the sea to work. Thus, he was made a Muslim. They thought that this way, their own man was not destroyed, and they could also say that he had prospered. The entire Malappuram district is like this.

[GV1:P2515] Disciple: It is said that religious conversion is also a political process, isn't it?

[GV1:P2516] Guru: Yes, it was made so. Those who were converted stood in the opposite camp and attacked us. These poor people were also caught and added to their side. Here, because of caste, one cannot walk freely, cannot eat together, cannot see each other—such meaningless actions flourished. By constantly talking about it, it spread to the lower-strata people. It became caste. God was placed within the caste—the one sitting here is my God. Your God is a little further away. This temple was consecrated by bringing a Namboothiri. In truth, there is no Tantric essence in this. It is just taking a stone, placing it, and entering a trance. Another person will say that there is a Mantramoorthi (deity of mantras) in his family. He is the smartest. When the deity comes over here in a trance, all one needs to do is chant to my Mantramoorthi once. Then nothing will happen. Yet another will say that the God in his sacred grove (kavu) is a Brahmarakshass. It has gone on worshipping Brahman. This is the real Brahmarakshass. Therefore, I will not worship any of these forests and bushes. But who will tell him how to cross his different states of being? Here, a deviation from the path occurs. But there have been paths here that clarified this deviation. As far as South India is concerned, a tradition called 'Parayi Petta Panthirukulam' (the twelve clans born to the Pariah woman) was implemented here. Each of those who belonged to it has proved different things. Even after all this knowledge came, this class called caste turned around and not only destroyed all this knowledge, but even erased their memories. Who eliminated them like this?

[GV1:P2517] Disciple: Wasn't it those who stood at the forefront?

[GV1:P2518] Guru: Yes.

[GV1:P2519] Disciple: Apart from the division between the lower castes and the upper castes, it is heard that there is division among the upper castes themselves. We hear that there was a controversy between the Iyers and the Iyengars regarding how to wear the long caste mark (Nedumkuri) on the forehead.

[GV1:P2520] Guru: All this is an absurdity forged by the Vedic religion. The Iyengar has one extra line. Then he claims that he is the greater one. The other claims, no, he is the greater one. Drawing it blindly without sense, and then studying that it is the greatest—this mischief spread again. That became the religion of the Iyengars. Ego (Ahankara) is his religion.

[GV1:P2521] Guruvani
